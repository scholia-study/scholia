# Privacy

*Last updated: 29 July 2026*

Scholia ([scholia.study](https://scholia.study)) is an interactive reader for
primary-source texts, operated by **Dialogos AS** (Norwegian org. no.
935 999 464), which is the data controller for the personal data described in
this policy. This
policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and what
your rights are. All personal data we hold is stored and processed within the
European Union, and we comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation
(GDPR).

## What we collect

**Account data.** When you create an account we store your email address,
display name, handle, and — if you sign up with a password — a hashed form of
that password (never the password itself). If you sign in with Google or
GitHub, we receive your email address and name from that provider; we never
see the password you use there.

**Content you create.** Quotations, notes, articles, and feedback you submit
are stored so we can show them to you (and, where you choose to publish, to
other readers).

**Billing data.** Paid subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Stripe holds
your payment details; we store only what we need to manage your subscription
(such as your subscription status and a Stripe customer reference). We never
see or store your card number.

**Technical data.** When you use the site we process technical information
such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. We use this
solely to keep the service running: authentication, security, traffic
measurement, and error monitoring.

## Why there is no cookie banner

We don't show a cookie banner because we don't need one: every cookie we set
and every piece of data we collect is used **for our own operation of the
service only** — keeping you signed in, measuring traffic, and diagnosing
errors. We use no third-party advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking,
and we never share or sell your data for marketing purposes.

## Service providers

We use a small number of service providers to run Scholia. Each processes
data on our behalf, under contract, and only for the purposes described here:

- **Better Stack** (error and performance monitoring, via the Sentry SDK) —
  receives error reports and performance traces so we can find and fix
  problems. Hosted in the EU.
- **PostHog** (product analytics) — will receive first-party 
  usage analytics so we can understand
  how the site is used. EU hosting.
- **Stripe** (payments) — processes subscription payments.

We also send transactional emails (email verification, password reset) to the
address on your account.

## What we don't do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not run third-party ad trackers or cross-site tracking of any kind.
- We do not share what you've read or annotated with anyone else, unless you
  publish it yourself.

## Retention

We keep your account data and content for as long as your account exists. If
you delete your account, your personal data is deleted; error logs and
traffic records are kept only for a limited period and then discarded.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, and erase your personal
data, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, to object to or restrict
certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory
authority.
