# hegel4 translation followups

Cases a translator or verifier wants the user to rule on: a `proposed` term that reads badly, a sentence that cannot keep 1:1 parity, a source oddity. One bullet per case, with file and §.

## Terms (pilot, files 002–029)

- Nine `proposed` rows added from the pilot: Nachdenken = reflective thinking, Gehalt = import, Räsonnement = ratiocination, Empirie = empirical observation, Stoff = material, Gegenständlichkeit = objecthood, Entgegensetzung = counterposition, Eigenschaft = property (a person's: traits), Meinung = opinion.
- 019 § 99 A: Sache = "the thing at issue" (the matter at issue, for instance a house or red); Materie occurs a few sentences later, and "the fact" read badly.
- 004 §§ 2, 4, 13: Verschiedenheit = diversity reads stiffly ("a diversity from thinking"); kept for consistency with Unterschied = difference.
- 027 § 119: zu Grunde gehen = "go to ground", keeping the Grund play at the cost of "perish".
- 015 § 87 A: höchstes Wesen = "the supreme essence"; Allerrealste = "the all-real".
- 016 § 95 A: Vernichtung / vernichtigt = nullification / nullified, kept apart from aufheben.
- 021 § 105: an ihm selbst = "in itself", the same as an sich.
- 017 § 98 A: Kant's "Anfangsgründe" = "first principles", not the standard English title.
- 004: new single renderings Urwahre = original truth, Werkmeister = master-builder, Altklugheit = precocious wisdom, Gesinnungen = sentiments, Rechtswissenschaft = science of right.

## Parity-driven renderings

- 026 § 115 A: "A cannot be A and not A at once." reordered, because English ending on a capital "A." reads as an initial and does not split.
- 027 § 119 A: "+A and as −A alike": "alike" added for the same initial trap.
- 004 § 10 A: "(p. XIX)" put in parentheses so the citation does not split.

## Source oddities

- 004 § 12 A: "das Wissen von Gott wir von allem Übersinnlichen": "wir" is evidently a typo for "wie"; translated "like".
- 006 § 20 A: `{{{ 20.65 }}}` sits before "ist" in "das Werk des Gedankens {{{ 20.65 }}} ist"; the English marker sits after "work".
- 003: "V. 39." and "im folg. 39. V." both name verse 39; the German probably intends 38 and 39; mirrored literally.
- 003: a single dash in "seiner – jedoch bereits verstorbener Zeitgenossen" rendered as a pair.
- 003, last paragraph: "baldige Verschluss der zweiten Ausgabe" taken as "sell-out" from context.
- 023 § 112 A: a stray semicolon ("vom unmittelbaren Sein; macht jene Reflexion") rendered as one clause.
- 004 footnote 1: Thomson's journal title is given in its real English form; check it against the actual title.

## Gate notes

- 007 § 35 A: the list of paired categories ("contingency and necessity; … good and evil") converges with gsh/bd at 30–34 words because it is pure locked terms; without it the file measures 2.2–2.6%. Passed on review.
- 008, 005, 014, 018, 024, 025: title-only files, too short to measure.
- 155 § 487 (145 words): the division list (formal, abstract right / morality / ethical life in family, civil society and state) converges at 8% on locked terms; no run over 11 words. Passed on review.

### Term table friction

- 001 fn1: "dem Schein nach" = "in semblance"; Schein = shine reads badly in that idiom (main text keeps "a mere shine").
- 001: the Nachdenken / nachfolgendes Denken play is lost under the locked "reflective thinking".
- 011 § 62: beschränkt (adj.) = limited reads oddly beside Schranke = restriction.
- 035/036 §§ 147–148 vs § 153: Sache = "fact" in the condition/fact/activity triad, "the original matter" in § 153; ruled by the orchestrator to stay as is.
- 048 § 185: Mitte as plain noun = "middle" rather than "middle term".

### Parity-driven renderings

- 001: "p. IX" → "page IX and following"; "Mr. v. Bader" → "Mr. von Bader"; a clause reworded so "Th." does not end a sentence before a dash.
- 011 § 64 A: `Respons. ad II\. Objekt\. De Methodo IV\. Ep\. I. 118.`: the English needs escapes the German doesn't.
- 011 fn3: "Traktat. cur Deus homo" → "Treatise, cur Deus homo".
- 035 § 143 A: Kant quotation uses straight quotes, not » «.
- 042 § 171: "(§ 170.) – In the copula…" keeps the German split.

### Source oddities

- 001, paragraph 4: "diese Wissenschaft nur mit jenem Widerspruche selbst und mit dessen Übertünchung im Widerspruche ist" looks garbled; rendered literally.
- 001: Fermentis Cognitionis (ablative in the German) given as "Fermenta Cognitionis".
- 010 § 51 A: Dasein = existence beside Existenz = concrete existence reads clumsily; 010 § 48 A "content and import" (Gehalt) stiff.
- 034 § 140 A: emphasis on "_to_" carried from the German reads stiff.
- 001: `{{{ 20.6 }}}` and `{{{ 20.9 }}}` sit a word or two off in the English.
- 030 § 130: "in der Reflexion- des Dings in-sich" is a typo for "Reflexion-in-sich des Dings".
- 035 § 148: "ist dieser Prozess als die äußere Notwendigkeit" looks garbled; rendered "counts as external necessity".
- 054 § 204 A: "einerseits" without "andererseits"; possibly corrupt.
- 055 § 215 A: stray enumerator "a\. _Prozess_": the English drops the escape (lowercase follows).
- 060 § 236: Gegenstand beside Objekt = "the thing it faces" (cf. 050 § 193 A "something confronted"); a shared rendering for Gegenstand-vs-Objekt contrasts may be wanted.
