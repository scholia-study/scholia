## c. The Elemental Process

## {{ 286 }} § 286.

The individual identity, under which the different elements and their diversity from one another and from their unity are bound, is a dialectic that makes up the physical life of the earth, the _meteorological_ _process_; the elements, as non-self-subsistent moments, have their subsistence in it alone, just as they are _generated_ in it and _posited_ as existent, after they have previously been developed from the _in-itself_ as moments of the concept.

::: remark
{{ 286 A }} As the determinations of common mechanics and of non-self-subsistent bodies are applied to absolute mechanics and the free central bodies, so the _finite_ physics of _isolated_ individual bodies is taken for the very same thing as the earth's process, which is a free and self-subsistent physics. It is held to be a triumph of science to recognize again, and to demonstrate, in the universal process of the earth the same determinations that show themselves in the processes of isolated corporeality. But in the field of these isolated bodies the determinations immanent in the free existence of the concept are {{{ 20.288 }}} reduced to the relation of entering _externally_ into relation to one another, of existing as circumstances independent of one another; likewise activity appears as externally conditioned, hence contingent, so that its products remain just as external formations of corporealities presupposed as self-subsistent and persisting so. – The demonstration of that equality or rather analogy is brought about by abstracting from the peculiar differences and conditions, and this abstraction thus produces superficial universalities, such as attraction, forces and laws in which the particular and the determinate conditions are lacking. In applying _concrete_ modes of the activities that show themselves in _isolated_ corporeality to the sphere in which the differentiated corporealities are only _moments_, the external circumstances required in that circle are usually, in this one, partly overlooked, partly added by invention according to analogy. – These are, in general, applications of categories of a field in which the relations are _finite_ to a sphere within which they are _infinite_, that is, according to the concept.

The fundamental defect in the consideration of this field rests on the fixed representation of the substantial, unalterable _diversity_ of the elements, which has once been established by the understanding from the processes of _isolated_ materials. Where higher transitions also show themselves in these, for instance that in the crystal water becomes solid, light and heat disappear and so forth, reflection provides itself with a help through nebulous and empty representations of _dissolution_, of _becoming-bound-latent_ and the like. Here belongs essentially the transformation of all the relations in the phenomena into _materials_ and _matters_, in part _imponderable_, whereby every physical existence is turned into the already mentioned _chaos_ of matters and of their going out and in in the invented pores of every other, where not only the {{{ 20.289 }}} concept but also representation gives out. Above all _experience_ itself gives out; an empirical existence is still assumed, while it no longer shows itself empirically.
:::

## {{ 287 }} § 287.

The process of the earth is continuously fanned by its _universal_ _self_, the activity of _light_, its original relation to the sun, and then further particularized according to the position of the earth toward the sun (climates, seasons and so forth). – The _one_ _moment_ of this process is the _diremption_ of the individual identity, the tension into the moments of self-subsistent opposition in rigidity and in selfless neutrality, whereby the earth proceeds toward dissolution, on one side into becoming a crystal, a moon, on the other side a body of water, a comet, while _the_ _moments_ of individuality strive to make real their connection to their _self-subsistent_ roots.

## {{ 288 }} § 288.

The _other_ _moment_ of the process is that being-for-itself, to which the sides of the counterposition proceed, sublates itself as negativity driven to its extreme; – the _self-igniting_ _consumption_ of the attempted differentiated subsistence, through which their essential linkage is restored, and the earth has become real and _fruitful_ _individuality_.

::: remark
{{ 288 A }} One may count earthquakes, volcanoes and their eruptions as belonging to the process of _rigidity_, of the negativity of being-for-itself becoming free, that is, to the process of fire; the like is also said to appear on the moon. Clouds, by contrast, may perhaps be viewed as the start of _cometary_ corporeality. The _thunderstorm_, however, is the complete appearance of this process, to which the other meteorological phenomena attach themselves as beginnings or moments and unripe {{{ 20.290 }}} executions of it. Physics has so far been able to come to terms neither with the formation of rain (notwithstanding the conclusions drawn from observations by _de_ _Luc_ and pressed against the _theories_ _of_ _dissolution_ among the Germans by the ingenious _Lichtenberg_), nor with lightning, nor even with thunder; just as little with other meteorological phenomena, in particular the _atmospherics_, in which the process itself proceeds as far as the beginning of an earthly nucleus. For the understanding of those most everyday phenomena, least of all has anything satisfactory so far been done in physics.
:::

## {{ 289 }} § 289.

Since the _concept_ of matter, gravity, lays out its moments first as self-subsistent but elemental realities, the earth is the _abstract_ ground of individuality. Within its process it posits itself as the _negative_ _unity_ of the abstract elements lying outside one another, and so as _real_ individuality.
