## b. Cognition

## {{ 223 }} § 223.

The idea has free existence _for_ _itself_ when it takes universality as the element of _its_ _existence_, that is, when objectivity itself is the concept and the idea has itself as its object. Subjectivity, determined in the idea as universality, is _pure_ _distinguishing_ _inside_ the idea, – an intuiting that holds to itself in this identical universality. Determinate distinguishing, however, is the further _judgment_ of repelling itself from itself as a totality, and, indeed, of _presupposing_ _itself_ initially as an _external_ _universe_. These are two judgments, identical _in_ _themselves_ though not yet _posited_ as identical.

## {{{ 20.222 }}} {{ 224 }} § 224.

Hence the relation between these two ideas, identical _in_ _themselves_ or as life, is the _relative_ one, and that constitutes the determination of _finitude_ in this sphere. The relation is that of _reflection_: the idea's distinguishing within itself is just the _first_ judgment, the _presupposing_ not yet being _a_ _positing_, and for the subjective idea the objective is accordingly the immediate world _found_ ready, that is, the idea as life in the appearance of _singular_ _concrete_ _existence_. Because this judgment is pure distinguishing _within_ the idea itself (preceding §), however, the idea is, all at once, both itself and _its_ _other_ _for_ _itself_, and it is therefore the _certainty_ that this objective world is identical with it _in_ _itself_. – Reason comes to the world with absolute faith that it can posit this identity and raise its certainty to _truth_, driven also to posit as null the opposition that is, _for_ _reason_, null _in_ _itself_.

## {{ 225 }} § 225.

Generally, this process is _cognition_. It sublates, _in_ _itself_ and in _One_ activity, the opposition, that is, the one-sidedness of subjectivity along with that of objectivity. At first, however, the sublating happens only _in_ _itself_; the process as such is therefore itself directly burdened with the finitude of this sphere, and it divides into the _doubled_ movement of the drive, posited as different: the one movement sublates the one-sidedness of the idea's _subjectivity_ by receiving the _existing_ world into subjective representing and thinking, and fills the abstract certainty of itself with this objectivity, which then counts as true, as _content_; – the other, conversely, sublates the _one-sidedness_ of the objective world, which here, in contrast, counts merely as a _shine_, a collection of contingencies and shapes null in themselves, by determining that world through the _inner_ of the subjective, which here counts as the truly existent objective, and by forming the latter into it. The first is the drive of knowing toward truth, _cognition_ _as_ _such_, – the {{{ 20.223 }}} _theoretical_ activity, – the second the drive of the _good_ toward its accomplishment, – _willing_, the idea's _practical_ activity.
