## a. The Natural Soul

## {{ 391 }} § 391.

The _universal_ _soul_ must not be fixed as a _world-soul_, as it were as a subject, since what it is, is merely the universal _substance_, and this possesses actual truth solely as _singularity_, as subjectivity. Thus it shows itself as singular, but immediately only as _being_ soul, which has natural determinatenesses in it. These have, so to speak, _free_ existence _behind_ its ideality, that is, they are for consciousness natural objects, to which, however, the soul as such does not relate as to something external. Rather it has these determinations in itself as _natural_ _qualities_.
