## β) The Sex-Relation

## {{ 369 }} § 369.

This first diremption of the genus into species, and the further determination of these to the immediate, exclusive being-for-itself of singularity, is only a negative and hostile conduct toward others. But the genus is just as essentially an affirmative relation of singularity to itself in it: so that, in being, exclusively, one individual against another individual, it continues itself into this _other_ and feels itself in this _other_. This relation is a _process_ that begins with the _need_, in that the individual, as a _single_ one, is not adequate to the immanent genus and is at the same time its identical relation to itself in one unity; it thus has the _feeling_ of this lack. The genus in it is therefore, as a tension against the {{{ 20.370 }}} inadequacy of its single actuality, the drive to attain its self-feeling in the other of its genus, to integrate itself through the union with it, and through this mediation to close the genus together with itself and bring it to concrete existence – _copulation_.

## {{ 370 }} § 370.

The product is the _negative_ _identity_ of the different singularities, as _genus_ _that_ _has_ _come_ _to_ _be_ a sexless life. But on the _natural_ side it is this genus only _in_ _itself_, different from the singular ones whose difference has perished in it, itself an immediately _singular_ one, which has the determination to develop into the same natural individuality, the same difference and transience. This process of procreation issues in the bad infinity of progress. The genus preserves itself only through the perishing of the individuals, who in the process of copulation have fulfilled their determination, and, insofar as they have no higher one, go thereby toward death.
