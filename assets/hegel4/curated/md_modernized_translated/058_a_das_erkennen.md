## a. Cognition

## {{ 226 }} § 226.

Cognition is finite in a general way, owing to the one judgment that lies in the _presupposition_ of the opposition (§ 224), a judgment against which cognition's own doing is the contradiction lodged within it; and this finitude takes further shape in cognition's own idea, where the idea's moments assume the form of diversity from one another and, though complete, come to stand in the relation of reflection, not of the concept, to each other. The material, as something given, is therefore assimilated by being _received_ into determinations of the concept which at once remain _external_ to it, and which likewise confront one another in diversity. Reason is here at work as _understanding_. Hence the truth reached by such cognition is likewise only _the_ _finite_ truth; the concept's infinite truth is fixed for it as an aim existing merely _in_ _itself_, a _beyond_. In its external doing, nonetheless, it is guided by the concept, whose determinations form the inner thread of its advance.

## {{ 227 }} § 227.

Finite cognition presupposes the _distinguished_ as an existent found standing over against it, – the manifold _facts_ of external nature or of consciousness, – and so 1) its activity has, in the first place, _formal_ _identity_ or the _abstraction_ of universality as its form. This activity consists, accordingly, in dissolving the concrete that is given, in isolating its differences and giving these the form of _abstract_ _universality_; or else in letting the concrete stand as _ground_ and, by abstracting from the particularities that seem inessential, in raising out of it a concrete universal, the _genus_, or the force and the law; – _analytic_ _method_.

## {{{ 20.224 }}} {{ 228 }} § 228.

2) This _universality_ is also a _determinate_ one; here the activity proceeds along the moments of the concept, which in finite _cognition_ is the _determinate_ _concept_ of the understanding and lacks infinity. Receiving the object into this concept's forms is the _synthetic_ _method_.

## {{ 229 }} § 229.

α) The _definition_ arises when cognition first brings the object into the form of the determinate concept in general, thereby positing its _genus_ and its universal _determinateness_. The analytic method (§ 227) supplies the definition's material and its grounding. The determinateness is meant, however, to be merely a _mark_, that is, to serve a cognition that is external to the object and only subjective.

## {{ 230 }} § 230.

β) Stating the concept's second moment, the determinateness of the universal as _particularization_, yields the _division_, which follows some external point of view.

## {{ 231 }} § 231.

γ) Taken in _concrete_ _singularity_, where the determinateness that was simple in the definition is grasped as _a_ _relation_, the object is a synthetic relating of _distinct_ determinations; – a _theorem_. Because they are diverse, their identity is a _mediated_ one. Providing the material that forms the intermediate links is the _construction_, and the mediation itself, which makes the necessity of that relation emerge for cognition, is the _proof_.

::: remark
{{ 231 A }} Going by the usual accounts of how the synthetic and the analytic method differ, it looks on the whole arbitrary which {{{ 20.225 }}} of the two one chooses to use. Suppose the concrete, which the synthetic method presents as a _result_, is _presupposed_; the abstract determinations that formed the _presuppositions_ and the _material_ of the proof can then be analyzed out of it as _consequences_. In the geometrical course the algebraic _definitions_ of curves are _theorems_; similarly, if the Pythagorean proposition were adopted as the definition of the right-angled triangle, analysis would turn up the propositions that geometry had earlier proved for its sake. That the choice is arbitrary rests on the fact that each method starts from something _externally_ _presupposed_. According to the concept's nature, analyzing takes precedence, since it first has to raise the empirical-concrete material, as given, into the form of universal abstractions, and only these can then be placed ahead as definitions in the synthetic method.

These methods are essential and have had brilliant success in their own field, but that they cannot be used for philosophical cognition is obvious of itself, since they have presuppositions and since cognition within them behaves as understanding, advancing along formal identity. In _Spinoza_, who used the geometrical method especially, and indeed for _speculative_ concepts, its formalism is at once striking. The _Wolffian_ philosophy, which carried the method to the extreme of pedantry, is also by content a metaphysics of the understanding. – In more recent times the abuse of so-called _construction_ has replaced the abuse made of these methods' formalism in philosophy and in the sciences. _Kant_ brought into circulation the notion that mathematics _constructs_ its _concepts_; this meant only that mathematics deals with _no_ _concepts_, but with abstract determinations of _sensible_ _intuitions_. And so the specifying of _sensible_ determinations taken up from _perception_, with the concept bypassed, and the further formalism of classifying philosophical and scientific objects in tables on a presupposed schema, moreover at will and at one's discretion, – have been called a _construction_ _of_ _concepts_. In the background there is {{{ 20.226 }}} doubtless an obscure representation of the _idea_, of the unity of _concept_ _and_ _objectivity_, and of the idea's being concrete. That game of so-called constructing is far from presenting this _unity_, however, which only the _concept_ as such is, and the sensible-concrete of intuition is just as little a concrete of reason and of the idea.

Geometry, incidentally, is concerned with the _sensible_ yet _abstract_ _intuition_ of space, in which it can without hindrance fix simple determinations of the understanding; for this reason it alone possesses the synthetic method of finite cognition in perfected form. Yet in its course – and this is much to be remarked – it finally runs into _incommensurabilities_ and _irrationalities_, where, should it wish to determine further, it is _driven_ _out_ beyond the principle of the understanding. Here as so often elsewhere a reversal occurs in the terminology: what is called _rational_ is what belongs to the _understanding_, while what is called _irrational_ is rather the beginning and trace of _rationality_. Other sciences, once they come, as of necessity and often happens to them (since they do not move within the simplicity of space or number), to the limit of advancing by way of the understanding, find an easy way out. They break off its consistency and import from outside, from representation, opinion, perception or wherever else, whatever they need, frequently the contrary of what came before. This finite cognition is unconscious of its method's nature and of the method's relation to the content; so it perceives neither that, as it proceeds through definitions, divisions and so forth, it is guided onward by the necessity of _the_ _determinations_ _of_ _the_ _concept_, nor where its limit lies, nor, once it has passed beyond it, that it is in a field where the determinations of the understanding no longer hold, even though it still crudely uses them there.
:::

## {{{ 20.227 }}} {{ 232 }} § 232.

The _necessity_ produced by finite cognition in the _proof_ is at first an external one, determined only for subjective insight. In necessity as such, however, cognition has itself abandoned its presupposition and starting point, the _finding_ and _givenness_ of its content. In itself, necessity as such is the concept that relates itself to itself. The subjective idea has therefore arrived in itself at what is determined in and for itself, at the _non-given_ and hence at what is _immanent_ in it as _subject_, and it passes over into the _idea_ of _willing_.
