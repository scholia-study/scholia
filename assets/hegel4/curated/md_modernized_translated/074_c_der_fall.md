## c. Fall

## {{ 267 }} § 267.

Fall is the _relatively free_ motion, _free_ in that, posited through the _concept_ of the body, it is the appearance of its own gravity; it is therefore _immanent_ to the body. But at the same time, as only the _first_ negation of externality, it is _conditioned_; the _distance_ from the connection with the center is therefore still the _externally_ posited, _contingent_ determination.

::: remark
{{ 267 A }} The laws of motion concern _magnitude_, and essentially that of the time elapsed and of the space traversed in it; they are immortal discoveries, which do the highest honor to the analysis of the understanding. The non-empirical _proof_ of them is a further matter, and this too has been supplied by mathematical mechanics, so that even the science grounded on the empirical is not content with merely empirical _showing_ (demonstrating). The presupposition in this a priori proof is that the velocity in fall _is_ uniformly accelerated; the proof, however, consists in transforming the _moments_ of the _mathematical_ formula into _physical_ forces, into an _accelerating_ force that is supposed to give an (identical) impulse in each moment of time,[^1] and into a force of _inertia_ {{{ 20.262 }}} that is supposed to continue the (greater) velocity attained in each moment of time, – determinations wholly without empirical warrant, just as the concept has nothing to do with them. More closely, the determination of magnitude, which here contains a ratio of <i>powers</i>, is brought into the shape of a _sum_ of two mutually independent elements, and the qualitative determination connected with the concept is thereby killed. As a _consequence_ of the law thus supposedly proved it is stated »that in uniformly accelerated motion the velocities are proportional to the times.« In fact, however, this proposition is nothing but the quite simple definition of uniformly accelerated motion itself. Plainly uniform motion has the spaces traversed proportional to the times; _accelerated_ motion is that in which the _velocity_ becomes greater in each of the following parts of time, uniformly accelerated motion therefore that in which the {{{ 20.263 }}} velocities are proportional to the times elapsed; thus V/t, that is, s/t². This is the simple true proof. – V is velocity _in general_, the still _indeterminate_; so it is _at the same time_ the _abstract_, that is, the plainly uniform. The difficulty that arises for that proving lies in this, that V is at first in question as indeterminate velocity in general, yet presents itself in the mathematical expression as s/t, that is, as plainly uniform. That detour of proving, drawn from the mathematical exposition, serves this need, to take velocity as the plainly uniform s/t and to pass from it to s/t². In the proposition that velocity is proportional to the times, velocity is at first said in general; it is then superfluously posited mathematically as s/t, the plainly uniform, the force of inertia is thereby brought in and this moment ascribed to it. By its being proportional to the times, however, it is rather determined as the uniformly accelerated s/t², and that determination of s/t has no place here and is excluded[^2].

{{{ 20.264 }}} The law of _fall_ is, as against the abstract uniform velocity of the dead, externally determined mechanism, a _free_ law of nature, that is, one that has a side in it determined from the _concept_ of the body. Since it follows that it must be possible to derive it from this concept, the latter is to be placed before us and the way indicated in which Galileo's law, »that the spaces traversed are related as the _squares_ of the times elapsed,« is connected with the determination by the concept.

This connection, however, is to be regarded as lying simply in this, that because the concept here comes to determining, the determinations of time and space by the concept become _free_ in relation to each other, that is, their _determinations_ _of_ _magnitude_ conduct themselves according to them. Now _time_ is the moment of _negation_, of being-for-itself, the principle of the one, and its magnitude (some empirical number) is to be taken, in relation to space, as the _unit_ or as denominator. _Space_, on the other hand, is _being-outside-one-another_, and indeed of _no_ _other_ _magnitude_ than precisely the magnitude of time; for the velocity of this _free_ motion is this, that time and space are not _external_, not contingent to one another, but their determination is _one_. The form of the being-outside-one-another of space, opposed as such to the form of time, to unity, and without any other determinateness mingling in, is the _square_, – the {{{ 20.265 }}} magnitude _coming_ _out_ _of_ _itself_, positing itself in a second dimension, thus increasing itself, but according to _no_ _determinateness_ _other_ _than_ _its_ _own_, – making itself its own limit in this expanding and in its becoming-other thus relating itself only to itself.

This is the proof of the law of fall from the _concept_ of the fact. The ratio of <i>powers</i> is essentially a _qualitative_ ratio and is alone the ratio that belongs to the concept. – It is further to be added, with a view to what follows, that because _fall_ still contains conditionedness within freedom, time remains only abstract unity as the _immediate_ number, just as the determination of magnitude of space attains only to the second dimension.

## {{ 268 }} § 268.

Fall is the merely abstract positing of one _center_, in whose unity the difference of the particular masses and bodies posits itself as sublated; mass and weight therefore have no significance in the magnitude of this motion. But the simple being-for-itself of the center, as this _negative_ relation to itself, is essentially _repulsion_ of itself; – _formal_ repulsion into the many resting centers (_stars_); – _living_ repulsion, as determination of these according to _the_ _moments_ _of_ _the_ _concept_ and the essential relation of these centers, thus posited as distinguished, to one another. This relation is the _contradiction_ of their self-subsistent being-for-itself and of their being joined together in the concept; the appearance of this contradiction of their reality and their ideality is motion, and indeed _absolutely_ _free_ _motion_.

[^1]: It could be said that this so-called _accelerating_ force bears its name very improperly, since the effect supposed to derive from it is _equal_ (constant) in each moment of time, – the _empirical_ factor in the magnitude of fall, the _unit_ (the 15 feet at the earth's surface). The acceleration consists solely in the _adding_ of this empirical unit in each moment of time. The so-called force of _inertia_, on the other hand, can at least in the same way be credited with _acceleration_, for it is ascribed the effect that it is the _duration_ of the _velocity_ _attained_ at the end of each moment of time, that is, that it for its part _adds_ this velocity to that empirical magnitude; and this velocity is said to be _greater_ at the end of each moment of time than at the end of the preceding one.

[^2]: _Lagrange_ goes, after his fashion, in the Théorie des fonctions 3me P. Applikation de la Théorie à la Mécanique, Ch\. I., the simple and wholly correct way; he presupposes the mathematical treatment of functions, and now _finds_, in the _application_ to mechanics, for s = ft, in nature ft _also_ bt²; s = ct³ does not present itself in nature. Here there is rightly no question of wanting to set up a _proof_ of s = bt², but this relation is taken up as _found_ in nature. In the development of the function, when t becomes t + ϑ, the circumstance that of the series resulting for the space traversed in ϑ only the first two terms can be used and the others are to be omitted is disposed of in its usual way for the analytic interest. But those first two terms are used, for the interest of the subject matter, only because only they have a real determination (ibid. 4. 5. on voit que les fonctions primes et secondes se présentent naturellement dans la mécanique, où elles ont une valeur et une signification déterminées). From here he does fall in with the <i>Newton</i>ian expressions of abstract, that is, plainly uniform velocity, which devolves on the force of inertia, and of the accelerating force, with which the fictions of reflection about an infinitely small period of time (the ϑ), its beginning and end, also come in. But this has no influence on that correct course, which does not want to use these determinations for a _proof_ _of_ _the_ _law_, but, as is proper here, takes the law from experience and then applies the mathematical treatment to it.
