## C. Actuality

## {{ 142 }} § 142.

Actuality is the unity of essence and concrete existence, or of the inner and the outer, as it has come to be immediately. What the actual expresses is the actual itself; in that expression it remains just as essential, and it is essential solely in so far as it stands in immediate external concrete existence.

::: remark
{{ 142 A }} _Being_ and _concrete_ _existence_ have turned up before as forms of the immediate: _being_ is immediacy that has not been reflected, a _passing_ _over_ into an other. _Concrete_ _existence_ is the immediate unity of being with reflection, and for that reason _appearance_; it issues from the ground and goes under into it. The actual is that unity's _positedness_, the relation become self-identical; it has thereby been taken out of _passing_ _over_, and its _externality_ constitutes its energy; within that externality it is reflected into itself; its existence is nothing but the _manifestation_ _of_ _itself_, and not of an other.
:::

## {{ 143 }} § 143.

As the concrete that contains those determinations and their difference, actuality is also their development, with the result that in it they are at once determined as shine, as merely posited (§ 141.). α) Taken as _identity_ in general, it is first of all _possibility_; – the reflection-into-itself that, set against {{{ 20.165 }}} the _concrete_ unity of the actual, is posited as _abstract_ and _unessential_ _essentiality_. For actuality, possibility is what is _essential_, though so that it remains, at once, _merely_ possibility.

::: remark
{{ 143 A }} What led _Kant_ to regard possibility, and with it actuality and necessity, as _modalities_ is surely the determination of _possibility_: these determinations, he says, "do not in the least augment the concept as object, but only express the relation to the faculty of cognition." And it is true that possibility is the empty abstraction of reflection-into-itself, what was formerly called the inner, except that it is now determined as the inner that has been sublated, as a _merely_ _posited_, external inner; thus it is indeed _posited_ as a mere modality, as an inadequate abstraction which, taken more concretely, belongs only to subjective thinking. Actuality and necessity, however, are anything but a mere _manner_ _and_ _way_ pertaining to an other; they are rather precisely the opposite, posited as the concrete that is not merely posited but complete in itself. – Possibility is at first, over against the concrete as actual, merely the form of _identity-with-itself_; hence the only rule it has is that something must not contradict itself, and in this way _everything_ _is_ _possible_, since abstraction can lend this form of identity to any content whatever. Yet _everything_ is equally _impossible_, for in any content, being a concrete, the determinateness can be taken as determinate opposition and so as contradiction. – No talk is emptier, therefore, than talk of such possibility and impossibility. Philosophy least of all should busy itself with showing _that_ _something_ _is_ _possible_ or _that_ _something_ _else_ _is_ _possible_ _too_, or that something is, however it is put, _conceivable_. The historian, likewise, is directly bound to leave this category unused, it being already pronounced untrue in itself; but the acuteness of the empty understanding delights most {{{ 20.166 }}} of all in the hollow contriving of possibilities, and of as many possibilities as can be had.
:::

## {{ 144 }} § 144.

β) But the actual, in its difference from possibility as reflection-into-itself, is itself merely the _external_ concrete, the _unessential_ immediate. Put otherwise: insofar as it is at first (§ 142.) the simple unity, itself immediate, of inner and outer, it is an _unessential_ outer and is thus at the same time (§ 140.) the _merely_ inward, the abstraction of reflection-into-itself; it is accordingly determined itself as a _merely_ possible. Since it counts as a mere possibility, the actual is something _contingent_; and possibility, inversely, is itself nothing but _chance_.

## {{ 145 }} § 145.

Posited as mere forms, possibility and contingency are moments of actuality, inner and outer, and make up the _externality_ of the actual. Their reflection-into-itself lies in the actual determined _in_ _itself_, the _content_, which is their essential determining ground. The finitude of the contingent and of the possible accordingly lies, more closely, in the form-determination's being distinct from the content, and _whether_ _something_ _is_ _contingent_ _and_ _possible_ _depends_ _therefore_ _on_ _the_ _content_.

## {{ 146 }} § 146.

That _externality_ of actuality involves, more closely, the following: contingency, as immediate actuality, is the self-identical only essentially as _positedness_, but a positedness equally sublated, an existent externality. It is thus a _presupposed_ whose immediate existence is at the same time a {{{ 20.167 }}} _possibility_, destined to be sublated, – to be the possibility of an other, – the _condition_.

## {{ 147 }} § 147.

γ) Thus developed, this externality is a _circle_ made up of the determinations of possibility and of immediate actuality, their _mediation_ through one another, _real_ _possibility_ in general. Being such a circle, it is moreover the totality; hence it is the _content_, the _fact_ determined in and for itself, and, in accordance with the difference of the determinations within this unity, likewise the concrete _totality_ _of_ _form_ for itself, in which the inner is at once translated into the outer and the outer into the inner. This movement of the form is _activity_: the activation of the fact as the _real_ ground that sublates itself into actuality, and the activation of contingent actuality, of the conditions, that is, their reflection-into-itself and their sublating of themselves into another actuality, the actuality of the _fact_. Once _all_ _conditions_ are at hand, the fact _must_ become actual; and the fact is itself one of the conditions, since at first, as an inner, it too is only something presupposed. _Developed_ actuality, as the alternation of inner and outer that falls together into one, the alternation of their opposed movements united in One movement, is _necessity_.

::: remark
{{ 147 A }} It is true that necessity has been defined correctly as the unity of possibility and actuality. Put only in this way, however, the determination remains superficial and thus unintelligible. The concept of necessity is exceedingly hard, and this is because it is the concept itself, while its moments still stand as actualities that must nonetheless be grasped, at once, merely as forms, as broken within themselves and as passing over. The moments that make up necessity are therefore to be set out more fully in the next two sections.
:::

## {{{ 20.168 }}} {{ 148 }} § 148.

The three moments, the _condition_, the _fact_ and the _activity_, are as follows:
+ a\. The _condition_: α) it is what is presupposed; being merely _posited_, it exists only in relation to the fact, but being _prior_, it is for itself, – a contingent, external circumstance existing with no regard to the fact; yet in this contingency, at the same time with regard to the fact, which is the totality, the presupposed is a _complete_ _circle_ _of_ _conditions_. β) The conditions are _passive_; they are employed as material for the fact and so enter into its _content_; they likewise correspond to this content, containing its _entire_ _determination_ in themselves already.
+ b\. The _fact_ is likewise: α) something presupposed; as _posited_ it is as yet only an inner and possible, and as _prior_ a content self-subsistent for itself; β) by the employment of the conditions it gains its external concrete existence, the realization of its content-determinations, which correspond to the conditions in turn, so that it shows itself to be the fact just as much from these and emerges out of them.
+ c\. The _activity_: α) it too exists self-subsistently for itself, (a human being, a character) and yet has its possibility solely in the conditions and in the fact; β) it consists in the movement that translates the conditions into the fact and this into them, as into the side of concrete existence; more exactly, though, it is only the positing of the fact out of the conditions, in which it is present _in_ _itself_, and the giving of concrete existence to the fact by sublating the concrete existence the conditions possess.

To the extent that these three moments confront one another in the shape of _self-subsistent_ _concrete_ _existence_, this process counts as _external_ necessity. – The fact of this necessity is a _limited_ content. For the fact is this whole in _simple_ determinateness; but since in its form it is external to itself, it is thereby external to itself also in itself and in its content, and this externality within the fact is the restriction of its content.

## {{{ 20.169 }}} {{ 149 }} § 149.

Necessity is therefore in itself the _one_ essence that is identical _with_ _itself_ yet rich in content, and that shines within itself in such a way that its differences take the form of _self-subsistent_ _actuals_; and this identical is at the same time, as absolute _form_, the _activity_ of sublating [immediacy] into being-mediated and mediation into immediacy. – What is necessary is through an _other_, which has split into the _mediating_ _ground_ (the fact and the activity) and an _immediate_ actuality, a contingent that is at the same time the condition. Being through an other, the necessary is not something in and for itself; it is merely _posited_. Yet this mediation is itself immediately its own sublating: ground and contingent condition are transposed into immediacy, whereby that positedness is sublated into actuality and the fact has _joined_ _together_ _with_ _itself_. Having returned into itself in this way, the necessary _is_ _absolutely_, as unconditioned actuality. – So the necessary is _mediated_ through a circle of circumstances: it is as it is because the circumstances are as they are, and at one and the same time it is so _unmediated_, – it is so because it is.
