## α) Sensuous Consciousness

## {{ 418 }} § 418.

At first consciousness is the _immediate_; its relation to the object is therefore a simple certainty of it without mediation, and the object too is accordingly determined as immediate, as _being_ and reflected into itself and, further, as immediately _singular_; – _sensuous_ consciousness.

::: remark
{{ 418 A }} As relation, consciousness contains only the categories that belong to the abstract I or to formal thinking, which for it are determinations of the object (§ 415). Sensuous consciousness therefore knows the object merely as a _being_, a _something_, a _concretely_ _existing_ _thing_, a _singular_, and the like. It seems richest in content, yet is poorest in thoughts. That rich fullness consists of the determinations of feeling; these make up the _material_ of consciousness (§ 414), that substantial, qualitative element which in the anthropological sphere the soul _is_ and discovers _in_ _itself_. This material is detached from itself by the soul's reflection into itself, I, which gives it first of all the determination of _being_. – Spatial {{{ 20.425 }}} and temporal singularity, _Here_ and _Now_, by which I determined the object of sensuous consciousness in the _Phenomenology_ _of_ _Spirit_, pp. 25 ff., properly belongs to intuiting. The object is here to be taken at first only according to the relation it has to _consciousness_, namely to be determined as something _external_ to it, not yet as external in itself or as being-outside-itself.
:::

## {{ 419 }} § 419.

As a something, the _sensuous_ turns into an _other_; the _something_'s reflection into itself, the _thing_, possesses _many_ properties, and, being a singular in its immediacy, _manifold_ _predicates_. The _many_ _singulars_ of sensibility thereby turn into an _expanse_, – a manifoldness of _relations_, _determinations_ _of_ _reflection_ and _universalities_. – These are logical determinations, posited by the thinking, that is, here by the I itself. But _for_ _it_, as appearing, the object has changed in this way. In this determination of the object, sensuous consciousness is _perceiving_.
