## Division

## {{ 487 }} § 487.

The will that is free is:

+ A. to begin with itself _immediate_, and hence _single_ – the _person_; the existence that the person gives its freedom is _property_. _Right_ as such is _formal_, _abstract_ right;
+ B. turned back into itself, so that it has its existence within itself and is thereby at once determined as _particular_: the right of the _subjective_ will – _morality_.
+ C. the _substantial_ will, which is the actuality suited to its concept in the subject and the totality of necessity – _ethical life_, in family, civil society and state.

Since I have worked this part of philosophy out in my Outlines of the Philosophy of Right (Berlin 1821), I can keep myself briefer here than over the other parts.
