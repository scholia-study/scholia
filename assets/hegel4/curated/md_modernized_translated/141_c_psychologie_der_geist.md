## C. Psychology. Spirit

## {{ 440 }} § 440.

_Spirit_ has proved to be the truth of soul and of consciousness: of the former's simple, immediate totality, and of the latter's knowing, which, as infinite form, is not limited by that content and does not stand to it in the relation of an object, but is knowing of the substantial totality, neither subjective nor objective. Spirit therefore begins only from its own being and relates itself only to its own determinations.

::: remark
{{ 440 A }} _Psychology_ accordingly considers the faculties, or universal modes of activity, of _spirit_ _as_ _such_, intuiting, representing, recollecting {{{ 20.435 }}} and so forth, desires and so forth, partly without the content that, according to _appearance_, is found in empirical representing, in thinking as well, and likewise in desire and will, partly without the forms of being in the soul as a determination of nature, or in consciousness itself as an object of it existing for itself. This, however, is not an arbitrary abstraction; spirit is itself this, to be raised above nature and natural determinateness, and above entanglement with an external object, that is, above the material generally, as its concept has turned out. All that it now has to do is to realize this concept of its freedom, that is, only to sublate the _form_ of immediacy with which it begins anew. The content that is raised to intuitions consists of _its_ sensations, as also of its intuitions, which are altered into representations, and further of representations that are altered into thoughts, and so on.
:::

## {{ 441 }} § 441.

The soul is _finite_ insofar as it is immediate or determined by nature; consciousness, insofar as it has an object; spirit, insofar as it indeed no longer has an object but has a determinateness in its knowing, namely through its immediacy, which is the same as its being subjective or being the concept. And it is a matter of indifference what is determined as its concept and what as the reality of that concept. If the absolutely infinite objective _reason_ is posited as its _concept_, the reality is _knowing_ or _intelligence_; or if _knowing_ is taken as the concept, its reality is this _reason_, and the realization of knowing is to appropriate that reason to itself. Spirit's finitude therefore lies in knowing's failure to grasp the in-and-for-itself being of its reason, or, equally, in this reason's not having {{{ 20.436 }}} fully manifested itself in knowing. At the same time reason is infinite only as absolute freedom, and for this reason it _presupposes_ itself to its knowing and so makes itself finite; it is the eternal movement of sublating this immediacy, of comprehending itself and of being the knowing of reason.

## {{ 442 }} § 442.

The progress of spirit is _development_, insofar as its concrete existence, _knowing_, has within itself, as its import and purpose, being determined in and for itself, that is, the rational, so that the activity of translating is purely the formal transition into manifestation and, in this, return into itself. Insofar as knowing, encumbered with its first determinateness, is as yet only _abstract_ or _formal_, the goal of spirit is to _bring_ _forth_ the objective fulfilment, and with it at the same time the freedom, of its knowing.

::: remark
{{ 442 A }} Here one is not to think of the development of the individual connected with the _anthropological_ one, according to which the faculties and forces are considered as emerging one after another and expressing themselves in concrete existence, – a progression the cognition of which was for a time (by the philosophy of Condillac) held in great esteem, as if this supposed _natural_ emergence were to set out the _arising_ of these faculties and to _explain_ them. The tendency in this is not to be mistaken: to make the _manifold_ modes of activity of spirit comprehensible in its _unity_, and to show a connection of necessity. But the categories employed for it are of a meagre kind throughout. Above all, the governing determination is that the sensuous is indeed rightly taken as the first, as the initial foundation, but that from this {{{ 20.437 }}} point of departure the further determinations appear to proceed only in an _affirmative_ way, while the _negative_ of the activity of spirit, through which that material is spiritualized and sublated as sensuous, is misjudged and overlooked. In that position the sensuous is not merely the empirical first, but remains such that it is supposed to be the truly substantial foundation.

Likewise, if the activities of spirit are considered only as _expressions_, as forces generally, perhaps with the determination of _utility_, that is, as serving some other interest of intelligence or of the heart, there is no _final_ _purpose_ present. Only the concept itself can be this, and the concept's activity can have no purpose but itself: to sublate the form of immediacy or subjectivity, to reach itself and grasp itself, to free itself into _itself_. In this way the so-called faculties of spirit, in their distinctness, are to be considered only as stages of this liberation. And this alone is to be held as the _rational_ way of considering spirit and its various activities.
:::

## {{ 443 }} § 443.

Just as the previous stage, the natural soul, is the object of consciousness (§ 413), so consciousness is the object of spirit, which has it, or rather makes it so; that is, since consciousness is the identity of the I with its other only _in_ _itself_ (§ 415), spirit posits it _for_ _itself_, so that it now knows it, this _concrete_ unity. Its productions accord with the determination of reason, that the content be both the _existing_ _in_ _itself_, and, according to freedom, _its_ _own_. Thus, since in its beginning it is _determined_, this determinateness is twofold, that of the _existing_ and that of the _its_ _own_; according to the former, to find something in itself as _existing_, according to the latter, to posit it only as _its_ _own_. The path of spirit is therefore:

+ {{{ 20.438 }}} a) to be _theoretical_, to have to do with the rational as its immediate determinateness and now to posit it as _its_ _own_; or to free knowing from the presupposition and thereby from its abstraction, and to make the determinateness subjective. Since knowing is thus determined in _itself_ in and for itself, the determinateness posited as _its_ _own_, and it is hence _free_ _intelligence_, it is
+ b) _will_, _practical_ _spirit_, which at first is likewise formal, has a content as _only_ _its_ _own_, wills immediately, and now frees its determination of will from its subjectivity as the one-sided form of its content, so that it
+ c) becomes for itself _free_ _spirit_, in which that twofold one-sidedness is sublated.

## {{ 444 }} § 444.

Theoretical spirit as well as practical spirit are still within the sphere of _subjective_ _spirit_ generally. They are not to be distinguished as passive and active. Subjective spirit is productive; but its productions are formal. Inwardly, the production of the theoretical is only its ideal world and the gaining of abstract self-determination within itself. The practical has indeed to do only with self-determinations, with its own material, which however is likewise still formal and thus has a limited content, for which it gains the form of universality. Outwardly, since subjective spirit is the unity of soul and consciousness, hence also an _existing_ reality, at once anthropological and in accord with consciousness, its products are, in the theoretical, the _word_, and in the practical (not yet deed and action) _enjoyment_.

::: remark
{{ 444 A }} Psychology belongs, like logic, to those sciences that in recent times have drawn the least benefit from the more general culture of spirit and the deeper concept of reason, {{{ 20.439 }}} and it is still in an extremely bad condition. By the turn that the Kantian philosophy took, to be sure, a greater importance has been attributed to it, to the point that it, and indeed in its _empirical_ condition, _is_ _supposed_ _to_ _constitute_ the foundation of metaphysics, a science said to consist in nothing other than apprehending _empirically_ the _facts_ of human _consciousness_, and indeed as _facts_ just as they are _given_, and in analyzing them. With this position of psychology, in which it is mixed up with forms from the standpoint of consciousness and with anthropology, nothing has changed in its condition itself, and only this has been added, that for metaphysics and philosophy generally, as for spirit as such, the _cognition_ _of_ _the_ _necessity_ of that _which_ _is_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, of the _concept_ and of _truth_, has been renounced.
:::
