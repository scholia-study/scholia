## {{{ 20.475 }}} γ) Happiness

## {{ 479 }} § 479.

In this representation of a universal satisfaction produced by reflective thinking, the drives are posited in their particularity as _negative_, and are supposed to be sacrificed, in part one to another for the sake of that purpose, in part directly to it, wholly or in part. Their delimitation by one another is, on the one hand, a mixing of qualitative and quantitative determination; on the other, since happiness has the _affirmative_ content solely in the drives, the decision lies in them, and it is subjective feeling and liking that must give the casting vote as to what it makes happiness consist in.

## {{ 480 }} § 480.

Happiness is the merely represented, abstract _universality_ of the content, which only _ought_ to be. The truth of the _particular_ determinateness, however, which is _sublated_ no less than it _is_, and of the _abstract_ _singularity_, of arbitrary will, which in happiness just as much gives itself a purpose as it does not, is the _universal_ determinateness of the will in itself, that is, its self-determining itself, _freedom_. Arbitrary will is in this way will only as pure subjectivity, which is at once pure and concrete by having for its content and purpose only that infinite determinateness, freedom itself. In this truth of its self-determination, in which concept and object are identical, the will is, – _actual_ _free_ _will_.
