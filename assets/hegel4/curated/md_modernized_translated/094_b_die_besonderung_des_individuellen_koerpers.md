## b. The Particularization of the Individual Body

## {{ 316 }} § 316.

Shaping, the space-determining individualizing of _mechanism_, passes over into _physical_ _particularization_. The individual body is _in_ _itself_ _the_ _physical_ totality; this is to be posited in it in _difference_, yet as it is determined and held in individuality. The body, as the _subject_ of these determinations, contains them as _properties_ or _predicates_; but in such a way that they are at the same time a relating to their unbound, universal elements, and processes with them. It is their immediate particularization, not yet _posited_ (the positing being the _chemical_ process), according to which they, not yet led back into individuality, are only relations to those elements and not the real totality of the process. Their distinction from one another is that of their elements, whose logical determinateness has been shown in its sphere. § 282 ff.

::: remark
{{ 316 A }} With the old universal thought that every body consists of the four elements, or with the more recent _Paracelsian_ one that it consists of mercury or fluid, sulfur or oil, and salt, and with many other thoughts of this kind, the refutation was _in_ _the_ _first_ _place_ easy, since one chose to understand by those names the single empirical materials {{{ 20.312 }}} which are first designated by such names. But it is not to be overlooked that they were meant far more essentially to contain and express determinations of the concept; one must therefore rather admire the forcefulness with which thought recognized and held fast, in such sensible particular existences, only its own determination and universal meaning. _In_ _the_ _second_ _place_, such a grasping and determining, since it has reason for its source, which lets itself be misled neither by the sensible playfulness of appearance and its confusion nor be brought into oblivion, is far above the mere seeking out and chaotic recounting of the _properties_ of bodies. In this seeking it still counts as merit and renown to have come up with something _particular_, instead of bringing the many particulars back to the universal and the concept and recognizing the latter in them.
:::
