## c. Sound

## {{ 300 }} § 300.

The specific _simplicity_ of determinateness that the body has in density and in the principle of its cohesion, this at first _inner_ _form_, having passed through its submersion in the material being-outside-one-another, becomes _free_ in the _negation_ of the subsisting-for-itself of this its being-outside-one-another. This is the passing over of material <i>spatial</i>ity into material <i>temporal</i>ity. By this form's being thus _in_ _trembling_, that is, through the momentary negation of the parts just as through the negation of this their negation, which, bound to one another, is awakened one through the other, and so as an oscillating of the subsistence and negation of specific gravity and {{{ 20.298 }}} cohesion, in the material as its _ideality_, the simple form is _existing_ _for_ _itself_ and comes to appearance as this mechanical ensoulment.

::: remark
{{ 300 A }} The purity or impurity of sound proper, its differences from mere clang (through a blow on a solid body), noise and so forth, depends on whether the body that trembles through and through is homogeneous in itself, and then further on the specific cohesion, on its otherwise spatial determination of dimension, whether it is a material line, a material surface, and in that case a bounded line and surface, or a solid body. – Water, lacking cohesion, is without sound, and its motion, as merely _external_ friction of its absolutely displaceable parts, gives only a rushing. The continuity of glass, existing with its inner brittleness, rings, and still more the unbrittle continuity of metal rings through and through in itself, and so forth.

The _communicability_ of sound, its propagation, so to speak _soundless_, lacking the repetition and return of the trembling, through all bodies however differently determined in brittleness and so forth (better through solid bodies than through air – through the earth for many miles, through metals, by calculation, ten times faster than through air) – shows the ideality that moves freely through them, which lays claim wholly only to their _abstract_ materiality without the specific determinations of their density, cohesion and further formations, and brings their parts into negation, into trembling; this idealizing is itself only the communicating.

The _qualitative_ aspect of sound in general, as of the sound that articulates itself, the tone, depends on the density, cohesion and further specified mode of cohesion of the sounding body, because the ideality or subjectivity that the trembling is, as negation of those specific qualities, has them as its content and determinateness; with this the trembling and sound itself is specified accordingly, and the instruments have their peculiar sound and timbre.
:::

## {{{ 20.299 }}} {{ 301 }} § 301.

In trembling, _vibrating_ is to be distinguished as _external_ change of place, namely of the spatial relation to _other_ bodies, which is ordinary, proper motion. But though distinguished, it is at the same time identical with the inner motion determined above, which is the subjectivity becoming free, the appearance of sound as such.

The existence of this ideality has, by reason of its abstract universality, only _quantitative_ differences. In the realm of sound and tones, therefore, their further difference from one another, their harmony and disharmony, rests on _numerical ratios_ and their simpler or more complicated and more remote concord.

::: remark
{{ 301 A }} The vibrating of strings, columns of air, rods and so forth is an alternating passage from the straight line into the arc, and indeed into opposite ones; with this external change of place, thus only apparent, in relation to other bodies, the inner, the alternating change of specific gravity and of cohesion, is immediately linked; the side of the material line lying toward the center of the arc of vibration has been shortened, the outer side, however, lengthened, the specific gravity and cohesion of the former thus diminished, of the latter increased, and this itself simultaneously.

With regard to the power of quantitative determination in this ideal ground, one is to recall the phenomena in which such a determination, set by mechanical interruptions in a vibrating line or plane, _communicates_ _itself_ to the whole line or plane beyond the mechanical point of interruption, to its vibrating, and forms nodes of vibration in it, which is made visible by _Chladni's_ presentations. – Likewise belonging here are the awakenings of harmonic tones in neighboring strings, to which determinate {{{ 20.300 }}} ratios of size to the sounding one have been given; most of all the experiences to which _Tartini_ first drew attention, of tones that arise from other sounds sounding simultaneously, which stand in determinate numerical ratios to one another with regard to vibrations, are different from these, and are produced only through these ratios.
:::

## {{ 302 }} § 302.

Sound is the _alternation_ of the specific being-outside-one-another of the material parts and of its being negated; – only _abstract_ or, so to speak, only ideal _ideality_ of this specific. But this alternation is thereby itself immediately the negation of the material specific subsistence; this is thereby _real_ _ideality_ of specific gravity and cohesion, – _heat_.

::: remark
{{ 302 A }} The heating of sounding bodies, as of struck ones, and also of ones rubbed against one another, is the appearance of the heat that arises with sound according to the concept.
:::
