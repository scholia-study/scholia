## Concept of Spirit

## {{ 381 }} § 381.

Spirit has _for_ _us_ _nature_ as its _presupposition_, of which it is the _truth_ and thereby the _absolutely_ _first_. In this truth nature has vanished, and spirit has shown itself to be the idea that has attained its being-for-itself, whose _object_ no less than its _subject_ _is_ _the_ _concept_. This identity is _absolute_ _negativity_, because in nature the concept has {{{ 20.382 }}} its perfect external objectivity, but this externalization of it is sublated, and in it the concept has become identical with itself. Thus it is this identity at the same time only as a return out of nature.

## {{ 382 }} § 382.

The _essence_ of spirit is therefore formally _freedom_, the absolute negativity of the concept as identity with itself. By this formal determination it is _able_ to leave aside all that is external, even its own externality, which is its very existence; it is able to endure the negation of its individual immediacy, the infinite _pain_, which is to say, to hold itself affirmatively in this negativity and to be identical for itself. Such a possibility is its abstract universality, existing for itself within itself.

## {{ 383 }} § 383.

This universality is also its _existence_. As being for itself, the universal is _particularizing_ itself and in this is identity with itself. The determinateness of spirit is therefore _manifestation_. It is not some determinateness or content whose expression and externality would be only a form distinct from it, so that it reveals not _something_, but its determinateness and content is this revealing itself. Its possibility is therefore immediately infinite, absolute _actuality_.

## {{ 384 }} § 384.

_Revealing_, which as the _abstract_ idea is immediate transition, the _becoming_ of nature, is, as the revealing of spirit that is free, the _positing_ of nature as _its_ world; a positing that, as reflection, is at the same time the _presupposing_ of the world as self-subsistent nature. Revealing in the concept is the creation of that nature as spirit's being, in which spirit gives itself the _affirmation_ and _truth_ of its freedom.

::: remark
{{ 384 A }} _The_ _absolute_ _is_ _spirit_; that is the loftiest definition of the absolute. – One may say that all culture and philosophy was absolutely bent on finding this definition and on comprehending its sense and content; upon this point all religion and science have {{{ 20.383 }}} pressed; from this urge alone is world history to be comprehended. – The word and the _representation_ of spirit was found early, and the content of the Christian religion is to give God to be known as spirit. What is here _given_ to representation and is _in_ _itself_ the essence has to be grasped _in_ its own element, the concept, and this is philosophy's task, which is not truly and immanently solved so long as the concept and freedom are not its object and its soul.
:::
