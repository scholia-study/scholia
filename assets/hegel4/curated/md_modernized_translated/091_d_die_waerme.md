## d. Heat

## {{ 303 }} § 303.

Heat is matter's restoring itself into its formlessness, its fluidity, the triumph of its abstract homogeneity over the specific determinatenesses; its abstract, merely <i>in-itself</i> _continuity_, as negation of negation, is here _posited_ as activity. Formally, that is, in relation to determination of space in general, heat therefore appears {{{ 20.301 }}} _expanding_, as sublating the limitation that is the _specifying_ of the _indifferent_ taking-up of space.

## {{ 304 }} § 304.

This real negation of the body's peculiarity is therefore the state in which the body, in its existence, does not affirmatively belong to itself; this its existence is thus rather community with _others_ and _communication_ to them, _external_ heat. The passivity of the corporeal toward it rests on the continuity of the material that is present _in_ _itself_ in specific gravity and cohesion, through which original ideality the modification of specific gravity and cohesion cannot be an actual limit for that communication, for the positing of community.

::: remark
{{ 304 A }} The incoherent, like wool, and what is incoherent _in_ _itself_ (that is, brittle, like glass) are poorer conductors of heat than the metals, whose peculiarity is to possess solid uninterrupted continuity in themselves. Air and water are poor conductors of heat, by reason of their lack of cohesion, as matters still incorporeal in general. – The communicability, according to which heat is separable from the body in which it is at first present and thus appears as something self-subsistent against it as well as something coming to it from _outside_, further the related additional mechanical determinations that can be put into _spreading_ (for instance the repercussion by concave mirrors), likewise the quantitative determinations that occur in heat, – are chiefly what has led to the representation of heat as something existing self-subsistently, a _heat-matter_. One will, however, at least hesitate to call heat a _body_ or even only something corporeal; in which it already lies that the _appearance_ of _particular_ _existence_ is at once capable of different categories. Thus the limited particularity that appears in heat, and its {{{ 20.302 }}} distinguishability from the bodies in which it is, is not sufficient to apply to it the category of matter, which is essentially such a totality in itself that it is at least _heavy_. That appearance of particularity lies chiefly only in the _external_ way in which heat appears in _communication_ toward the bodies present. – _Rumford's_ experiments on the heating of bodies by friction in boring cannon, for instance, could long ago have wholly removed the representation of a particular, self-subsistent existence of heat; here it is shown, against all evasions, purely in its arising, and its nature as a _mode_ _of_ _state_. The abstract representation of matter contains in itself the determination of _continuity_, which is the possibility of communication and, as activity, its actuality, and this continuity that is in itself becomes activity as the negation against the form, – the specific gravity and cohesion, as further against shape.
:::

## {{ 305 }} § 305.

The communication of heat to different bodies contains in itself only the abstract continuing of this determination through indeterminate materiality, and insofar heat is capable not of qualitative dimensions in itself, but only of the abstract opposition of positive and negative, and of quantum and degree, as of an abstract equilibrium, in being an equal temperature of the bodies, among which the degree is distributed. Since heat, however, is alteration of specific gravity and cohesion, it is at the same time bound to these determinations, and the external communicated temperature owes the determinateness of its existence to a condition, namely the particular specific {{{ 20.303 }}} gravity and cohesion of the body that receives the communication; – _specific_ _heat-capacity_.

::: remark
{{ 305 A }} Specific heat-capacity, combined with the category of _matter_ and _material_, has led to the representation of a _latent_, _imperceptible_, _bound_ _heat-material_. Since such a determination is _not_ _perceptible_, _observation_ and _experience_ do not warrant it, and, being inferred, it rests on _the_ _presupposition_ _of_ _a_ _material_ _self-subsistence_ of heat (compare § 286, Remark). In its way this assumption helps to render _empirically_ irrefutable the self-subsistence of heat as a matter, and does so just because the assumption is itself nothing empirical. Whenever it is demonstrated that heat disappears, or that it appears where none was present before, the former is declared a mere hiding or _binding_ itself to imperceptibility, the latter an emerging from mere imperceptibility; the metaphysics of self-subsistence is _opposed_ _to_ _that_ _experience_, indeed presupposed a priori to experience.

What matters for the determination that has here been given of heat is that it be confirmed _empirically_ that the determination necessary in itself through the concept, namely the _alteration_ of specific gravity and cohesion, shows itself in the appearance as _heat_. The _close_ _connection_ of the two is at first readily recognized in the manifold generations (and in just as manifold ways of disappearing) of heat, in fermentations, the other chemical processes, crystallizations and dissolutions of them, in the mechanical inner shocks, already mentioned, combined with external ones, the striking of bells, the beating of metal, frictions and so forth. The friction of two pieces of wood, or in the ordinary striking of fire, brings the material being-outside-one-another of the one body, by the pressing motion of the other, together momentarily in _one_ point, – a {{{ 20.304 }}} negation of the spatial subsistence of the material parts, which turns out in heat and flame of the body or a spark separating from it. – The further difficulty is to grasp the connection of heat with specific gravity and cohesion as the _existing_ ideality of the material, – for this, an existence of the negative that itself contains the determinateness of what is negated, that further has the determinateness of a quantum, and, as ideality of a subsistent, is its being-outside-itself and its positing-itself in another, communication. – What is at issue here, as everywhere in the philosophy of nature, is only to put in place of the categories of the understanding the thought-relations of the speculative concept, and to grasp and determine the appearance according to them.
:::

## {{ 306 }} § 306.

Heat as temperature in general is at first the still abstract dissolution of specified materiality, conditioned in its existence and determinateness. But carrying itself out, in fact realized, the consuming of the corporeal peculiarity gains the existence of pure physical ideality, of the negation of the material becoming free, and emerges as _light_, yet as _flame_, as negation of matter bound to matter. As _fire_ first (§ 283) developed out of the _in-itself_, so here it is _posited_ that, as externally conditioned, it generates itself from the existing moments of the concept within the sphere of conditioned existence. – It furthermore consumes itself, as finite, along with the conditions whose consuming it is.

## {{ 307 }} § 307.

The development of real matter, that is, matter containing _the_ _form_ in it, thus passes over in its totality into the pure ideality of its determinations, into selfhood abstractly identical with itself, which in this circle of _external_ individuality itself (as flame) becomes external and so vanishes. {{{ 20.305 }}} The _conditionedness_ of this sphere is that the _form_ was a _specifying_ of heavy matter, and individuality as totality was as yet only _in_ _itself_. In heat the moment of the real _dissolution_ of _immediacy_ and of the indifference, present at first, of the specified material toward one another is posited. The form is therefore now immanent, _as_ _totality_, in the material, which is without resistance against it. – Selfhood, as the infinite form relating itself to itself, has as such entered into existence; it maintains itself in the externality subjected to it, and is, as the _totality_ freely determining this material, – _free_ _individuality_.
