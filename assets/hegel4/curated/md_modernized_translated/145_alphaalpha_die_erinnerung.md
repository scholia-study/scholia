## αα) Recollection

## {{ 452 }} § 452.

Intuition is first of all taken back inward by intelligence: intelligence sets the _content_ of _feeling_ within its own interior, in its _own_ _space_ and its _own_ _time_. The content is then 1) _image_, released from its first immediacy and its abstract singularity vis-à-vis other things, received as it is into the universality of the I generally. The image no longer carries the full determinateness that intuition possesses; it is arbitrary or contingent, cut off generally from the external place, the time and the immediate connection in which the intuition stood.

## {{ 453 }} § 453.

2) Taken for itself the image passes away, and intelligence itself, as attention, is its time and likewise its space, its when and where. Yet intelligence is not merely consciousness and existence; as such it is the subject and the _in-itself_ of its determinations, and once _recollected_ in it, the image no longer exists but is _kept_ _without_ _consciousness_.

::: remark
{{ 453 A }} If intelligence is to be grasped as this nocturnal shaft, where a world of countless images and representations is kept although none of them is in consciousness, then, on one side, what is generally demanded {{{ 20.447 }}} is that the concept be grasped as concrete; the germ, for instance, is to be grasped so that it holds within itself, _affirmatively_ and in _virtual_ possibility, every _determinateness_ that attains _existence_ only as the tree develops. Because people could not grasp this universal, which is concrete in itself and yet stays _simple_, they were led to store particular representations in particular _fibers_ and _places_; what is different is supposed to have essentially a spatial existence only, even if each item is isolated. – The germ, however, finds its way back from the existing determinatenesses into its simplicity, into the existence of being-in-itself once more, only in an other, the germ of the fruit. Intelligence as such, though, is the free _existence_ of the _being-in-itself_ that, in its development, recollects itself into itself. On the other side, then, intelligence must be grasped as this _unconscious_ shaft, that is, as the _existing_ universal wherein the different is not yet posited as discrete. And this _in-itself_ is, to be precise, the first form of universality that offers itself in representing.
:::

## {{ 454 }} § 454.

3) For its existence an image preserved in this abstract way requires an existent intuition; recollection in the proper sense is the relating of the image to an intuition, namely as the _subsumption_ of the immediate singular intuition under what is universal in form, under the _representation_ that is the same content, so that intelligence is inward to itself within the determinate sensation and its intuition, and _cognizes_ the latter as _already_ _its_ _own_; at the same time it knows its image, hitherto merely inner, as now immediate too in intuition and as _borne_ _out_ there. – The image, which in the shaft of intelligence was only its property, is now also in the possession of intelligence, with the determination of externality. At once it is thus posited as distinguishable from intuition and as separable from the simple night into which it was first sunk. Intelligence is accordingly the power {{{ 20.448 }}} of being able to externalize its property and of no longer needing external intuition for the image's existence in it. This synthesis of the inner image with the recollected existence is representation proper, since the inner image now also has on it the determination that it can be _placed_ before intelligence and have its existence in it.
