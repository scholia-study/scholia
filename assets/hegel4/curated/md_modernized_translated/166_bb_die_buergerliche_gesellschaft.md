## BB. Civil Society

## {{ 523 }} § 523.

As spirit, the substance particularizes itself abstractly into many _persons_ (the family counts as only one person), into families or single ones that exist for themselves, in self-subsistent freedom and as _particulars_; in so doing it forfeits at first its ethical determination, because what these persons as such hold in consciousness and set before themselves as purpose is their own particularity and their being-for-themselves, and not the absolute unity, – the system of atomistics. In this way the substance becomes merely a universal, mediating connection between self-subsistent extremes and their particular interests; this connection's totality, once developed within itself, constitutes the state as civil society, or as the _external_ _state_.
