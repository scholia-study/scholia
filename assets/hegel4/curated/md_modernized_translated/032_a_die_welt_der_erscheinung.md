## a. The World of Appearance

## {{ 132 }} § 132.

What appears exists in such a manner that its _subsistence_ is directly sublated, being only a single moment of the form itself; form encloses subsistence, or {{{ 20.158 }}} matter, as one determination among its own. Hence what appears finds its ground in form, taken as its essence, that is, as its reflection-into-itself set against its immediacy, yet so only in a different determinateness of form. This ground of it is no less an appearing thing, and so appearance advances to an infinite mediation of subsistence by form, hence equally by non-subsistence. At the same time this infinite mediation is a unity of self-relation; and concrete existence is developed into a _totality_, a _world_ of appearance, of reflected finitude.
