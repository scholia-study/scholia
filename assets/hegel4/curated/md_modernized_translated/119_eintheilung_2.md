## Division

## {{ 385 }} § 385.

The development of spirit is that it
+ I. is in the form of _relation_ _to_ _itself_, within itself the _ideal_ totality of the idea, that is, that what its concept is becomes for it, and its being is this, to be at home with itself, that is, to be free, – _subjective_ _spirit_;
+ II. is in the form of _reality_ as a _world_ to be produced by it and produced, in which freedom is as present necessity, – _objective_ _spirit_;
+ III. is in the unity, _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_ and eternally producing itself, of the objectivity of spirit and its ideality or its concept, spirit in its absolute truth, – _the_ _absolute_ _spirit_.

## {{ 386 }} § 386.

The first two parts of the _doctrine_ _of_ _spirit_ comprise _finite_ spirit. Spirit is the infinite idea, and finitude has here its meaning of the inadequacy of concept and reality, with the determination that it is the shining within spirit, – a shine that spirit _in_ _itself_ posits for itself as a restriction, so as, by sublating it, to have and to know freedom {{{ 20.384 }}} _for_ _itself_ as _its_ essence, that is, to be absolutely _manifested_. The different stages of this activity, on which, as on the shine, it is the determination of finite spirit to dwell and which it is its determination to pass through, are stages of its liberation, in whose absolute truth the _finding_ of a world as one presupposed, the _generating_ of it as one posited by spirit, and the liberation from it and in it are one and the same, – a truth to whose infinite form the shine purifies itself as to the knowledge of it.

::: remark
{{ 386 A }} The determination of _finitude_ is fixed above all by the _understanding_ in relation to spirit and _reason_; in this it counts not merely as a matter of the understanding but also as a moral and religious concern to hold fast to the _standpoint_ of finitude as a _final_ one, just as, on the other hand, it counts as a presumption of thinking, indeed as a derangement of it, to want to go beyond it. – Rather, however, such a _modesty_ of thinking, which makes the _finite_ into something absolutely firm, an _absolute_, is surely the worst of the virtues, and to stop short at what has its ground not in itself is the most groundless of cognitions. The determination of _finitude_ was long ago illuminated and discussed in its place, in the logic; this, then, for the further determined but still simple thought-forms of finitude, as the rest of philosophy for the concrete forms of it, is only this showing, [that] the finite _is_ not, that is, is not what is true, but is absolutely only a _passing over_ and a _going beyond itself_. – This finite of the spheres hitherto is the dialectic of having its ceasing-to-be through an _other_ and in an other; but spirit, the concept and what is eternal _in_ _itself_, is itself the accomplishing, within itself, of this annihilating of the nullity, this frustrating of the vain. – The modesty mentioned is the holding fast of this vain, of the finite, against the true, and for that reason is itself vain. This vanity will show itself, in {{{ 20.385 }}} the development of spirit itself, as its highest deepening into its subjectivity and innermost contradiction, and thereby as the turning point, as _evil_.
:::
