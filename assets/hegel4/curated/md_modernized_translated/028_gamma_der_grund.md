## {{{ 20.152 }}} γ) Ground

## {{ 121 }} § 121.

Unity of identity and difference is the _ground_: the truth of what difference and identity have shown themselves to be, – reflection-into-itself that is just as much reflection-into-other, and the reverse. It is _essence_ posited as _totality_.

::: remark
{{ 121 A }} The _proposition_ _of_ _ground_ states: everything has its sufficient _ground_; that is, what something truly is in essence is not its determination as self-identical, nor as diverse, nor as merely positive or merely negative, but this, that its being lies in an other, which as its self-identity is its essence. This other is likewise not abstract reflection into _itself_ but into an _other_. Essence that is within _itself_ is the ground; it is essentially ground, and ground only as ground of something, of an other.
:::

## {{ 122 }} § 122.

Essence is at first shining and mediation _in_ _itself_; being the totality of mediation, it now has its unity with itself _posited_ as the self-sublation of difference, and thereby of mediation. This, then, is the reinstatement of _immediacy_ or of _being_, though of a being _mediated_ _by_ _the_ _sublation_ _of_ _mediation_; – _concrete_ _existence_.

::: remark
{{ 122 A }} As yet the ground holds no content that is determined in and for itself, and it is not _purpose_ either; accordingly it is neither _active_ nor _producing_, and a concrete existence merely _comes_ _forth_ from it. The _determinate_ ground is thus something formal: any determinateness, in so far as it is posited as _related_ _to_ _itself_, as affirmation, in its relation to the immediate concrete existence tied to it. A _good_ ground it is by the very fact of being _ground_, for _good_, quite abstractly, signifies no more than an affirmative, and any determinateness is good that can somehow be pronounced something granted to be affirmative. For this reason a ground can be discovered and named for anything, {{{ 20.153 }}} and a _good_ _ground_ (a good motive for acting, say) may bring something about or _also_ _not_, may have a consequence or _also_ _not_. A motive becomes one that brings something about, for instance, only by being taken into a will, which first makes it active and a cause.
:::
