## {{{ 20.189 }}} γ) Judgment of Necessity

## {{ 177 }} § 177.

As the identity of the content in its difference, the judgment of necessity 1) contains in its predicate, first, the subject's _substance_ or _nature_, the _concrete_ universal, – _the_ _genus_; – and second, since this universal likewise holds determinateness within itself as a negative, the _excluding_ essential determinateness – the _species_; – _categorical_ judgment.

2) By their substantiality the two sides take on the shape of self-subsistent actuality, and their identity is merely _inner_; hence the actuality of the one is at once _not_ _its_ _own_ but the being of _the_ _other_; – _hypothetical_ judgment.

3) Where the inner identity is, in this externalization of the concept, posited too, the universal is the genus, self-identical in its excluding singularity; and the judgment that has this universal on both its sides, on one as such, on the other as the circle of its mutually excluding particularization, whose _either-or_ is no less the genus than its _both-and_, – is the _disjunctive_ _judgment_. Universality, first as genus and now as the circumference of its species too, is thereby determined and posited as totality.
