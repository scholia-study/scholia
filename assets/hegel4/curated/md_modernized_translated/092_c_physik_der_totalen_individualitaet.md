## C. Physics of Total Individuality

## {{ 308 }} § 308.

Matter is at first, _in_ _itself_, the totality of the concept as _heavy_; as such it is not formed within itself; the concept, posited in it in its particular determinations, shows to begin with a finite individuality falling apart into its particularities. Now that the totality of the concept is _posited_, the center of gravity is no longer the _subjectivity_ sought by matter, but is immanent to it, as the ideality of those form-determinations, at first immediate and conditioned, which are henceforth moments developed from within. Material individuality, thus self-identical in its development, is infinite _for_ _itself_ and yet at the same time _conditioned_; it is subjective totality only _immediately_ as yet; hence, though infinite for itself, it contains {{{ 20.306 }}} the relation to an _other_; and only _in_ the process does it come to have this externality and conditionedness posited as sublating themselves; thus it becomes the existent totality of material being-for-itself, which is then _in_ _itself_ _life_, and in the concept passes over into it.

## {{ 309 }} § 309.

Total individuality is:

+ a\. Immediately _shape_ as such, and its abstract principle appearing in free existence – _magnetism_;
+ b\. It determines itself to _difference_, the particular forms of the bodily totality; this particularization, heightened to the extreme, is _electricity_.
+ c\. The _reality_ of this particularization is the _chemically_ different body, and the _relation_ of such bodies – individuality, which has bodies for its moments, realizing itself as totality – the _chemical_ _process_.
