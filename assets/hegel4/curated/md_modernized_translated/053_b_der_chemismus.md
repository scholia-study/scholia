## b. Chemism

## {{ 200 }} § 200.

An immanent _determinateness_ belongs to the _different_ _object_; it makes up the object's nature, and in it the object has concrete existence. Yet since the object is the _concept's_ totality in posited form, its totality and the determinateness of its concrete existence stand in contradiction; the object is accordingly a striving to sublate that contradiction and to bring its existence into equality with the concept.

## {{ 201 }} § 201.

What the chemical process yields as product is the _neutral_, which its tensed extremes are _in itself_; by way of the objects' difference, that is, of particularization, the concept – the concrete {{{ 20.208 }}} universal – closes together with singularity, the product, and thereby only with itself. The other syllogisms, too, are contained in this process: singularity as activity is also a mediating element, and the same holds of the concrete universal, which is the tensed extremes' essence and gains existence in the product.

## {{ 202 }} § 202.

Chemism is still the reflective relation of objectivity, and so it presupposes, along with the objects' different nature, their _immediate_ self-subsistence. The process runs back and forth from one form to the other, and these continue to be external to each other. – Within the neutral product, the properties by which the extremes were determinate against each other have been sublated. The product does accord with the concept; but the _spiriting_ principle of differentiation has no existence in it, since it has sunk back into immediacy, and the neutral is therefore separable. The judging principle, however, which dirempts the neutral into different extremes and gives the indifferent object generally its difference from, and its spiriting toward, an other, together with the process as a tensing separation, lies outside that first process.

## {{ 203 }} § 203.

These two processes – the reduction of the different to the neutral, and the differentiation of the indifferent or neutral – are _external_ to each other and so look self-subsistent in relation to each other; yet they reveal their finitude by passing into products, where they are sublated. In reverse, the process exhibits the different objects' presupposed immediacy as null. – When this _negation_ removes the externality and immediacy in which the concept lay immersed as object, the concept is posited as _free_ and _for_ _itself_ _in_ _contrast_ _to_ that externality and immediacy – _as_ _purpose_.
