## {{{ 20.357 }}} b. Assimilation

## {{ 357 }} § 357.

The self-feeling of _singularity_, however, is just as immediately _exclusive_ and tenses itself against an inorganic nature as against its _external_ condition and material. Inasmuch as

α) the animal organization is, in this external relation, reflected into itself _immediately_, this ideal conduct is the _theoretical_ process, sensibility as external process, and indeed as _determinate_ _feeling_, which differentiates itself into the _many-sidedness_ _of_ _the_ _senses_ answering to inorganic nature.

## {{ 358 }} § 358.

The senses and the theoretical processes are therefore 1) the sense belonging to the mechanical sphere – _feeling_ as such, which senses _gravity_, cohesion and its alteration, and heat; 2) the senses of _opposition_, of the particularized _airiness_ and of the likewise realized _neutrality_ of concrete water, and of the oppositions of the dissolution of concrete neutrality – _smell_ and _taste_. 3) The sense of _ideality_ is likewise a double one, insofar as in it, as abstract relation to itself, the particularization, which cannot be lacking to it, falls apart into two indifferent determinations; α) the sense of ideality as manifestation of the _external_ for what is external – of _light_ in general and more closely of light as it comes to be determined in concrete externality, _color_; and β) the sense of the manifestation of _inwardness_, which makes itself known as such in its expression – of _tone_; – _sight_ and _hearing_.

::: remark
{{ 358 A }} What is stated here is the way in which the _triad_ of moments of the concept passes over into a _quintet_ in number; the more general ground for this transition taking place here is that the animal organism is the reduction of the inorganic nature fallen apart into the infinite unity of _subjectivity_, but at the same time in this unity is its {{{ 20.358 }}} developed totality, whose moments, because it is still _natural_ subjectivity, exist separately.
:::

## {{ 359 }} § 359.

β) The _real_ _process_ or the _practical_ relation to inorganic nature begins with the diremption within itself, the feeling of externality as the _negation_ of the subject, which is at the same time the positive relation to itself and the _certainty_ of this against its negation – with the feeling _of_ _lack_ and the _drive_ to sublate it, in which appears the condition of a _being-excited_ from without, and the negation of the subject posited therein in the manner of an _object_, against which the subject is tensed.

::: remark
{{ 359 A }} Only a living thing feels _lack_; for only it is, in nature, the _concept_ that is the unity of _itself_ and _of_ _its_ _determinate_ _opposite_. Where there is a _restriction_, it is a negation only _for_ _a_ _third_, for an external comparison. It is _lack_, however, insofar as the _being-beyond_ is present in _one_ and the same thing, and the _contradiction_ as such is immanent and posited in it. What is capable of having within itself the contradiction of itself and of _enduring_ it is the _subject_; this constitutes its _infinity_. – Even when _finite_ reason is spoken of, it proves that it is infinite precisely by determining itself as _finite_; for negation is finitude, lack, only for that which is the _sublatedness_ of it, the _infinite_ relation to itself. (Compare § 60, Remark.) – Thoughtlessness stops at the abstraction of the _restriction_ and likewise does not grasp the concept in life, where the _concept_ itself enters _concrete_ _existence_; it holds to the determinations of representation, such as _drive_, _instinct_, _need_ and so forth, without asking what these determinations themselves are in themselves; the analysis of its representation will show that they are negations, posited as contained in the affirmation of the subject itself.

{{{ 20.359 }}} That for the organism the determination of _being-excited_ by _external_ _powers_ has taken the place of the _acting_ _of_ _external_ _causes_ is an important step in the true representation of it. Idealism begins therein, to the effect that nothing at all can have a positive relation to the living thing whose possibility the living thing does not hold in and for itself, that is, which would not be determined by the concept and thus absolutely immanent to the subject. But as unphilosophical as any scientific brew of determinations of reflection is the introduction of such formal and material relations in the _theory_ _of_ _excitation_, which long passed for philosophical; for instance the quite abstract opposition of _receptivity_ and _capacity_ _to_ _act_, which are supposed to stand to one another as factors in inverse ratio of magnitude, whereby all difference to be grasped in the organism has fallen into the _formalism_ of a merely _quantitative_ diversity, _heightening_ and _diminishing_, _strengthening_ and _weakening_, that is, into the highest possible _absence_ _of_ _the_ _concept_. A theory of medicine built upon these arid determinations of the understanding is completed with half a dozen propositions, and it is no wonder that it found rapid dissemination and many adherents. The occasion for this aberration lay in the basic error that, after the absolute had been determined as the absolute indifference of the subjective and the objective, all determination was now supposed to be only a _quantitative_ difference. The absolute form, the _concept_ and livingness, has rather as its soul solely the qualitative difference sublating itself in itself, the dialectic of absolute counterposition. Insofar as this true infinite negativity is not cognized, one can suppose that the absolute identity of life cannot be held fast, as with _Spinoza_ attributes and modes occur in an _external_ understanding, without making the difference into something merely external to reflection; whereby life lacks the _leaping_ _point_ of selfhood, the principle of self-motion, diremption of itself within itself in general.

Further, the procedure is to be held entirely unphilosophical and crudely sensuous that set _carbon_ and _nitrogen_, oxygen and hydrogen directly in the place of determinations of the concept, and {{{ 20.360 }}} determined the difference, formerly intensive, now more closely as the _more_ or _less_ of the one material and the other, but the effective and positive relation of external stimuli as an _adding_ of a missing material. In an _asthenia_, for instance – a nervous fever – _nitrogen_, it was said, has the upper hand in the organism, because the brain and the nerve in general are _potentiated_ nitrogen, since _chemical_ analysis shows it as the _chief_ _constituent_ of these organic formations; the addition of _carbon_ is therefore indicated, to restore the equilibrium of these _materials_, health. The means that have empirically shown themselves effective against nervous fever are for this very reason regarded as belonging on the side of _carbon_, and such superficial putting together and supposing is passed off as _construction_ and _proof_. – The crudity consists in taking the external caput mortuum, the dead material, in which chemistry has killed a second time a life already dead, for the _essence_ of a living organ, indeed for its _concept_.

Ignorance and disregard of the concept in general ground the convenient formalism of using, instead of determinations of the concept, sensuous materials such as the chemical materials, further relations that fall within inorganic nature, such as the polarity of magnetism between north and south, or even the difference of magnetism itself and electricity, and of comprehending and developing the natural universe in such a way that a schema made ready from such material is attached externally to its spheres and differences. A great manifoldness of forms is possible in this regard, since it remains arbitrary to adopt for the schema the determinations as they appear in the _chemical_ sphere, for instance, oxygen, hydrogen and so forth, and to transfer them to magnetism, mechanism, vegetation, animality and so forth, or else to take magnetism, electricity, the masculine and feminine, contraction and expansion and so forth, and in general to seize upon the oppositions of any other sphere and then to employ them in the rest.
:::

## {{{ 20.361 }}} {{ 360 }} § 360.

The need is a _determinate_ one, and its _determinateness_ is a moment of its universal concept, though particularized in infinitely manifold ways. The drive is the activity of sublating the lack of such determinateness, that is, of its _form_ of being at first only a _subjective_ one. Since the content of the determinateness is original, _maintains_ itself in the activity and is only carried out by it, it is _purpose_ (§ 204), and the drive, as in the merely living thing, is _instinct_. That formal lack is the inner _excitation_, whose determinateness, specific according to content, appears at the same time as a relation of the animal to the particular individualizations of the spheres of nature.

::: remark
{{ 360 A }} The mysterious, which is supposed to constitute the difficulty of grasping instinct, lies solely in this, that purpose can be grasped only as the inner _concept_, so that merely intellectual explanations and relations soon show themselves inadequate to instinct. The thorough determination that _Aristotle_ grasped of the living thing, that it is to be regarded as acting according to purpose, was almost lost in more recent times, until _Kant_, in _inner_ purposiveness, in that the living thing is to be regarded as a _purpose_ _unto_ _itself_, reawakened this concept in his own way. What chiefly makes the difficulty here is that the relation of purpose is usually represented as _external_, and the opinion prevails as if purpose existed _only_ in a _conscious_ manner. Instinct is purposive activity working in an unconscious manner.
:::

## {{ 361 }} § 361.

Insofar as the need is a connection with the _universal_ mechanism and the abstract powers of nature, instinct exists only as _inner_ excitation, not even sympathetic (as in sleeping and waking, in climatic and other migrations and so forth). But as relation of the animal to _its_ inorganic, _singularized_ nature, it is in general {{{ 20.362 }}} _determinate_, and according to further particularity only a limited circle of the universal inorganic nature is its own. Instinct is a _practical_ conduct toward it, inner excitation joined with the show of an external excitation, and its activity is partly _formal_, partly _real_ _assimilation_ of inorganic nature.

## {{ 362 }} § 362.

Insofar as it goes to formal assimilation, it forms its determination into the externalities, gives them, as material, an _external_ _form_ conformed to the purpose, and lets the objectivity of these things _subsist_ (as in the building of nests and other resting places). But it is _real_ process insofar as it singularizes the inorganic things or relates itself to those already singularized, and assimilates them with consumption of them, annihilation of their peculiar qualities; – the process with _air_ (breathing and skin-process), with _water_ (thirst), and with individualized _earth_, namely particular formations of it (hunger). Life, the subject of these moments of the totality, tenses itself within itself as concept and into the moments as a reality external to it, and is the continuing conflict in which it overcomes this externality. Because the animal, which here relates itself as immediate singular, can do this only in the singular according to all determinations of singularity (of this place, this time and so forth), this realization of itself is not adequate to its concept, and it passes back continually from satisfaction into the state of need.

## {{ 363 }} § 363.

The _mechanical_ _seizure_ of the external object is the beginning; _assimilation_ itself is the turning over of externality into the self-like unity; since the animal is subject, simple negativity, it can be neither of a mechanical nor of a chemical nature, since in these processes both the materials {{{ 20.363 }}} and the conditions and the activity _remain_ _external_ to one another, so that the living absolute unity is missing from them.

## {{ 364 }} § 364.

Assimilation is, first, because the living thing is the _universal_ power over its external nature opposed to it, the _immediate_ going-together of what is taken in internally with animality; an infection by this and _simple_ _transformation_ (§ 345, Remark, § 346). Second, as _mediation_ assimilation is _digestion_; – counterposition of the subject to the external, and, according to the further difference, as process of animal _water_ (of the gastric and pancreatic juice, of animal lymph in general) and of animal _fire_ (_of_ _the_ _bile_, in which the _being-turned-in-upon-itself_ of the organism, starting from its concentration, which it has in the spleen, is determined to _being-for-itself_ and to active consumption); – processes that, however, are likewise particularized infections.

## {{ 365 }} § 365.

This _engaging_ with the external, the excitation and the process itself, has, however, _as_ _against_ _the_ _universality_ and _simple_ relation of the living thing to itself, likewise the determination of _externality_; this engaging itself therefore properly constitutes the object and the negative as against the subjectivity of the organism, which it has to overcome and digest. This inversion of the view is the principle of the reflection of the organism into itself; the return into itself is the negation of its activity directed outward. It has the double determination that the organism, on the one hand, excretes its activity, which has been brought into conflict with the externality of the object, and on the other hand, having become _for_ _itself_ as immediately identical with this activity, has reproduced itself in this medium. The outward-going process is thus transformed into the first {{{ 20.364 }}} formal one, that of simple reproduction out of itself, into the closing-together of itself with itself.

::: remark
{{ 365 A }} The chief moment in digestion is the _immediate_ effect of life, as the _power_ over its inorganic object, which it presupposes to itself as its exciting stimulus only insofar as it is _in_ _itself_ identical with it but at the same time is its ideality and being-for-itself. This effect is _infection_ and immediate transformation; to it corresponds the _immediate_ seizure of the object shown in the exposition of purposive activity, § 208. – _Spallanzani's_ experiments and those of others, and the more recent physiology, have also proved empirically this immediacy with which the living thing, as _universal_, _continues_ _itself_ _into_ _it_ _without_ _further_ _mediation_ through its mere contact and through the taking of the foodstuff into its heat and sphere in general, and have shown it in accordance with the concept, – against the representation of a merely mechanical, fabricated _secreting_ and _separating-off_ of already finished, usable parts, as well as of a _chemical_ process. The investigations of the _mediating_ actions, however, have not yielded more _determinate_ moments of this transformation (as, for instance, with vegetable materials a series of _fermentations_ presents itself). It has, on the contrary, been shown, for instance, that much passes from the stomach into the mass of the fluids without having to go through the remaining stages of mediation, that the pancreatic juice is nothing other than saliva, and that the pancreas can well be dispensed with, and so forth. The final product, the chyle, which the _thoracic_ _duct_ receives and _pours_ into the blood, is the same lymph that every single viscus and organ excretes, that the skin and the lymphatic system obtain on every side through immediate transformation, and that is in every place already prepared. The lower animal organizations, which are in any case nothing but lymph coagulated into a membranous point or little tube – a simple intestinal canal – do not go beyond this immediate transformation. The {{{ 20.365 }}} _mediated_ process of digestion in the [higher] animal organizations is, with regard to its _peculiar_ _product_, just such a _superfluity_ as in plants is their production of seed mediated by the so-called difference of sex. – The faeces, especially in children, in whom the increase of matter nevertheless stands out most, frequently show the greatest part of the foodstuffs unchanged, mixed chiefly with _animal_ materials, _bile_, phosphorus and the like, and show as the chief effect of the organism this overcoming and removal of its own productions. – The syllogism of the organism is not for that reason the syllogism of _external_ _purposiveness_, because it does not content itself with turning its activity and form against the external object, but makes this process itself, which because of its externality is on the verge of becoming mechanical and chemical, into object. This conduct has been set out as the second premise in the universal syllogism of purposive activity, § 209. – The organism is a going-together of itself with itself in its external process, it takes and gains from it nothing but the chyle, that universal animalization of itself, and is thus, as the living concept existing for itself, just as much disjunctive activity, which removes this process from itself, abstracts from its _wrath_ against the object, this one-sided subjectivity, and thereby becomes _for_ _itself_ what it is in itself – the subjective, not neutral, identity of its concept and its reality – and so finds, in the end and product of its activity, what it already is from the beginning and originally. Hereby the _satisfaction_ is _rational_; the process that enters into external difference turns over into the organism's process with itself, and what results is not merely the bringing-forth of a means but that of the purpose, a closing-together with itself.
:::

## {{ 366 }} § 366.

Through the process with external nature the animal gives to the certainty of itself, to its subjective concept, truth, objectivity, as a _single_ individual. This _production_ of itself is thus self-preservation or {{{ 20.366 }}} _reproduction_, but further, _in_ _itself_, subjectivity, having become product, is at the same time sublated as an _immediate_ one; the concept, thus gone together with itself, is determined as the _concrete_ _universal_, the _genus_, which enters into relation and process with the singularity of subjectivity.
