## b. Cohesion

## {{ 295 }} § 295.

In _cohesion_ the immanent form posits a mode of the spatial side-by-sideness of the material parts other than that determined by the direction of gravity. This thus specific mode of the holding-together of the material is posited only in the diverse in general, and has not yet returned to a totality (shape) closed within itself; it therefore comes to appearance only against masses likewise different and cohesively different, and shows itself accordingly as a peculiar _mode_ _of_ _resistance_ in mechanical conduct toward _other_ masses.

## {{ 296 }} § 296.

This unity of form of the manifold being-outside-one-another is itself manifold. α) Its _first_ determinateness is the wholly indeterminate holding-together, insofar as cohesion of what in itself lacks cohesion, hence _adhesion_ with another. β) The coherence of matter _with_ _itself_ is at first the merely _quantitative_, – common cohesion, the strength of the holding-together against weight, – but further the _qualitative_, the peculiarity of yielding and, precisely thereby, of showing itself self-subsistently in its form against the pressure and impact of external force. According to the determinate manner of the forms of space, the inwardly mechanizing geometry produces the peculiarity of asserting a determinate {{{ 20.295 }}} _dimension_ in the holding-together, _punctuality_, – brittleness, _linearity_, – rigidity in general and, more closely, toughness, – _planarity_, – ductility, malleability.

## {{ 297 }} § 297.

γ) The corporeal, against whose force a corporeal asserts its peculiarity in yielding, is an _other_ _individual_ _body_. But as coherent, the body is also in itself a materiality that is outside itself, whose parts, as the whole suffers force, exert force _against_ _one_ _another_ and yield, but, as equally self-subsistent, sublate the negation suffered and restore themselves. Yielding, and in it the peculiar self-preservation toward the _outside_, is therefore immediately linked with this _inner_ yielding and self-preservation against itself, – _elasticity_.

## {{ 298 }} § 298.

_Ideality_ here comes to _existence_, which the material parts as matter _only_ _seek_, the point of unity that is _for_ _itself_, in which they, as actually attracted, would be only negated. This point of unity, insofar as they are only heavy, is at first _outside_ them and so only _in_ _itself_; in the negation shown, which they undergo, this ideality is now posited. But it is still conditioned, being only one side of the relation, whose other side is the subsistence of the parts that are _outside_ _one_ _another_, so that their negation passes over into their restoration. Elasticity is therefore only alteration of specific gravity that restores itself.

::: remark
{{ 298 A }} When material _parts_ are spoken of here and elsewhere, what is to be understood is not atoms or molecules, that is, not parts subsisting separately for themselves, but parts differentiated only quantitatively or contingently, so that {{{ 20.296 }}} their continuity is essentially not to be separated from their distinctness; elasticity is the existence of the dialectic of these moments itself. The _place_ of the material is its _indifferent_ determinate _subsistence_, the _ideality_ of this subsistence is thus the _continuity_ posited as _real_ unity, that is, that two material parts previously _subsisting_ outside one another, which are therefore to be represented as located in different places, are now located in _one_ and _the_ _same_ place. This is the _contradiction_, and it exists here materially. It is the same contradiction that underlies the Zenonian dialectic of motion, except that in motion it concerns abstract places, but here _material_ places, material parts. In motion space posits itself temporally and time spatially (§ 260); motion falls into the Zenonian antinomy, which is insoluble if the places are _isolated_ as points of space and the moments of time as points of time, and the solution of the antinomy, that is, motion, can be grasped only in this way, that space and time are continuous in themselves, and the moving body is at the _same_ place and at the same time _not_, that is, is at the same time in _another_, and likewise the same point of time both is and is not, that is, is at the same time an _other_. So in elasticity the material part, atom, molecule, is posited at the same time as affirmatively taking up its space, as _subsisting_, and equally at the same time as not subsisting, – as quantum in one, as extensive magnitude and as only intensive magnitude. – Against the positing-into-one of the material parts in elasticity, the often-mentioned invention of _pores_ is likewise called on for help in the so-called explanation. Although elsewhere it is conceded in the abstract that matter is transient, not absolute, there is nevertheless resistance to this in the application, when matter is _in_ _fact_ to be grasped as negative, when negation is to be posited _in_ _it_. The pores are indeed the negative, – for it is no use, one must go on to this determination, – but they are the negative only _alongside_ matter, the negative _not_ _of_ _matter_ _itself_, but _where_ _it_ _is_ _not_; so that matter, in fact, is assumed only as affirmative, as {{{ 20.297 }}} _absolutely_ _self-subsistent_, _eternal_. This error is introduced by the understanding's general error that the metaphysical is only a thought-thing _alongside_, that is, _outside_ actuality; so _alongside_ the belief in the non-absoluteness of matter there is _also_ belief _in_ its absoluteness; the former takes place outside science, if it takes place; the latter, however, is valid essentially in science.
:::

## {{ 299 }} § 299.

The ideality posited in this is an alteration that is a doubled negating. The negating of the (outside-one-another) subsistence of the material parts is negated just as the restoring of their being-outside-one-another and of their cohesion; it is _one_ ideality as alternation of determinations that sublate one another, the inner trembling of the body in itself, – _sound_.
