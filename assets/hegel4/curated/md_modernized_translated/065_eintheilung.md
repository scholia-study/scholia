## Division

## {{ 252 }} § 252.

The idea as nature is:

+ I. in the determination of _being-outside-one-another_, of infinite _isolation_, outside of which the unity of form, and thus this unity as an _ideal_ one, is only {{{ 20.242 }}} _in_ _itself_ and therefore only _sought_: matter and its ideal system, – _mechanics_.
+ II. in the determination of _particularity_, so that reality is posited with immanent determinateness of form and with difference existing in it – a relation of reflection whose being-within-itself is natural _individuality_, – _physics_.
+ III. in the determination of _subjectivity_, in which the real differences of form are likewise brought back to the _ideal_ unity that has found itself and is for itself, – _organics_.
