## {{{ 20.354 }}} a. Shape

## {{ 353 }} § 353.

Shape is the animal subject as a whole _only_ _in_ _relation_ _to_ _itself_. It presents on itself the _concept_ in its _determinations_, developed and existing as such in it. These are, though concrete in themselves as in subjectivity, 1) as its simple elements. The animal subject is therefore α) its simple, _universal_ _being-within-itself_ in its externality, whereby the actual determinateness is taken up _immediately_ as particularity into the _universal_, and this is in it the undivided identity of the subject with itself – _sensibility_; – β) _particularity_ as excitability from without and the reaction coming from the receiving subject against it, outward – _irritability_; – γ) the unity of these moments, the _negative_ return to itself out of the relation of externality, and thereby the generating and positing of itself as a _singular_ – _reproduction_; the reality and ground of the first moments.

## {{ 354 }} § 354.

These three moments of the _concept_ are, 2) not only concrete elements in themselves, but have their reality in three systems, the _nerve-_, _blood-_ and _digestive_ _systems_, each of which, as a totality, distinguishes itself within itself according to the same determinations of the concept.

a\. The system of _sensibility_ thus determines itself, α) to the extreme of the _abstract_ relation of itself to itself, which is thereby a passing over into _immediacy_, into inorganic being and absence of sensation, but not a having-passed-over into it – the <i>bone</i> system, which, toward the _interior_, is an envelope, and toward the _exterior_ the firm hold of the interior against the {{{ 20.355 }}} exterior; β) to the moment of _irritability_, the system of the brain and its further dispersal into the nerves, which are, just as much, inward nerves of sensation, outward nerves of moving; γ) to the system belonging to _reproduction_, the sympathetic nerve with the ganglia, into which falls only dull, indeterminate and will-less self-feeling.

b\. _Irritability_ is just as much excitability by an other and reaction of self-preservation against it, as, conversely, active self-preservation and, therein, giving oneself up to an other. Its system is α) _abstract_ _(sensible)_ irritability, the _simple_ alteration of receptivity into reactivity – _muscle_ in general; which, gaining at the bony frame its external hold (immediate relation to itself for its disjunction), at first differentiates itself into extensor and flexor muscle and then forms further into the peculiar system of the extremities. β) Irritability for itself and different from others, relating itself concretely to itself and holding itself _within_ _itself_, is activity within itself, _pulsation_, living self-motion, whose material is only a _fluid_, the living _blood_ – and which can only be circulation, which, first specified to the _particularity_ from which it comes, is in itself a double one and herein at the same time directed _outward_ – as the pulmonary and portal-vein systems, in the former of which the blood _kindles_ in itself, in the latter against an other. γ) _Pulsation_, as an irritable totality closing together with itself, is the circulation returning into itself from its center, _the_ _heart_, in the difference of arteries and veins, which is just as much an _immanent_ process as a universal giving over to the _reproduction_ of the remaining members, in that they take their nourishment from the blood.

c\. The digestive system is, as glandular system with skin and cellular tissue, the _immediate_, vegetative, but in the proper system of the viscera the _mediating_ reproduction.

## {{{ 20.356 }}} {{ 355 }} § 355.

3) But for shape the differences of the elements and of their systems unite just as much into a universal concrete interpenetration, so that every formation of the shape contains them linked in itself, as it itself divides _itself_ α) into the _centers_ _of_ _the_ _three_ _systems_ (insectum), head, breast and abdomen, to which the extremities, for mechanical motion and grasping, add the moment of singularity setting itself _outward_ as distinguished. β) According to the abstract difference, shape distinguishes itself into the _two_ _directions_, _inward_ and _outward_. From each of the systems, the side going inward is allotted to the one direction, the side going outward to the other, of which the latter, as the _different_ in itself, presents this difference through the symmetrical duality of its organs and members (_Bichat's_ vie organique et animale). γ) The whole, as shape perfected into self-subsistent individuality, is in this universality relating to itself at the same time _particularized_ in it into the <i>sex</i>-relation, into a relation turned outward to an other individual. Shape points in itself, being closed within itself, to its two directions outward.

## {{ 356 }} § 356.

4) As living it is essentially process, and indeed as such it is the _abstract_ one, the _process_ _of_ _shaping_ _within_ _itself_, in which the organism makes its own members into its inorganic nature, into means, consumes itself and itself produces itself, that is, just this totality of articulation, so that each member is reciprocally purpose and means, maintains itself from the others and against them; – the process that has the simple immediate self-feeling for its result.
