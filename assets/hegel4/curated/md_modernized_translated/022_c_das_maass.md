## {{{ 20.141 }}} C. Measure

## {{ 107 }} § 107.

Measure is the qualitative quantum, first of all as _immediate_, a quantum to which an existence or a quality is bound.

## {{ 108 }} § 108.

Since quality and quantity stand in measure only in _immediate_ unity, their difference likewise comes forward in them immediately. To that extent the specific quantum is, first, a mere quantum, so that the existence can grow or shrink without sublating the measure, which in this respect acts as a _rule_; second, though, the quantum's alteration brings with it an alteration in the quality as well.

## {{ 109 }} § 109.

The _measureless_ is first of all this passing of a measure, through its quantitative nature, beyond its qualitative determinateness. But since the other quantitative ratio, the measureless of the first, is just as much qualitative, the measureless is likewise a measure; both of which transitions, from quality into quantum and from this into that, can again be represented as _infinite_ _progress_, – as the measure's sublating and restoring itself in the measureless.

## {{ 110 }} § 110.

What happens here in fact is the sublation of the _immediacy_ that still attaches to measure as such; in it, quality and quantity are initially themselves _immediate_, and what exists is only their _relative_ _identity_. Measure, though, shows itself to sublate itself into the measureless, {{{ 20.142 }}} yet in the measureless, which negates it but is itself a unity of quantity and quality, to be merging just as much only _with_ _itself_.

## {{ 111 }} § 111.

The infinite, affirmation as negation of the negation, formerly had the more abstract sides, being and nothing, something and an other, and so forth; its sides are now quality and quantity. These have, α) first of all, _passed_ _over_ into each other, the passage running from quality into quantity (§ 98) and from quantity into quality (§ 105), and with it both are shown as _negations_; β) in their _unity_ (the measure), however, they are at first kept apart, each being only _by_ _means_ _of_ the other; γ) once the immediacy of this unity has turned out to sublate itself, the unity is now _posited_ as what it is _in_ _itself_: a simple relation-to-itself that holds being in general, with its forms, as sublated within it. – Being, or immediacy, that through negating itself is mediation _with_ _itself_ and relation to itself, and thereby equally a mediation sublating itself into relation-to-itself, into immediacy: this is _essence_.
