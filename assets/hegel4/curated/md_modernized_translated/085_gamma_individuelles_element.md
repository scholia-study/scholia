## {{{ 20.287 }}} γ) Individual Element

## {{ 285 }} § 285.

The element of the _developed_ difference and of its _individual_ determination is earthiness in general, still indeterminate at first, as distinguished from the other moments; but as the totality that holds them together, in their diversity, in individual unity, it is the power that fans them into the process and sustains it.
