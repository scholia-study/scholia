## α) Desire

## {{ 426 }} § 426.

Taken in its immediacy, self-consciousness is a _singular_ and is _desire_, – the contradiction between its abstraction, which is meant to be objective, and its immediacy, which wears the shape of an external object and is meant to be subjective. The object is determined as a nullity for the certainty of itself that has proceeded from the sublating of consciousness, and equally, for the way {{{ 20.429 }}} self-consciousness relates to the object, its abstract ideality is so determined.

## {{ 427 }} § 427.

Self-consciousness is therefore, _in_ _itself_, present to itself in the object, which in this relation corresponds to the drive. Through the negation of the two one-sided moments, carried out as the I's own activity, this identity arises _for_ the I itself. In itself, and for self-consciousness, the object is the selfless, and hence it cannot oppose this activity; its dialectic, which is its very nature, consists in sublating itself, and that dialectic exists here as the I's activity just mentioned. The given object is in this way posited as subjective to precisely the degree that subjectivity gives up its one-sidedness and turns objective to itself.

## {{ 428 }} § 428.

The process produces an I that closes together with itself and so, satisfied _for_ _itself_, is something actual. Outwardly, however, it stays in this return at first determined as _singular_ and has preserved itself as such, since it relates to the selfless object solely in a negative way, and the object, in that regard, is merely consumed. Desire, in its satisfaction, is therefore _destructive_ throughout, and as regards its content _selfish_; and because satisfaction has come about only in the singular, which passes away, desire is generated afresh in satisfaction.

## {{ 429 }} § 429.

Yet the self-feeling that arises for it in satisfaction does not, inwardly or _in_ _itself_, remain confined to abstract _being-for-itself_ or to its {{{ 20.430 }}} singularity alone; because the result negates _immediacy_ and singularity, it contains the determination of _universality_ and of the _identity_ of self-consciousness with its object. When this self-consciousness judges, or dirempts, itself, it becomes conscious of a _free_ object; in that object I has knowledge of itself as I, and yet the object also still lies outside it.
