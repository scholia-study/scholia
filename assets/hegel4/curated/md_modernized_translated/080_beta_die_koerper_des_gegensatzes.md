## β) The Bodies of Opposition

## {{ 279 }} § 279.

The dark, at first the negative of light, is the opposition against light's abstractly identical ideality, – _opposition_ in itself; it has material reality and falls apart in itself into the _duality_ of α) corporeal _diversity_, that is, of material being-for-itself, of _rigidity_, β) of _counterposition_ as such, which, not held by individuality and for itself, has merely collapsed into itself, is dissolution and _neutrality_; the former is the _lunar_, the latter the _cometary_ body.

::: remark
{{{ 20.283 }}} {{ 279 A }} These two bodies also have in the system of gravity, as _relative_ _central_ _bodies_, a peculiarity, resting on the same concept, as their physical one, which can be noted more precisely here. – They do not rotate on their axis. The _body_ _of_ _rigidity_, as formal being-for-itself, which is self-subsistence entangled in opposition and therefore not individuality, is accordingly _serving_ and the _satellite_ of another, in which it has its _axis_. The body of _dissolution_, the opposite of rigidity, is on the other hand _erratic_ in its conduct, and, in its eccentric orbit as in its physical existence, presents contingency; – they show themselves as a superficial concretion, which may just as contingently disperse again. – The _moon_ has no atmosphere, and therefore lacks the meteorological process. It shows only high mountains and craters, and the inflammation of this rigidity in itself; – the shape of a crystal, which _Heim_ (one of the ingenious geognosts) has also shown to be the original shape of the merely rigid earth. – The _comet_ appears as a formal process, a restless mass of vapor; none has shown anything rigid, a _nucleus_. In the most recent times astronomers no longer act so coy and aloof as formerly toward the ancients' representation that comets are merely meteors formed for a moment. So far only the return of a few has been demonstrated; others were expected by calculation but did not come. Before the thought that the solar system is in fact a system, a totality essentially coherent in itself, the formal view must be given up that comets are an appearance contingent to the whole of the system and cutting across it every which way. Thus the thought can be grasped that the other bodies of the system _defend_ themselves against them, that is, must conduct themselves and maintain themselves as necessary organic moments; with this, better grounds of consolation than hitherto can be offered against the dangers feared from comets; – grounds of consolation that rest chiefly only on this, that comets otherwise have so much room in the wide heavens for their paths, and for that reason _surely_ _will_ _not_ (a _surely_ _will_ {{{ 20.284 }}} _not_ that is turned more learnedly into a theory of probability) strike the earth.
:::
