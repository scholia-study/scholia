## {{{ 20.137 }}} b. Quantum

## {{ 101 }} § 101.

Quantity, essentially _posited_ with the excluding determinateness contained in it, is _quantum_: limited quantity.

## {{ 102 }} § 102.

The quantum has its development and complete determinateness in _number_, which contains within itself, as its element, the one, according to the moment of discreteness the _amount_, according to that of continuity the _unit_, as its qualitative moments.

::: remark
{{ 102 A }} In arithmetic the _modes_ _of_ _calculation_ are usually presented as contingent ways of handling numbers. If a necessity, and with it a sense, is to lie in them, it must lie in a principle, and this can lie only in the determinations contained in the concept of number itself; this principle shall be briefly displayed here. – The determinations of the concept of number are the _amount_ and the _unit_, and number itself is the unity of the two. But the unit, applied to empirical numbers, is only their _equality_; so the principle of the modes of calculation must be to put numbers into the relation of unit and amount and to bring about the equality of these determinations.

Since the ones, or the numbers themselves, are indifferent to one another, the unity into which they are brought appears, in general, as an external gathering together. Calculating is therefore in general _counting_, and the difference among the _kinds_ of calculating lies solely in the qualitative constitution of the numbers that are counted together, and for this constitution the determination of unit and amount is the principle.

{{{ 20.138 }}} _Numbering_ is the first, making number _in_ _general_, a gathering together of any number of _ones_. – A <i>mode</i> of calculation, however, is the counting together of such as are already numbers, no longer the mere one.

Numbers are _immediately_ and _at_ _first_ entirely indeterminate numbers in general, hence unequal in general; the gathering together or counting of such is _adding_.

The _next_ determination is that the numbers are _equal_ in general; thereby they make up one _unit_, and there is an _amount_ of such; to count such numbers is _multiplying_; – whereby it is indifferent how the determinations of amount and unit are distributed between the two numbers, the factors, which is taken for the amount and which, by contrast, for the unit.

The _third_ determinateness is, finally, the _equality_ of the _amount_ and the _unit_. The counting together of numbers so determined is _raising_ _to_ _a_ _power_ – and first of all to the _square_. – Further raising to powers is the formal continuation, issuing again into indeterminate amount, of multiplying the number by itself. – Since in this third determination the complete equality of the only difference present, that of amount and unit, is attained, there can be no more than these three modes of calculation. – Corresponding to the counting together is the resolving of numbers according to the same determinatenesses. Hence, besides the three kinds mentioned, which can to that extent be called the _positive_ ones, there are also three _negative_ ones.
:::
