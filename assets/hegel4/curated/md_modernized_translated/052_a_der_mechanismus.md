## {{{ 20.205 }}} a. Mechanism

## {{ 195 }} § 195.

1) In its immediacy the object is the concept only _in itself_; it has the concept initially _outside_ _itself_, and all determinateness is one posited externally. As a unity of distinct items it is therefore something _compounded_, an aggregate, and its efficacy upon an other remains an external relation – _formal_ _mechanism_. – Within this relation and this lack of self-subsistence the objects remain just as self-subsistent, offering resistance and standing _external_ to one another.

::: remark
{{ 195 A }} Just as pressure and impact are mechanical relations, so we also know mechanically, _by_ _rote_, insofar as the words are without sense for us and remain external to meaning, representing and thinking; they are equally external to one another, a senseless succession. Action, piety and so forth are _mechanical_ in the same way whenever ceremonial laws, a director of conscience and the like fix for a person what he does; his own spirit and will then have no part in his deeds, and these are therefore external to himself.
:::

## {{ 196 }} § 196.

The lack of self-subsistence by which the object suffers _force_ it has (by the preceding §) only insofar as it is self-subsistent; and as the posited concept in itself, the one of these determinations does not sublate itself in its other, but the object closes together with itself through the negation of itself, of its lack of self-subsistence, and only so is it self-subsistent. Thus, standing at once in difference from externality and negating that externality in its own self-subsistence, this [self-subsistence] is _negative_ _unity_ with itself, _centrality_, subjectivity – and in it the object has its own direction and reference toward what is external. This external item is equally {{{ 20.206 }}} central within itself and therein equally related only to the other center, equally having its centrality in the other; – 2) _different_ _mechanism_ (fall, desire, the social drive and the like).

## {{ 197 }} § 197.

Developing this relation yields a syllogism: immanent negativity – the _central_ singularity of one object, the abstract center – refers to unself-subsistent objects, which are the other extreme, by way of a middle term, the relative center, in which the objects' centrality and their lack of self-subsistence are combined; – 3) _absolute_ _mechanism_.

## {{ 198 }} § 198.

The syllogism just stated (S–P–U) is a threefold of syllogisms. The bad _singularity_ of the _non-self-subsistent_ objects, in which formal mechanism is at home, is, as lack of self-subsistence, just as much an external _universality_. Hence these objects also serve as the _middle_ _term_ linking the _absolute_ center and the _relative_ one (the syllogistic form U–S–P), since their lack of self-subsistence is what dirempts those two into extremes and at the same time relates them to each other. In the same way _absolute_ _centrality_, the substantially universal (– gravity that remains self-identical), which as pure negativity also holds singularity within it, mediates between the _relative_ _center_ and the _non-self-subsistent_ objects, in the form of the syllogism P–U–S; and it does so essentially both by dirempting, in virtue of its immanent singularity, and by keeping together in identity and resting undisturbed within itself, in virtue of its universality.

::: remark
{{ 198 A }} Like the solar system, the state, for instance in the practical sphere, is a system of three syllogisms. 1) By way of his _particularity_ (physical and spiritual needs, whose independent elaboration gives civil society), the _singular_ (the person) joins with the _universal_ (society, right, law, government). 2) The will and activity of individuals is the mediating element that supplies the {{{ 20.207 }}} needs with their satisfaction from society, right and so forth, and supplies society, right and so forth with their fulfilment and actualization; 3) but the universal (state, government, right) is the substantial middle in which the individuals and their satisfaction have and receive their fulfilled reality, mediation and subsistence. Each of the determinations, in that the mediation closes it together with the other extreme, closes itself together with itself in that very act, produces itself, and this production is self-preservation. – Only through the nature of this closing-together, through this triad of syllogisms with the same terms, is a whole truly understood in its organization.
:::

## {{ 199 }} § 199.

In absolute mechanism the objects possess an _immediacy_ of existence, and this is negated _in itself_ by the fact that their self-subsistence is mediated through their relation to one another, that is, through their lack of self-subsistence. Thus the object, in its _existence_, is to be posited as _different_ from _its_ other.
