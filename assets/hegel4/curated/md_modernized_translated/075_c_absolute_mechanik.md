## {{{ 20.266 }}} C. Absolute Mechanics

## {{ 269 }} § 269.

Gravitation is the true and determinate _concept_ of material corporeality, realized as _idea_. _Universal_ corporeality judges itself essentially into _particular_ bodies and closes itself together into the moment of _singularity_ or subjectivity, as appearing existence in _motion_, which is thereby immediately a system of _several_ _bodies_.

::: remark
{{ 269 A }} Universal gravitation must be acknowledged in itself as a profound thought, even though it has drawn attention and confidence to itself chiefly through the quantitative determination bound up with it, and has had its confirmation placed upon the _experience_ pursued from the solar system down to the phenomenon of capillary tubes, so that, grasped in the sphere of reflection, it has only the significance of abstraction in general and, more concretely, only that of _gravity_ in the determination of magnitude of fall, not the significance of the idea stated in the paragraph, developed in its reality. Gravitation immediately contradicts the law of inertia, for by virtue of the former matter strives _of_ _itself_ toward the other. – In the _concept_ of _gravity_ itself, as has been shown, both the moments of being-for-itself and of the continuity that sublates being-for-itself are contained. These moments of the concept meet the fate of being grasped as particular forces, corresponding to attractive and repulsive force, and more closely determined as _centripetal_ and _centrifugal_ _force_, which, like gravity, are to _act_ _on_ _the_ _bodies_, independent of one another and colliding contingently in a third, the body. By this, whatever is more profound in the thought of universal gravity is again brought to nothing, and for as long as the so highly praised discoveries of _forces_ prevail in it, concept and reason cannot penetrate into the doctrine of absolute motion {{{ 20.267 }}}. In the syllogism that contains the _idea_ of gravity, namely gravity itself as the concept that opens itself out through the particularity of bodies into external reality, and at the same time shows itself in their ideality and reflection-into-itself, in motion, as _joined_ _together_ _with_ _itself_, – there is contained the rational identity and inseparability of the moments that are otherwise represented as self-subsistent. – Motion as such has meaning and existence at all only in a system of _several_ bodies, and indeed bodies standing in relation to one another according to different _determination_. This closer determination in the syllogism of totality, which is itself a system of three syllogisms, is given in the concept of objectivity, see § 198.
:::

## {{ 270 }} § 270.

As regards the bodies in which the concept of gravity is realized freely for itself, they have the moments of their concept as determinations of their differentiated nature. One of them is thus the _universal_ center of abstract relation to itself. Opposed to this extreme stands _immediate_ singularity, outside itself and centerless, likewise appearing as self-subsistent corporeality. The _particular_ ones, however, are those that stand both in the determination of being-outside-itself and at the same time of being-within-itself, are centers for themselves and relate themselves to the first [as] to their essential unity.

::: remark
{{ 270 A }} The _planetary_ bodies, as the immediately _concrete_ ones, are the most perfect in their existence. One is accustomed to take the sun as the most excellent, insofar as the understanding prefers the abstract to the concrete, just as even the fixed stars are esteemed more highly than the bodies of the solar system. – Centerless corporeality, as belonging to externality, particularizes itself in itself, into the opposition of the _lunar_ and the _cometary_ body.

{{{ 20.268 }}} The _laws_ of absolutely free motion were, as is well known, discovered by _Kepler_; a discovery of immortal fame. _Kepler_ _proved_ them in the sense that he found, for the empirical data, their _universal_ expression (§ 227.) Since then it has become a commonplace that _Newton_ first found the proofs of those laws. Hardly ever has fame passed more unjustly from one first discoverer to another. I remark on this the following:

+ 1) that it is conceded by mathematicians that the Newtonian formulas can be derived from the Keplerian laws. The wholly immediate derivation, however, is simply this: In the third Keplerian law A³/T² is the constant. If this is set as A.A²/T², and A/T² is called, with Newton, universal gravity, then there is given his expression of the effect of this so-called gravity in inverse ratio to the square of the distances;
+ 2) that the Newtonian proof of the proposition that a body subject to the law of gravitation moves in an _ellipse_ around the central body concerns a _conic_ _section_ in general, whereas the principal proposition that was to be proved consists precisely in this, that the orbit of such a body is _not_ _a_ _circle_ _or_ _any_ _other_ _conic_ _section_, but _the_ _ellipse_ _alone_. Against that proof taken by itself (Princ. Math. l. I. Sect. II. prop. I.) objections are anyway to be made; analysis, too, no longer needs it, the foundation of the Newtonian theory. The conditions that make the body's orbit a _determinate_ conic section are _constants_ in the analytical formula, and their determination is traced back to an _empirical_ circumstance, namely a particular position of the body at a determinate point of time and the _contingent_ strength of an _impact_ that it is supposed to have originally received; so that the circumstance that determines the curved line to be an ellipse falls outside the {{{ 20.269 }}} formula that is supposed to have been proved, and no thought is even given to proving it;
+ 3) that the Newtonian law of the so-called force of gravity is likewise shown only from experience by induction.

Nothing is to be seen but the difference that what Kepler expressed in a simple and sublime manner, in the form of _laws_ _of_ _celestial_ _motion_, Newton has transformed into the _form_ _of_ _reflection_ _of_ _a_ _force_ _of_ _gravity_, indeed the same as in the case of fall, in which the law of its magnitude emerges. If the Newtonian form has for the analytical method not merely convenience but necessity, this is only a difference of the mathematical formula; analysis has long known how to derive the Newtonian expression and the propositions connected with it from the form of the Keplerian laws; (I keep to the elegant exposition on this in Francoeur's Traité élém. de Mécanique Liv. II. Ch. II. n. IV.). – In general the older manner of so-called proving presents a confused web, made of _lines_ of merely geometrical construction to which a physical significance of _self-subsistent_ _forces_ is given, and of empty determinations of reflection, from the _accelerating_ _force_ and the _force_ _of_ _inertia_ already mentioned, above all the relation of so-called gravity itself to centripetal force and centrifugal force and so on.

The remarks made here would require a more extensive discussion than a compendium has room for. Propositions that do not agree with what is accepted appear as assertions; and, in contradicting such high authorities, as something still worse, namely as presumptions. What has been cited, however, are not so much propositions as bare facts, and the reflection demanded is {{{ 20.270 }}} only this, that the distinctions and determinations which mathematical analysis brings about, and the course it has to take according to its method, are wholly to be distinguished from what is supposed to have a physical reality. The presuppositions, the course and the results which analysis needs and gives remain wholly outside the considerations that concern the _physical_ value and the _physical_ significance of those determinations and of that course. It is to this that attention should be directed; what is at issue is a consciousness about the inundation of physical mechanics with an _unspeakable_ _metaphysics_ that – against experience and concept – has those mathematical determinations alone as its source.

It is recognized that the <i>content</i>ful moment which _Newton_ added to the import of the Keplerian laws, beyond the foundation of the _analytical_ treatment (whose development has, moreover, itself made superfluous, indeed rejected, much that belonged to his essential principles and to his fame), is the principle of _perturbation_, – a principle whose importance is to be cited here insofar as it rests on the proposition that the so-called attraction is an effect of all the single parts of bodies, as material. It lies in this that matter in general posits the center for itself. The mass of the particular body is accordingly to be considered as a moment in the _determination_ _of_ _its_ _place_, and the bodies of the system as a whole posit their sun for themselves, but even the single bodies, according to the relative position into which they come toward one another by their universal motion, form a momentary relation of gravity _on_ _one_ _another_, and conduct themselves not merely in the abstract spatial relation, that of distance, but posit with one another a _particular_ center, which, however, partly dissolves again in the universal system, and partly at least, if {{{ 20.271 }}} such a relation is lasting (in the mutual disturbances of Jupiter and Saturn), remains subject to it.

If, then, some basic outlines are given according to this of how the main determinations of free motion are connected _with_ _the_ _concept_, these cannot be developed at greater length for their grounding, and must therefore first of all be left to their fate. The principle in this is that the rational proof of the quantitative determinations of free motion can rest only on the _determinations_ _of_ _space_ _and_ _time_ _by_ _the_ _concept_, the moments whose relation (though not an external one) motion is. When will science at last come to attain a consciousness of the metaphysical categories that it uses, and to take the concept of the fact as its basis instead of them!

That, _first_, motion in general is one that is _returning_ _into_ _itself_ lies in the determination of bodies of particularity and singularity in general (§ 269.), partly to have a center in themselves and self-subsistent existence, partly at the same time to have their center in another. These are the determinations by the concept that underlie the representations of a _centripetal_ _force_ and a _centrifugal_ _force_ but are perverted into the notion that each of these exists for itself as _self-subsistent_, outside the other, and acts independently, and that they meet one another only in their effects, _externally_ and thereby contingently. They are, as already remarked, the lines that must be drawn for mathematical determination, transformed into physical actualities.

Further, this motion is _uniformly_ _accelerated_ (and – as returning into itself – alternately uniformly _retarded_). In motion as _free_, space and time come to assert themselves, as what they are, as _diverse_ in the determination of the magnitude of motion (§ 267, Remark), and not to conduct themselves as in the abstract, {{{ 20.272 }}} plainly uniform velocity. – In the so-called _explanation_ of uniformly accelerated and retarded motion from the _reciprocal_ _decrease_ and _increase_ in the magnitude of centripetal force and centrifugal force, the _confusion_ that the assumption of such self-subsistent forces brings about is greatest. According to this explanation, in a planet's motion from aphelion to perihelion the centrifugal force is _smaller_ than the centripetal force, whereas at perihelion itself the centrifugal force is supposed to become at once larger again than the centripetal force; for the motion from perihelion to aphelion the forces are made to enter in just the same way into the opposite relation. One sees that such a _sudden_ _reversal_ of the preponderance attained by one force into a succumbing to the other is nothing taken from the nature of the forces. On the contrary, it would have to be inferred that a preponderance which the one force had attained over the other would not merely maintain itself but would have to issue in the complete annihilation of the other force, and the motion, either by the preponderance of the centripetal force, into rest, namely into the planet's plunging into the central body, or by the preponderance of the centrifugal force, into a straight line. The simple inference that is drawn is: because from its perihelion on the body moves farther from the sun, the centrifugal force becomes larger again; because at aphelion it is farthest from it, the force is greatest there. This metaphysical non-entity of a self-subsistent centrifugal force as of a centripetal force is presupposed; no further understanding is, however, to be applied to these fictions of the understanding, and it is not to be asked how such a force, since it is self-subsistent, _of_ _itself_ now makes itself weaker than the other, now makes itself preponderant, and lets itself be so made, and then cancels its preponderance again or lets it be taken from it. – If this groundless alternating increase and decrease is looked at further, then, at the mean distance from the apsides, there turn up points at which {{{ 20.273 }}} the forces are in _equilibrium_. Their supposed emergence from equilibrium thereafter is something just as unmotivated as that suddenness of reversal. In general one readily finds that with this way of explaining, the remedying of one defect by a further determination brings about new and greater confusions. – A similar confusion enters in the explanation of the phenomenon that the pendulum swings more slowly at the equator. This phenomenon is ascribed to the centrifugal force, supposed to be greater there; one could just as easily come to ascribe it to the enlarged force of gravity, which holds the pendulum more strongly toward the perpendicular line of rest.

As regards the _shape_ _of_ _the_ _orbit_, the _circle_ is to be grasped only as the orbit of a _plainly_ _uniform_ motion. It is indeed _conceivable_, as it is called, that a uniformly _increasing_ and <i>de</i>creasing motion should also take place in a circle. But this conceivability or possibility means only an abstract representability, which leaves out the determinate that matters and is therefore not merely superficial but false. The circle is the line returning into itself in which all radii are _equal_; that is, it is completely determined by the radius; there is only _one_, and it is the _whole_ determinateness. In free motion, however, where spatial and temporal determination enter into _diversity_, into a qualitative relation to each other, this relation necessarily emerges _in_ _the_ _spatial_ itself as a _difference_ of it, which accordingly requires _two_ determinations. Hereby the shape of the orbit returning into itself becomes essentially an _ellipse_. – The abstract determinateness that makes up the circle also appears in this way, that the arc or angle enclosed by two radii is, _independent_ _of_ _them_, a magnitude wholly empirical in relation to them. But in the motion determined by the concept, the distance from the center and the arc traversed in a time {{{ 20.274 }}} must be comprised in _one_ determinateness, must make up _one_ _whole_, the moments of the concept not being in contingency toward one another; so there results a determination of space of two dimensions, the _sector_. The arc is in this way essentially a function of the radius vector, and, being unequal in equal times, carries with it the inequality of the radii. That the spatial determination by time appears as a determination of two dimensions, as a _determination_ _of_ _surface_, is connected with what was said above (§ 267) in the case of fall about the exposition of the same determinateness, once as time in the root, the other time as space in the _square_. Here, however, the _square_ character of space is limited to the sector by the return of the line of motion into itself. – These are, as one sees, the universal principles on which the Keplerian law rests, that _in_ _equal_ _times_ _equal_ _sectors_ _are_ _cut_ _off_.

This law concerns only the relation of the arc to the radius vector, and time is here the abstract unit in which the different sectors are compared, because it is the determining factor as unit. But the further relation is that of time, not as unit, but as quantum in general, as time of revolution, to the magnitude of the orbit or, what is the same, the distance from the center. As root and square we saw time and space relate to each other in _fall_, the semi-free motion, which is indeed determined on one side by the concept, but on the other side externally. But in absolute motion, the realm of _free_ mass, every determinateness attains its totality. As root, time is a merely empirical magnitude, and, qualitatively, only abstract unity. As _moment_ of the developed totality, however, it is at the same time determinate unity in itself, totality for itself, produces itself and relates itself _therein_ _to_ _itself_; as what is dimensionless in itself, it arrives in its production only at formal identity with itself, the _square_, whereas space, as the positive being-outside-one-another, arrives at the dimension of the concept, _the_ _cube_. Their {{{ 20.275 }}} realization thus retains at the same time the original difference between them. This is the third Keplerian law, the relation of the _cube_ _of_ _the_ _distances_ _to_ the _squares_ of the times; – a law that is so great because it presents so simply and immediately the _reason_ _of_ _the_ _matter_. The Newtonian formula, on the other hand, by which it is turned into a law for the _force_ of gravity, shows the distortion and inversion of the _reflection_ that stops halfway.
:::

## {{ 271 }} § 271.

The substance of matter, gravity, developed to the _totality_ of form, no longer has matter's being-outside-itself outside itself. The _form_ appears at first, according to its differences in the ideal determinations of space, time and motion and according to its being-for-itself, as a _center_ determined _outside_ the matter that is outside itself; but in the developed totality this being-outside-one-another is posited as one determined absolutely by the form, and matter is nothing outside this its being-outside-one-another. The form is in this way materialized. Considered conversely, matter in this negation of its being-outside-itself in totality has received the formal determinateness in itself, in the center, formerly only sought, its self. Its abstract, dull being-within-itself, as heavy in general, is resolved into form; it is _qualified_ _matter_; – _physics_.
