## {{{ 20.276 }}} Second Division of the Philosophy of Nature. Physics

## {{ 272 }} § 272.

Matter has _individuality_ insofar as it has being-for-itself in itself in such a way that this is developed in it and matter is thereby _determined_ _in_ _itself_. In this way matter wrests itself free from gravity, manifests itself, determining itself in itself, and determines by means of the form immanent in it the spatial out of itself, over against gravity, to which this determining formerly belonged as to a center other than matter and only sought by it.

## {{ 273 }} § 273.

Physics has as its content:

+ A. _Universal_ _individuality_, _the_ _immediate_ _free_ _physical_ _qualities_.
+ B. _Particular_ _individuality_, _relation_ _of_ _the_ _form_ _as_ _physical_ _determination_ _to_ _gravity_ _and_ _determination_ _of_ _gravity_ _by_ _it_.
+ C. _Total_ _free_ _individuality_.
