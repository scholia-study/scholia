## {{{ 20.485 }}} c. Right against Wrong

## {{ 496 }} § 496.

Right, as the _existence_ of freedom in the _external_, falls into a _plurality_ of _relations_ to this external and to the other persons (§§ 491, 493 ff.). Hence there are 1) several _legal_ _grounds_, of which, since property is exclusively individual both on the side of the person and on that of the thing, only one is the _right_ one, but which, because they are _against_ one another, are jointly posited as the _shine_ of right, against which right is now determined as _right_ _in_ _itself_.

## {{ 497 }} § 497.

Since against this shine the one _right_ _in_ _itself_ is posited as affirmative, willed and _recognized_, still in immediate unity with the different legal grounds, the diversity lies only in this, that _this_ thing is subsumed under right by the _particular_ will of _these_ persons; – the _unprejudiced_ _wrong_. – This wrong is a simple _negative_ _judgment_, which expresses the _civil_ _legal_ _dispute_, and to settle it a _third_ judgment is demanded: one that, being the judgment of _right_ _in_ _itself_, takes no interest in the matter, and is the power to give itself existence in the face of that shine.

## {{{ 20.486 }}} {{ 498 }} § 498.

2) If, however, the shine of right as such is willed _against_ right-in-itself by the particular will, which thereby becomes _evil_, the external _recognition_ of right gets severed from its _value_, so that the former is respected while the latter is violated. This gives the wrong of _fraud_; – the identical form of the infinite judgment (§ 173), – the formal relation retained and the import omitted.

## {{ 499 }} § 499.

3) In so far as the particular will finally opposes itself to right-in-itself in the negation both of it itself and of its recognition or shine (– negatively infinite judgment, § 173, in which both the genus and the particular determinateness, here the appearing recognition, is negated) – it is a _violently_ _evil_ will, which commits a _crime_.

## {{ 500 }} § 500.

Such an action is, as a violation of right, null in and for itself. As will and as a thinking being, the one who acts sets up in it a law that is, however, formal and recognized only by that one, a universal that holds _for_ _the_ _agent_, and under which the agent has by the action at the same time subsumed himself or herself. The exhibited nullity of this action, the carrying out in one act of this formal law and of right-in-itself, at first through a _subjective_ singular will, is _revenge_, which, because it proceeds from the interest of _immediate_ {{{ 20.487 }}} _particular_ personality, is at the same time only a new violation, _on_ _into_ _infinity_. This progress likewise sublates itself in a third judgment that is without interest, in _punishment_.

## {{ 501 }} § 501.

The self-assertion of right-in-itself is mediated α) by a particular will, the judge, being adequate to right and having the interest of directing itself against the crime, – which is at first contingent in revenge, and β) by the (at first likewise contingent) power to carry out the negation of that negation of right which the criminal has posited. In the criminal's will this negation of right has its concrete existence; revenge or punishment therefore addresses itself 1) to the criminal's _person_ or _property_, 2) and exerts _coercion_ upon him or her. Throughout this sphere of right coercion occurs already against the thing, in seizing it and in maintaining it against another's seizure, because here the will's existence lies immediately in an _external_ _thing_ (as such, or in corporeality), and only there can it be seized. – Yet coercion is no more than possible, inasmuch as I, being free, can draw myself out of any concrete existence, even out of its whole compass, life. It is rightful only as the sublating of a first, immediate coercion.

## {{ 502 }} § 502.

Between right and the subjective will a difference has unfolded. The personal will first gives itself a reality of right in an immediate manner; this reality shows itself to be mediated by the subjective will, the moment that either lends existence to right-in-itself or else splits off from it and sets itself against it. Conversely, taken in this abstraction of being the power over right, the subjective will is for itself {{{ 20.488 }}} a nullity; truth and reality are essentially its own only when, within itself, it is the existence of the rational will, – _morality_.

::: remark
{{ 502 A }} The expression _natural right_, which has been customary for the philosophical doctrine of right, contains the ambiguity whether right is meant as something present in an _immediate_ _natural_ _manner_, or as what is determined through the nature of the matter, that is, the _concept_. The former sense is the one that used to be meant as a rule; so that at the same time a _state_ _of_ _nature_ was invented, in which natural right is supposed to hold, whereas the condition of society and the state requires and brings with it rather a limitation of freedom and a sacrifice of natural rights. In fact, however, right and all its determinations are grounded solely on _free_ _personality_, a _self-determination_ that is rather the opposite of _natural_ _determination_. The right of nature is therefore the existence of strength and the asserting of force, and a state of nature is a condition of violence and wrong, about which nothing truer can be said than that one is _to_ _leave_ _it_. Society, by contrast, is the very condition in which alone right has its actuality; what has to be limited and sacrificed is exactly the arbitrary will and violence of the state of nature.
:::
