## γ) Thinking

## {{ 465 }} § 465.

Intelligence is _re-cognizing_; – an intuition it _cognizes_ to the extent that the intuition is already its own (§ 454), and further, in the name, the matter (§ 462); {{{ 20.464 }}} but now what is universal to it is _its_ universal, taken in the twofold meaning of the universal as such and of that same universal as immediate or existent, and thus as the true universal, the unity of itself that reaches over its other, being. Intelligence is in this way cognizing _for_ _itself_, _within_ _itself_; – the _universal_ within itself, _its_ product, the _thought_, is the matter; a simple identity of the subjective and the objective. Intelligence knows what is _thought_ to _be_, and knows that what _is_ has being only in so far as it is thought; (compare §§ 5, 21) – _for_ _itself_; the _thinking_ of intelligence is a _having_ of _thoughts_; these are there as its content and object.

## {{ 466 }} § 466.

Thinking cognition, however, is likewise formal to begin with; universality and its being are the simple subjectivity of intelligence. Accordingly the thoughts are not determined in and for themselves, and, to that extent, the representations that thinking has recollected remain the given content.

## {{ 467 }} § 467.

In relation to this content it is α) formally identical _understanding_, working the recollected representations up into genera, species, laws, forces and so forth, in general into categories, in the sense that the material has the truth of its being only in these thought-forms. Being negativity infinite in itself, thinking is β) essentially _diremption_, – _judgment_; this no longer dissolves the concept into the earlier opposition of universality and being, but draws its distinctions along the connections peculiar to the concept, {{{ 20.465 }}} and γ) thinking sublates the determination of form while positing the identity of the differences; – _formal_ _reason_, _syllogizing_ _understanding_. – Intelligence _cognizes_ as thinking; and to be precise, α) when the understanding _explains_ the singular _from_ _its_ universalities (the categories), it calls itself _comprehending_; β) in judgment it _pronounces_ the same singular to be a universal (genus, species); in these forms the _content_ appears as given; γ) in the _syllogism_, however, it _determines_ [the] _content_ from itself, by sublating that difference of form. Once necessity is seen into, the last immediacy still clinging to formal thinking has disappeared.

::: remark
{{ 467 A }} Thinking in the _logic_ is thinking as it is at first _in_ _itself_, and reason unfolds in this element without opposition. In _consciousness_ thinking likewise comes up as a stage (see § 437, Remark). Here reason is the truth of the opposition in the form it had taken on within spirit itself. – Thinking keeps emerging in these different parts of the science because the parts differ only through the element and form of opposition, whereas thinking is the one and same center into which the oppositions return as into their truth.
:::

## {{ 468 }} § 468.

Theoretical intelligence appropriates immediate determinateness; once this _taking_ _possession_ is complete, it is in its _property_, and through the last negation of immediacy it is posited in itself that the content is determined by it, _for_ _it_. Thinking, as the free concept, is now free in its _content_ as well. Intelligence that knows itself as what determines the content, which is as much its own as it is determined as existent, is _will_.
