## β) Representation

## {{ 451 }} § 451.

Representation, as recollected intuition, is the middle between the immediate finding-itself-determined of intelligence and intelligence in its freedom, thinking. Representation is the _own_ of intelligence still with one-sided subjectivity, since this own is still conditioned by immediacy and is not itself _being_. The path of intelligence in representations is to make immediacy inward, to posit itself as _intuiting_ within _itself_ {{{ 20.446 }}}, and just as much to sublate the subjectivity of inwardness and externalize itself in it, and _to_ _be_ _in_ _itself_ in its _own_ _externality_. But since representing starts from intuition and its _found_ material, this activity remains burdened with that difference, and the concrete productions it yields there are still _syntheses_; only in thinking do they become the concept's concrete immanence.
