## CC. The State

## {{ 535 }} § 535.

The _self-conscious_ ethical substance is the state, – the uniting of the family's principle with that of civil society; the very unity that exists in the family as the feeling of love constitutes its essence, but through the second principle, that of a willing which knows and acts out of itself, this essence at the same time takes on the _form_ _of_ _universality_ _as_ _known_; and this universality, together with its determinations as they develop in knowing, has knowing subjectivity for its content and absolute purpose, which is to say it wills this rational element for itself.

## {{ 536 }} § 536.

The state is, α) first, its own inner shaping, a development relating itself to itself, – _internal_ _constitutional_ _law_, or _the_ _constitution_; β) it is a particular individual, standing thus in relation to other particular individuals, – _external_ _public_ _law_; γ) yet these particular spirits are merely moments in the course by which the universal idea of spirit {{{ 20.508 }}} develops in its actuality – _world_ _history_.
