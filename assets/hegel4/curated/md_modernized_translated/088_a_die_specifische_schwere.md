## a. Specific Gravity

## {{ 293 }} § 293.

The _simple_, abstract specification is _specific_ _gravity_ or _density_ of matter, a relation of the _weight_ of the mass to the _volume_, whereby the material, as self-like, tears itself loose from the abstract relation to the central body, from universal gravity, ceases to be the uniform filling of space and opposes to the abstract being-outside-one-another a specific being-within-itself.

::: remark
{{ 293 A }} The different density of matter is explained by the assumption of _pores_, – condensation by the invention of empty {{{ 20.293 }}} interspaces, which are spoken of as something _present_ that physics does not show, though it professes to rest on experience and observation. – An example of _existing_ specification of gravity is the phenomenon that an iron bar balanced in equilibrium on its point of support, when it is _magnetized_, loses its equilibrium and shows itself now heavier at the one pole than at the other. Here the one part is so affected that it becomes heavier without changing its volume; the matter, whose mass has not been increased, has thus become _specifically_ heavier. – The propositions that physics presupposes in its way of representing density are: 1) that an equal number of material parts of equal size is equally heavy; whereby 2) the measure of the number of parts is the quantum of weight, but 3) also space, so that what has an equal quantum of weight also takes up equal space; if therefore 4) equal weights nevertheless appear in different volumes, the equality of the space that is materially _filled_ is preserved by the assumption of pores. The invention of pores in the fourth proposition is made necessary by the first three propositions, which do not rest on experience but are founded only on the proposition of the understanding's identity, and are therefore formal, a priori inventions, like the pores. – _Kant_ already set _intensity_ over against the quantitative determination of _number_, and in place of _more_ parts in an equal volume posited an equal number but with a stronger _degree_ of _filling_ _of_ _space_, and thereby gave the origin to a so-called _dynamic_ _physics_. – At least the determination of the _intensive_ quantum would have as much right as that of the _extensive_, to which latter category that common representation of density is confined. The _intensive_ determination of magnitude has this advantage here, however, that it points to measure and at first indicates a _being-within-itself_ that in its determination by the concept is _immanent_ _determinateness_ _of_ _form_, which first appears in _comparison_ as quantum in general. Its differences as extensive or intensive, however, – and dynamic physics goes no further, – express no reality (§ 103, Remark).
:::

## {{{ 20.294 }}} {{ 294 }} § 294.

Density is as yet only _simple_ determinateness of heavy matter; but since matter remains the essential being-outside-one-another, the determination of form is further a specific mode of the spatial relation of its manifold to one another – _cohesion_.
