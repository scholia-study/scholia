## {{{ 20.386 }}} First Division of the Philosophy of Spirit. Subjective Spirit

## {{ 387 }} § 387.

Spirit developing itself in its ideality is spirit as _cognizing_. But cognition is here grasped not merely as it is the determinateness of the idea as logical (§ 223), but as the _concrete_ spirit determines itself toward it.

Subjective spirit is:

+ A. _In_ _itself_ or _immediate_; so it is _soul_ or _natural_ _spirit_; – object of ANTHROPOLOGY.
+ B. _For_ _itself_ or _mediated_, still being the identical reflection in itself and into an other; spirit in _relation_ or particularization; _consciousness_, – the object of the PHENOMENOLOGY _of_ _spirit_.
+ C. _Spirit_ _determining_ _itself_ _in_ _itself_, as _subject_ for itself, the object of PSYCHOLOGY.

In the _soul_ _consciousness_ _awakens_; consciousness _posits_ _itself_ _as_ _reason_, which has awakened immediately to reason knowing itself, and which through its activity frees itself to objectivity, to the consciousness of its concept.

::: remark
{{ 387 A }} As in the concept generally the _determinateness_ that occurs in it is _progress_ of _development_, so too in spirit every determinateness in which it shows itself is a moment of development and, in the further determining, a going forward toward its _goal_, so that {{{ 20.387 }}} it may make itself what it is _in_ _itself_ and become that _for_ _itself_. Each stage is within itself this process, and its product is that _for_ _spirit_ (that is, the form that it has in this stage) is what it was at the beginning of the stage _in_ _itself_, or thereby only _for_ _us_. – The psychological mode of consideration otherwise customary relates in narrative fashion what spirit or the soul _is_, what _befalls_ it, what it _does_; thereby the soul is presupposed as a ready-made subject within which such determinations show up merely as _expressions_, from which it is to be cognized what it _is_, what faculties and forces it possesses within itself; without consciousness that the _expression_ of what it _is_ posits in the concept the same _for_ _it_, whereby it has gained a higher determination. – From the progression [to be] considered here, that which is culture and education is to be distinguished and is excluded. This sphere relates _only_ to the _singular_ subjects as such, so that the universal spirit may be brought to concrete existence in them. In the philosophical view of spirit as such, it itself is considered as forming and educating itself in its concept, and its expressions as the moments of its producing itself to itself, of its closing together with itself, whereby it first is actual spirit.
:::
