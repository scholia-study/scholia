## A. Geological Nature

## {{ 338 }} § 338.

The first organism, already insofar as it is determined at first as immediate or existing _in_ _itself_, does not exist as a _living_ thing; life, as subject and process, is essentially an activity _mediating_ itself with itself. Considered from subjective life, the first moment of _particularization_ is to make itself its own _presupposition_, thus to give itself the manner of _immediacy_, and in it to set over _against_ itself its condition and external subsistence. The _recollection_ of the idea of nature into itself, to subjective and still more to spiritual livingness, is the _judgment_ in itself and into that processless immediacy. This immediate totality, presupposed to itself by the _subjective_ totality, is only the _shape_ of the organism – the _body_ _of_ _the_ _earth_ as the _universal_ _system_ of the individual bodies.

## {{ 339 }} § 339.

The members of this organism, existing only in itself, therefore do not contain the life-process within themselves and make up an external _system_, whose formations present the unfolding of an underlying idea, but whose _process_ _of_ _formation_ is a _past_ one. – The powers of this process, {{{ 20.346 }}} which nature leaves behind beyond the earth as self-subsistent things, are the connection and position of the earth in the solar system, its solar, lunar and cometary life, the inclination of its axis to the orbit, and the magnetic axis. In closer relation to these axes and their polarization stands the distribution of sea and land, the continuous expansion of the latter in the north, the division and pointed narrowing of its parts toward the south, the further separation into an old world and a new, and the further distribution of the former into the parts of the world, different from one another and from the new world by their physical, organic and anthropological character, to which a still younger and immature one is attached; – the mountain ranges and so forth.

## {{ 340 }} § 340.

Physical organization begins, as immediate, not with the simple, enveloped form of the germ but with a point of departure that has fallen apart into a double one: into the concrete _granitic_ principle, the mountain core, which presents the triad of moments already developed within itself, and into the _calcareous_, the difference reduced to _neutrality_. The development of the moments of the _first_ principle into formations has a graded course in which the further formations are _in_ _part_ transitions in which the granitic principle remains the basis, only as unequal and unformed in itself; _in_ _part_ a stepping apart of its moments into more determinate difference and into more abstract mineral moments, the metals and the oryctognostic objects in general, until the development loses itself in mechanical strata and in alluvia lacking immanent formation. With this, the further formation of the _other_, the neutral principle, goes alongside, _in_ _part_ as a weaker transformation, _in_ _part_ both principles then engage with one another in concrescent formations, up to external mixing.

## {{{ 20.347 }}} {{ 341 }} § 341.

This crystal of life, the organism of the earth lying dead, which has its _concept_ outside itself in the sidereal connection and its peculiar process as a presupposed past, is the _immediate_ _subject_ of the meteorological process, through which it, as the totality of life existing _in_ _itself_, is fertilized no longer merely to individual shaping (see § 287) but to _livingness_. – Land, and in particular the sea, thus as the real possibility of life, breaks out infinitely at every point into _punctual_ and _transitory_ livingness; – lichens, infusoria, immeasurable multitudes of phosphorescent points of life in the sea. But the Generatio Aequivoca, having that objective organism outside itself, consists precisely in this, in being limited to such punctual organizing, which neither develops itself within itself into determinate articulation nor reproduces itself (ex ovo).

## {{ 342 }} § 342.

This separation of the universal organism, external to itself, and of this merely punctual, transitory subjectivity sublates itself, by virtue of the identity of their concept existing in itself, into the _existence_ of this identity, into the _animated_ _organism_, the subjectivity articulating itself within itself, which excludes from itself the organism existing only _in_ _itself_, the physical universal and individual nature, and steps over against it, but at the same time has in these powers the condition of its existence, the stimulation as well as the material of its process.
