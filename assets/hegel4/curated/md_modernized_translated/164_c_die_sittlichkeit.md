## C. Ethical Life

## {{ 513 }} § 513.

In _ethical_ _life_, objective spirit is brought to completion; it is the truth of subjective and of objective spirit alike. The objective one's one-sidedness is that it has its freedom partly _immediately_ in reality, hence in the external, the _thing_, and partly in the good as an abstract universal; the subjective one's one-sidedness is that it is self-determining, likewise abstractly, against the universal within its inner singularity. With these one-sidednesses {{{ 20.495 }}} sublated, subjective _freedom_ is the rational will that is _universal_ in and for itself; in the consciousness of the single subjectivity it has at once its knowing of itself and its disposition, as well as its activation and immediate universal _actuality_ as _custom_ – self-conscious _freedom_ become _nature_.

## {{ 514 }} § 514.

The substance that knows itself _free_, and in which the absolute _ought_ is every bit as much _being_, has actuality as the spirit of a _people_. This spirit's abstract diremption is its singularization _into_ _persons_, whose self-subsistence it is the inner power and necessity of. Yet the person, as thinking intelligence, knows the substance to be its own essence and in this disposition ceases to be an accident of it; the person beholds the substance as its absolute final purpose, as an achieved _this-world_ in actuality and at once as something its own _activity_ _brings_ _forth_, though as something that rather simply _is_; so, without choosing reflection, the person fulfils its duty as its _own_ and as _being_, and in this necessity possesses itself and its actual freedom.

## {{ 515 }} § 515.

The substance being the absolute unity of freedom's singularity and universality, the _actuality_ and _activity_ of each _single_ _one_, in being and caring _for_ _itself_, are conditioned by the presupposed whole, in whose connection alone they are at hand, and are just as much a passing over into a universal product. – For the individuals, _disposition_ consists in _knowing_ the substance, and knowing that all their interests are identical with the whole; and that the other single ones in turn know themselves, and are actual, only in this identity – this is the _trust_, the genuine ethical disposition.

## {{{ 20.496 }}} {{ 516 }} § 516.

The relations of the single one within the relationships into which the substance particularizes itself make up its _ethical_ _duties_. Virtue is ethical personality, which is to say subjectivity pervaded by the substantial life. Toward external immediacy, toward a _fate_, virtue behaves to _being_ as to something not negative, and so rests calmly in itself; – toward the substantial objectivity, the whole of ethical actuality, it takes the form of trust, of purposeful work for that whole and readiness to sacrifice oneself to it; – as for the contingency of relationships with others, virtue appears first as justice and then as benevolent inclination; and in this sphere, as in its behavior toward its own existence and corporeality, individuality expresses its particular character, temperament and so forth as _virtues_.

## {{ 517 }} § 517.

Ethical substance is
+ AA. immediate or _natural_ spirit, – the _family_;
+ BB. the totality, though a relative one, that the individuals, as self-subsistent persons, form by their relative relations to one another in a formal universality, – _civil_ _society_;
+ CC. self-conscious substance, as the spirit developed into an organic actuality, – the _constitution_ _of_ _the_ _state_.
