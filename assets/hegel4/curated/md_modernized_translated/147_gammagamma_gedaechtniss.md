## γγ) Memory

## {{ 461 }} § 461.

As memory, intelligence runs through, in relation to the intuition of the word, the same activities of recollecting as it does as representation generally in relation to the first immediate intuition (§ 451 ff.) – 1) Making that connection, which the sign is, its own, it raises by this recollection the _single_ connection to a _universal_, that is, lasting connection, in which name and meaning are objectively bound together for it, and makes the intuition, which the name at first is, into a _representation_, so that the content, the meaning, and the sign, identified, are One representation, and representing, concrete in its inwardness, is the content as its existence; – the memory that _retains_ names.

## {{ 462 }} § 462.

The _name_ is thus the _matter_ as it is present and has validity in the _realm_ _of_ _representation_. The 2) _reproductive_ memory has and recognizes in the name the matter, and with the matter the name, without intuition and image. The name, as the _existence_ of the content in intelligence, is the {{{ 20.460 }}} _externality_ of itself within it, and the _recollection_ of the name, as of the intuition produced by it, is at the same time the _externalization_ in which it posits itself within itself. What binds particular names together is found in the meaning of the determinations of intelligence, whether sensing, representing or thinking, and by way of these, as sensing and so on, it runs through its own series.

::: remark
{{ 462 A }} Consider the name lion: grasping it needs no intuition of the animal, and no image of it either; as we _understand_ it, the name is the simple representation stripped of image. Names are what we _think_ in.

The _mnemonics_ of the ancients, which was heated up anew a while ago and is by now deservedly forgotten again, lies in turning names into images and so demoting memory once more to imagination. Where the _power_ of memory should be, there stands instead a lasting tableau of a series of images, fixed in the imagination, and to this the text that is to be learned by heart, the sequence of its representations, is afterwards fastened. Since the content of these representations is so unlike that of those permanent images, and since the attaching must moreover be done quickly, this attaching can take place only through insipid, silly, wholly contingent connections. Not only is spirit put on the rack, to plague itself with crazy stuff, but what has been learned by heart in such a way is, for that very reason, quickly forgotten again, for the same tableau serves in any case to learn any other series of representations by heart, and whatever hung on it earlier is in this way erased once more. What is imprinted mnemonically is not, like what is retained in memory, produced and recited _by_ _heart_, that is, properly from _within_, out of the deep shaft of the I, but is read off, so to speak, from the tableau of imagination. – Mnemonics is connected with the usual prejudices that one has about memory in relation to imagination, as if the latter were a higher, more spiritual activity than memory. Memory, on the contrary, is no longer concerned with the _image_, which stems from intelligence's immediate, unspiritual being-determined, from intuition; its concern is an existence that intelligence itself has produced, – with such an _outward_, which remains enclosed {{{ 20.461 }}} in the inward of intelligence, and is only _within_ _itself_ its outward, existing side.
:::

## {{ 463 }} § 463.

3) Insofar as the connection of the names lies in the meaning, their connection with being as names is still a synthesis, and intelligence has not in this its externality simply returned into itself. Intelligence, however, is the universal and the simple truth of its particular externalizations, and when its appropriating is carried through, that difference between meaning and name is sublated; this highest recollection of representing is its highest externalization, in which it posits itself as _being_, the universal space of names as such, that is, of senseless words. As subjectivity, the I that is this abstract being is likewise the power over the various names, the empty _bond_ that fastens series of them together in itself and retains them in a fixed order. Since the names merely _are_, and intelligence is here, within itself, this its being, it is this power as _wholly_ _abstract_ _subjectivity_, – _memory_; because the members of such series stand wholly external to one another, and memory is itself this externality, though a subjective one, it is called _mechanical_ (§ 195).

::: remark
{{ 463 A }} It is well known that a text is known properly by heart only when one attaches no sense to the words, and the recitation of what is known by heart in this way is accordingly of itself without accent. The right accent, which is brought into it, goes to the sense; the meaning, the representation, that is called up disturbs, by contrast, the mechanical connection and therefore easily confuses the recitation. The ability to retain by heart series of words in {{{ 20.462 }}} whose connection there is no understanding, or which are already senseless in themselves (a series of proper names), is so exceedingly wonderful because spirit is essentially this, to be _with_ _itself_, whereas here it is externalized _in_ _itself_, and its activity is a mechanism. But spirit is _with_ _itself_ only as the _unity_ of _subjectivity_ and _objectivity_; and here in memory, after in intuition it is at first external in such a way that it _finds_ the determinations, and in representation recollects _this_ _found_ into itself and makes it its own, it makes itself, as memory, into something external within itself, so that what is its own appears as something found. One of the moments of thinking, _objectivity_, is here posited in intelligence itself as its quality. – It is natural to grasp memory as a mechanical activity, as an activity of the senseless, in which case it is justified perhaps only by its usefulness, its indispensability for other purposes and activities of spirit. But thereby its own meaning, which it has in spirit, is overlooked.
:::

## {{ 464 }} § 464.

What is existent as _name_ needs an _other_, the _meaning_ of representing intelligence, in order to be the matter, true objectivity. As mechanical memory, intelligence is in one both that external objectivity itself and the _meaning_. It is thus _posited_ as the _existence_ of this identity, that is, it is active _for_ _itself_ as such an identity, which it is as reason _in_ _itself_. _Memory_ is in this way the transition into the activity of _thought_, which no longer has any _meaning_, that is, from whose objectivity the subjective is no longer different, just as this inwardness is itself _existent_.

::: remark
{{{ 20.463 }}} {{ 464 A }} Our language itself already accords _memory_, about which it has become a prejudice to speak slightingly, the high rank of an immediate kinship with thought. – Youth has not by chance a better memory than the old, and its memory is not practiced merely for the sake of usefulness, but it has the good memory because it does not yet behave reflectively, and it is practiced, intentionally or unintentionally, in order to level the ground of its inwardness to pure being, to pure space, in which the matter, the content that is in itself, could prevail and unfold without the opposition to a subjective inwardness. A thorough talent is wont to be combined with a good memory in youth. But such empirical statements do nothing toward cognizing what memory is in itself; it is one of the hitherto wholly unnoticed and indeed hardest points in the doctrine of spirit, in the systematizing of intelligence, to grasp the position and significance of memory and to comprehend its organic connection with thinking. Memory as such is itself only the external manner, the one-sided moment of the _existence_ of thinking, the transition is, for us or in itself, the identity of reason and this manner of existence; because of this identity reason now exists in the subject and is its activity; it is thus _thinking_.
:::
