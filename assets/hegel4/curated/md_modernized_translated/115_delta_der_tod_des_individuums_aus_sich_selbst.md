## δ) The Death of the Individual from Itself

## {{ 375 }} § 375.

The universality according to which the animal, as single, is a _finite_ existence shows itself in it as the abstract power in the outcome of the process, itself abstract, proceeding within it (§ 356). Its {{{ 20.375 }}} inadequacy to universality is its _original_ _disease_ and [the] innate _germ_ _of_ _death_. The sublating of this inadequacy is itself the carrying out of this fate. The individual sublates it by forming its singularity into the universality, but thereby, insofar as this is abstract and immediate, reaches only an _abstract_ _objectivity_, in which its activity [has] become blunted, ossified, and life has become processless _habit_, so that it thus kills itself out of itself.

## {{ 376 }} § 376.

But this attained identity with the universal is the sublating of the _formal_ _opposition_ of _immediate_ singularity and the _universality_ of individuality, and this is only the one, indeed the abstract, side, the _death_ _of_ _the_ _natural_. Subjectivity, however, is in the idea of life the concept; it is thus _in_ _itself_ the absolute _being-within-itself_ of _actuality_ and the concrete universality; through the sublating, shown, of the _immediacy_ of its reality, it has gone together with itself; the last _being-outside-itself_ of nature is sublated, and the concept, existing in it only _in_ _itself_, has thereby become _for_ _itself_. – Nature has thereby passed over into its truth, into the subjectivity of the concept, whose _objectivity_ is itself the sublated immediacy of singularity, the _concrete_ _universality_, so that the concept is posited that has the reality corresponding to it, the concept, for its _concrete_ _existence_ – _spirit_.
