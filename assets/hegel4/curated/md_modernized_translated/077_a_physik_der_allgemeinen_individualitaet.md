## {{{ 20.277 }}} A. Physics of Universal Individuality

## {{ 274 }} § 274.

The physical qualities are a. as _immediate_, outside one another in a self-subsistent manner, as the now physically determined _heavenly_ _bodies_; b. as related to the _individual_ unity of their totality, – the _physical_ _elements_; c. as the _process_ that brings forth their individual, – the _meteorological_ _process_.
