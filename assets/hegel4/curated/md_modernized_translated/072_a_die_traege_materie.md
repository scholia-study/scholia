## a. Inert Matter

## {{ 263 }} § 263.

Matter, as merely universal and immediate at first, has only a _quantitative_ difference and is particularized into various quanta, – _masses_, which in the superficial determination of a whole or one are _bodies_. Equally immediate, the body is distinguished from its ideality, and is indeed _essentially_ spatial and temporal, but as _in_ space and time, and appears as their _content_, indifferent to this form.

## {{ 264 }} § 264.

According to the determination of space, in which time is sublated, the body is _enduring_; according to the determination of time, in which the indifferent spatial subsistence is sublated, it is _transient_; in general a wholly _contingent_ one. It is indeed the unity that binds both moments in _their_ _opposition_, _motion_; but since it is indifferent to space and time (preceding §), and so to their relation (§ 261), motion, it is _external_ to it, as is its negation of motion, rest, – it is _inert_.

::: remark
{{{ 20.257 }}} {{ 264 A }} The finitude of the body, its not being in conformity with its concept, consists in this sphere in the fact that, as matter, it is posited as having only the _abstract_ immediate unity of time and space, but not their developed, restless unity in one, motion, as _immanent_ in it. – In this determination the body is taken in physical mechanics generally, so that it is an axiom of that science that the body is set in motion as in a _state_, and likewise in rest, absolutely only by an _external_ _cause_. What hovers before representation here are only the _selfless_ bodies of the earth, of which those determinations do indeed hold. But this is only immediate, and precisely thereby _abstract_ and finite corporeality. The body qua body is what is called this abstraction of the body. But the untruth of this abstract existence is sublated in the concretely existing body, and this sublating is already beginning to be posited in the selfless body. The determinations of inertia, impact, pressure, attraction, fall and so forth are transferred, inadmissibly, from common mechanics, from the sphere of finite corporeality and thereby of _finite_ _motion_, into the absolute sphere, in which corporeality and motion exist rather in their free concept.
:::
