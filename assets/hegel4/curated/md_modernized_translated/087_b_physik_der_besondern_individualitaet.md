## B. Physics of Particular Individuality

## {{ 290 }} § 290.

With the previously elemental determinatenesses now subjected to the individual unity, this unity is the immanent form which determines matter for itself _against_ its gravity. Gravity, as the seeking of the point of unity, does no damage to the _being-outside-one-another_ of matter; that is, space, and indeed according to a quantum, is the measure of the particularizations of the differences of the {{{ 20.291 }}} heavy matter, of the masses; the determinations of the physical elements are not yet a _concrete_ _being-for-itself_ _in_ _themselves_, and thus not yet opposed to the sought being-for-itself of heavy matter. Now, through the _posited_ individuality of matter, matter in its being-outside-one-another is itself a centralizing against this its being-outside-one-another and against the _seeking_ of individuality in it, different from the ideal centralizing of gravity, an immanent determining of _material_ _spatiality_ other than by gravity and in its direction. This part of physics is _individualizing_ _mechanics_, in that matter is determined by the immanent form, and indeed with respect to the spatial. At first this gives a _relation_ between the two, spatial determinateness as such and the matter belonging to it.

## {{ 291 }} § 291.

This individualizing determination of form is at first _in_ _itself_ or immediate, thus not yet posited as totality. The particular moments of the form therefore come to existence as indifferent and outside one another, and the relation of form is as a _relation_ of diverse items. It is corporeality in finite determinations, being conditioned by something external and falling apart into many particular bodies. The difference thus appears partly in the _comparison_ of different bodies with one another, partly in their _more real_, yet still <i>mechanical</i>, _relation_. The self-subsistent manifestation of the form, which needs neither comparison nor excitation, belongs only to shape.

::: remark
{{{ 20.292 }}} {{ 291 A }} As the sphere of finitude and conditionedness everywhere, so here the sphere of conditioned individuality is the object hardest to separate from the rest of the connection of the concrete and to hold fast for itself, all the more since the _finitude_ of its content stands in contrast and contradiction with the speculative unity of the concept, which at the same time can only be the determining factor.
:::

## {{ 292 }} § 292.

The determinateness that gravity undergoes is a. abstractly _simple_ determinateness and thereby, as a merely quantitative relation in it, – _specific_ _gravity_; b. a specific mode of the _relation_ of material _parts_, – _cohesion_. c\. This relation of the material parts for itself, as _existing_ _ideality_, and indeed α) as the merely _ideal_ sublating, – _sound_; β) as _real_ sublating of cohesion – _heat_.
