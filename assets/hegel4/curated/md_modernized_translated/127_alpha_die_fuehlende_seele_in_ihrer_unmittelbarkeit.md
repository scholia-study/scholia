## α) The Feeling Soul in Its Immediacy

## {{ 405 }} § 405.

αα) Feeling individuality is at first, to be sure, a monadic individual, but as _immediate_ not yet as _It_ _itself_, not a subject reflected into itself, and therefore _passive_. Thus its _self-like_ individuality is a subject distinct from it, which can also be an other individual, by whose self-likeness it, as a substance that is only a non-self-subsistent predicate, is made to tremble through and is determined in a thoroughly resistanceless way; this subject can thus be called its _genius_.

::: remark
{{{ 20.404 }}} {{ 405 A }} In immediate concrete existence this is the relation in which the child stands in the mother's womb, – a relation that is neither merely bodily nor merely spiritual, but _psychic_, – a relation of the soul. There are two individuals, and yet in a still unseparated unity of soul; the one is as yet no _self_, not yet impenetrable but offering no resistance; the other is its subject, the _singular_ self of both. – It is the mother who is the _genius_ of the child; by genius one usually understands the self-like totality of spirit as existing _for_ _itself_, which makes up the subjective substantiality of an other that is posited as an individual only externally; this other has merely a formal being-for-itself. What is substantial in the genius is the entire totality of existence, life and character, taken not as bare possibility, capacity or in-itself but as efficacy and activity, as concrete subjectivity.

If one stops at the spatial and material, according to which the child exists as embryo in its particular membranes and so forth, and its connection with the mother is mediated by the umbilical cord, the placenta and so forth, then only the external anatomical and physiological concrete existence comes into consideration, sensuous and reflective; for what is essential, the psychic relation, that sensuous and material being-outside-one-another and being-mediated has no truth. In this connection one has to have in view not merely the astonishing communications of determinations that become fixed in the child through violent emotions, injuries and so forth of the mother, but the whole psychic _judgment_ of the substance, into which the female nature, as in the vegetative the monocotyledons, can break apart in itself, and in which the child has not had dispositions _communicated_ to it, but has originally received in itself the dispositions to disease as well as the further ones of shape, disposition, character, talent, idiosyncrasies and so forth.

Of this _magical_ relation there occur elsewhere, in the sphere of conscious, reflective life, sporadic examples and traces, say {{{ 20.405 }}} between friends, in particular nervously weak women friends (– a relation that can develop into the magnetic phenomena), spouses, members of a family.

The totality of feeling has as its self a subjectivity distinct from it, which in the cited form of immediate concrete existence of this life of feeling is also an other individual over against it. But the totality of feeling is determined to raise its being-for-itself out of itself, in one and the same individuality, to subjectivity; this is the reflective, understanding, rational consciousness that then dwells within it. For this consciousness that life of feeling is the merely in-itself substantial material, whose rational, self-conscious, determining genius reflective subjectivity has become. But that core of the being of feeling contains not only the native disposition, unconscious for itself, the temperament and so forth, but also retains (see below, under habit) all further bonds and essential relations, fates, principles, – in general everything that belongs to character and in the working out of which self-conscious activity has had its most important share, – in its enveloping simplicity; the being of feeling is thus a soul perfectly determined in itself. The totality of the individual in this compressed manner is distinguished from the existing unfolding of its consciousness, its representation of the world, developed interests, inclinations and so forth. Against this mediated being-outside-one-another, that intensive form of individuality has been called the _genius_, which gives the last determination in the shine of mediations, intentions, grounds, in which developed consciousness spreads itself out. This concentrated individuality also brings itself to appearance in the manner that is called the _heart_ or _feeling_ _heart_. One says of a person that they have no feeling heart insofar as they consider and act with reflective consciousness according to his determinate purposes, be they substantial great purposes or petty and wrongful interests; a person of feeling heart means rather someone who lets their individuality of feeling, however limited, hold sway, and finds themselves with this whole individuality in its particularities and wholly filled by them. – Of such warmth of heart, however, it may be said that it is the Indulgere genio rather than the genius itself.

## {{{ 20.406 }}} {{ 406 }} § 406.

ββ) The life of feeling as _form_, _state_ of the self-conscious, cultivated, reflective human being is a disease, in which the individual relates _unmediatedly_ to the concrete content of itself, and has its reflective consciousness of itself and of the understanding connection of the world as a state distinct from that, – _magnetic_ _somnambulism_ and states related to it.

::: remark
{{ 406 A }} In this encyclopaedic exposition one cannot accomplish what would be needed to prove the given determination of the remarkable state evoked chiefly by animal magnetism, namely, that experience agrees with it. To that end the phenomena, so manifold within themselves and so widely different from one another, would first have to be ordered under their universal viewpoints. If the factual above all might seem to need confirmation, such a confirmation would again be superfluous for those for whose sake it would be needed, because they make the consideration extremely easy for themselves by declaring the accounts, however infinitely numerous and however well attested by the culture, character and so forth of the witnesses, out of hand to be delusion and fraud, and are so firm in their a priori understanding that not only does all attestation avail nothing against it, but they have already denied even what they have seen with their eyes. On this terrain, to credit even what lies before one's own eyes, and still more to comprehend it, requires above all that one not be caught up in the categories of the understanding. – The chief moments that matter may be stated here.

{{{ 20.407 }}} α) To the _concrete_ being of an individual belongs the entirety of its basic interests, of the essential and particular, empirical relations in which it stands to other human beings and to the world generally. This totality constitutes _its_ actuality in such a way that it is _immanent_ to it and was previously called its _genius_. This is not the willing and thinking free spirit; the form of feeling, sunk into which the individual is here considered, is rather the giving up of its concrete existence as spirituality that is at home with itself. The nearest consequence from the determination shown, in relation to the _content_, is that in somnambulism only the circle of the individually determined world, particular interests and limited relations enters consciousness. Scientific cognitions or philosophical concepts and universal truths require another ground, thinking developed into free consciousness out of the dullness of feeling life; it is foolish to expect revelations about ideas from the somnambulistic state.

β) A human being of sound sense and understanding knows this actuality, which constitutes the concrete fulfilment of that being's individuality, in a self-conscious, understanding manner; one knows it awake, in the form of one's connection with its determinations as an external world distinct from oneself, and one knows of this world as a manifoldness likewise _intelligibly_ _coherent_ _in_ _itself_. In one's subjective representations and plans one likewise has before one's eyes this understanding connection of one's world and the _mediation_ of one's representations and purposes with the objective concrete existences that are thoroughly mediated in themselves (compare § 398, Remark). – In this, the world that is outside has its threads in the human being in such a way that what one is actually _for_ _oneself_ consists of _them_; so that one would also die away within oneself as these externalities vanish, if one were not, more expressly in {{{ 20.408 }}} oneself, through religion, subjective reason and character, self-subsistent and independent of them. In this case one is less capable of the form of the state of which we are speaking here. – As an appearance of that identity, one may think of what the death of beloved relatives, friends and so forth can do to the survivors, in that when the one dies the other dies too, or wastes away (so too _Cato_ could no longer live after the fall of the Roman republic, his inner actuality was no further or higher than it) – homesickness and the like.

γ) But where the fulfilment of consciousness, its external world and its relation to this world, is wrapped up and the soul therefore lies sunk in sleep (magnetic sleep, catalepsy, other diseases such as those of female development, the nearness of death and so forth), the individual's _immanent_ _actuality_ stays what it was, the same substantial totality, as a _life_ _of_ _feeling_ that sees and knows within itself. Because it is the developed, matured, cultivated consciousness that is lowered into that state of feeling, it retains with its content, to be sure, the formal element of its being-for-itself, a _formal_ intuiting and knowing, which however does not proceed to the judgment of consciousness, through which its content is for it external objectivity, when it is healthy and awake. Thus the individual is the monad knowing its actuality within itself, the self-intuiting of the genius. What is characteristic in this knowing is therefore that the same content that is objective, as understanding actuality, for healthy consciousness, in order to know of which, as reflective, it requires the understanding _mediation_ in its whole real extension, can be known by it in this immanence _immediately_, _beheld_. This intuiting is a _clairvoyance_ insofar as it is knowing in the unseparated substantiality of the genius and finds itself in the _essence_ of the connection, and is therefore not bound to the series of the mediating, mutually external conditions that reflective consciousness has to run through and with regard to which it is limited according to its own external singularity. But this clairvoyance, because in its {{{ 20.409 }}} cloudedness the content is not laid out as an understanding connection, is _abandoned_ to all the proper _contingency_ of feeling, imagining and so forth, besides the fact that _foreign_ representations (see below) enter into its beholding. It is therefore not to be decided whether there is more of what the clairvoyants behold correctly or of what they deceive themselves in. – It is absurd, however, to take the beholding of this state for an elevation of spirit and for a truer state capable of cognitions _universal_ in themselves[^1].

δ) This life of feeling lacks the personality of understanding and will, and it is essentially determined as a state _of_ _passivity_, like that of the child in the mother's womb. The sick subject therefore comes to stand, according to this state, _under_ _the_ {{{ 20.410 }}} _power_ _of_ _another_, the magnetizer, so that in this psychic connection of the two, the selfless individual, not actual as person, has as its subjective consciousness the consciousness of that reflective individual, namely that this other is its present subjective soul, its genius, which can also fill it with content. That the somnambulistic individual senses within itself tastes and smells that are present in the one with whom it is [in] rapport, that it knows of that one's other present intuitions and inner representations, but as its own, shows this _substantial_ _identity_, in which the soul, which even as concrete is truly immaterial, is capable of being with another. Within this substantial identity there is only one subjectivity of consciousness; the patient's individuality has a being-for-itself, to be sure, yet an empty one, neither present to itself nor actual; this formal self therefore has its fulfilments in the sensations, representations of the other, sees, smells, tastes, reads, hears also in the other. It is further to be noted in this respect that the somnambulist thus comes to stand in a relation to two geniuses and a twofold content, to its own and to that of the magnetizer. Which sensations or visions this formal perceiving now receives, beholds and brings to knowledge from its own inner or from the representing of the one with whom it is in rapport, is indeterminate. This uncertainty can be the source of many deceptions, and grounds among other things also the necessary difference that has come to light among the views of somnambulists from different countries and under rapport with differently cultivated persons, on states of disease and their ways of healing, medicines, also scientific and spiritual categories, and so forth.

{{{ 20.411 }}} ε) As in this feeling substantiality the opposition to the externally objective is not present, so too _within_ itself the subject is in this oneness, in which the particularities of feeling have vanished, so that, since the activity of the sense organs has fallen asleep, the common feeling then determines itself into the particular functions, and seeing, hearing and so forth are done with the fingers – in particular the pit of the stomach, the stomach.

_Comprehending_ means for reflection by understanding to cognize the series of _mediations_ between an appearance and another existence with which it is connected, to see into the so-called natural course, that is, according to laws and relations of the understanding (for instance of causality, of ground and so forth). The life of feeling, even when it still retains merely formal knowing, as in the states of disease mentioned, is precisely this form of _immediacy_, in which the differences of the subjective and objective, of understanding personality against an external world, and those relations of finitude between them, are not present. Anyone who presupposes self-subsistent personalities standing against one another and against the content as an objective world, and who presupposes the absoluteness of spatial and material being-asunder generally, makes it impossible to comprehend this relationless and yet perfectly filled connection.
:::

[^1]: _Plato_ recognized the relation of _prophecy_ generally to the knowing of reflective consciousness better than many moderns, who thought they could easily find in the Platonic representations of _enthusiasm_ an authority for their belief in the loftiness of the revelations of somnambulistic beholding. _Plato_ says in the Timaeus (ed. Steph. III. p. 71. f.) that, in order that the _unreasoning_ part of the soul too might to some extent partake of truth, God created the _liver_ and gave it the _manteia_, the faculty of having visions. That God gave this prophesying to _human_ _unreason_, he adds, a sufficient proof of this is that no reflective human being partakes of a true vision, unless it be that in sleep the understanding is fettered or, through _disease_ or an enthusiasm, brought out of itself. Rightly was it said long ago, »to do and to know what is one's own and oneself belongs only to the reflective.« _Plato_ remarks very rightly both the corporeal element of such beholding and knowing and the possibility of the truth of the visions, but the subordination of them to rational consciousness.
