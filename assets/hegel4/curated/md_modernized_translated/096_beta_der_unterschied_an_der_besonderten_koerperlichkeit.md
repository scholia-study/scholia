## β) The Difference in Particularized Corporeality

## {{ 321 }} § 321.

The principle of the one member of the difference (being-for-itself) is fire (§ 283), but not yet as real chemical process (§ 316), also {{{ 20.323 }}} no longer mechanical brittleness, but, in physical particularity, combustibility in itself, which, at the same time different outwardly, is the relation to the negative in elemental universality, to air, the inconspicuously consuming (§ 282), the process of that combustibility upon the corporeal; specific individuality as a _simple_ theoretical process, the inconspicuous volatilization of the body in the air – _smell_.

::: remark
{{ 321 A }} The property of the smell of bodies, as matter existing for itself (§ 126), the _odorous_ _material_, is oil, which burns as flame. As mere property, smelling exists, for instance, in the loathsome smell of metal.
:::

## {{ 322 }} § 322.

_Neutrality_ (§ 284), the other moment of the opposition, individualizes itself into the determinate physical neutrality of saltiness and its determinations, acidity and so forth – into _taste_, a property that remains at the same time a relation to the _element_, the abstract neutrality of water, in which the body, only as neutral, is _soluble_. Conversely, the abstract neutrality contained in it can be separated off from the physical constituents of its concrete neutrality and exhibited as water of crystallization, which, however, of course does not exist as water in the neutral still undissolved. (§ 286, Remark)
