## c. Genus-Process

## {{ 367 }} § 367.

The genus is in a simple unity, existing _in_ _itself_, with the singularity of the subject, whose concrete substance it is. But the universal is judgment, in order to become out of this its diremption in itself a _unity_ _existing_ _for_ _itself_, in order to posit itself in concrete existence as _subjective_ universality. This process of its closing together with [itself] contains, as the negation of the merely inner universality of the genus, so also the negation of the merely immediate singularity in which the living thing is, as still natural; the negation of it shown in the preceding process (preceding §) is only the first, only the immediate one. In this process of the genus the merely living thing only perishes, for as such it does not step beyond naturalness. The moments of the process of the genus, however, since they have for their basis the universal that is not yet subjective, not yet one subject, fall apart and exist as several particular processes, which issue in modes of the _death_ of the living thing.
