## {{{ 20.490 }}} a. Resolve

## {{ 504 }} § 504.

In so far as the action directly concerns _existence_, the _mine_ is formal to the extent that external existence is also _self-subsistent_ against the subject. This externality can distort the subject's action and bring forth something other than what lay in it. Although every alteration _as such_ that is posited through the activity of the subject is that subject's _deed_, the subject does not therefore recognize it as its _action_, but recognizes as _its_ _own_ only that existence in the deed that lay in its _knowing_ and _willing_, that was its _resolve_, – as its _guilt_.
