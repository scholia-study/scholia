## β) Fire-Process

## {{ 331 }} § 331.

The activity, existing in the preceding process only _in_ _itself_ in the different determinateness of the metals brought into relation, posited as existing for itself, is _fire_, whereby what is combustible in itself (like sulfur) – the _third_ _kind_ of corporeality – is _set_ _afire_, and in general what is found in still indifferent, blunted difference (as in neutrality) is inspirited to _the_ _chemical_ _opposition_, of _acid_ and of the (caustic) _alkaline_, – not so much of a distinct kind of real corporeality, since these cannot exist for themselves, as only of the _positedness_ of the corporeal moments of the _third_ form.
