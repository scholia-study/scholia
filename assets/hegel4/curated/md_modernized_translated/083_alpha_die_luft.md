## α) Air

## {{ 282 }} § 282.

No longer positive identity with itself, no longer the self-manifestation that _light_ as such is, the element of undifferentiated simplicity is merely _negative_ _universality_; it has sunk to a selfless moment of _an_ _other_, and for that reason is also _heavy_. This identity, as _negative_ universality, is the unsuspected, yet creeping and consuming power over the individual and the organic; the fluid that is passive toward light, _transparent_, but volatilizes everything individual in itself, is mechanically elastic outward, and penetrates into everything, – _air_.
