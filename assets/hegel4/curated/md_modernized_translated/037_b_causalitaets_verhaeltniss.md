## b. Relation of Causality

## {{ 153 }} § 153.

Substance is _cause_ to the extent that, in opposition to its passing over into accidentality, it is reflected into itself, and in this way is the _original_ _matter_; yet it equally {{{ 20.171 }}} sublates the reflection-into-itself, its mere possibility, posits itself as its own negative, and so produces an _effect_ – an actuality that is thus merely _posited_, but that, through the process of acting, is at once necessary.

::: remark
{{ 153 A }} As the _original_ _matter_ the cause carries the determination of absolute self-subsistence and of a subsistence that holds its own against the effect; but within necessity, whose identity is that very originality, the cause has simply gone over into the effect. No content is found in the effect (in so far as determinate content can be spoken of at all) that is not in the cause; – the absolute content is nothing but that identity; equally, however, it is the form-determination too: in the effect the cause's originality is sublated, since there the cause _makes_ itself into a _positedness_. But the cause has not thereby disappeared, as though the actual were only the effect. For this _positedness_ is itself sublated just as immediately; it is rather the cause's reflection into itself, its originality: only in the effect is the cause actual and cause. The cause is therefore, in and for itself, Causa sui. – _Jacobi_, firmly held by the one-sided representation of _mediation_, took the Causa sui (Effectus sui comes to the same), this absolute truth of the cause, for mere formalism (Letters on Spinoza, 2nd ed., p. 416.). He further stated that God is to be determined not as ground but essentially as cause; had he reflected more thoroughly on the nature of the cause, it would have become plain that this does not gain him what he intended. Even in the _finite_ cause and the way it is represented, this identity is there so far as content goes: the rain, as cause, and the wetness, as effect, are one and the same water, existing. As to form, the cause (the rain) drops out in the effect (the wetness); but along with it goes the determination of the effect, which is nothing without a cause, and only the indifferent wetness remains.

In the ordinary sense of the causal relation the cause is _finite_ to the extent that its content is finite (as with finite substance) and to the extent that cause and effect are represented as two different, self-subsistent concrete existences, – which they are only when abstraction is made from the {{{ 20.172 }}} causal relation. Since in finitude one stays with the _difference_ of the form-determinations in their relation, the cause is in turn determined _also_ as something _posited_, or as _effect_; this has once more an _other_ cause; and so here too the progress from effects to causes into infinity arises. The same holds for the _descending_ progress: owing to its identity with the cause, the effect is itself determined as cause, and as such also as an _other_, which has further effects, and so on into infinity.
:::

## {{ 154 }} § 154.

The effect is _different_ from the cause; as such it is _positedness_. Yet positedness is just as much reflection-into-itself and immediacy, and the cause's acting, its positing, is at the same time _presupposing_, provided the difference between effect and cause is held fast. There is thus an _other_ _substance_ present, upon which the effect is brought about. As _immediate_, this substance is not self-relating negativity and _active_, but _passive_. As substance, however, it is equally active: it sublates the presupposed immediacy and the effect posited in it, and _reacts_, which means that it sublates the activity of the first substance; and the latter is likewise a sublating of its own immediacy, or of the effect posited in it, so it in turn sublates the other's activity and reacts. Causality has thereby gone over into the relation of _reciprocity_.

::: remark
{{ 154 A }} Even though causality is not yet posited in its true determination within reciprocity, the progress of causes and effects into infinity is truly sublated as progress, since the advance in a straight line from causes to effects, and then from effects to causes, is _turned_ _about_ and _back_ upon itself. This turning of the infinite progress into a relation that closes in on itself is, here as everywhere, the simple reflection that in that thoughtless repetition one and the same thing is at hand, namely _one_ cause and an {{{ 20.173 }}} _other_ cause and the relation between them. The development of this relation, reciprocal acting, is nonetheless itself an alternation of _distinguishing_, though not of causes but of the moments, at _each_ _of_ _which_ _for_ _itself_ _the_ _other_ moment is also posited, in accordance with the _identity_ that cause is cause in the effect and conversely, – by this inseparability.
:::
