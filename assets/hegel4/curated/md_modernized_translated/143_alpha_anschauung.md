## α) Intuition

## {{ 446 }} § 446.

Spirit, which as _soul_ is determined _naturally_, as _consciousness_ stands in relation to this determinateness as to an _external_ object, but as intelligence 1) _finds_ _itself_ so determined, is its dull weaving in _itself_, in which it is _material_ to itself and has the whole _material_ of its knowing. By reason of the _immediacy_ in which it thus at first is, it is therein absolutely only as _singular_ and _commonly-subjective_, and thus appears as feeling.

::: remark
{{ 446 A }} If feeling already occurred earlier (§ 399 ff.) as a mode of existence of the soul, the _finding_, or immediacy, was there essentially determined as natural being, as corporeality, whereas here it is determined only _abstractly_, as immediacy in general.
:::

## {{{ 20.443 }}} {{ 447 }} § 447.

The _form_ of feeling is that it is indeed a _determinate_ affection, but this _determinateness_ is simple. Therefore, even where the content of a feeling is the soundest and truest, the feeling bears the form of contingent particularity, and the content may just as well be the poorest and least true.

::: remark
{{ 447 A }} A very common presupposition holds that spirit possesses the _material_ of its representations in its feeling, though it is usually meant in a sense opposite to the one the proposition has here. Against the simplicity of feeling, it is rather the _judgment_ generally, the distinguishing of consciousness into a subject and an object, that is usually presupposed as the original; then the determinateness of sensation is derived from a _self-subsistent_ external or internal _object_. Here, in the truth of spirit, this standpoint of consciousness, opposed to its idealism, has perished, and the material of feeling is rather already posited as immanent in spirit. With regard to the content, a common prejudice has it _that_ _feeling_ _holds_ _more_ _than_ _thinking_ _does_, and this is asserted especially of moral and religious feelings. The material that spirit is to itself as feeling has here too turned out to be the being-determined-in-and-for-itself of reason; all rational content, and more closely all spiritual content, therefore enters into feeling. But the form of self-like singularity that spirit has in feeling is the lowest and worst, in which it is not free, as infinite universality, – its import and content are rather something contingent, subjective, particular. _Cultivated_, {{{ 20.444 }}} true sensation is the sensation of a cultivated spirit, which has acquired the consciousness of determinate differences, essential relations, true determinations and so forth, and in which it is this rectified material that enters its feeling, that is, receives this form. Feeling is the immediate, as it were most present, form in which the subject relates itself to a given content; it reacts to it first with its particular self-feeling, and that can be sturdier and more comprehensive than a one-sided standpoint of the understanding, yet is just as well limited and bad; in any case it is the form of the particular and the subjective. When a human being appeals, concerning something, not to the nature and the concept of the matter or at least to grounds, to the universality of the understanding, but to his _feeling_, there is nothing else to do than to leave him standing, because he thereby refuses the community of rationality and shuts himself up in his isolated subjectivity, in _particularity_.
:::

## {{ 448 }} § 448.

2) In the diremption of this immediate finding, the one moment is the abstract _identical_ direction of spirit in feeling, as in all its other further determinations, _attention_, without which nothing is for it; – active _recollection_, the moment of _its_ _own_, but as the still _formal_ self-determination of intelligence. The other moment is that, over against this its inwardness, it posits the determinateness of feeling as an _existing_ one, but as a _negative_, as the abstract otherness of itself. In this way intelligence determines the content of sensation as _existing_ _outside_ _itself_ and casts it out into _space_ _and_ _time_, the _forms_ in which it intuits. According to consciousness, the material is only the object {{{ 20.445 }}} of it, a relative other; from spirit, however, it acquires the rational determination of being the _other_ _of_ _itself_ (compare §§ 247, 254).

## {{ 449 }} § 449.

3) Intelligence, as this concrete unity of the two moments, namely as immediately recollected into itself in this externally existing material and, in its recollection into itself, sunk into being-outside-itself, is _intuition_.

## {{ 450 }} § 450.

Intelligence just as essentially directs its attention to and against this own being-outside-itself, and is the awakening to itself in this its immediacy, its _recollection_ _into_ _itself_ in it; intuition is thus this concrete of the material and of itself, its _own_, with the result that it has no further need of this immediacy and of finding the content. –
