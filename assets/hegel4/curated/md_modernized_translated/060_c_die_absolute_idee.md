## c. The Absolute Idea

## {{ 236 }} § 236.

When subjective and objective idea are unified, the result is the concept of the idea, for which the idea as such is the thing it faces and for which the object is that idea; – an object in which all determinations have merged. Such unity is accordingly _absolute_ _and_ _total_ _truth_, the idea thinking itself, and thinking itself here _qua_ thinking, as _logical_ idea.

## {{ 237 }} § 237.

The _absolute_ _idea_, being for itself, is the _pure_ _form_ of the concept, and it intuits _its_ _content_ as itself {{{ 20.229 }}}, since it contains no passing over, no presupposing, and in general no determinateness that would not be fluid and transparent. It is its own _content_ in that it ideally distinguishes itself from itself, one of the distinguished being identity with itself, and yet the form's totality is contained in this identity as the system of the determinations of content. The system _of_ _the_ _logical_ is this content. _As_ _form_, nothing is left to the idea here but the _method_ of this content, – the determinate knowledge of how its moments stand.

## {{ 238 }} § 238.

Of the speculative method's moments, a. is the _beginning_, which is _being_, the _immediate_; taken for itself it is so for the plain reason that it is the _beginning_. Seen from the speculative idea, however, it is that idea's _self-determining_, which, as the concept's absolute negativity or movement, _judges_ and posits itself as its own negative. _Being_, which looks like abstract affirmation when taken as the beginning, is thus rather the _negation_, _positedness_, mediatedness in general, and <i>pre</i>supposedness. Yet as negation of the _concept_, which in its otherness is simply identical with itself and certain of itself, being is the concept not yet posited as concept, or the concept _in_ _itself_. – For that reason this being, as the concept still indeterminate, that is, determined only in itself or immediately, is equally the _universal_.

::: remark
{{ 238 A }} If the _beginning_ is understood as immediate being, it is taken from intuition and perception, – the beginning of finite cognition's _analytic_ <i>me</i>thod; if it is understood as universality, it is the beginning of that cognition's synthetic method. Since the logical, however, is immediately a universal as much as an existent, and as much something that the concept presupposes for itself as immediately the concept itself, its beginning is a synthetic beginning as much as an analytic one.
:::

## {{ 239 }} § 239.

b\. The idea's _judgment_, once posited, is the _progression_. As the concept in itself, the immediate universal is a dialectic that in itself reduces its immediacy and universality to a moment. In this way the beginning's _negative_, or the first in its _determinateness_, is posited; it is _for_ _one_, the _relation_ of distinct items, – a _moment_ _of_ _reflection_.

::: remark
{{{ 20.230 }}} {{ 239 A }} This progression is _analytic_ as much as it is _synthetic_: analytic in that the immanent dialectic posits only what the immediate concept contains; – synthetic, since that difference was not yet posited in the concept.
:::

## {{ 240 }} § 240.

The progression's abstract form is, in being, an _other_ and a _passing over_ into something else; in essence, _shining_ in _what_ _is_ _opposed_; in the _concept_, the singular's distinctness from _universality_, which as such _continues_ into what differs from it and _is_ with it _as_ _identity_.

## {{ 241 }} § 241.

In the second sphere the concept, existent at first _in_ _itself_, has attained _shining_ and thereby is already the _idea_ _in_ _itself_. – The development of this sphere turns into a return to the first, just as that of the first is a transition to the second; only this doubled movement gives difference its due, each of the two distinguished items, viewed in itself, perfecting itself into the totality and in doing so proving itself one with the other. The unity is kept from becoming one-sided only by the _two_ sides' sublating of their one-sidedness _in_ _themselves_.

## {{ 242 }} § 242.

The second sphere develops the relation of the distinguished to what it at first is, to _contradiction_ in itself, – in the _infinite_ _progress_, – and this dissolves c. into the _end_, with the different posited as what it is in the concept. The different is the negative of the first, and, in being identical with the first, the negativity of itself; it is therefore the unity in which these two firsts exist as ideal and as moments, as sublated, that is, at the same time preserved. Thus, by means of its difference and the sublating of this, the concept closes together with itself out of its _being-in-itself_, and it is the _realized_ concept, that is, the concept whose _being-for-itself_ holds the _positedness_ of its determinations, – the _idea_, for which, being at the same time absolute {{{ 20.231 }}} first (in the method), this end is merely the _vanishing_ of the _shine_ as though the beginning were an immediate and the idea a result; – the cognition that the idea is the One totality.

## {{ 243 }} § 243.

In this way the method is no external form but the content's soul and concept, from which it differs only because the moments of the _concept_, _in_ _themselves_ too, come in their _determinateness_ to appear as the concept's totality. Because this determinateness, or the content, takes itself back with the form into the idea, the idea shows itself as a _systematic_ totality, only _One_ idea, whose particular moments are both the same idea _in_ _itself_ and, through the concept's dialectic, bring forth the idea's simple _being-for-itself_. – Science therefore ends with its grasp of its own concept, the pure idea that has the idea as its object.

## {{ 244 }} § 244.

When the idea that exists _for_ _itself_ is _regarded_ according to this its _unity_ with itself, it is _intuiting_; and the intuiting idea is _nature_. External reflection, however, posits the idea as intuiting under the one-sided determination of immediacy or negation. The idea's absolute _freedom_ lies in this, that it neither merely _passes_ _over_ into _life_ nor, as finite cognition, lets life _shine_ within it, but in the absolute truth of itself _resolves_ freely to _release_ _itself_ _from_ _itself_ as _nature_: the moment of its particularity or of first determining and otherness, the _immediate_ _idea_, as its reflected shine.
