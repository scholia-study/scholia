## c. Reason

## {{ 438 }} § 438.

The truth that exists in and for itself, which reason is, is the simple _identity_ of the concept's _subjectivity_ with its _objectivity_ and universality. Reason's universality therefore means, just as much, the _object_ that in consciousness as such was merely given but is now itself _universal_, penetrating and embracing the I, and the pure {{{ 20.434 }}} _I_, the pure form that reaches across the object and embraces it within itself.

## {{ 439 }} § 439.

Self-consciousness that is thus certain that its determinations are as objective, as determinations of the essence of things, as they are its own thoughts, is reason; as this identity reason is not merely the absolute _substance_ but _truth_ as knowing. For the _determinateness_ proper to it here, its immanent form, is the pure concept that exists for itself, I, which is the certainty of itself as infinite universality. – It is _spirit_ that is this knowing truth.
