## γ) The Body of Individuality

## {{ 280 }} § 280.

The _earth_, or the _planet_ in general, is opposition that has gone back into itself; it is the body of _individual_ totality, where rigidity has been _opened_ _up_ so as to separate into real differences, while the _self-like_ _point_ _of_ _unity_ holds this dissolution together.

::: remark
{{ 280 A }} As the motion of the planet, as rotation on its axis and at the same time motion around a central body, is the most concrete and the expression of livingness, so the light-nature of the central body is the _abstract_ identity, whose truth, as that of thinking in the concrete idea, lies in individuality.

As regards the series of the planets, astronomy has discovered no actual law for their nearest determinateness, their _distances_. Likewise the attempts of the philosophy of nature to show the rationality of the series in the physical constitution and in analogies with a series of metals can hardly be regarded even as beginnings of finding the viewpoints that matter. – What is unreasonable, however, is to take the thought of contingency as the basis here, and, for instance, to see in _Kepler's_ thought, which conceives the arrangement of the solar system after the laws of musical harmony, only an _aberration_ of a dreamy _imagination_ (with _Laplace_), and not to esteem highly the deep faith that _reason_ _is_ _in_ _this_ _system_; – a faith that was the sole ground of the brilliant discoveries of this great man. – The wholly clumsy application, erroneous even according to the facts, of the numerical ratios of tones, which _Newton_ made to the _colors_, has by contrast retained fame and credence.
:::
