## c. Reciprocity

## {{ 155 }} § 155.

The determinations that reciprocity holds apart as distinct are _in_ _themselves_ the same: one side is cause, original, active, passive and so forth, just as the other is. Equally, presupposing an other and acting upon it, immediate originality and positedness through the alternation, are one and the same. The cause assumed as _first_ is, by reason of its immediacy, _passive_, _positedness_ and _effect_. The difference between the causes named as _two_ is therefore empty, and what is there _in_ _itself_ is only One cause, one that sublates itself in its effect as substance and yet first gains self-subsistence in this very acting.

## {{ 156 }} § 156.

Yet this unity also exists _for_ _itself_, because this whole alternation is the cause's own _positing_, and this positing of its own is all its being. That the differences are null is not merely in itself or something for our reflection (the preceding section); reciprocity is itself the sublating again of each posited determination and its inversion into the opposite, and so the positing of that nullity of the moments which is in itself. An {{{ 20.174 }}} effect is posited in originality, that is, originality is sublated; a cause's action turns into reaction and so forth.

## {{ 157 }} § 157.

This pure alternation with itself is, consequently, _unveiled_ or _posited_ _necessity_. The bond uniting necessity as such is identity still _inner_ and hidden, since it is the identity of items that count as _actuals_, though their self-subsistence is supposed to be precisely the necessity. Substance's passage through causality and reciprocity is accordingly only the _positing_ that _self-subsistence_ _is_ infinite _negative_ _relation_ _to_ _itself_, – _negative_ in general, in that distinguishing and mediating turn into an originality of _self-subsistent_ _actuals_ toward one another, – infinite _relation_ _to_ _itself_, in that their self-subsistence is just their identity.

## {{ 158 }} § 158.

Hence _freedom_ is the _truth_ of _necessity_, and the _concept_ the _truth_ of _substance_: the self-subsistence that is repelling itself from itself into distinct self-subsistents, which as this repelling is identical with itself, and whose reciprocal movement, keeping _by_ _itself_, is only with itself.

## {{ 159 }} § 159.

The _concept_ is therefore the _truth_ _of_ _being_ _and_ _of_ _essence_: the shining of reflection within itself is at once self-subsistent immediacy, and this _being_ of different actuality is immediately only a shining _within_ _itself_.

::: remark
{{ 159 A }} Having proved itself the truth of being and essence, which have both _gone_ _back_ into it as their _ground_, the concept has, _conversely_, _developed_ out of _being_ as its _ground_. The one side of this advance may be seen as a _deepening_ of being into {{{ 20.175 }}} itself, whose inner is unveiled by the advance; the other as the issuing of the _more_ _perfect_ _from_ _the_ _less_ _perfect_. Philosophy has been reproached on this account, because the development was considered only from the latter side. The more determinate import carried here by the superficial thoughts of less and more perfect is the difference between _being_ as _immediate_ unity with itself and the _concept_ as _free_ _mediation_ with itself. When _being_ turned out to be a _moment_ of the concept, the concept proved itself in so doing the truth of being; as its own reflection-into-itself and as the sublating of mediation, it is the _presupposing_ of the _immediate_, – a presupposing identical with the return-into-itself, and this identity is what constitutes freedom and the concept. So if the _moment_ is termed imperfect, then the concept, the perfect, does indeed develop itself out of the imperfect, for it is essentially the sublating of its presupposition. But it alone also makes the presupposition, in positing itself, as has been shown in causality in general and more closely in reciprocity.

Relative to being and essence, the concept is accordingly determined as the _essence_ _that_ _has_ _returned_ _to_ _being_ as _simple_ _immediacy_; its shining thereby possesses actuality, and its actuality is at the same time _free_ _shining_ _within_ _itself_. Being is thus what the concept possesses as its simple relation to itself, as the immediacy of the unity it is _within_ itself; so impoverished a determination is being that it is the very least one can point out in the concept.

What is hardest is to pass from necessity to freedom, or from the actual into the concept, because self-subsistent actuality is to be thought as having its substantiality only in passing over into, and being identical with, the _other_ self-subsistent actuality; and the concept is hardest for the same reason, being itself this very identity. But actual substance as such, the cause, which in its {{{ 20.176 }}} being-for-itself wants to let nothing in, is already subject to _necessity_ or fate, which makes it go over into positedness, and this subjection is the harder thing. The _thinking_ of necessity, by contrast, is rather the dissolving of that hardness; it is the coming together of oneself with _oneself_ in the other, – _liberation_, which is no flight of abstraction but consists in possessing, in the other actual to which the actual is tied by the power of necessity, not an other but one's own being and positing. In its _existing_ _for_ _itself_ this liberation is called _I_; developed to its totality it is _free_ _spirit_; as sensation it is _love_; as enjoyment, _blessedness_. – Spinoza's great intuition of substance _is_ the _liberation_ from finite being-for-itself only _in_ _itself_; the concept itself, however, is _for_ _itself_ the power of necessity and _actual_ freedom.
:::
