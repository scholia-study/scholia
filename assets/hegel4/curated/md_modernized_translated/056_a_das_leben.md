## {{{ 20.219 }}} a. Life

## {{ 216 }} § 216.

Life is the idea in its _immediate_ form. Realized as soul in a _body_, the concept relates to the body's externality as the immediate _universality_ that is self-related, and equally as its _particularization_, so that the body expresses no differences beyond the concept's determinations in it, and finally as _singularity_, infinite negativity, – the dialectic of the body's objectivity that is mutually external, which is brought back from the shine of self-subsistent standing into subjectivity, so that all members serve one another as momentary _means_ and also as momentary purposes, and life, being the _initial_ particularization, has as its _result_ the _negative_ unity existing _for_ _itself_, and in its corporeality, as dialectical, closes together only with itself. – Life is accordingly essentially a _living_ _being_, and in its immediacy _this_ _single_ living being. In this sphere finitude takes the form that, owing to the idea's immediacy, soul and body are _separable_, which is what makes the living being mortal. Only when it is dead, however, are those two sides of the idea different _component_ _parts_.

## {{ 217 }} § 217.

The syllogism that the living being is has moments that are themselves systems and syllogisms (§§ 198, 201, 207), though active ones, processes, which in the living being's subjective unity are only _One_ process. In this way the living being is the process of its joining with itself, and this process passes through _three_ _processes_.

## {{{ 20.220 }}} {{ 218 }} § 218.

1) The first process takes place _within_ the living being, which dirempts itself in itself and turns its corporeality, its _inorganic_ nature, into its object. As what is relatively external, this nature itself enters into the difference and opposition of its moments, and these yield themselves up to one another, each assimilating another and maintaining itself by producing itself. The members' activity, however, is only the subject's One activity, to which their products revert, with the result that only the subject is produced in it, that is, it merely reproduces itself.

## {{ 219 }} § 219.

2) Being free, however, the concept's _judgment_ goes on to let the _objective_ go forth from itself as a self-subsistent totality; and the living being's negative relation to itself, as _immediate_ singularity, constitutes the _presupposition_ of an inorganic nature that confronts it. This negative of the living being is equally a moment of its own concept, and so within the living being, which is also the concrete universal, it is present as a _lack_. The dialectic by which the object, as null _in_ _itself_, sublates itself is the activity of the living being that has certainty of itself, and in _this_ _process_ _against_ _an_ _inorganic_ _nature_ the living being _preserves_ _itself_, _develops_ and _objectifies_ itself.

## {{ 220 }} § 220.

3) In its first process the living individual conducts itself as subject and concept within itself; through its second it assimilates its external objectivity, and so _posits_ the real determinateness _in_ _itself_; being this, it is now _genus_ _in_ _itself_, substantial universality. Its particularization is the subject's relation to _another_ _subject_ of its genus, and the judgment is the genus's relation to these individuals, so determined against one another; – _sexual_ _difference_.

## {{{ 20.221 }}} {{ 221 }} § 221.

The _genus_ is brought to _being-for-itself_ by its process. Since life is still the immediate idea, the product of that process divides into two sides: on the _one_ the living individual in general, at first presupposed as immediate, now emerges as mediated and _engendered_; on _the_ _other_ the living _singularity_, because of its first _immediacy_ related _negatively_ to universality, _goes_ _under_ in universality as the power.

## {{ 222 }} § 222.

With this the idea of life has become free not just of _some_ (particular) _immediate_ this, but of this first immediacy altogether; thereby it arrives _at_ _itself_, at its _truth_; thereby it enters _into_ _existence_ as the _free_ _genus_ _for_ _itself_. The _coming-forth_ _of_ _spirit_ is the death of merely immediate singular liveliness.
