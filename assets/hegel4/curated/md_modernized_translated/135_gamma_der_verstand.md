## γ) The Understanding

## {{ 422 }} § 422.

Perceiving's next _truth_ is this: the object is rather _appearance_, and the reflection-into-itself of it is an _inner_, universal element that exists for itself over against it. The consciousness of this object is the {{{ 20.427 }}} _understanding_. – That _inner_ is, on the one hand, the _sublated_ _manifoldness_ of the sensuous, and in this way the abstract identity, but on the other hand it therefore contains the manifoldness too, but as _inner_ _simple_ _difference_, which remains identical with itself in the alternation of appearance. This simple difference is the realm _of_ _laws_ of appearance, its tranquil universal image.

## {{ 423 }} § 423.

The law, which at first has the relation of universal, abiding determinations, has, insofar as its difference is the inner one, its necessity in itself; the one of the determinations, as not externally distinguished from the other, lies immediately itself in the other. The inner difference is, however, in this way what it is in truth, the difference in itself, or _the_ _difference_ _that_ _is_ _none_. – In this determination of form generally, consciousness, which as such contains the _self-subsistence_ of subject and object against each other, has _in_ _itself_ vanished; I, as judging, has an object that is not distinguished from it, – _itself_; – _self-consciousness_.
