## b. Willing

## {{ 233 }} § 233.

In the subjective idea, which is determined in and for itself and has simple _content_ equal to itself, we have the _good_. The drive of this idea to realize itself stands to the idea of the _true_ in an inverted relation, aiming instead at determining the found world in accordance with its _purpose_. – On one side this _willing_ possesses certainty of the _nullity_ of the presupposed object, – yet on the other it is finite, and for that reason it also presupposes the _self-subsistence_ of the object, together with the good's purpose as nothing more than a _subjective_ idea.

## {{ 234 }} § 234.

For this reason the finitude of this activity lies in a _contradiction_: the objective world's determinations contradict themselves, and in them the _purpose_ _of_ _the_ _good_ is executed and yet is not executed, being posited as inessential just as much as essential, as actual and simultaneously as merely possible. The contradiction presents itself as the _infinite_ _progress_ in realizing the good, which is held fast there only as an _ought_. In _formal_ terms, though, the contradiction vanishes because activity sublates the {{{ 20.228 }}} purpose's subjectivity and with it objectivity, the opposition by which both are finite, and what it removes is not just the one-sidedness of _this_ subjectivity but subjectivity as such; an _other_ subjectivity of the kind, that is, a _renewed_ production of the opposition, does not differ from the earlier one it was supposed to follow. This return into itself is at once the content's _recollection_ into itself, the content that is the _good_ and the identity of both sides existing in itself, – the recollection of what theoretical comportment presupposes (§ 224), that the object is the substantial and true in itself.

## {{ 235 }} § 235.

With the good attained in and for itself, the _truth_ of the good is thereby _posited_, as what unifies the theoretical idea with the practical, – the objective world is in and for itself the idea, just as the idea eternally posits itself as _purpose_ and generates its actuality through activity. – This life, returned to itself out of the difference and finitude of cognition and, by the concept's activity, become identical with the concept, is the _speculative_ _or_ _absolute_ _idea_.
