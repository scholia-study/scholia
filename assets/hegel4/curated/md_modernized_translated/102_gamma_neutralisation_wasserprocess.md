## {{{ 20.337 }}} γ) Neutralization, Water-Process

## {{ 332 }} § 332.

What is thus different is absolutely opposed to its other, and this is its quality, so that it essentially is only in its relation to this other; its corporeality in self-subsistent, separated existence is therefore only a violent state, and in its one-sidedness it is in itself the process (if only with the air, on which acid and caustic potash are blunted, that is, reduced to formal neutrality) of positing itself as identical with its negative. The product is the concrete _neutral_, _salt_, – the _fourth_, and indeed as a real body.
