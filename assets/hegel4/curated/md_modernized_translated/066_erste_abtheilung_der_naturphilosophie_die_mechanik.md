## {{{ 20.243 }}} First Division of the Philosophy of Nature. Mechanics

## {{ 253 }} § 253.

Mechanics considers:

+ A. The wholly abstract being-outside-one-another, – _space_ _and_ _time_.
+ B. The _isolated_ being-outside-one-another and its relation within that abstraction, – _matter_ and _motion_, – _finite_ mechanics.
+ C. _Matter_ in the freedom of its concept that is in itself, of free _motion_, – _absolute_ mechanics.
