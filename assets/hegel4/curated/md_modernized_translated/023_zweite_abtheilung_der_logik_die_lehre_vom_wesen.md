## {{{ 20.143 }}} Second Division of the Logic. The Doctrine of Essence

## {{ 112 }} § 112.

Essence is the concept in its _posited_ form; its determinations are merely _relative_ here, not yet reflected absolutely into themselves, which is why the concept is not yet _for-itself_. Through its own negativity being mediates itself with itself, and this is essence: a self-relation that exists only as relation to an other, which however is immediately not something that is but something _posited_ and _mediated_. – Being has not disappeared; rather, first, essence, as simple self-relation, is being, while second, being has been _lowered_, in view of its one-sided determination of being _immediate_, to something merely negative, to a _shine_. – Essence is thus being as _shining_ within itself.

::: remark
{{ 112 A }} The absolute is _essence_. – To define it so amounts to defining it as _being_, since being too is simple self-relation; yet the former definition is the higher one, since essence is being that has _turned_ _inward_, that is, its simple self-relation is this very relation posited as negation of the negative, as its mediation with itself within itself. – When the absolute is defined as _essence_, however, negativity is often understood merely as _an_ _abstraction_ from every determinate predicate. This negative activity, abstracting, then lies outside essence, and essence itself becomes a mere result _without_ _this_ _its_ _premise_, the caput mortuum of abstraction. Yet this negativity is no external affair of being but its own dialectic, and so its truth, essence, is being _turned_ _inward_, being that is _within_ _itself_; the difference between it and {{{ 20.144 }}} immediate being is made up by that _reflection_, its shining within itself, and this reflection is the proper determination of essence itself.
:::

## {{ 113 }} § 113.

In essence the self-relation has the form of _identity_, of _reflection-into-itself_, which here steps in for the _immediacy_ of being; the two are the same abstractions of self-relation.

::: remark
{{ 113 A }} Sensibility's thoughtlessness in taking everything restricted and finite for something _that_ _is_ turns into the understanding's stubbornness in grasping it as _one_ _identical-with-itself_, _not_ _contradicting_ _itself_ _in_ _itself_.
:::

## {{ 114 }} § 114.

This identity appears, as issuing from being, at first as encumbered only with the determinations of being and then as referred to something _external_. When it is taken in this way as cut off from essence, it is named the _inessential_. Essence, however, is being-within-itself and counts as _essential_ solely because it carries within itself the negative of itself, the relation-to-other, the mediation. So it has the inessential in itself as its own shine. But since distinguishing is contained in shining, or mediating, and what is distinguished takes on the form of identity itself, in the difference from the identity from which it comes and in which [it] is not, or lies as shine, it stands in the manner of self-referring immediacy, of being; and the sphere of essence thereby turns into {{{ 20.145 }}} a still imperfect linkage of _immediacy_ and _mediation_. Each thing here is posited as self-referring while already surpassed, – it is a _being_ _of_ _reflection_: an other shines in it, and it itself shines in an other. – It is therefore likewise the sphere of the _posited_ _contradiction_, which in the sphere of being exists only _in_ _itself_.

::: remark
{{ 114 A }} Because the one concept is what is substantial in everything, the development of essence brings back the determinations of the development of being, but in _reflected_ form. The _positive_ and the _negative_ now appear where _being_ and _nothing_ stood, the first corresponding initially to being free of opposition as _identity_, the second developed (shining in itself) as _difference_; – and likewise _becoming_ reappears as _ground_, which is at once itself the ground of _existence_, and existence, reflected upon the ground, is _concrete_ _existence_, and so on. – This part of logic, the hardest, chiefly holds the categories of metaphysics and of the sciences in general; – they are products of the reflecting understanding, which at once takes the differences as _self-subsistent_ and _also_ posits their relativity, joining the two merely beside or after one another by an _also_, and neither brings these thoughts together nor unites them into the concept.
:::
