## {{{ 20.324 }}} γ) The Totality in Particular Individuality; Electricity.

## {{ 323 }} § 323.

In accordance with their determinate particularity, bodies stand in relation to the _elements_, but as shaped wholes they also enter into relation to one another, as _physical_ individualities. In accordance with their particularity, which does not yet enter the chemical process, they are _self-subsistent_ items and _maintain_ themselves indifferently toward one another, wholly in the mechanical relation. As in this relation they make their self known in ideal motion, as a vibration _within_ _themselves_, as sound, so now, in _physical_ tension of particularity against one another, they show their _real_ selfhood, which however is at the same time still of abstract reality, as their _light_, but a light _different_ in itself – _electrical_ relation.

## {{ 324 }} § 324.

Mechanical contact posits the physical difference of the one body in the other; this difference is, because they at the same time remain mechanically self-subsistent toward one another, an opposed _tension_. Into this, therefore, the physical nature of the body does not enter in its concrete determinateness, but it is only as the reality of the _abstract_ self as _light_, and indeed an opposed one, that individuality manifests itself and sends itself into the process {{{ 20.325 }}}. – The sublation of the diremption, the other moment of this superficial process, has for its product an indifferent light, which, being incorporeal, vanishes immediately, and, apart from this abstract physical appearance, has chiefly only the mechanical effect of concussion.

::: remark
{{ 324 A }} What constitutes the difficulty in the _concept_ of electricity is, on the one hand, the basic determination of the physical as well as mechanical inertia of the body-individual in this process. The electrical tension is therefore ascribed to an other, to a matter, to which the light belongs that emerges, abstractly distinct for itself from the concrete reality of the body, which remains in its self-subsistence. On the other hand the difficulty is the universal one of the concept in general, namely to grasp light in its connection as a _moment_ of the totality, and indeed here no longer freely as sunlight, but as a moment of the particular body, in that it is _in_ _itself_ the pure self of that body and, generated from its immanence, steps into existence. As the first light, that of the sun (§ 275), arises only from the concept as such, so here (as in § 306) there takes place a _coming-to-be_ of light, but of a different light, from an existence, from the concept existing as particular body.

As is well known, the earlier distinction, bound to a determinate sensible existence, between _glass-_ and _resin-electricity_ has been idealized by completed empirical observation into the _distinction_ _of_ _thought_ between _positive_ and _negative_ electricity; – a remarkable example of how empirical observation, which at first wants to grasp and hold fast the universal in _sensible_ form, itself sublates its sensible element. – If in recent times much has been said of the _polarization_ _of_ _light_, this expression would have been reserved with greater right for electricity than for the _Malusian_ phenomena, where it is transparent media, reflecting surfaces and the various positions of these toward one another and many other circumstances that produce an _external_ difference in the _shining_ of light, but not one in it itself. – The conditions under which positive and {{{ 20.326 }}} negative electricity emerge, the smoother or duller surface, for instance, a breath and so on, prove the _superficiality_ of the electrical process and how little the concrete physical nature of the body enters into it. Likewise the faint coloring of the two electrical lights, smell, taste, indicate only the _beginning_ of a corporeality in the abstract self of light, in which the process holds, which, though physical, is yet not a concrete process. Negativity, which is the sublating of the opposed tension, is chiefly a _shock_; – the self, positing itself as identical out of its self-division, remains, even as this totalizing, within the external sphere of _mechanism_. Light as discharge spark has scarcely a beginning of materializing itself into _heat_, and the _ignition_ that can arise from the so-called discharge is (Berthollet Statique Chimique. I. Partie, Sect. III. not. XI.) more a direct _effect_ of the concussion than the consequence of a realization of light into fire. – Insofar as the two electricities are kept separate from one another on different bodies, then, as with magnetism (§ 314), the determination of the concept enters, that the activity consists in positing the opposed as identical, and the identical as opposed. It is on the one hand mechanizing activity as _spatial_ attraction and repulsion, which side, insofar as it can be isolated for appearance, grounds the connection with the appearance of magnetism as such; on the other hand physical, in the interesting phenomena of electrical communication, as such or conduction, and as distribution.
:::

## {{ 325 }} § 325.

The _particularization_ of the individual body does not, however, stop at the inert diversity and self-activity of the diverse, out of which the {{{ 20.327 }}} abstract pure selfhood, the principle of light, emerges into process, into tension of opposites, and the sublating of these in their indifference. Since the particular properties are only the reality of this simple concept, the body of its soul, of _light_, and the complex of properties, the particular body, is not truly self-subsistent, the _whole_ corporeality enters into the tension and into the process, and this process is at once the individual body's coming-to-be. Shape, which at first emerged only from the concept and was thus posited only _in_ _itself_, now emerges also from the existing process and presents itself as what is posited out of existence – the _chemical_ _process_.
