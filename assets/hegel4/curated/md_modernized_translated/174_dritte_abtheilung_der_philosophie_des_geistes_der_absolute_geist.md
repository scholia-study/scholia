## {{{ 20.542 }}} Third Division of the Philosophy of Spirit. Absolute Spirit

## {{ 553 }} § 553.

The _concept_ of spirit has its _reality_ in spirit. That this reality, in identity with the concept, be the _knowing_ of the absolute idea contains the necessary side that intelligence, free _in_ _itself_, is liberated in its actuality to its concept, so as to be the _shape_ worthy of it. Subjective and objective spirit are to be viewed as the path on which this side of _reality_, or of concrete existence, forms itself.

## {{ 554 }} § 554.

Absolute spirit is an _identity_ that is eternally in itself and just as much returning into itself and returned; the one and universal _substance_ as spiritual, the judgment _in_ _itself_ and _into_ _a_ _knowing_ _for_ _which_ it is as such. _Religion_, as this highest sphere may be designated in general, is to be regarded as proceeding from the subject and as located in it, just as much as objectively proceeding from absolute spirit, which as spirit is in its community.

::: remark
{{ 554 A }} That faith is not opposed to knowing here or anywhere, but that faith is rather a knowing, and the former only a particular form of the latter, was remarked above in § 63 Remark. – That nowadays so little is known of God and so little dwelt on his objective essence, while all the more is said of religion, that is, of the indwelling of God on the subjective side, and that this, not truth as such, is demanded, contains at least this correct determination, that God must be grasped as spirit in his community.
:::

## {{{ 20.543 }}} {{ 555 }} § 555.

The subjective consciousness of absolute spirit is essentially a process within itself, whose immediate and substantial unity is _faith_, in the witness of the spirit, as the _certainty_ of objective truth. Faith, at once this immediate unity and containing it as the relation of those different determinations, has passed over in _devotion_, the implicit or more explicated _cult_, into the process of sublating the opposition into spiritual liberation, of _verifying_ that first certainty through this mediation, and of gaining its concrete determination, namely reconciliation, the actuality of spirit.
