## β) Self-Feeling

## {{ 407 }} § 407.

αα) As individuality, the feeling totality is essentially this, to distinguish itself within itself and to _awaken_ _to_ _the_ _judgment_ _within_ _itself_, according to which it has _particular_ feelings and is, as _subject_, in relation to these its determinations. The subject as such posits them as _its_ feelings {{{ 20.412 }}} _within_ _itself_. It is immersed in this _particularity_ of the sensations, and yet at the same moment the ideality of the particular lets it close together with itself in that very place, as a subjective one. In this way it is _self-feeling_ – and is this at the same time only in the _particular_ _feeling_.

## {{ 408 }} § 408.

ββ) On account of the _immediacy_ in which self-feeling is still determined, that is, on account of the moment of corporeality, which in it is still unseparated from spirituality, and since feeling itself is also a particular one, hence a particular embodiment, the subject, although formed to understanding consciousness, is still capable of _disease_, in that it remains persisting in a _particularity_ of its self-feeling, which it is unable to work up into ideality and overcome. The filled _self_ of understanding consciousness is the subject as a consciousness that is consistent in itself and that orders and sustains itself in accordance with its individual standing and its connection with the external world, a world likewise ordered within itself. Yet when it stays caught in one particular determinateness, it does not grant that content the understanding place and subordination which, in the individual world-system that a subject is, are due to it. The subject is thus placed in a _contradiction_: on one side its totality, systematized in its consciousness, on the other the particular determinateness, which within that totality is neither fluid nor co-ordinated or subordinated, – _derangement_.

::: remark
{{ 408 A }} In considering derangement, the developed, understanding consciousness is likewise to be anticipated, which is subject at the same time as _natural_ _self_ _of_ _self-feeling_. So determined, it can fall into a contradiction: its subjectivity is free for itself, but a particularity in it fails to become ideal and stays fixed in self-feeling. Spirit is free, and therefore for itself not {{{ 20.413 }}} capable of this disease. It was considered by earlier metaphysics as _soul_, as _thing_, and only as thing, that is, as _natural_ and _being_, is it capable of derangement, of the finitude holding itself fast in it. For that reason derangement is a disease of the psychic, in which corporeal and spiritual are unseparated; the beginning can seem to proceed more from the one side or the other, and likewise the cure.

In health and reflectiveness the subject is conscious, as present to it, of the ordered totality of its individual world; every _particular_ content that turns up, whether sensation, representation, desire, inclination and so forth, it _subsumes_ within that world's system and assigns to its understanding place, and over these particularities it is the _ruling_ _genius_. The difference is that of waking and dreaming; only here the dream occurs inside waking itself, and so belongs to actual self-feeling. Error and the like is a content taken up consistently into that objective connection. But in the concrete it is often hard to say where it begins to become madness. Thus a violent but, in its import, slight passion of hatred and so forth can, against the higher reflectiveness and hold in itself that are to be presupposed, appear as a being-outside-itself of madness. But this essentially contains the _contradiction_ of a feeling that has become corporeally _being_ _against_ the totality of mediations that concrete consciousness is. Spirit determined as only _being_, insofar as such being is unresolved in its consciousness, is sick. – The content that becomes free in this its naturalness consists of the selfish determinations of the heart, vanity, pride and the other passions, and the imaginations, hopes, love and hatred of the subject. This earthly element becomes free as the power of reflectiveness and of the universal, of the theoretical or moral principles, over the natural, by which it is otherwise held subjected and concealed, lets up; for this evil is in itself present in the heart, because the latter is immediate, natural and self-like. It is the evil genius of the human being that becomes dominant in derangement, but in opposition and contradiction to the better and understanding element that is at the same time in the human being, so that this state is a disarray and {{{ 20.414 }}} unhappiness of spirit within itself. – The true _psychic_ treatment therefore also holds fast the point of view that derangement is not an abstract _loss_ of reason, neither on the side of intelligence nor on that of the will and its accountability, but only derangement, only contradiction within the reason still present, just as psychic disease is not an abstract, that is, total loss of health (such a loss would be death), but a contradiction within it. This humane, that is, as benevolent as rational, treatment – _Pinel_ deserves the highest recognition for the services he has rendered to it – presupposes the patient as rational and has in this the firm hold by which it can take hold of the patient on this side, as on the side of corporeality in vitality, which as such still contains health within itself.
:::
