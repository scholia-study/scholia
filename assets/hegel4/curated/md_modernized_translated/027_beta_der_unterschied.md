## β) Difference

## {{ 116 }} § 116.

Essence is pure identity and shine within itself only in that it is the negativity relating itself to itself, and thus a repelling of itself from itself; it therefore essentially contains the determination of _difference_.

::: remark
{{{ 20.148 }}} {{ 116 A }} Otherness here is no longer the _qualitative_ kind, determinateness, limit; since essence [itself] relates to itself, negation counts there at once as relation, as _difference_, as _positedness_, as _mediatedness_.
:::

## {{ 117 }} § 117.

Difference is 1) _immediate_ difference, _diversity_: the differentiated each _is_ _for_ _itself_ what it is, unconcerned with how it relates to the other, a relation that is therefore external to it. Since the diverse are indifferent to their difference, the latter falls outside them, into a third that _compares_. As the identity of the things related, this external difference is _equality_; as their non-identity, _inequality_.

::: remark
{{ 117 A }} The understanding lets these determinations fall asunder so that, even though one and the same substrate underlies the comparison for both equality and inequality, they are meant to be different _sides_ and _respects_ of it; yet equality by itself is merely the earlier determination, identity, and inequality by itself is difference.

Diversity too has been turned into a proposition: _that_ _all_ _things_ _differ_, or _that_ _no_ _two_ _things_ _are_ _entirely_ _alike_. Here _everything_ receives the _opposite_ predicate from the _identity_ assigned to it in the _first_ proposition, so a law contradicting the first is laid down. Yet, if diversity belongs only to external comparison, something is _supposed_ to be, _for_ _itself_, merely _identical_ with itself, and then the second proposition would not contradict the first. In that case, however, diversity _does_ _not_ _belong_ to the something or to everything and constitutes no essential determination of this subject; the second {{{ 20.149 }}} proposition could not then be asserted at all. – If, on the other hand, the something is _itself_ diverse, as the proposition says, it is so by _its_ _own_ determinateness; but then what is meant is no longer diversity as such but _determinate_ difference. – That too is the sense of Leibniz's proposition.
:::

## {{ 118 }} § 118.

Equality is the identity solely of things that are _not_ _the_ _same_, not identical with each other, – and inequality is the _relation_ among unequals. The two therefore do not drift indifferently apart onto separate sides or respects; each rather shines into the other. Diversity is thus difference of reflection, or _difference_ _in_ _itself_, _determinate_ difference.

## {{ 119 }} § 119.

2) Difference _in_ _itself_ is the _essential_ difference, _the_ _positive_ and _the_ _negative_: the former is the identical self-relation in the sense that it is _not_ the negative, and the latter is the distinct, for itself, in the sense that it is _not_ the positive. Each is for itself by being _not_ _the_ _other_, and for that reason each _shines_ in the other and exists only to the extent that the other does. Essential difference is therefore _counterposition_: what is differentiated does not face _an_ _other_ _in_ _general_ but _its_ other; that is, each possesses its determination solely in its relation to the other, is reflected into itself only by being reflected into the other, and the other likewise; each is thus the other's _own_ other.

::: remark
{{ 119 A }} Difference in itself gives the proposition: _everything_ _is_ _something_ _essentially_ _differentiated_, – or, in another formulation, _of_ _two_ _opposed_ _predicates_ _only_ _one_ _belongs_ _to_ _a_ _something_, and _there_ _is_ _no_ _third_. – This proposition of opposition flatly contradicts the proposition of identity, for according to {{{ 20.150 }}} the one a something is only _relation_ _to_ _itself_, according to the other, however, it is an _opposed_ thing, _relation_ _to_ _its_ _other_. It is abstraction's own thoughtlessness to set two such contradictory propositions next to each other as laws, without so much as comparing them. – The proposition of the _excluded_ _third_ belongs to the determinate understanding, which means to keep contradiction at bay and by doing so commits it. A is supposed to be either +A or −A; thus the third, A, has already been stated, which is _neither_ + _nor_ −, and which is _equally_ _also_ posited as +A and as −A together. Let +W mean a distance of 6 miles toward the west and −W 6 miles toward the east, and let + and − cancel each other: then the 6 miles of way or space stay what they were with and without the opposition. Even the bare plus and minus of number or of abstract direction have, if one likes, zero as their third; but it is not to be denied that the empty opposition of the understanding between + and − also has a place in abstractions of that kind, such as number, direction, and so forth.

In the doctrine of contradictory concepts, one concept is named, say, blue (even something like the sensory representation of a color is called a concept in such a doctrine), the other _non-blue_, so the latter is not to be an affirmative, _yellow_ for instance, but is to be held fast only [as] the abstract negative. – That the negative is within itself just as positive (see the following §) is already implied by the determination that what is set against an other is _its_ other. – So-called contradictory concepts show their empty opposition most fully in the, so to speak, grandiose statement of a universal law: of _all_ predicates opposed in this way, one belongs to _every_ thing and the other does not, so that spirit is either white or not white, yellow or not yellow, and so on without end.

Because it is forgotten that identity and counterposition are themselves opposed, the proposition of counterposition gets taken as that of identity as well, in the shape of the proposition of contradiction, and a _concept_ that has neither (see above) or both of two mutually contradictory marks is declared logically false, a square circle for instance. A polygonal circle and a {{{ 20.151 }}} straight-lined arc of a circle violate this proposition just as much, yet geometers have no qualms about regarding and handling the circle as a polygon with straight sides. But something like a circle (its bare determinateness) is not yet a _concept_; in the concept of the circle, center and periphery are equally essential, both marks belong to it; and still periphery and center are opposed and contradict one another.

The representation of _polarity_, much relied upon in physics, holds within it the more correct determination of opposition; yet if physics, in matters of thought, stays with ordinary logic, it would easily be alarmed upon developing polarity and arriving at the thoughts that lie in it.
:::

## {{ 120 }} § 120.

The _positive_ is that _diverse_ thing which is meant to exist for itself and yet _not_ to be indifferent to its relation _to_ _its_ _other_. The _negative_ is meant likewise to be self-subsistent, a negative self-relation, to exist _for_ _itself_, while as a negative having this relation to itself, its positive, solely in the other. Both are accordingly the posited contradiction, and both are _in_ _themselves_ the same. They are so _for_ _themselves_ as well, since each sublates both the other and itself. They thus go to _ground_. – Or, immediately: the essential difference, as difference in and for itself, is only the difference of itself from itself and thus includes the identical; the whole difference that is in and for itself therefore comprises both itself and identity. – _As_ _self-relating_ difference it is likewise already stated _as_ _self-identical_, and the _opposed_ is in general whatever includes within itself _the_ _one_ and _its_ _other_, _itself_ and _what_ _opposes_ _it_. The being-within-itself of essence determined in this way is the _ground_.
