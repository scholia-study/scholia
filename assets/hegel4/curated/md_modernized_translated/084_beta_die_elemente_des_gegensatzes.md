## {{{ 20.286 }}} β) The Elements of Opposition

## {{ 283 }} § 283.

The elements of opposition are, first, being-for-itself, but not the _indifferent_ being-for-itself of rigidity, rather that posited in individuality as a moment, as its restlessness that is for itself, – _fire_. – Air is fire _in_ _itself_ (as it shows itself by compression), and it is fire _posited_ as _negative_ universality or negativity relating itself to itself. It is materialized _time_ or selfhood (light identical with heat), – the absolutely restless and consuming, into which the body's self-consumption turns out just as, conversely, coming to the body externally, it destroys it, – a consuming of another, – which at the same time consumes itself and so passes over into neutrality.

## {{ 284 }} § 284.

The other [element] is the neutral, opposition gone together into itself, which, without singularity that is for itself and hence without rigidity and determination in itself, is a thoroughgoing equilibrium, dissolves every determinateness posited mechanically in it, receives limitedness of shape only from outside and seeks it outside (adhesion), without the restlessness of the process in itself, is absolutely the possibility of it, the dissolvability, like the capacity for the form of airiness and of rigidity as a state outside its own, of indeterminateness in itself; – _water_.
