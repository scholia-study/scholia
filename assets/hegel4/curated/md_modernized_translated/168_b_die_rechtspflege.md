## {{{ 20.501 }}} b. The Administration of Justice

## {{ 529 }} § 529.

The principle of contingent particularity, developed into the system mediated by natural need and free arbitrary will, into universal relations of that system and a course of external necessity, has in it, as the determination of freedom that is fixed for itself, at first _formal_ _right_. 1) The realization due to right in this sphere of understanding consciousness is that right be brought to consciousness as the fixed universal, _known_ in its determinateness and _posited_ as what holds; – the _law_.

::: remark
{{ 529 A }} The _positive_ element of laws concerns only their form of being _valid_ and _known_ in general, whereby the possibility is at the same time given of being known by _all_ in the ordinary external way. The content may in this regard be rational in itself or else irrational and thereby wrong. But since right, as comprehended in determinate existence, is a developed one, and its content analyzes itself in order to gain determinateness, this analysis falls, because of the finitude of the material, into the progress of bad infinity; the _ultimate_ determinateness, absolutely essential as it is and the one thing that halts this progress into unreality, is attainable in this finite sphere solely by a route entangled with contingency and arbitrary will; whether three years, 10 thalers and so forth, or only 2½, 2¾, 2⅘ and so forth years, and so on into infinity, would be what is just, cannot be decided in any way by the concept, and yet it is the higher thing that a decision is made. Thus the positive enters right of itself, but admittedly only at the _ends_ _of_ _determining_, on the side of {{{ 20.502 }}} external existence, as contingency and arbitrariness. This happens, and has always happened of itself in all legislations; it is only necessary to have a determinate consciousness of this, against the supposed aim and prattle as if the law could and should be determined on _all_ sides by reason or by juridical understanding, through nothing but rational and intelligible grounds. The empty opinion of _perfection_ consists in directing such an expectation and demand at the sphere of the finite.

Those for whom _laws_ are even an evil and something unholy, and who hold government and being governed from natural love, inherited divinity or nobility, through faith and trust, to be the genuine condition, but the rule of laws to be the corrupted and unjust one, overlook the circumstance that the stars and so forth, as well as cattle, are governed by laws, and well governed, – laws that, however, in these objects are only inner, not _for_ _them_ themselves, not _posited_ laws, whereas the human being is this, to _know_ its law, and therefore can truly obey only such a known law, just as its law can be a just law only as _known_, but otherwise must already, according to its essential content, be contingency and arbitrary will, or at least mixed and contaminated with them.

The same empty demand for perfection is used for the opposite of the above, namely for the opinion of the impossibility or impracticability of a code of law. In this there occurs the further deficiency of thought of placing the essential and universal determinations in one class with the particular detail. Finite material is determinable onward in bad infinity; but this progress is not, as it is represented in space for instance, a generating of spatial determinations of the same quality as the preceding ones, but a going forth into the more specific and ever more specific through the acuteness of the analyzing understanding, which invents new distinctions that make new decisions necessary. If determinations of this kind likewise receive the name of _new_ decisions or _new_ _laws_, then, in proportion as this development proceeds, the _interest_ and {{{ 20.503 }}} the _import_ of these determinations diminish. They lie _inside_ the substantial, universal laws already in force, as improvements to a floor, a door and so forth lie inside a house: something _new_, certainly, but no house. Where the legislation of an uncultivated condition began from _single_ determinations and, by their nature, keeps adding to them, the growth of this multitude produces, conversely, a need for a _simpler_ code, that is, of the summing up of that multitude of singularities into their _universal_ determinations, which it befits the understanding and culture of a people to know how to find and to state; – in England the minister _Peel_ has lately begun, in certain respects, such a reduction of singularities to universal forms (which alone truly merit the name of laws), and has earned his countrymen's thanks and even admiration thereby.
:::

## {{ 530 }} § 530.

2) The positive form of laws, that of _being_ _pronounced_ _and_ _made_ _known_ as laws, is the condition of the _external_ _obligation_ toward them, in that, as laws of strict right, they concern only the abstract (that is, itself in itself external) will, not the moral or ethical will. The subjectivity to which the will has a right on this side is here only being known. This subjective existence, as the existence of what is in and for itself in this sphere, of right, is at the same time external _objective_ existence, as universal validity and necessity.

The rightful element of property, and of private actions concerning it, receives its _universal_ _guarantee_ through the formalities, in accordance with the determination that what is rightful is something posited, recognized and thereby valid.

## {{{ 20.504 }}} {{ 531 }} § 531.

3) What is right receives the _necessity_ to which objective existence determines itself in the _administration_ _of_ _justice_. Before the _court_, right individualized, right-in-itself must show itself as _proven_; and here right-in-itself can differ from what is provable. The court cognizes and acts in the interest of right as such, takes from the concrete existence of right its contingency, and in particular transforms this concrete existence, as it is as revenge, into _punishment_. (§ 500)

::: remark
{{ 531 A }} The comparison of the two kinds, or rather moments, of the judges' conviction about the facts of an act in relation to the accused, – through the mere circumstances and the testimony of others, – alone, – or through the additional requirement of the defendant's confession, makes up the main point in the question about the so-called _jury_ _courts_. The two components of a judicial finding – judgment on _the_ _facts_ _of_ _the_ _case_, and judgment as the _application_ of law to them – are sides different in themselves, and it is essential that they be carried out as _different_ _functions_. The institution mentioned even hands them to bodies of different qualification, one of which is expressly not to be composed of persons from the profession of official judges. To drive that difference of functions to this separation in the courts rests more on inessential considerations; the main point remains only the separate exercise of those sides that are different in themselves. – Of greater importance is the question whether the confession of a person accused of a crime should be made a condition for a penal judgment. The institution of the jury court abstracts from this condition. What counts is that certainty, wholly on this ground, cannot be separated from _truth_; confession {{{ 20.505 }}}, however, is to be viewed as the topmost point of _assurance_, which is subjective by nature; the ultimate decision therefore lies in confession, and in respect of it the defendant has an absolute right to the conclusiveness of the proof and of the judges' conviction. – This moment is incomplete, being only one moment; yet the other one, taken just as abstractly – proof from mere circumstances and testimony – is more imperfect still; and jurors are essentially judges, who deliver a judgment. Inasmuch as they are referred to such objective proofs, but at the same time incomplete certainty is admitted, insofar as it is only _in_ _them_, the jury court contains the (properly barbarous-age) mixing and confusing of objective proof and of subjective, so-called moral conviction. – It is easy to declare _extraordinary_ penalties an absurdity, and rather too shallow to stumble so over the mere name. In substance this determination contains the difference between objective proof with or without the moment of that absolute assurance which lies in confession.
:::

## {{ 532 }} § 532.

It is the vocation of the administration of justice to activate into necessity merely the abstract side of the person's freedom in civil society. This activation, however, depends at first on the judge's particular subjectivity, because the necessary unity of that subjectivity with right-in-itself is not yet at hand here. Conversely, the blind necessity of the system of needs has not yet been raised into consciousness of the universal, nor is it activated from out of that consciousness.
