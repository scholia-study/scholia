## B. Appearance

## {{ 131 }} § 131.

Essence must _appear_. Its shining in itself is the sublating of it into immediacy, which as reflection-into-itself is _subsistence_ (matter) just as it is _form_, reflection-into-other, _self-sublating_ subsistence. Shining is the determination through which essence is not being but essence, and developed shining is appearance. Essence is therefore not _behind_ or _beyond_ appearance, but because it is essence that exists, concrete existence is appearance.
