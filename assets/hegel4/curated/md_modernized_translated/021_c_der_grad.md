## {{{ 20.139 }}} c. Degree

## {{ 103 }} § 103.

The _limit_ is identical with the whole quantum itself: as _in_ _itself_ multiple, it is the _extensive_ magnitude, whereas as a determinateness _simple_ in itself it is the _intensive_ magnitude or _degree_.

::: remark
{{ 103 A }} The difference between continuous and discrete magnitudes and extensive and intensive ones therefore consists in this, that the former concern _quantity_ _in_ _general_, while the latter concern the _limit_ or determinateness of it as such. – Likewise extensive and intensive magnitude are not two kinds, each of which would contain a determinateness that the other did not have; what is extensive magnitude is just as much intensive, and conversely.
:::

## {{ 104 }} § 104.

In the degree the _concept_ of the quantum is _posited_. It is magnitude as indifferent _for_ _itself_ and simple, yet in such a way that it has the determinateness whereby it is a quantum absolutely _outside_ _itself_ in other magnitudes. In this contradiction, that _the_ indifferent limit _that_ _is_ _for_ _itself_ is absolute _externality_, the _infinite_ quantitative _progress_ is posited, – an _immediacy_ that turns immediately into its opposite, into _mediatedness_ (the going beyond the quantum just posited), and conversely.

::: remark
{{ 104 A }} _Number_ is thought, yet thought as a being wholly external to itself. Because it is thought, it is not part of intuition; it is rather thought that takes the externality of intuition as its determination. – Hence the quantum _can_ not merely be increased or decreased into infinity but is itself, by its concept, this _sending-out_ beyond itself. The infinite quantitative progress is equally the thoughtless repetition of one and the same {{{ 20.140 }}} contradiction, which the quantum is in general and which, posited in its determinateness, is the degree. As for the superfluity of voicing this contradiction in the form of infinite progress, _Zeno_ in Aristotle rightly observes that saying something _once_ and saying it _always_ come to the same.
:::

## {{ 105 }} § 105.

This _being-external_ to itself of the quantum in its determinateness that is _for-itself_ constitutes its _quality_; in this it is precisely itself and related to itself. Externality, that is, the quantitative, and being-for-itself, the qualitative, are united in it. – The quantum posited thus _in_ _itself_ is the quantitative _ratio_, – a determinateness that is just as much an _immediate_ quantum, the exponent, as it is _mediation_, namely the _relation_ of some quantum to another, – the two sides of the ratio, which at the same time do not count according to their immediate value, but whose value is only in this relation.

## {{ 106 }} § 106.

The ratio's _sides_ remain immediate quanta, and the qualitative and the quantitative determination remain external to each other. Yet in their truth, which consists in the quantitative being itself self-relation in its externality, or in being-for-itself and the indifference of determinateness being united, it is the _measure_.
