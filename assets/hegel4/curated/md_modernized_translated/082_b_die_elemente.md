## {{{ 20.285 }}} b. The Elements

## {{ 281 }} § 281.

The body of individuality has, as subjugated moments in it, the determinations of the elemental totality, which immediately are bodies subsisting freely for themselves; thus they make up its universal _physical_ _elements_.

::: remark
{{ 281 A }} For the determination of an element, _chemical_ _simplicity_ has been arbitrarily adopted in recent times, which has nothing to do with the concept of a _physical_ element, which is a real matter not yet volatilized into chemical abstraction.
:::
