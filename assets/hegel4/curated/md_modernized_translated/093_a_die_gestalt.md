## a. Shape

## {{ 310 }} § 310.

The body as total individuality is _immediately_ a _resting_ totality, hence the form of the spatial being-together of what is material, and therefore at first once again _mechanism_. Shape is thus the material mechanism of an individuality that now determines itself unconditionally and freely – the body in which not only the specific kind of inner coherence but also the _external_ _delimitation_ in _space_ is determined by the _immanent_ and _developed_ form. In this way the form is manifested _of_ _itself_, and does not first show itself as a peculiarity of _resistance_ against _alien_ force.

## {{{ 20.307 }}} {{ 311 }} § 311.

α) The _immediate_ shape, that is, the shape posited as _formless_ in itself, is on the one side the extreme of _the_ _punctuality_ of brittleness, on the other the extreme of the _spheroidizing_ fluidity; shape as inner shapelessness.

## {{ 312 }} § 312.

β) The brittle, as the totality that exists _in_ _itself_ of the individuality that forms, opens itself out into the difference of the concept. The point passes over first into the line, and on it the form posits itself in opposed extremes, which as moments have no subsistence of their own and are held only by their relation, which, appearing, is their middle and the point of indifference of the opposition. This syllogism constitutes the _principle_ of _shaping_ in its developed determinateness and, in this still abstract strictness, is _magnetism_.

::: remark
{{ 312 A }} Magnetism is one of the determinations that had to present themselves above all when the _concept_ suspected itself in determinate nature and conceived the idea of a _philosophy_ _of_ _nature_. For the magnet exhibits, in a simple, naive way, the nature of the concept, and indeed in its developed form as syllogism (§ 181). The poles are the sensibly existing ends of a real line (of a rod, or also of a body extended further in all dimensions); as poles, however, they have not sensible, mechanical reality but an ideal one; they are absolutely inseparable. The point of indifference, in which they have their substance, is the unity in which they are as determinations of the concept, so that they have sense and existence solely in this unity, and polarity is the relation of only such moments. Magnetism has, beyond the determination thereby posited, no further particular property. That the single magnetic needle orients itself toward the _north_ and thereby at the same time {{{ 20.308 }}} toward the _south_ is an appearance of the universal _terrestrial_ _magnetism_. – That all bodies are magnetic, however, carries a skewed double sense; the correct one is that every real shape, not merely the brittle, contains this principle of determination; the incorrect one, however, that all bodies also bring to appearance in themselves this principle as it _exists_ in its strict _abstraction_, that is, as magnetism. To want to point out a form of the concept as present in nature in such a way that, in the determinateness it has as an abstraction, it should _exist_ _universally_ would be an unphilosophical thought. Nature is rather the idea in the element of externality-to-one-another, so that, just like the understanding, it holds the moments of the concept fast as _scattered_ and presents them in reality, but in the higher things unites the distinct forms of the concept in one highest concretion (see the Remark to the following §).
:::

## {{ 313 }} § 313.

Insofar as this self-relating form exists at first in this _abstract_ determination, of being the identity of _persisting_ differences, and thus has not yet become product and been paralyzed in the total shape, it is as _activity_ – and indeed, in the sphere of shape, the immanent activity of free _mechanism_, namely to determine the _local_ relations.

::: remark
{{ 313 A }} A word needs to be said here about the _identity_ of magnetism, electricity and chemism, so widely acknowledged at present and even become fundamental in physics: the _opposition_ of the form in the individual material goes on to determine itself into the more real, _electrical_ opposition, and into the still more real, the _chemical_. One and the same universal totality of form underlies all these particular forms as their substance. Further, electricity and chemism, as processes, are activities of the more real, physically further determined {{{ 20.309 }}} opposition; but beyond that these processes contain above all alterations in the relations of material spatiality. On this side, that this concrete activity is at the same time a determination that mechanizes, it is _in_ _itself_ magnetic activity. To the extent that it can also be brought to _appearance_ as such within these more concrete processes, the empirical conditions for this have been found in recent times. It is therefore to be reckoned an essential advance of empirical science that the identity of these phenomena has been acknowledged in the representation that is called electro-chemism, or perhaps also magneto-electro-chemism, or whatever else. But the _particular_ forms in which the universal one exists, and their _particular_ _appearances_, are just as essentially _to_ _be_ _distinguished_ _from_ _one_ _another_. The name magnetism is therefore to be reserved for the express form and its appearance in the sphere of shape as such, relating only to _determinations_ _of_ _space_, just as the name electricity is likewise to be reserved for the determinations of appearance expressly designated by it. Formerly magnetism, electricity and chemism were considered wholly separate, without connection with one another, each as a self-subsistent force. Philosophy has grasped the idea of their _identity_, _but_ with express _reservation_ of their _difference_; in the most recent modes of representation in physics a leap seems to have been made to the extreme of the _identity_ of these phenomena, and the predicament seems to be – that, and how, they are at the same time to be kept apart. The difficulty lies in the need to unite both; it is solved only in the nature of the concept, but not in the identity that is a confusion of names in a magneto-electro-chemism.
:::

## {{ 314 }} § 314.

The activity of the form is no other than that of the concept in general: to posit _the_ _identical_ as _different_, and _the_ _different_ as _identical_; here, then, in the sphere of material spatiality, to posit what is identical in space as different {{{ 20.310 }}}, that is, to remove it from itself (– to _repel_), and to posit what is _different_ in space as identical, that is, to bring it near and into contact (– to _attract_). Since this activity exists in a material, but still _abstractly_ (and only as such is it magnetism), it animates only a _linear_ item (§ 256). In such an item the two determinations of the form can emerge separately only at its difference, that is, at the two ends, and their active, magnetic difference consists only in this, that the one end (the one _pole_) posits _the_ _same_ – a third – as identical with itself that the other (the other _pole_) removes from itself.

::: remark
{{ 314 A }} The law of magnetism is stated in this way: _like-named_ poles repel one another and the _unlike-named_ attract one another; the like-named are _inimical_, the unlike-named, however, _amicable_. For likeness of name, however, there is no other determination than that those are like-named which are both alike attracted, or both repelled, by a third. This third, however, likewise has its determination solely in repelling or attracting those like-named items, or an other in general. All determinations are throughout only _relative_, without differing, indifferent sensible existence; it was remarked above (Remark to § 312) that something such as north and south contains no such original, first or immediate determination. The _amicability_ of the _unlike-named_ and the _inimicality_ of the _like-named_ are thus not a subsequent or further particular phenomenon in a presupposed, peculiarly already determined magnetism, but express nothing other than the nature of magnetism itself, and with it the nature of the concept when it is posited as activity in this sphere.
:::

## {{ 315 }} § 315.

γ) Activity, passed over into its product, is _shape_, and determined as _crystal_. In this totality the different magnetic poles are reduced to neutrality, the abstract linearity of the place-determining activity is realized into the plane and surface of the whole body; more precisely, brittle punctuality is on one side expanded into the developed form, while the formal {{{ 20.311 }}} expansion of the sphere is reduced to delimitation. The _one_ form is at work crystallizing the body outwardly (delimiting the sphere) and, shaping the punctuality, its inner continuity _through_ _and_ _through_ (passage of the laminae, nuclear shape).
