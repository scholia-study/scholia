## {{{ 20.391 }}} α) Natural Qualities

## {{ 392 }} § 392.

Spirit lives along, 1) in its substance, the natural soul, with the universal planetary life, the difference of the climates, the change of the seasons, of the times of day, and the like, – a natural life that in spirit in part comes only to obscure moods.

::: remark
{{ 392 A }} In recent times much has been said about the _cosmic_, _sidereal_, _telluric_ life of the human being. The animal lives essentially in this sympathy; its specific character, as well as its particular developments, depends in many cases entirely on it, in every case more or less. In the human being such connections lose significance the more, the more cultivated the person is and the more the whole condition is thereby placed on a free spiritual foundation. Neither does world history depend on upheavals in the solar system, nor do the fates of individuals depend on the positions of the planets. – The difference of the climates contains a firmer and more powerful determinateness. But to the seasons and times of day correspond only weaker moods, which can show themselves chiefly only in states of disease, to which derangement also belongs, in the depression of self-conscious life. – Among the superstition of peoples and the aberrations of the weak understanding there are found, in peoples who have advanced less in spiritual freedom and therefore still live more in unity with nature, also _some_ real connections and, grounded upon them, foreknowings of states, and of the events tied to them, that seem wonderful. But with the more deeply grasping freedom of spirit these few and slight dispositions, which are grounded on living along with nature, vanish as well. The animal, like the plant, remains by contrast bound to them.
:::

## {{{ 20.392 }}} {{ 393 }} § 393.

The universal planetary life of the natural spirit, 2), takes on particularity in the concrete differences of the earth, and divides into the _particular_ _natural_ _spirits_; by and large these give expression to the nature of the geographical continents, and they make up the _difference_ _of_ _races_.

::: remark
{{ 393 A }} The opposition of terrestrial polarity, through which the land toward the north is more compressed and has the preponderance over the sea, while toward the southern hemisphere it runs apart, separated into points, brings into the difference of the continents at the same time a modification that _Treviranus_ (Biologie, Part II) has shown with regard to plants and animals.
:::

## {{ 394 }} § 394.

This difference extends into the particularities that can be called _local_ _spirits_, and that show themselves in the external way of life, occupation, bodily formation and disposition, and more so still in the inner bent and ability of the intelligent and ethical character of peoples.

::: remark
{{ 394 A }} As far back as the history of peoples reaches, it shows the persistence of this type of the particular nations.
:::

## {{ 395 }} § 395.

The soul is, 3), individuated into the _individual_ _subject_. But this subjectivity comes into consideration here only as individuation of the _natural_ _determinateness_. It is as the _mode_ of the different temperament, talent, character, physiognomy and other dispositions and idiosyncrasies of families or of the singular individuals.
