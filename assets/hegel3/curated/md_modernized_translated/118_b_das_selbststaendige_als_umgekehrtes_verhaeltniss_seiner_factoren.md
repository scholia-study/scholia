## {{{ 323 }}} B. The Self-subsistent as Inverse Ratio of Its Factors

Indifference is only the unity of the qualitative and the quantitative that is in itself; it has the determinate reference to its other, towards which it is indifferent; as ***being in itself*** it is indeed immediacy, but an immediacy from which its mediation is distinct; it is therefore in fact itself mediated. Thus it is the ***specific*** self-subsistent that mediates itself with itself through its negation and thereby becomes absolute self-subsistence, in such a way that it still has the two determinations, specific self-subsistence and the negation of it, at itself as ***moments*** which are distinguished from its indifference; through this these moments regain for an instant their determinate sense over against each other. But not as self-subsistents which are such immediately for themselves, rather as ones that have their self-subsistence solely in their indifference and are its moments.

There is first at hand the moment of specific self-subsistence, which in its determinateness is the qualitative one; it is not at its own self the indifferent unity with itself, but is over against that unity the ***determinate***, hence what is differentiated within itself. There are thus two self-subsistences at large; they are self-subsistent only in themselves, in their unity; for in it they are not determinate over against an other; only in the negation of its own self is the specific self-subsistent. In their specific determinateness, {{{ 324 }}} however, the one of them is what the other is not, and hence each is at the same time only insofar as the other is. But their determinate diversity over against each other is made up by the quantitative difference; the qualitative one makes up its constitution, its specific reference to an other, through the quantum. This quantum, which makes up the being of the sides of the ratio, is qualitatively determined the one against the other, so that the being of the one is the non-being of the other. They accordingly stand in an ***inverse ratio***.

The inverse ratio returns here; it is not, however, the first, formal one. In that one the qualitative reference — that the one is not what the other is — was itself indifferent to the sides, for they were only quanta at large. Here, in the reality of this ratio, it is the sides' own qualitativity that refers them in this way; their specific determination contains the moment of excluding, of being at their own selves insofar as the other is not. At the same time this return of the sides into themselves is the indifferent reference to self, the quantum; they have it as the determination of their indifferent subsistence over against each other. The quantum for its part is thus, as something specific, not the immediate external determinateness but a qualitative reference.

The two sides of this inverse ratio have their self-subsistence in the indifference of their moments that is in itself; they are this unity itself; but the differentiated unity; they are specified self-subsistents. As the unity of the qualitative and the quantitative, both are in themselves the same and self-subsistent, but they are this unity as mediated through their negation or through otherness; each has its return into itself in the other; the qualitative is what is determined in itself {{{ 325 }}} only within quantity as the indifferent subsistence, and the quantitative is something specific only within the qualitative. This negative unity, in which they are a self-subsistent, is still distinguished from their absolute indifference; hence ***the negative steps over against itself in its determinateness***. Their unity is therefore at hand, as their self-subsistence at large, ***in the difference of two*** which, as has already been determined, stand to each other in an inverse ratio. They are specifically self-subsistent, as determinate ones, and each is the mediation of itself with itself through its negation; but its negation is distinguished from its self-subsistence that is as yet only in itself; the negation is therefore an ***other*** self-subsistent. The return into self accordingly consists likewise as yet only in the immediacy of each, in which it maintains itself over against its alteration; the immediacy of its ***subsistence*** and its equality with itself as within alteration, or its ***mediation*** with itself, fall apart. There is at each an immediate part which is for itself and does not accrue to it out of the negation of the other, a part that can be regarded as greater or smaller or as infinitely small, and which is at large the moment of specific self-subsistence.

What is at hand, then, in the real inverse ratio are two specific self-subsistences; ones which ***in themselves*** are the same, and are distinguished as quanta. Their indifference that is in itself is thus only their ***sum***; a determinate quantum. This sum has its qualitative ***determinateness*** in the differing ratios into which the indifferent quanta enter with each other; for the reference which such qualitatively determined quanta of the whole have within the ratio is the specific diversity of this whole. These sides of the ratio are ***factors*** which, being in themselves the same {{{ 326 }}} whole, determine the whole through their determinate quantitative alteration, not according to its indifference but according to its ***determinateness***; for it is precisely this that they make up.

This determinateness of the factors now consists in the differing ratio of their quanta. But at the same time they are essentially determined qualitatively over against each other; their quantitative diversity is not a falling apart of them but is within One unity. The one therefore has a magnitude only insofar as the other side does not have it; by as much as is taken from the one, so much accrues to the other. But now for that very reason, ***because their quantitativity is downright of this qualitative nature, each reaches only so far as the other does***. Insofar as they were supposed to be diverse as quanta and to make up a determinate ratio, the one would run out beyond the other and would have in its more a being which the other would not have. But in their qualitative reference each is only insofar as the other is. — They are therefore in equilibrium, such that by however much the one increased or diminished, the other would likewise grow or shrink, and would grow or shrink in the very same ratio.

By reason of their qualitative reference, therefore, it cannot come to any quantitative difference. — But the quantitative determination can be taken as the first, and hence the one can be assumed immediately as greater than the other. In that case, however, it goes out beyond its own self; for in its determination it is equal to the other; as unequal to the other, it goes out beyond itself and contains the other. ***More*** than the other it has only from the other; but nothing is left over to the latter, for on account of the qualitative determination which the quantitative difference has, what the one would be ***over*** and ***beyond the other, is only this other itself***. {{{ 327 }}} There is only the one and the other; insofar as the one were to undergo an increment, this increment is only the other.

Insofar, then, as starting from the quantitative representation the equilibrium is disturbed and one factor is taken as greater, it becomes the preponderant one in such a way that the other diminishes at accelerating speed and is overpowered by the first, so that this first makes itself the sole self-subsistent, but is thereby no longer a specific one but the whole.

Insofar, then, as a determinateness is at hand at the whole whose factors the sides of the inverse ratio are supposed to be, and an alteration goes on, this alteration goes on only at the whole itself, not at the factors, which have no determinateness over against each other. Further, insofar as such factors, — though, as has been shown, superfluously, — are assumed as determinations of the whole, it is a matter of complete indifference which of them one lets alter, or else the other alters just as much; there is only one thing at all, the whole, that alters; the difference of the factors is without significance.

But the whole too does not alter; for this whole, the indifference that is in itself, is at large no longer determined qualitatively or quantitatively; it is not sum or quantum, nor any other qualitative determinateness. Determinateness is no longer ***first negation***, but absolute negativity. Indifference is in itself only this, to be indifferent towards quality and quantum. The determinateness, however, which it still had as something ***that is in itself***, and which consisted in the difference of its factors, has completely sublated itself in this, that it showed itself to be what goes back into itself; through this these factors are the same not only ***in themselves***, but in ***their determinate other*** {{{ 328 }}} ***ness***; it is ***their negative reference*** through which they are one.
