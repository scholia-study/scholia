## {{{ 156 }}} Chapter 2. Quantum

Quantum is real quantity, as existence is real being. It is ***at first*** quantity with a determinateness or limit at large, but in its complete determinateness it is ***number***. Quantum marks itself off

***secondly*** into ***extensive*** and ***intensive*** quantum, whose difference is on the one hand a matter of indifference, so that one and the same numerical determinateness is at hand in the one manner just as much as in the other. On the other hand, however, therein lies the difference of quantum upon its own self, which

***thirdly***, as being in itself external to its own self, goes over into ***quantitative infinity***.
