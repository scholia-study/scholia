## c.) Quality

Determinateness is thus at first the simple limit that is within itself. But it thereby has the two moments that have been considered. Determinateness in this closer reflection is ***quality,*** which unites in itself the meaning of determination as well as that of constitution. Quality as this union is the determinate nature of something, not as one resting within itself, but insofar as the something has in it at the same time a manner of its own that determines itself through the reference to an other.

Insofar as, in their separate consideration, determination and constitution were distinguished from each other, something taken according to its determination is indifferent {{{ 68 }}} towards its constitution. But the two are essentially moments of one and the same, or more precisely constitution is properly the limit contained in determination itself. Constitution, insofar as it appears at the same time to be grounded in something external, in an other in general, therefore depends on determination as well, and the alien determination is at once determined by the something's own, immanent determination. Conversely constitution belongs to what the something is in itself; with its constitution something alters.
