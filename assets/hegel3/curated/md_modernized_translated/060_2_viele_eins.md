## 2. Many Ones

(Repulsion.)

The one and the void constitute being-for-itself in its existence.

{{{ 105 }}} Each of these moments is at the same time the negation; the one and the void therefore constitute the reference of negation to negation. But the determinateness of this existence, as it has emerged, is that the one is the negation in the determination of being, whereas the void is the negation in the determination of non-being. This as yet abstract difference has to determine itself further.

The one has immediacy; it is reference to itself and, on its own account, indifferent towards the nothing that is outside it. But the one is essentially not indifferent towards the void; for it is reference to itself only as referring negation, that is, as that which the void outside it is supposed to be. Insofar, therefore, as ***first*** the one as something immediate refers itself to the void, which likewise has the shape of something immediate, the reference of existence is at hand; the one thus refers itself to the void ***as to an other of itself***, and goes out beyond itself into the void. But since ***second*** in the ideality of being-for-itself there is no other, since the reference to its non-being is essentially reference to itself, the existent other is at the same time ***itself***, and at the same time its non-being. The one is thus a ***becoming*** into ***many ones***.

This movement of the one into many ones is, however, not so much a becoming; for becoming is a passing over into the opposite, from being into nothing, and it is a reference which is not immediately the referred term itself. Here, by contrast, one becomes only one; further, the one, the referred term, is this negative reference itself.

For the one is reference to itself as negative referring; thus it is being-for-itself in general, a referring without anything {{{ 106 }}} referred. But insofar as it is one, it is immediate; and it is therewith essentially reference to itself as ***to something immediate***; there is therewith a ***referred term*** at hand, but through absolutely negative reference, which is infinite sublating of otherness. The one therefore does ***not pass over into an other***; rather, ***it repels*** itself ***from itself***. The negative reference of the one to itself is ***repulsion***.

Repulsion is therefore indeed a becoming of the many ones, but through the one itself.

For that reason the one is also not as something ***that has become***; the becoming into many vanishes immediately as becoming; those that have become are ones, are not for an other, but refer themselves infinitely to themselves. The one repels only itself from its own self; it therefore does not become, but ***already is***; this becoming is accordingly no passing over.

Plurality is thus not an ***otherness***, and is a determination wholly external to the one. The one, in repelling its own self, remains reference to itself, does not become a referring to an other. That the ones are others over against one another, that they are gathered together into the determinateness of plurality, is therefore no concern of the ones. Were plurality a reference of the ones to each other, they would set limits to each other or would have a being-for-other. Their reference, insofar as they are represented as immediate, is the void, or no reference at all. The limit is that wherein what is limited just as much ***is*** as is not; the void, however, has the determination of pure non-being, and only this makes up their limit.

{{{ 107 }}} The repulsion of the one from its own self is therefore infinity come outside itself; it is just as much a simple referring of one to one as rather the absolute referencelessn[e]ss of the ones. Or the plurality of the one is the one's own positing; the one is only the negative reference of the one to its own self, and this reference — that is, the one itself — is the many ones. But just as much is plurality no concern of the one, it is simply external to it; for the one is just this sublating of otherness, repulsion is the one's reference to itself, and simple equality with itself.
