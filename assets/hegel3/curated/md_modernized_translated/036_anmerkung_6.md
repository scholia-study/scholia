## Remark

Quality in this respect is preeminently ***property***, in that it shows itself in an ***external reference*** as an ***immanent determination***. For by properties, for instance those of herbs, one understands determinations that are not merely proper to a something in general, but that are proper to it insofar as it thereby comports itself in a peculiar manner in reference to other things, and does not let the alien effects posited in it hold sway within it, but rather shows its restriction as a being-within-itself, and makes that restriction ***tell*** in its otherness — although it does not keep that otherness away from itself. The more inert determinatenesses, for instance figure, shape, magnitude, are on the other hand not readily called properties.

Insofar as one speaks of good or bad quality, quality has the meaning of that one moment of it, of constitution. For ***good*** and ***bad*** are determinations of judgment concerning the ***agreement*** of ***constitution*** with ***determination***, with the concept. At the same time, however, this constitution is not a merely unessential, detachable externality, {{{ 69 }}} or a mere ***state***, but is a determinateness of the being of the matter itself. Constitution is not sundered from determination; rather, as the matter is ***constituted***, so it also ***is***. Quality is just this, that determinateness, distinguished into determination and constitution, is essentially the unity of both moments.

The ***Qualirung*** or ***Inqualirung*** of a philosophy that goes into the depths, but into a murky depth, refers to determinateness insofar as it is ***in itself,*** but is at the same time an ***other*** in itself; or it refers to the closer nature of opposition as it is in essence, insofar as opposition makes up the inner nature of quality and essentially its self-movement within itself. In that philosophy Qualirung therefore signifies the movement of a determinateness within its own self, insofar as in its negative nature (in its ***Qual***) it posits and secures itself out of an other, is in general the unrest that it has in its own self, according to which it brings itself forth and maintains itself only in struggle.
