## {{{ 47 }}} Chapter 2. Existence

Existence is ***determinate*** being. Existence itself is at the same time distinguished from its ***determinateness***. In determinateness the concept of ***quality*** makes its entry. But determinateness passes over into ***constitution*** and alteration, and then into the opposition of the ***finite*** and the ***infinite***, which resolves itself in being-for-itself.
