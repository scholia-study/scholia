## 1. Existence in General

Existence is the ***simple*** being-one of being and of nothing. Just because it is thus simple, it wears the {{{ 48 }}} form of something ***immediate***. Its mediation, becoming, lies behind it; that mediation has sublated its own self, and existence for that reason shows up as a first, one that gets started out from.

It is not mere being, but ***existence***. Etymologically taken, being at a certain ***place***; but the representation of space does not belong here. Existence, in the wake of its becoming, is ***being*** in general with a ***non-being***, though in such fashion that this non-being has been received into simple unity together with being; existence is ***determinate*** being in general.

Owing to the immediacy in which being and nothing are one within existence, neither reaches out past the other; rather, just so far as the existent is something that is, just so far is it something that is not, just so far is it determinate. Being here is not the ***universal***, nor is determinateness the ***particular***. Determinateness has not as yet come loose from being; or rather it will never again come loose from it; for what now lies at the ground as the true is precisely this unity of non-being with being; on it, as their ground, every further determination arises. Whatever being steps up against determinateness from here on is no longer that first, immediate being.
