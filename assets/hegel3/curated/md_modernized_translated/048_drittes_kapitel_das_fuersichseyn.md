## {{{ 91 }}} Chapter 3. Being-for-itself

In ***being-for-itself*** the ***qualitative being is completed***; it is infinite being. The being of the beginning is without any determination. Existence is sublated being, but only immediately sublated being. It therefore contains at first only the first, immediate negation; being is likewise present as preserved, and determinateness is as yet only limit. The movement of existence consists in shifting this limit out of its externality and into itself. In being-for-itself this reversal is completed. The negative as being-within-itself and the negative as limit, as otherness, are posited as identical; being-for-itself is ***the negative that refers itself to itself, absolute being-determined***.

Now just as existence determines or makes itself into the existent, so ***first*** being-for-itself determines itself into that which is for itself, or into the ***one***.

***Second***, the one is ***repulsion*** and passes over into a ***plurality of ones***.

***Third***, however, this otherness of the one sublates itself through ***attraction***, and quality, which in being-for-itself drove itself to its extreme point, passes over into ***quantity***.
