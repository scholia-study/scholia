## Remark

This ***ratio*** of a whole which is meant to possess its determinateness in the difference of magnitude between factors qualitatively determined against each other is put to use, for instance, in the elliptical motion of the heavenly bodies. In this motion their speed accelerates as they approach the perihelion, and it slackens as they approach the aphelion. For what passes as an explanation of this phenomenon, one posits a ***centripetal*** and a ***centrifugal force*** as qualitative moments of motion along the curved line. Their qualitative difference consists in the diversity of direction. In quantitative respect they are determined as unequal and opposed, so that as the one is to grow the other is to wane, and conversely, and at the same time so that their ratio should also turn about once more, so that once the centripetal force has increased for a while and the centrifugal force has decreased, a point should set in at which the centripetal force decreases while the centrifugal force increases. I have shed light on this subject in an earlier dissertation and laid bare the nullity of this distinction and of the explanations built upon it. Closer consideration readily shows that in the operations and formulas built upon that distinction there in fact occurs no quantitative diversity of these moments at all, but rather that what is always in question is only ***the whole***, the speed of the motion; so that what comes out as the magnitude of the effect of the one factor is just as much the magnitude of the other, as it is likewise the magnitude of the whole. {{{ 329 }}} Because they have their qualitative determinateness over against each other, they simply cannot be pulled apart from one another; each has sense only with respect to the other; insofar, therefore, as one of them had a surplus beyond the other, just so far would it stand in no reference to the other and would not be at hand. — Suppose it assumed that at some time the one is greater than the other: were it, as the greater, to stand in reference to what is smaller, then what has been said above sets in, namely that it would gain preponderance absolutely, and the other would vanish. It is a very simple consideration that if, for instance, as is pretended, the centripetal force of the body is to increase as the body approaches the perihelion while the centrifugal force is to decrease by just as much, then the latter can no longer wrench the body loose from the former and remove it once more from its central body; on the contrary, since preponderance has once fallen to the former, the other is overpowered, and with accelerating speed the body is borne towards its central body. Just as, conversely, when the centrifugal force holds the upper hand at the infinite nearness of the aphelion, it is equally contradictory that in the aphelion itself it should now be overpowered by the weaker one. — It is evident that it would take an alien force to bring about this reversal; which means that the now accelerated, now retarded speed of the motion cannot be cognized from those factors which were assumed precisely in order to explain this very difference.

The same ratio was later applied to attractive force and repulsive force in order to grasp how bodies differ in density; and the inverse ratio between sensibility and irritability, too, was supposed to serve for grasping, from the diversity that these two factors of life exhibit, the differing determinations of the whole, {{{ 330 }}} of health, as also the diversity among the genera of living beings. But the confusion and the galimatias in which this explaining entangled itself through the uncritical use of these conceptual determinations has had the consequence that this formalism appears to have been soon abandoned again, whereas in science, and especially in physical astronomy, it is carried on in its full extent.

— With regard to ***absolute indifference***, the fundamental concept ***of Spinoza's substance***, it may still be recalled that this concept is the last determination of being before being becomes essence, but that it does not itself reach essence. Absolute indifference contains the ***absolute unity*** of the specifically self-subsistent in their highest determination, as that of thinking and of being, and therein at large of all the other modifications of these attributes. Yet with this only the absolute that ***is in itself***, not the absolute that ***is for itself***, has been thought. Or it is the ***external reflection*** which halts at the thought that the specifically self-subsistent are ***in themselves*** or in the absolute ***the same and one***, that their difference is merely an indifferent one, no difference in itself. What is still lacking here consists in this, that this reflection should not be the ***external*** reflection of the ***thinking subject***, but that it should itself be cognized, and indeed as the self-subsistents' own determination and movement to sublate their difference and to be one not merely in themselves but in their qualitative difference, whereby then the ***concept of essence***, not to have the negative outside it but to be at its own self the absolute negativity, indifference towards its own self just as much as of its otherness towards itself.
