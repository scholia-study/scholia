## c) Reality

Being-in-itself and being-for-other are the moments, or the inner differences, of existence. They are being and nothing as differentiated ***within*** existence. Or, through this difference existence is not dissolved; rather these moments are held essentially in the unity which existence is; for they themselves, as was just shown, are these unities.

Existence itself is at first an immediate, simple unity of being and nothing. So far as {{{ 54 }}} being and nothing have determined themselves more closely within it as the moments just considered, it is no longer in the first form of immediacy, but is reflected existence; it is existence insofar as it has determined itself as being-in-itself and as being-for-other, and is the unity of these as its moments. As this reflected existence it is ***reality***.
