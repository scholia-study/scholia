## {{{ 75 }}} c.) Negation

1. Existence, ***determinate*** being, as the unity of its moments, of being-in-itself and of being-for-other, was above ***reality***.

Determinateness that has become free is, likewise as the unity of determination and of constitution, ***quality***. Over against reality stands ***negation***. Quality makes up the middle and the transition between reality and negation; it contains these two in simple unity. But in negation non-being comes forward as the truth into which reality has passed over.

To the ***Reelle*** there also stands opposed the ***Ideelle***, and to the ***negative*** the ***positive***. The opposition of the Reelle and the Ideelle will emerge below, with being-for-itself; the opposition of the positive and the negative, however, belongs among the proper determinations of reflection, or is the opposition as it is in essence, and comes forward there. — Insofar as position in general is set over against negation, this position means nothing other than reality.

Just as reality is the same as existence, insofar as existence has in it the moments of being-in-itself and of being-for-other, so ***negation*** too may be taken for reflected determinateness, namely according to what has emerged as the truth of that determinateness, namely to be the unity of the ought and of restriction.
