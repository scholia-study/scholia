## {{{ 315 }}} C. The Measureless

Measure is magnitude that is in itself, which withstands the externality and indifference of immediate quantum and maintains itself against it. But this indifferent self-subsistence of the specific measures rests upon quantitative difference, and is therefore capable of ascending and descending along the scale of quantum upon which the ratios change; a something or a quality is driven out beyond itself into the ***measureless***, and founders through the mere change of its quantum. Magnitude is the indifferent external constitution at which an existence can be seized and through which it can be destroyed.

The qualitative relation passes over into merely quantitative ratios which have no negative unity and are thereby no qualitative ratios; the change that has set in along with them is not a change of quality. But conversely this at first indifferent externality of the ratio becomes once again a qualifying determinateness ***and so forth into infinity***. To that extent the bad infinity of the infinite progress is at hand. — The measureless consists in the merely quantitative into which a measure passes over; quantum as such is the measureless. But since conversely the measureless quantitative ratio itself becomes once more a specific one, the measureless thus sublates itself again at its own self. What is at hand, then, is {{{ 316 }}} not only the negation of the specific relation but also the negation of the quantitative going-on itself. The ***infinite*** is this negation of both moments; it is the absolute determination which they lack. The specific relation is at first what is determined in itself, because as a relation it has difference at its own self, and because its sides too are not immediate magnitudes, not units as in the immediate direct ratio, but specified, posited determinations of quantity. But this being-determined in itself does not hold; it continues itself with its other and passes over into the merely quantitative difference; a difference which consists in immediate quanta not specified through the negative unity; this difference, however, rather goes back into the specific relation. Neither of the two, then, is absolute being-determined. This infinity accordingly consists at large in the negation of both sides. But at the same time this negation is not the beyond of each, an infinity situated outside them or merely their inner infinity, but their infinity posited at their own selves. — The ***qualitative*** infinity, namely, was the breaking-forth of the infinite at the finite, the immediate transition and the vanishing of the this-side in its beyond. Quantitative infinity, by contrast, is the continuity of quantum, a continuity of it out beyond itself. The qualitatively finite ***becomes*** the infinite; the quantitatively finite has its beyond at its own self, and ***points beyond itself***. But the infinity of the specification of measure is at its own self this totality, which does not have the other as a beyond of itself but posits, in its negation that goes out beyond itself, only this: that it is totality, that it does ***not*** have or posit an ***other*** over against itself. The specific relation is the negative unity of quantities which {{{ 317 }}} are determined through it; as this negative unity it is the self-subsistent, indifferent subsistence. But that into which it has specified itself are determinations of quantity; it therefore does not pass over into the quantitative ratio but only refers itself therein to itself; and measurelessness, or its negation, namely the quantitative aspect of the relation, is its negative reference to itself. Its infinity is accordingly the sublating not of itself, but of its being an other; this is the negation of it as that through which it is. The qualitative relation, then, as reference of specific quantities, thereby makes itself external, makes itself into a subsistence devoid of quality; but it is precisely this its negation through which it is what makes up its specific constitution. — This is its nature, but it is at the same time at hand within the infinite progress. Namely, the specific relation, as indifferent towards itself, repels itself away from itself and makes itself into another specific relation. This is another quantitative ratio; both are therefore indifferent towards each other, and their qualitative reference is sublated. But precisely thereby they are only externally distinguished; the reference to the other is thus a reference to what is not distinguished from it, to itself, as to its negation. This repelling of the specific away from itself is its self-subsistence; this consists, then, in referring itself to its other, which is only quantitatively distinguished, in such a way that in its negation it is what it is. — So conversely the quantitative determination overturns into specific determination, but because the latter is at its own self the quantitative, the quantitative maintains itself in its becoming-other, and is thus in its constitution what it is according to its determination: to be in its negation what it is.

{{{ 318 }}} The infinite progress as such consists only in this, that the specific self-subsistent passes over into the quantitative and the latter into the former, and that in this passing-over the passing-over sublates itself, inasmuch as the new relation is once again as an immediate, indifferent one. But infinity itself is the unity of the qualitative and the quantitative, a unity that repels itself away from itself and immediately is only this repelling itself. — The quantitative and the qualitative are in immediate measure an immediate unity. But they are just as much qualitatively opposed to each other, each is what the other is not; thus in the infinite progress they are the determinations of the relations over against one another. But in this qualitative determination each of them is at the same time, simply at its own self, the passing-over into its other. This passing-over is, considered according to form, the same through which quality at large becomes quantity and quantity becomes quality; namely, the specific aspect of the relation, as excluding repulsion, coalesces into one with what is excluded, or becomes attraction and thereby quantity. Conversely quantity, as externality in itself, which within the progress becomes the externality of its own self, has thereby returned into itself, and quantum is, as what it is in itself, quality.

But what is at hand is not only this passing-over of quality and quantity into each other, which shows itself at them as the determinations of being; rather it comes back here in the ***reference*** of the two; their passing-over into each other happens ***upon the ground of their unity***. It is a passing-over which is at the same time the recoil of itself against itself, and which sublates itself. The qualitative, namely, passes over into the quantitative at first as into its other; but here the latter is itself as such what is in itself the passing-over into the qualitative {{{ 319 }}}; — and so reciprocally. Each, then, passes over into its other, which, however, sublates itself at its own self and is only the becoming towards its other. Inasmuch as each thus becomes an other, ***it rather sublates only this, being an other; in its change it therefore comes together only with itself***.

Inasmuch, then, as the passing-over of specifically self-subsistent things into each other is at the same time the negating of this passing-over as a becoming-other, that which changes is no self-subsistence; the alteration is only the change of a ***state***, and what passes over remains ***in itself the same***. The quantitative reference, the specific aspect of the difference of the qualitative and the quantitative, is sublated in this infinity. Just as at first the immediate measure-relation sublated itself in specific self-subsistence, so has the latter now likewise vanished, inasmuch as it is reduced to a ***state***. It was supposed to be at large the sublated immediate measure-relation; the quantitative within it was supposed to be something qualitatively determined, and, as excluding negative unity with itself, a being-for-itself absolutely determined in itself. But this self-subsistence, as passing over into the quantitative, which within it was supposed to be only sublated, has reduced itself to a moment. But inasmuch as the passing-over has sublated itself at large, self-subsistence in its passing-over has come together only with itself. Through the fact that in passing over it sublates its passing-over, it is for the first time genuine self-subsistence. The self-subsistent posits itself as self-subsistent in one and the same respect in which it sublates its specific quality; for only thereby is it the genuine reference to itself.

{{{ 320 }}} What is at hand, then, is the self-subsistence which ***mediates itself with itself through its negation***. Specific self-subsistence, the excluding reference to itself, has its indifference towards itself, the quantitative, as its negation, for its mediating moment, and as this return into itself it is absolute self-subsistence.
