## {{{ 93 }}} a.) the moment of its being-in-itself,

up. This being-in-itself, however, is determined further than it was in existence. The being-in-itself of existence is inert, gets determined, and does not maintain itself against the limit and against being limited; just as the being-in-itself, as determination, is indeed equal to its restriction, or becomes its own restriction, yet in such a way that in it it is simply the non-being of itself. In the being-in-itself of existence being-for-other is likewise sublated; but this sublating consists rather only in the distinguishing and separating of the two from one another, and this separating belongs to an external reflection. — Determination, or the ought, and restriction are indeed ***in itself*** one and the same determinateness, which is however posited the one time as being-in-itself over against non-being, and the other time as this non-being or as absolute otherness; they are the same only ***in itself***, and this because they have not yet, in their very differentiatedness, sublated themselves in their own selves and are not yet the same ***for itself***.

The being-in-itself of being-for-itself, by contrast, has the determination of this sublating; being-for-itself is thereby, even in the differentiation, in existence, the unity which the ought and the restriction, or the infinite progress, only is in itself. It is existence closed within itself, infinite reference to itself. Insofar as it is reference to an other, it is reference to it only as to something sublated; in the other, therefore, it is reference only to itself.
