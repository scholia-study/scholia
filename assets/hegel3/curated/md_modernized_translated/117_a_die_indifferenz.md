## A. Indifference

The specific self-subsistent is mediated with its own self through its negation, and so it is no longer specific but absolute self-subsistence. — At first, to be sure, the specific is immediate being at large, the qualitative, whereas the negation of it is the quantitative, whereby it returns into itself. Yet the quantitative is just as much quality, and thereby something specific over against quality as such, while quality is its negation; the two therefore have only an indeterminate sense left over against each other. — Further, the specific is no longer to be called a self-subsistent; the qualitative and the quantitative are downright nothing but moments any more. The absolute self-subsistent, which is their infinite, in which they are sublated, is their unity insofar as it comes forth out of them. It is not their becoming, — this was the nodal line and the infinite advance of them, for it is not their unity in which they would still have their qualitative difference. Nor has it gone back into the determination of their ***immediate*** unity; for the moments are one as ***in difference*** from each other, or their unity is not at hand in which they would be one only as sublated, but rather they are this, to continue themselves the one in the other. Their unity is therefore {{{ 322 }}} the unity ***that is in them and indifferent towards them***, their ***indifference***.

This indifference of the qualitative and the quantitative is the indifferentness of both determinations at large, each of which in the other does not pass over but only comes together with its own self. For that reason, however, it is further also only the self-subsistence that ***is in itself***, not yet the one that ***is for itself***. Since it is the ***indifferentness*** towards the difference of the two determinations, it does not yet have that difference at its own self. It is a unity that is in itself and indifferent, so it is posited in the determination of quantity, and the qualitative difference stands over against it, as that towards which it is indifferent. — Or insofar as it is the infinite that ***results*** from the going-into-itself of the qualitative and the quantitative, it has this movement ***behind it***, and is referred to it. — What indifference thus lacks is this, to have at its own self the qualitative unity with itself, the absolute negativity. It stands in the ***determination*** of indifferentness towards the negative; hence not the absolutely self-subsistent.
