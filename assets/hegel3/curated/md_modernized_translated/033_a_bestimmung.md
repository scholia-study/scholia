## a.) Determination

As the limit turned back into itself, determinateness is ***in itself***; it is the determinate as referring only to itself; as the non-being of the other, in such a way that the determinate is not thereby limited itself.

Determinateness on this side may more precisely be called ***determination***. In its determination something rests within its own self; in determination it is what it ***ought*** to be. There is indeed an other outside it, yet in such a way that something is not what it is in this reference to an other; rather it is taken back out of the reference to an other into itself. ***Limit*** as ***determination*** is no longer the referring middle between the something and an other; it belongs to the something alone, which does not hold it in common, but it is the something's reference to its own self.
