## Unity of Being and Nothing

***The pure being and the pure nothing are the same***. What the truth is, is neither the being nor the nothing, but rather that the being into nothing, and the nothing into being, — is not passing over, — but has passed over. Yet the truth is equally not their undifferentiatedness; it lies rather in this, that they are absolutely distinguished, while each of them, with the same immediacy, disappears in its opposite. Their truth, then, is this movement in which the one immediately disappears in the other; ***becoming***; a movement wherein both stand distinguished, though through a difference that has dissolved itself just as immediately.
