## {{{ 60 }}} B. Determinateness

Existence is being with a non-being. It is being, simple reference to its own self, yet no longer as immediacy, but as negative reference to its own self, and this is what makes up its being. So taken it is something. Here, then, the moment of non-being turns itself outward on existence.

Something, as an existent, ***first of all*** distinguishes its moment of negativity from its own self, as its ***limit***.

***Thereupon***, however, the limit shows itself as the essentiality of something, and is its ***determinateness***, which differentiates itself into determinateness as that which is in itself, into ***determination***, and into determinateness as that which is for-other, into ***constitution***. Determinateness, as the reference of these moments, is ***quality***.

***Third***, however, quality passes over through constitution into ***alteration***.
