## {{{ 21 }}} First Section. Determinateness

(Quality.)

---

Being is the immediate that is indeterminate; it stands free of the first determinateness over against essence, and free of the second within its own self. Such being, void of reflection, is what being immediately is in and for itself.

Being indeterminate, it is being without quality; but the character of indeterminateness falls to it only in contrast with what is determinate or qualitative. Determinate being as such therefore confronts being at large; or, with that, its very indeterminateness makes up its quality. It will therefore show itself that the ***first*** being, determinate in itself, thus

***Secondly*** is existence, or that it passes over into ***existence***; but that this, as finite being, sublates itself, and into the infinite reference of being to its own self,

***Thirdly***, into ***being-for-itself***, passes over.
