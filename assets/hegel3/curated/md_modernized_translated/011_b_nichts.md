## B. Nothing

***Nothing, the pure nothing***; it is simple sameness with itself, an utter emptiness, lack of determination and lack of content; undifferentiatedness inside it. — To the extent that intuiting or thinking may be brought up at this point, whether something or ***nothing*** is what gets intuited or thought is taken to make a difference. Intuiting or thinking nothing has a significance, then; nothing is present in our intuiting or thinking; or, better, it {{{ 23 }}} is that empty intuiting and thinking itself; and it is the same empty intuiting or thinking as pure being is. — So nothing carries the same determination, or rather the same want of determination, and with that it is out and out the same as what pure ***being*** is.
