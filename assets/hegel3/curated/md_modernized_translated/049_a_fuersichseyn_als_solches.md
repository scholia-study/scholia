## {{{ 92 }}} A. Being-for-itself as Such

What has now resulted is the general concept of being-for-itself. Within it there fall apart the moments of its infinite ***reference to itself*** and of ***being-for-one***. Taken as this reflected being-for-itself, it is ***ideality***. Taken, however, as the unity of its moments, a unity that in its own self turns back into itself, it is the ***one***.
