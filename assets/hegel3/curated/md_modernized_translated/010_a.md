## A. Being

***Being, pure being***, — without any further determination. Within its indeterminate immediacy it is nothing but self-same, and neither is it unlike an other; it holds no diversity inside itself, nor any facing outward. Should some determination or content be distinguished within it, or should it through such content be posited as distinguished from an other, it would then not be maintained in its purity. It is indeterminateness and emptiness in their purity. — In it there is ***nothing*** to intuit, supposing intuiting may be spoken of at this point; or else it is just this pure, empty intuiting itself. There is just as little anything in it to be thought, or it is, in the same way, only this empty thinking. In truth being, the indeterminate immediate, is ***nothing***, and no more and no less than nothing.
