## Remark

The expression our language has for the question about quality, an expression that at first appears peculiar, ***what kind of*** thing something may be, chiefly brings out the moment considered here. Determinateness is voiced in it not as something that is in itself, but as something that only is ***for one***. This idealistic expression does not, in so asking, inquire what thing A may be ***for another*** thing B, nor what this human being may be for some other one; — but rather, ***what kind of thing, what kind of human being, this*** is? in such a way that the being-for-one is at once withdrawn into the thing, into the human being himself, or that the one ***which is***, and the one ***for which*** it is, come to a single selfsame thing, — an identity that is now to be considered in the case of ideality.
