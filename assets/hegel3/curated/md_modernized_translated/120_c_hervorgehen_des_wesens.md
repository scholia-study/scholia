## {{{ 331 }}} C. Emergence of Essence

Absolute indifference consisted in this, that it is the ***specifically*** self-subsistent which mediates itself with its own self through its ***negation***, and, purified by this negation, is the ***absolutely*** self-subsistent. It is thus the indifference which ***presupposes*** the specific self-subsistent and the negation of it, and is thereby determined as what ***is in itself***.

***In the first place***, this ***determinateness*** is essential to it; coming back out of those moments, indifference, as the ***determination*** or as being-in-itself, is distinguished from them as from its ***constitution*** or its being-for-other.

***Secondly***, however, this determinateness has more closely turned out to be the inverse ratio of factors. For since it is essentially at the absolutely self-subsistent, that specific self-subsistence and the negation of it are to be considered only ***in their reference to it***; or rather they are only ***moments of this their unity***.

***Thirdly***, in this determination of being factors of the determinateness of essence, their incomplete unity, their inverse ratio, has sublated itself. Or the ***determinateness*** of absolute indifference has shown itself to be the ***negative reference to its own self***. Determinateness is the difference and the mutual negation of the factors; but the negation is no longer the negation of something immediate, such that it would itself be only an immediate one and determined through an other, but rather {{{ 332 }}} the negation of the other is negation of one's own specific self-subsistence over against the other; the negative reference to the other, which as such is a being-determined or a passing over, is rather a sublating of one's own determinateness, the passing over into one's own self; negation of the negative.

The ***determinateness*** of the indifference that ***is in itself*** is with this the ***infinity*** of its self-subsistence, the ***absolute negativity***. It is therefore not the ***specific*** self-subsistence which mediates itself with its own self through its negation, but it is the ***absolute*** self-subsistence, which is only this, to mediate itself with itself through its negativity that is equal to it. — In this, to be sure, a ***first*** absolute self-subsistence is enunciated over against the ***other***, which is as mediated with itself. To that extent the former is the immediate one, or the one that only ***is in itself***; to that extent it is the ***specific***, a ***determinate*** self-subsistence at large. But the result is precisely this, ***first***, that the self-subsistence which ***is in itself***, indifference as such, is not absolute but is itself determined, and that it is this essentially, but that its determinateness is the negation of the negation, hence a self-subsistence which ***is for itself***, one that contains that self-subsistence which ***is in itself*** as something sublated, as a ***moment***. There is therefore at hand the self-subsistence which is this, namely to be, in the complete negation of its own self, that is to say in the self-subsistence of otherness, which means of the first immediate, simple reference to itself, and hence at the same time negative reference to itself.

With this being has absolutely gone back into itself; it has sublated its determinateness of being the simple immediate, and has recollected itself.

Being is first ***quality***, which according to its determination is the being determined in itself, a determinateness which is, in that it is the negation of the {{{ 333 }}} other. ***Quantity***, according to its determination, is the indifferent determinateness, which is not the negation of an other but is indifferent towards it and to which the other is external. It first showed itself at them, in their immediacy, that quality passes over into quantity, but quantity into quality. Their unity is ***measure***; whereas before each was posited for itself, in measure they have entered into the reference, at first according to their determinateness over against each other, hence only in a relative unity. Through the movement of measure, however, there is ***posited*** what they, each considered for itself, showed themselves to be ***in themselves***, and out of this their absolute unity emerges.

This unity of the qualitative and the quantitative, at first an indifferentness towards the one and towards the other, is essentially not indifference ***as towards the one and the other***, but indifferentness ***towards its own self***, a falling apart within itself. This is the quantitativity of indifference, but just as much its qualitativity; for as indifference it is a self-subsistence that ***is in itself and immediate***; but precisely this being-in-itself, or the immediacy, is ***determinateness***; hence the negation of indifference, an ***other*** self-subsistent. The negative reference of the two self-subsistents to each other makes up their qualitative determinateness, in which that first immediacy is thus posited only as determinateness and is thereby sublated; and since this sublating is not merely a sublating of immediacy but of immediacy as negation, it is negation of the negation; the determinateness which is the self-subsistence of the whole; the ***negative indifference***.

***This unity with itself of determinateness and of indifference towards it is the truth of being***. It is the simple {{{ 334 }}} being, indifferent immediacy, as mediated with itself through its negation, through its indifference towards its own self; or it is mediation as pure equality with itself, as simple immediacy, — being which is only this, to have converged with itself in its negation and thereby to be pure being.

Being as this being that is downright recollected is ***essence***. The truth of being is thus to be something immediate as absolutely sublated immediacy. It is only as negative reference to itself; thus it repels itself from itself, this repelling is its non-being, and so it is the ***first*** immediacy, ***qualitative*** being. But this non-being is indifference towards itself, the sublating of itself, and so it is at first quantitative being; — this is already ***in itself*** the return into itself; insofar, namely, as that first is determined as ***non-being***; but it is as yet only quantitative being insofar as that first is ***immediate*** being. Quantitative being makes itself into measure and as measure into the indifference that is for itself; this indifference is precisely this, to determine immediate ***being*** as a ***non-being***, and ***being-in-itself*** as ***determinateness***. ***Being***, in that it is this, not to be what it is and to be what it is not; — ***as this simple negativity of its own self***, is ***essence***.
