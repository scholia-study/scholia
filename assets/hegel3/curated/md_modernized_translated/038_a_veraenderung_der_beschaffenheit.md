## a) Alteration of Constitution

Alteration at first falls only into constitution; determination is the limit withdrawn from the reference to an other; constitution, by contrast, is the side open to the other, or the side in which the other is as other. To that extent there is still a being-within-itself present in determination, one that is distinct from constitution and from alteration; the something is still present and surrenders only the one of its sides. — Becoming is here more closely determined as ***alteration*** for this reason too, that it is not purely abstract moments that stand in reference, but such as are themselves unities of each other, whereby determination {{{ 71 }}} maintains itself in the passing over, and whereby here not a vanishing but only a becoming-other is posited.

At first, then, it is constitution that alters in such a way that it becomes only ***another constitution***; for a constitution is ***a determinate one***, and determinateness passes over into alteration. But it is this alteration of determinateness itself that is here considered more closely; determinateness passes over into alteration because it is constitution.

It is therefore ***the*** constitution as such that alters; not ***a*** constitution, in such a way that constitution as such would remain; hence it must not so much be said that it alters, but rather it is itself the alteration.
