## {{{ 19 }}} General Division of Being

Being is ***first*** determined over against an other;

***Secondly***, it is determined within its own self;

***Thirdly***, it returns out of the determining into itself, throws away this preliminariness of dividing, and restores itself to the indeterminateness and immediacy wherein it is able to be a beginning.

In its ***first*** determination being separates itself off over against ***essence***, as was indicated already.

This division is here, as was recalled in the Introduction about such divisions generally, a preliminary citation; its determinations have first to arise out of the movement of being itself, and to justify themselves therein. About its divergence from the usual enumeration of the categories — quantity, quality, relation and modality, that is — nothing further need be recalled here, since the whole exposition will display what diverges at large from the usual {{{ 20 }}} order and meaning of the categories.

Only this much can be remarked more closely, that elsewhere the determination of ***quantity*** is listed before ***quality***, — and this — like most such things — without further ground. It has been shown already that the start is taken with being as such, and hence with qualitative being. From the comparison of quality with quantity it readily appears that quality is by nature the first; for quantity is only quality that has become negative. Magnitude is that determinateness which is no longer at one with being but already differentiated from it, quality sublated and turned indifferent. It carries within it the alterability of being, without the matter itself — the being whose determination it is — being altered thereby; whereas the qualitative determinateness is one with its being, does not go out beyond it nor stand within it, but is its immediate limitedness. Quality is therefore, as the ***immediate*** determinateness, the first, and the beginning is to be made with it.

***Measure*** is a ***relation***, but not relation at large, rather the determinate relation of quality and quantity to one another. It can also count as a modality, should one wish; since modality is meant to make up no determination of content any longer, but to bear merely on content's reference to thinking, to the subjective. Measure contains the dissolution of the content, its reference to an other; it constitutes the transition into ***essence***.

The ***third division*** falls within the section on quality.
