## c. The Reciprocal Action of Things

Essentially the thing-in-itself _concretely_ _exists_; the external immediacy and the determinateness belong to its being-in-itself, or else to its reflection-into-itself. Thereby the thing in itself is a thing having properties, and thereby there are several things which differentiate themselves from one another not through some respect foreign to them, but through their own selves. These several diverse things stand in essential reciprocal action by way of their properties; the property is this reciprocal reference itself, and the thing is nothing apart from it; the mutual determining, the middle term of the things-in-themselves which as extremes were to stay indifferent {{{ 11.333 }}} towards this reference of theirs, is itself that reflection identical with itself, the thing-in-itself which those extremes were meant to be. Thinghood is with this brought down to the form of indeterminate identity with itself, an identity having its essentiality only in its property. Whenever, then, talk is of a thing, or of things at large, without the determinate property, their difference is a merely indifferent, quantitative one. One and the same that gets regarded as _one_ thing can quite as well be made into several things, or be regarded as several things; here is an _external_ _severing_ or _joining_. – A book is a thing, and every one of its leaves is a thing too, and likewise every little scrap of its leaves, and so on into infinity. The determinateness whereby _a_ thing is only _this_ thing lies solely in its properties. By them it differentiates itself from other things, since property is negative reflection and the differentiating; only in its property, accordingly, does the thing have on itself its difference from others. Property is the difference reflected into itself, whereby the thing, in its positedness, i.e. in its reference to another, is at once indifferent towards the other and towards its own reference. For the thing without its properties there consequently remains nothing beyond abstract being-in-itself, an unessential compass and an external gathering-together. Genuine being-in-itself is being-in-itself in its positedness; and this is the property. With this, _thinghood_ _has_ _passed_ _over_ _into_ _property_.

As the extreme that is in itself, the thing was to comport itself over against the property, and this latter was to make up the middle term between the things standing in reference. Yet such reference is what the things meet in as _the_ _reflection_ _repelling_ _itself_ _from_ _its_ _own_ _self_, what they are differentiated and referred in. This difference of theirs and their reference is one reflection and _one_ _continuity_ of it. Herewith the things themselves fall merely into this continuity, which is the property, and vanish as subsisting extremes that would have a concrete existence apart from the property.

The _property_, which was to make up the _reference_ of the self-subsistent extremes, is therefore _the_ _self-subsistent_ _itself_. The things, by contrast, are the unessential. Something _essential_ they are only as the reflection which, in differentiating itself, refers itself to itself; but that is the property. Property, then, is not what is sublated in the thing, nor a mere moment of it; rather the thing is in truth nothing but that unessential compass, which is indeed negative unity, yet only in the way the one of something is, namely an _immediate_ one. If earlier the thing was determined as unessential compass insofar as it is made into such by an external abstraction that leaves the property out of it, by now this abstraction has come about through the passing over of the thing-in-itself into the property itself, though with reversed worth, so {{{ 11.334 }}} that, whereas in that abstracting the abstract thing without its property still hovered before one as the essential while the property counted as an external determination, here the thing as such determines itself through its own self into an indifferent external form of the property. – Set free by now, then, from the indeterminate and impotent _combination_ which the one of the thing is, the property is what makes up the thing's _subsistence_; a _self-subsistent_ _matter_. – Being simple continuity with itself, it has form on it at first only as _diversity_; hence there are _manifold_ self-subsistent matters of the sort, and _the_ _thing_ _consists_ _of_ _them_.
