## B. Finitude

a. Something _and_ an other; to begin with, each is indifferent toward the other; an other is likewise an immediate existent, a something; negation therefore falls outside the two of them. Over against its _being-for_ _other_, something is _in_ _itself_. Determinateness belongs, however, to its _in-itself_ as well, and is

b. its _determination_, which passes over no less into _constitution_; and constitution, being one with that determination, makes up the immanent and simultaneously negated being-for-other, the _limit_ of something, which

c. is the something's own immanent determination, whereby something is the _finite_.

Within the first division, where _existence_ at large came under consideration, existence, as first taken up, bore the determination of _what_ _has_ _being_. For that reason the moments of its unfolding — quality and something — are likewise of affirmative determination. Here, on the other hand, it is the negative determination resident in {{{ 21.105 }}} existence that unfolds; there it was still only negation at large, _first_ negation, whereas now it stands determined up to the point of the something's _being-within-itself_, up to negation of negation.
