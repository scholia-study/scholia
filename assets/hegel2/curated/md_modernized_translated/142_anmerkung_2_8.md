## Remark 2

The determination of opposition has likewise been made into a principle, the so-called _principle_ _of_ _the_ _excluded_ _third_.

_Something_ _is_ _either_ _A_ _or_ _NotA_; _there_ _is_ _no_ _third_.

This principle states _first_ that everything is an _opposite_, something _determined_ either as positive or as negative. – An important principle, whose necessity rests on this, that identity passes over into diversity and the latter into opposition. Only, it is not usually understood in this sense; ordinarily it is supposed to mean that of all predicates a thing possesses either the predicate itself or the non-being of that predicate. What is opposite here signifies merely the lack, or rather _indeterminateness_; and so insignificant is the principle that it is not [worth] the trouble to state it. If the determinations sweet, green, and square are taken – and all predicates are supposed to be taken – and it is then said of spirit that it is either sweet or else not sweet, green or else not green, and so forth, then this is a triviality leading nowhere. Determinateness, the predicate, is related to a something; the something is determinate, so the principle declares; now the principle is supposed essentially to contain this, that determinateness determine itself more closely, that it become determinateness _in_ _itself_, become opposition. Instead of that, however, in that trivial sense it passes over from determinateness merely to its non-being in general, back to indeterminateness.

The principle of the excluded third is to be distinguished further from the principle of identity or of contradiction considered above, which ran: there is not {{{ 11.286 }}} anything that is _at_ _once_ A _and_ NotA. It states that there _is_ not a something which is _neither_ A _nor_ NotA, that no third is to be had which would be indifferent towards the opposition. In fact, though, within this very principle _there_ _is_ the third that is indifferent towards the opposition, namely A itself is at hand in it. Neither + A nor - A is this A, and it is quite as much + A as - A. – That something which was supposed to be either + A or NotA is herewith related to + A no less than to Not A; and again, since it is related to A, it is supposed _not_ to stand related to NotA, just as _not_ to A when it is related to NotA. The something itself, then, is the third that was supposed to be excluded. Inasmuch as the opposed determinations are in the something just as much posited as, in this positing, sublated, the third – which here has the shape of a dead something – taken more deeply is that unity of reflection into which, as into its ground, the opposition goes back.
