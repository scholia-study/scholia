## b. The Real Mechanical Process

Into _rest_ the mechanical process passes over. For the determinateness which the object gains through it is only an _external_ one. This rest is for the object something just as external, since rest is the determinateness set over against the object's _efficacy_, while either of the two leaves the object indifferent; rest may therefore just as well be looked upon as brought about by an _external_ cause, as indifferent as it was to the object to be something that acts.

Since now, further, the determinateness is something _posited_, and the concept of the object has _travelled_ _back_ _through_ _the_ _mediation_ _to_ _itself_, the object carries determinateness at it as one reflected into itself. The objects, and the mechanical process itself, have therefore from now on a more closely determined relation within that process. Merely diverse they are not; they stand _determinately_ _differentiated_ from each other. The result of the formal process, determinationless rest on the one side, is on the other side, thanks to the determinateness reflected into itself, the _distribution_ _of_ _the_ _opposition_ that the object carries at it at large among several objects that comport themselves mechanically towards each other. The object, which on the one side is the determinationless, behaving _unelastically_ and _non-self-subsistently_, possesses on the other side a _self-subsistence_ impenetrable to others. The objects now also carry _towards_ _each_ _other_ this more determinate opposition of _self-subsistent_ _singularity_ and _non-self-subsistent_ _universality_. – The closer difference may be grasped as a merely _quantitative_ one, of the differing magnitude of _mass_ in the corporeal sphere, or of _intensity_, or in a variety of other ways. In general, however, it is not to be kept fixed in that abstraction alone; as objects the two are also _positive_ self-subsistent ones.

The first moment of this real _process_ is now, as before, _communication_. By the _stronger_ the _weaker_ can be seized and pervaded only insofar as the weaker takes the stronger up and makes up One _sphere_ along with it. As in the material sphere the weak is safe against what is disproportionately strong (a linen sheet hanging loose in the air is not shot through by a musket ball; a feeble organic receptivity is assailed less by strong stimulants than by weak ones), so a wholly feeble spirit is more secure {{{ 12.141 }}} against a strong one than is a spirit that stands closer to it; imagine something altogether stupid and ignoble, and neither high understanding nor nobility can make any impression upon it; the sole consistent means _against_ reason is to refuse all involvement with it. – Insofar as the non-self-subsistent is unable to go together with the self-subsistent, so that no communication is possible between the two, neither can the latter offer any _resistance_, that is, it cannot specify for itself the universal communicated. – Did they not stand within One sphere, an infinite judgment would be their reference to each other, and between them no process at all could occur.

_Resistance_ is the closer moment in the one object's being overpowered by the other, in that it is the moment with which the distribution of the communicated universal begins, and with it the positing of self-referring negativity, of the singularity that is to be restored. Resistance gets _overpowered_ insofar as its determinateness proves not _adequate_ to that communicated universal which the object has taken up and which is meant to singularize itself within the object. The relative non-self-subsistence of that resistance shows itself in this, that its _singularity_ has not the _capacity_ _for_ _what_ _has_ _been_ _communicated_ and is therefore burst apart by it, since it cannot set itself up as _subject_ at this universal, cannot make the universal into its _predicate_. – Only on this second side is _violence_ against an object something _alien_ to it. _Power_ turns into _violence_ through this, that as an objective universality it stands _identical_ with the object's _nature_, while its determinateness or negativity is not the object's own _negative_ _reflection_ into itself, by which the object is a singular. Insofar as the object's negativity is not reflected into itself at the power, and the power is no reference of the object to itself, power counts towards it as merely _abstract_ negativity, manifesting itself as a going under.

What is called _fate_ is power in the shape of _objective_ _universality_ and of violence _against_ the object; – a concept falling within mechanism insofar as fate is termed _blind_, that is, insofar as its _objective_ _universality_ goes uncognized by the subject in the subject's own specific ownness. – To offer a few remarks on this: the fate of what lives is at large the _genus_, manifesting itself through the perishability of living individuals, who in their _actual_ _singularity_ do not have it as genus. Merely living natures, being mere objects, have no fate, any more than the remaining things of a lower stage; whatever befalls them is a contingency; but _in_ _their_ _concept_ _as_ _objects_ they are _external_ _to_ _themselves_; hence the alien power of fate is nothing whatever except their _own_ _immediate_ _nature_, is externality and contingency itself. Fate in the proper sense belongs to self-consciousness alone; for self-consciousness, being _free_, is on that account, in the _singularity_ of the I that is its own, outright {{{ 12.142 }}} _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, and can set itself over against its objective universality and _estrange_ itself from it. But by this very separation it stirs up against itself the mechanical relation of a fate. If such a fate is to be able to hold violence over it, then, it must have conferred on itself one determinateness or another against the essential universality, must have carried out a _deed_. Herewith it has made itself into something _particular_, and this existence, as abstract universality, is at once the open side for the communication of that essence which has been estranged from it; at this side it gets torn into the process. Blameless is the people that has done no deed; wrapped in the objective, ethical universality and dissolved therein, it lacks that individuality which moves what is unmoved, which confers on itself a determinateness directed outwards and an abstract universality cut off from the objective one, whereby, however, the subject too becomes something divested of its essence, an _object_, and has entered into the relation of _externality_ towards its own nature, the relation of mechanism.
