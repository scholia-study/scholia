## B. The Mechanical Process

Should objects be regarded solely as totalities sealed off within themselves, no acting of one upon another is possible for them. In this determination they amount to the same as the _monads_, which precisely on that score were thought without any effect whatever upon each other. But just this is why the concept of a monad is a deficient reflection. For, to start with, the monad is a _determinate_ representation of its _totality_ _which_ _merely_ _is_ _in_ _itself_; as a _certain_ _degree_ of development and of the _positedness_ of the world it represents, it is something _determinate_; and since it is the totality shut up within itself, it is indifferent towards this determinateness as well; the determinateness, accordingly, is not its own but one _posited_ through _another_ _object_. _Secondly_, it is _an_ _immediate_ at large, insofar as it is supposed to be _a_ _merely_ _representing_ _one_; its reference to itself is on that account _abstract_ _universality_, and through this it is _an_ _existence_ _standing_ _open_ _to_ _others_. – To secure the freedom of substance it does not suffice to represent substance as a totality _which_ _is_ _complete_ _in_ _itself_, _having_ _nothing_ _from_ _without_ _to_ receive. On the contrary, precisely this conceptless, merely representing reference to itself is a _passivity_ towards an other. – Likewise the _determinateness_, whether it be grasped as the determinateness of something _which_ _is_ or of something _representing_, as a _degree_ of a development proper to it and coming from within, is something _external_; – the _degree_ that the development attains has its _limit_ _in_ _an_ _other_. To defer the reciprocity of substances into a _pre-established_ _harmony_ says nothing more than that it is turned into a _presupposition_, that is, into something withdrawn from the concept. – The _need_ to escape any _acting_ of substances upon one another was grounded in the moment of absolute _self-subsistence_ and of _originariness_ which had been made the basis. But because the {{{ 12.137 }}} _positedness_, the degree of development, fails to correspond to this _being-in-itself_, precisely on that account it has its ground in an _other_.

The relation of substantiality was shown, in its own place, to pass over into that of causality. Here, however, that which is bears no longer the determination of _substance_ but that of an _object_; the relation of causality has gone under within the concept; the originariness of the one substance over against the other showed itself to be a shine, and its efficacy a passing over into what stands opposed. Hence this relation has no objectivity. Insofar, then, as one of the objects gets posited under the form of subjective unity, as an efficient cause, that no longer counts for an _originary_ determination but for something _mediated_; only by the mediation of a further object does the acting object possess this determination of its own. – _Mechanism_, since it belongs to the sphere of the concept, has posited at it that which turned out to be the truth of the causal relation; that the cause, supposed to be what is in and for itself, is essentially effect no less, positedness. Within mechanism, accordingly, the object's causality is straightway a non-originariness; the object is indifferent towards this determination of its own; that it should be cause is for it therefore something contingent. – So far one may well say that the substances' causality is _merely_ _something_ _represented_. But this very represented causality is _mechanism_, mechanism consisting in just this, that causality, as the _identical_ determinateness of diverse substances and hence as the going-under of their self-subsistence within this identity, is _a_ _bare_ _positedness_; the objects are indifferent towards this unity and hold themselves up against it. Yet quite as much is this indifferent _self-subsistence_ of theirs a bare _positedness_ too; hence they are capable of _mingling_ and of _aggregating_ themselves, and, as an _aggregate_, of turning _into_ _One_ _object_. By this indifference alike towards their transition and towards their _self-subsistence_, substances are _objects_.
