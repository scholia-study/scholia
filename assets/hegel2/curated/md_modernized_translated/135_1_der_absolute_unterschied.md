## 1. Absolute Difference

Difference is that negativity which reflection possesses within itself; the nothing spoken by identical talk; the essential moment of identity itself, which, being at the same time negativity of its own self, determines itself and stands distinguished from difference.

{{{ 11.266 }}} 1. Difference of this sort is the difference that is _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, _absolute_ difference, _essence's_ _difference_. – Being in and for itself, it is not made a difference through anything external, but is _self-referring_ and therefore _simple_ difference. – To grasp absolute difference as _simple_ is essential. Where A and _not-A_ absolutely differ, that difference is made up by the _simple_ _not_ taken as such. Difference itself is a simple concept. Two things are _different_ _in_ _this_, as one puts it, that they are, and so forth. – _In_ _this_ — that is to say, under one and the same regard, within the same determining ground. What we have here is _reflection's_ _difference_, not the _otherness_ _that_ _belongs_ _to_ _existence_. An existence and another existence are posited as falling apart from one another; each of the existences determined over against the other has an _immediate_ _being_ of its own. The _other_ _of_ _essence_, by contrast, is an other that is in and for itself; it is not an other over against something else situated outside it, but determinateness simple in itself. Even within the sphere of existence, otherness and determinateness proved to have this same nature — simple determinateness, identical opposition; there, however, such identity displayed itself merely as one determinateness _passing_ _over_ into another. Here, within reflection's sphere, difference makes its appearance as reflected, posited in the very way that it is in itself.

2. Difference taken in itself is self-referring difference; as such it is the negativity of itself, a difference not from something else but _of_ _itself_ _from_ _itself_; it is its own other rather than itself. What stands distinguished from difference, however, is identity. It is therefore itself and identity together. The two of them together make up difference; difference is the whole and a moment of that whole. – One can equally say that difference taken as simple is no difference at all; it becomes difference only in relation to identity; but as difference it rather holds identity within it, and holds that relation itself as well. – Difference is at once the whole and a _moment_ of its own; and identity likewise is at once its whole and its moment. – In this we must recognize reflection's essential nature and the _determinate_ _primordial_ _ground_ _of_ _all_ _activity_ _and_ _self-movement_. – Difference no less than identity makes itself into a _moment_, into a _positedness_, since as reflection each is the negative referring of itself to itself.

Difference so understood, as the unity of itself with identity, is _determinate_ difference _in_ _its_ _own_ _self_. There is here no passing over into an other, no relation to an other lying outside; difference has its own other, identity, within it — just as identity, having entered the determination of difference, has not been lost in it as in its other but preserves itself there, being difference's reflection into itself and difference's moment.

{{{ 11.267 }}} 3. Difference has the two moments, identity and difference; both are in this way a _positedness_, a determinateness. But within this positedness each is a _referring_ _of_ _itself_ _to_ _itself_. The one of them, identity, is immediately itself the moment of reflection into itself; and just so the other, difference, is difference in itself, reflected difference. Since difference has two moments of this kind, each of them a reflection into itself, difference is _diversity_.
