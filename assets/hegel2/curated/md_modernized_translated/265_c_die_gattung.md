## C. The Genus

Severed at the start from the universal concept of life, the living individual is a presupposition that has not yet borne itself out through its own self. Through the process carried on with the world presupposed along with it, the individual has posited its own self, _for_ _itself_, as the negative unity of the otherness belonging to it, as the basis of its own self; with that it is the idea's actuality, so that the individual produces itself henceforth out of _actuality_, where formerly it issued only from the _concept_, {{{ 12.190 }}} and its coming-to-be, once a _presupposing_, now turns into its production.

The further determination it has won by sublating the opposition, however, is to be _genus_, identity of itself with the indifferent otherness it formerly had. Being this essential identity, such an idea of the individual is essentially the particularizing of its own self. Reckoned by the totality out of which it issues, this diremption of the idea is a doubling of the individual, – a presupposing of an objectivity identical with the individual, and a bearing of the living being towards its own self as towards another living being.

The universal in question is the third stage, the truth of life so long as life remains enclosed within its own sphere. That stage is the individual's self-referring process, where externality counts as its immanent moment, and _secondly_ this externality is itself, as living totality, an objectivity that counts for the individual as the individual's own self; – an objectivity in which the individual holds the certainty of its own self not as something _sublated_ but as something that _subsists_.

Now because the relation of the genus is an identity of individual self-feeling within one that is at the same time a second self-subsistent individual, that relation is _contradiction_; hence the living being is once more drive. – With the genus the idea of life is indeed brought to completion, yet at first it still lies within the sphere of immediacy; such universality is therefore _actual_ in a _singular_ shape, the concept whose reality wears the form of immediate objectivity. _In_ _itself_ the individual is accordingly genus, but genus it is not _for_ _itself_; what is there for it is at first no more than a second living individual; the concept, in being distinguished from itself, has as its object, with which it stands identical, not itself qua concept, but a concept which, being alive, carries external objectivity for it besides, and hence a form that is at once mutual.

Identity with the other, the individual's universality, is on that account as yet only _inward_ or _subjective_; hence the individual has the longing to posit that identity and to give itself reality as universal. This drive of the genus can be realized only by sublating individualities which, singular, are still particular over against one another. So far as it is these that, universal _in_ _themselves_, appease the tension of their longing and dissolve into their genus-universality, the identity they realize is the negative unity of a genus that reflects itself into itself out of the sundering. It is to that extent the individuality of life itself, no longer _brought_ _forth_ out of life's concept, but out of the _actual_ idea. At the outset it is itself nothing but the concept, one that has still to objectify itself, yet _the_ _actual_ _concept_; – _the_ _germ_ _of_ _a_ _living_ _individual_. Here there lies _before_ _ordinary_ _perception_ what the concept {{{ 12.191 }}} is, and that the _subjective_ _concept_ has _outward_ _actuality_. For the germ of what lives is individuality wholly concentrated, holding all the various sides, properties and articulated differences of that individual in their _entire_ _determinateness_, while the subjective totality, at first _immaterial_, remains undeveloped, simple and without sensuous character; so the germ is the whole living being under the inward form of the concept.

On this side the genus's reflection into itself is what secures it _actuality_, since the moment of negative unity and individuality gets _posited_ in it, – the _propagation_ of living kinds. The idea, which as life still wears the form of immediacy, falls back to that extent into actuality, and this reflection of it amounts to no more than repetition, the infinite progress wherein it never steps beyond the finitude of its immediacy. Yet this return into its first concept has a higher side as well: the idea has not only traversed the mediation of its processes inside immediacy, but by that very course has sublated immediacy and raised itself thereby into a higher form of its existence.

For the genus-process, in which the singular individuals sublate in one another their indifferent, immediate concrete existence and die away in that negative unity, has for the other side of its product the _realized_ _genus_, which has posited itself as identical with the concept. – Within the genus-process the sundered singularities of individual life go under; the negative identity whereby the genus goes back into itself is on one hand the _engendering_ _of_ _singularity_ and on the other _the_ _sublating_ _of_ _it_, and so is the genus coming together with itself, the idea's _universality_ _in_ _process_ _of_ _becoming_ _for_ _itself_. Copulation is where the immediacy of living individuality dies away; the death of this life is the coming forth of spirit. The idea, which as genus is _in_ _itself_, is _for_ _itself_ once it has sublated the particularity that made up the living kinds and has given itself, in so doing, a _reality_ which is _itself_ _simple_ _universality_; so it is the idea which _bears_ _itself_ _towards_ _itself_ as _idea_, the universal whose determinateness and existence is universality itself; – the _idea_ of _cognition_.
