## A. The Living Individual

1. Life's concept, or life taken universally, is the idea in immediate shape, the concept for which its objectivity is a fitting one; yet that objectivity fits it only so far as the concept is the negative unity of this externality — only, that is, so far as it _posits_ the objectivity as fitting. The concept's infinite reference to its own self, being negativity, is self-determination, the diremption whereby it parts itself within itself _as_ _subjective_ _singularity_, _and_ _within_ _itself_ _as_ _indifferent_ _universality_. Taken in its immediacy, the idea of life is at first nothing more than the creative universal soul. Just because of this immediacy, what the idea first does negatively is to refer to itself within itself, determining itself as _concept_, – a positing that is _in_ _itself_ and becomes _being-for-itself_ only on returning into itself; the creative act of _presupposing_. By this self-determining, life in its _universality_ turns into something _particular_; with that it has broken apart into the two extremes of a judgment which at once becomes a syllogism.

The determinations belonging to the opposition are the concept's own universal _determinations_, since it is the concept to which such breaking-apart belongs; what _fills_ them out, however, is the idea. One of the two is that _unity_ of concept and reality which the idea is, taken as the _immediate_ unity, the one that earlier showed itself to be _objectivity_. Here, though, it stands under a different determination. There it was {{{ 12.183 }}} the unity of concept and reality in the sense that the concept had gone over into reality and was simply lost in it; there was no standing opposite, and since the concept is merely reality's _inner_, it amounts only to a reflection _external_ to it. Objectivity in that sense is therefore immediacy itself taken immediately. In the present case, by contrast, objectivity is nothing but what has issued from the concept, so that positedness is its essence, so that it has the standing of a _negative_. – One has to look at it as the _side_ of _the_ _concept's_ _universality_, and hence as _abstract_ universality, something which by its very nature merely _inheres_ in the subject and wears the form of immediate _being_ that, once posited on its own, would be indifferent to the subject. Whatever totality of the concept falls to objectivity is on this showing merely _lent_ to it; the last remnant of self-subsistence objectivity keeps against the subject is that _being_ which in truth amounts only to the concept's moment of _presupposing_, caught in the first determinateness of a _positing_ that is _in_ _itself_, a positing not yet present _as_ positing, not yet as unity reflected into itself. Issuing from the idea, then, self-subsistent objectivity is immediate being merely in the capacity of _predicate_ in the judgment by which the concept determines itself, – a being distinct, to be sure, from the subject, yet posited at the same time and essentially as a _moment_ of the concept.

So far as content goes, this objectivity is the concept's totality, but a totality confronted by the concept's subjectivity, or negative unity, and it is the latter that makes up the genuine centre, namely the concept's free unity with its own self. Such a _subject_ is the idea under the form of _singularity_; as identity with self that is simple and yet negative; the _living_ _individual_.

It is, to begin with, life in the shape of _soul_; the concept of its own self, thoroughly determined inwardly, the _principle_ that begins and moves itself. Simple as the concept is, it holds determinate externality enclosed within it as a _simple_ moment. – Yet further, this soul _in_ _its_ _immediacy_ is external without mediation and carries an objective being on its own self; – reality made subject to the purpose, the immediate _means_, at the outset objectivity in the role of the subject's _predicate_, though later on it is the syllogism's _middle_ _term_ as well; the soul's corporeality is what lets the soul conclude with external objectivity. – Corporeality belongs to the living being at first as reality immediately one with the concept; in that respect it holds such reality simply from _nature_.

Now since this objectivity is the individual's predicate and has been drawn into the subjective unity, none of the object's earlier determinations attaches to it, neither the mechanical nor the chemical relation, and still less the abstract relations of reflection such as whole and parts and the like. Being externality, it is admittedly _susceptible_ of such relations, but just so far it is no living existence; if one takes the living being for a whole put together out of parts, for something {{{ 12.184 }}} that mechanical or chemical causes work upon, for a mechanical or chemical product, whether merely that or something determined by an external purpose besides, then the concept counts as external to it and what one has taken is a _dead_ thing. The concept being immanent to it, the living being's _purposiveness_ has to be conceived as _inner_; the concept is present in it as determinate, marked off from its externality, and in the very act of marking itself off pervading that externality and staying identical with itself. Such objectivity of the living being is _organism_; it is the purpose's _means_ _and_ _tool_, purposive through and through because the concept makes up its substance; yet for that very reason this means and tool is itself the purpose carried out, in which the subjective purpose has thus far immediately concluded with its own self. On the side of its externality the organism is not a manifold of _parts_ but of _members_, and these, as such, a) have subsistence only in the individuality; being external, they can be sundered and can be laid hold of in that externality; once sundered, however, they fall back under the mechanical and chemical relations of ordinary objectivity. b) The externality of the members runs counter to the negative unity of living individuality, which is therefore _drive_, drive to posit the abstract moment of the concept's determinateness as a real difference; and since the difference _is_ _immediate_, it is the _drive_ of every _single_, _specific_ _moment_ to bring itself forth, and equally to lift its particularity into universality, to sublate the others that lie outside it, to produce itself at their expense, but no less to sublate its own self and make itself a means for the rest.

2. Living individuality's _process_ of this sort keeps within its own bounds and still falls wholly inside it. – When the syllogism of external purposiveness was under discussion, its first premise, that the purpose refers itself immediately to objectivity and turns it into a means, was taken in this way: within objectivity the purpose does remain equal to itself and has gone back into itself, yet objectivity has not yet sublated itself _on_ _its_ _own_ _self_, so that the purpose is thus far not _in_ and _for_ _itself_ within it, and comes to be so only in the conclusion. What the living being carries on with its own self is that premise, but a premise that is at once conclusion, since the subject's immediate reference to objectivity, which thereby turns into means and tool, is at the same time present as the concept's _negative_ _unity_ on its own self; the purpose carries itself out within this externality of its own by being the subjective power over it, by being the process wherein externality displays its self-dissolution and its going back into the negative unity of the purpose. The restlessness and mutability on the outward side of the living being is how the {{{ 12.185 }}} concept manifests itself in it, a concept which, being negativity on its own self, possesses objectivity only in the measure that objectivity's indifferent subsistence shows itself to be self-sublating. Driven by its drive, therefore, the concept brings itself forth in such fashion that the product, whose essence the concept is, is itself what produces, is product, that is, only as the externality that posits itself just as negatively, or as the very process of producing.

3. What was examined a moment ago is now the _living_ _subject's_ _concept_ and that of _its_ _process_; the determinations which are in relation to one another are, on one side, the concept's _negative_ _unity_ referring itself to itself, on the other the _objectivity_ that serves it as _means_ and within which it has nonetheless _gone_ _back_ into its own self. Since these are moments belonging to the idea of life _within_ _its_ _concept_, they do not count as the determinate concept-moments proper to the _living_ _individual_ _in_ _its_ _reality_. That individual's objectivity, or corporeality, is a concrete totality; the moments just named are the sides out of which livingness gets constituted, and so they are not moments of a livingness the idea has already constituted. The living _objectivity_ of the individual as such, however, ensouled by the concept and having it for substance, carries on itself, as its essential differences, differences that are the concept's determinations, _universality_, _particularity_ and _singularity_; hence the _shape_ in which they stand externally apart is partitioned, or notched, according to them (insectum).

_First_, then, it is _universality_, the trembling of livingness purely and solely within its own self, _sensibility_. Universality in its concept, as this emerged above, is the simple immediacy that is such only by being absolute negativity within. Sensibility brings this concept of _absolute_ _difference_ before intuition, the concept as its negativity is _dissolved_ into _simplicity_ and stands equal to itself. It is being-within-itself, not in the sense of abstract simplicity, but an infinite _determinable_ receptivity that in its _determinateness_ does not turn into anything manifold or outward but is thoroughly reflected into itself. In such universality _determinateness_ is present as a simple _principle_; a single outward determinateness, what is called an _impression_, comes back out of its outward and manifold determining into this simple _self-feeling_. One may accordingly take sensibility for the existence of a soul that is within itself, since sensibility receives every externality into itself yet carries it back into the perfect simplicity of a universality equal to itself.

_Particularity_ is the concept's second determination, the moment of difference as _posited_; the unlocking of a negativity that lay shut up in simple self-feeling, or that was there an ideal and not yet a real determinateness; – _irritability_. Because its negativity is abstract, feeling {{{ 12.186 }}} is drive; it _determines_ itself; and the living being's self-determination is its judgment, its making itself finite, whereby it refers itself to what is outward as to a _presupposed_ objectivity and enters into reciprocity with it. – Taken in its particularity it is now partly a _species_ standing beside other species of living things; the _formal_ way in which such _indifferent_ _diversity_ is reflected into itself gives the formal _genus_ and its arrangement into a system; the individual way of reflecting, by contrast, lies in this, that particularity is the negativity of its own determinateness taken as a direction outwards, and so is the concept's negativity referring itself to itself.

Under this _third_ determination the living being stands _as_ _something_ _singular_. That reflection-into-self determines itself, more closely, thus: in irritability the living being is externality of itself as against its own self, as against the objectivity which it holds immediately on itself for means and tool and which admits of outward determining. Reflection-into-self does away with this immediacy, – partly as theoretical reflection, namely so far as negativity is present as the simple moment of sensibility that was examined there and that makes up _feeling_, – partly as real reflection, in that the concept's unity posits itself _in_ _its_ _outward_ _objectivity_ as negative unity, which is _reproduction_. – Of these, the first two, sensibility and irritability, are abstract determinations; in reproduction, as in its truth, life is something _concrete_, is livingness, and only there does it likewise possess feeling and the strength to resist. Reproduction is the negativity that constitutes the simple moment of sensibility, and irritability is a living strength to resist only in that the relation to what is outward is reproduction and individual identity with self. Every one of the single moments is essentially the totality of them all; what differentiates them is the ideal determinateness of form, and in reproduction this is posited as the concrete totality of the whole. This whole stands therefore, on one side, as a third, namely as _real_ totality, opposed to those determinate totalities, while on the other side it is their essentiality existing in itself, and equally that in which they are held together as moments and have their subject and their subsistence.

With reproduction, which is the moment of singularity, what the living being posits is its own _actual_ individuality, a being-for-itself referring itself to itself; but at once it is also a real _reference_ _outwards_; the reflection of _particularity_, or irritability, _against_ _an_ _other_, as against the _objective_ world. Shut up hitherto inside the individual, the process of life goes over into the reference to presupposed objectivity as such by this, that in positing itself as a _subjective_ totality the individual becomes a _totality_ also in the _moment_ _of_ _its_ _determinateness_ as _reference_ to externality.
