## B. Shine

1. _Being_ _is_ _shine_. What the being of shine consists in is solely the sublatedness of being, its nullity; this nullity it has in essence, and apart from its nullity, apart from essence, shine is not. Shine is the negative posited as negative.

Shine is the entire remainder still left over out of the sphere of being. Of itself, though, it still seems to possess an immediate side independent of essence, and to be an _other_ of essence at large. What the _other_ contains at large are two moments, that of existence and that of non-existence. Because the unessential no longer possesses a being, all that remains to it out of otherness is the _pure_ _moment_ _of_ _non-existence_; shine amounts to this _immediate_ non-existence, so placed within the determinateness of being that it has existence only in the reference to an other, in its non-existence; the non-self-subsistent, which is only in its own negation. All that stays with it, then, is the pure determinateness of _immediacy_; it stands there as the _reflected_ immediacy, that is, an immediacy which is only _by_ _way_ _of_ its negation, and which, set against its own _mediation_, amounts to nothing but the empty determination of the immediacy of non-existence.

– Thus _shine_, the phenomenon of _skepticism_, or likewise the appearance of idealism, is an _immediacy_ of the kind that is no something and no thing, generally not a being of indifference which would lie outside its determinateness and outside its reference to the subject. That it _is_ — this the skeptic did not allow himself to say; the more recent idealism did not allow itself to view cognitions as a knowledge of the thing-in-itself; that shine was to have no groundwork of being whatever, and into those cognitions the thing-in-itself was not to enter. And yet, simultaneously, skepticism let manifold determinations of its shine pass; or rather its shine had for content the whole manifold wealth of the world. Just so, the appearance of idealism gathers into itself the entire compass of these manifold determinacies. That shine and this {{{ 11.247 }}} appearance are _immediately_ determined in this manifold manner. So there may well be no being, no thing or thing-in-itself lying at the base of this content; for itself the content stays as it is; it has merely been carried over out of being into shine; so that shine holds within its own self those manifold determinacies which are immediate, which are, and which are other towards one another. Shine, accordingly, is itself something _immediately_ determined. This or that content it may have; but the content it has is not posited by shine itself; shine has it immediately. Leibnizian idealism, or Kantian, Fichtean idealism, and other shapes of it besides, have got no further past being as determinateness, past this immediacy, than skepticism did. _Skepticism_ lets the content of its shine be _given_ to it; what content it is to have is for it a matter of the _immediate_. The _Leibnizian_ _monad_ unfolds its representations out of its own self; but it is not the force that generates and binds them; instead they rise up inside it like bubbles; they are indifferent, immediate towards one another, and so towards the monad itself as well. Just so, the _Kantian_ appearance is a content of perception that is _given_; it has affections for its presupposition, determinations of the subject that are immediate towards their own selves and towards the subject. The infinite check of _Fichtean_ idealism may well have no thing-in-itself at its base, so that the check becomes purely a determinateness within the I itself. Yet this determinateness, for the I that makes it its own and sublates its externality, is simultaneously an _immediate_, a _restriction_ upon the I beyond which the I can go, but one which carries at itself a side of indifference on which, though it lie within the I, it holds an _immediate_ non-being of the I itself. –

2. Shine, accordingly, harbours an immediate presupposition, a side that is independent as against essence. Of shine, however, insofar as it is told apart from essence, there is no need to demonstrate that it sublates itself and goes back into essence; for being in its totality has already gone back into essence; shine is the null in itself; all there is to demonstrate is that the determinations which tell it apart from essence belong to essence itself, and beyond that, that this _determinateness_ _of_ _essence_ which shine is, stands sublated within essence itself.

What makes up shine is the immediacy of _non-being_; but this non-being is nothing else than the negativity of essence at essence's own self. Being is non-being in essence. Its _nullity_ in itself amounts to _the_ _negative_ _nature_ _of_ _essence_ _itself_. The immediacy or indifference, however, that this non-being harbours, is essence's very own absolute being-in-itself. Essence's negativity is its equality with its own self, or its simple immediacy and indifference. Being has kept itself in essence insofar as essence, at its infinite negativity, has this equality with its own self; through that, essence is itself being. Hence the immediacy which the {{{ 11.248 }}} determinateness at shine possesses over against essence is nothing else than essence's very own immediacy; not, though, the immediacy that has being, but the utterly mediated or reflected immediacy which shine is; – being not as being, but only as being's determinateness, set against mediation; being as moment.

These two moments – nullity, but taken as subsistence, and being, but taken as moment, or negativity that is in itself and immediacy that is reflected – which make up _the_ _moments_ _of_ _shine_, are on that account _the_ _moments_ _of_ _essence_ _itself_; there is no shine of being present _at_ essence, nor a shine of essence _at_ being; the shine within essence is not the shine of some other; rather it is _the_ _shine_ _in_ _itself_, _the_ _shine_ _of_ _essence_ _itself_.

Shine is essence itself under the determinateness of being. That through which essence has a shine lies in its being _determinate_ within itself, and thereby told apart from its absolute unity. But this determinateness is quite as utterly sublated at its own self. For the self-subsistent is what essence is, a self-subsistent that _is_ in mediating itself with itself by means of its negation, which negation essence itself is; essence is therefore that identical unity in which absolute negativity and immediacy are one. – Negativity is the negativity that is in itself; it is the reference of itself to itself, and so in itself it is immediacy; but it is negative reference to itself, a repelling negating of its own self, and so the immediacy that is in itself is, as against negativity, the negative or the _determinate_. Yet determinateness of this sort is itself the absolute negativity and this determining, a determining which, immediately as determining, is the sublating of its own self, is return into itself.

Shine is the negative that possesses a being, though in an other, in its negation; it is the non-self-subsistence that at its own self is sublated and null. So it is the negative that goes back into itself, the non-self-subsistent as the non-self-subsistent at its own self. This _reference_ of the negative, or of non-self-subsistence, _to_ _itself_ is the negative's _immediacy_; the reference is something _other_ than the negative itself; it is the negative's determinateness as against itself, or it is negation as against the negative. Negation as against the negative, however, is the negativity that refers itself only to itself, the absolute sublating of determinateness as such.

The _determinateness_, then, which shine is within essence, is infinite determinateness; it is only the negative that goes together _with_ _itself_; and so it is a determinateness which, as such, is self-subsistence, and is not determinate. – Conversely, self-subsistence as _immediacy_ referring itself to itself is just as utterly determinateness and moment, and is only as negativity referring itself to itself. – This negativity, identical with immediacy, and hence this immediacy, identical with negativity, is _essence_. {{{ 11.249 }}} Shine, accordingly, is essence itself, essence under a determinateness, yet so that the determinateness is only essence's moment, and _essence_ is the shining of itself within its own self.

Within being's sphere, opposite being as something _immediate_ there _arises_ non-being, likewise as something _immediate_, and the truth of the two is becoming. Within the sphere of essence there stand opposite each other first essence and the unessential, then essence and shine; the unessential and shine as leavings of being. But the pair of them, and equally essence's difference from them, consist in nothing further than this, that essence is at first taken as something _immediate_, not as it is in itself, that is to say, not as the immediacy which is immediacy qua pure mediation or qua absolute negativity. That first immediacy, accordingly, is no more than the _determinateness_ of immediacy. Sublating this determinateness of essence therefore comes to nothing beyond the pointing out that the unessential is shine merely, and that essence rather harbours shine within its own self, as the infinite movement within itself which determines its immediacy as the negativity, and its negativity as the immediacy, and so is the shining of itself within its own self. Essence in this its self-movement is _reflection_.
