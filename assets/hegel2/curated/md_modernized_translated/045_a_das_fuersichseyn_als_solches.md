## {{{ 21.145 }}} A. Being-for-itself as Such

The universal concept of being-for-itself has now emerged. All that would remain is to show that the representation we connect with the expression: _being-for-itself_ answers to that concept, so as to be entitled to employ the expression for it. And so indeed it appears; we say that something is for itself insofar as it sublates otherness, its reference to and community with an other, has repelled these, has abstracted from them. Within it the other is present only _as_ a sublated one, as _its_ _moment_; being-for-itself amounts to this, that it has gone out beyond the restriction, beyond its own otherness, in such a way that as this negation it is the infinite _return_ into itself. – Consciousness already as such contains in itself the determination of being-for-itself, in that an object which it feels, intuits and so forth it _represents_ to itself, i.e. has that object's content _within_ _it_, a content which in that manner is as something _ideal_; in its very intuiting, and generally in its entanglement with what is negative of itself, with the other, it is _with_ _its_ _own_ _self_. Being-for-itself is the polemical, negative comportment towards the delimiting other, and, by negating that other in this way, being-reflected-into-self, even though _alongside_ this return of consciousness into itself, and alongside the object's ideality, there is _also_ still retained the object's _reality_, inasmuch as it is known _at_ _the_ _same_ _time_ as an external existence. Consciousness is thus _appearing_, or the dualism of knowing on the one side of an object other than itself, an external one, and on the other side of being for-itself, of holding that object within it as ideal, of being not merely with such an other but therein with its own self as well. _Self-consciousness_, by contrast, is _being-for-itself_ as _accomplished_ and _posited_; that side of the reference to an _other_, to an external object, is done away with. In self-consciousness, then, we have the nearest example of infinity's presence; – of an infinity that stays abstract enough, to be sure, yet is at the same time of an altogether more concrete determination than being-for-itself as such, whose infinity has as yet nothing but qualitative determinateness.
