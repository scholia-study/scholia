## {{{ 11.341 }}} Chapter 2. Appearance

Concrete existence is that immediacy of being into which essence has re-established itself. _In_ _itself_ this immediacy is essence's reflection into itself. Essence has stepped up out of its ground as concrete existence, and the ground has itself passed over into that existence. Insofar as it is in its own self the absolute negativity, concrete existence is this _reflected_ immediacy. And now it is _posited_ as such as well, having determined itself as _appearance_.

Appearance is therefore, to begin with, essence as it is in its concrete existence; essence is immediately at hand in it. That this existence is _reflected_ rather than immediate is what makes up in it the moment of essence; or, taken as _essential_ concrete existence, concrete existence is appearance.

A something is _only_ appearance – meaning that concrete existence as such is merely a posited being, not one that is in and for itself. To have in its own self the negativity of reflection, the nature of essence, is what constitutes its essentiality. This is not some alien reflection, external to it, one to which essence would belong and which, through a comparison of essence with concrete existence, would pronounce this existence to be appearance. Rather, as has emerged, the essentiality of concrete existence which consists in being appearance is concrete existence's own truth. The reflection through which it is this belongs to it itself.

If, however, it is claimed that something is _only_ appearance, taking this to mean that _immediate_ _concrete_ _existence_ would by contrast be the truth, then appearance is on the contrary the higher truth; for appearance is the concrete existence that is essential, while concrete existence is the appearance that is still void of essence, since it has in it only the one moment of appearance, namely concrete existence as immediate, and not yet appearance's negative reflection. When _appearance_ is called _void_ _of_ _essence_, the moment of its negativity is thought of as though by contrast the immediate were the positive and the genuine; yet it is this immediate that does not yet have the essential truth in it. Concrete existence ceases, rather, to be void of essence precisely by passing over into appearance.

Essence at first _shines_ within its own self, in its simple identity; so understood it is abstract reflection, the pure movement which, from nothing and through nothing, returns to itself. Essence _appears_, and it is now therefore _real_ shine, since {{{ 11.342 }}} the moments of shine have concrete existence. Appearance, as has emerged, is the thing taken as the negative _mediation_ of itself with its own self; the differences that it contains are _self-subsistent_ matters, and these are the contradiction of being an immediate subsistence while at the same time having their subsistence only in an alien self-subsistence, that is, in the negation of their own, and, once more precisely for that reason, only in the negation of that alien one, or in the negation of their own negation. Shine is this same mediation, but in appearance its unstable moments have the shape of immediate self-subsistence. The immediate self-subsistence that belongs to concrete existence, by contrast, is for its part brought down to a moment. Appearance is accordingly the unity of shine and concrete existence.

Appearance now determines itself more closely. It is essential concrete existence; the essentiality of this existence distinguishes itself from it as unessential, and these two sides enter into reference to each other. – _First_, then, appearance is simple identity with itself which at the same time contains diverse determinations of content, such that these determinations, as well as their reference, are what remains equal to itself in the flux of appearance; the _law_ of _appearance_.

_Second_, however, the law, simple in its diversity, passes over into opposition; what is essential to appearance is set in opposition to appearance itself, and over against the _world_ _of_ _appearance_ there stands the _world_ _that_ _is_ _in_ _itself_.

_Third_, this opposition goes back into its ground; what is in itself is present in appearance, and conversely what appears is determined as taken up into its being-in-itself; appearance turns into _relation_.
