## {{{ 12.84 }}} D. The Judgment of the Concept

Knowing how to pass _judgments_ _of_ _existence_: _the_ _rose_ _is_ _red_, snow is white, and so forth, will hardly count as evidence of a great power of judgment. The _judgments_ _of_ _reflection_ come closer to being _propositions_; the object in the judgment of necessity stands, admittedly, in its objective universality, yet only in the judgment now to be considered is _its_ _reference_ _to_ _the_ _concept_ at hand. Therein the concept is laid down as ground, and since it stands in reference to the object, it is there as _an_ _ought_ to which reality may correspond or may fail to. – Hence only a judgment of this sort holds a genuine appraisal; the predicates _good_, _bad_, _true_, _beautiful_, _correct_, and so forth express that the fact, _measured_ against its universal _concept_ as against the simply _presupposed_ _ought_, either stands in _agreement_ with it or does not.

The judgment of the concept has been called judgment of _modality_, and is looked upon as containing the form in which the reference of subject and predicate stands within an _external_ _understanding_, and as concerning the _value_ of the copula only _in_ _reference_ _to_ _thinking_. On this account the _problematic_ judgment is said to consist in one's taking the affirming or denying as optional or as _possible_; – the _assertoric_, in one's taking it as _true_, that is, _actual_; and the _apodictic_, in one's taking it as _necessary_. – It is easy to see why, with this judgment, it lies so near to hand to step outside the judgment itself and to view its determination as something merely _subjective_. For what steps forward once more in the judgment here is the concept, the subjective, and it comports itself towards an immediate actuality. Only, this subjective is not to be confounded with _external_ _reflection_, which is admittedly also something subjective, but in another sense than the concept itself; the concept, stepping forward again out of the disjunctive judgment, is on the contrary the very reverse of a mere _manner_ _and_ _mode_. In this sense the earlier judgments are only something subjective, since they rest upon an abstraction and one-sidedness wherein the concept has gone lost. As against them, the judgment of the concept is rather the objective one and the truth, precisely because what lies at its ground is the concept in its determinateness as concept, and not the concept in external reflection or _in_ _reference_ _to_ a subjective, that is, contingent _thinking_.

In the disjunctive judgment the concept had been posited as identity of the universal nature with its particularization; therewith the relation of the judgment had sublated itself. This _concrete_ unity of universality and particularization is at the outset a simple result; it has now to build itself out further into totality, seeing that the {{{ 12.85 }}} moments it holds have at first gone under within it and do not yet confront one another in determinate self-subsistence. – The deficiency of the result may also be put more determinately thus: that in the disjunctive judgment the objective _universality_ has indeed become complete _in_ _its_ _particularization_, but that the negative unity of the latter goes back only _into_ _the_ _former_ and has not yet determined itself into the _third_, into _singularity_. – Insofar, however, as the result is itself the _negative_ _unity_, it already is this _singularity_; but so taken it is only this _one_ determinateness, which now has _to_ _posit_ its own negativity, to sunder itself into the _extremes_, and thus to develop itself fully _into_ _the_ _syllogism_.

The next diremption of this unity is the judgment, wherein it is posited the one time as subject, as an _immediately_ _singular_, and then as predicate, as the determinate reference of its moments.
