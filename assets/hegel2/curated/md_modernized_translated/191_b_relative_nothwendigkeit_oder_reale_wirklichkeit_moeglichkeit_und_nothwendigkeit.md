## B. Relative Necessity, or Real Actuality, Possibility and Necessity

1. Necessity as it has now emerged is _formal_, its moments being formal, that is, simple determinations which make up a totality only as immediate unity, or as the immediate turning over of the one into the other, and which accordingly do not wear the shape of self-subsistence. – Within this formal necessity, then, the unity is at first simple and stands indifferent to its own differences. Being the _immediate_ unity of the form-determinations, such necessity is _actuality_; but an actuality possessed of a _content_, since its unity now stands _determined_ _as_ _indifferent_ to the _difference_ of the form-determinations, namely of actuality itself and of possibility. Being an indifferent identity, this content harbours the form too as indifferent, that is, as determinations merely diverse, and it is _manifold_ content at large. Such actuality is _real_ _actuality_.

_As_ _such_, real actuality is at first the thing of many properties, the world in its existing; it is not, however, that concrete existence which dissolves into appearance; being actuality, it is rather at once being-in-itself and reflection-into-itself; amid the manifoldness of bare concrete existence it maintains itself; its externality is an inward comporting _to_ _itself_ alone. Whatever is actual _can_ _act_; a thing announces its actuality _by_ _what_ _it_ {{{ 11.386 }}} _brings_ _forth_. Its comporting towards an other is the manifestation _of_ _itself_ – not a passing over, for that is how the something which is refers itself to an other; – nor yet an appearing, for that is how the thing stands only in relation to others; it is a self-subsistent, but one which has its reflection-into-itself, its determinate essentiality, in another self-subsistent.

Real actuality now likewise carries _possibility_ immediately _in_ _its_ _own_ _self_. The moment of being-in-itself is contained in it; but being as yet the _immediate_ unity only, it stands within _one_ of form's determinations, and so, as that which is, is marked off from being-in-itself or possibility.

2. Being the being-in-itself of _real_ actuality, this possibility is itself _real_ _possibility_, at first the _content-filled_ being-in-itself. – Formal possibility is reflection-into-itself only as that abstract identity whereby something does not within itself contradict itself. But once one goes into the determinations, circumstances and conditions of a fact so as to cognize its possibility from them, one no longer halts at the formal sort; one is considering its real possibility.

_Immediate_ _concrete existence_ is what this real possibility itself is – no longer, though, for the reason that possibility as such, as a formal moment, is immediately its own contrary, an unreflected actuality; rather, being _real_ possibility, it carries this determination at once in its own self. Hence the real possibility of a fact is the existent manifoldness of circumstances bearing upon it.

This manifoldness of existence is thus indeed possibility as much as actuality, yet their identity is as yet the _content_ only, which stands indifferent to these determinations of form; they, accordingly, make up the _form_ as _determined_ over against their identity. – Or again, _immediate_ real actuality, just because immediate, stands determined over against its possibility; being determinate in this way and hence reflected, it is _real_ _possibility_. Real possibility, now, is indeed form's posited _whole_, but form in its determinateness – actuality, namely, as formal or immediate, and possibility likewise, as abstract being-in-itself. The actuality which makes up a fact's possibility is therefore not _its_ _own_ _possibility_; it is the being-in-itself of _another_ actual; it is itself the actuality due to be sublated, possibility as possibility _only_. – Real possibility, then, makes up the _whole_ _of_ _conditions_, an actuality dispersed and unreflected into itself, yet determined to be the being-in-itself of an other and destined to go back into itself.

{{{ 11.387 }}} Whatever is really possible is thus, by its _being-in-itself_, a formally identical thing which by its _simple_ content-determination does not contradict itself; but by its developed and differentiated circumstances too, and by everything with which it hangs together, it must, as the self-identical thing it is, not contradict itself. Yet _second_, being manifold within and hanging together manifoldly with an other, while diversity in its own self passes into opposition, it is a contradicting thing. Should a possibility be under discussion and its contradiction be required to show, one need only hold to the manifoldness which it harbours as content or as the concrete existence conditioning it; out of that its contradiction is easily hunted up. – This, however, is no contradiction of comparison; rather, manifold concrete existence is _in_ _its_ _own_ _self_ just this, to sublate itself and to founder; and therein it carries essentially, in its own self, the determination of being _only_ _a_ _possible_. – Let all the conditions of a fact be completely at hand and the fact steps into actuality; – completeness of conditions is totality as it stands at the content, and _the_ _fact_ _itself_ is this content, determined to be an actual as much as a possible. Within the sphere of conditioned ground the conditions have their form – the ground, namely, or the reflection that is for itself – _outside_ _them_, a reflection which refers them into moments of the fact and brings forth concrete existence _in_ _them_. Here, by contrast, no presupposing reflection determines immediate actuality to be a condition; it is posited instead that actuality is itself the possibility.

Within real possibility as it sublates itself, what gets sublated is now a twofold; for possibility is itself the twofold of being actuality and possibility. 1) The actuality is the formal one, a concrete existence which showed itself as self-subsistent and immediate, and which by being sublated turns into reflected being, into the moment of an other, thereby gaining _being-in-itself_ in it. 2) That concrete existence stood determined also as _possibility_, or as the _being-in-itself_, but of an other. As it sublates itself, then, this being-in-itself is sublated along with it and crosses over into _actuality_. – The movement of real possibility sublating its own self accordingly brings forth _the_ _very_ _moments_ _already_ _at hand_, each merely coming to be out of the other; in this negation, therefore, it is no _passing_ _over_ either, but _a_ _closing_ _together_ _with_ _itself_. – On formal possibility it held that, something being possible, not _it_ _itself_ but its _other_ was possible as well. Real possibility no longer confronts _such_ _an other_, being real precisely so far as it is itself actuality too. As the _immediate_ _concrete existence_ belonging to it, the circle of conditions, sublates itself, real possibility makes of itself the _being-in-itself_ which it already is, namely the _being-in-itself_ of an other. {{{ 11.388 }}} And as conversely its moment of being-in-itself thereby sublates itself at the same stroke, it turns into actuality, into the very moment which it likewise already is. – What disappears is thus this: that actuality stood determined as the possibility or the being-in-itself of an _other_, and possibility conversely as an actuality which is _not_ _that_ _one_ whose possibility it is.

3. The _negation_ of real possibility is accordingly _its_ _identity_ with itself; being thus, within its own sublating, the recoil of that sublating into its own self, it is _real_ _necessity_.

What is necessary cannot be _otherwise_ – what is _possible_ at all certainly can; for possibility is that being-in-itself which is positedness merely and hence essentially otherness. Formal possibility is this identity as a crossing over into what is simply other; real possibility, though, having in it the other moment, actuality, is already necessity itself. Whatever is really possible can therefore no longer be otherwise; under such conditions and circumstances nothing else is able to ensue. Real possibility and necessity are thus distinguished in _semblance_ only; necessity is an _identity_ which does not first _come to be_ but lies already _presupposed_ at the ground. Real necessity is accordingly a _content-filled_ reference; for the content is that identity which is in itself and stands indifferent to the differences of form.

This necessity, however, is at the same time _relative_. – It has, that is, a _presupposition_ from which it starts; in the _contingent_ lies its _point of departure_. For the real actual as such is the _determinate_ actual, and it has its _determinateness_ at first as _immediate_ _being_ in this, that it is a manifoldness of existing circumstances; but with this immediate being taken as determinateness it is also the _negative_ of itself, is being-in-itself or possibility; so it is real possibility. As this unity of the two moments it constitutes form's totality, a totality still _external_ _to_ _itself_; its way of uniting possibility and actuality is that 1) manifold concrete existence is _immediately_ or _positively_ the possibility – a possible, a self-identical thing at large, just because it is an actual; 2) so far as this possibility of concrete existence gets posited, it stands determined as possibility _only_, as actuality's immediate turning over into its contrary, – as _contingency_, in other words. That possibility, then, which immediate actuality carries in it in being a condition, is the being-in-itself only as the possibility of an _other_. Because this otherness, as was shown, sublates itself, and because this positedness comes in its turn to be posited, real possibility does indeed turn into necessity; but necessity so arrived at starts out from that unity of possible and actual which is not yet reflected into itself; – such _presupposing_ and the _movement_ that _returns_ into itself lie {{{ 11.389 }}} still apart; – or _necessity_ _has_ not yet _determined_ _itself_ _out_ _of_ _itself_ _into_ _contingency_.

At the _content_ the relativity of real necessity shows itself thus, that the content is as yet the identity indifferent to form only, hence distinct from form and a _determinate_ _content_ at large. On that account the really necessary is some limited actuality or other, which for the sake of this limitedness is in another respect a _contingent_ merely.

So in fact _real_ _necessity_ is _in_ _itself_ _contingency_ as well. – At first this shows up thus, that the really necessary is, _by_ _form_, indeed a necessary, but by content a limited thing, and through its content has its contingency. Yet contingency lies contained in the form of real necessity too; for as was shown, real possibility is the necessary _in_ _itself_ only, while posited it is the _otherness_ of actuality and possibility to one another. Contingency is therefore contained in real necessity; real necessity is the return into itself out of that restless _otherness_ of actuality and possibility to one another, but not a return out of its own self to itself.

_In_ _itself_, then, the unity of necessity and contingency is here at hand; _absolute_ _actuality_ is the name to give this unity.
