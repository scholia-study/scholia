## C. The Dissolution of the Thing

_This_ thing, as it has come to determine itself, namely as the merely quantitative interconnection of the free matters, is what is absolutely alterable. Its alteration consists in this, that one or more matters are separated out of the collection or added to this _also_, or that their quantitative ratio to one another is altered. The coming-to-be and ceasing-to-be of _this_ thing amounts to the external dissolution of a combination that is itself external, or the combination of such items as are indifferent to being combined or not. The matters circulate out of and into _this_ thing unimpeded; the thing itself is absolute porosity without a measure or form of its own.

Thus, in the absolute determinateness whereby it is _this_ thing, the thing is what is absolutely dissolvable. This dissolution is an external becoming-determined, just as the thing's being is too; but what is essential to this being is precisely its dissolution together with the externality of its being; it is only the also; it subsists only in this externality. But it subsists out of its matters as well, and what dissolves its own self is not merely the abstract _this_ as such, but the _whole_ of _this_ _thing_. For what the thing is determined to be is an external gathering of self-subsistent matters; such matters are not things, negative self-subsistence not belonging to them; they are rather the properties in the shape of what is self-subsistent, that is, a being-determined which as such stands reflected into itself. The matters are accordingly simple and refer only to themselves; but _their_ _content_ is a _determinateness_; the reflection-into-itself is only _the_ _form_ of this content, which as {{{ 11.337 }}} such is not reflected into itself but instead, according to its determinateness, _refers_ _to_ _an_ _other_. The thing is therefore not only the _also_ of these matters – their reference as mutually indifferent – but just as much their _negative_ reference; – by virtue of their determinateness the matters are themselves this negative reflection of theirs, which is the punctiformity of the thing. The one matter, taken according to the determinateness of their content over against each other, is not _what_ the other is; and the one, taken according to their self-subsistence, _is_ not insofar as the other is.

The thing is accordingly the reference to one another of the matters out of which it subsists in such a way that in it the one and the other _also_ _subsist_, yet that at the same time therein the one does _not_ subsist insofar as the other subsists. So far, then, as the one matter is present in the thing, the other stands sublated thereby; yet the thing is equally the _also_, that is, the subsistence of that other. In the subsisting of the one matter, therefore, the other does _not_ subsist, while equally it does _also_ subsist in the first; and so reciprocally for all these diverse matters. Since, then, in the very respect in which the one subsists the others subsist too – which one subsistence of them is the punctiformity or negative unity of the thing – they _interpenetrate_ each other absolutely; and because the thing is, at the same time, nothing but their _also_, while the matters stand reflected into their determinateness, they are indifferent towards one another and do _not_ _touch_ each other in their interpenetration. Essentially, then, the matters are _porous_, the one having its subsistence in the _pores_, that is, in the non-subsistence, of the other; but those others are themselves porous, and in their pores, or in their non-subsistence, the first subsists too, along with all the remaining ones; their _subsistence_ is at once their _sublatedness_ and the subsisting of _others_; and that subsistence of the others is no less the sublatedness of these and the _subsistence_ _of_ _the_ _first_, and in like manner of all the rest. The thing is therefore that self-contradictory mediation whereby self-subsistent subsistence is mediated with itself by way of its own opposite, that is, by way of its negation, or whereby _one_ self-subsistent matter is mediated by means of the _subsistence_ and the _non-subsistence_ of an _other_. – Concrete existence has attained its completeness in _this_ _thing_, namely to be, _in_ _one_, being that is in itself, or _self-subsistent_ subsistence, and _unessential_ concrete existence; hence the truth of concrete existence consists in having its being-in-itself in unessentiality, or its subsistence in an other and indeed in the absolutely other, or in having _its_ _nullity_ for its foundation. It is therefore _appearance_.
