## c. The Infinity of Force

_Finite_ force is, so far as its moments still carry the form of immediacy; presupposing reflection and self-referring reflection stand distinguished in it under this determination; the one shows up as an external force subsisting for itself, the other, in referring to that one, as passive. So far as form goes, then, force is conditioned, and so far as content goes it is limited as well; for determinateness on the side of form carries a limitation of content along with it. But what force's activity consists in is _expressing_ _itself_; that is, as has come out, in sublating externality and determining it as that wherein force stands identical with itself. What force in truth expresses, accordingly, is this: that its reference to another is its reference to its own self, that its passivity subsists within its activity itself. The impulse whereby it gets solicited into activity is a soliciting of its own; the externality arriving upon it is nothing immediate, but something it has itself mediated, just as its own essential identity with itself is not immediate either, but mediated through its negation; or force expresses this, that its _externality_ _is_ _identical_ _with_ _its_ _inwardness_.
