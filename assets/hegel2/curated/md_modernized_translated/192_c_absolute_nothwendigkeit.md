## C. Absolute Necessity

Real necessity is _determinate_ necessity; formal necessity as yet carries neither content nor determinateness upon it. Wherein the _determinateness_ of necessity consists is this, that necessity has its own negation, contingency, present in it. Such is the result it has yielded.

In _its_ _first_ _simplicity_, however, this determinateness is actuality; hence _determinate_ necessity is, and immediately so, _actual_ _necessity_. Such actuality, _which_ _itself_ _as_ _such_ _is_ _necessary_, holding necessity within it as its _being-in-itself_, counts as _absolute_ _actuality_; – actuality that admits of being no otherwise, its _being-in-itself_ being not possibility at all but necessity itself.

Yet just because it stands posited as _absolute_ — posited, that is, as _being_ _itself_ _the_ _unity_ _of_ _itself_ _and_ _of_ _possibility_ — this _actuality_ amounts to no more than an _empty_ determination; or it is _contingency_. – That its determination is thus _empty_ reduces it to a _mere_ _possibility_, to something able just as readily to be _otherwise_ and to be determined as merely possible. But that possibility is the _absolute_ one; for it consists precisely in being open to determination as possibility {{{ 11.390 }}} no less than as actuality. Since it is this indifference towards its own self, it stands posited as an empty, _contingent_ determination.

Real necessity, then, does not merely harbour contingency _in_ _itself_: contingency also _becomes_ in it; but such _becoming_, taken as externality, amounts to nothing beyond that necessity's own _being-in-itself_, since it is no more than an _immediate_ _being-determined_. Nor is that all it is: it is necessity's _own_ becoming – the _presupposition_ necessity had turns out to be a positing that it does itself. For qua real necessity it is the sublatedness of actuality within possibility and inversely; – and since it is this _simple_ _turning_ _over_ of either moment into the other, it is likewise their simple _positive_ _unity_, each of them, as we saw, _going_ _together_ _with_ _itself_ alone within the other. On that showing it is _actuality_ — but an actuality having being solely as this simple going together of form with itself. Its negative positing of those moments is accordingly _the_ _presupposing_ itself, the positing of _itself_ _as_ _sublated_, of _immediacy_, in other words.

Precisely therein, however, does this actuality get determined as something negative; out of the actuality that was real possibility it is a going together with itself; the new actuality therefore emerges only out of its own being-in-itself, out of _the_ _negation_ _of_ _itself_. – Herewith it stands at once determined immediately as _possibility_ too, as something _mediated_ by way of its negation. Such possibility, though, comes immediately to nothing more than _this_ _mediating_, wherein being-in-itself — the possibility itself, that is — and immediacy count equally as _positedness_. – Necessity it is, then, that sublates this positedness no less than it posits _immediacy_ and _being-in-itself_, and that in the very same stroke _determines_ such sublating as _positedness_. So it is _it_ _itself_ that determines itself into _contingency_; – repelling itself from itself in its being, having in that very repelling done nothing but return into itself, and having, in that return taken as its being, repelled itself from its own self.

Thus has _form_, in realizing itself, worked through every one of its differences and rendered itself transparent, and qua _absolute_ _necessity_ it is nothing but this simple _identity_ _of_ _being_ _in_ _its_ _negation_ or in _essence_ _with_ _its_ _own_ _self_. – Gone likewise is the difference between _content_ and form itself; for that unity of possibility within actuality, and inversely, is _form_ standing indifferent towards itself in its determinateness or positedness, the _content-laden_ _fact_ upon whose surface the form of necessity externally ran itself out. Taken so, however, it is the _reflected_ identity of the pair of determinations, _indifferent_ as against them, and thereby the form-determination {{{ 11.391 }}} of _being-in-itself_ set over against _positedness_; and it is this possibility that makes up how limited the content of real necessity was. Absolute necessity, though, is the dissolving of that difference, and its content is just this difference as it penetrates itself within it.

Absolute necessity, then, is the truth into which possibility and actuality generally withdraw, and formal and real necessity along with them. – What has emerged is that it is being which, within its negation, within essence, refers itself to itself and is being. Simple immediacy or _pure_ _being_ it is, no less than simple reflection-into-itself or _pure_ _essence_; it consists in the two of these being one and the same. – Whatever is necessary without qualification _is_ for the sole reason that it _is_; beyond that neither condition nor ground belongs to it. – Equally, though, it is pure _essence_, its _being_ being simple reflection-into-itself; it is, _because_ it is. Qua reflection it does have ground and condition — save that _itself_ is the only ground and condition it has. Being-in-itself belongs to it, but this being-in-itself of its is its immediacy, and its possibility its actuality. – _It_ _is_, _then_, _because_ _it_ _is_; as the _going_ _together_ of being with its own self it is essence; yet since this simple thing is at the same time immediate simplicity, it is _being_.

Absolute necessity is in this way the _reflection_ _or_ _form_ _of_ _the_ _absolute_: unity of being and essence, a simple immediacy that is absolute negativity. _On the one hand_, accordingly, the differences it has do not stand as determinations of reflection but _as_ _a_ _manifoldness_ _that_ _is_, as differentiated actuality wearing the shape of self-subsistent others set against each other. _On the other hand_, their reference being absolute identity, necessity is the _absolute_ _turning_ _round_ of its actuality into its possibility, and of its possibility into actuality. – Hence absolute necessity is _blind_. On the first side, the differentiated terms, determined as actuality and as possibility, wear the shape of _reflection-into-self_ as of _being_; each of the two is therefore a _free_ _actuality_, _of_ _which_ _neither_ _shines_ _in_ _the_ _other_, neither prepared to display upon itself so much as a trace of its reference to the other; grounded within itself, each is in its own self what is necessary. Necessity qua _essence_ lies shut up inside this _being_; so the contact these actualities have through one another looks like a mere empty externality, and the actuality _of_ _the_ _one_ _in_ _the_ _other_ is _mere_ possibility, _contingency_. Being, after all, stands posited as absolutely necessary, as that mediation-with-itself which is the absolute negation of mediation-through-another, or as a being identical with being alone; an _other_ having its actuality in _being_ therefore counts as something unqualifiedly _merely_ _possible_, as empty positedness.

This _contingency_, however, is rather the absolute necessity itself; it is the _essence_ belonging to those free actualities that are necessary in themselves. That essence {{{ 11.392 }}} _shuns_ _the_ _light_, for in such actualities there is no _shining_, no reflex; grounded purely within themselves, shaped for themselves, they make themselves manifest to _themselves_ alone, – for they are nothing but _being_. – Their _essence_, though, will break forth upon them and lay open what _it_ is and what _they_ are. The _simplicity_ their being has, their resting upon themselves, is absolute negativity; it is the _freedom_ their shineless immediacy possesses. This negative breaks forth upon them since being, through this its essence, is contradiction with its own self; – breaks forth, moreover, against such being in the shape of being, and so as the _negation_ of those actualities, a negation _absolutely_ _diverse_ from the being they have, as their _nothing_, as an _otherness_ confronting them with just as much _freedom_ as their being has. – And yet in them it was not to be misread. In the configuration wherein they rest upon themselves they stand indifferent towards form, a _content_, and so _differentiated_ actualities with a _determinate_ content; and this content is the _mark_ pressed upon them by necessity — which, absolute return into its own self within its _determination_, let them go free as absolutely actual — the mark it appeals to as witness of its right, the mark whereby they are now laid hold of and go under. This manifesting of what _determinateness_ is in truth, namely negative reference to its own self, is a _blind_ going-under into otherness; the _shining_ that breaks forth, or _reflection_, occurs in the beings as _becoming_, as the _transition_ of being into nothing. But _being_, conversely, is quite as much _essence_, and _becoming_ is _reflection_ or _shining_. Their externality is in that way their inwardness, their reference absolute identity; the _transition_ of what is actual into what is possible, of being into nothing, is a _going_ _together_ _with_ _its_ _own_ _self_; contingency is absolute necessity; and it is contingency itself that presupposes those first absolute actualities.

This _identity_ _of_ _being_ within its negation _with_ _its_ _own_ _self_ — such identity is now _substance_. As _in_ _its_ _negation_, or as _in_ _contingency_, it is this unity; and so it is _substance_ _as_ _relation_ _to_ _its_ _own_ _self_. What the _blind_ transition of necessity amounts to is rather the absolute's _own_ _exposition_, its own movement within itself — an absolute that in externalizing itself rather displays that very self.
