## {{{ 21.96 }}} Chapter 2. Existence

_Determinate_ being is what existence is; and its determinateness, determinateness _that_ _is_, is _quality_. By virtue of its quality _something_ is set over against an _other_, is _alterable_ and _finite_, and stands determined negatively outright, not merely over against an other but within itself. The _infinite_ is this negation of its own, standing at first over against the finite something; and the abstract opposition wherein these determinations make their appearance resolves itself into infinity without opposition, into _being-for-itself_.

Accordingly, three divisions make up the exposition of existence:

A. _existence_ _as_ _such_,

B. _something_ _and_ _an_ _other_, _finitude_,

C. _qualitative_ _infinity_.
