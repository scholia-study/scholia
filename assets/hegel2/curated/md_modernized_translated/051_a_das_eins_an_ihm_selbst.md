## a. The One in Its Own Self

In its own self the one simply _is_; this being of it is no existence, no determination as a relating to other, no constitution, it is rather this, to have negated that whole circle of categories. Of any becoming-other, therefore, the one is incapable; it _is_ _unalterable_.

Indeterminate it is, though no longer after the fashion of being; its indeterminateness is the determinateness which consists in relating to its own self, absolute determinedness; _posited_ being-within-itself. Being, according to its concept, the negation that relates itself to itself, it has difference upon it, – a direction away from itself out towards an other, one that is nevertheless immediately reversed, because in keeping with this moment of self-determining there is no other for it to go out to, and one that has turned back into itself.

Within this simple immediacy there have vanished the mediation of existence and of ideality itself, and along with them all diversity and manifoldness. There is _nothing_ in it; this _nothing_, the abstraction of relating to one's own self, is here distinguished from being-within-itself itself, it is something _posited_, since this being-within-itself is no longer the simple being-within-itself of something, but carries the determination of being concrete as mediation; taken abstractly, however, it is indeed identical with the one, yet diverse from the determination of the one. Posited in this way, as being _in_ _a_ _one_, the nothing is the nothing as _void_. – Thus the void is the _quality_ of the one in its immediacy.
