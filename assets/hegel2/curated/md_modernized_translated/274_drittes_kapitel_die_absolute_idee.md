## {{{ 12.236 }}} Chapter 3. The Absolute Idea

As it has now resulted, the absolute idea is the identity of the theoretical with the practical idea; each of those, taken alone, stays one-sided and carries the idea itself within it merely as a beyond still being looked for, a goal never reached; – each is on that account a _synthesis_ _of_ _striving_, at once possessing the idea and _not_ possessing it, crossing over from the one to the other without ever holding the two thoughts together, so that it comes to a halt in their contradiction. Being the rational concept, which inside its own reality unites with nothing but itself, the absolute idea is, on one side and just because its objective identity is thus immediate, a going back to _life_; and yet that very form of immediacy it has equally sublated, harbouring within itself the sharpest opposition of all. Here the concept is no longer merely _soul_: it is the free subjective concept, existing for itself and owning on that account _personality_ – the practical concept, determined in and for itself, objective, and as person an impenetrable, atomic subjectivity – which nonetheless is no exclusionary singularity but is, for itself, _universality_ and _cognition_, having for its subject matter, in its other, _its_ _own_ objectivity. All else is error, cloudiness, mere opinion, striving, caprice and transience; the absolute idea by itself is _being_, _life_ that does not perish, _truth_ _knowing_ _itself_, and it is _all_ _truth_.

Philosophy has no other subject matter and no other content. Since _all_ _determinateness_ is held within it, and since its essence lies in coming back to itself by way of its own self-determination or particularizing, it takes on a variety of configurations, and philosophy's task is to know it in them. Nature and spirit are, broadly, two distinct ways of setting out _its_ _existence_; art and religion are its differing ways of laying hold of itself and of providing itself an existence that fits it; what philosophy contains and aims at is the same as with art and religion, yet philosophy lays hold of the absolute idea in the highest way, since the way proper to it, the concept, is the highest. Those configurations of finitude, real and ideal alike, along with those of infinity and of holiness, philosophy accordingly gathers into itself, comprehending them and comprehending itself. To derive and to know these particular ways falls now to the several philosophical sciences as their further task. What is _logical_ about the absolute idea may itself be called one _mode_ {{{ 12.237 }}} of it; but a _mode_ designates a _particular_ sort, a _determinateness_ of form, whereas the logical is the universal mode within which every particular one lies sublated and wrapped up. In its pure essence the logical idea is that idea itself, shut up within its concept in plain identity, not yet having entered upon _shining_ in some determinateness of form. For this reason logic sets out the self-movement of the absolute idea only as the primal _word_ – a word which is an _utterance_, yet an utterance that, being outward, has vanished again the instant it is; so the idea exists only in this self-determining of _apprehending_ _itself_, exists in the _pure_ _thought_ where difference is not yet any _otherness_ but stays and remains wholly translucent to itself. – What the logical idea has for its content is accordingly itself as the _infinite_ _form_; – the _form_ stands opposed to _content_ just to the degree that content is the form-determination gone back into itself and sublated in identity, such that this concrete identity confronts the identity spelled out as form; over against form, content wears the look of something other and given, while form as such stands out and out in _reference_ and has its determinateness posited at the same time as shine. – Looked at more closely, the absolute idea itself has for content nothing but this: that the form-determination is its own totality brought to completion, the pure concept. It was the _determinateness_ of the idea, together with the entire course this determinateness ran, that made up what the science of logic dealt with, and from that course the absolute idea has emerged _for_ _itself_; for itself, though, what it has shown itself to be is this, that determinateness wears no shape of _content_ at all but stands purely as _form_, so that on this footing the idea is the purely _universal_ _idea_. Hence what remains for consideration here is no content as such, but the universal belonging to its form – in other words, the _method_.

To begin with, the _method_ can look like nothing more than the _manner_ _and_ _way_ in which cognition proceeds, and such indeed is its nature. Yet as method that manner and way is not just a modality of _being_ _determined_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_; being a modality of cognition, it is posited as something the _concept_ determines, and as the form — to the degree that form is the soul of everything objective, while any content otherwise determined holds its truth nowhere but in the form. Should content once again be assumed as given to the method and as having a peculiar nature of its own, then method, so determined, turns into a merely _external_ form, as does the logical altogether. But one need not appeal merely to the basic concept of the logical: its whole course, throughout which every shape of a given content and of objects has come up, has displayed the transition and the untruth of those shapes, and rather than some given object serving as the substrate to which absolute form would stand only as an outward, accidental {{{ 12.238 }}} determination, it is this form that has turned out to be the absolute substrate and the last truth. Out of that, method has come forth as _the_ _concept_ _knowing_ _itself_, holding _itself_ — the absolute, subjective and objective alike — _for_ _its_ _subject_ _matter_, and so as the sheer correspondence between concept and its reality, as a concrete existence that the concept itself is.

Method, then, as it comes up for treatment here, is nothing but the movement of the _concept_ itself, whose nature has already been made out, only that _firstly_ it now carries the _significance_ that the _concept_ is _everything_ and its movement the _universal_ _absolute_ _activity_, the movement that determines and realizes itself. Method is therefore to be acknowledged as the unrestrictedly universal mode, inward and outward at once, and as the utterly infinite might, against which nothing objective — however much it may present itself as something outward, far from reason and independent of it — could hold out, could possess a nature of its own over against it, or could stay unpervaded by it. That is why it is _soul_ _and_ _substance_: nothing whatever is comprehended, nothing known in its truth, except as it stands _wholly_ _under_ _the_ _method_; and it is the method belonging to each fact in its own right, since what the fact does is the concept. This too is the truer sense of its _universality_: on the universality of reflection method passes merely for the method applicable to _everything_; on the universality of the idea, though, it is at once the manner and way of cognition — of the concept that knows itself _subjectively_ — and the _objective_ manner and way, or rather the _substantiality_ of _things_, which is to say of concepts, so far as these at first look to _representation_ and to _reflection_ like _others_. Reason therefore finds in it not only its highest _might_, or rather its _one_ absolute _might_, but likewise its highest and only _drive_: _through_ _itself_ _to_ _find_ _and_ _know_ _itself_ _in_ _everything_. – With this, _secondly_, we have stated the _difference_ _between_ _method_ _and_ _the_ _concept_ _as_ _such_, that is, the _particular_ side of method. Where the concept came under review on its own, it showed itself in its immediacy; the _reflection_ upon it, _the_ _concept_ _that_ _reviews_ _it_, fell to _our_ knowing. Method is that knowing itself: for it the concept counts not merely as its subject matter but as its own subjective doing, as the _instrument_ and means of cognizing activity, marked off from that activity yet constituting its very essentiality. Where cognition searches, method is likewise set up as a _tool_, a means lying on the subjective side by which that side refers itself to the object. In that syllogism subject makes one extreme and object the other; the former closes with the latter by way of its method, yet in doing so does not for itself close _together_ _with_ _itself_. Because subject, method and object are not posited as _the_ _one_ _identical_ _concept_, the extremes stay diverse and the syllogism stays a {{{ 12.239 }}} formal one throughout; the premise in which the subject puts form on its own side as its method is an _immediate_ determination and consequently holds the determinations of form — those of definition, division and so on, as we saw — as so many facts _already_ _found_ _in_ _the_ _subject_. In genuine cognition, by contrast, method is no mere aggregate of certain determinations but the concept's being-determined in and for itself; the concept is the middle only because it carries the meaning of the objective just as much, so that in the conclusion the objective picks up not just an outward determinateness through method but stands posited in its identity with the subjective concept.

1. What constitutes the method, then, are the determinations of the concept itself along with their references, and these must now be looked at in their significance as determinations of method. – Here one has to start, _firstly_, from the _beginning_. About the beginning enough was said both where logic itself began and a moment ago under subjective cognition, and it was shown there that, provided it is not undertaken arbitrarily and with a categorical want of awareness, it can indeed look like a source of many difficulties while being in fact of the utmost simplicity. Its content, since it is the beginning, is something _immediate_, though an immediate carrying the sense and the form of _abstract_ _universality_. Whether its content otherwise belongs to _being_, to _essence_ or to the _concept_, it counts as something _picked_ _up_, _come_ _upon_, _assertoric_, precisely so far as it is something _immediate_. _In_ _the_ _first_ _place_, though, what it is immediate is not _of_ _sensuous_ _intuition_ nor _of_ _representation_, but of _thinking_, which for the sake of its immediacy may be styled a supersensuous, _inward_ _intuiting_ as well. Whatever is immediate in sensuous intuition is a _manifold_ and something _singular_. Cognition, however, is comprehending thought, so that where it starts lies _solely_ _inside_ _the_ _element_ _of_ _thinking_; it is something _simple_, something _universal_. – That form already came up earlier under definition. Finite cognition, at its start, likewise grants universality the rank of an essential determination, but grants it only as a determination of thought and of concept set against being. This _first_ universality is in truth an _immediate_ one, and for that very reason carries the meaning of _being_ no less; for being is precisely that abstract self-reference. No further derivation is needed for being, as though it fell to the abstractum of the definition only on the ground of having been fetched out of sensuous intuition or out of somewhere else and to that extent pointed out. Such pointing out and deducing has to do with a _mediation_ that amounts to more than a bare beginning, and with a mediation of a kind that belongs not to thinking comprehension but to the {{{ 12.240 }}} lifting of representation, of empirical and ratiocinating consciousness, up to the standpoint of thought. On the familiar antithesis of thought or concept and being it looks like a weighty truth that no being as yet belongs to the former on its own, and that being has a ground of its own which is independent of thought. But so meagre in itself is the plain determination of _being_ that on that score alone there is little occasion to make a stir over it; the universal is straightaway itself this immediate, since as something abstract it too is nothing but that abstract self-reference which being is. In truth the requirement that being be exhibited carries a deeper inner sense than that abstract determination; what it intends is rather the requirement that the _concept_ _be_ _realized_ at large, and that lies not in the _beginning_ but is the aim and the labour of the entire further unfolding of cognition. Further, where the _content_ of the beginning is supposed to gain justification by being pointed out in inner or outer perception, and thereby credentials as something true or right, then what one has in view is no longer universality's _form_ as such but rather its _determinateness_, which we must speak of at once. Credentials for the _determinate_ _content_ that the start is made with seem to lie _behind_ the start; in truth, however, they count as a going forward, so long as they belong to comprehending cognition.

For method, accordingly, the beginning carries no determinateness beyond that of being simple and universal; and this is itself the _determinateness_ that makes it defective. The pure, plain concept is universality, and method, as consciousness of that concept, knows universality to be a mere moment and knows that within it the concept is not yet determined in and for itself. Were the beginning carried further merely on the strength of such a consciousness, merely for method's sake, method would be a formal affair posited in outward reflection. Since it is instead the objective, immanent form, what is immediate at the beginning has to be the defective thing _on_ _its_ _own_ _account_, and has to be furnished with the _drive_ to carry itself on. Within the absolute method the universal holds good not as something merely abstract but as the objectively universal, in other words as that which _in_ _itself_ is the _concrete_ _totality_, a totality nevertheless not yet _posited_, not yet _for_ _itself_. Even the abstract universal as such, taken within the concept, taken that is according to its truth, is not the _simple_ alone; being an _abstractum_, it stands already _posited_ as burdened with a _negation_. Hence there also _is_ nothing, whether in _actuality_ or in _thought_, of the simplicity and abstractness people ordinarily picture. A simple of that sort is mere _supposing_, grounded in nothing but obliviousness to what is really present. – Earlier we determined what begins as the immediate; the _immediacy_ _of_ _the_ _universal_ is the same thing that gets expressed here as _being-in-itself_ lacking {{{ 12.241 }}} _being-for-itself_. – One may well say, then, that every beginning has to be made with the _absolute_ and that all advance is nothing but the exhibiting of it, so far as _what_ _has_ _being_ _in_ _itself_ is the concept. Yet just because it as yet only _is_ _in_ _itself_, it is equally _not_ the absolute, not the posited concept, not the idea either; for what these amount to is precisely that _being-in-itself_ is only an abstract, one-sided moment. Advance is thus no sort of _excess_; it would be that if what begins were truly the absolute already; going forward consists rather in this, that the universal determines itself and is _for_ _itself_ the universal, which is to say, is a singular and a subject just as much. It is the absolute only where it is complete.

One may recall that a beginning which _in_ _itself_ is concrete totality can as such be _free_ too, and that its immediacy can bear the determination of an _outward_ _existence_; the _germ_ of what _lives_, and the _subjective_ _purpose_ generally, showed themselves to be beginnings of that kind, which is why both are themselves _drives_. In what is neither spirit nor alive, on the other hand, the concrete concept is present only as _real_ _possibility_; the highest rung on which the concrete concept, as a beginning inside the sphere of necessity, has immediate existence is the _cause_; but a cause is not yet a subject keeping itself intact as such through its actual realization as well. The _sun_, for example, and everything lifeless whatever, are determinate concrete existences where real possibility stays an _inner_ totality, whose moments are not _posited_ in them in subjective form, and which, so far as they do realize themselves, gain a concrete existence by way of _other_ corporeal individuals.

2. Concrete totality, being what makes the beginning, contains as such within itself the beginning of going forward and of development. Concrete as it is, it is _differentiated_ _within_ _itself_; but owing to its _first_ _immediacy_ what is first differentiated stands at the outset merely as _diverse_. As self-referring universality, as subject, however, the immediate is also the _unity_ of those diverse terms. – That reflection makes the first rung of the onward movement – the coming forth of _difference_, _the_ _judgment_, _determining_ at large. The essential thing is that absolute method should find and know the _determination_ of the universal inside the universal itself. The finite cognition of the understanding goes about it by putting back into the concrete, just as externally, whatever it dropped out of the concrete when it abstractively produced that universal. Absolute method, on the contrary, does not conduct itself as outward reflection: it draws the determinate out of its own subject matter, being itself that subject matter's immanent principle and soul. – Just this is what _Plato_ required of cognition, {{{ 12.242 }}} _that_ _things_ _be_ _considered_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _themselves_, partly in their universality, partly in such a way that one does not wander off from them and clutch at circumstances, instances and comparisons, but keeps them alone in view and brings to consciousness what is immanent in them. – So far, then, the method of absolute cognition is _analytic_. That it _finds_ the further determination of its initial universal within that universal and nowhere else is the absolute objectivity of the concept, of which objectivity method is the certainty. – Equally, though, it is _synthetic_, since its subject matter, determined immediately as a _simple_ _universal_, reveals itself as an _other_ through the determinateness it possesses in that very immediacy and universality. Such reference of something diverse, which the subject matter thus is inwardly, is nevertheless no longer what finite cognition means by synthesis; already through the equally analytic determination generally belonging to it, namely that it is reference inside the _concept_, it parts company altogether with that synthetic sort.

That moment of _judgment_ — synthetic and analytic in equal measure — by which the initial universal determines itself out of itself as the _other_ _of_ _itself_ is what deserves the name of the _dialectical_. Among the ancient sciences _dialectic_ is one of those that the metaphysics of the moderns, and popular philosophy generally, ancient and modern alike, have most misjudged. Diogenes Laertius reports of _Plato_ that, Thales having originated natural philosophy and Socrates moral philosophy, Plato originated the third science belonging to philosophy, _dialectic_; – antiquity reckoned this to him as his highest merit, though it commonly goes altogether unnoticed by those who have his name oftenest on their lips. Often enough dialectic has been treated as an _art_, as if it rested upon a subjective _gift_ and had no part in the objectivity of the concept. The shape it took and the result it yielded in the Kantian philosophy have already been displayed in determinate examples of that philosophy's outlook. That dialectic has again been acknowledged as belonging necessarily to reason must count as a step of infinite importance, even though the result to be drawn has to be the opposite of the one drawn there.

Apart from the fact that dialectic ordinarily looks like something accidental, the more precise form it usually takes is this: of some subject matter or other, world, motion, point and so on, for example, one shows that some determination or other belongs to it (following the order of the objects just listed: finitude in space or in time, being at _this_ place, absolute negation of space), and then, no less necessarily, that the contrary determination belongs to it too (infinity in space and in time, not being at this place, reference to space and therefore spatiality). Chiefly it was against motion that the older Eleatic school turned its dialectic; Plato turned his repeatedly against the representations and {{{ 12.243 }}} concepts current in his day, those of the sophists above all, yet also against the pure categories and determinations of reflection; and the educated scepticism of a later time stretched it beyond the immediate so-called facts of consciousness and the maxims of everyday living to every scientific concept as well. The conclusion that gets drawn from dialectic of this sort is on the whole the _contradiction_ and the _nullity_ of the assertions advanced. That, however, admits of two senses – objectively, that the _subject_ _matter_ contradicting itself in such fashion cancels itself and is null; – so, for example, ran the conclusion of the Eleatics, who on this ground refused _truth_ to world, to motion, to point; – or subjectively, that _cognition_ _is_ _defective_. By this second conclusion one may understand either that it is dialectic alone which puts on the conjuring act of a false shine. Such is the customary view of what passes for sound human understanding, which clings to _sensuous_ evidence and to _familiar_ _representations_ and _sayings_ – now more placidly, as Diogenes the Dog laid bare the dialectic of motion by pacing to and fro without a word, now flying into a fury about it, treating it as mere tomfoolery, or, where matters of ethical weight are at stake, as an outrage bent on unsettling what is essentially firm and on teaching vice how to supply itself with reasons – an outlook met with in the Socratic dialectic against the sophistic one, and a rage which in turn cost Socrates his own life. Rabble-style refutation of the kind Diogenes practised, which confronts thought with _sensuous_ _consciousness_ and supposes truth to lie there, one had better leave to itself; but where dialectic cancels ethical determinations, one should trust reason to know how to reinstate them, in their truth this time, together with a consciousness of their right and of their restriction as well. – Or else the outcome of subjective nullity bears not on dialectic but rather on the cognition dialectic is aimed at; and, in the sense of scepticism and equally of the Kantian philosophy, on _cognition_ _at_ _large_.

The root prejudice in all this is that dialectic yields _nothing_ _but_ _a_ _negative_ _result_ — a claim that will shortly get its closer determination. About the _form_ cited, the one in which dialectic habitually appears, this much should be noted first: taken so, dialectic and its result bear upon the _subject_ _matter_ under discussion or else upon subjective _cognition_, pronouncing the one or the other null, while the _determinations_ pointed out on it as on some _third_ go unremarked and are presupposed to hold good on their own. That the Kantian {{{ 12.244 }}} philosophy drew notice to this uncritical way of proceeding, and so gave the impulse toward reinstating logic and dialectic in the sense of examining the _determinations_ _of_ _thought_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _themselves_, is a merit of infinite worth. Take the subject matter apart from thinking and the concept, and it is a representation, or else a name; it is in the determinations of thought and of concept that it _is_ whatever it _is_. Everything therefore hangs on these alone; they are what reason genuinely deals with and genuinely contains, and whatever else one takes subject matter and content to mean apart from them holds good only through them and within them. One must not, then, lay it to the charge of a subject matter or of cognition that they turn out dialectical by reason of their constitution and of some outward linkage. The one and the other get pictured in this way as a subject into which the _determinations_ have been introduced under the form of predicates, properties, self-subsistent universals, so that these, firm and correct on their own, are thrown into dialectical relations and into contradiction only by the alien, accidental combining in and by some third. But a subject of that outward, fixed sort, belonging to representation and understanding, and likewise the abstract determinations, far from being able to pass for _ultimates_ that securely stay underneath, must themselves be viewed as an immediate, as just such a presupposed and beginning term which, as shown above, has to succumb to dialectic in and for itself, since it is to be taken as concept _in_ _itself_. So too every opposition supposed to be fixed, finite and infinite, say, or singular and universal, falls into contradiction through no outward linkage; rather, as examining their nature has shown, they are the passing over in and for themselves; the synthesis, and the subject in which they turn up, is the product of their own concept's reflection. Should conceptless examination halt at their outward relation, leaving them isolated as fixed presuppositions, it is the concept, on the contrary, that keeps its eye upon them, stirs them as their soul, and brings their dialectic out.

This is precisely the standpoint marked out above, on which a universal first, _examined_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, turns out to be the other of itself. Grasped in the most general terms, the determination may be put thus: what was at first _immediate_ is herewith posited as _mediated_, as _referred_ to an other; or the universal is posited as a particular. The _second_ term arising in this way is accordingly the _negative_ of the first; and, with an eye already on the further course, the _first_ _negative_. On this negative side the immediate has _gone_ _under_ in the other, yet the other is essentially not the _empty_ _negative_, the _nothing_ {{{ 12.245 }}} usually taken for dialectic's result, but is the _other_ _of_ _the_ _first_, the _negative_ of the _immediate_; determined therefore as the _mediated_, – it _holds_ the _determination_ _of_ _the_ _first_ within it throughout. Essentially, then, the first is _stored_ _up_ and _kept_ in the other as well. – Holding on to the positive within _its_ negative, to the content of the presupposition within the result, is what counts for most in rational cognition; the barest reflection suffices, moreover, to convince one of the absolute truth and necessity of this requirement, and as for _instances_ of its proof, the whole of logic is nothing else.

What we now have before us is the _mediated_, which, taken at first or equally immediately, is likewise a _simple_ determination, for once the first has gone under in it only the second is there. And since the first is also _held_ in the second, the second being the first's truth, this unity admits of expression as a proposition in which the immediate stands as subject and the mediated stands as predicate of it: _the_ _finite_ _is_ _infinite_, for example, or _one_ _is_ _many_, or _the_ _singular_ _is_ _the_ _universal_. How inadequate the form of propositions and judgments of that sort is, though, strikes one of itself. Under the heading of _judgment_ it was shown that judgment's form generally, and most of all the immediate form of the _positive_ judgment, cannot contain the speculative and the truth. One would at least have to append the nearest supplement to it, the _negative_ judgment. Within the judgment the first, as subject, carries the shine of subsisting on its own, whereas it is rather sublated in its predicate as in its other; the content of those propositions does indeed contain this negation, but their positive form runs counter to that content; what they contain therefore fails to get posited, which is exactly what using a proposition was meant to achieve.

The second determination — the _negative_ or _mediated_ — is furthermore the _mediating_ one at the same time. At first it may be taken for a simple determination, but by its truth it is a _reference_ or _relation_; for it is the negative, _yet_ _of_ _the_ _positive_, and encloses the positive within it. So it is the _other_ not in the sense of being other than something towards which it stands indifferent — in that case it would be no other, nor a reference or relation at all; – it is rather the _other_ _in_ _its_ _own_ _self_, the _other_ _of_ _an_ _other_; hence it encloses _its_ own other within it and thus is, _as_ _contradiction_, the _posited_ _dialectic_ _of_ _itself_. – Since the first or immediate is the concept _in_ _itself_ and hence is the negative also only _in_ _itself_, its dialectical moment lies in the _difference_ it contains _in_ _itself_ getting posited {{{ 12.246 }}} in it. The second, on the other hand, is itself the _determinate_, the _difference_ or relation; its dialectical moment therefore lies in positing the _unity_ that it contains. – If, then, the negative, the determinate, relation, judgment and all the determinations coming under this second moment do not already of themselves show up as contradiction and as dialectical, that is mere shortcoming in a thinking which fails to put its thoughts together. For the material — the _opposed_ determinations within _one_ _reference_ — is already _posited_ and there for thought. Formal thinking, however, sets identity up as its law, lets the contradictory content lying before it sink down into the sphere of representation, into space and time, where the contradictory terms are kept _outside_ _one_ _another_ in juxtaposition and succession and so present themselves to consciousness without touching. About this it lays down the express principle that contradiction cannot be thought; in fact, though, thinking contradiction is the concept's essential moment. Formal thinking does think it, as a matter of fact, only it looks away from it at once and in that pronouncement merely goes over from it to abstract negation.

It is this negativity we have been looking at that now makes the _turning_ _point_ in the concept's movement. It is the _simple_ _point_ _of_ _negative_ _reference_ to itself, the innermost wellspring of all activity, of living and spiritual self-movement, the dialectical soul which everything true possesses in itself and by which alone it is true; for it is upon this subjectivity alone that there rests the sublating of the opposition between concept and reality, and the unity that constitutes truth. – The _second_ negative, the negative of the negative we have reached, is that sublating of contradiction; but no more than contradiction is it a _deed_ _of_ _outward_ _reflection_ — it is the _innermost_, _most_ _objective_ _moment_ of life and of spirit, by which there is a _subject_, a _person_, something _free_. – _The_ _negative's_ _reference_ _to_ _itself_ is to be looked upon as the _second_ _premise_ of the syllogism as a whole. Should the determinations _analytic_ and _synthetic_ be employed in their antithesis, the _first_ premise may be viewed as the _analytic_ moment, since in it the immediate stands _immediately_ related to its other and hence _goes_ _over_ into it, or rather has gone over; – though this reference, as was already recalled, is on that very account synthetic too, because that into which it goes over is its _other_. The second premise, the one under review here, admits of being determined as the _synthetic_ one, for it is the reference of what is _differentiated_ _as_ _such_ to _what_ _is_ _differentiated_ _from_ _it_. – Where the first is the moment of _universality_ and of _imparting_, the second is determined by _singularity_, which at the outset, exclusive and as something for itself and diverse, refers itself to the {{{ 12.247 }}} other. The negative shows up as the _mediating_ term because it encloses within itself both itself and the immediate whose negation it is. So far as the two determinations are taken as referred outwardly to each other in some relation, we have only the mediating _formality_; as absolute negativity, however, the negative moment of absolute mediation is the unity that constitutes subjectivity and soul.

At this turn of the method, the course cognition runs also goes back into itself. Being the contradiction that sublates itself, this negativity is the _reinstating_ of the _first_ _immediacy_, of plain universality; for immediately the other's other, the negation of the negative, is the _positive_, the _identical_, the _universal_. Should one care to _count_ at all, this _second_ immediate is, within the whole course, the _third_, reckoned against the first immediate and against the mediated. But it is also the third reckoned against the first or formal negative and against absolute negativity, the second negative; and since that first negative is already the second term, whatever gets counted as _third_ may be counted as _fourth_ too, and in place of _triplicity_ the abstract form may be taken for a _quadruplicity_; the negative, or _difference_, is on this reckoning counted as a twofoldness. – Third or fourth, it is at all events the unity of the first and second moments, of the immediate and of the mediated. – Admittedly, that it is this _unity_, and that the whole form of the method is a _triplicity_, belongs merely to the surface, the outward side of the way cognition goes; but to have pointed out even this much, and in a more determinate application at that — since the abstract numerical form itself, as everyone knows, was put forward long ago, only without concept and hence without consequence — [is] again to be counted an infinite merit of the Kantian philosophy. The _syllogism_, threefold likewise, has all along been recognized as reason's universal form; but in part it passed for a wholly outward form settling nothing about the nature of the content, and in part, since in the formal sense it runs itself out in the understanding's determination of _identity_, it wants the essential, _dialectical_ moment, _negativity_; this moment does enter with the triplicity of determinations, however, because the third is the unity of the first two, and these, being diverse, can be in unity only _as_ _sublated_. – Formalism, to be sure, laid hold of triplicity as well and stuck to its empty _schema_; the shallow mischief and the bareness of the modern philosophical so-called _constructing_, which amounts to nothing beyond hanging that formal schema on everywhere, without concept and without immanent determination, and using it for an outward tidying-up, {{{ 12.248 }}} has made the form tiresome and given it a bad name. Its inner worth cannot be diminished by the vapidity of such use, and it always deserves high regard that the shape of what is rational should have been discovered at least, even if uncomprehended.

More closely, the _third_ is now the immediate, but _by_ _sublation_ _of_ _the_ _mediation_, the simple by _sublating_ _of_ _difference_, the positive by sublating of the negative — the concept that has realized itself by way of otherness and, sublating that reality, has closed with itself and reinstated its absolute reality, its _plain_ self-reference. This _result_ is accordingly the _truth_. It _is_ _no_ _less_ immediacy _than_ mediation; – yet those judgment forms, the third _is_ immediacy and mediation, or it _is_ _the_ _unity_ of them, cannot capture it, since it is no third at rest but is, precisely as this unity, the movement and activity that mediates itself with itself. – What begins is the _universal_; the result, by contrast, is the _singular_, the _concrete_, the _subject_; and what the one is _in_ _itself_ the other now is _for_ _itself_ just as much, the universal being _posited_ in the subject. Those first two moments of the triplicity are the _abstract_, untrue moments, dialectical for that very reason, and by this negativity of theirs they make themselves into the subject. _For_ _us_, to begin with, the concept itself is _at_ _once_ the universal having being in itself _and_ the negative having being for itself, and also the third having being in and for itself, the _universal_ that runs through every moment of the syllogism; the third, however, is the conclusion, wherein the concept, mediated with itself by its negativity, thereby stands posited _for_ _itself_ as the _universal_ and as what is _identical_ _in_ _its_ _moments_.

As the whole gone back into itself and _identical_ with itself, this result has now given itself the form of _immediacy_ once more. It is therefore now itself such as what _begins_ had determined itself to be. Being plain self-reference, it is a universal, and the _negativity_ which made up its dialectic and mediation has, within this universality, likewise collapsed into the _plain_ _determinateness_ that can serve as a beginning again. One might suppose at first that knowing this result must be an analysis of it and must therefore unpack once more the determinations and their course, by which it came about and which has been under review. But should the subject matter really be handled in this analytic way, that belongs to the stage of the idea reviewed above, to searching cognition, which of its subject matter states nothing but what _is_, leaving out the necessity of its concrete identity and the concept thereof. The method of truth, which comprehends the subject matter, is indeed, as shown, analytic itself, staying utterly within the concept, but it is synthetic just as much, for by {{{ 12.249 }}} the concept the subject matter gets determined dialectically and as an other. Upon the new foundation constituted by the result as the subject matter now in hand, the method stays what it was with the previous one. The difference touches only how the foundation stands as foundation; it is that now as well, to be sure, yet its immediacy is nothing but _form_, seeing that it was a result at the same time; hence its determinateness as content is no longer something merely picked up but something _derived_ and _proved_.

Only here does the _content_ of cognition as such step into the circle of consideration, since as derived it now belongs to the method. By this moment the method itself widens out into a _system_. – At the outset the beginning had to be for it entirely indeterminate as far as content goes; to that extent it shows up as the merely formal soul, for and through which the beginning stood determined solely as regards its _form_, namely as the immediate and universal. By the movement pointed out, the subject matter has acquired a _determinateness_ of its own which is a _content_, because the negativity collapsed into simplicity is the sublated form and, as plain determinateness, confronts its own unfolding, in the first instance its own opposition to universality.

Now that this determinateness is the nearest truth of the indeterminate beginning, it faults that beginning as something incomplete, and faults the method too, which starting from it was merely formal. One may put this as the now determinate demand that the beginning, being itself something determinate over against the determinateness of the result, ought to be taken not as immediate but as mediated and derived; which can look like a demand for the endless progress _backwards_ in proving and deriving; just as out of the new beginning obtained there likewise issues a result by the course of the method, so that the advance rolls on _forwards_ into infinity in the same way.

That endless progress belongs on the whole to conceptless reflection has been shown often enough; the absolute method, having the concept for soul and content, cannot lead into it. Beginnings like _being_, _essence_, _universality_ may at first look to be of a kind possessing that entire universality and lack of content which a wholly formal beginning, such as it ought to be, requires, and hence, as absolutely first beginnings, to demand and permit no going back behind them. Being pure self-references, immediate and indeterminate, they certainly do not carry on them the difference which in any other beginning gets posited straightaway between the universality of its form and its content. But the very indeterminateness which those logical beginnings have for their sole content {{{ 12.250 }}} is what makes their determinateness: that determinateness consists in their negativity as sublated mediation; and the particularity of the mediation lends their indeterminateness a particularity as well, by which _being_, _essence_ and _universality_ get told apart. The determinateness that falls to them, then, taken as they are taken for themselves, is their _immediate_ _determinateness_, quite as much as that of any content whatever, and it therefore calls for a derivation; whether determinateness be taken as determinateness of _form_ or of _content_ is a matter of indifference for the method. So in fact no new mode sets [in] for the method with a content having determined itself through the first of its results; it thereby stays neither more nor less formal than before. For being the absolute form, the concept that knows itself and knows everything as concept, it has no content confronting it that might determine it into a one-sided, outward form. Just as the contentlessness of those beginnings does not turn them into absolute beginnings, so neither is it the content that as such would carry the method into an endless progress forwards or backwards. From one side the _determinateness_ the method generates for itself in its result is the moment by which it is mediation with itself and turns _the_ _immediate_ _beginning_ _into_ _something_ _mediated_. Conversely, though, determinateness is that through which this mediation of the method runs its course; the method travels _through_ a _content_ as through an apparent _other_ of its own, and comes back to its beginning in such fashion that it not merely reinstates it but reinstates it as a _determinate_ one, while the result is quite as much the sublated determinateness and so the reinstating too of that first indeterminateness with which it set out. This it achieves as _a_ _system_ _of_ _totality_. Under that determination it has still to be considered.

The determinateness that was result is, as shown, itself a new beginning, thanks to the form of simplicity into which it has collapsed; and since this beginning differs from the one preceding by just that determinateness, cognition rolls on from content to content. In the first instance this going forward determines itself thus, that it sets out from simple determinatenesses and that those which follow grow ever _richer_ and more _concrete_. For the result holds its own beginning, and the course that beginning ran has enriched it by a fresh determinateness. _The_ _universal_ makes the foundation; the advance is therefore not to be taken for a _flowing_ from one _other_ to another _other_. In the absolute method the concept _keeps_ _itself_ in its otherness, the universal keeps itself in its particularizing, in judgment and in reality; at every rung of further determination it lifts up the entire mass of its earlier content, and by its dialectical going forward it not only loses nothing and leaves nothing behind, but bears everything gained along with it, growing richer and denser within itself.

{{{ 12.251 }}} One may view this _widening_ as the content's moment, and, taken in the whole, as the first premise; to the wealth of content the universal has been _imparted_ and is immediately preserved in it. But the relation has its second, negative or dialectical side as well. Enrichment goes forward along the _necessity_ of the concept, is held fast by the concept, and every determination is a reflection into self. Every fresh rung of _going-out-of-itself_, which is to say _of_ _further_ _determining_, is at once a going-into-itself, and greater _extension_ is quite as much heightened _intensity_. What is richest is therefore what is most _concrete_ and most _subjective_, and what draws itself back into the simplest depth is what is mightiest and reaches over the most. The highest, keenest peak is _pure_ _personality_, which through the absolute dialectic that is its nature, and through that alone, no less _embraces_ _everything_ _within_ _itself_ and holds it, since it makes itself into the freest thing there is – into that simplicity which is the first immediacy and universality.

In this fashion every step of the _advance_ in determining further, while withdrawing from the indeterminate beginning, is at the same time an _approach_ _back_ to it, so that what may at first look like two different things, the _backward_-_running_ _grounding_ of the beginning and the _forward-running_ _further_ _determining_ of it, coincide and are one and the same. The method, which thereby winds itself into a circle, cannot, however, anticipate in a temporal unfolding that the beginning is as such already something derived; for the beginning in its immediacy it is enough that it be plain universality. So far as it is that, it has its condition complete; and there is no call to beg that it be let pass merely as _provisional_ and _hypothetical_. Whatever might be urged against it – about the restrictions of human cognition, say, or about the requirement of critically examining the instrument of cognition before one takes up the fact – is itself so many _presuppositions_ which, being _concrete_ _determinations_, bring with them the demand that they be mediated and grounded. Since formally they have thus no advantage over the _beginning_ with the fact against which they enter their protest, and since on account of their more concrete content they stand rather in need of derivation, they can only be taken for idle pretensions to be attended to sooner than anything else. Their content is untrue, for they make what is acknowledged to be finite and untrue into something unshakeable and absolute, namely a _cognition_ that is _limited_ and determined _as_ _form_ _and_ _instrument_ _over_ _against_ _its_ _content_; and this untrue cognition is itself likewise the form, the backward-running grounding. – The method of truth also knows the beginning to be something incomplete, because it is beginning, but knows at the same time that this incompleteness in general is something necessary, since truth is nothing but the coming-to-itself by way of the negativity of immediacy. Impatience, which wants merely to get past the _determinate_ — {{{ 12.252 }}} call it beginning, object, finite, or take it in whatever other form — and to be in the absolute straight away, has as knowledge nothing before it save the empty negative, the abstract infinite; – or else an absolute that is merely _supposed_, and merely _supposed_ because it is not _posited_, not _laid_ _hold_ _of_; laid hold of it can be only through the _mediation_ of cognition, of which the _universal_ and immediate is one moment, whereas truth itself lies only in the unfolded course and at the end. – For the subjective need of those unacquainted with it, and for their impatience, a survey of the _whole_ may indeed be offered _beforehand_ – by way of a division for reflection which, out of the universal and after the manner of finite cognition, states the particular as something _on_ _hand_ and to be looked for in the science. Yet that yields no more than a picture for _representation_; for the genuine transition from the universal to the particular and to the whole determined in and for itself, wherein that first universal is itself once more a moment according to its genuine determination, is foreign to such a way of dividing and is the mediation of the science itself and nothing else.

Owing to the nature of the method as pointed out, science presents itself as a _circle_ wound into itself, a circle into whose beginning, the simple ground, mediation winds the end back; and this circle is moreover a _circle_ _of_ _circles_, for each single link, ensouled as it is by the method, is reflection-into-self which, returning into the beginning, is at once the beginning of a fresh link. The individual sciences are pieces broken off this chain, each having a _before_ and an _after_ – or, to speak more exactly, each _having_ only the _before_, while _showing_ its _after_ in its own conclusion.

Thus in the absolute idea logic too has gone back to that plain unity which is its beginning; the pure immediacy of being, in which every determination looks at first extinguished, or dropped by way of abstraction, is the idea come by mediation, that is, by the sublating of mediation, to the equality with itself that answers to it. The method is the pure concept that relates to nothing but itself; hence it is the _plain_ _reference_ _to_ _itself_ which is _being_. But now this is _fulfilled_ being as well, the _concept_ _comprehending_ _itself_, being as the _concrete_ and equally _utterly_ _intensive_ _totality_. – Of this idea there is, by way of close, only this further to be mentioned: that in it, _firstly_, the _science_ _of_ _logic_ has _laid_ _hold_ _of_ its own concept. At _being_, the _beginning_ _of_ _its_ _content_, its concept shows up as a knowing external to that content, in subjective reflection. In the idea of absolute cognition, however, the concept has become the science's own content. The science is itself the pure concept having itself for subject matter, and this concept, running through the totality of its determinations as its own subject matter, builds itself into the whole of its {{{ 12.253 }}} reality, into the system of science, closing with the act of laying hold of this comprehending of itself, thereby sublating its standing as content and subject matter and coming to know the concept of the science. – _Secondly_, this idea is still logical, shut up within pure thought, the science of the _divine_ _concept_ only. Carrying the system out is indeed itself a realization, but one kept inside the same sphere. Because the pure idea of cognition is to that extent shut up within subjectivity, it is a _drive_ to sublate that subjectivity, and pure truth, as the last result, becomes the _beginning_ _of_ _another_ _sphere_ _and_ _science_ as well. Here that transition needs no more than to be intimated.

For in positing itself as absolute _unity_ of the pure concept and its reality, and so drawing itself together into the immediacy of _being_, the idea, as _totality_ under this form, is – _nature_. – This determination is, however, no _having-become_ and no _transition_, of the sort in which, higher up, the subjective concept in its totality becomes _objectivity_, or the _subjective_ _purpose_ becomes _life_. The pure idea, in which the determinateness or reality of the concept has itself been raised into concept, is rather absolute _liberation_, for which there is no immediate determination left that is not equally _posited_ and concept; within this freedom, accordingly, no transition takes place, the plain being into which the idea determines itself stays wholly transparent to it and is the concept that in its determination stays with itself. The passing over must accordingly be grasped here rather in this way, that the idea _freely_ _releases_ itself, utterly sure of itself and reposing within itself. For the sake of this freedom the _form_ _of_ _its_ _determinateness_ is just as utterly free – the _externality_ _of_ _space_ _and_ _time_ existing absolutely for itself without subjectivity. – So far as this externality only is according to the abstract immediacy of being and is grasped by consciousness, it is mere objectivity and outward life; within the idea, though, it remains in and for itself the totality of the concept, and science remains within the relation of divine cognition to nature. This nearest resolve of the pure idea to determine itself as external idea posits for itself thereby only the mediation out of which the concept lifts itself up as free concrete existence gone into itself out of externality, brings its liberation through itself to completion in the _science_ _of_ _spirit_, and finds the highest concept of itself in the science of logic, as the pure concept that comprehends itself.
