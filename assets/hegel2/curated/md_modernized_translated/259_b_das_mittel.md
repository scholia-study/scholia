## B. The Means

The first immediate positing in the purpose is at once the positing of an _inner_ — of what is determined, that is, as _posited_ — and equally the presupposing of an objective world indifferent to the determination of purpose. The subjectivity of the purpose, however, is the _absolute_ _negative_ _unity_; hence its _second_ determining consists in sublating this presupposition altogether; and such sublating counts as _the_ _return_ _into_ _itself_ insofar as what gets sublated thereby is that moment of the _first_ _negation_, the positing, over against the subject, of the negative — the external object. Yet as measured against the presupposition, or against the immediacy of the determining, against the objective world, it is still no more than the _first_ negation, one that is itself immediate and hence external. Such positing, accordingly, is still not the accomplished purpose itself but merely the _beginning_ of it. The object determined in this fashion is as yet nothing but the _means_.

By way of a means the purpose conjoins itself with objectivity, and within objectivity with its own self. It is the means that constitutes the middle term of the syllogism. For its accomplishment the purpose {{{ 12.163 }}} stands in need of a means, since it is finite; – of a means, which is to say of a middle term that carries at once the shape of an _external_ existence, one indifferent to the purpose itself and to the purpose's accomplishment. In the absolute concept mediation is so constituted that the concept's first positing is no presupposing in whose object indifferent externality would rank as the fundamental determination; the world as creature bears only the form of such externality, whereas what makes up its fundamental determination is rather its negativity and its positedness. – Wherein the finitude of the purpose consists is accordingly this, that its determining is on the whole external to itself, so that its first determining, as we saw, breaks apart into a positing and a presupposing; and so the _negation_ of this determining is on one side only already reflection into itself, while on the other side it amounts rather to mere _first_ negation; – or: reflection-into-itself is itself external to itself as well, and reflection outward.

What the means amounts to, then, is the _formal_ middle term of a _formal_ syllogism; measured against the _extreme_ of the subjective purpose it is an _external_ something, and for that very reason measured against the extreme of the objective purpose too; much as, in the formal syllogism, particularity amounts to a medius terminus that is indifferent and in whose room others may equally step. And just as that particularity is a middle term solely because in reference to the one extreme it is determinateness while in reference to the other it is a universal, so that it holds its mediating determination only relatively, by way of others, so likewise the means is the mediating middle term solely, in the first place, in being an immediate object, and in the second place in being a means through that reference to the extreme of the purpose which is _external_ to it; – a reference that is for it a form towards which it stands indifferent.

In the means, accordingly, concept and objectivity are joined only externally; so far it is a merely _mechanical_ _object_. The reference of the object to the purpose is one premise, or the immediate reference which, regarded from the side of the purpose, is, as was shown, _reflection_ _into_ _itself_; the means is a predicate that inheres; and its objectivity stands subsumed under the determination of purpose, this being universality on account of its concretion. Through this determination of purpose that attaches to it, the means is now in turn the subsuming factor over against the other extreme, the objectivity that for the present remains undetermined. – Conversely, as _immediate_ _objectivity_ the means possesses over against the subjective purpose the _universality_ of _existence_, which the subjective singularity of the purpose still goes without. – Since the purpose is thus present in the means at first only as an external determinateness, the purpose itself, as the negative unity, lies outside the means, and the means for its part is a mechanical object holding the purpose in it merely as a determinateness, not as the simple concretion of the totality. As the conjoining factor, however, the middle term has to be the purpose's totality in its own right. We have seen that the determination of purpose present in the means {{{ 12.164 }}} is at once reflection into itself; so far it is a _formal_ reference to self, inasmuch as the _determinateness_ has been posited as _real_ _indifference_, that is, as the _objectivity_ belonging to the means. But just on that account this reference, on the one side sheer subjectivity, is equally _activity_. – Within the subjective purpose the negative reference to self is still identical with determinateness as such, with the content and with externality. In the incipient objectification of the purpose, however — a becoming-other of the simple concept — those moments step apart from each other; or, conversely, it is precisely in this that such becoming-other, or externality itself, resides.

This entire middle term is thus, in its own self, the syllogism's totality, the extremes of which are made up by the abstract activity and the external means, while its middle is made up by the determinateness that the object has through the purpose, the determinateness whereby the object is a means. – Further, however, _universality_ is the _reference_ of purposive activity and means. An object is what the means is, _in_ _itself_ the concept's totality; against the purpose it musters no force of resistance such as it musters, in the first instance, against another immediate object. Being _in_ _itself_ identical with the purpose — the purpose being the posited concept — it is therefore through and through penetrable by it and open to this communication. Now, however, it is also _posited_ as penetrable by the concept, for in centrality it is a thing striving towards the negative unity; and in chemism likewise, whether as neutral or as differentiated, it has come to be something non-self-subsistent. – Its non-self-subsistence lies precisely in this, that it is the concept's totality only _in_ _itself_; the concept, though, is being-for-itself. Against the purpose the object accordingly bears the character of being powerless and of serving the purpose; the purpose is the object's subjectivity, its soul, which has its external side in the object.

Thus _immediately_ subjected to the purpose, the object is no extreme of the syllogism; this reference rather makes up one of the syllogism's premises. Yet the means has a side too on which it retains self-subsistence over against the purpose. The objectivity conjoined with the purpose in the means is, being conjoined with it only immediately, still external to it; and so the _presupposition_ persists. That is why the purpose's activity through the means remains directed against this presupposition; and the purpose counts as activity, no longer as mere drive and striving, just to the extent that in the means the moment of objectivity stands posited in its determinateness as an external, and that the concept's simple unity now has that moment within itself _as_ _such_.
