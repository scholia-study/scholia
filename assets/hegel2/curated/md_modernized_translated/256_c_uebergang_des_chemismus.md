## C. Transition of Chemism

Even ordinary chemistry supplies instances of chemical alteration where a body, for instance, allots a higher oxidation to one portion of its mass and so brings a further portion down to a lower degree of it, at which lower degree it first becomes capable of a neutral combination with another differentiated body brought into contact with it — a combination to which, at that first immediate degree, it would have been unreceptive. What takes place here is this: the object relates to another not according to some immediate, one-sided determinateness but, following the inner totality of an original _relation_, it _posits_ that _presupposition_ which a real reference demands of it, thereby furnishing itself with a middle whereby its concept is conjoined with its reality; here we have singularity that is determined in and for itself, the concrete concept serving as principle of _disjunction_ into extremes, and the _reunion_ of those extremes is the work of the _selfsame_ negative principle, which by that route returns to its first determination — returns, though, _objectified_.

Of _indifferent_ objectivity, and of determinateness in its _externality_, chemism is itself _the_ _first_ _negation_; hence the immediate self-subsistence of the object, and externality with it, still cling to it. On its own account it is thus not yet the totality of self-determination that comes forth out of it and in which it rather sublates itself. – Its totality is made up by the three syllogisms which have resulted: formal neutrality serves the first as its middle and the objects in tension as its extremes; for the second, the middle is what the first produced, namely real neutrality, while its extremes are the sundering activity and the indifferent element that this activity yields; the third, by contrast, is the concept in the course of realizing itself, laying down for itself the presupposition upon which the process of that realization depends, – a syllogism whose essence is the universal. Because of the immediacy and externality within whose determination chemical objectivity lies, _these_ _syllogisms_ _do_ _still_ _fall_ _asunder_. In its own product the first process expires — the product being the neutrality of the objects in tension — and only a differentiation coming to it from outside can rouse it once more; being conditioned by an {{{ 12.153 }}} immediate presupposition, it spends itself upon that. – Likewise the driving out of the differentiated extremes from the neutral, and equally their breaking down into abstract elements, has to start from _conditions_ _accruing_ _externally_ and from stimulations of the activity. Yet insofar as both essential moments of the process — neutralizing on one hand, parting and reduction on the other — are combined within a single and identical process, and the _joining_ and blunting of the tensed extremes is at the same time a _severance_ into such extremes, they do, owing to the externality still underlying everything, constitute _two_ _different_ sides; the extremes driven out in the same process are objects or materials other than those which come together in it; and insofar as the former emerge from it differentiated afresh, they have to turn outwards; their renewed neutralizing is not the process that occurred in the first case but another.

These various processes, however, which have proved necessary, are so many _steps_ whereby _externality_ and _being-conditioned_ get sublated, and from which the concept comes forth as a totality determined in and for itself, one not conditioned by anything external. What the first sublates is the externality that the differentiated extremes, which constitute the whole reality, have against each other — or the difference between the determinate concept _in_ _itself_ and its determinateness as _existent_; in the second, what gets sublated is the externality of the real unity, the joining taken as merely _neutral_; – more nearly, formal activity sublates itself to begin with in bases just as formal, in indifferent determinatenesses whose _inner_ _concept_ is by now the absolute activity gone into itself and realizing itself in its own self, that is, an activity that _posits_ determinate differences inside itself and, by such _mediation_, constitutes itself as real unity, – a mediation, therefore, belonging to the concept as its _own_, being its self-determination and, with regard to the concept's reflection out of it into itself, an immanent _presupposing_. As for the third syllogism, it is on one hand the restoration of the processes gone before, while on the other it sublates the final moment of _indifferent_ bases too, – that quite abstract external _immediacy_ which thus turns into a moment _proper_ to the concept's mediating of itself by itself. Having thus sublated every moment of its objective existence as external and set them within its simple unity, the concept stands completely liberated from objective externality, to which it now relates only as to an unessential reality; and this objective, free concept is _purpose_.
