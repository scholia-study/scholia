## Remark

Of late the ought has been given a large part to play in philosophy, above all where morality is concerned, and metaphysically too as the ultimate and absolute concept of the identity of being-in-itself, or of reference to _itself_, with _determinateness_, or the limit.

{{{ 21.121 }}} _You_ _can_, _because_ _you_ _ought_ – this expression, from which much was expected, lies in the concept of the ought. For being beyond the restriction is just what the ought is; in it the limit is sublated, and so the being-in-itself of the ought is an identical reference to self, hence the abstraction of _being able_. – Conversely, though, it holds just as well: _You_ _cannot_, _precisely_ _because_ _you_ _ought_. For within the ought there lies just as much the restriction as restriction; that formalism of possibility has confronting it, in the restriction, a reality, a qualitative otherness, and the reference of the two to each other is contradiction, hence a not-being-able, or rather impossibility.

It is in the ought that the going out beyond finitude, infinity, has its beginning. The ought is that which, as development proceeds and in consequence of that impossibility, presents itself as the infinite progress.

Two prejudices touching the form of _restriction_ and of the _ought_ deserve a closer censure here. In the first place, a _great deal_ is customarily made of the restrictions of thinking, of reason and so forth, and the assertion is put forward that the restriction _cannot_ be gone beyond. What such an assertion fails to notice is that, in the very determining of something as a restriction, that restriction has already been passed beyond. For only in contrast to its other as such, that is, to its _unrestricted_, does a determinateness, a limit, count as determined into a restriction; the other of a restriction is precisely the _beyond_ of it. Stone and metal do not pass beyond their restriction, and this because the restriction is not a restriction _for_ _them_. If, however, in the face of such general propositions of understanding-bound thinking – that the restriction cannot be gone beyond – thinking declines to put itself to work and see what lies in the concept, then one can point it to actuality, where propositions of that sort turn out to be the most unactual of all. Just because thinking _is_ _supposed_ to be something higher than actuality, to hold itself at a distance from it in loftier regions, and so is itself determined as an _ought_, on the one hand it does not advance to the concept, and on the other it befalls it that its bearing is as untrue towards actuality as it is towards the concept. – Since the stone does not think, does not so much as feel, its limitedness is no restriction _for_ _it_, which is to say, in it there is no negation for the feeling, representation, thinking and so forth that it does not have. Yet even the stone, being a something, is differentiated into its determination or being-in-itself and its existence, and to that extent it too passes beyond its restriction; the concept that it is in itself contains {{{ 21.122 }}} identity with its other. Should it be an acidifiable base, then it is oxidizable, neutralizable and so forth. In oxidation, neutralization and so forth its restriction of being there only as a base sublates itself; it passes beyond it; just as the acid sublates its restriction of being an acid, and in the acid as in the caustic base the _ought_ to go beyond their restriction is so much at hand that they can be held fast as acid and caustic base – water-free, that is, purely non-neutral – only by force.

Where the concept is held in a concrete existence not merely as abstract being-in-itself, but as a totality that is for itself – as drive, as life, sensation, representing and so forth – there that existence accomplishes out of itself this being beyond the restriction and this going beyond it. The plant passes beyond the restriction of being a seed, and likewise beyond that of being blossom, of being fruit, of being leaf; the seed becomes the unfolded plant, the blossom fades and so forth. That which feels is, within the restriction of hunger, thirst and so forth, the drive to go beyond this restriction, and it carries this going-beyond through. It feels _pain_, and the privilege of a feeling nature is to feel pain; a negation is in its self, and that negation is determined in its feeling _as_ _a_ _restriction_, precisely because what feels has the feeling of its _self_, which is the totality and as such lies beyond that determinateness. Were it not beyond it, it would not feel that determinateness as its negation and would have no pain. – Reason, however, thinking, is supposed to be incapable of going beyond the restriction – reason, which is the _universal_, which for itself is beyond _that_, that is, beyond _all_ particularity, and which is nothing other than the going beyond the restriction. – Admittedly not every going beyond and being beyond a restriction is a genuine liberation from it, a genuine affirmation; the ought itself is already such an incomplete going-beyond, and so is abstraction generally. But pointing to the wholly abstract universal suffices against the equally abstract assurance that the restriction cannot be gone beyond, just as pointing to the infinite as such already suffices against the assurance that the finite cannot be gone beyond.

A notion of _Leibniz's_ that has an air of ingenuity may be mentioned here – that a magnet, if it had consciousness, would take its northward orientation for something its will had determined, a law of its own freedom. On the contrary: had it consciousness and with it will and freedom, it would be a thinking thing, so that space would be for it a _universal_ space containing _every_ direction, and the _one_ direction northward would accordingly be a restriction upon its freedom, quite as it restricts a human being, though not a plant, to be held fast in one single spot.

{{{ 21.123 }}} The _ought_, on the other side, is the going beyond the restriction, but a _going-beyond_ that is itself only _finite_. Its place and its validity therefore lie in the field of finitude, where it holds fast to being-in-itself against what is restricted and asserts it, against what is null, as the rule and the essential. Duty is an _ought_ turned against the particular will, against selfish desire and arbitrary interest; to the will, insofar as in its mobility it can isolate itself from what is true, the true is held up as an ought. Those who set the ought of morality so high, and who suppose that morality is to be destroyed if the ought is not acknowledged as ultimate and true – and likewise the reasoners whose understanding grants itself the unceasing satisfaction of being able to bring an ought, and with it a knowing-better, against everything that is, and who for that reason are just as unwilling to have the ought taken from them – do not see that, for the finitude of their spheres, the ought is fully acknowledged. – But in actuality itself things do not stand so sadly with rationality and law that they merely _ought_ to be, with nothing remaining but the abstractum of being-in-itself, any more than that the ought were perennial on its own self and – what comes to the same – finitude absolute. Kant's and Fichte's philosophy gives out the _ought_ as the highest point at which the contradictions of reason are resolved, whereas it is rather only the standpoint of persisting in finitude and thereby in contradiction.
