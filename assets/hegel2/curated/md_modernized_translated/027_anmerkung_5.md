## Remark

Reality may look like a word of many meanings, being used of determinations that differ and even stand opposed. In the philosophical sense one speaks, say, of _merely_ _empirical_ reality as an existence without worth. Say of thoughts, of concepts, of theories that they _have_ _no_ _reality_, and what is meant is that no _actuality_ belongs to them; _in_ _itself_, or in the concept, the idea of a Platonic republic, for instance, may well be true. The idea is not here refused its worth, and it gets left standing _beside_ reality into the bargain. But when so-called _mere_ ideas, _mere_ concepts, are what it faces, the real passes for the sole truth. – One-sided in equal measure is the sense in which, on the one hand, external existence is credited with deciding the truth of a content, and the way in which, on the other, the idea, the essence, or the inner feeling too, is represented as indifferent towards external existence and even held the more excellent the further it stands removed from reality.

With the expression reality goes a mention of the older metaphysical _concept_ _of_ _God_, the concept laid at the base, above all, of the so-called ontological proof that God exists. _The_ _sum_ _total_ _of_ _all_ _realities_ was the determination given to God, and of that sum total it was said to contain no contradiction within itself, no reality there sublating another; a reality, after all, is to be taken solely as a perfection, as something _affirmative_ with no negation in it. So taken, the realities stand in no opposition to one another and contradict one another not at all.

This concept of reality carries the assumption that reality would still remain were all negation thought away; but thereby every determinateness of reality is {{{ 21.100 }}} sublated. Reality is quality, existence; the moment of the negative is therefore contained in it, and by that alone is it the determinate thing it is. Where it gets taken in the so-called _eminent_ _sense_, or as _infinite_ – in the word's ordinary meaning – as reality is supposed to be taken, it gets stretched into the determinationless and forfeits its meaning. God's goodness, it is said, must be goodness not in the usual but in the eminent sense, not distinct from justice but _tempered_ by justice (a _Leibnizian_ term of mediation), and conversely justice tempered by goodness; then goodness is goodness no longer, nor justice justice. Power, it is said, must be tempered by wisdom; but then it is not power as such, since it would stand subjected to wisdom, – wisdom must be stretched into power, but then it vanishes as the wisdom determining purpose and measure. The true concept of the infinite, together with its _absolute_ unity, which is to emerge later on, is not to be grasped as a _tempering_, a _mutual_ _limiting_ or _blending_ – such being a superficial relation kept in indeterminate fog, one with which only conceptless representing can rest content. – Reality, taken in that definition of God as determinate quality, ceases to be reality once carried out past its determinateness; it turns into abstract being; and God as the _purely_ real in all that is real, or as _sum_ _total_ of all realities, is as void of determination and content as the empty absolute in which everything is one.

Take reality, on the contrary, in its determinateness, and then, because the moment of the negative is essentially contained in it, the sum total of all realities turns quite as much into a sum total of all negations, into the sum total of all contradictions, in the first instance perhaps into absolute _power_, wherein everything determinate is absorbed; but since such power itself only is insofar as something not sublated by it still stands over against it, it turns, once thought as stretched into carried-out, restrictionless power, into the abstract nothing. That real in all that is real, the _being_ in all _existence_, which is supposed to give voice to the concept of God, is nothing else than abstract being, the same as what nothing is.

{{{ 21.101 }}} Determinateness is negation posited as affirmative, and this is Spinoza's proposition: Omnis determinatio est negatio, a proposition whose importance is infinite; only, negation as such is abstraction without form; speculative philosophy, though, must not be blamed for treating negation, or the nothing, as something ultimate; the nothing is that for it just as little as reality is the true.

_The_ _unity_ _of_ _Spinozistic_ _substance_ – that only One substance is – follows as the necessary consequence from this proposition, that determinateness is negation. _Thinking_ and _being_, or extension, the two determinations Spinoza in fact has before him, he was bound to set as one within this unity, since, being determinate realities, they are negations, and their infinity is their unity; by Spinoza's definition, treated of further on, the infinity of something is its affirmation. Attributes, therefore, is how he conceived them, that is, as such as lack a particular subsistence, a being-in-and-for-itself, and are only as sublated, as moments; or rather they are not even moments for him, since substance is what in its own self is wholly without determination, while the attributes, like the modes as well, are distinctions drawn by an understanding external to it. – Nor, in the same way, can the substantiality of individuals hold up against that proposition. The individual is reference to itself through setting limits to everything other; but these limits are thereby limits of itself as well, references to an other, and its existence does not lie within its own self. No doubt the individual is _more_ than what is merely limited on every side, yet this _more_ belongs in another sphere of the concept; within the metaphysics of being it is something determinate outright; and against any such thing, against the finite as such, being in and for itself, determinateness asserts itself essentially as negation and sweeps the individual into that same negative movement of the understanding which lets everything vanish into the abstract unity, substance.

Reality has negation standing immediately over against it; further on, within the proper sphere of the reflected determinations, negation is set over against the _positive_, {{{ 21.102 }}} which is the reality that reflects upon negation, – reality wherein the negative _shines_, the negative that within reality as such still lies hidden.

Quality counts pre-eminently as _property_ only in the respect wherein it shows itself, within an _external_ _relation_, to be an _immanent_ _determination_. By the properties of herbs, for instance, one understands determinations that are not merely _proper_ to a something at large, but such that through them the something _maintains_ itself in a peculiar manner in relation to others, refusing to let the alien effects posited in it hold sway within it, and making its own determinations _tell_ in the other instead – though it does not thereby keep the other off itself. Determinatenesses of a more quiescent sort, figure or shape for instance, one is not apt to call properties, nor indeed qualities, insofar as they are represented as alterable, as not identical with _being_.

_Qualirung_ or _Inqualirung_, an expression of the Jacob-_Böhmean_ philosophy, a philosophy going into the depths but into a murky depth, signifies the movement of a quality (the sour, the astringent, the fiery, and so on) within its own self, insofar as in its negative nature (in its _torment_) it posits and consolidates itself out of another, and is in general that unrest of its own within itself by which it brings itself forth and maintains itself only in struggle.
