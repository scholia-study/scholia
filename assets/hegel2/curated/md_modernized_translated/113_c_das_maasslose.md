## C. The Measureless

Even in its realized being-for-itself the exclusive measure stays saddled with the moment of quantitative existence, and is on that account capable of climbing and sinking along the scale of quantum, upon which the ratios change. Something, or a quality, resting as it does on such a ratio, is driven out beyond itself into the _measureless_, and perishes through the mere change of its magnitude. Magnitude is the constitution by which an existence can be laid hold of with the shine of innocuousness, and through which it can be destroyed.

The abstract measureless is quantum as such, as determinationless within itself and as only an indifferent determinateness by which the measure is not altered. Within the nodal line of measures, however, this same determinateness stands posited as specifying; the abstract measureless just mentioned raises itself, sublated, to qualitative determinateness; and the new measure-relation, the one into which the relation first at hand passes over, is a measureless in view of that first one, while at {{{ 21.370 }}} its own self it is equally a quality that is for itself; thus posited is the alternation of specific concrete existences with one another, and of these same existences with relations that stay merely quantitative, – and so forth into _infinity_. What is thus at hand in this passing-over is the negation of the specific relations no less than the negation of the quantitative advance itself; the _infinite_ that is for itself. – The _qualitative_ infinity, such as it is at existence, was the breaking-forth of the infinite at the finite, as _immediate_ _transition_ and vanishing of the here into its beyond. The _quantitative_ infinity, on the other hand, is by its determinateness already the _continuity_ of quantum, a continuity of the quantum out past itself. The qualitatively finite _becomes_ the infinite; the quantitatively finite has its beyond at its own self, and _points_ _out_ _beyond_ _itself_. This infinity of the specification of measure, however, _posits_ the qualitative and equally the quantitative as _sublating_ each other within one another, and with that it posits the immediate _unity_ they first had, the unity that measure is as such, as one returned into itself and therefore as itself _posited_. The qualitative, a specific concrete existence, passes over into another in such fashion that nothing occurs but a change in the magnitude-determinateness of a ratio; the alteration of the qualitative itself into something qualitative is thereby posited as an external and indifferent one, and as a _going-together_ _with_ _its own_ _self_; the quantitative in any case sublates itself as overturning into the qualitative, into determinedness in and for itself. This unity, continuing itself in this way within its own self throughout the alternation of measures, is the genuinely abiding, self-subsistent _material_, the _matter_.

What is at hand herewith is α) one and the same matter, posited as foundation in its differentiations and as perennial. Already in quantum as such this severing of being from its determinateness begins; something is _large_ as indifferent towards its determinateness that is. In measure the matter itself is in itself already the unity of what is qualitative and what is quantitative, – of those two moments which inside the universal sphere of being make up the difference, the one being the beyond of the other; the perennial substrate thereby has, at its own self and to begin with, the determination of an infinity that is. β) This selfsameness of the substrate is {{{ 21.371 }}} _posited_ in this, that the qualitative self-subsistences into which the measure-determining unity has been repelled subsist only in differences of quantity, so that into this its own differentiating the substrate carries itself over continuously; γ) within the nodal series' infinite progress what stands posited is the continuing of the qualitative into the going-on of quantity, as into an indifferent alteration, but just as much the _negation_ of the qualitative that this contains, and therewith at the same time the negation of merely quantitative externality. The quantitative pointing out past itself towards an other, as another quantitative, founders in the coming-forth of a ratio-measure, of a quality, and the qualitative passing-over sublates itself in just this, that the new quality is for its part nothing but a quantitative ratio. This mutual passing-over of the qualitative and the quantitative proceeds upon the ground of their unity, and the sense of the process is nothing else than the _existence_, the _showing_ or _positing_, that underlying it there is such a substrate, one that would be their unity.

Within the series of self-subsistent measure-relations, the members that stand one-sidedly in them are immediate qualitative somethings (the specific gravities, or the chemical materials, the basic or alkaline ones, the acids for example), and then the neutralizations of these (– among which the combinations of materials of differing specific gravity are here to be comprehended as well –) are self-subsistent and themselves exclusive measure-relations, totalities of a being-for-itself existence, indifferent towards one another. Now such relations have their determination only as nodes upon one and the same substrate. With this the measures, and the self-subsistences posited along with them, are brought down to _states_. The alteration is only the change of a _state_, and what _passes over_ is posited as remaining _the same_ within it.

To survey the further determining which measure has run through, its moments gather together thus: that measure is at first the itself _immediate_ unity of quality and of quantity, in the shape of an ordinary quantum, one that is however specific. Herewith, as a determinateness of quantity referring not to an other but to itself, it is essentially _ratio_. Hence, further, it contains its moments within itself as sublated and undivided; as always within a concept, the difference in it runs thus, that every one of its moments is on its own account unity of the qualitative and the quantitative. This difference, herewith a _real_ one, yields a multitude of measure-relations, self-subsistent within themselves as formal totalities. The series which form the sides of these relations are, for each single member {{{ 21.372 }}} which as belonging to one side comports itself towards the whole series facing it, the same constant order. This unity, still wholly external as mere _order_, does indeed show itself as the immanent specifying unity of a measure that is for itself, distinguished from its specifications; but the principle that specifies is not yet the free concept, the only thing that gives immanent determination to its differences; the principle is rather at first only substrate, a material, for whose differences, if they are to be as totalities, that is, to have within them the nature of the substrate that stays equal to itself, only the external quantitative determination is at hand, which at the same time shows itself as diversity of quality. In this unity of the substrate with its own self the determination of measure is a sublated one, and its quality an external state that quantum determines. – This whole course is just as much the realizing further determination of measure as it is the bringing of measure down to a moment.
