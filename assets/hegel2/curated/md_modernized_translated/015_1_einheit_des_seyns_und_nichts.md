## 1. Unity of Being and Nothing

_Thus_ _pure_ _being_ _and_ _pure_ _nothing_ _are_ _one_ _and_ _the_ _same_. What the truth is, is not being and not nothing either, but rather that being into nothing, and nothing into being, – does not pass over, – but has passed over. Yet no less is the truth not their undifferentiatedness; it lies rather in this, that _they_ _are_ _not_ _one_ _and_ _the_ _same_, that they stand _absolutely_ _distinguished_, and yet are equally unseparated and inseparable, and that immediately _each_ _vanishes_ _into_ _its_ _opposite_. Their truth, then, is this _movement_ of the one's immediate vanishing into the other; _the_ {{{ 21.70 }}} _becoming_; a movement wherein the two stand distinguished, yet by a difference that has dissolved itself just as immediately.
