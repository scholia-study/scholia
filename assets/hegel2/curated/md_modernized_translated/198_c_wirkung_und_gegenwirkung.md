## c. Action and Reaction

Causality is a _presupposing_ doing. The cause stands _conditioned_; it refers itself negatively to itself as to something presupposed, to an external other which, however, is _in_ _itself_ nothing but causality itself, and that only _in_ _itself_. As has emerged, that other is _the_ _substantial_ _identity_ into which formal causality passes over, an identity that has by now determined itself as the negative _of_ _that_ _causality_. Put otherwise, it is the very thing that was the substance of the causal relation, only that facing it now stands the power of accidentality as {{{ 11.405 }}} itself _substantial_ _activity_. – It is the _passive_ substance. – What is _passive_ is the immediate, or what is in itself without also being _for_ _itself_; – pure being, or essence in the sole determinateness of _abstract_ _identity_ _with_ _itself_. – Facing the passive substance stands the _acting_ substance, which refers itself negatively to itself. This latter is the cause to the extent that, within determinate causality, it has restored itself out of the effect by negating itself; in its otherness, or as an immediate, it comports itself essentially as something _positing_, and by its own negation mediates itself with itself. Here, then, causality no longer has a _substrate_ that it might _inhere_ in, nor is it a form-determination as against this identity; it is the substance itself, or rather: what is original is causality alone. – The _substrate_ is that passive substance which causality presupposed for itself.

And now this cause _acts_; for it is the negative power directed _upon_ _itself_, while being at once its own _presupposed_; so it acts upon itself as _upon_ _an_ _other_, namely upon _the_ _passive_ _substance_. – In doing so it _firstly_ _sublates_ that substance's _otherness_ and returns in it into itself; secondly it _determines_ that substance, positing this sublating of its otherness, or this return into itself, as a _determinateness_. Since that positedness is simultaneously the cause's return into itself, it is in the first place _its_ _effect_. Conversely, however, since in presupposing it determines itself as its own other, the cause posits the effect in the _other_, that is, in the passive substance. – Or: because that passive substance is itself the _doubled_ thing, namely a self-subsistent _other_ and simultaneously a _presupposed_ one, already _identical_ in itself with the acting cause, the acting of that cause is itself doubled; it is both at once: a sublating of the substance's _being-determined_, that is, of its condition, or a sublating of the passive substance's self-subsistence; – and a sublating of its own identity with that substance, whereby it posits itself in _advance_, or as an _other_. – This last moment is what _preserves_ the passive substance; in relation to it that first sublating shows itself simultaneously thus, that _only_ _some_ _determinations_ in the substance get sublated and that its identity with the first substance happens to it externally, in the effect.

To that extent the passive substance suffers _violence_. – _The_ _appearance_ _of_ _power_ is what violence is, or _power_ _as_ _something_ _external_. But power is external only to the extent that the causal substance, in acting, that is, in positing itself, is presupposing as well, which means that it posits itself as something sublated. So conversely the doing of violence is quite as much a doing of power. The other upon which the violent cause acts is only an other which that power has itself presupposed; the cause's effect upon it is a negative reference _to_ _itself_, a manifesting _of_ _itself_. What is passive is a self-subsistent thing that is only something _posited_, broken within itself, – an actuality which is condition, and indeed condition as it now stands in its truth, namely an actuality which {{{ 11.406 }}} is nothing but a possibility, or, put the other way, a _being-in-itself_ that is merely the _determinateness_ _of_ _being-in-itself_, merely passive. Hence, for whatever has violence done to it, violence is not merely something that can be done: it _must_ be done to it; and whatever holds violence over the other holds it solely because it is that other's own power which therein _manifests_ itself and manifests the other. What violence does to the passive substance is merely to _posit_ it as what it _in_ _truth_ _is_, namely, since it is the simple positive, or immediate substance, to be for that very reason nothing but something _posited_; the _advance_ status it has as condition is the shine of immediacy, and acting causality strips that shine from it.

Through the impact of another's violence, then, the passive substance has nothing done to it but its right. What it _loses_ is that _immediacy_, a substantiality _foreign_ _to_ _it_. What it _receives_ as something _foreign_, namely being determined as a _positedness_, is its own determination. – But once it is posited in its positedness, in _its_ _own_ determination, it is far from being sublated thereby; rather it _thereby_ _merely_ _closes_ _with_ _itself_, and so is _originality_ _in_ _the_ _very_ _being-determined_ it undergoes. – On one side, then, the active substance _preserves_ or _posits_ the passive one, namely insofar as the active makes itself into something sublated; – on the other side it is _the_ _passive's_ _own_ _doing_ to close with itself and thereby make itself the original and a _cause_. Its _being-posited_ by an other and its own _coming-to-be_ are one and the same thing.

Since the passive substance has now been turned into cause itself, the effect in it is _firstly_ sublated; that is what its _reaction_ consists in generally. As passive substance it is _in_ _itself_ the positedness; and the positedness was also _posited_ in it by the other substance, to the extent, that is, that it took on the other's _effect_ upon itself. Its reaction accordingly holds the same doubling: firstly, that what it is _in_ _itself_ becomes _posited_; secondly, that what it becomes _posited_ as exhibits itself as its _being-in-itself_; being _in_ _itself_ positedness, it receives an effect upon itself from the other, but since that positedness is conversely _its_ own being-in-itself, this is _its_ effect, and it exhibits itself as cause.

_Secondly_, the reaction turns _against_ _the_ _first_ _acting_ _cause_. For the effect which the formerly passive substance sublates within itself is precisely that effect of the first. But a cause has its substantial actuality only in its effect; with the sublating of that effect, its causal substantiality is sublated too. This happens firstly _in_ _itself_ _through_ _itself_, in that the cause makes itself into effect; within that identity its negative determination disappears and it turns into something passive; secondly it happens _through_ _the_ _formerly_ _passive_ substance, now reacting, which sublates the first cause's effect. – {{{ 11.407 }}} Within _determinate_ _causality_ the substance acted upon does likewise become cause again, and it acts _in_ _opposition_ to an _effect_ having been posited in it. It did not, however, react back _against_ _that_ _cause_; it lodged its effect instead in _yet_ _another_ substance, and so the progress of effects into infinity made its appearance; for there the cause is as yet identical with itself in its effect only _in_ _itself_, and hence on one side disappears in an _immediate_ identity, in its _repose_, while on the other it rouses itself once more in an _other_ substance. – Within conditioned causality, by contrast, the cause in the effect _refers_ _to_ _itself_, since its own other is its condition and thus something _presupposed_, and since its acting is on that account quite as much a _coming-to-be_ as it is a positing and a _sublating_ _of_ _the_ _other_.

Further, the cause thereby comports itself as passive substance; but, as we saw, this passive substance _emerges_ as causal substance by way of the effect that has been worked upon it. That first cause, the one that acts first and takes its effect back into itself as reaction, thereby comes forward once more as cause; and by this the acting which under finite causality ran off into the bad-infinite progress is _bent_ _back_, becoming an acting that returns into itself, an infinite _reciprocal_ _acting_.
