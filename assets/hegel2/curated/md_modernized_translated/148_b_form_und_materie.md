## b. Form and Matter

[1.] Essence turns into matter when its reflection determines itself so as to bear towards essence as towards what is formless and indeterminate. Matter, accordingly, is that simple identity, without difference in it, which essence is, though now carrying the determination of being form's other. Hence it is form's proper _groundwork_, its substrate, since what it makes up is the inward reflection of the form-determinations — the self-subsistent term to which those determinations refer as to the positive subsistence they have.

When abstraction is made from every determination, from the whole form of a something, indeterminate matter is what remains over. Matter is something _abstract_ pure and simple. (– Matter admits of no seeing, no feeling, and so on – what one sees and feels is _matter_ that is _determinate_, which is to say matter and form in unity). Yet the abstraction from which matter proceeds is no merely _external_ stripping off and sublating of form; form rather, as has come to light, brings itself down by its own doing to this simple identity.

Further, a matter is _presupposed_ by form, one to which form refers. The two, however, do not for that reason simply _find_ themselves confronting one another externally and by accident; neither the one nor the other is out of its own self, or, to put it otherwise, _eternal_. Towards form matter is what is indifferent; but that indifference is precisely the _determinateness_ of self-identity, and it is into this, as into its groundwork, that form withdraws. What makes form _presuppose_ matter is just this, that form posits itself as sublated and hence bears upon this identity of its own as upon something other. Conversely it is matter that presupposes form; for matter is not the simple essence which immediately is absolute reflection, but that essence determined as the positive, that is, as what only is qua negation sublated. – From the other side, however, since form posits itself as matter only {{{ 11.298 }}} insofar as it sublates its own self, and so _presupposes_ matter, matter carries also the determination of _groundless_ subsistence. Nor, correspondingly, is matter determined as form's ground; for in positing itself as the abstract identity of a form-determination sublated, matter is not identity qua ground, and to that extent form has no ground in it. So each of the two, form as well as matter, is determined not to be posited by way of the other, not to be the other's ground. Matter is instead the identity of ground and grounded, a groundwork confronting this reference of form. Indifference, the determination they hold in common, is what matter as such is determined to be, and it likewise makes up how the two refer to one another. And the determination of form — being the reference of terms distinguished — is likewise the second moment in the way the two comport themselves towards one another. – As what is determined to be indifferent, matter is the _passive_, while form is the _active_. Form, being the negative that refers to itself, is the contradiction within, that which dissolves itself, thrusts itself away from itself, and determines. Its reference falls upon matter, and it is _posited_ to refer to that subsistence of its own as to something other. Matter, on the contrary, is posited to refer to nothing but itself and to be indifferent towards what is other; and yet _in_ _itself_ its reference is to form, since sublated negativity is what it contains and only by that determination is it matter at all. Only because form is not posited in matter, because matter is form merely _in_ _itself_, does matter refer to form as to an _other_. Matter keeps form shut up inside it and is absolutely receptive to it, and this for no other reason than that form is absolutely within matter, that such is the determination matter has in itself. _Matter_ _must_ _therefore_ _be_ _formed_, and _form_ must _materialize_ itself, procuring for itself, in matter, self-identity or subsistence.

2. Form, accordingly, is what determines matter, and matter what is determined by form. – Since form is of itself absolute self-identity and so holds matter within it, and since matter, in its pure abstraction or absolute negativity, likewise has form within its own self, the operation of form upon matter and matter's becoming determined by it amount to nothing more than _the_ _sublating_ _of_ _the_ _shine_ of their _indifference_ and distinctness. Such a reference of determining is thus how each of the two mediates itself with itself by way of its own non-being, – but a single movement is what these two mediations are, one that reinstates the identity they originally had; – the recollection of what they had externalized.

_First_, each of the two, form and matter, _presupposes_ the other. This, as has come to light, says that the _one_ essential unity is negative reference to itself, so that it splits in two: into essential identity determined as indifferent groundwork, and into essential difference, negativity, which is the {{{ 11.299 }}} form that determines. The unity of essence and of form just named, which counterpose themselves as form and matter, is the _absolute_ _ground_ in the act of _determining_ itself. By making itself into something diverse, and because an identity underlies the diverse terms, the reference turns into reciprocal presupposition.

_Secondly_, form taken as self-subsistent is already the contradiction that sublates itself; but posited as such it also is, for self-subsistent and at the same time essentially referred to something other is what it is; – accordingly it sublates itself. Being itself two-sided, it makes this sublating doubled as well: _first_, it sublates _its_ _self-subsistence_ and turns itself into a _posited_ term, into what has its being in an other, and matter is this other of it. _Secondly_, it sublates its determinateness over against matter, its reference to matter, thus its _positedness_, and by that gives itself _subsistence_. With the sublating of its positedness, this reflection of form is the identity proper to it, into which form passes over; but since form at the same time externalizes that identity and sets it over against itself as matter, the reflection of positedness into itself is a uniting with a matter wherein form gets subsistence; in this uniting, then, form comes together with matter _as_ _with_ _an_ _other_ – in accordance with the first side, its making itself into something posited – and therein no less _with_ _its_ _own_ _identity_.

_The_ _activity_ _of_ _form_, then, by which matter gets determined, lies in form's negative comportment towards itself. Conversely, however, form thereby comports itself negatively towards matter too; only, matter's becoming determined is no less form's own movement. Free of matter form is, yet it sublates this self-subsistence of its; and its self-subsistence is matter itself, for in matter lies its essential identity. Thus its making itself into a posited term and its making matter into something determinate are one and the same. – Viewed from the other side, though, the identity proper to form has at the same time been externalized, matter being form's other; to that extent matter does not get determined by form's sublating the self-subsistence proper to it. Only, matter is self-subsistent solely over against form; with the sublating of the negative the positive is sublated as well. Hence with form's sublating of itself there falls away also the determinateness matter had over against form, namely that of being indeterminate subsistence.

Moreover, what shows itself as the _activity_ _of_ _form_ is no less _the_ _own_ _movement_ _of_ _matter_ itself. Matter's determination as it is <i>in itself</i>, its ought, consists in its absolute negativity. By virtue of that negativity matter simply does not refer to form merely as to something other; this external is rather the very form matter holds shut up inside it. Matter {{{ 11.300 }}} is in its own self that very contradiction which form harbours, and one alone is this contradiction, as one alone is its resolution. Within its own self matter is contradictory because, being the indeterminate self-identity, it is withal absolute negativity; so it sublates itself upon itself, its identity breaking up within its negativity while that negativity obtains subsistence in the identity. When matter is thus determined by form as by something external, it thereby attains its determination, and for form no less than for matter the externality of the comportment lies in this, that each of them — or rather the unity they originally are — is in its positing at the same time _presupposing_; whereby reference to self is at once reference to self as sublated, that is, reference to its other.

_Thirdly_, the movement of form and matter has on the one side reinstated the unity they originally were, and on the other made that unity a _posited_ one. Matter determines its own self quite as much as this determining is a doing of form external to matter; conversely form determines nothing but itself, having within its own self the matter that it determines, quite as much as in determining it comports itself towards something other; and the doing of form and the movement of matter are one and the same, except that the first is a doing, negativity as posited, whereas the second is movement or becoming, negativity as a determination that is _in_ _itself_. What results, therefore, is being-in-itself and positedness in unity. Matter as such is something determined, or it necessarily has a form; and form, for its part, is material form throughout, form possessed of subsistence.

So far as form presupposes a matter as its own other, form is _finite_. It is no ground, but merely what is active. Likewise matter, so far as it presupposes form as the non-being of matter, is _finite_ matter; no more than form is it the ground of their unity — it is only the groundwork upon which form rests. But truth belongs neither to this finite matter nor to the finite form; each refers to the other, or their unity alone is what is true of them. The two determinations go back into this unity and there sublate their self-subsistence; the unity thus proves to be their ground. Matter, then, grounds its form-determination only so far as it is not matter qua matter but essence and form in absolute unity; and form likewise grounds the subsistence of its determinations only so far as it is that selfsame unity. Yet this one unity, being absolute negativity and, more precisely, an excluding unity, is presupposing in its reflection; or there is one single doing here — in positing, to keep itself within the unity as posited and to thrust itself away from its own self, to refer to itself as to itself and to refer to itself as to something other. Or again: matter's becoming determined by form is essence, as ground, mediating itself with itself in one unity, by way of itself and by way of the negation of itself.

{{{ 11.301 }}} Matter that has been formed, or form possessed of subsistence, is now not merely that absolute unity of ground with itself but the _posited_ unity as well. In the movement just examined absolute ground has set out its moments as at once self-sublating and thus posited. Or: in coming together with itself the reinstated unity has just as much thrust itself away from its own self and determined itself; for a unity that has come about by way of negation is a negative unity too. Hence it is the groundwork of form and matter, their unity, though their _determinate_ _groundwork_ — matter formed, yet at the same time indifferent towards form and matter as towards what is sublated and unessential. It is the _content_.
