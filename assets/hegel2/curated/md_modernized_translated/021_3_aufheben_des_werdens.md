## 3. Sublation of Becoming

Coming-to-be and ceasing-to-be settle into an equilibrium, and that equilibrium is at first becoming itself. But becoming likewise collapses into _quiescent_ _unity_. Within it being and nothing occur only as vanishing; and yet becoming as such subsists solely through their differentiatedness. Their vanishing is therefore becoming's vanishing, or the vanishing of vanishing itself. Becoming is an unrest without footing, one that sinks together into a quiescent result.

One might put the point otherwise: what becoming amounts to is being's vanishing into nothing and nothing's vanishing into being, and the vanishing of the two of them altogether; yet it rests at the same time upon the difference between them. In its own self, then, it stands in contradiction {{{ 21.94 }}} with itself, since it unites within it terms opposed to each other; and a union of that kind wrecks itself.

The result here is having-vanished, though not in the manner of _nothing_; so taken it would amount merely to a relapse into one of the determinations already sublated, and not to a result of nothing _and_ _of_ _being_. Rather, it is that unity of being and nothing which has become simplicity at rest. Simplicity at rest, however, is _being_, though equally so no longer for itself, but as determination of the whole.

_Existence_ is becoming taken as this transition into the unity of being with nothing, a unity that is in the manner of what _is_, or that bears the shape belonging to the _immediate_, one-sided unity of these moments.
