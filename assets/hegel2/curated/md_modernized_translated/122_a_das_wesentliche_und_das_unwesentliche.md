## {{{ 11.245 }}} A. The Essential and the Unessential

Essence is _sublated_ _being_. Simple equality with its own self is what it is, though only insofar as it is _the_ _negation_ of being's sphere at large. Essence thus has immediacy set opposite it, as an immediacy out of which it has come to be, and one which in this sublating has kept and preserved itself. Essence in this determination is itself an essence that _is_, an immediate essence, and being is something negative only _in_ _reference_ to essence, not in and for its own self; essence, then, is a _determinate_ negation. Being and essence, on this footing, again bear towards each other the relation of _others_ at large, since _each_ _has_ _a_ _being_, _an_ _immediacy_, and the two beings stand indifferent towards one another; measured by that being, each counts for as much as the other.

Simultaneously, though, being in its opposition to essence is the _unessential_; over against essence it carries the determination of what has been sublated. Insofar, however, as being bears towards essence merely the relation of an other at large, essence is not properly essence but only an existence otherwise determined, the _essential_.

The difference between essential and unessential has let essence tumble back into the sphere of _existence_, seeing that essence, as it at first is, stands determined as an immediate that has being, and hence only as an _other_ over against being. With that, the sphere of existence has been laid down as the base, and the point that what being is within this existence is being-in-and-for-itself is one determination more, external to existence itself; just as, the other way about, essence is indeed the being-in-and-for-itself, yet only over against an other, under a _determinate_ respect. – Insofar, then, as at some existence an _essential_ and an _unessential_ are told apart from one another, this difference is an external positing, a setting aside of one portion of that existence from another portion, which leaves the existence itself untouched; a severance falling into a _third_. Which of the two anything belongs to, the essential or the unessential, is left undetermined thereby. It is some external respect and consideration that draws the difference, and for that reason the very same content may be looked upon now as essential, now as unessential.

Examined more nearly, essence turns into a merely essential over against an unessential through this, that essence is taken only as being or existence sublated. Essence on this footing amounts only to the _first_ negation, the negation which is _determinateness_, by which being turns into mere existence, or existence into a mere _other_. Essence, though, is being's absolute negativity; it is being's own self, only not determined merely as an _other_; rather it is the being that has sublated its own self in both respects, as being that is immediate and as negation that is immediate, as {{{ 11.246 }}} negation encumbered with an otherness. Being, or existence, has thus not held itself over as an other than what essence is, and the immediate still told apart from essence is not merely an unessential existence but the immediate that is null _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_; it is a _non-essence_ merely, _shine_.
