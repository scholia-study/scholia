## b. Determination, Constitution and Limit

No longer an abstract in-itself is the _in-itself_ into which something has been reflected back into itself away from its being-for-other; as the negation of that being-for-other it is instead mediated by it, and being-for-other is thus its own moment. Not merely the immediate self-identity of the something, it is the identity through which the something is _in_ _it_ what it is _in_ _itself_ as well; being-for-other is _in_ _it_, since the _in-itself_ is what sublates that being-for-other, is _out_ _of_ _it_ and in itself; but equally so already, since the in-itself is abstract and hence saddled essentially with negation, with being-for-other. Present here is not quality and reality alone, determinateness as merely being, but a determinateness that is _in itself_, and the development consists in _positing_ it as just this determinateness reflected into itself.

1. Within the simple something the in-itself essentially stands in unity with that something's other moment, its _being-in-it_, and the quality which it there is may be called the something's _determination_, so long as this word is taken in the stricter sense that marks it off from _determinateness_ in general. Determination is affirmative determinateness – the being-in-itself to which something in its existence stays true, over against the entanglement with an other that would determine it, holding itself in its self-equality, {{{ 21.111 }}} and asserting that self-equality within its being-for-other. It _fulfils_ its determination to the extent that the further determinateness which at first grows up in manifold ways out of its comportment towards an other becomes, in keeping with its being-in-itself, the something's own fullness. Contained in determination is this: that whatever something is _in_ _itself_ shall be _in_ _it_ as well.

Thinking reason is the _determination_ of the _human being_: thinking in general constitutes his simple _determinateness_, and it is what marks him off from the animal; thinking _in_ _itself_ he is, insofar as thinking is set apart also from his being-for-other, from that naturalness and sensibility of his own by which he hangs together immediately with an other. Yet _in_ _him_ thinking is present too; the human being is himself thinking, he _is_ _there_ as one who thinks, and in thinking lies his concrete existence, his actuality; and further, thinking being in his existence and his existence in thinking, thinking is _concrete_, to be taken with content and fulfilment, thinking reason, and in this way the _determination_ of the human being. Yet this determination too is once again only _in_ _itself_, an _ought_ – that is, it stands, with the fulfilment built into its in-itself and in the form of the in-itself generally, _against_ the existence not built into it, an existence which meanwhile persists as immediate sensibility and nature confronting it from outside.

2. Also distinct from the fulfilment of being-in-itself with determinateness is the determinateness that is only being-for-other and stays outside the determination. For in the field of the qualitative, differences keep an immediate qualitative being over against each other even in their sublatedness. Thus what the something has _in_ _it_ divides, and from this side it is an external existence of the something, one that is indeed _its_ existence and yet does not belong to its being-in-itself. – So determinateness is _constitution_.

Something constituted this way or that is grasped as caught up in external influence and in external relations. Contingent is how this outward connection appears, the connection on which constitution hangs, and so too the being-determined by an other. But to be handed over to such externality and to have a _constitution_ is itself the quality of the something.

Where something alters, the alteration lands in the constitution; constitution is {{{ 21.112 }}} what _in_ the something turns into an other. Something preserves itself in the alteration, which strikes only this unsteady surface of its otherness, not its determination.

Determination and constitution stand distinguished from one another in this way; something, taken according to its determination, is indifferent towards its constitution. What binds the two, though, the middle of this syllogism, is what something has _in_ _it_. Being _in_ _the something_, however, proved rather to break apart into those two extremes. _Determinateness_ as such is the simple middle; to its identity determination belongs no less than constitution. But of its own accord determination goes over into constitution, and constitution into determination. This lies in what precedes; the connection, more precisely, runs thus: to the extent that what something _is_ _in_ _itself_ is _in_ _it_ as well, being-for-other clings to it; determination as such therefore lies open to the relation to an other. Determinateness is a moment and yet harbours at the same time the qualitative difference, differing from being-in-itself, of being the negative of the something, another existence. United with being-in-itself, determinateness that thus takes the other up into itself brings otherness into being-in-itself, into determination, and determination is thereby demoted to constitution. – Conversely, when being-for-other is isolated as constitution and posited on its own, it is in it just what the other as such is, the other in its own self, that is, of its own self; taken so, however, it is existence _relating_ _to_ _itself_, being-in-itself with a determinateness, and hence _determination_. – Hence, so far as the two are also to be kept apart, constitution – which looks as though it were grounded in an externality, in an other generally – _depends_ on determination too, and the determining that comes from elsewhere is at once determined by the something's own, immanent determining. But constitution belongs, furthermore, to what the something is in itself; along with its constitution something changes.

No longer is this change of the something the first alteration, the one merely with respect to its being-for-other; that first alteration was only the one existing in itself, belonging to the inner concept; now the alteration is posited in the something as well. – Further determination accrues to the something itself, and negation gets posited as immanent to it, as the being-within-itself it has developed.

{{{ 21.113 }}} At first the passing over of determination and constitution into each other is the sublating of their difference, and existence, or something in general, is thereby posited; and since this result comes out of that difference, which equally holds qualitative otherness within it, there are two somethings, though not merely others over against each other in general – the negation would then still be abstract and would fall only within the comparison – for the negation is now _immanent_ to the somethings. Indifferent to each other they are as _existent_, yet this affirmation of theirs is immediate no longer; each relates to itself _by means of_ the sublating of otherness, an otherness reflected in determination into being-in-itself.

_Thus_ _out_ _of_ _itself_ does something comport itself towards the other, for otherness stands posited within it as a moment of its own; negation is embraced within its being-within-itself, and only by means of that negation does it now have affirmative existence at all. From this existence, however, the other is qualitatively distinct too, and is on that account posited outside the something. Nothing but the quality of the something is the negation of its other, for something is what it is by sublating its other. Only now does the other genuinely come to stand over against an existence itself; over against the first something the other stands merely externally, or else – the two hanging together in fact absolutely, that is, by their concept – their connection runs thus, that existence has _passed over_ into otherness, something into an other, and something is an other quite as much as the other is. To the extent, then, that being-within-itself is the non-being of the otherness which it contains and which is nevertheless distinguished from it as being, the something is itself the negation, _the_ _ceasing_ _of_ _an_ _other_ _in_ _it_; posited it is as comporting itself negatively against that other and as thereby preserving itself; – this other, the being-within-itself of the something as negation of negation, is its _being-in-itself_, and this sublating is at once _in_ _it_ as simple negation, namely as the something's negation of the other something lying outside it. _One_ determinateness of the two there is, identical with the being-within-itself of the somethings as negation of negation, and, since these negations stand against each other as other somethings, it closes them together out of themselves and equally parts them from one another, each negating the other, – the _limit_.

3. Indeterminate, affirmative community of something with its other is what _being-for-other_ amounts to; within the limit the _non-being_-for-other comes to the fore, the qualitative negation of the other, which is thereby fended off from the something reflected into itself. The development of this concept is what has to be watched, though it turns out to be rather an entanglement and a contradiction. That contradiction is at hand right away in this: as the negation of the something reflected into itself, the limit holds the moments of something and other within it as _ideal_, while these same moments, as distinct moments, stand posited at the same time in the sphere of existence as _real_, as _qualitatively_ _distinct_.

α. Something, then, is immediate existence relating to itself, and it carries a limit {{{ 21.114 }}} first of all as against an other; not of the something itself but of the other is this limit the non-being; in it the something sets bounds to its other. – Yet a something in general is what the other itself is; so the limit which the something has as against the other is likewise the other's limit as a something, the limit by which the other fends off the first something as _its_ own other, or it is a _non-being_ _of_ _that_ _something_; the limit is therefore non-being not of the other alone, but of the one and of the other something alike, and so of _something_ in general.

But since it is essentially the non-being of the other just as much, something at the same time _is_ by virtue of its limit. In doing the limiting, something is indeed brought down to being limited itself; its limit, though, as the ceasing of the other in it, is at the same time nothing other than the being of the something; _this_ _is_ _through_ _the_ _limit_ _what_ _it_ _is_, and has _in_ _it_ _its_ _quality_. – Outwardly this relation displays the fact that limit is simple negation, the _first_ negation, while the other is at once negation of negation, the being-within-itself of the something.

As immediate existence, then, something is the limit as against another something, but it possesses that limit _in_ _its_ _own_ _self_ and is something by the mediation of a limit which is equally its non-being. Mediation is what the limit is, that by which something and other _both_ _are_ and _are_ _not_.

β. To the extent, now, that something within its limit _is_ and _is_ _not_, these moments being an immediate, qualitative difference, the existence of the something and its non-existence fall apart from each other. _Outside_ its limit (or, however one may picture it, _inside_) something has its existence; and the other, being a something, lies outside that limit as well. _Between_ the two the limit is the _middle_, and in it they cease. Their _existence_ they have _beyond_ each other, away _from_ _their_ _limit_; the limit, as the non-being of each, is what is other to them both.

– Given this diversity of the something from its limit, only outside its limit, the point, does the _line_ appear as line; the _surface_ as surface outside the line; the _body_ as body only outside the surface bounding it. – This is the side on which limit falls first into representation, – the being-outside-itself of the concept, – and it is chiefly in this aspect that limit is taken up in spatial objects too.

γ. Further, however, the something as it stands outside the limit is the unlimited something, existence merely in general. So taken it is not marked off from its other; it is {{{ 21.115 }}} existence and nothing more, and therefore shares the same determination with its other, each being merely something in general, or each being an other; both are in this way _the same_. But this existence of theirs, immediate to begin with, now stands posited together with determinateness as limit, wherein the two are what they are, distinguished from one another. The limit, though, is equally their _common_ distinguishedness, being their unity and their distinguishedness alike, as existence is. Contained in this twofold identity of the two, of existence and limit, is this: that the something has its existence in the limit alone, and that, limit and immediate existence being at the same time each the negative of the other, the something which is only in its limit separates itself just as much from itself, points out past itself to its non-being and declares that non-being to be its being, passing over into it accordingly. Applied to the earlier example: the one determination says that something is only in its limit what it is; – the _point_, accordingly, is not the limit of the _line_ in the sense merely that the line ceases in it and has its existence outside it; – nor the _line_ the limit of the _surface_ in the sense merely that the surface ceases in the line, and so likewise with the _surface_ as limit of the _body_. In the point the line also _begins_; the point is the line's absolute beginning, and even where the line is pictured as unlimited towards both its sides, or, as the phrase goes, as prolonged into infinity, the point makes up its _element_, as the line makes up the element of the surface, the surface that of the body. Of what they limit these _limits_ are the _principle_, in the way that the one, as the hundredth for example, is a limit and yet an element of the entire hundred too.

The other determination is the unrest of the something within its limit, wherein it is immanent, the unrest of being the _contradiction_ that despatches it out past itself. The point, therefore, is this dialectic of its own, to become line, the line the dialectic of becoming surface, the surface that of becoming total space. A second definition of line, surface and total space is given to the effect that the line comes about through the _motion_ of the point, the surface through the motion of the line, and so on. This _motion_ of the point, of the line and so on, is regarded, however, as something contingent, or as merely so pictured. In fact this is taken back again by the claim that the determinations out of which line and so on are supposed to come about are their _elements_ and _principles_, and these are nothing but their limits at the same time; the coming-about is thus not held to be contingent or merely a matter of picturing. That point, line and surface, each for itself, are self-contradictory beginnings which repel themselves away from themselves, so that the point by its own concept passes out of itself into the line, _moves_ _in_ _itself_ and lets the line {{{ 21.116 }}} come about, and so on – lies in the concept of the limit immanent to the something. The application belongs, however, to the consideration of space; to hint at it here: the point is limit made wholly abstract, yet limit _in_ _an_ _existence_; that existence is still taken quite indeterminately, it is so-called absolute, that is to say abstract, _space_, being-outside-one-another that is utterly continuous. Because limit here is not abstract negation but is _in_ _this_ _existence_, because it is _spatial_ determinateness, the point is spatial, is the contradiction between abstract negation and continuity, and with that the passing over and having-passed-over into line and so on, just as indeed there _is_ no point, and no line and no surface either.

Posited with its immanent limit as the contradiction of its own self, and by that contradiction pointed and driven out past itself, something is the _finite_.
