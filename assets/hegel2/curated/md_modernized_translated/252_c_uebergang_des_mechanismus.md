## c. Transition of Mechanism

This soul, however, is still sunk in its body; the concept of objective totality, _now_ _determinate_ but _inward_, is free necessity in such a way that law has not yet stepped over against its object; it is _concrete_ centrality as universality spread _immediately_ through its own objectivity. That ideality, then, does not have the _objects_ _themselves_ for its determinate difference; the objects are _self-subsistent_ _individuals_ of the totality, or, should we look back to the formal stage, external _objects_ that are not individual at all. Immanent in them law certainly is, constituting their nature and their power; but its difference remains shut up within its ideality, and the objects are not themselves parted according to law's ideal differentiation. But it is in {{{ 12.147 }}} ideal centrality and its law alone that the object has its essential self-subsistence; hence it has no force with which to withstand the concept's judgment and to keep itself in abstract, indeterminate self-subsistence and seclusion. Its existence, owing to the ideal difference that dwells within it, is a _determinateness_ _which_ _the_ _concept_ _has_ _posited_. In this way its non-self-subsistence is no longer merely a _striving_ towards the _central_ _point_ — over against which, just because its reference is only a striving, it still bears the appearance of a self-subsistent external object — but is rather a striving towards the _object_ _determinately_ _opposed_ _to_ _it_; and correspondingly the centre has itself fallen apart, its negative unity having passed over into _objectified_ _opposition_. Centrality is therefore now the _reference_ of these objectivities, negative towards one another and in tension. Free mechanism thus determines itself into _chemism_.
