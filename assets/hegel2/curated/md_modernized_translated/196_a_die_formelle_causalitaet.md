## {{{ 11.397 }}} a. Formal Causality

1. The cause is what is _original_ over against the effect. – Substance, qua power, _is_ _the_ _shining_, or _has_ accidentality. Qua power, however, it is quite as much reflection-into-self within its shine; and so it _expounds_ its transition, _and_ _this_ _shining_ _is_ _determined_ _as_ _shine_, or the accident stands _posited_ as being merely something _posited_. – In its determining, though, substance does not set out from accidentality as though accidentality were an _other_ _in_ _advance_, only now to be posited as determinateness; the two are rather One actuosity. Substance _determines_ _itself_ as power; yet this determining immediately amounts of itself to a sublating of the determining, and to the return. _It_ _determines_ _itself_, – _it_, the determining term, is in this way what is _immediate_ and at the same time what is already determined; – so, in determining _itself_, it posits this already _determined_ term as _determined_, has thereby sublated positedness, and has gone back into itself. – Conversely, this return, being substance's _negative_ reference to itself, is itself a _determining_, a repelling of substance away from itself; the determinate term from which substance appears to make its start, and which it appears only now to posit as such, as a determinate found on hand, _comes to be_ through this very return. – So the absolute actuosity is _cause_; – substance's power in _its_ _truth_ as manifestation, a manifestation which, immediately in the becoming of what is _in_ _itself_, namely of the accident, which is the positedness, also _expounds_ it, _positing_ it as _positedness_; _the_ _effect_. – The effect, then, is _first_ just what the accidentality of the substantiality relation is, namely substance as _positedness_; but _second_, whereas the accident as such is substantial only through its vanishing, as something in transition, as effect it is positedness identical with itself; within the effect the cause has been manifested as whole substance, manifested, that is, as reflected into itself within positedness itself as such.

2. Substance as the _unposited_ original confronts this _positedness_ reflected into itself, the determinate taken as determinate. Being as absolute power a return into itself, and this return being itself a _determining_, substance is not any longer the bare _in-itself_ belonging to its accident; it stands _posited_ as that being-in-itself as well. Only as cause, accordingly, does substance possess _actuality_. But this actuality — that its _being-in-itself_, its determinateness within the relation of substantiality, now stands posited _as_ _determinateness_ — is the _effect_; whatever actuality substance has as cause it therefore has _only_ _within_ _its_ _effect_. – Herein lies the _necessity_ which the cause is. – Cause is the _actual_ substance, seeing that substance as power determines its own self; but {{{ 11.398 }}} it is at the same time cause, seeing that it expounds this determinateness or posits it as positedness; and so it posits its actuality as the positedness or as the effect. Effect is the other of the cause, the positedness set against what is original and _mediated_ by that original. Being necessity, however, the cause sublates this mediating of its own no less, and in _determining_ itself as what originally refers itself to itself _over_ _against_ the mediated it is the return into itself; for positedness is determined _as_ positedness and hence is identical with itself; hence it is within its effect first of all that the cause is what is genuinely actual and self-identical. – On that account the effect is _necessary_, precisely because it is manifestation of the cause, or is that necessity which the cause is. – Only as this necessity is the cause self-moving, making its start from itself without being solicited by another, and the _self-subsistent_ _source_ _of_ _bringing_ _forth_ _out_ _of_ _itself_; – it must _act_, and its originality consists in this, that its reflection-into-self is determining positing and the reverse, both being one unity.

_The_ _effect_ _therefore_ _contains_ _nothing_ _at_ _all_ _that_ _the_ _cause_ _does_ _not_ _contain_. _The_ _cause_, conversely, _contains_ _nothing_ _that_ _is_ _not_ _within_ _its_ _effect_. Cause is cause only so far as it brings an effect forth; and _the_ _cause_ _is_ _nothing_ _but_ _this_ _determination_, _to_ _have_ _an_ _effect_, and _the_ _effect_ _nothing_ _but_ _this_, _to_ _have_ _a_ _cause_. Within the cause as such there lies its effect, and within the effect the cause; so far as the cause had not yet acted, or so far as it were to have left off acting, it would not be cause; – and the effect, so far as its cause has vanished, is effect no longer but an indifferent actuality. –

3. Within this _identity_ of cause and effect the form whereby the two are distinguished, as what is in itself and as the positedness, is now sublated. The cause _goes out_ within its effect; and with that the effect has gone out as well, for the effect is nothing but the determinateness of the cause. This causality gone out within the effect is accordingly an _immediacy_ indifferent towards the relation of cause and effect, an immediacy that has that relation upon it externally.
