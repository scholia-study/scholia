## {{{ 21.364 }}} B. Nodal Line of Measure-Relations

The measure-relation's final determination was that, being specific, it is _exclusive_; the excluding accrues to neutrality as the _negative_ unity of the distinguished moments. For this unity that is _for itself_, elective affinity, no further principle of specification has come out with regard to its connection to the other neutralities; that specification remains only in the quantitative determination of affinity as such, according to which it is determinate multitudes that neutralize one another and thereby stand over against other relative elective affinities of their moments. But further, for the sake of the fundamental quantitative determination, the _exclusive_ elective affinity also _continues itself_ into the neutralities that are other to it, and this continuity is not merely an external connection of the various neutrality-relations, in the way a comparison would be; rather, neutrality as such has a _separability_ within it, inasmuch as those out of whose unity it has come to be step, as self-subsistent somethings, each of them indifferently, into connection with this or with others of the series facing them, so as to combine with them, albeit in differing, specifically determined multitudes. Herewith this measure, resting as it does on such a relation within its own self, is saddled with an indifference of its own; it is something external at its own self and, in its connection to itself, something alterable.

This _connection_ of the ratio-measure _to_ _itself_ is different from its externality and alterability, which are its quantitative side; over against these it is, as connection to itself, a qualitative foundation that is; – an abiding, material substrate which, as at once the measure's continuity _with_ _itself_ within its externality, would in its quality have to hold that principle for the specification of this externality.

Now on this closer determination the exclusive measure, external to itself in its being-for-itself, repels itself away from its own self, positing itself as another relation that is merely quantitative and likewise as such another relation which is simultaneously another measure; it is determined as a unity that specifies at its own self, one which produces measure-relations at itself. These relations differ from the kind of affinity considered above, in which one self-subsistent comports itself towards self-subsistents of another quality and towards a series of such; they occur at _one_ _and_ _the_ _same_ substrate, inside the very moments of neutrality; repelling itself from itself, the measure determines itself {{{ 21.365 }}} into other relations that differ only quantitatively, which likewise form _affinities_ and _measures_, _alternating_ with such as remain merely _quantitative_ _diversities_. In such a way they form a _nodal line_ of measures along a scale of the more and the less.

A measure-relation is at hand; a self-subsistent reality distinguished qualitatively from others. Because it is at the same time essentially a ratio of quanta, a being-for-itself of this sort stands open to externality and to quantum-alteration; it has a latitude within which it stays indifferent towards that alteration and leaves its quality unchanged. But a point of this change of the quantitative sets in at which the quality gets changed, at which the quantum proves to be specifying, so that the ratio, quantitatively altered, has overturned into a measure and with that into a new quality, a new something. This relation, having stepped into the place of the first, is determined by the latter partly according to the qualitative selfsameness of the moments standing in affinity, partly according to quantitative continuity. But since the difference falls within this quantitative side, the new something comports itself indifferently towards the preceding one; their difference is the external one of quantum. It has therefore come forth not out of the preceding one but immediately out of itself; that is, out of the inward specifying unity which has not yet stepped into existence. – The new quality, or new something, is subjected to the very same onward course of its alteration, and so forth into infinity.

Insofar as the advance from one quality runs in the steady continuity of quantity, the ratios drawing near to a qualifying point are, quantitatively regarded, distinguished only by more and less. On this side the alteration is a _gradual_ one. But gradualness concerns merely what is external about the alteration, not what is qualitative about it; the preceding quantitative ratio, {{{ 21.366 }}} infinitely close as it is to the one that follows, is still another qualitative existence. On the qualitative side, accordingly, the merely quantitative going-on of gradualness, which is no limit at its own self, is absolutely broken off; since the newly entering quality, in respect of its merely quantitative connection, is one indeterminately other than the vanishing quality, an indifferent one, the transition is a _leap_; both are posited as wholly external to one another. – People are fond of making an alteration _comprehensible_ to themselves by way of the gradualness of the transition; but gradualness is rather just the merely indifferent change, the opposite of the qualitative one. In gradualness the coherence of the two realities – they are taken as states, or as self-subsistent things – is on the contrary sublated; it is posited that neither is the limit of the other, but that the one is simply external to the other; and thereby precisely what is needed for _comprehending_, however little may be called for, is done away with.
