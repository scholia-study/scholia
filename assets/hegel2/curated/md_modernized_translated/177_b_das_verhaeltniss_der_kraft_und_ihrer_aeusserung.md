## B. The Relation of Force and Its Expression

_Force_ is that negative unity wherein the contradiction of whole and parts has found its dissolution, the truth of that first relation. Whole and parts is the thoughtless relation upon which representation lights first of all; or, objectively taken, it is the dead, mechanical aggregate, which does indeed carry form-determinations by which the manifoldness of its self-subsistent matter gets referred together into a unity, but a unity external to that matter. – The relation of _force_, by contrast, is the higher return into self, in which the whole's unity, the very thing that constituted the reference of self-subsistent otherness, ceases to be something external and indifferent to this manifoldness.

As the essential relation has by now determined itself, the immediate and the reflected self-subsistence — sides or extremes that in the foregoing relation subsisted for themselves — stand posited within it as sublated, that is, as moments. Contained in this is, _first_, that the reflected unity together with its immediate existence, so far as either is a first and an immediate, sublate themselves in their own selves and pass over into their other; the former, _force_, goes over into _its_ _expression_, and the external is a vanishing thing which retreats into force as into its ground, and is only in being carried {{{ 11.360 }}} and posited by force. _Second_, this going-over is not a mere becoming and vanishing; it is negative reference to self, or _what_ _alters_ _its_ _determination_ is therein at the same time reflected into itself and maintains itself; the movement of force is not so much a _going-over_ as force's _setting_ _itself_ _across_, and throughout this alteration posited by force itself force stays what it is. – _Third_, this _reflected_ unity, referring itself to itself, is itself sublated too and a moment; mediated through its other, it has that other for its _condition_; and its negative reference to itself, which is a first and starts the movement of its going-over _out_ _of_ _itself_, has no less a presupposition that _solicits_ it, and an other whence its starting is taken.
