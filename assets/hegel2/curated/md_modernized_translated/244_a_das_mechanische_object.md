## A. The Mechanical Object

1. The object is, as has come out, the _syllogism_ whose mediation has been evened out and which has therefore _become_ immediate identity. It is on that account universal in and {{{ 12.134 }}} for itself; a universality, though, that does not mean some fund of properties held in common, but one that pervades particularity and is within particularity immediate singularity.

1. In the first place, then, the object does not part itself into _matter_ and _form_, the former of which would be its self-subsistent universal, the latter its particular and its singular; a difference so abstract, between singularity and universality, is by the object's own concept not to be found at it; taken as matter, it has to be taken as matter shaped in its own self. Just as well it admits of being determined as a thing bearing properties, as a whole made up of parts, as substance carrying accidents, and by the remaining relations of reflection; only, those relations have one and all gone under already within the concept; hence properties do not belong to the object, nor do accidents, such things being detachable from a thing or from a substance; in the object, on the contrary, particularity stands reflected outright into the totality. Within the parts of a whole one does find the sort of self-subsistence that attaches to the object's own differences, yet those differences are at once and essentially objects in their own right, totalities, which do not, as parts do, bear this determinateness in contrast with the whole.

To begin with, then, the object is _indeterminate_ in this respect, that no determinate opposition is to be found at it; for it is mediation gathered together into immediate identity. Insofar as the _concept_ is _essentially_ _determined_, the object carries determinateness at it as one that is indeed complete but for the rest _indeterminate_, that is, as a _relationless_ _manifoldness_, which makes up a totality equally not further determined to begin with; _sides_, _parts_, that admit of being told apart at it belong to an external reflection. Hence that entirely indeterminate difference amounts to no more than the presence of _several_ objects, each holding its own determinateness only as reflected into its universality, without shining _outwards_. – Since this indeterminate determinateness is essential to it, the object is within its own self a _multiplicity_ of that kind, and so has to be looked upon as something _composite_, as an _aggregate_. – Out of _atoms_, though, it is not built up, atoms being no objects at all, since they are no totalities. Something more of an object would be the _Leibnizian_ _monad_, inasmuch as it is a totality of the world's representation; but, locked into its _intensive_ _subjectivity_, it is meant to be at any rate essentially _one_ inside itself. Still, once determined as an _exclusive_ _one_, the monad amounts to no more than a principle _assumed_ by _reflection_. It is, however, an object partly to this extent, that what grounds its manifold representations — the developed, which is to say the _posited_, determinations of a totality merely _in_ _itself_ — lies _outside_ _it_, and partly to this extent, that the monad is just as indifferent about making up an object _together_ _with_ _others_; so in truth it is nothing _exclusive_, nothing _self-determined_ _for_ _itself_.

{{{ 12.135 }}} 2. Since the object is now the totality of _being-determined_, though on account of its indeterminateness and its immediacy not the _negative_ _unity_ of that being-determined, it stands towards the _determinations_ as _singular_ ones determined in and for themselves, just as these stand towards one another, _indifferently_. These are for that reason to be comprehended neither out of it nor out of one another; its totality is the form of the universal being-reflected of its manifoldness into a singularity at large that is in its own self not determined. The determinatenesses that it carries at it do indeed accrue to it; the _form_, however, that constitutes their difference and ties them together into one unity is external and indifferent; whether it be a _mixture_, or beyond that an _order_, some given _arrangement_ of parts and sides, such combinations leave the terms so referred quite indifferent.

So the object, like any existence whatever, holds what determines its totality _outside_ _it_, in _other_ objects, and these in turn hold theirs _outside_ _them_, and so forth into infinity. This going-out into infinity does have to be taken as returning into itself, and that return has to be represented as a _totality_, as a _world_ — which is nothing else, though, than the universality shut up within itself by indeterminate singularity, a _universe_.

Since, then, the object in its determinateness is equally indifferent towards that determinateness, it points of its own self, for its being-determined, _out_ _beyond_ _itself_, again to objects, to which however it is in like manner _indifferent_ _to_ _be_ _determining_. Nowhere, therefore, is a principle of self-determination present; – _determinism_, – the standpoint at which cognition stands so long as the object, as it has here at first come out, counts for it as the true, – supplies for each determination of the object the determination of some other object; but that other is no less indifferent, alike towards its own being-determined and towards its active comportment. – Determinism is therefore itself equally indeterminate in going on into infinity; it may halt wherever it pleases and be satisfied, because the object over to which it has passed is, as a formal totality, closed within itself and indifferent towards being determined through an other. For this reason the _explaining_ of an object's determination, together with the advance of this representation undertaken to that end, remains a mere _empty_ _word_, seeing that no self-determination is lodged in the further object towards which the advance is made.

3. Since now the _determinateness_ of an object _lies_ _in_ _another_, no determinate diversity is present between them; the determinateness is merely _doubled_, once at the one and then at the other object, something simply and only _identical_, and the explanation or the comprehending is to that extent _tautological_. This tautology is the external, empty going to and fro; since the determinateness receives no peculiar differentiation from the objects that are indifferent towards it, and for that reason is only identical, there is present but One {{{ 12.136 }}} determinateness; and that it should be doubled gives expression precisely to this externality, to the nothingness of such a difference. Yet at one and the same time the objects stand _self-subsistent_ over against each other; within that identity, accordingly, they remain outright _external_ to one another. – What is present here, then, is the _contradiction_ between the objects' perfect _indifference_ towards one another and the _identity_ of their _determinateness_, or their perfect _externality_ _in_ _the_ _identity_ of their _determinateness_. Thus this contradiction is _the_ _negative_ _unity_ of a plurality of objects that within it simply repel one another, – _the_ _mechanical_ _process_.
