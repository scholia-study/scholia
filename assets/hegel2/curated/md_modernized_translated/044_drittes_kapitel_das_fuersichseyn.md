## {{{ 21.144 }}} Chapter 3. Being-for-itself

_Qualitative_ _being_ reaches its _completion_ in _being-for-itself_; it is infinite being. Being at the beginning is without determination. Existence is being sublated, yet only immediately sublated; at first, therefore, it holds nothing but the first, itself immediate negation; being, admittedly, is preserved along with it, and the two are joined in existence in simple unity, yet precisely on that account they remain in themselves _unequal_ to one another, and their unity is still _not_ _posited_. That is why existence is the sphere of difference, of dualism, and the field of finitude. Determinateness here is determinateness as such; a relative, not an absolute being-determined. In being-for-itself what stands posited and equalized is the difference of being and determinateness or negation; quality, otherness, limit, as also reality, being-in-itself, the ought and the like – these are the imperfect embeddings of negation into being, embeddings in which the difference of the two still lies at the base. Since in finitude, however, the negation has passed over into infinity, into the _posited_ negation of the negation, it is simple reference to itself, hence on its own self the equalizing with being; – _absolute_ _being-determined_.

Being-for-itself is _first_ immediately something existing-for-itself, a _one_.

_Second_, the one passes over into the _plurality_ _of_ _ones_, – _repulsion_; which otherness of the one sublates itself in the one's ideality, _attraction_.

_Third_, repulsion and attraction in their reciprocal determination, wherein the two sink together into equilibrium, and quality, driven in being-for-itself to its peak, passes over into _quantity_.
