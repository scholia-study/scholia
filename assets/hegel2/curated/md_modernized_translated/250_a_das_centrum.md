## a. The Centre

First, the object's empty manifoldness now stands gathered into objective singularity, into the simple self-determining _central_ _point_. Second, insofar as the object, being immediate totality, keeps its indifference towards determinateness, that determinateness is at hand in it also as unessential, or as an _outside-one-another_ of many objects. The first-named determinateness, the essential one, by contrast makes up the _real_ _middle_ _term_ among the many objects that work mechanically upon each other — the term by which they are conjoined _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_ — and it is the objective universality belonging to them. In the relation of _communication_ universality showed itself at first as something at hand only through _positing_; as _objective_, however, it is that essence of the objects which permeates them and is immanent in them.

Within the material world this role falls to the _central_ _body_, which is the _genus_, yet the _individual_ universality, of the singular objects and of their mechanical process. Towards each other the unessential singular bodies behave by _thrust_ and _pressure_; no such relation holds between them and the central body that is their essence, for externality has ceased to make up what fundamentally determines them. Rather, their identity with it is rest — namely, _being_ _in_ _their_ _centre_ — and this unity is their concept existing in and for itself. Yet it stays a mere _ought_, for the objects' externality, still posited alongside, fails to answer to that unity. Hence the _striving_ they have towards the centre is their absolute universality, one not posited by way of _communication_; what it makes up is the genuine _rest_, itself _concrete_ and not _laid_ _down_ _from_ _without_, the rest into which the process of non-self-subsistence has to withdraw. – On this account it is an empty abstraction when mechanics supposes that a body put into motion would carry on in a straight line without end unless external resistance robbed it of its motion. _Friction_, under whatever shape resistance may appear, is merely centrality showing itself; centrality is what absolutely restores the body to it; for whatever the moving body rubs against owes the strength of a resistance solely to its oneness with the centre. – Where _spirit_ is concerned, both the centre and oneness with it take on loftier shapes; yet even there the fundamental determination has to be made up by the unity of the concept and by its reality, which here {{{ 12.144 }}} is at first mechanical centrality.

To that extent the central body has stopped being a mere _object_, in which determinateness counts as something unessential; for what it now has is not only the _being-in-itself_ of objective totality but its _being-for-itself_ as well. On that account one may look upon it as an _individual_. Essentially, its determinateness differs from any mere _order_ or _arrangement_ and _external_ _connectedness_ of parts; being a determinateness in and for itself, it is _immanent_ form, a principle that determines itself, one to which the objects adhere and by which they are joined into a genuine one.

So taken, however, this central individual is still only a _middle_ _term_ lacking genuine extremes; but as the negative unity of the total concept it sunders itself into extremes. Or: through the concept's withdrawal into itself, the objects that were previously non-self-subsistent and outside one another likewise get determined as individuals; the central body's identity with itself, still a _striving_, carries _externality_ with it, and to that externality, taken up as it is into the body's _objective_ _singularity_, this singularity is imparted. Owing to a centrality of their own, they are, once placed outside that first centre, themselves centres for whatever objects lack self-subsistence. Those second centres, together with the objects lacking self-subsistence, stand conjoined by that absolute middle term.

Yet the relative central individuals also make up on their own account the _middle_ _term_ _of_ _a_ _second_ _syllogism_: on one side this term falls under a higher extreme, namely the _objective_ _universality_ and _power_ of the absolute centre, while on the other it brings beneath itself the objects without self-subsistence, whose surface or formal singularization it carries. – Even these objects without self-subsistence are the middle term of a _third_ syllogism, the _formal_ one; for they are the bond tying absolute to relative central individuality, in that the latter has in them its externality, by which its _reference_ _to_ _itself_ is at once a _striving_ after an absolute central point. What the formal objects have as their essence is the selfsame _gravity_ of the central body immediately theirs, a body in which they adhere as in their subject and their extreme of singularity; by way of the externality that they make up, this body falls under the absolute central body; they are, accordingly, particularity's formal middle term. – The absolute individual, though, is the objectively universal middle term, conjoining and holding together the relative individual's being-within-itself with its externality. – In the same way the _government_, the {{{ 12.145 }}} _citizen_ _individuals_, and the _needs_, or the _external_ _life_, of the _singulars_ are three terms of which each is the middle of the other two. _Government_ is the absolute centre wherein the extreme of the singulars gets conjoined with their external subsistence; and just as much the _singulars_ are the _middle_ that puts that universal individual into external concrete existence and carries over its ethical essence into the extreme of actuality. As for the third syllogism, it is the formal one, the syllogism of shine, according to which the singulars are attached to this universal absolute individuality by their _needs_ and by external existence; being the merely subjective syllogism, it goes over into the other two and has its truth there.

_Free_ _mechanism_ is made up by this totality, whose moments are themselves the concept's complete relations, the _syllogisms_, and within which each of the three distinguished objects runs through the determination of middle term and of extremes. Within it, what the distinguished objects have as their fundamental determination is objective universality — _the_ _gravity_ _that_ _permeates_ _and_ _keeps_ _itself_ _selfsame_ _throughout_ _particularization_. References such as _pressure_, _thrust_, _attraction_ and the like, and likewise _aggregations_ or _minglings_, fall to that relation of externality on which the third of the assembled syllogisms rests. _Order_, which is only the objects' external determinateness, has gone over into determination that is immanent and objective; and this determination is _law_.
