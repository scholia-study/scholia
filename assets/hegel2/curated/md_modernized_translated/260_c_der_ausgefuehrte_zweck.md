## {{{ 12.165 }}} C. The Accomplished Purpose

1. In its reference to the means the purpose stands already reflected into itself; what has not yet been posited, however, is its _objective_ return into itself. Directed as it still is against objectivity taken as the original presupposition, the activity of the purpose by way of its means meets in _that_ presupposition just this, to be indifferent towards determinateness. Were the activity once again to consist in nothing but determining the immediate objectivity, then what issued from it would once more be only a means, and so on into infinity; a purposive means would be all that came out, and not the objectivity of the purpose itself. The purpose at work in its means must therefore not determine the immediate object _as_ _an_ _external_, so that the object may rather come together with the concept's unity through its own self; or else that external activity which the purpose exercises by way of its means must determine itself _as_ _mediation_ and thereby sublate itself.

Referring as it does to the external object, the purpose's activity by way of the means is at first the _second_ _premise_ of the syllogism, – a reference, and an _immediate_ one, of the middle term to the other extreme. It counts as _immediate_ because an external object is what the middle term has in it, and the other extreme is just such an object. Efficacious and mighty against the latter the means is, because its own object is joined with the self-determining activity, while for that other the immediate determinateness it carries counts as indifferent. No other process obtains in this reference than the mechanical or the chemical; within this objective externality the earlier relations come out again, but now under the dominion of the purpose. – Such processes, however, as was exhibited in them, run of their own accord back into the purpose. If, then, the reference of the means to the external object that is to be worked upon counts at first as immediate, that reference has nonetheless presented itself even earlier as a syllogism, the purpose having proved to be its genuine middle term and unity. Since the means, then, is the object standing on the side of the purpose and carrying the purpose's activity within it, the mechanism taking place here is at the same time objectivity's return into itself, into the concept — a concept, though, already presupposed as the purpose; so far, the negative comportment of purposive activity towards the object is not an _external_ comportment but the alteration, and the passing over, of objectivity in its own self into that concept.

That the purpose refers itself immediately to an object and turns it into a means, and likewise that by way of this object it determines another, may be looked upon as _violence_, so far as the purpose comes on the scene as having a nature altogether unlike the object's, and the two objects are for their part mutually self-subsistent {{{ 12.166 }}} totalities. But that the purpose sets itself in a _mediated_ reference to the object, _shoving_ a further object in _between_ itself and it, may be looked upon as reason's _cunning_. Rationality in its finitude has, as was remarked, this side to it, that the purpose comports itself towards the presupposition, that is, towards the object's externality. Were its reference to that object an _immediate_ _reference_, the purpose would step into mechanism or chemism itself and would thereby lie open to contingency and to the wreck of its determination of being a concept that is in and for itself. As things stand, though, it puts an object forward as means, lets that object do the outward drudgery in its stead, abandons it to attrition, and shelters itself behind it from mechanical violence.

Being finite, the purpose has furthermore a finite content; on this score it is nothing absolute, nor a _rational_ simply in and for itself. The _means_, however, is the external middle term of that syllogism which is the purpose's accomplishment; and so it is in the means that the rationality present in the purpose declares itself as such — declares itself as a self-preserving within _this_ _external_ _other_, and precisely _through_ such externality. So far the _means_ ranks _higher_ than do the _finite_ purposes belonging to _external_ purposiveness; – more honour attaches to the _plough_ than to the enjoyments that are immediately got ready by it and that count as the purposes. The _tool_ lives on, while the immediate enjoyments perish and drop out of memory. It is in tools that the human being holds power over outward nature, however much, as regards his purposes, he is rather subject to it.

The purpose, though, does not merely keep itself outside the mechanical process; it preserves itself within that process and is the determination of it. Being just as much the truth of mechanism that is in and for itself, the purpose — the concept, namely, which concretely exists in freedom against the object and the object's process, and which is self-determining activity — comes together in that process with nothing but itself. This identity that is for itself is the power of the purpose over the object; and the purpose's activity is the manifestation of that identity. As _content_ the purpose is the _determinateness_ in and for itself, which in the object holds as indifferent and external, whereas the purpose's activity is, on the one side, the process's _truth_ and, being negative unity, the _sublating_ _of_ _the_ _shine_ of _externality_. Taken in the way of _abstraction_, what happens is that the indifferent determinateness of the object gets replaced, just as externally, by another; but in its _truth_ the simple _abstraction_ of determinateness is the negative's totality, the concrete concept, the concept that posits externality inside itself.

The purpose's _content_ is its negativity as _simple_ _particularity_ _reflected_ _into_ _itself_, marked off from its totality taken as _form_. Owing to such _simplicity_, the determinateness of which is in and for itself the concept's totality, the content shows up as what _stays_ _identical_ throughout the realization of the {{{ 12.167 }}} purpose. What the teleological process is, is the _translation_ into objectivity of the concept concretely existing distinctly as the concept's concept; and it turns out that this translating into a presupposed other is the concept's coming together _through_ _itself_, _with_ _itself_. So the purpose's content is that identity which concretely exists under the form of the identical. Through every transition the concept preserves itself — when, for instance, the cause turns into effect, the cause is what comes together, in the effect, with nothing but itself; in the teleological transition, though, it is the concept, already concretely existing as such _as_ _cause_, as the concrete unity absolutely _free_ against objectivity and its external determinability. The externality the purpose translates itself into has, as we saw, already been posited as a moment of the concept itself, as the form under which the concept differentiates itself within. In externality the purpose therefore has _its_ _own_ _moment_; and the content, being the content of the concrete unity, is the purpose's _simple_ _form_, a form which throughout the differentiated moments of the purpose — as subjective purpose, as means and mediated activity, and as objective purpose — not only stays the same _in_ _itself_ but concretely exists as what stays the same.

Of teleological activity one may therefore say that within it the end amounts to the beginning, the consequence to the ground, the effect to the cause; that it constitutes a becoming of what has already become; that within it nothing enters concrete existence save what concretely exists already, and so on; which means that all determinations of relation that belong to the sphere of reflection, or to that of immediate being, have on the whole lost their differences, and that whatever gets pronounced as an _other_ — end, consequence, effect and so on — no longer carries, in the purposive reference, the determination of an _other_, but is posited rather as identical with the simple concept.

2. Look now more closely at the product of teleological activity, and it has the purpose in it only externally, so far as the product stands as absolute presupposition over against the subjective purpose, so far, that is, as one halts at the thought that purposive activity by way of its means comports itself towards the object only mechanically, setting in the room of one indifferent determinateness of the object _another_ every bit as external to it. A determinateness of that kind, which an object owes to the purpose, is on the whole marked off from another, merely mechanical one by carrying that moment of a _unity_, so that, external though it be to the object, it is nonetheless in its own self no merely external thing. An object exhibiting such a unity is a whole, and towards it its parts, its own externality, stand indifferent; a determinate, _concrete_ unity gathering differentiated references and determinatenesses into itself. Such a unity, not to be comprehended out of the object's specific nature and, by its determinate content, other than the content peculiar to the object, is _for_ {{{ 12.168 }}} _itself_ no mechanical determinateness, though in the object it stays mechanical. Within this product of purposive activity the purpose's content and the object's content lie external to one another, and just so do the determinations of the two stand towards each other in the remaining moments of the syllogism, – in the conjoining middle term, purposive activity and the object that serves as means, and in the subjective purpose, the other extreme, the infinite form as the concept's totality and the form's content. Take the _reference_ by which the subjective purpose is conjoined with objectivity, and the one premise — namely the reference to the still external object of the object determined as means — no less than the other, namely the reference of the subjective purpose to the object that is made into a means, is an immediate reference. The syllogism therefore labours under the defect of the formal syllogism at large, that the references composing it are not themselves conclusions or mediations, but presuppose already that very conclusion which they are supposed to serve as means for producing.

Take the one _premise_, the immediate reference of the subjective purpose to the object that thereby becomes a means, and the former cannot refer itself to the latter immediately; for the latter is every bit as immediate as the object of that other extreme where the purpose is supposed to reach accomplishment _through_ _mediation_. So far as the two are posited as _diverse_, a means of their reference has to be shoved in between the subjective purpose and this objectivity; but a means of that sort is itself an object the purpose has already determined, and between its objectivity and its teleological determination a fresh means has to be shoved in, and so on into infinity. Herewith the _infinite_ _progress_ _of_ _mediation_ stands posited. – The like holds with respect to the other premise, the reference of the means to the object that is still undetermined. Utterly self-subsistent as the two are, only in a third can they be united, and so on into infinity. – Or conversely, since the premises presuppose the _conclusion_ already, the conclusion, being what it is by way of premises merely immediate, can only be defective. The conclusion, or the _product_ of purposive doing, is nothing beyond an object determined by a purpose external to it; it _is_ _therefore_ _the_ _same_ _as_ _the_ _means_. What has come out in such a product is accordingly itself _only_ _a_ _means_, not _an_ _accomplished_ _purpose_; or: in it the purpose has genuinely reached no objectivity at all. – Whether one regards an object determined by the external purpose as an accomplished purpose or merely as a means is therefore wholly a matter of indifference; what we have here is a relative determination, external to the object itself, and not an objective one. Every object, then, in which an external purpose has been accomplished is quite as much a mere means of the purpose. Whatever gets used for the accomplishment of a purpose and {{{ 12.169 }}} is to be taken essentially as means is means according to its determination of being worn away. But the object too, the one supposed to contain the accomplished purpose and to exhibit itself as the purpose's objectivity, is perishable; it likewise fulfils its purpose not by way of a quiescent, self-preserving existence, but only so far as it is worn away; for to that extent alone does it answer to the concept's unity, its externality — that is, its objectivity — sublating itself therein. – A house or a clock may show up as the purposes over against the tools used in bringing them forth; but the stones, beams, or the wheels, axles and so on which make up the purpose's actuality fulfil it only through the pressure they undergo, through the chemical processes to which they lie exposed along with air, light and water, processes they take off the human being's hands, through their friction and so on. Their determination they therefore fulfil only through being used and worn down, and only through their negation do they answer to what they are supposed to be. Positively they are not united with the purpose, since self-determination attaches to them only externally, and they are relative purposes only, or essentially just means as well.

Such purposes have on the whole, as was shown, a limited content; their form is the concept's infinite self-determination, which through that content has limited itself down to external singularity. Because of the limited content these purposes are inadequate to the concept's infinity and turn into an untruth; determinateness of that sort has already, by way of the sphere of necessity, by way of being, been handed over to becoming and alteration, and is a perishable thing.

3. What emerges as result is accordingly this, that external purposiveness, having as yet only the form of teleology, gets properly no further than means and never as far as an objective purpose, – because the subjective purpose stays an external, subjective determination, – or, so far as it is active and does carry itself out, though only in a means, it remains bound up _immediately_ with objectivity, sunk into it; it is itself an object, and one may say that so far the purpose never gets as far as a means, because the purpose's accomplishment is called for beforehand, before that accomplishment could ever come about by way of a means.

In fact, however, the result is not merely an external purposive reference but the truth of such a reference, an inner purposive reference and an objective purpose. That externality of the object which is self-subsistent against the concept, and which the purpose presupposes for itself, is in this presupposing _posited_ as an unessential shine, and in and for itself it has been sublated already; properly, then, the purpose's activity is nothing but the exhibiting of this shine and the sublating of it. – As the concept made evident, the first object becomes means through the communication, because it is in itself the concept's totality and because its determinateness, which is nothing other than externality itself, is posited only _as_ an external and unessential, {{{ 12.170 }}} hence stands within the purpose itself as the purpose's own moment and not as something self-subsistent against it. Thereby [the] determination of the object into a means is an utterly immediate one. For the subjective purpose no violence is called for, then, nor any corroboration against the object beyond the corroboration of itself, in order to make the object into a means; the _resolve_, the disclosure, this self-determining of the concept, is the object's _merely_ _posited_ externality, and the object therein lies immediately subjected to the purpose and carries against it no determination other than the nullity of its being-in-and-for-itself.

From this the second sublating of objectivity by objectivity differs in that the first one just described is the purpose in objective _immediacy_, so that the second sublates not a first immediacy alone but both, the objective as a merely posited and the immediate. Negativity in this fashion returns into itself so as to be equally a restoring of objectivity — of an objectivity identical with it, though — and therein at once a positing of objectivity as an external one determined merely by the purpose. By virtue of the latter this product stays, as before, means as well; by virtue of the former it is the objectivity identical with the concept, the realized purpose, where the side of being a means is the very reality of the purpose. Within the accomplished purpose the means vanishes because it would be objectivity as yet only immediately subsumed under the purpose, objectivity which in the realized purpose figures as the purpose's return into itself; and along with it there vanishes further the mediation itself, being as it is a comportment of something external, vanishing partly into the concrete identity of the objective purpose, partly into that identity taken as abstract identity and as immediacy of existence.

Contained herein is also the mediation that was called for in the case of the first premise, the immediate reference of the purpose to the object. The accomplished purpose is means as well; conversely, it belongs quite as much to the truth of the means to be real purpose itself; and objectivity's first sublating is already the second too, just as the second showed itself to hold the first within it. The concept, namely, _determines_ _itself_, and its determinateness is that external indifference which immediately, in the resolve, gets determined as _sublated_ — namely as _inner_, as _subjective_ — and at once as a _presupposed_ _object_. The concept's further going forth out of itself, which showed up, namely, as the _immediate_ communicating to the presupposed object and the subsuming of it under the concept, is at once a sublating of that inner determinateness of externality _shut_ _up_ _within_ _the_ _concept_ — the determinateness, that is, posited as sublated — and at once a sublating of the presupposing of an object; so this apparently first sublating of {{{ 12.171 }}} indifferent objectivity turns out to be the second as well, a reflection-into-itself having passed through the mediation, and is the accomplished purpose.

Since the concept stands here in reciprocity with its own self, within the sphere of objectivity, where the concept's determinateness carries the form of _indifferent_ _externality_, the exposition of its movement turns doubly hard and tangled at this point, that movement being immediately the doubled one, so that a first is invariably a second too. In the concept for itself, that is, in the concept's subjectivity, the difference of the concept from itself is for itself as an _immediate_ identical totality; but since here the concept's determinateness is indifferent externality, the identity therein with itself is immediately once more a repelling of itself, such that what is determined as identity's external and indifferent is rather identity itself, while identity as its own self, reflected into itself, is rather identity's other. It is only in holding fast to this that one grasps the objective return of the concept into itself, that is, the concept's genuine objectification; – grasps it, namely, in such wise that every one of the single moments along which this mediation runs is itself the mediation's whole syllogism. Thus the concept's original _inner_ externality, whereby the concept is the unity repelling itself from itself, purpose and the purpose's striving out towards objectification, amounts to the immediate positing, or presupposing, of an external object; the _self-determination_ is at the same time a determining of an _external_ object as one the concept has not determined; and conversely it is self-determination, that is, the externality sublated and posited as _inner_; – or, in other words, the _certainty_ that the outer object is _unessential_. – As for the second reference, where the object is determined as means, it has just now been made plain how in its own self it is the purpose's mediation with itself in the object. – Likewise the third, the mechanism running its course under the dominion of the purpose and sublating the object through the object, counts on the one side as sublating the means, the object posited as sublated already, and so as a second sublating and reflection-into-itself, on the other side as a first determining of the object that is external. That last, as was remarked, amounts once again in the accomplished purpose to bringing forth nothing but a means; when the subjectivity of the finite concept flings the means away in contempt, it has reached nothing better in its goal. Yet this reflection — that in the means the purpose is reached, and that in the fulfilled purpose means and mediation alike are preserved — is the _final_ _result_ _of_ _the_ _external_ _purposive_ _reference_, wherein that reference has sublated its own self and has exhibited this reflection as its truth. – The third syllogism considered last is marked off by this, that it is, in the first place, the subjective purposive activity of the syllogisms that went before, but is also the sublation, _through_ _itself_, of external objectivity and thereby of {{{ 12.172 }}} externality at large, and hence _the_ _totality_ _in_ _its_ _positedness_.

Now that we have watched _subjectivity_, the _being-for-itself_ of the concept, pass over into that concept's _being-in-itself_, namely into _objectivity_, we find that within the latter the negativity of its being-for-itself has come forth once more; in objectivity the concept has determined itself so that its _particularity_ is _external_ _objectivity_, or so that it is the simple concrete unity, a unity whose externality constitutes its self-determination. What the movement of the purpose has now reached is this, that externality's moment is not merely posited within the concept, that the concept is not merely an _ought_ and a _striving_, but that as concrete totality it is identical with immediate objectivity. On one side this identity is the simple concept and quite as much _immediate_ objectivity, on the other side it is just as essentially _mediation_, and is that simple immediacy only by way of mediation, mediation sublating its own self; so the concept is essentially this, to be marked off, as identity that is for itself, from its objectivity that is _in-itself_, and thereby to have externality, yet within this external totality to be the self-determining identity of it. Thus the concept is now _the_ IDEA.
