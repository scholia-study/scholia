## {{{ 11.393 }}} Chapter 3. The Absolute Relation

Absolute necessity is not so much what is _necessary_, and still less _a_ necessary something, but rather _necessity_; – being purely and simply as reflection. Relation it is, for it is a differentiating whose moments are themselves its whole totality and which therefore absolutely _subsist_, though in such fashion that all this is but One subsisting, the difference being merely the _shine_ belonging to the expounding, and that shine the absolute itself. – Essence taken as such _is_ reflection, or the shining; essence taken as absolute relation, however, is _shine_ _posited_ _as_ _shine_, and as this referring to itself it is _absolute_ _actuality_. – Expounded at the outset _by_ _external_ _reflection_, the absolute now expounds its own self, doing so as absolute form or as necessity; and such expounding of itself is its positing of itself, so that it _is_ nothing beyond this self-positing. – Just as the _light_ of nature is neither a something nor a thing, its being lying wholly in its shining, so manifestation is that absolute actuality which stands equal to its own self.

Hence the sides of the absolute relation are no _attributes_. Within the attribute the absolute shines in one of its moments only, a moment _presupposed_ and taken up by _external_ _reflection_. What _expounds_ the absolute, though, is _absolute_ _necessity_, identical with itself in that it determines its own self. Being the shining that stands posited as shine, it makes the sides of this relation into _totalities_, since they are as shine; for as shine the differences are themselves together with what is opposed to them, that is, the whole; – and conversely, they are shine on the ground that they are totalities. Such differentiating, or shining, of the absolute is accordingly no more than the identical positing of its own self.

This relation, in the immediate concept it has, is that of _substance_ and _accidents_: the absolute shine's immediate vanishing, and its becoming, within its own self. So far as substance determines itself into _being-for-itself_ as against an _other_, or the absolute relation determines itself as real, there is the _relation_ _of_ _causality_. Lastly, so far as this latter, being something that refers itself to itself, passes over into _reciprocity_, the absolute relation stands thereby _posited_ too according to the determinations it holds within it; and this _posited_ _unity_ of it within its _determinations_, _which_ _as_ _the_ _whole_ _itself_, and thereby just as much as determinations, _are_ _posited_, is then the _concept_.
