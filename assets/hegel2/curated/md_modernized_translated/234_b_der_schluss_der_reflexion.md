## B. The Syllogism of Reflection

What the qualitative syllogism ran through has sublated the _abstract_ character of its determinations; the term has thereby posited itself as such a determinateness that the other _shines_ within it too. Over and above the abstract terms, there is present in the syllogism their _reference_ as well, and the conclusion posits this as mediated and necessary; in truth, then, no determinateness is posited as a single one on its own account, but each as a referring of the others, as _concrete_ determinateness.

As abstract particularity, the _middle_ _term_ was for itself a simple determinateness, and a middle only in an external, relative way as against extremes that subsist on their own. Now it stands posited as the _totality_ of determinations, and so is the _posited_ unity of the extremes, though to begin with the unity of reflection embracing them; – an embracing which, as a _first_ sublating of immediacy and a first referring of determinations, falls short still of the concept's absolute identity.

{{{ 12.111 }}} The extremes here are determinations belonging to the judgment of reflection; _singularity_ in the proper sense, and _universality_ as determination of relation, or a reflection drawing a manifold together in itself. Yet the singular subject, as the judgment of reflection showed, harbours besides the bare singularity that pertains to form the determinateness as well, as a universality reflected simply into itself, as a _genus_ presupposed, that is, one here still taken up immediately.

Out of this determinateness of the extremes, belonging as it does to the course taken by the determining of judgment, there issues the closer content of the _middle_ _term_, on which everything in the syllogism essentially turns, since it is what sets the syllogism apart from the judgment. What it contains is 1) _singularity_, 2) singularity widened, however, to universality, as _all_, 3) the universality that underlies these and unites singularity and abstract universality outright within itself, _the_ _genus_. – Not until now, with the middle term _posited_ as the totality of the determinations, does the syllogism of reflection possess the _proper_ _determinateness_ of form; measured against it the immediate syllogism is for that very reason the _indeterminate_ one, its middle term being as yet no more than the abstract particularity, wherein the moments of its concept remain unposited. – One may call this first syllogism of reflection the _syllogism_ _of_ _allness_.
