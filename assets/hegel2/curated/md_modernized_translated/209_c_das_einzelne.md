## {{{ 12.49 }}} C) The Singular

_Singularity_, as has come out, is posited already through particularity; the latter is _determinate_ _universality_; hence the determinateness that refers to itself, the _determinate_ _determinate_.

1. At first, accordingly, singularity shows up as _the_ _reflection_ of the concept _into_ _itself_ out of its determinateness. It is that concept's _mediation_ by way of itself, so far as its _otherness_ has once more turned itself into an _other_, whereby the concept is re-established as equal to itself, though standing in the determination of _absolute_ _negativity_. – What is negative in the universal, whereby the universal is a _particular_, was fixed above as the doubled shine; so far as this is a shining _inwards_, the particular stays a universal; by the shining _outwards_ it is _determinate_; the return of this side into the universal runs two ways, _either_ by the _abstraction_ which drops the determinateness and mounts to the _higher_ and _highest_ _genus_, _or_ else by the _singularity_ into which the universal comes down within the determinateness itself. – At this point there branches off the side-road along which abstraction quits the path of the concept and abandons truth. That higher and highest universal to which abstraction lifts itself is nothing but a surface ever emptier of content; the singularity it spurns is the depth wherein the concept lays hold of itself and is posited as concept.

On one side, _universality_ and _particularity_ made their appearance as the moments of singularity's _becoming_. Yet it has been shown already that in their own selves they are the whole concept, so that in _singularity_ they pass over into no _other_; what is posited there is only what they are in and for themselves. The _universal_ is _for_ _itself_ in that it is, on its own part, the absolute mediation, self-reference only as absolute negativity. It is an _abstract_ universal so far as this sublating is an _external_ activity and with that a _dropping_ of determinateness. Such negativity does indeed hang upon the abstract, but it remains _outside_ it, a bare _condition_ of it; it is abstraction itself, which sets its universal _facing_ it, and which for that reason has no singularity within and is left conceptless. – Life, spirit, God, and the pure concept along with them, abstraction cannot grasp, and this because it withholds from its own products singularity, the principle of individuality and of personality, and so gets no further than universalities void of life and spirit, of colour and content.

So indivisible, however, is the unity of the concept that even these products of {{{ 12.50 }}} abstraction, though meant to let singularity fall away, are themselves rather _singulars_. Since abstraction lifts the concrete into universality while apprehending the universal only as determinate universality, what results is precisely singularity, which turned out to be determinateness referring to itself. Abstraction is thus a _sundering_ of the concrete and a _singularizing_ of its determinations; by it nothing but _singular_ properties or moments get taken up, since its product has to hold within it what abstraction itself is. What marks off the singularity of abstraction's products from that of the concept is this, that in the former the singular counts as _content_ and the universal as _form_, the two being diverse; – just because that content is not there as the absolute form, as the concept itself, or that form not as the totality of form. – Looked at more closely, though, the abstract shows itself as the unity of singular content with abstract universality, therefore as something _concrete_, as the very opposite of what it means to be.

On that same ground, being nothing but the determinate universal, the _particular_ is also a _singular_; and the other way round, since the singular is the determinate universal, it is equally a particular. Clinging to this abstract determinateness, one finds in the concept three particular determinations, the universal, the particular and the singular; whereas before, only universal and particular were named as the species of the particular. Because singularity is the concept's return into itself as the negative, that very return can be ranged _beside_ the others and counted off as an indifferent moment by the abstraction which in it is properly sublated.

Where singularity gets entered among the _particular_ determinations of the concept, particularity becomes the _totality_ embracing them all; and being that totality, it is their concrete, that is, singularity itself. It is the concrete on the side remarked upon earlier too, as _determinate_ _universality_; taken so, it is the _immediate_ unity wherein no one of these moments is posited as differentiated or as the determinant, and under this form it will serve as the _middle_ _term_ of the _formal_ _syllogism_.

One notices without prompting that each determination made in the exposition of the concept thus far has straightway dissolved and lost itself in its other. Every distinction runs together in the very examination meant to isolate and secure it. _Representation_ alone, for which abstracting has set them apart, manages to keep universal, particular and singular firmly asunder; on that footing they are countable, and for any further difference representation falls back on the _wholly_ _external_ difference of _being_, on _quantity_, which is nowhere less in place than here. – Singularity is where that true relation, the _inseparability_ of the concept's determinations, stands _posited_; for being negation of {{{ 12.51 }}} negation, it holds their opposition and holds it at once within its ground or unity, the coming-together of each with its other. Since universality is in and for itself within this reflection, singularity is essentially the negativity of those determinations, and not merely so as to be a third term diverse from them; what is now _posited_, rather, is that _positedness_ is _being-in-_ _and_ _for-itself_; that is, that each of the determinations belonging to the difference is itself the _totality_. The determinate concept's return into itself consists in its having the determination of being, _in_ _its_ _determinateness_, the _whole_ concept.

2. Yet singularity is not the concept's return into itself alone; it is immediately the concept's loss. By way of singularity, in which the concept is thus _within_ _itself_, it gets _outside_ _itself_ and enters actuality. _Abstraction_, being as the _soul_ of singularity the reference of negative to negative, has proved to be nothing outward for universal and particular but immanent in both, and by it they are concrete, content, singular. Singularity, however, as this negativity, is the determinate determinateness, the act of _distinguishing_ as such; by this reflection of the difference into itself the difference turns firm; the determining of the particular first arises through singularity; for _singularity_ is that abstraction which now, just as singularity, is _posited_ _abstraction_.

So the singular, as negativity referring to itself, is the immediate identity of the negative with itself; it is something _that_ _is_ _for_ _itself_. Or else it is the abstraction which fixes the concept, in respect of its ideal moment of _being_, as an _immediate_. – The singular is accordingly a qualitative _one_ or _this_. In virtue of this quality it is, first, the repelling of itself away from _itself_, and thereby the many _other_ ones stand presupposed; _secondly_, it is now negative reference to these presupposed _others_, and so far forth the singular is _exclusive_. Universality brought into reference with these singulars as indifferent ones, – and into that reference it has to be brought, since it is a moment of the concept of singularity, – is merely what they have _in common_. Take the universal to mean whatever several singulars possess _in common_, and one has set out from their _indifferent_ subsistence, mixing the immediacy of _being_ into the determination of the concept. The poorest representation available of the universal, as it stands in reference to the singular, is just this external relation of it, as of something merely _held in common_.

In the reflective sphere of concrete existence the singular is as _this_, and it lacks that _exclusive_ reference to another one which attaches to qualitative {{{ 12.52 }}} being-for-itself. As the one _reflected_ _into_ _itself_, _this_ is for itself without repulsion; or else repulsion is here at one with abstraction and is the reflecting _mediation_, present in the this in such a way that the this is a _posited_ immediacy, one _shown_ by something external to it. _This_ IS; it is _immediate_; yet it is only _this_ so far as it is _indicated_. Indicating is the reflecting movement that draws itself together into itself and posits immediacy, though as something external to it. – The singular too, of course, is a this, the immediate brought forth out of mediation; only it does not carry that mediation outside itself, it is itself a repelling sundering, _posited_ _abstraction_, and yet, in that very sundering, positive reference.

This abstracting on the part of the singular, being the reflection of difference into itself, is first of all a positing of the differentiated terms as _self-subsistent_, as reflected into themselves. They _are_ immediately; but this severing is reflection as such besides, the _shining_ _of_ _the_ _one_ _in_ _the_ _other_; hence they stand in essential reference. Nor, further, are they merely singulars _that_ _are_, set against one another; plurality of that kind belongs to being; _singularity_, positing itself as determinate, posits itself not in a difference that is external but in the difference of the concept; it shuts the _universal_ out from itself, and yet, this being a moment of singularity itself, [it] refers itself to singularity no less essentially.

Taken as this reference among its _self-subsistent_ determinations, the concept has lost itself; for so it is no more their _posited_ _unity_, and they are no more as _moments_, as its _shine_, but as subsisting in and for themselves. – As singularity it turns back into itself within the determinateness; with that, the determinate has itself become totality. Its turning back into itself is therefore the absolute, original _dividing_ of itself; or, as singularity, it is posited as _judgment_.
