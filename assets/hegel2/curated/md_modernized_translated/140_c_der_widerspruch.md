## {{{ 11.279 }}} C. Contradiction

1. _Difference_ in general holds its two sides within it as _moments_; in _diversity_ these fall apart _indifferently_; in _opposition_ as such they are the difference's sides, the one determined only through the other, hence only moments; but they are just as much determined upon their own selves, indifferent towards each other and mutually excluding one another; the _self-subsistent_ _determinations_ _of_ _reflection_.

The one of them is the _positive_ and the other the _negative_, the first, however, as what is positive upon its own self, the second as what is negative upon its own self. Each of them has indifferent self-subsistence for itself by having upon its own self the relation to its other moment; each is therefore the entire opposition, closed within itself. – Being this whole, each is mediated with its own self _through_ _its_ _other_ and _contains_ that other. But it is further mediated with itself through _the_ _non-being_ _of_ _its_ _other_; it is thus a unity that is for itself and _shuts_ the other _out_ of itself.

Inasmuch as the self-subsistent determination of reflection excludes the other in the very respect in which it contains the other and is self-subsistent through containing it, in its self-subsistence it shuts out from itself its own self-subsistence; for that self-subsistence consists in holding within itself the determination other to it, and on that account alone in not being a relation to something external, while it consists no less immediately in being itself and in shutting out from itself the determination that is its own negative. In this way it is the _contradiction_.

Difference in general is already contradiction _in_ _itself_; for it is the _unity_ of such as are only insofar as they are _not_ _one_, – and the _separation_ of such as are only as sundered _in_ _the_ _same_ _relation_. The positive and the negative, however, are contradiction as _posited_, since, being negative unities, they are the positing of their own selves, and therein each is at once what sublates its own self and what posits its contrary. – What they make up is determining reflection in its character as _excluding_; since excluding is _one_ distinguishing, and each of the distinguished terms, being itself an excluding, is the whole excluding, each shuts its own self out within itself.

Taking the two self-subsistent determinations of reflection for themselves, the positive is the _positedness_ that stands reflected _into_ _self-sameness_ _with_ _itself_; the positedness which is no relation to an other, hence subsistence insofar as positedness is _sublated_ and _excluded_. With this, though, the positive makes of itself the _relation_ _of_ _a_ _non-being_, – a _positedness_. – {{{ 11.280 }}} So the contradiction here is that, being the positing of self-identity through _excluding_ the negative, it makes of its own self the _negative_ of something, hence the other which it shuts out from itself. This other, as excluded, is posited as free from the excluding one; hence as reflected into itself and as itself excluding. Excluding reflection is in this way a positing of the positive as that which excludes the other, so that such positing immediately posits its other too, the other which excludes it.

Such is the absolute contradiction of the positive, though immediately it is likewise the negative's absolute contradiction; the positing of the two is one reflection. – Taken for itself over against the positive, the negative is positedness _as_ reflected _into_ _unlikeness_ _with_ _its_ _own_ _self_, the negative as negative. Yet the negative is itself what is unlike, the non-being of an other; its reflection into its own unlikeness is thus rather a relation to its own self. – Negation _in_ _general_ is the negative taken as quality, or as _immediate_ determinateness; the negative _as_ _negative_, on the other hand, stands in relation to its own negative, to its other. If this negative is taken only as identical with the first, then it, like the first, is only immediate; taken so, they are not taken as others over against each other, hence not as negatives; the negative is in general not something immediate. – But now, since further each is just as much the same as the other, this relation of the unlike terms is just as much their identical relation.

This, then, is the very same contradiction that the positive is: positedness, or negation, as a relation to self. But the positive is this contradiction only _in_ _itself_; the negative, by contrast, is the _posited_ contradiction; for in its reflection into itself, in being negative in and for itself, or in being identical with itself as negative, it has the determination of being what is non-identical, the shutting out of identity. It is this, to be _identical_ _with_ _itself_ _over_ _against_ _identity_, and hence through its excluding reflection to shut its own self out from itself.

The negative is therefore the whole opposition, opposition that rests upon itself as opposition, the absolute difference that does _not_ _relate_ _itself_ _to_ _an_ _other_; as opposition it shuts identity out from itself, but thereby its own self as well, for as _relation_ _to_ _itself_ it determines itself as the very identity that it excludes.

## 2. The Contradiction Resolves Itself.

Within the self-excluding reflection just considered, the positive and the negative each sublate their own selves in their self-subsistence; each is simply and solely the passing over, or rather the transposing of itself into its opposite. This restless vanishing of the opposites upon their own selves is the _proximate_ _unity_ that comes about through contradiction; it is _zero_.

{{{ 11.281 }}} Contradiction, however, holds within it not the _negative_ alone but the _positive_ too; or, self-excluding reflection is at once also _positing_ reflection; the outcome of contradiction is not zero alone. – The positive and the negative make up the _positedness_ of self-subsistence; the negation of them through their own selves sublates the _positedness_ of self-subsistence. This is what in truth founders in contradiction.

The reflection into itself whereby the sides of the opposition make of themselves self-subsistent relations to self is at first their self-subsistence as _distinct_ moments; they possess this self-subsistence as yet only _in_ _themselves_, since they are still terms opposed to one another, and their being it _in_ _themselves_ constitutes their positedness. Their excluding reflection, however, sublates this positedness, makes them into self-subsistent terms that are for themselves, into such as are self-subsistent not merely _in_ _themselves_ but by virtue of their negative relation to their other; their self-subsistence is in this way also posited. But further, through this positing of theirs they make themselves into a positedness. _They_ _run_ _themselves_ _aground_, in that they determine themselves as what is identical with itself, yet therein rather as the negative, as something identical with itself that is a relation to an other.

Only, this excluding reflection, considered more closely, is not just this formal determination. It is a self-subsistence _that_ _is_ _in_ _itself_, and it is the sublating of this positedness, and only through this sublating is it a unity that is for itself and in fact self-subsistent. Through the sublating of otherness or of positedness there is indeed once more positedness at hand, the negative of an other. But in fact this negation is not once more merely a first, immediate relation to another, not positedness as sublated immediacy, but as sublated positedness. In being excluding, the excluding reflection of self-subsistence makes itself into positedness, but is just as much the sublating of its positedness. It relates to itself as sublating; therein it _first_ sublates the negative and _second_ posits itself as negative, and only this is the negative which it sublates; in the sublating of the negative it at once posits and sublates it. The _excluding_ _determination_ _itself_ is in this way its own _other_, the other whose negation it is; the sublating of this positedness is therefore not once more a positedness, the negative of an other; it is rather the going-together with its own self that constitutes positive unity with itself. Self-subsistence is in this way a unity returning into itself by way of _its_ _own_ negation, in that it comes back into itself through negating _its_ positedness. It is the unity of essence: to be self-identical not through negating an other but through negating its very self.

3. On this positive side, that self-subsistence in opposition, as excluding reflection, makes itself into positedness and just as much {{{ 11.282 }}} sublates its being positedness, opposition has not only _foundered_ but has gone back _into_ _its_ _ground_. – The self-subsistent opposition's excluding reflection makes it into something negative, something merely posited; it thereby demotes its at first self-subsistent _determinations_, the positive and the negative, to such as are _only_ _determinations_; and in that positedness is thereby made into positedness, it has as a whole gone back into unity with itself; it is _simple_ _essence_, though essence as _ground_. Essence is restored by the sublating of those of its determinations that contradict themselves upon their own selves, yet restored with the determination of being an excluding unity of reflection, – a simple unity which determines its own self as negative but in this positedness is immediately self-same and has gone together with itself.

At first, then, the self-subsistent opposition _goes_ _back_ into ground through its contradiction; that opposition is the first, the immediate, from which the start is made, and the opposition once sublated, or positedness once sublated, is itself a positedness. _Essence_ _as_ _ground_ _is_ _accordingly_ _a_ _positedness_, _something_ _become_. Conversely, however, the only thing that has been posited is that the opposition, or positedness, is something sublated, is only as positedness. Essence as ground is therefore excluding reflection in this sense, that it makes of its own self a positedness; that the opposition with which the beginning was made a moment ago, the opposition which was the immediate, is essence's determinate self-subsistence as merely posited; and that this opposition is only what sublates itself upon its own self, whereas essence is what stands reflected into itself in its determinateness. Essence as ground shuts _itself_ out from its own self, it posits _itself_; and its positedness – the very thing excluded – has being only as positedness, as the negative's identity with its own self. This self-subsistent term is the negative, _posited_ as negative; something self-contradicting, which therefore remains immediately in essence as its ground.

The contradiction thus resolved is ground, essence as the unity of the positive and the negative. In opposition, determination has matured into self-subsistence; ground, however, is this completed self-subsistence; the negative is in it self-subsistent essence, but as negative; ground is thus just as much the positive, as what is identical with itself in this negativity. Opposition and its contradiction are therefore in ground as much sublated as preserved. Ground is essence as the positive identity with itself; but an identity which at once relates itself to itself as negativity, thus determines itself and makes of itself an excluded positedness; this positedness, however, is the entire self-subsistent essence, and as being identical with its own self and positive in this its negation, essence is ground. The self-contradicting self-subsistent opposition was therefore already itself the ground; all that was added was the determination of unity with its own self, and this comes forth in that {{{ 11.283 }}} the self-subsistent opposites each sublate their own selves and make themselves into the other of themselves, hence founder, but therein at the same time only go together with their own selves, and so, in their going-under – that is, in their positedness, or in negation – are rather for the first time the essence reflected into itself and self-identical.
