## Remark

_Sublating_, and the _sublated_ (the _ideal_), counts among the most important concepts of philosophy, a fundamental determination recurring absolutely everywhere, whose sense wants grasping with precision and, above all, keeping distinct from nothing. – Whatever sublates itself does not on that account become nothing. The _immediate_ is what nothing is; a sublated something, by contrast, is a _mediated_ one – non-being, but non-being as _result_, issuing from a being; and so it has the _determinateness_ _out_ _of_ _which_ _it_ _comes_ _still_ _within_ _itself_.

In the language, _sublate_ bears a twofold sense: it says as much as to store up, to _preserve_, and at the same time as much as to bring to a halt, to _make_ _an_ _end_. Already the storing up encloses the negative within it, namely that something gets withdrawn from its immediacy, and thereby from an existence lying open to external effects, so as to be preserved. – The sublated is thus at once something stored up, something that has forfeited its immediacy alone and is not for that reason destroyed. – Lexically, the two determinations of _sublating_ set out here may be entered as two _meanings_ of the word. Yet it must strike one that a language should have arrived at employing a single word for two opposed determinations. Speculative thinking finds it gratifying that language should supply words carrying a speculative meaning in their own selves; German has several such. The double sense of the {{{ 21.95 }}} Latin: tollere (famous through the Ciceronian jest: tollendum esse Octavium) does not extend so far, its affirmative determination reaching only to lifting up. Something counts as sublated only insofar as it has entered into unity with what stands opposed to it; under this closer determination, as something reflected, the name _moment_ suits it well. _Weight_ and _distance_ from a point go by the name of the lever's mechanical _moments_, owing to the _selfsameness_ of their effect for all the diversity otherwise between something real, as a weight is, and something ideal, the bare spatial determination, the line; (see the Encyclopaedia of the Philosophical Sciences, 3rd ed., § 261, Remark) – The observation will force itself upon one still oftener that philosophical terminology draws on Latin expressions for reflected determinations, either where the mother tongue supplies none, or, where it does supply them, as here, because its own expression recalls the immediate rather, while the foreign tongue recalls the reflected.

What closer sense and expression being and nothing acquire, now that they have become _moments_, must emerge when existence is considered, existence being the unity in which the two are stored up. Only in their differentiatedness from one another is being being and nothing nothing; in their truth, that is, in their unity, both have vanished as the determinations they were and are now something other. Being and nothing are the same; and _precisely_ _because_ _they_ _are_ _the_ _same_, _they_ _are_ _being_ _and_ _nothing_ _no_ _longer_, carrying instead a different determination; within becoming they figured as coming-to-be and ceasing-to-be; within existence, a unity determined otherwise, they figure again as moments otherwise determined. Henceforth this unity stays their foundation, and out of it they no longer step forth into the abstract meaning of being and of nothing.
