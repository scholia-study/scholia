## Remark

At this point the concept of the _positive_ and the _negative_ should be brought in as it turns up in _arithmetic_. There it is presupposed as something familiar; but since it is not grasped in its determinate difference, it fails to escape difficulties and entanglements that admit of no resolution. Just now the two _real_ determinations of the positive and the negative have come out – over and above the simple concept of their opposition – namely that _the_ _first_ _time_ what lies at the base is a merely diverse, immediate existence, whose simple reflection into itself is distinguished from its positedness, from opposition itself. Opposition accordingly counts as something not in and for itself, and as accruing to the diverse in such a way that each is an opposite as such, yet also subsists for itself indifferently towards it, and it comes to the same thing which of the {{{ 11.276 }}} two opposed diverse terms is regarded as positive or as negative. – _The_ _second_ _time_, however, the positive is what is positive upon its own self, the negative what is negative upon its own self, so that the diverse is not indifferent towards this, but this is rather its determination in and for itself. – Both of these forms of the positive and the negative show up straightaway in the first determinations for which arithmetic puts them to use.

The + a and - a are at the outset _opposed_ _magnitudes_ _as_ _such_; a is what underlies the two, the _unity_ _that_ _is_ _in_ _itself_, the term indifferent towards opposition itself, serving here as a dead foundation with no further concept to it. The - a is indeed designated as the negative and the + a as the positive, but _the_ _one_ is quite as much _an_ _opposite_ _as_ _the_ _other_.

Further, a is not only the _simple_ unity lying at the base; in the shape of + a and - a it is these opposites reflected into itself; _two_ _diverse_ a's are at hand, and it makes no difference which of the two one cares to call the positive or the negative; both have a particular subsistence and are positive.

Taken on that first side, + y - y = 0; or, in - 8 + 3, the 3, which are positive, count as negative within the 8. In their combination the opposites sublate themselves. An hour's journey made eastward, and just as much of it back westward, sublates the journey first made; so much debt, so much less in assets, and however much in assets is at hand, that much of the debt is sublated. The hour's journey eastward is at the same time not the positive journey in itself, nor the westward one the negative journey; rather these directions stand indifferent towards this determinateness of opposition; only a third consideration falling outside them makes the one positive and the other negative. So too the debts are not in and for themselves the negative; only in relation to the debtor are they that; in the creditor's eyes they are positive assets of his; they are a sum of money, or whatever it may be of a certain value, which is debt or assets according to considerations falling outside it.

The opposites do indeed sublate themselves in their relation, so that the result comes out as zero; yet _their_ _identical_ _relation_ is present in them as well, a relation indifferent towards opposition itself; in this way they constitute _one_ thing. As has just been recalled of the sum of money, which is only one sum, or of the a, which is only one a in the + a and the - a; the journey too, which is only one stretch of road, not two roads of which one would run eastward and the other westward. So too an ordinate y, which is the same thing whether taken on this or on that side of the axis; to that extent + y - y = y; it is only _the_ ordinate, and there is only one determination and law belonging to it.

{{{ 11.277 }}} Further, though, the opposites are not merely one indifferent thing but _two_ _indifferent_ ones. For as opposites they are also reflected into themselves, and so subsist as diverse ones.

In - 8 + 3 there are thus eleven units altogether; + y, - y, are ordinates on the opposite side of the axis, where each is an existence indifferent towards this limit and towards its opposition; thus + y - y = 2 y. – The road covered eastward and westward, too, is the sum of a twofold exertion, or the sum of two stretches of time. In political economy, likewise, a quantum of money, or of value, is not merely this one quantum as a means of subsistence, but is a doubled one; it is a means of subsistence for the creditor as much as for the debtor. The assets of a state are reckoned not merely as the sum of the ready money and of the remaining value in immovables and movables that is at hand within the state, and still less as the sum that would be left over once the passive assets were deducted from the active, but rather the capital, even if its active and its passive determination reduced themselves to zero, remains _first_ positive capital; as + a - a = a; but secondly, being in manifold ways passive, lent out and lent out again, it is thereby a very much multiplied means.

Yet the opposed magnitudes are not only, on the one hand, merely opposites as such and, on the other, real or indifferent ones. Rather, although the quantum itself is indifferently limited being, what is positive in itself and what is negative in itself nonetheless show up upon it. The a, for instance, insofar as it carries no sign, counts as something to be taken as positive whenever it is to be given a sign. If it were only to become an opposite as such, then taking it as - a would do equally well. The positive sign, though, is what gets assigned to it immediately, because the positive for itself carries the peculiar meaning of the immediate, of what is identical with itself, over against opposition.

Further, when positive and negative magnitudes are added or subtracted, they count as being positive and negative for themselves, and as not becoming so merely through the relation of adding or subtracting, in this external fashion. In 8 - (- 3) the first minus means opposed _to_ 8, whereas the second minus (- 3) counts as an opposite _in_ _itself_, outside this relation.

This emerges more distinctly with multiplication and division; here the positive has essentially to be taken as the _non-opposite_, the negative on the contrary as the opposite, and not both determinations in like fashion as merely opposites as such. Since the textbooks, in their proofs of how the signs behave in these two kinds of calculation, stop short at the concept {{{ 11.278 }}} of opposed magnitudes as such, these proofs are incomplete and get entangled in contradictions. – With multiplication and division, however, plus and minus take on the more determinate meaning of positive and negative in themselves, because the relation of the factors, that of being unit and amount towards each other, is no mere relation of increasing and decreasing, as with adding and subtracting, but a qualitative one; whereby plus and minus also take on the qualitative meaning of the positive and the negative. – Lacking this determination, and going merely by the concept of opposed magnitudes, one may easily draw the skewed inference that if - a · + a = - a², then conversely + a · - a would yield + a². Since the one factor means the amount and the other the unit, the one standing in front meaning as usual the former, the two expressions - a · + a and + a · - a differ in this, that in the first + a is the unit and - a the amount, whereas in the other it is the other way round. Of the first the usual thing said is this: should + a be taken - a times, then + a is taken not merely a times but at once in the way opposed to it, + a times - a; and so, plus being what it is, it has to be taken negatively, which makes the product - a². – Should - a in the second case be taken + a times, however, then - a too is supposed to be taken, not - a times, but in the determination opposed to it, that is, + a times. Going by the reasoning of the first case it follows, then, that the product would have to be + a². – Likewise with division.

This consequence is unavoidable insofar as plus and minus are taken merely as opposed magnitudes as such; in the first case the power of altering the plus is ascribed to the minus; but in the other, plus was supposed not to hold the same power over minus, notwithstanding that it is quite as much an _opposed_ determination of magnitude as minus is. Plus in fact does not hold this power, for here it has to be taken against minus according to its qualitative determination, seeing that the factors stand to each other in a qualitative relation. To that extent, then, the negative is here what is opposed in itself as such, whereas the positive is what is indeterminate, indifferent as such; it is indeed the negative too, but the negative of the other, not upon its own self. – A determination as negation thus enters solely through the negative, not through the positive.

So too, then, - a · - a = + a², and this for the reason that the negative a is not to be taken simply in the opposed way (which is how it would have to be taken if multiplied by - a) but is to be taken negatively. The negation of negation, however, is the positive.
