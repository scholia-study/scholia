## {{{ 12.215 }}} 2. Division

The universal must _particularize_ itself; to that extent the necessity of division lies in the universal. But since the definition already begins of itself with the particular, its necessity of passing over into division lies in the particular, which of itself points to another particular. Conversely, it is precisely therein that the particular separates itself off from the universal, inasmuch as the determinateness is held fast in the need of its difference from the determinateness other to it; the latter is thereby _presupposed_ for the division. The course is therefore indeed this, that the singular content of the definition ascends through particularity to the extreme of universality, but this universality must now be taken as the objective foundation, and starting out from it the division presents itself as the disjunction of the universal, of the universal as the first.

A transition has hereby set in, and, taking place as it does from universality to particularity, it is determined through the form belonging to the concept. The definition taken for itself is something singular; a multiplicity of definitions belongs to the multiplicity of subject matters. That progression out of the universal into the particular which pertains to the concept is what grounds and makes possible a _synthetic_ _science_, a _system_, and _systematic_ _cognition_.

What is required first of all here, as was shown, is that one begin with the subject matter under the form of a _universal_. If in actuality, be it that of nature or that of spirit, it is concrete singularity that is given as the first thing to subjective, natural cognition, then by contrast in that cognition which is a comprehending at least insofar as it has the form of the concept for its foundation, the _simple_, that which has been _separated out_ from the concrete, must be the first, for only under this form does the subject matter carry the form of the self-referring universal and of what is immediate in accordance with the concept. Against this course within the scientific sphere one might perhaps hold that, since intuiting is easier than cognizing, the intuitable, that is, concrete actuality, ought likewise to be made the beginning of science, and that this course is _more in conformity with nature_ than the one which begins from the subject matter in its abstraction and from there proceeds in the reverse direction to the subject matter's particularization and concrete singularization. – But inasmuch as what is to happen is _cognizing_, the comparison with _intuition_ has already been decided and given up; and the question can only be what is to be the first _within_ _cognition_ and how the sequence is to be constituted; what is demanded is no longer a path _in conformity with nature_ but one _in conformity with cognition_. – If the question is merely one of _ease_, then it is {{{ 12.216 }}} in any case self-evident that it is easier for cognition to grasp the abstract simple determination of thought than the concrete, which is a manifold interlinking of such determinations of thought and of their relations; and the concrete is to be apprehended in this way, and no longer as it stands within intuition. In and for itself the _universal_ is the concept's first moment, since it is the _simple_ one, whereas the particular comes only afterwards, since it is what is mediated; and conversely the _simple_ is the more universal, while the concrete, as what is differentiated within itself and thereby mediated, is what already has behind it a transition out of some first. – This remark concerns not only the order of the course within the determinate forms of definitions, divisions, and propositions, but also the order of cognition in general, and merely with respect to the difference of abstract and concrete as such. – Hence also, for instance, in _learning to read_ one reasonably begins not with the reading of whole words or even of syllables, but rather with the _elements_ out of which words and syllables are built, and with the signs for the _abstract_ sounds; in alphabetic writing the concrete word has already been analysed into its abstract sounds together with their signs, and just for that reason learning to read becomes a first occupation with abstract subject matters. In _geometry_ one is to begin not with some concrete spatial shape but with point and line, going on from there to plane figures, and among these not with polygons but with the triangle, and among curved lines with the circle. In _physics_ the individual natural properties or matters are to be freed from the manifold entanglements in which they find themselves within concrete actuality, and are to be exhibited along with their simple, necessary conditions; they too, like the figures of space, are something intuitable, but their intuition must be so prepared that they first come into view and are held fast as released from all modifications by circumstances external to their own determinateness. Magnetism, electricity, the varieties of gas, and so forth are subject matters of such a kind that the cognition of them attains determinateness only in this way, that they are taken out of the concrete states in which they show themselves within actuality, and are apprehended so. The experiment does indeed present them to intuition in a concrete case; but partly it must, in order to be scientific, take up only the conditions necessary for this, and partly it must multiply itself so as to exhibit the inseparable concreteness of these conditions as unessential, by letting them appear in one concrete shape and then again in another, so that nothing but their abstract form is left over for cognition. – To mention yet one further {{{ 12.217 }}} example, it could well seem to be in conformity with nature and ingenious to consider _colour_ first within the concrete appearing of the animal subjective sense, then outside the subject as a spectral, floating appearance, and finally as fixed upon objects in external actuality. For cognition, however, the universal and hence genuinely first form is the middle one among those just named, where colour, still without any entanglement in subjective and objective circumstances, hovers between subjectivity and objectivity as the familiar spectrum. The latter are at first merely disturbing when it is a matter of considering purely the nature of this subject matter, for they conduct themselves as efficient causes and thus leave it undecided whether the determinate alterations, transitions, and relations of colour have their ground in colour's own specific nature, or are rather to be attributed to the diseased specific constitution of those circumstances, to the healthy and diseased particular affections and effects of the subject's organs, or to the chemical, vegetable, and animal forces of the objects. – Examples both more numerous and of other kinds might be adduced out of the cognition of organic nature and of the spiritual world; in every domain it is the abstract that must constitute the beginning and the element within which and out of which the particularities and the rich configurations of the concrete spread themselves out.

It is with division, or with the particular, that the particular's difference from the universal properly comes on the scene, but this universal is itself already something determinate, and hence itself only one member of a division. There is therefore a higher universal for it; but for this a higher one once again, and so at first onwards into infinity. The cognition considered here has no immanent limit, since it proceeds from the given, and since the form of abstract universality is what is peculiar to its first term. Any subject matter whatever, then, that appears to have an elementary universality gets made into the subject matter of a determinate science, and it is an absolute beginning to this extent, that acquaintance of _representation_ with it is _presupposed_ and that it is taken, for itself, as needing no derivation. Definition takes it as an immediate.

The further advance from it is in the first instance _the_ _division_. For this advance nothing would be required but an immanent principle, that is, a beginning out of the universal and out of the concept; the cognition considered here, however, lacks any such principle, since it pursues merely the form-determination of the concept without the concept's reflection-into-itself, and hence draws the determinateness of content from the given. For the particular that steps forth in the division there is no ground of its own at hand, neither with respect to what is to make up the ground of division, nor with respect to the determinate relation in which the members of the disjunction are supposed to stand to {{{ 12.218 }}} one another. In this respect, then, the business of cognition can consist only, on the one hand, in ordering the particular that is come upon in the empirical material, and, on the other, in also finding universal determinations of that particular by way of comparison. These last then hold as grounds of division; of such grounds there may be many, and accordingly the divisions too are just as manifold. The relation of the members of a division to one another, that is, of the species, has only this universal determination, that they be determined over against one another _according_ _to_ _the_ _assumed_ _ground_ _of_ _division_; were their diversity to rest upon some other respect, they would not be coordinate with one another on the same line.

Since the principle of being-determined-through-oneself is wanting, the laws for this business of division can consist only in formal, empty rules that lead to nothing. – Thus we see it set up as a rule that a division ought to _exhaust_ the concept; in fact, however, it is every individual member of the division that must exhaust _the_ _concept_. What is properly meant, though, is the _determinateness_ of the concept, and this is what is to be exhausted; but with the empirical manifoldness of the species, a manifoldness devoid of determination within itself, nothing is contributed to the exhaustion of the concept by whether more or fewer of them are come upon; whether, for instance, a further dozen are turned up alongside the 67 species of parrot is a matter of indifference for the exhaustion of the genus. The demand for exhaustion can signify nothing but the tautological proposition that all species are to be listed _completely_. – As empirical acquaintance widens, it may now very well come about that species turn up which do not fit under the assumed determination of the genus, because the genus is frequently assumed on the strength of a dim representation of the total habitus rather than on the strength of the more or less individual mark that is expressly supposed to serve for its determination. – The genus would in such a case have to be altered, and it would have to be justified that some other number of species are to be viewed as species of One new genus; that is to say, the genus would determine itself out of whatever one puts together under some respect or other that one chooses to take as the unity, and this respect would itself thereby become the ground of division. Conversely, if one holds fast to the determinateness first assumed as being what is peculiar to the genus, then the material that one wanted to bring together into one with the earlier species would exclude itself. This carrying on without the concept, which at one time assumes some determinateness as the essential moment of the genus and accordingly places the particulars under it or shuts them out of it, and at another time starts from the particular and, in putting the particulars together, lets itself be guided once more by a {{{ 12.219 }}} different determinateness, yields the appearance of a play of arbitrariness that is left to settle for itself which portion or which aspect of the concrete it wishes to hold fast to and to order things by. – Physical nature offers such a contingency in the principles of division of its own accord; in virtue of its dependent, external actuality it stands within the manifold interconnection that is likewise given for it; hence a multitude of principles is come upon to which it must accommodate itself, so that in one series of its forms it follows the one while in other series it follows others, and it just as much brings forth mixed hybrid beings that run off towards the different sides at once. It thereby comes about that in one series of natural things marks stand out as highly indicative and essential which in others turn inconspicuous and purposeless, so that adherence to a principle of division of this kind becomes impossible.

The universal _determinateness_ of empirical species can only be that they are, quite generally, _diverse_ from each other, without standing in opposition. Earlier, the _disjunction_ of the _concept_ was exhibited in its determinateness; if particularity is taken up without the negative unity of the concept, as something immediate and given, then difference remains merely at that form of reflection considered earlier, diversity in general. The externality in which the concept preeminently is within nature brings with it the complete indifference of difference; a determination for division is therefore frequently borrowed from _number_.

Contingent as the particular is here over against the universal, and hence division in general, it may still be ascribed to an _instinct_ of reason when, within this cognition, one lights upon grounds of division and divisions which, so far as sensible properties allow, prove more conformable to the concept. For instance, in the case of _animals_ the organs of feeding, teeth and claws, are used in the systems as a widely reaching ground of division; at first these are taken merely as sides on which the marks can be picked out more easily for cognition's subjective convenience. In fact, however, there lies in those organs not merely a distinguishing that falls to an external reflection; they are rather the vital point of animal individuality, at which the animal posits itself, over against the other of the nature external to it, as a singularity that refers itself to itself and severs itself from continuity with an other. – With the _plant_ the parts of fructification make up that supreme point of vegetable life through which the plant points ahead to the transition into sexual difference, and thereby into individual singularity. The system has therefore rightly turned to {{{ 12.220 }}} this point for a ground of division that is admittedly not all-sufficing yet reaches far, and has thereby laid down as basis a determinateness which is not merely a determinateness serving external reflection for comparison, but is the highest of which the plant is in and for itself capable.
