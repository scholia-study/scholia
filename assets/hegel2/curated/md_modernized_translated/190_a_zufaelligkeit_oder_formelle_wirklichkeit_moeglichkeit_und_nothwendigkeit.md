## A. Contingency, or Formal Actuality, Possibility and Necessity

1. Actuality counts as formal to the extent that, being actuality in its first shape, it is _immediate_, _unreflected_ actuality merely, and so stands within this determination of form alone, not as the totality of form. Nothing further, then, does it amount to than a _being_ or a _concrete existence_ at large. Yet since it is _essentially_ not bare immediate concrete existence but the unity of form joining being-in-itself, or inwardness, with externality, it harbours _being-in-itself_ or _possibility_ straightaway. _What_ _is_ _actual_ _is_ _possible_.

{{{ 11.382 }}} 2. Actuality reflected into itself: such is this possibility. But this _being-reflected_, a first on its own account, is likewise something formal, and hence amounts at large to no more than _the_ _determination_ _of_ _identity_ _with_ _itself_, or of being-in-itself taken generally. Because, however, what the determination here is, is _totality_ _of_ _form_, this being-in-itself gets determined as _sublated_, or as standing essentially only in reference to actuality: as the negative of actuality, _posited_ in the character of a negative. Two moments are thus harboured in possibility; _first_ the _positive_ one, that possibility is a being-reflected into its own self; but seeing that within the absolute form it has been lowered to a moment, being-reflected-into-itself no longer counts for _essence_, and so carries, _second_, the _negative_ import that possibility is a deficient thing which points away to an other, to actuality, and rounds itself out there.

Taken on the first, the merely positive side, possibility is the bare form-determination _of_ _identity_ _with_ _itself_, the form of essentiality. So construed, it is that relationless, undetermined vessel which holds anything whatever. – On the reading given by this formal possibility, _whatever_ _does_ _not_ _contradict_ _itself_ _is_ _possible_; and so the realm of possibility is a manifoldness without bound. Yet every manifold thing carries negation in it, being _determined_ _within_ _itself_ and _against_ _an other_; indifferent _diversity_ passes generally over into _opposition_, and opposition is nothing other than contradiction. Whence _everything_ is quite as much a contradicting thing and hence an _impossible_ one.

– To pronounce this merely formal thing of something – _it_ _is_ _possible_ – is accordingly as shallow and as empty as the principle of contradiction together with any content taken up into it; A is possible amounts to just this much, that A is A. Where one declines to go into the unfolding of the content, that content wears the form of _simplicity_; only by its being dissolved into its determinations does _difference_ come forth in it. Holding fast to that simple form, one leaves the content as an item identical with itself, hence as a _possible_. But by this, as by the formal proposition of identity, _nothing_ whatever gets said.

More than the merely identical proposition is contained, however, in the possible. The possible is _reflected_ _being-reflected-into-itself_; or the identical simply as _moment_ of the totality, determined thereby as also not _being_ _in_ _itself_; hence it carries the second determination of being a possible _merely_, together with the _ought_ of form's totality. Strip possibility of this ought and it is _essentiality_ as such; the absolute form, though, carries with it that essence is itself only a moment and has its truth not without being. Possibility is this bare essentiality, so _posited_ that it is a moment merely and falls short of the absolute form. Being-in-itself is what it is, yet determined as no more than a _posited_; or equally {{{ 11.383 }}} determined _not_ _to_ _be_ _in_ _itself_. – In its own self, then, possibility is likewise the contradiction; it is _the_ _impossibility_.

The nearest expression of this is that possibility, being a _form-determination_ _posited as sublated_, has some _content_ or other in it. As possible, this content is a being-in-itself which is at once a sublated one, an _otherness_. Since the content is thus a merely possible one, an _other_ content, its very contrary, is quite as _possible_. A is A; just so - A is - A. What each of the two propositions expresses is the possibility of its own content-determination. As these identical propositions, however, they stand indifferent to one another; the one does _not_ _posit_ that the other should join it. Possibility is the comparing reference of the pair; its determination, as a reflection of the totality, carries the point that the contrary too shall be possible. Hence it is the referring _ground_ whereby, _on that very account_, since A = A, - A = - A as well; the possible A harbours the possible Not-A also, and this reference is itself what determines the two as possible.

Being this reference, though – that within the one possible its other is likewise contained – it is the contradiction which sublates itself. And since by its determination it is the reflected, and, as has now shown itself, the self-sublating reflected, it is thereby the immediate too, and with that it turns into _actuality_.

3. Not the first actuality but the reflected one is this, _posited_ _as_ _the_ _unity_ of itself with possibility. Possible is what the actual as such is; it stands with possibility in immediate positive identity; possibility, however, has determined itself as _merely_ possibility; whence the actual too stands determined _as_ _merely_ _a_ _possible_. And immediately so, since possibility is harboured _immediately_ within actuality and is harboured there as a sublated one, as possibility _merely_. Turn it round: actuality, being in unity with possibility, amounts to immediacy sublated; – or, since formal actuality is nothing but an _immediate_ first, it is a moment merely, actuality sublated merely, that is to say, _possibility_ merely.

Herewith the determination is at once more closely expressed, of the respect in which _possibility_ _is_ _actuality_. For not yet is _all_ actuality meant by possibility; real and absolute actuality have not yet come up for discussion; – it is as yet that actuality alone which turned up first, the formal one namely, which determined itself to be possibility _merely_, and hence formal actuality, which is nothing but _being_ or _concrete existence_ at large. To everything possible there belongs, accordingly, some _being_ or some _concrete existence_.

_Contingency_ is this unity of possibility with actuality. – A contingent is an actual determined at once as no more than possible, one whose other {{{ 11.384 }}} or contrary obtains just as well. Such actuality is accordingly bare being or concrete existence, yet posited in its truth so as to carry the worth of a positedness or of possibility. Turn it round: possibility, being _reflection-into-itself_ or _being-in-itself_, stands posited as positedness; whatever is possible is an actual in this sense of actuality, carrying no more worth than contingent actuality carries; it is itself a contingent.

Two sides, then, are offered by the contingent; _first_, to the extent that possibility belongs to it _immediately_, or, which comes to the same, to the extent that possibility lies sublated within it, it is _not_ _positedness_ and not mediated either, but _immediate_ actuality; _no_ _ground_ belongs to it. – Since this immediate actuality accrues to the possible as well, the possible stands determined as contingent quite as much as the actual does, and is likewise a _groundless_ thing.

But _second_, the contingent is the actual taken as a _merely_ possible or as a _positedness_; and just so the possible, being formal being-in-itself, is positedness merely. Neither of the two, therefore, is in and for its own self; each has its genuine reflection-into-itself rather in an other, _or_ _it_ _has_ _a_ _ground_.

So the contingent lacks a ground for the reason that it is contingent; and quite as much does it possess a ground for the reason that it is contingent.

It is the _posited_, unmediated _turning over_ into one another of inner and outer, or of being-reflected-into-itself and of being; _posited_ in that possibility and actuality each carries this determination in its own self, and this because they are moments of the absolute form. – Thus in its _immediate_ unity with possibility actuality amounts to concrete existence alone, determined as a groundless thing which is _only_ _a_ _posited_ or _only_ possible; – whereas taken as reflected and determined _against_ possibility it stands severed from possibility, from being-reflected-into-itself, and so is quite as immediately a _mere_ possible too. – Just so possibility, being _simple_ being-in-itself, is an immediate, a mere being at large; or set _in opposition_ to actuality it is quite as much a being-in-itself void of actuality, a _mere_ possible, but precisely on that account once more a concrete existence at large that is not reflected into itself.

_Contingency_ is this _absolute_ _restlessness_ of the _becoming_ belonging to these two determinations. Yet just because each turns over without mediation into the one opposed to it, within that opposite it equally and absolutely _closes_ _with_ _itself_, and this _identity_ of the two, of each within the other, is _necessity_.

An _actual_ is what the necessary is; as immediate it is therefore a _groundless_ thing; quite as much, though, does it have its actuality _through_ _an_ _other_, that is, in its ground, while being at once that ground's positedness and the ground's {{{ 11.385 }}} own reflection into itself; whatever possibility the necessary has is a sublated one. So the contingent is necessary, both because the actual, determined as a possible, has thereby had its immediacy sublated and thrust off into _ground_ or _being-in-itself_ and into a _grounded_, and because this its _possibility_, the _ground-reference_, stands absolutely sublated and posited as being. The necessary _is_, and this being of it is _itself_ _the_ _necessary_. _In_ _itself_ it likewise is; this reflection-into-itself differs as an _other_ from that immediacy of being; and the necessity belonging to what is amounts to _something_ _other_. So what is, taken on its own, is not the necessary; yet this being-in-itself is for its part positedness merely, sublated and itself immediate. In this way actuality, within what is distinguished from it, within possibility, is identical with its own self. Being this identity, it is necessity.
