## b. The Problematic Judgment

The _problematic_ judgment is nothing but the assertoric one, insofar as the latter has to be taken positively just as much as negatively. – On this qualitative side the _particular_ judgment is a problematic one as well, for it holds positively just as much as negatively; – in like manner the being of subject and predicate is problematic in the _hypothetical_ judgment; – through these judgments too it is posited that the singular and the categorical judgment are as yet something merely subjective. Within the problematic judgment as such, though, this positing is more immanent than it is in the judgments mentioned above, since in the latter _the_ _content_ _of_ _the_ _predicate_ _is_ _the_ _reference_ _of_ _the_ _subject_ _to_ _the_ _concept_, and here therewith _the_ _determination_ _of_ _the_ _immediate_ _as_ _a_ _contingent_ _thing_ _is_ _itself_ _at_ _hand_.

At the outset it appears merely problematic whether the predicate is to be joined with some given subject or not, and to that extent the indeterminateness falls within the copula. Nothing determinate can issue from this for the _predicate_, for the predicate already is objective, concrete universality. What is problematic concerns, then, the immediacy of the _subject_, an immediacy thereby determined as _contingency_. – Yet the singularity of the subject is not on that account to be abstracted from; cleansed of singularity altogether, the subject would be a universal merely; the predicate holds precisely this, that the subject's concept is to be posited in reference to its singularity. – There is no saying: _the_ _house_ or a house is good; the answer runs rather: _according_ _to_ _how_ _it_ _is_ _constituted_. – What is problematic about the subject in its own self makes up its _contingency_ as a _moment_: the {{{ 12.87 }}} _subjectivity_ _of_ _the_ _fact_ set over against its objective nature or its concept, the bare _manner_ _and_ _mode_, or the _constitution_.

The _subject_ is thus itself differentiated into its universality or objective nature, its _ought_, and into the particular _constitution_ _of_ _existence_. Therewith it holds the _ground_ _of_ _whether_ _it_ _is_ _as_ _it_ _ought_ _to_ _be_. In this way it has been brought into balance with the predicate. – The _negativity_ of what is problematic, insofar as it is turned against the immediacy of the _subject_, accordingly signifies nothing but this original dividing of the subject, which _in_ _itself_ already is as unity of universal and particular, _into_ _these_ _its_ _moments_; – a dividing which is the judgment itself.

One may further remark that each of the subject's _two_ sides, its concept and its constitution, might be called that subject's _subjectivity_. The _concept_ is a fact's universal essence gone into itself, the fact's negative unity with itself; and this unity constitutes its subjectivity. But a fact is also essentially _contingent_ and carries an _external_ _constitution_; this too is called its bare subjectivity, over against the objectivity just named. The fact itself is precisely this, that its concept, as the negative unity of itself, negates its universality and puts itself forth into the externality of singularity. – It is as this doubled thing that the _subject_ of the judgment is posited here; those conflicting significations of subjectivity are, according to their truth, in one. – The signification of the subjective has itself turned problematic through this, that it _has_ _lost_ the immediate _determinateness_ which belonged to it in the immediate judgment, together with its determinate _opposition_ to the _predicate_. – That conflicting signification of the subjective, occurring as it does in the reasoning of ordinary reflection as well, might at least on its own draw notice to the point that the subjective has no truth _in_ _one_ of the two alone. The doubled signification is the appearance of this, that each on its own, taken singly, is one-sided.

Once what is problematic is posited in this way as the problematic of the _fact_, as the fact along with its _constitution_, then the judgment is itself no longer problematic but _apodictic_.
