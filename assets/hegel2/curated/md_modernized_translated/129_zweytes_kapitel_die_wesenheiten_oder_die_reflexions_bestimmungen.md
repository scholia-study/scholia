## {{{ 11.258 }}} Chapter 2. The Essentialities, or the Determinations of Reflection

Reflection is reflection that is determinate; essence is thereby determinate essence, or it is _essentiality_.

Reflection is essence's _shining_ _within_ _its_ _own_ _self_. Essence as infinite return into itself is no immediate simplicity but a negative one; it is a movement traversing distinguished moments, absolute mediation with itself. But it shines into these moments of its own; they are therefore themselves determinations reflected into themselves.

Essence is _first_ simple relation to itself; pure _identity_. This is its determination, according to which it is rather an absence of determination.

_Secondly_, the determination proper is _difference_; and indeed partly as external or indifferent difference, _diversity_ in general, partly, however, as opposed diversity or as _opposition_.

_Thirdly_, as _contradiction_ the opposition reflects itself into its own self and goes back into its _ground_.
