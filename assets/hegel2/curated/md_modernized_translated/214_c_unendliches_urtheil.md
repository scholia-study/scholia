## c. The Infinite Judgment

The negative judgment is no more a true judgment than the positive one is. But the infinite judgment, the one meant to be the negative judgment's truth, comes down on its negative side of expression to the _negative-infinite_; a judgment wherein the judgment-form itself has been sublated too. – Yet a thing of that sort is an _absurd_ _judgment_. A _judgment_ it is meant to be, hence to carry within it a reference of subject to predicate; and yet that very reference is _at_ _the_ _same_ _time_ meant _not_ to be there. – Ordinary logics do, to be sure, list the name of the infinite judgment, though what its point really is never comes clearly to light. – Instances of negative-infinite judgments cost little trouble: one has only to bind negatively, as subject and predicate, determinations of which the one lacks not merely the other's determinateness but its universal sphere besides; so, for instance, spirit [is] not red, yellow and so forth, not sour, {{{ 12.70 }}} not alkaline and so forth, the rose is no elephant, the understanding is no table, and the like. – Such judgments are _correct_, or _true_ as the word goes, and yet, truth of that sort notwithstanding, absurd and inane. – Or better, they are _no_ _judgments_. – The _evil_ _deed_ furnishes a more real instance of the infinite judgment. Where a _civil_ _lawsuit_ is in question, something gets negated purely as the property of the opposing party; yet with the admission that it would be theirs were the right to it on their side, and the claim is pressed under no other title than that of right; so right, the universal sphere, finds recognition and preservation within that negative judgment. _Crime_, by contrast, is the _infinite_ _judgment_: it negates not the _particular_ right alone but, along with it, the universal sphere, negating _right_ _as_ _right_. It carries _correctness_ with it insofar as an actual deed is what it is, but since this deed bears itself throughout negatively toward ethical life, which constitutes its universal sphere, it proves absurd.

What is _positive_ about the infinite judgment, about the negation of the negation, lies in the _reflection_ _of_ _singularity_ into its own self, through which singularity first gets posited as the _determinate_ _determinateness_. _The_ _singular_ _is_ _singular_, such ran the expression of it in the wake of that reflection. Within the judgment of existence the subject figures as an _immediate_ singular, hence at that stage rather as a mere _something_ in general. It first comes to be _posited_ as singular by way of the mediation carried out in the negative and the infinite judgment.

With this the singular stands _posited_ as _continuing_ itself _into_ _its_ _predicate_, a predicate identical with it; and correspondingly universality too is no longer the _immediate_ sort, but rather a _gathering_ _together_ of what is distinguished. Quite as much does the positive-infinite judgment run: the _universal_ is _universal_, and in that shape it is posited as the return into its own self.

By this reflection of the judgment's determinations into themselves the judgment has by now sublated itself; within the negative-infinite one the difference is, so to say, _too_ _great_ for anything like a judgment to survive; between subject and predicate there is no positive reference whatever; within the positive-infinite, conversely, nothing but identity is at hand, and with difference wholly wanting there is no judgment left.

Put more determinately, what has sublated itself is the _judgment_ _of_ _existence_; and with that there stands _posited_ what the _copula_ of the judgment holds within it, namely that in this their identity the qualitative extremes are sublated. Since this unity is, however, the concept, it is straightway sundered once more into its extremes, and exists as judgment, only that its determinations are no longer immediate ones but such as are reflected into themselves. With this the _judgment_ _of_ _existence_ has passed over into the _judgment_ _of_ _reflection_.
