## A. Existence as Such

Within existence

a. _as_ _such_, what falls to be distinguished first is its determinateness

b. as _quality_. Quality, though, has to be taken in the one determination of existence quite as much as in the other, namely as _reality_ and as _negation_. Within these determinatenesses, however, existence is quite as much reflected into its own self; and, posited so, it is

c. _Something_, an existent.
