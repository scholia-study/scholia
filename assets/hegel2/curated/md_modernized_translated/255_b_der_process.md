## B. The Process

1. Its start is made from the presupposition that objects standing in tension — tense as they are against themselves — are precisely thereby tense in the first instance towards one another; – a relation which one calls their _affinity_. Each, in virtue of its concept, contradicts the one-sidedness that its own concrete existence has and hence strives to sublate it, and posited immediately therein is a striving that the other's one-sidedness be sublated as well, and that by such reciprocal levelling and joining reality be posited conformably to the concept, which contains both moments.

Since each is posited as self-contradicting and self-sublating within its own self, what holds the two in separation from one another and from their reciprocal completion is nothing but _external_ _force_. _First_, then, the middle term by which these extremes now become conjoined is the nature of both that _is_ _in_ _itself_, the whole concept keeping the pair within it. _Second_, though, standing as they do over against one another in {{{ 12.150 }}} concrete existence, their absolute unity is besides an element that is still formal and _exists_ as something _distinguished_ from them; – the element of _communication_, in which the two come into outward _community_. The real difference falling to the extremes, this middle amounts merely to abstract neutrality, to the real possibility of those extremes; – it is, so to speak, the _theoretical_ _element_ in which chemical objects, their process and its result concretely exist; – in the corporeal realm the office of this medium is discharged by _water_; in the spiritual realm, so far as an analogue of such a relation is found there, one must look for it in the _sign_ generally, and more nearly in _language_.

Taken as bare communication in this element, the objects' relation is on one side a quiet convergence, on the other quite as much a _negative_ _comportment_, for the concrete concept that constitutes their nature, once posited into reality in the communication, brings the _real_ _differences_ among the objects down to its own unity. With that, the self-subsistent _determinateness_ they previously had is sublated in the union conformable to the concept — one and the same concept in both — and their opposition and tension are thereby blunted; whereby striving reaches, in this reciprocal completion, the quiet _neutrality_ that belongs to it.

So _extinguished_ is the process; the contradiction of concept and reality having been settled, the syllogism's extremes no longer hold their opposition and have thus stopped being extremes over against each other and over against the middle. A _neutral_ thing is the _product_ — one, that is, in which the ingredients, which can no longer bear the name of objects, retain neither their tension nor those properties that accrued to them while in tension, but in which the _capacity_ for their earlier self-subsistence and tension has been kept. For the neutral's negative unity sets out from a _presupposed_ difference; with the chemical object, _determinateness_ coincides with objectivity, being original to it. By the process we have examined that difference is so far sublated only _immediately_, so that determinateness is not yet absolutely reflected into itself, and the process yields no more than a formal unity as its product.

2. Granted, the opposition's tension and the negative unity as the process's activity are now extinguished in this product. Yet this unity, essential as it is to the concept and having itself come at the same time into concrete existence, remains at hand, though it has now stepped _outside_ the neutral object. Of itself the process does not kindle anew, seeing that difference was for it only a _presupposition_ and was not something it _posited_. – This negativity, self-subsistent outside the object — the concrete existence of _abstract_ singularity, a singularity whose being-for-itself finds its reality in the _indifferent_ _object_ — now stands in tension within itself against its own abstraction, an activity inwardly unquiet which turns consumingly {{{ 12.151 }}} outwards. _Immediately_ it refers to the object, in whose quiet neutrality lies the real possibility of the opposition it carries; and that object is henceforth the _middle_ of a neutrality formerly bare formality, now grown concrete and determinate in its own self.

More precisely, the immediate reference of the _extreme_ of _negative_ _unity_ to the object lies in the object's being determined by it and thereby sundered. One may at first take this sundering for the establishing of that opposition among objects in tension with which chemism made its start. This determination, however, is not what makes up the syllogism's other extreme; it belongs rather to the immediate reference borne by the differentiating principle towards the middle, wherein such a principle furnishes itself with immediate reality; it is the determinateness which in a disjunctive syllogism attaches to the middle over and above the middle's being the object's universal nature, and by which that object counts as objective universality no less than as determinate particularity. Over against the outer _self-subsistent_ _extreme_ of singularity stands the _other_ _extreme_ of the syllogism; hence it is the no less self-subsistent extreme of _universality_; and the sundering that the middle's real neutrality thereby undergoes in it consists in its being decomposed into moments that are not differentiated against each other but _indifferent_. So these moments are, on one side, the abstract and indifferent _base_, on the other the principle that _animates_ that base, a principle which by being severed from the base likewise acquires the form of indifferent objectivity.

Chemism's totality is this disjunctive syllogism, in which one and the same objective whole gets exhibited as the self-subsistent _negative_ unity, then, in the middle, as _real_ unity, – and finally as chemical reality broken up into its _abstract_ moments. In these last, determinateness has arrived at its _reflection-into-itself_ not in _an_ _other_, as happened in the neutral, but has withdrawn in itself into its abstraction, an _originally_ _determinate_ _element_.

3. Freed thereby from chemical tension are these elementary objects; in them the real process has _posited_ the original ground of the very _presupposition_ from which chemism set out. Insofar, further, as their inward _determinateness_ taken as such is on one side essentially the contradiction between their _simple_ _indifferent_ _subsistence_ and themselves qua _determinateness_, and is the outward drive that sunders itself and lays tension upon their object and upon an _other_ _in_ _order_ _to_ _have_ _one_ towards which it can bear itself as differentiated, in which it can neutralize itself and confer existent reality upon its simple determinateness — chemism has with that reverted to its beginning, where objects tensed {{{ 12.152 }}} against one another look for each other and then, through a formal external middle, combine into a neutral. On the other side, by such reversion into its _concept_ chemism sublates itself and has crossed over into a higher sphere.
