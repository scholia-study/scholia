## {{{ 21.146 }}} a. Existence and Being-for-itself

Being-for-itself, as has already been recalled, is the infinity that has collapsed together into simple being; it is _existence_ insofar as infinity's negative nature, which is negation of the negation, stands, in the henceforth posited form of being's _immediacy_, merely as negation in general, as simple qualitative determinateness. Being in such determinateness, wherein it is existence, is nevertheless at once distinguished also from being-for-itself itself, which is being-for-itself only insofar as its determinateness is that infinite one; existence, however, is likewise a moment of being-for-itself itself, for the latter does after all contain within it the being burdened with the negation. In this way the determinateness that on existence as such counts as an _other_ and as _being-for-other_ has been curved back into being-for-itself's infinite unity, and existence as a moment is present within being-for-itself as _being-for-one_.
