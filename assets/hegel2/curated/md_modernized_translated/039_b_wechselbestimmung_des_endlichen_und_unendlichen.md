## {{{ 21.126 }}} b. Reciprocal Determination of the Finite and the Infinite

The infinite _is_; such immediacy makes it at once the _negation_ of something _other_ than itself, namely the finite. As something that thus is, and that is simultaneously the _non-being_ of what is _other_, it has slipped back into the category of a something, of a determinate being generally – more precisely, because it stands _posited_ as existence reflected into itself, existence resulting through a sublating of determinateness generally, and hence as existence held apart from its own determinateness – slipped back, that is, into the category of a something bearing a limit. According to this determinateness the finite confronts the infinite as _real_ _existence_; so the two stand in a qualitative _reference_, as _remaining_ outside each other; the _immediate_ _being_ of the infinite awakens once more the _being_ of its negation, the being of the finite, which at first had seemed to vanish within the infinite.

Yet infinite and finite do not stand merely within these categories of reference; the two sides are determined further than merely to be _others_ over against one another. The finite, namely, is the restriction posited as restriction, is existence posited with the _determination_ of going over into its _being-in-itself_, of _becoming_ infinite. Infinity is the nothing of the finite, the finite's _being-in-itself_ and its _ought_, but this ought at the same time as reflected into itself, the ought carried through, wholly affirmative being that refers to nothing but itself. Present within infinity is the satisfaction that all determinateness, alteration, every restriction and with it the ought itself has vanished, stands sublated, and that what is posited is the nothing of the finite. Determined as this negation of the finite is being-in-itself, which, being negation of the negation, is thereby affirmative within itself. This affirmation, however, is a qualitatively _immediate_ reference to itself; _being_; and thereby the infinite finds itself referred back to the category in which an _other_, the finite, confronts it; its negative nature stands posited as one that _is_, hence as first and immediate negation. Saddled in this way with the opposition to the finite, the infinite leaves that finite to remain at once as determinate, real existence, even though within its being-in-itself, the infinite, the finite is at once posited as sublated; the infinite is what is not-finite; – being under the determinateness of negation. Over against the finite, over against the circle of realities, of determinatenesses that are, the infinite amounts to an indeterminate void, to the finite's beyond – the finite being that which does not have its being-in-itself in its existence, an existence that is determinate.

{{{ 21.127 }}} Thus posited against the finite in a qualitative reference of _others_ to one another, the infinite deserves the name _bad_ _infinite_, the _understanding's_ infinite, which takes it for the highest, for absolute truth; and what ought to bring the understanding to an awareness that, while fancying itself to have won satisfaction in the reconciliation of truth, it stands in unreconciled, unresolved, absolute contradiction, are the contradictions into which it lapses on every side the moment it embarks upon applying and explicating these its categories.

Immediately at hand in this contradiction is the fact that the finite remains over against the infinite as existence; there are thus _two_ determinatenesses; there _are_ two worlds, an infinite one and a finite one, and in their reference the infinite is only a _limit_ of the finite, and is thereby merely a determinate infinite, _itself_ _finite_.

This contradiction unfolds its content into forms more explicit. – Real existence is what the finite is, and such it stays even while the transition to its non-being, the infinite, is being made; – the infinite, as we saw, is determined over against the finite by nothing beyond the first, immediate negation, while the finite, faced with that negation, carries as something negated no more than the significance of an _other_, and so is still a something. Should the understanding, then, rise out of this finite world and climb to its highest, the infinite, this finite world stays standing for it as a this-side, so that the infinite is posited only _above_ the finite, is _severed_ from it, and precisely thereby the finite is severed from the infinite; – the two are _put_ in _different_ _places_, – the finite as existence here below, but the infinite, though it be the _in-itself_ of the finite, as a beyond off in the dim, unreachable distance, _outside_ of which the finite is to find itself and to stay.

Severed in this manner, they are just as essentially _referred_ to each other, and this precisely through the negation that parts them. This negation, which refers to each other these somethings reflected into themselves, is the mutual limit of the one against the other; and so indeed that each of them has that limit not merely _on_ _itself_ over against the other, but rather the negation is their _being-in-itself_, so that each has the limit on its own self for itself, in its severance from the other. But the limit is the first negation, and so both are limited, are finite on their own selves. Nonetheless each, as affirmatively referring itself to itself, is also the negation of its limit; hence it repels the limit immediately from itself as its non-being and, {{{ 21.128 }}} qualitatively parted from it, sets it down as an _other_ _being_ lying outside itself – the finite setting down its own non-being as this infinite, and the infinite doing likewise with the finite. That the passage from finite to infinite occurs of necessity – occurs, that is, by way of the finite's own determination – and that the finite gets raised into being-in-itself, is readily granted, seeing that the finite is determined as subsisting existence yet at the same time _also_ as null _in_ _itself_, and so as dissolving in accordance with its determination, whereas the infinite, though determined as saddled with negation and limit, is at the same time determined also as the one that _in itself_ is, so that this abstraction of self-referring affirmation makes up its determination, and so that finite existence, on this determination, does not lie within the infinite. Yet we have shown that the infinite results in affirmative being only _by way of_ negation, as negation of the negation, and that this affirmation of its own, taken as merely simple, qualitative being, brings the negation contained within it down to a simple immediate negation and thus to determinateness and limit, which is then likewise posited as contradicting its being-in-itself, as shut out from it, as not its own but rather as opposed to its being-in-itself: the finite. Since in this manner each, on its own self and out of its own determination, posits its other, the two prove _inseparable_. Their unity, however, lies _concealed_ in the qualitative otherness of them; it is the _inner_ unity, the one that _merely_ _underlies_ them.

Thereby the manner in which this unity appears is determined; posited in _existence_, the unity is a switching round or going over of the finite into the infinite, and conversely; so that the infinite only _comes forth_ on the finite, and the finite on the infinite, the one other on the other – which is to say, each has a coming-to-be of its own, an _immediate_ coming-to-be, on the other, and their reference is merely external.

The process of their going over has the following detailed shape. One goes out beyond the finite into the infinite. This going-beyond appears as an external doing. Within this void lying beyond the finite, what comes to be? What is the positive in it? By reason of the inseparability of the infinite and finite (or because this infinite standing on its own side is itself restricted), the limit comes to be; the infinite has vanished, its other, the finite, has entered. But the finite's entering thus looks like an occurrence external to the infinite, {{{ 21.129 }}} and the fresh limit looks like something that has not sprung from the infinite itself but is, again, simply found there. With that, the relapse into the earlier determination, sublated to no purpose, is at hand. This new limit, however, is itself only such as has to be sublated, or gone out beyond. So once again the void, the nothing, has come to be, in which that same determinateness, a new limit, is likewise met with, – _and_ _so_ _on_ _into_ _infinity_.

What is at hand is the _reciprocal_ _determination_ _of_ _the_ _finite_ _and_ _the_ _infinite_; finite is the finite only in reference to the ought or to the infinite, and infinite is the infinite only in reference to what is finite. Inseparable they are, and at the same time absolutely others over against each other; each has the other of itself on its own self; each is thus the unity of itself and of its other, and in its determinateness is an existence consisting in _not_ being what it itself is and what its other is.

It is this reciprocal determination, negating itself and its own negation, that steps forth as the _progress_ into _infinity_, which in so many shapes and applications counts as something _ultimate_, beyond which one goes no further, since, once arrived at that _And_ _so_ _on_ into infinity, thought is wont to have reached its end. – Wherever _relative_ determinations are driven as far as their opposition, so that they stand in inseparable unity and yet each is credited with a self-subsistent existence over against the other, there this progress sets in. This progress is accordingly the _contradiction_ that is not resolved but is only ever pronounced to be _at hand_.

Present here is an abstract going-beyond that stays incomplete, since _this_ _going-beyond_ is not itself _gone_ _beyond_. The infinite is present; it is indeed gone beyond, for a new limit is posited, but by that very act one has rather only come back to the finite. In itself this bad infinity is the same as the perennial _ought_; it is, to be sure, the negation of the finite, yet it is not capable of truly freeing itself from it; the finite steps forth again _on_ _the_ _infinite_ _itself_, as its other, because this infinite only is as standing _in_ _reference_ to the finite that is other to it. The progress into infinity therefore amounts to no more than a monotony that keeps repeating itself, the same tedious _alternation_ over and over between this finite and this infinite.

{{{ 21.130 }}} The infinity of the infinite progress stays saddled with the finite as such, is bounded by it, and is _finite_ itself. In fact, though, what would thereby be posited is precisely the unity of finite and infinite. Yet upon this unity no reflection is made. It alone, however, is what calls forth the infinite within the finite and the finite within the infinite; this unity is, so to speak, what drives the infinite progress along. The progress is the _outward_ side of that unity, the side at which representation comes to a halt, at that perennial repeating of one and the same alternation, at the empty restlessness of moving on past the limit to infinity – a moving-on that _finds_ a new limit within this infinite, and yet can hold to that limit as little as it can hold within the infinite. This infinite carries the fixed determination of a _beyond_ that cannot be reached, and that for the reason that it is not _supposed_ to be reached, because one does not let go of the determinateness of the beyond, of the negation that _is_. In accordance with this determination it has the finite over against it as a _this-side_, which can just as little raise itself into the infinite, and that for the reason that it carries this determination of an _other_, and hence of an _existence_ that is perennial, engendering itself once more in its beyond, and indeed as different from it.
