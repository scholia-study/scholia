## b. Law

Law brings out the more determinate difference between objectivity's _ideal_ _reality_ and its external reality. Being the _immediate_ totality of the concept, the object does not yet hold externality apart from the _concept_, which is not posited for itself. Having gone into itself by way of the process, it now displays the opposition of _simple_ _centrality_ to an _externality_ that stands determined _as_ externality, in other words that _is_ _posited_ as something not in and for itself. Because of its reference to externality, that identical or ideal side of individuality amounts to an _ought_; it is the self-determining unity of the concept, a unity determined in and for itself, and that external reality falls short of it, so that the unity reaches no further than _striving_. Yet, taken _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, individuality is the _concrete_ _principle_ belonging to _negative_ _unity_, and _as_ _such_ is _itself_ _totality_ — a unity which sunders into the _determinate_ _differences_ _of_ _the_ _concept_ while remaining in a universality equal to itself; the central point, accordingly, is one _widened_ {{{ 12.146 }}} _by_ _difference_ inside its own pure ideality. – Answering to the concept, this reality is the _ideal_ one and is thereby marked off from the merely striving reality; difference, which at first amounts to a plurality of objects, is here taken in its essentiality and gathered into pure universality. That real ideality is the _soul_ of the objective totality just developed, the system's _identity_ _determined_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_.

In its totality, therefore, objective _being_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_ comes out more determinately as the centre's negative unity, which parts itself into _subjective_ _individuality_ and _external_ _objectivity_, keeps the one within the other and determines it in ideal difference. Self-determining, and absolutely carrying external objectivity back into ideality, this unity is a principle of _self-movement_; and _the_ _determinateness_ of what so ensouls — a determinateness which is the concept's own difference — is _law_. – What dead mechanism amounted to was the mechanical process we examined, a process of objects that looked immediately self-subsistent yet on that very account lack self-subsistence in truth and carry their centre elsewhere than in themselves; such a process, since it passes over into _rest_, shows either _contingency_ together with indeterminate inequality, or else _formal_ _uniformity_. Uniformity of that sort does count as a _rule_, though not as a _law_. A _law_ belongs to free mechanism alone — the determination proper to pure individuality, or _of_ _the_ _concept_ _that_ _is_ _for_ _itself_; being difference within itself, law is an imperishable wellspring of motion that kindles from itself; and, referring in the ideality of its difference to nothing but itself, it is _free_ _necessity_.
