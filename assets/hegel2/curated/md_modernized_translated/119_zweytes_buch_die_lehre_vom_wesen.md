## {{{ 11.241 }}} Book Two. The Doctrine of Essence

The _truth_ of _being_ is _essence_.

Being is what is immediate. Because knowing wants to cognize the true, wants to know what being is _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, it refuses to halt at the immediate and at the immediate's determinations; it forces its way through, presupposing that _behind_ this being there lies something further than being itself, and that this background is what constitutes the truth of being. Such cognition is a knowledge that is mediated, since it does not stand straightway at essence and in it, but sets out from something else, from being, and has a preliminary road to travel — the road that leads out beyond being, or rather into it. Not until knowing _recollects_ itself out of immediate being, not until this mediation, does it come upon essence. – Language has kept essence stored in the past tense of the verb _Sein_: _gewesen_; for essence is being gone by, though gone by outside of time.

Represented as the pathway of knowing, this movement – the start made from being together with the advance that sublates being and reaches essence as a mediated term – looks like an activity of cognition external to being and of no concern to being's own nature.

But this course is being's own movement. Being showed at itself that by its nature it recollects itself, and that by this going-into-itself it becomes essence.

If the absolute stood at first determined as _being_, then, it now stands determined as _essence_. Cognition can find no resting place at manifold _existence_, nor yet at _being_, _at_ _pure_ _being_; there presses forward at once the reflection that this _pure_ _being_, the _negation_ of all that is finite, has for its presupposition a _recollection_, a movement that has purified immediate existence into pure being. On this footing being is determined as essence, as a being at which everything determinate and finite stands negated. It is thus the _determinationless_ simple unity from which the determinate has been carried off in an _external_ _fashion_; to this unity the determinate counted as an external, and even after being carried off it goes on facing {{{ 11.242 }}} the unity; for it was not sublated in itself but relatively, only with reference to this unity. – It has already been recalled above that when pure essence is determined as the _sum_ _total_ _of_ _all_ _realities_, those realities are subject no less to the nature of determinateness and to abstracting reflection, and the sum total shrinks to an empty simplicity. Essence on this footing is a product merely, a thing made. Abstraction, being the _external_ negation, does no more than lift the determinacies of being _off_ from whatever survives as essence; it shifts them, so to speak, to another spot, and lets them go on being what they were, before as after. On this footing, however, essence is neither _in_ _itself_ nor _for_ _itself_; it holds _through_ _an_ _other_, namely through the external reflection that abstracts; and it holds _for_ _an_ _other_, namely for abstraction and generally for whatever keeps its stand opposite it and has being. Within its determination it amounts, accordingly, to a lack of determination that is inwardly dead and empty.

Essence as it has come to be here, by contrast, is what it is not by virtue of a negativity alien to it but by virtue of its own, the infinite movement of being. Essence is _being-in-and-for-itself_; it is absolute _being-in-itself_ inasmuch as it stands indifferent to all determinateness of being, otherness and the reference to an other having been sublated outright. Yet it is not this being-in-itself alone; taken as bare being-in-itself it would amount to no more than the abstraction of pure essence; it is quite as essentially _being-for-itself_; essence is itself this negativity, the self-sublating of otherness and of determinateness.

As the consummated return of being into its own self, essence is thus to begin with the essence that is indeterminate; within it the determinacies of being stand sublated; it harbours them _in_ _itself_, though not in the shape in which they are posited _at_ _it_. Absolute essence, in this its simplicity with itself, possesses _no_ _existence_. Over into existence it must pass, however; for it is _being-in-and-for-itself_, which is to say that it _distinguishes_ the determinations it harbours _in_ _itself_; because it repels itself away from itself, or is indifferent towards itself, is _negative_ reference to itself, it thereby sets itself opposite its own self, and is infinite being-for-itself only in that it is unity with itself in this its difference from itself. – This determining, then, is of a different nature from the determining that went on in the sphere of being, and the determinations belonging to essence bear a different character from the determinacies of being. Essence is absolute unity of being-in-and-for-itself; its determining therefore never leaves this unity, and is no becoming and no passing over; nor, correspondingly, are the determinations themselves an _other_ in the sense of other, or references _to_ _an_ _other_; they are self-subsistent, and yet with that only as such as stand in their unity with one another. – Being at the outset _simple_ negativity, essence has now to posit within _its_ own sphere the determinateness it harbours only _in_ _itself_, so as to procure existence for itself and thereupon its being-for-itself.

{{{ 11.243 }}} Essence is _on_ _the_ _whole_ what _quantity_ was within the sphere of being; the absolute indifference towards limit. Quantity, though, is this indifference under _immediate_ determination, and limit at quantity is a determinateness immediately external; quantity _passes_ _over_ into quantum; for quantity the external limit is a necessity, and at quantity it is something that _is_. At essence, on the other hand, determinateness _is_ not; only through essence itself is it _posited_; not free, but only in _reference_ to the unity of essence. – What the negativity of essence amounts to is _reflection_, and the determinations amount to _reflected_ ones, posited by essence itself and abiding in it as sublated.

Essence has its stand between _being_ and _concept_, makes up the middle term of the two, and its movement makes up the _transition_ out of being into the concept. Essence is the _being-in-and-for-itself_, yet under the determination of being-in-itself; for what determines it universally is that it hails from being, or that it is the _first_ _negation_ _of_ _being_. Its movement lies in this, that it posits negation or determination at itself, thereby procuring _existence_ for itself, and, as infinite being-for-itself, comes to be that which in itself it already is. In this way it procures for itself an _existence_ that _matches_ its being-in-itself, and it becomes the _concept_. For the concept is the absolute in the way the absolute is, within its existence, absolute or in and for itself. The existence essence gives itself, however, is still not the existence that is in and for itself, but existence as essence _gives_ it to itself, or as it is _posited_, and so is still distinguished from the existence proper to the concept.

Essence _shines_ first _within_ _itself_, or is _reflection_; secondly it _appears_; thirdly it _reveals_ itself. Within its movement it posits itself in the determinations that follow,

I. as _simple_ essence, essence in itself, in the determinations it has within itself;

II. as stepping out into existence, that is, according to its concrete existence and _appearance_;

III. as essence in unity with its appearance, _as_ _actuality_.
