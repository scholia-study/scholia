## C. Transition into Essence

Absolute indifference is the last determination of _being_ before being becomes _essence_; yet indifference does not reach essence. It shows itself as still belonging to the sphere of _being_, in that, still determined as _indifferent_, it has the difference in it as an _external_, quantitative one. This is its _existence_, and therein indifference stands at once in the opposition of being determined, in contrast to that existence, as no more than what _is_ _in itself_, and of not being thought as the _being-for-itself_ absolute. Or else it is _external_ _reflection_ that halts at the thought that the specifics are, _in_ _themselves_ or in the absolute, {{{ 21.382 }}} _one_ _and_ _the_ _same_, that their difference is merely an indifferent one and no difference in itself. What is still lacking here consists in this, that such reflection should not be the _external_ reflection of a _thinking_, subjective consciousness, but rather the very determination of the differences of that unity to sublate themselves — a unity which then shows itself to be absolute negativity, its indifference _towards_ _its_ _own_ _self_, towards its own indifference, quite as much as towards otherness.

But this self-sublating of the determination of indifference has already emerged; in the development of its positedness indifference has shown itself on every side to be the contradiction. _In_ _itself_ it is the totality wherein all determinations of being are sublated and contained; so considered it is the foundation, but it is so far confined to the _one-sided_ _determination_ of _being-in-itself_, and hence the differences — quantitative difference and the inverse ratio of factors — attach to it as _external_. Being thus the contradiction of itself and of its being-determined, of its determination as it is in itself and of its posited determinateness, indifference is that negative totality in which the determinatenesses have sublated themselves in their own selves, and with them this their fundamental one-sidedness, their being-in-itself. Posited hereby as what indifference in fact is, it is simple and infinite negative reference to itself, the incompatibility of itself with itself, a repelling of itself from itself. The determining and the being determined is no passing over, no external alteration, no _coming forth_ of determinations in it, but indifference's own self-reference, and this self-reference is the negativity of indifference, of its being-in-itself.

But the determinations, as thus repelled, do not now belong to themselves, do not come forward into self-subsistence or externality; they are, on the contrary, _as_ moments; – belonging, first, to the unity that _is in itself_, not discharged from it but carried by it as their substrate and filled by nothing but it; and, second, as those determinations which, immanent to the unity that _is for itself_, are only through that unity's repelling of itself. Instead of being _ones that are_, as throughout the whole sphere of being, they now simply are _as_ _posited_ _ones_, simply with the determination and sense that they be _referred_ to their unity, each therefore to its own other and negation, – stamped with this relativity of theirs.

With that, being at large, and likewise the being or immediacy of the distinguished determinatenesses, has vanished quite as much as _being-in-itself_ has, and the unity is being, an _immediate_ _presupposed_ totality, in such wise that it is this {{{ 21.383 }}} _simple_ _reference_ _to_ _itself_ _only_ as _mediated_ through the _sublating_ _of_ _this_ _presupposition_, and that this being-presupposed, this immediate being, is for its part but a moment of the unity's repelling, while self-subsistence and identity with self, as originally given, only is as the _resulting_, _infinite_ _convergence_ _with_ _itself_; – being is thus determined into _essence_, being that, through the sublating of being, is simple being with itself.
