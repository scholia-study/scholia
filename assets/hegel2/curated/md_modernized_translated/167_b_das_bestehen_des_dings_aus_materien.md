## B. The Subsistence of the Thing in Matters

The passing over of the _property_ into a _matter_, or into a _stuff_ that is self-subsistent, is that familiar transition which chemistry performs upon sensuous matter, in that it seeks to exhibit the _properties_ of colour, of smell, of taste and so forth as _light-stuff_, _colouring-stuff_, _odour-stuff_, as sour or bitter _stuff_, and the like. Or else it simply assumes others outright, such as _heat-stuff_, electrical and magnetic matter, and is persuaded that it thereby handles the properties in their truth. – Quite as current is the expression that things _consist_ of diverse matters or stuffs. One takes care not to call these _matters_ or stuffs _things_; though one will readily grant that a pigment, say, is a thing; but consider, say, light-stuff, heat-stuff, or the electrical matter, and so forth. I do not know whether these are called things as well. Things and their constituents get distinguished without any precise statement whether the latter are things too and in [how] far, or are perhaps only half-things; but at any rate they are _something_ _concretely_ _existing_ in general.

The necessity of passing over from the properties to matters, or the necessity that the properties are in truth matters, has emerged from their being the essential and thereby the truly self-subsistent element of things. – Yet at the same time the property's reflection into itself makes up but one side of the reflection as a whole, namely the sublating of the difference, and the continuity, with its own self, of the property which was to be a concrete existence for another. Thinghood, as the negative reflection into itself and as the differentiating that repels itself away from another, is thereby brought down to an unessential moment; yet at the same time it has thereby determined itself further. This negative moment has, _in_ _the_ _first_ _place_, _preserved_ _itself_; for only insofar as the difference of things has {{{ 11.335 }}} _sublated_ itself has the property become continuous with itself and a self-subsistent matter; the property's continuity into otherness thus contains within itself the moment of the negative, and its self-subsistence, as this _negative_ _unity_, is at the same time the reinstated _something_ of thinghood; negative self-subsistence over against the positive self-subsistence of the stuff. _In_ _the_ _second_ _place_, the thing has hereby come out of its indeterminateness into complete determinateness. As _thing_ _in_ _itself_ it is the _abstract_ identity, the _simply_ _negative_ concrete existence, or that existence _determined_ as the _undetermined_; then it is determined by its properties, through which it is supposed to differentiate itself from others; but since through the property it is rather continuous with others, this incomplete difference sublates itself; the thing has thereby gone back into itself and is now determined _as_ _determined_; _in_ _itself_ it is _determined_, or is _this_ thing. –

But, _in_ _the_ _third_ _place_, this return into itself is indeed the determination referring itself to itself; yet it is at the same time unessential; the _subsistence_ continuous with itself constitutes the self-subsistent matter, wherein the difference of things, their determinateness that is in and for itself, is sublated and is an external one. The thing as _this_ thing is thus indeed complete determinateness, but this is determinateness within the element of unessentiality.

Viewed from the side of the property's movement, the matter comes out as follows. The property is not merely _external_ determination, but concrete existence that _is_ _in_ _itself_. Since it contains reflection-into-itself as well as reflection into another, this unity of externality and essentiality repels itself away from its own self, and is on the one side the determination as a _simple_ self-subsistent referring itself identically to itself, wherein the negative unity, that is, the one of the thing, is something sublated; – on the other side this determination as against an other, though likewise as a one reflected into itself and determined in itself; hence the _matters_, and _this_ _thing_. Such are the two moments of the self-identical externality, or of the property reflected into itself. – The property was that whereby things were supposed to differentiate themselves; in freeing itself from this negative side of its own, that of inhering in an other, it has thereby freed the thing too from its being-determined through other things, and has taken it back out of the reference to another into itself; yet at the same time the thing is only _the_ _thing-in-itself_ _that_ _has_ _become_ _other_ _to_ _itself_, since the manifold properties are for their part self-subsistent, and _their_ _negative_ _reference_ _in_ _the_ _one_ _of_ _the_ _thing_ has here accordingly become merely a sublated one; the self-identical negation is for that reason only _over_ _against_ the positive continuity of the stuff.

The _this_ makes up the thing's complete determinateness in such fashion that this determinateness is at the same time an external one. The thing consists of self-subsistent matters that are indifferent towards their reference within the thing. That reference is accordingly {{{ 11.336 }}} only an unessential linking of them, and the difference of one thing from others rests on whether several of the particular matters are to be found in it, and in what multitude. Out _beyond_ _this_ thing they reach, continuing themselves into others, and to belong to this thing is no restriction upon them. Just as little, further, are they a limitation for one another, since their negative reference is only the impotent this. Hence in being combined within it they do not sublate one another; as self-subsistents they are impenetrable for one another; in their determinateness they refer to themselves alone, and are a manifoldness of subsistence indifferent towards one another; of a quantitative limit alone are they capable. – The thing as _this_ thing is this merely quantitative reference of theirs, a mere collection, the _also_ of them. It _consists_ of some quantum or other of one stuff, _also_ of that of another, _also_ of others; this connection of having no connection is what alone makes up the thing.
