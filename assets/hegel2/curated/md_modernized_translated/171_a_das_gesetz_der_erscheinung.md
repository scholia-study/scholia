## A. The Law of Appearance

1. Appearance is what concretely exists, mediated through its _negation_, which makes up its _subsistence_. This negation of it is indeed an _other_ self-subsistent, but this other is just as essentially something sublated. What concretely exists is therefore the _return_ of itself into its own self by way of its negation and by way of the negation of that negation of it; it thus possesses _essential_ _self-subsistence_, just as it is with equal immediacy simply _positedness_, having a _ground_ and an other for its subsistence. – At first, then, appearance is concrete existence along with its essentiality, positedness along with its ground; this ground, however, is the _negation_; and that other self-subsistent, the ground of the first one, is itself likewise nothing but a positedness. Or: what concretely exists is, as what appears, reflected into an other, and this other, which is its ground, is itself nothing but {{{ 11.343 }}} this, namely to be reflected into an other. Since it is a return into its own self, the _essential_ self-subsistence accruing to it is, given the negativity of the moments, a returning of nothing through nothing back by way of itself; the self-subsistence of what concretely exists is therefore only the _essential_ _shine_. The interconnection of what concretely exists and reciprocally grounds itself consists for that reason in this reciprocal negation, namely that what the one subsists in is not the other's subsistence but the other's positedness, and this reference of positedness is alone what makes up their subsistence. The ground is at hand as it is in its truth, namely as a first that is only something _presupposed_.

This now makes up the _negative_ side of appearance. But in this negative mediation there is immediately contained the _positive_ _identity_ of what concretely exists with itself. For it is not _positedness_ _over_ _against_ an _essential_ _ground_, or it is _not_ the _shine_ _on_ _something_ _self-subsistent_; it is rather _positedness_ referring itself to a _positedness_, or a _shine_ only _in_ _a_ _shine_. Within this negation of it, or within its other, itself something sublated, it _refers_ _to_ _its_ _own_ _self_; hence it is essentiality identical with itself, or positive essentiality. – This identical element is not the _immediacy_ that accrues to concrete existence as such and is only the unessential feature, that of having its subsistence in something else. What it is, rather, is the _essential_ _content_ of appearance, and this content has two sides: _first_, that of standing in the form of _positedness_ or of external immediacy; second, that of being positedness which is identical with itself. Taken according to the first side, it is as an existence, but as a contingent, unessential one which, in accordance with its immediacy, is subject to transition, to coming-to-be and ceasing-to-be. Taken according to the other side, it is the simple determination of content withdrawn from that flux, the _abiding_ element in it.

Besides being in general the _simple_ element of what is transitory, this content is also a content that is _more_ _determinate_ and _diverse_ _within_ _itself_. It is the reflection of appearance, of negative existence, into itself, and hence contains _determinateness_ essentially. Appearance, however, is the manifold diversity _that_ _is_, tossing itself about in unessential manifoldness; its reflected content, by contrast, is its manifoldness reduced to _simple_ _difference_. For the determinate essential content is, more precisely, not merely determinate in general but, as what is essential to appearance, the complete determinateness: _one_ and its _other_. Within appearance each of the two has the other for its subsistence, in such a way that at the same time it is only in that other's _non-subsistence_. The contradiction sublates itself; and the reflection of it into itself is the {{{ 11.344 }}} _identity_ of their subsistence on both sides, namely that _the_ _one's_ _positedness_ _is_ _also_ _the_ _other's_ _positedness_. They make up _one_ subsistence, and are at the same time _diverse_ content indifferent towards each other. In the essential side of appearance the _negative_ of the unessential content, namely its sublating of itself, has thus gone back into identity; that content is an indifferent _subsistence_, one that is not the sublatedness but is rather _the_ _other's_ _subsistence_.

This unity is the _law_ of _appearance_.

2. Accordingly the law is the _positive_ element in the mediation of what appears. To begin with, appearance is concrete existence as the _negative_ mediation with itself, so that what concretely exists is mediated with itself by its _own_ _non-subsistence_, by an other, and once again by the _non-subsistence_ _of_ _this_ _other_. Contained in this is, _first_, the mere shining and the vanishing of both, unessential appearance; _second_, also the _abiding_ element, or the _law_; for in that sublating of the other _each_ of the two _concretely_ _exists_; and their positedness, taken as their negativity, is at once the _identical_, _positive_ positedness of the two.

The abiding subsistence that appearance possesses in the law is thus, in the shape it has determined itself into, _first_ set over against the _immediacy_ of being that belongs to concrete existence. This immediacy is indeed _in_ _itself_ the reflected one, that is, the ground returned into itself; but within appearance the simple immediacy now stands distinguished from the reflected immediacy, whereas in the thing the two had only begun to part. The concretely existing thing, in its dissolution, has become this opposition; what is _positive_ about its dissolution is that identity of what appears, as positedness, with itself in its other positedness. – _Second_, this reflected immediacy is itself determined as _positedness_ in contrast with the immediacy of concrete existence that is. This positedness is now what is essential and truly positive. The German expression _Gesetz_ likewise contains this determination. In this positedness there lies the essential _reference_ between the two sides of the difference which the law contains; the two are mutually diverse immediate content, and are so as the reflection of the vanishing content that belongs to appearance. Taken as essential diversity, the diverse ones are simple determinations of content that refer to themselves. But just as much, none of them is immediate for itself; each is rather essentially _positedness_, or _is_ _only_ _insofar_ _as_ _the_ _other_ _is_.

_Third_, one and the same content belongs to appearance and to the law. The law is appearance's _reflection_ into identity with itself; thus appearance, as the null _immediate_, stands over against what is _reflected-into-itself_, and according to this form the two are distinguished. But the reflection of {{{ 11.345 }}} appearance through which this difference obtains is at the same time the essential identity of appearance itself with its reflection – which is what reflection's nature amounts to in general; it is what is identical with itself in positedness and indifferent towards that difference which is the form or the positedness; hence a content that _continues_ itself out of appearance into the law, the content of the law and of appearance.

This content thereby makes up the _foundation_ of appearance; the law is this foundation itself, appearance is the same content but contains something more besides, namely the unessential content of its immediate being. For the form-determination too, through which appearance as such is distinguished from the law, is a _content_, and likewise one distinguished from the law's content. For concrete existence, taken as immediacy in general, is likewise an identity with itself of matter and form, indifferent towards its form-determinations and hence content; it is thinghood together with its properties and matters. But this content is one whose self-subsistent immediacy at the same time only is as a non-subsistence. The identity of that content with itself in this its non-subsistence, however, is the other, the essential content. This identity, the foundation of appearance that makes up the law, is a moment belonging to appearance itself; it is essentiality's positive side, through which concrete existence is appearance.

The law is therefore not beyond appearance but immediately _present_ in it; the realm of laws is the _tranquil_ image of the world that concretely exists or appears. Rather, both are one totality, and the world that concretely exists is itself the realm of laws, which as the simple identical is at the same time identical with itself within positedness, that is, within the self-dissolving self-subsistence of concrete existence. Concrete existence goes back into the law as into its ground; appearance contains the two together, the simple ground and that dissolving movement of the universe which appears, whose essentiality the ground is.

3. The law is therefore _essential_ appearance; it is the reflection of appearance into itself within its positedness, the content that is _identical_ in it and in unessential concrete existence. _First_, the identity of the law with its own concrete existence is as yet only the _immediate_, simple identity, and the law is indifferent towards its concrete existence; appearance still has another content over against the content of the law. The former content is indeed the unessential one and is what goes back into the latter; but for the law it is a first that is not posited by the law; as content, therefore, it is _externally_ _connected_ with the law. Appearance is a multitude of more precise determinations which belong to the _this_ or to what is concrete, and which are not contained in the law but are determined {{{ 11.346 }}} through an other. – _Second_, whatever appearance contains that is different from the law determined itself as a positive, or as another _content_; but essentially it is a negative; it is the form and the form's movement as such, which is what belongs to appearance. The realm of laws is appearance's _tranquil_ content; appearance is that same content, but exhibiting itself in restless flux and as the reflection into an other. It is the law as the negative concrete existence that alters itself absolutely, the _movement_ of passing over into what is opposed, of sublating itself and going back into unity. The law does not contain this side of the restless form, or of negativity; appearance is therefore the totality as against the law, for it contains the law but something more besides, namely the moment of the self-moving form. – This defect is present in the law, _third_, in this way, that the law's content is as yet only a _diverse_ content and hence one indifferent towards itself; the identity of its sides with one another is therefore as yet only an _immediate_ and hence _inner_ identity, or one that is not yet necessary. In the law two determinations of content are essentially connected (for example, in the law of the motion of a falling body, the magnitude of space and the magnitude of time; the spaces traversed stand in the ratio of the squares of the elapsed times); they _are_ connected; this reference is as yet only an immediate one. It is therefore likewise as yet only a _posited_ reference, just as in appearance the immediate has in general acquired the meaning of positedness. What would constitute the essential unity of the law's two sides is their negativity, namely that the one should contain its other in its own self; but this essential unity has not yet emerged in the law. (– Thus the concept of the space traversed in falling does not contain within it that time corresponds to it as a square. Because falling is a sensible motion, it is the reference of time and space; but, first, it is not to be found in the determination of time itself – that is, in the way time is taken according to its representation – that time refers to space, and conversely; one says that one can perfectly well represent time without space and space without time; the one, then, is added externally to the other, and this external reference is motion. Second, the more precise determination is a matter of indifference, according to which magnitudes space and time stand related to each other in motion. The law about this is cognized from experience; to that extent it _is_ only _immediate_; it still requires a _proof_, that is, a mediation, for cognition, so that the law does not merely _take_ _place_ but is _necessary_; this proof and its objective necessity the law as such does not contain. –) The law, therefore, is only the _positive_ essentiality of appearance and not its negative essentiality, in accordance with which the determinations of content are moments of the form, pass over as such into their {{{ 11.347 }}} other, and in their own selves are just as much not themselves but their other. In the law, then, the positedness of the one side of it is indeed the positedness of the other; but their content is indifferent towards this reference, it does not contain this positedness in its own self. The law is therefore certainly the essential form, but not yet the real form reflected into its sides as content.
