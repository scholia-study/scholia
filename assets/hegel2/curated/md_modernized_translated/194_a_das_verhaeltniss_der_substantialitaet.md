## {{{ 11.394 }}} A. The Relation of Substantiality

Absolute necessity is absolute relation, since it is not _being_ as such but that _being_ which is _because_ it is, being taken as the absolute mediation of itself with its own self. This being is _substance_; the ultimate unity of essence with being, substance is what is being in _all_ being; neither the unreflected immediate, nor yet an abstraction standing behind concrete existence and appearance, but immediate actuality itself, and that actuality as absolute reflectedness into itself, as _subsistence_ that is in and for itself. – Qua this unity of being with reflection, substance is essentially its own _shining_ and its own _positedness_. The shining is a shining that _refers_ _to_ _itself_, and in this way it _is_; substance as such is precisely this being. Conversely, taken as only the _positedness_ that is self-identical, this being is _shining_ _totality_, _accidentality_.

Such shining is the identity that belongs to form; – the unity of possibility and actuality. First of all it is _becoming_ — contingency, that is, as the sphere within which coming-to-be and ceasing-to-be occur; for on the determination of _immediacy_ the reference between possibility and actuality is an _immediate_ _turning_ _over_ of the two, as terms that _are_, into one another, of each into what to it is _merely_ _other_. – Yet since being is shine, their reference, as a reference of terms identical or shining in each other, is reflection as well. At each of its moments, therefore, the movement of accidentality displays how the _categories_ of being and the _determinations of reflection_ of essence shine within one another. – The immediate _something_ has a _content_; its immediacy is at once reflected indifference as against form. Determinate this content is, and inasmuch as such determinateness is a determinateness of being, the something _passes_ _over_ into an other. Quality, though, is a determinateness of reflection too; taken that way it is indifferent _diversity_. Diversity, however, quickens itself into _opposition_ and goes back into ground, which is the _nothing_ but is also _reflection-into-self_. Ground sublates itself; being itself, however, reflected being-in-itself, it is possibility, and in its transition — a transition that is quite as much reflection-into-self — this being-in-itself is the _necessary_ _actual_.

This movement of accidentality is substance's _actuosity_, _as_ _the_ _quiet_ _emerging_ _of_ _itself_. Active it is not _against_ something, but only against itself, as against a simple element offering no resistance. Sublating what is _presupposed_ is the vanishing shine; it is only in the doing which _sublates_ the immediate that this immediate first arises, or that the shining in question is; the {{{ 11.395 }}} beginning made from one's own self is above all a positing of that very self from which the beginning proceeds.

As this identity of the shining, substance is the totality of the whole; it grasps accidentality within itself, and accidentality is the whole of substance itself. That substance is differentiated into the _simple_ _identity_ _of_ _being_ and into the _alternation_ _of_ _the_ _accidents_ upon it is one form of its shine. _The former_ is the formless _substance_ _of_ _representation_ — representation for which shine has not been determined as shine, and which clings instead to such indeterminate identity as though to something absolute — an identity possessing no truth, being merely the determinateness of _immediate_ actuality or equally of _being-in-itself_ or of possibility; – form-determinations, these, and they fall within accidentality. –

The other determination, _the_ _alternation_ _of_ _the_ _accidents_, is accidentality's absolute _unity of form_, substance as the _absolute_ _power_. – An accident's ceasing-to-be is its going back, as actuality, into itself as into its being-in-itself or its possibility; but this being-in-itself of the accident is itself no more than a positedness, and hence it is actuality too, and since these determinations of form are just as much determinations of content, what is thus possible is, in point of content as well, an actual determined otherwise. By way of the actuality with its content, into which it translates what is possible, substance manifests itself as _creating_ power; by way of the possibility into which it carries back what is actual, as _destroying_ power. The two, however, are identical: creating is destructive and destroying creative, for within substantial necessity the negative and the positive, possibility and actuality, are absolutely conjoined.

The _accidents_ as such – and there are _several_ of them, multiplicity being one among the determinations of being – hold _no_ _power_ over one another. They are the something that is, or that is for itself, concretely existing things with manifold properties, or wholes consisting of parts, self-subsistent parts, forces standing in need of solicitation by one another and having one another for their condition. So far as an accidental thing of that sort seems to exercise a power over another, what is at work is the power of substance, which grasps both within itself and, being negativity, posits an unequal worth, determining the one as ceasing to be and the other, with another content, as coming to be, or determining the former as passing over into its possibility and the latter as thereby passing into actuality; – substance that eternally cleaves itself asunder into these differences of form and of content, eternally cleanses itself of such one-sidedness, and yet within that very cleansing has fallen back into determination and cleavage. – Hence one accident drives another out for no other reason than that its own _subsisting_ is this very totality of form and content, within which it goes under quite as much as its other does.

By reason of the substance's _immediate_ _identity_ with the {{{ 11.396 }}} accidents, and of its presence in them, there is as yet no _real_ difference at hand. Substance in this _first_ determination has not yet been manifested in the full measure of its concept. Should substance, as the self-identical _being-in-_ _and-for-itself_, be distinguished from itself as the totality _of_ _the_ _accidents_, then, as _power_, it is what _mediates_. Such mediating is _necessity_, the positive _persisting_ of the accidents within their negativity and their bare _positedness_ within their subsistence; and so this _middle_ _term_ is the unity of substantiality and accidentality itself, while its _extremes_ possess no subsistence of their own. Substantiality is on that account nothing but the relation as immediately vanishing; it does not refer itself to itself _as_ _something_ _negative_; being the immediate unity that power has with its own self, it stands in the _form_ of _its_ _identity_ merely, and not in that of its _negative_ _essence_; the one moment alone, namely the negative or the difference, is what vanishes without qualification, and not the other, the identical one. – This may be looked at in the following way too. Shine, or accidentality, is indeed _in_ _itself_ substance by virtue of the power, yet it is not _posited_ as this self-identical shine; so accidentality alone, and not its own self, serves substance for shape or positedness; it is not yet substance _as_ substance. To begin with, then, the relation of substantiality consists in nothing more than this, that substance _reveals_ itself as _formal_ _power_ whose differences are not substantial; in fact it is only as the _inner_ of the accidents, and these are only _upon_ _the_ _substance_. Or else: this relation is only the shining totality as _becoming_; but it is quite as much reflection; accidentality, which _in_ _itself_ is substance, is for that very reason _posited_ as such as well; so it is _determined_ as _negativity_ referring itself to itself, and, over against itself, _determined_ as simple _identity_ with itself referring itself to itself; and is _substance_ that is _for-itself_ and _powerful_. Thus does the relation of substantiality _pass_ _over_ into the _relation of causality_.
