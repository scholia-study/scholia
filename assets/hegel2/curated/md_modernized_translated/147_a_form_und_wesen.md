## a. Form and Essence

The determination of reflection, insofar as it goes back into ground, is a first, an immediate existence in general with which the beginning is made. But existence carries as yet only the meaning of positedness and _presupposes_ a ground essentially — in the sense that it rather does not _posit_ that ground; that this positing is a sublating of its own self, the immediate being rather the posited and the ground the non-posited. As has come out, such presupposing is the positing that strikes back at what posits; being determinateness sublated, ground is not the indeterminate but the essence determined through its own self, though _determinate_ as _indeterminate_ or as positedness sublated. It is _the_ _essence_ _which_ _in_ _its_ _negativity_ _is_ _identical_ _with_ _itself_.

With this, the _determinateness_ of essence as ground becomes a doubled one, that of _ground_ and that of _the_ _grounded_. _First_ it is essence as ground, _determined_ to be essence against positedness, as _non-positedness_. _Second_ it is the grounded, the immediate that is nevertheless not in and for itself, positedness as positedness. The grounded, accordingly, is likewise self-identical, though what it has is the negative's identity with itself. Negative and positive, each identical with itself, are _now_ _one_ _and_ _the_ _same_ _identity_. For as ground, the positive — indeed positedness itself — is identical with itself; the grounded is positedness as positedness, but its reflection into itself is precisely the identity of ground. – So this simple identity is not the ground itself, since ground is essence _posited_ as the non-posited _over_ _against_ _positedness_. As the unity of the determinate identity just described (of ground) with the negative identity (of the grounded), it is _essence_ _in_ _general_, marked off from its _mediation_.

Measured against the earlier reflections from which it descends, this mediation is _in_ _the_ _first_ _place_ not pure reflection, which stands undistinguished from essence and does not yet bear on it the negative and with it the determinations' self-subsistence. In ground, though, as reflection sublated, those determinations do have a subsistence. – Nor again is it determining reflection, whose determinations enjoy essential self-subsistence; for that reflection {{{ 11.295 }}} has foundered in ground, and in ground's unity they are posited merely. – Ground's mediation is consequently the unity of the two reflections, the pure and the determining; what it determines, the posited, has subsistence, and inversely that subsistence of theirs is a posited. And because this subsistence is itself a posited, or carries determinateness, the determinations stand distinguished from their simple identity and constitute the _form_ _over_ _against_ essence.

Essence _has_ a form, together with determinations of that form. A fixed immediacy accrues to it, or it is _substrate_, only as ground. Essence as such is at one with its reflection and, undistinguished from it, is that very movement. Not essence, then, is what traverses the movement; nor is essence that from which the movement takes its start as from a first. That circumstance is what makes the exposition of reflection difficult all through; for strictly one cannot say that _essence_ withdraws into its own self, that _essence_ shines within, seeing that essence is neither _before_ its movement nor _in_ it, and the movement has no groundwork over which to run. Something referred emerges only in ground, once the moment of sublated reflection has come. Essence as the referred substrate, however, is essence determinate; on account of this positedness form belongs to it essentially. – The form-determinations, on the other hand, are now determinations _as_ _on_ _essence_; _essence_ _lies_ _at_ _their_ _ground_, the indeterminate that within its determination remains indifferent towards them; in essence they possess their reflection into themselves. Subsistence in their own selves and self-subsistence were what the determinations of reflection were meant to have; but their self-subsistence is their own dissolution, and so it is in an other that they have it; and yet that dissolution is itself just this identity with self, the ground of the subsistence they confer on themselves.

To form belongs everything _determinate_ whatever; a thing counts as form-determination insofar as it is a posited and hence stands distinguished from _that_ _whose_ form it is; as _quality_, determinateness is at one with its substrate, being; being is what is immediately determinate, still undistinguished from its determinateness — or not yet reflected within it into itself, just as that determinateness is on this account one that is, not yet a posited one. – The form-determinations of essence, moreover, being the determinatenesses of reflection, are in their closer determinateness those moments of reflection which were considered above. _Identity_, and _difference_, this last partly as diversity and partly as _opposition_. Yet the _ground-reference_ belongs among them too, insofar as it is the sublated determination of reflection and thereby at once essence in the shape of a posited. Excluded from form, by contrast, is the identity ground possesses within itself, namely that positedness as sublated together with positedness as such — ground together with the grounded — form a single reflection, and this reflection constitutes _essence_ as that _simple_ _groundwork_ in which form has its _subsistence_. Yet in ground such subsistence is _posited_; or this essence is itself essentially determinate; so {{{ 11.296 }}} it is once again moment of the ground-reference and of form. – Just this is the absolute reciprocal reference between form and essence: that essence is simple unity of ground and grounded while therein being itself determinate or negative, marking itself off as groundwork from form, yet in that very act becoming itself ground and moment of form.

Form is thus reflection's completed whole; it carries in it reflection's determination of being sublated as well; therefore, in the same measure as it is a unity of its determining, it stands also _referred_ to its own sublatedness, _to_ _an_ _other_ that is not form but that _on_ _which_ form is to be. Being the _essential_ negativity that refers to its own self, form is, against this simple negative, the _positing_ and the _determining_ term; whereas simple essence is the indeterminate and _inactive_ groundwork on which the form-determinations find subsistence, or reflection into themselves. – At this distinguishing of essence from form external reflection is wont to come to rest; the distinguishing is necessary, but of itself it is their unity, as this ground-unity is the essence that thrusts itself away from itself and makes itself into positedness. Form is absolute negativity as such, the negative absolute identity with self, by which alone essence is essence rather than being. Taken in the abstract, that identity is essence against form, just as negativity taken in the abstract, as positedness, is the individual form-determination. Determination as it has exhibited itself, however, is in truth the total negativity that refers to its own self, which, being this identity, is thus the simple essence in its own self. In its own identity, accordingly, form has essence, as essence in its negative nature has absolute form. There can be no asking, then, _how_ _form_ _comes_ _to_ _be_ _added_ _to_ _essence_, for form is only essence's shining within its own self, the reflection that dwells in essence as its own. Form no less in its own self is reflection returning into itself, or identical essence; in determining, form turns determination into positedness as positedness. – It does not, therefore, determine essence as though it were genuinely presupposed and cut off from essence, since in that guise it is the unessential determination of reflection that founders restlessly; in that guise, rather, it is itself the ground of its own sublating, the identical reference among its determinations. To say that form determines essence therefore says that form, in distinguishing, sublates the distinguishing itself and is that identity with self which essence is as the subsistence of determination; form is the contradiction of being sublated in its positedness and of holding its subsistence in this sublatedness; hence it is ground, the essence that stays identical with itself in being determined, or negated.

{{{ 11.297 }}} Such differences of form and essence are consequently no more than _moments_ of the simple form-reference itself. Closer consideration and a firm hold on them are nevertheless in order. Determining form refers to its own self as positedness sublated, and so refers to its identity as to something other. It posits itself as sublated, and thereby _presupposes_ its identity; taken in this moment, essence is the indeterminate for which form counts as an other. Essence so taken is not the absolute reflection in its own self, but is _determined_ as the identity devoid of form; it is _material_.
