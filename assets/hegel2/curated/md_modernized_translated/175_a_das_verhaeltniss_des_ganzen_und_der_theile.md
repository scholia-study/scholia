## A. The Relation of Whole and Parts

[1.] What the essential relation contains is, _first_, the self-subsistence of concrete existence _reflected_ _into_ _itself_; so taken it is the _simple_ _form_, and its determinations, while they too are concrete existences, are at the same time posited ones, moments kept within the unity. Reflected thus into itself, that self-subsistence is at once reflection into what stands opposed to it, namely into _immediate_ self-subsistence; and the subsistence {{{ 11.355 }}} it has is essentially, no less than it is a self-subsistence of its own, just this identity with its opposite. – Precisely thereby there is also immediately posited, _second_, the further side: immediate self-subsistence, which, under the determination of the _other_, is a manifoldness of many kinds within itself, yet so that this manifoldness bears upon it essentially _also_ the reference belonging to the other side, the unity of reflected self-subsistence. The one side, the _whole_, is that self-subsistence which the world in and for itself constituted; _the_ _parts_, the other side, are the immediate concrete existence which the appearing world was. Within the relation of whole and parts both sides are self-subsistences of this kind, but so that each lets the other shine inside it, and each equally is only as this identity of the two. Since for now the essential relation is only the first, the immediate one, negative unity and positive self-subsistence are tied together by the _also_; both sides, posited though they are as _moments_, are _just_ _as_ _much_ concretely existing _self-subsistences_. – That the two are posited as moments is accordingly parcelled out thus: first the _whole_, self-subsistence as reflected, is the concretely existing one, and within it the other, the immediate one, has the standing of a moment; – here it is the _whole_ that constitutes the unity of the two sides, the _foundation_, and immediate concrete existence stands there _as_ _positedness_. – Reversed, on the further side, that of the _parts_, the immediate concrete existence, manifold within, is the self-subsistent foundation, whereas the reflected unity, the whole, is nothing but external reference.

2. Accordingly this relation harbours the self-subsistence of its sides and no less their sublatedness, and the two of them outright in a single reference. Self-subsistent is the whole, and the parts are merely moments of this unity; yet no less are the parts themselves the self-subsistent ones, with their reflected unity for a mere moment; and each of them, in its very _self-subsistence_, is outright the _relative_ of an other. Hence this relation is contradiction immediate in its own self, and it sublates itself.

Looked at more closely, the _whole_ is the reflected unity possessing self-subsistent subsistence for itself; but this subsistence of its own has no less been repelled from it; being the negative unity, the whole is negative reference to its own self; so it has divested itself; its _subsistence_ it has in what stands opposed to it, in the manifold immediacy, _the_ _parts_. _The_ _whole_ _therefore_ _consists_ _of_ _the_ _parts_; so much so that without them it is nothing. It is thus the entire relation and the self-subsistent totality; but upon that very same ground it is only a relative, since what constitutes it a totality is rather its _other_, the parts; and its subsistence lies not in it but in that other of it.

The parts, likewise, are the entire relation. Immediate {{{ 11.356 }}} self-subsistence is what they are, _over_ _against_ the reflected kind, and they subsist not in the whole but for themselves. They have this whole, further, upon them as their moment; it constitutes their reference; where there is no whole there are no parts. But since they are the self-subsistent ones, that reference is a merely external moment, one to which they remain in and for themselves indifferent. At the same time, however, the parts, being manifold concrete existence, collapse within themselves, for such existence is being devoid of reflection; self-subsistence accrues to them only within the reflected unity, which is at once this unity and the concretely existing manifoldness; that is, self-subsistence is theirs only _in_ _the_ _whole_, and this is at the same time the self-subsistence that is _other_ to the parts.

Whole and parts _condition_ one another reciprocally, then; but the relation under consideration here stands at the same time higher than the reference of _the_ _conditioned_ and _the_ _condition_ to one another as that was determined above. Here that reference is _realized_: it is _posited_, namely, that the condition is the essential self-subsistence of the conditioned precisely in being _presupposed_ by the latter. Taken as such, the condition is nothing but the _immediate_, and only _in_ _itself_ pre<i>supposed</i>. The whole, though certainly the condition of the parts, immediately contains at the same time this as well, that it too only is so far as it has the parts for presupposition. With both sides of the relation posited in this way as reciprocally conditioning, each is an immediate self-subsistence in its own self, but its self-subsistence is no less mediated, or posited, by the other. By virtue of this reciprocity the _whole_ _relation_ is the return of conditioning into its own self, the non-relative, the _unconditioned_.

Now since neither side of the relation has its self-subsistence in its own self but each in the other, only one identity of the two is at hand, in which both are moments merely; but since each is self-subsistent in its own self, they are two self-subsistent concrete existences that stand indifferent to one another.

Taken in the first respect, that of these sides' essential identity, _the_ _whole_ _is_ _equal_ _to_ _the_ _parts_ _and_ _the_ _parts_ _to_ _the_ _whole_. In the whole there is nothing absent from the parts, and in the parts nothing absent from the whole. No abstract unity is the whole; it is unity as that of a _diverse_ _manifoldness_; but this unity, as that wherein the _manifold_ refers to one another, is the _determinateness_ of the manifold whereby the latter is part. An inseparable identity, then, is what the relation has, and one self-subsistence only.

But further, the whole is equal to the parts, though not to _them_ qua parts; for the whole is unity as reflected, whereas the parts constitute the determinate moment, the _otherness_, of that unity, and they are the diverse {{{ 11.357 }}} manifold. Equal the whole is, not to them as this self-subsistent diversity, but to them _together_. This _together_ of theirs, however, is nothing else than their unity, the whole as such. Within the parts, therefore, the whole is equal to nothing but itself, and the equality of whole with parts voices only the tautology that _the_ _whole_ _as_ _whole_ _is_ _equal_ to the whole, and not to the parts.

The parts, inversely, are equal to the whole; but since otherness is the moment which they themselves are, their equality is not with it as the unity, but runs thus, that _one_ among its manifold determinations devolves upon the part, or that they are equal to _it_ qua something _manifold_; they are equal to it, that is, as a _divided_ _whole_, which means _as_ _the_ _parts_. So here too the very same tautology is at hand: the _parts_ _as_ _parts_ stand equal _not_ _to_ _the_ _whole_ as such, but within it to _themselves_, _to_ _the_ _parts_.

Whole and parts fall in this manner indifferently asunder; each of these sides refers to itself alone. Yet held asunder so, they wreck themselves. That whole which is indifferent towards the parts is the _abstract_ _identity_, undifferentiated within; and such identity is a whole only as _differentiated_ _within_ _its_ _own_ _self_, differentiated within, moreover, in such a manner that these manifold determinations stand reflected into themselves and possess immediate self-subsistence. And identity of reflection, through its own movement, has already displayed this _reflection_ _into_ _its_ _other_ as its truth. – Just so the parts, indifferent as they are towards the unity of the whole, are the unrelated manifold merely, _the_ _other_ _within_ _itself_, which as such is the other of its own self and only what sublates itself. – This reference-to-self of either side is its self-subsistence; but this self-subsistence, which each has _for_ _itself_, is rather the negation of its own self. Neither, therefore, has its self-subsistence in its own self, but in the other; that other — the one constituting the subsistence — counts as its presupposed immediate, the thing that _ought_ to be a first and its beginning; but this first belonging to each is itself only such a one as is not first, but has its beginning in the other.

The relation's truth lies accordingly _in_ _the_ _mediation_; its essence is that negative unity wherein reflected immediacy and the immediacy that is are alike sublated. Contradiction is what the relation is, contradiction going back into its ground, into the unity which, as returning, is the reflected unity; but since this unity has no less posited itself as sublated, it refers negatively to its own self, sublates itself, and turns itself into the immediacy that is. This negative reference of it, though, in that it counts as a first, an immediate one, has its mediation solely through its other, and is no less a posited. That other, the immediacy that is, equally is only as sublated; {{{ 11.358 }}} a first is its self-subsistence, but a first only in order to vanish, and the existence it has is posited and mediated.

So determined, the relation is that of _whole_ and _parts_ no longer; the immediacy possessed by its sides has gone over into positedness and mediation; each is posited, so far as it is immediate, as sublating itself and passing over into the other, and, so far as it is itself negative reference, as being at the same time conditioned by the other as by its positive; just as its immediate passing over is no less something mediated, a sublating, namely, posited by the other. – With that, the relation of whole and parts has gone over into the relation of _force_ _and_ _its_ _expression_.
