## c. The Universal Judgment

Universality, as it is met with at the subject of the universal judgment, is that external universality belonging to reflection, _allness_; _all_ are all the _singulars_; the singular remains unaltered within it. Such universality is therefore no more than a _gathering_ _together_ of singulars that subsist on their own; it is a _commonality_ accruing to them only upon _comparison_. – Where universality comes up for discussion, it is commonality of this kind that habitually presents itself first to subjective _representation_. When a ground is wanted, closest to hand, for why some determination should count as a universal one, what gets alleged is _that_ _it_ _belongs_ _to_ _several_. – In _analysis_ chiefly, too, this concept of universality is what hovers before one, seeing that, for instance, developing a function by a _polynomial_ passes for the _more_ _universal_ procedure over against developing the same function by a _binomial_; because the _polynomial_ exhibits _more_ _singularities_ than the _binomial_ does. Strictly, the requirement that the function be exhibited in its universality calls for a _pantonomial_, for infinity run through to the end; but at this point the restriction upon that requirement sets in of its own accord, and the _exhibition_ _of_ _the_ _infinite_ _multitude_ has to rest content with the _ought_ of such exhibition, and hence with a _polynomial_ after all. In point of fact, however, the _binomial_ already is the _pantonomial_ in every case where the _method_ or {{{ 12.75 }}} _rule_ concerns nothing beyond the dependence of one term upon one other, and where the dependence of the several terms upon those preceding them does not particularize itself, one and the same function staying at the base. It is the _method_ or _rule_ that has to be looked upon as the truly _universal_; carrying the development onward, or developing a polynomial, merely _repeats_ it; through the enlarged multiplicity of terms it accordingly gains nothing in universality. Bad infinity and the deception belonging to it have been discussed earlier already; the universality of the concept is the _beyond_ _attained_; that infinity, though, stays saddled with the beyond as with something unattainable, so far as it stays the mere _progress_ into infinity. Should nothing but _allness_ hover before one in the case of universality, a universality supposed to be exhausted in the singulars taken as singulars, then here is a relapse into that bad infinity; or else _plurality_ is simply passed off for _allness_. Plurality, be it ever so vast, stays sheer particularity all the same, and is not allness. – Dimly, however, there hovers along with all this the universality of the _concept_, which is in and for itself; it is the concept that drives forcibly out beyond the stubborn singularity to which representation clings, and out beyond the externality of representation's reflection, slipping _allness_ _as_ _totality_ in its place, or rather the categorical being-in-and-for-itself.

The same shows itself elsewhere in allness, which at large is the _empirical_ universality. So far as the singular is presupposed as something immediate, and hence _come_ _upon_ and _taken_ _up_ externally, the reflection that gathers it into allness is external to it in just the same measure. But since the singular as _this_ is utterly indifferent toward such reflection, universality and a singular of that sort cannot close into one unity. Empirical allness therefore _remains_ a _task_; an _ought_ which, so taken, admits of no exhibition as being. An empirically universal proposition, for such are put forward all the same, rests, then, upon the tacit agreement that, so long as no _instance_ to the _contrary_ can be adduced, the _multiplicity_ of cases is to hold as _allness_; or that the _subjective_ _allness_, namely that of the cases which have _come_ _to_ _knowledge_, may be taken for an _objective_ allness.

Looking now more closely at the _universal_ _judgment_ where we stand: the subject, which, as was noted just now, holds within it the universality that is in and for itself _as_ _presupposed_, now carries [that same universality] at itself as _posited_ too. _All_ _human_ _beings_ expresses _first_ the _genus_ human being, _second_ this genus in its singularization, though in such wise that the singulars are simultaneously widened out into the universality of the {{{ 12.76 }}} genus; conversely, through this linkage with singularity universality is determined every bit as perfectly as singularity is; hereby the _posited_ universality has become EQUAL to the _presupposed_ one.

Properly, however, no advance account is to be taken of what is _presupposed_; the outcome is rather to be considered on its own at the form-determination. – Singularity, in having widened itself out to allness, is _posited_, as negativity that is identical reference to itself. It has thereby not stayed that first singularity, such as, for instance, the singularity of a Cajus, but is the determination identical with universality, or the universal's absolute being-determined. – The _first_ singularity belonging to the singular judgment was no _immediate_ one such as the positive judgment has, but had arisen out of the dialectical movement of the judgment of existence taken as a whole; already it stood determined as the _negative_ _identity_ of that judgment's determinations. Here is the genuine presupposition within the judgment of reflection; over against the positing that runs its course in the latter, that _first_ determinateness of singularity was singularity's _in-itself_; what singularity accordingly is _in_ _itself_ now stands _posited_ through the movement of the judgment of reflection, namely singularity as the identical reference of what is determinate to its own self. Hence that _reflection_ which widens singularity out to allness is none that would be external to it; what singularity already is _in_ _itself_ merely comes thereby to be _for_ _itself_. – So the outcome is in truth _objective_ _universality_. To that extent the subject has stripped off the form-determination of the judgment of reflection, which travelled from _this_ by way of _some_ to _allness_; in place of _all_ _human_ _beings_ one has henceforth to say: _the_ _human_ _being_.

Universality thus arisen is _the_ _genus_; the universality that is concrete at its own self. The genus does not _inhere_ in the subject, nor is it a _single_ property, and at large not a property of the subject at all; every singularized determinateness it holds dissolved within its substantial solidity. – Precisely because it stands posited as this negative identity with itself, the genus is essentially subject; but it is no longer _subsumed_ under its predicate. Herewith the nature of the judgment of reflection alters altogether.

That judgment was essentially one of _subsumption_. Its predicate stood determined over against its subject as the universal that _is_ _in_ _itself_; on the side of content it admitted of being taken as an essential relational determination, or else as a mark; – a determination whereby the subject is nothing but an essential _appearance_. Determined as _objective_ _universality_, however, the subject ceases to be subsumed under any such relational determination, or gathering reflection; a predicate of that sort is rather, measured against this universality, a particular one. The relation of subject and predicate has thus turned itself about, and to that extent the judgment has for the time being sublated itself.

{{{ 12.77 }}} This sublation of the judgment coincides with what turns into the _determination_ _of_ _the_ _copula_, still awaiting our consideration; the sublating of the judgment's determinations and their transition into the copula are one and the same. – For so far as the subject has raised itself into universality, in this determination it has become equal to the predicate, which as the reflected universality comprises particularity too within itself; subject and predicate are accordingly identical, that is, they have run together into the copula. Such identity is the genus, or the nature of a thing as it is in and for itself. So far, then, as this identity sunders itself once more into a judgment, it is the _inner_ _nature_ by which subject and predicate refer to each other; – a reference of _necessity_, wherein those determinations of the judgment are unessential differences merely. – _What_ _belongs_ _to_ _all_ _the_ _singulars_ _of_ _a_ _genus_, _belongs_ _by_ _their_ _nature_, _to_ _the_ _genus_, – this is an immediate consequence, and the expression of what came out just now, that the subject, for instance _all_ _human_ _beings_, strips off its form-determination, and _the_ _human_ _being_ is to be said instead. – Such interconnection, which is in and for itself, makes up the groundwork of a new judgment; – _the_ _judgment_ _of_ _necessity_.
