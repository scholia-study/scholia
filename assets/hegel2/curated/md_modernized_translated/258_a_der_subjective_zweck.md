## A. The Subjective Purpose

What the _subjective_ concept recovered and posited first of all, in the _centrality_ belonging to the objective sphere, a centrality that is an indifference towards determinateness, was the _negative_ _point_ _of_ _unity_; in chemism, on the other hand, the objectivity of the _concept's_ _determinations_, through which the concept comes to be posited as _concrete_ _objective_ _concept_ at all. Its determinateness, its simple difference, carries at its own self by now the _determinateness_ _of_ _externality_, and its simple unity is on that account a unity that repels itself away from itself and maintains itself in that repelling. Purpose accordingly is the subjective concept taken as essential striving and as the drive to posit itself in an external way. Herein it is exempt from passing over. It is not a force that gives expression to itself, nor yet a substance or cause manifesting itself in accidents and effects. A force stays a merely abstract inner so long as it has given itself no expression; or rather it attains existence only within the expression, to which it must first be solicited; the same holds of cause and of substance; having actuality only in the accidents and in the effect, their activity is the transition, and over against that transition they do not maintain themselves in freedom. Purpose may certainly be determined as force and as cause as well, yet these expressions cover only one incomplete side of what it means; if they are to be uttered of it according to its truth, they can be so only in a way that sublates the concept proper to them; as a force soliciting its own self into expression, as a cause that is the cause of itself, or whose effect is immediately the cause.

When what is purposive gets ascribed to an _understanding_, as was mentioned above, what is being kept in view is _the_ _determinate_ _character_ _of_ _the_ _content_. Purpose is nevertheless to be taken, quite generally, as the _rational_ _in_ _its_ _concrete_ _existence_. _Rationality_ is what it manifests, and it does so because it is the concrete concept, the concept that keeps the _objective_ _difference_ _inside_ _its_ _absolute_ _unity_. Essentially, then, it is at its own self the _syllogism_. It is the _universal_ that stays equal to itself, and this as harbouring the negativity that repels itself away from itself; to begin with, the universal and so far still _undetermined_ _activity_; but since {{{ 12.161 }}} such activity is the negative reference to its own self, it _determines_ itself without further ado and confers on itself the moment of _particularity_, which, being the _totality_ of the _form_ that is likewise _reflected_ _into_ _itself_, stands as _content_ _over_ _against_ the _posited_ differences of the form. With equal immediacy this negativity, by way of its reference to itself, is absolute reflection of the form into itself, and _singularity_. Taken one way, such reflection is the _subject's_ _inner_ _universality_; taken the other way, it is _reflection_ _outwards_; and to that extent purpose is still something subjective, its activity being aimed at an external objectivity.

For purpose is the concept which, at objectivity, has come round to its own self; the determinateness it has conferred on itself there is that of the _objective_ _indifference_ and _externality_ of being-determined; its self-repelling negativity is thus one whose moments, being nothing but determinations of the concept itself, carry as well the form of an objective indifference towards one another. – Within the formal _judgment_, _subject_ and _predicate_ already stand determined as self-subsistent, each against the other; their self-subsistence, though, is as yet no more than abstract universality; by now it has gained the determination of _objectivity_; but as a moment of the concept this consummate diversity is shut up inside the simple unity of the concept. Now insofar as purpose is this total _reflection_ of objectivity _into_ _itself_, and is such _immediately_, the self-determination or particularity, taken as _simple_ reflection into itself, is _firstly_ distinguished from the _concrete_ form and constitutes a _determinate_ _content_. On that showing purpose is _finite_, however much it may be infinite subjectivity as regards its form. In the second place, its determinateness having the form of objective indifference, that determinateness takes on the shape of a _presupposition_, and on this side the finitude of purpose lies in this, that there stands before it an _objective_ _world_, mechanical and chemical, upon which its activity bears as upon something _at_ _hand_; thus its self-determining activity is, within its own identity, immediately _external_ _to_ _itself_, and is reflection outwards quite as much as reflection into itself. To that extent purpose has still a genuinely _extramundane_ concrete existence, namely insofar as that objectivity confronts it, just as the objectivity in turn confronts purpose as a whole that is mechanical and chemical, not yet determined and shot through by purpose.

The movement of purpose may therefore now be put in this way, that what it is bent upon is the sublating of its _presupposition_, namely the object's immediacy, and the _positing_ of the object as determined through the concept. This negative bearing towards the object is every bit as much a negative bearing towards its own self, a sublating of the subjectivity of purpose. On the positive side it is the realizing of purpose, {{{ 12.162 }}} the uniting, that is, of objective being with purpose, so that objective being, which qua moment of purpose is immediately the determinateness identical with purpose, may be there _as_ _something_ _external_, while conversely the objective, qua _presupposition_, gets _posited_ rather as determined through the concept. – Within itself purpose is the drive towards its own realization; externality is what the determinateness of the concept's moments comes to, yet the _simplicity_ of these moments inside the unity of the concept is unequal to what that simplicity is, and so the concept repels itself away from itself. Such repelling is _resolve_ as such, the negative unity's reference to itself, by which that unity is an _excluding_ singularity; and yet by this very _excluding_ it _resolves_ upon itself, that is, unlocks itself, since the excluding is a _self-determining_, a positing _of_ _its_ _own_ _self_. On the one side, subjectivity in determining itself turns itself into particularity and confers on itself a content which, being shut up inside the unity of the concept, remains an inward one; but this _positing_, the simple reflection into itself, is, as has come out, at once and immediately a _presupposing_ as well; and at the very moment when the subject of purpose determines _itself_, it stands referred to an indifferent, external objectivity, one that purpose is to render equal to that inner determinateness, that is, is to posit as something _determined_ _through_ _the_ _concept_, in the first instance as _means_.
