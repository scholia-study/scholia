## C. Reciprocity

In finite causality, what relate to each other in acting are substances. _Mechanism_ lies in this _externality_ of causality: namely, that within its effect the cause's _reflection_ _into_ _itself_ is simultaneously a repellent _being_; or that, within the _identity_ the causal substance keeps _with_ _itself_ in its effect, that substance stays no less immediately _external_ to itself, while the effect _has_ _gone_ _over_ into _another_ _substance_. In reciprocity this mechanism now stands sublated; for reciprocity holds, _first_, that the original _persisting_ belonging to _immediate_ substantiality _disappears_; _second_, that the _cause_ _comes_ _to_ _be_; and thereby _originariness_ as _mediating_ itself with itself through its own _negation_.

To begin with, reciprocity exhibits itself as a causality running both ways between _presupposed_ _substances_ that _condition_ each other; toward the other, each is _at_ _once_ an active and _at_ _once_ a passive _substance_. Since on this footing both are passive no less than active, whatever difference there was between them has already sublated itself; the difference is a shine of perfect transparency; what makes them substances is solely their being the identity of active and passive. Hence reciprocity of itself amounts to no more than an _empty_ _manner_ _and_ _mode_; nothing further is called for than an external {{{ 11.408 }}} drawing together of what is already there both _in_ _itself_ and as _posited_. For the present, what stand in reference to one another are no longer _substrates_ but substances; through the movement of conditioned causality whatever _presupposed_ _immediacy_ was left over has sublated itself, and nothing now _conditions_ the causal activity except the _acting-upon_, that is, the cause's _own_ passivity. Further, this acting-upon does not proceed from some _other_ original substance, but precisely from a causality that is itself conditioned by an acting-upon, that is, is something _mediated_. What is thus at first _external_, coming upon the cause and constituting the side of its passivity, turns out to be mediated _by_ _the_ _cause_ _itself_, produced by the cause's own activity, and hence to be the _passivity_ that _this_ _activity_ _has_ _itself_ _posited_. – Causality is at once conditioned and conditioning; what _conditions_ is the _passive_ side, yet no less is what is _conditioned_ _passive_. Such conditioning, or passivity, amounts to the cause's _negation_ of itself, since the cause essentially turns itself into _effect_ and is cause for just that reason. _Reciprocity_ is accordingly causality and nothing else; the cause does not simply _have_ an effect, rather in the effect it refers _as_ _cause_ to its own self.

With this, causality has gone back into _its_ _absolute_ _concept_ and has at the same time reached the _concept_ as such. To begin with it is real necessity; absolute _identity_ with itself, in such a way that necessity's difference, together with the determinations that inside it refer to each other, substances, are _free_ _actualities_ over against each other. So understood, necessity is _the_ _inner_ _identity_, while causality is that identity's manifestation, in which the shine _of_ _substantial_ _otherness_ belonging to it has sublated itself and necessity has been lifted into _freedom_. – Within reciprocity, original causality displays itself as _coming-to-be_ out of its negation, that is, out of passivity, and as _ceasing-to-be_ into that same negation, hence as a _becoming_; yet in such fashion that this becoming is equally nothing more than a _shining_; going over into an _other_ is reflection-into-itself; the _negation_ that constitutes the cause's ground is the cause's _positive_ _coming-together_ with itself.

Necessity and causality have thus disappeared in all this; they hold together both the _immediate_ _identity_ as _connection_ and _reference_, and the _absolute_ _substantiality_ _of_ _the_ _differentiated_ terms, and so their absolute _contingency_; the original _unity_ of substantial _diversity_; in short, the absolute contradiction. Necessity is that being which is _because_ it is; the unity that being has with itself in taking itself for _ground_; conversely, though, precisely in having a ground it fails to be being, and is nothing whatever but _shine_, _reference_, or _mediation_. Causality is this _posited_ transition whereby original being, the _cause_, goes over into shine or bare _positedness_, {{{ 11.409 }}} and positedness in turn into originariness; the very _identity_ of being with shine, however, is still _inner_ necessity. This _inwardness_, this being-in-itself, sublates the movement of causality; therewith the two sides that stand within the relation forfeit their substantiality, and necessity discloses itself. What turns necessity into _freedom_ is not its disappearing, but solely the _manifesting_ of that identity of its own which is as yet merely _inner_; and such manifestation is the identical movement of the differentiated within itself, the reflection of shine as shine into itself. – Contingency, conversely, thereby becomes _freedom_ at the same stroke, since the sides of necessity, which wear the shape of actualities that are free on their own and do not shine within one another, now stand _posited_ _as_ _identity_, so that these totalities of reflection-into-itself, for all their difference, also _shine_ _as_ _identical_, or are posited as nothing but one and the same reflection.

Absolute substance, which as absolute form distinguishes itself from itself, accordingly no longer repels itself away from itself as necessity, nor does it break apart as contingency into indifferent substances lying outside one another; instead it _differentiates_ itself, _on_ _the_ _one_ _side_, into the totality which – formerly the passive substance – counts as something original, being the reflection out of determinateness back into itself, a simple whole holding its own _positedness_ within it and _posited_ _therein_ _as_ _identical_ _with_ _itself_, namely the _universal_; – _on_ _the_ _other_ _side_, into the totality – formerly the causal substance – as into the reflection that likewise runs out of determinateness back into itself, toward a negative determinateness, which, being the _determinateness_ _identical_ with itself, is just as much the whole, though posited as the _negativity_ _identical_ _with_ _itself_; – namely the _singular_. Immediately, however, since the _universal_ is self-identical only by holding _determinateness_ within it as _sublated_, and hence is the negative qua negative, – it is that _very_ _negativity_ which _singularity_ is; – and singularity, being in like manner the determined determinate, the negative qua negative, is immediately that _very_ _identity_ which _universality_ is. Their _simple_ identity is _particularity_, holding in immediate unity the moment of _determinateness_ that comes from the singular and the moment _of_ _reflection-into-itself_ that comes from the universal. These three totalities are consequently one single reflection, which as _negative_ _reference_ _to_ _itself_ differentiates itself into those two, though into a _completely_ _transparent_ _difference_, namely into _determinate_ _simplicity_, or _simple_ _determinateness_, and that is their one single identity. – Such is the _concept_, the realm of _subjectivity_, or of _freedom_.
