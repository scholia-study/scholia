## B. The Absolute Attribute

The expression that has been used, _the_ _absolutely-absolute_, designates the absolute as _returned_ into itself in _its_ _form_, the absolute whose form equals its content. Attribute is the merely _relative_ _absolute_, a conjunction that means nothing else than the absolute standing in a _form-determination_. For form, ahead of its completed exposition, is at first _only_ as yet _inward_, or, what comes to the same, _only_ _outward_, in general, to start with, _determinate_ form, that is, negation in general. Since form at the same time is as the absolute's form, however, attribute is the absolute's whole content; it is the totality that showed itself earlier under the shape of a _world_, or as one of the two _sides_ of the _essential_ _relation_, either of them being itself the whole. Yet the two worlds, the appearing one and the one that is in and for itself, were each meant to be _opposed_ to the other in its essence. Of the essential relation it did hold that its one side was equal to the other: the whole came to as much as the parts; force's expression to a content the same as force's own; and the outer in general to what the inner is. Yet at the same time each of these sides was meant to have an _immediate_ subsistence of its own besides, the one as the immediacy that is, the other as the reflected immediacy. In the absolute, by contrast, these distinct immediacies are brought down to shine, and the _totality_ which attribute is stands _posited_ _as_ _its_ _true_ _and_ _sole_ _subsistence_; whereas the _determination_ in which that totality stands is posited as the _unessential_ one.

The absolute is attribute for this reason, that as simple absolute identity it stands in the determination of identity; and to determination in general other determinations can now be attached, for instance that there are _several_ attributes. But since the meaning of absolute identity is just this, not only that every determination is sublated, but that identity is itself the reflection which has sublated its own self, all determinations are _posited_ in it _as_ _sublated_ ones. Or totality is posited as the absolute totality, that is, attribute has the absolute {{{ 11.374 }}} for its content and its subsistence; the form-determination it carries, by which it is attribute, is therefore likewise posited, immediately as mere shine, the negative as negative. The positive shine that the exposition gives itself through attribute, in taking the finite within its restriction not for something that is in and for itself but dissolving the finite's subsistence into the absolute and widening the finite into attribute, sublates the very thing that attribute is; it sinks attribute, and its own distinguishing activity, into the _simple_ _absolute_.

But since reflection, distinguishing in this fashion, thereby comes back only to the absolute's _identity_, it has at the same time not got clear of its externality and reached the genuine absolute. All it has attained is the indeterminate, abstract identity; that is to say, the identity which stands in the _determinateness_ of identity. – Or, where reflection in the capacity of _inner_ form determines the absolute to attribute, this determining still remains diverse from externality; the absolute is not penetrated by that inner determination; its expression consists in vanishing, as something merely posited, at the absolute.

Form, then, whether it be taken as outer or as inner, the form by which the absolute would be attribute, is at the same time posited as null in its own self, as an external shine, or as a mere _way_ _and_ _manner_ of being.
