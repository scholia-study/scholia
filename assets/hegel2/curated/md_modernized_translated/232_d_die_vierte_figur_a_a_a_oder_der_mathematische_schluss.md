## d. The Fourth Figure: U-U-U, or the Mathematical Syllogism

1. Thus runs the mathematical syllogism: _when_ _two_ _things_ _or_ _determinations_ _are_ _equal_ _to_ _some_ _third_, _they_ _are_ _equal_ _to_ _one_ _another_. – The relation of inherence or of subsumption among the terms is extinguished in it.

_A_ _third_ in general is what does the mediating; yet it carries no determination whatever over against its extremes. Any one of the three, therefore, may just as well serve as the mediating third. Which of them is employed for the purpose, and hence which of the three references count as the immediate ones and which as the mediated one, hangs upon outward circumstances and other conditions; – namely upon which two of them happen to be the immediately _given_ ones. This determination, however, in no way concerns the syllogism itself and is wholly external.

2. In mathematics the mathematical syllogism passes for an _axiom_; – _as_ _something_ _evident_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, a first proposition neither capable of nor in need of proof, that is, of mediation, presupposing nothing further, nor derivable from anything. – Look more closely at the merit it has of being immediately {{{ 12.105 }}} _evident_, and this shows itself to lie in the formalism of this syllogism, which abstracts from every qualitative diversity among the determinations and takes up only their quantitative equality or inequality. Yet for that very reason it is not free of presupposition or unmediated; the quantitative determination, the sole one that comes into view in it, exists only _through_ _the_ _abstraction_ from qualitative difference and from the determinations of the concept. – Lines and figures set equal to one another are grasped solely as regards their magnitude; a triangle is set equal to a square, yet not as triangle to square, but in point of magnitude alone and so forth. The concept and its determinations likewise gain no entry into this inferring; nothing whatever gets _comprehended_ by it; indeed the understanding does not so much as have before it the formal, abstract determinations of the concept; what is evident in this syllogism therefore rests on nothing but its poverty and abstractness in point of thought-determination.

3. _But_ _what_ _the_ _syllogism_ _of_ _existence_ _results_ _in_ is not merely this abstracting from every determinateness of the concept; the _negativity_ of the immediate, abstract determinations that came forth from it has a further, _positive_ side as well, namely that into the abstract determinateness _its_ _other_ _has_ _been_ _posited_, whereby it has become _concrete_.

In the first place, the syllogisms of existence taken all together have one another for their _presupposition_, and the extremes conjoined in the conclusion are conjoined truly and in and for themselves only insofar as they are _otherwise_ held together by an identity grounded elsewhere; the middle term, constituted as it is in the syllogisms we have examined, _is_ _to_ _be_ their unity of concept, yet it is only a formal determinateness and not posited as the concrete unity of the two. _But_ _that_ _which_ _is_ _presupposed_ by each of those mediations is not merely _a_ _given_ _immediacy_ as such, as in the mathematical syllogism; it is itself a mediation, namely, for each, the other two syllogisms. What is truly at hand, then, is a mediation founded not upon a given immediacy but upon mediation. It is thus not the quantitative mediation that abstracts from the form of mediating, but rather _the_ _mediation_ _that_ _refers_ _itself_ _to_ _mediation_, or the _mediation_ _of_ _reflection_. The circle of mutual presupposing which these syllogisms close with one another is the turning back of this presupposing into its own self, whereby it forms a totality and holds the _other_ to which each single syllogism points not _outside_ itself by force of abstraction, but rather encloses it _within_ the circle.

_Furthermore_, _as_ _regards_ _the_ _single_ _form_ _determinations_, it has shown itself that within this whole of formal syllogisms every one of them has come _to_ _the_ _place_ _of_ _the_ _middle_ _term_ {{{ 12.106 }}}. At first the middle term stood determined as _particularity_; thereupon it determined itself, through the dialectical movement, as _singularity_ and _universality_. Each of these determinations ran through _the_ _places_ _of_ _the_ _two_ _extremes_ as well. _The_ _bare_ _negative_ _result_ lies in the extinguishing of the qualitative form determinations within the merely quantitative, mathematical syllogism. But what is truly at hand is the _positive_ _result_ that mediation takes place not through a _single_ qualitative form determinateness, but through the _concrete_ _identity_ of these determinacies. Just here lies the defect and the formalism of the three figures of syllogism we have examined, that in them one such single determinateness was to make up the middle term. – Mediation has determined itself, then, as the indifference belonging to the immediate or abstract form determinations and as the positive _reflection_ of the one into the other. With this the immediate syllogism of existence has passed over into the _syllogism_ _of_ _reflection_.
