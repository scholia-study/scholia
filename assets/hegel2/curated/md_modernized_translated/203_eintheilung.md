## {{{ 12.29 }}} Division

Looked at from the outside, the concept shows itself as the unity of _being_ and _essence_. Of being, essence is the _first_ _negation_, whereby being has turned into _shine_; the concept is the _second_, namely the negation of this negation; hence being reinstated, though as being's infinite mediation and negativity within its own self. – Within the concept, accordingly, _being_ and _essence_ no longer carry the determination in which they are as _being_ and _essence_, nor do they stand merely in such a unity that each _shines_ in the other. Into these determinations, therefore, the concept does not differentiate itself. It is the truth of the relation of substantiality, wherein being and essence reach, through one another, a self-subsistence and a determination that are fulfilled. What showed itself to be the truth of substantiality was _substantial_ _identity_, an identity that equally, and solely, is as _positedness_. Positedness is _existence_ and _differentiating_; hence being-in-and-for-itself has, within the concept, arrived at an existence commensurate with it and true, since that positedness is being-in-and-for-itself itself. This positedness makes up the concept's difference within it; its _differences_, because the concept is immediately being-in-and-for-itself, are themselves _the_ _whole_ _concept_; _in_ _their_ _determinateness_ _universal_, _and_ _identical_ _with_ _their_ _negation_.

This, now, is the very concept of the concept. But it is _only_ _yet_ the concept of the concept; – or else the concept is itself likewise _only_ _the_ _concept_. Since it is being-in-and-for-itself so far as this is positedness, or the absolute substance so far as substance discloses the _necessity_ of distinct substances as _identity_, what this identity is it must itself posit. Those moments in the movement of the substantiality relation through which the concept has _become_, together with the reality they exhibit, still lie within the transition into the concept; that reality is not yet there as a determination _of_ _its_ _own_, one sprung from the concept; it fell within the sphere of necessity, whereas the concept's own reality can only be its _free_ determination, an existence wherein it is identical with itself, an existence whose moments are concepts and are _posited_ by the concept itself.

_First_, then, the concept is the truth only _in_ _itself_; being _only_ _something_ _inner_, it is to just the same degree _only_ _something_ _outer_. It is _first_ of all, quite generally, an {{{ 12.30 }}} _im__mediate_, and in this shape the form belonging to its moments is that of _immediate_, _fixed_ _determinations_. It makes its appearance as the _determinate_ _concept_, as the sphere of the mere _understanding_. – Since a form of immediacy such as this amounts to an existence not yet befitting the concept's nature — the concept being the _free_ that refers to nothing but itself — that form is an _external_ one, within which the concept can count not as something in and for itself but as _merely_ _posited_, as something _subjective_. – What the shape of the _immediate_ concept constitutes is the standpoint from which the concept counts as a subjective thinking, a reflection lying outside the fact. This stage accordingly makes up SUBJECTIVITY, or the _formal_ _concept_. Its externality shows in _the_ _fixed_ _being_ of its _determinations_, by which each steps forward on its own as something isolated and qualitative, standing merely in an outward reference to its other. The concept's _identity_, however, being precisely the _inner_ or _subjective_ essence of those determinations, sets them into dialectical movement, whereby their singularization, and with it the concept's separation from the fact, is sublated, and their truth comes forth as the _totality_ that is _the_ _objective_ _concept_.

II. The concept in its OBJECTIVITY is the _fact_ _itself_ _as_ _it_ _is_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_. By its necessary further determination the _formal_ concept turns itself into the fact, and so loses the relation of subjectivity and externality over against the fact. Or, put the other way round, objectivity is the _real_ _concept_ that has _emerged_ out of its _inwardness_ and gone over into existence. – Thus in this identity with the fact it possesses an existence that is _its_ _own_ and _free_. Yet this freedom is still an _immediate_ one, not yet _negative_. Being one with the fact, the concept is _submerged_ in it; its differences are objective concrete existences within which it is once more itself the _inner_. Being the soul of objective existence, it must _give_ to itself the form of _subjectivity_ which as _formal_ concept it possessed _immediately_; and so it comes to stand over against objectivity _in_ _the_ _form_ of the free — a form it did not yet possess within objectivity — and therein renders the identity that _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, as _objective_ concept, it has with objectivity, into one that is _posited_ as well.

In this consummation, where within its objectivity the concept has the form of freedom as well, lies the _adequate_ _concept_, the IDEA. REASON, being the sphere of the idea, is the _truth_ that has _unveiled_ itself to itself, wherein the concept possesses the realization utterly commensurate with it, and is free just so far as it cognizes this objective world of its own within its subjectivity, and its subjectivity within that world.
