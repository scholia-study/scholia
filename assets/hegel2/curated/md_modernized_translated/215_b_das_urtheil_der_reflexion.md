## {{{ 12.71 }}} B. The Judgment of Reflection

A singular as such is what the subject is in the judgment that has by now emerged; likewise the universal is no longer _abstract_ universality, or a _single_ _property_, but stands posited as a universal that has drawn itself into one through the reference of things distinguished, or, taken with respect to the content of diverse determinations at large, as that which is _the taking together_ of manifold properties and concrete existences. – Should examples of predicates belonging to judgments of reflection be called for, they must be of a different cast from those serving judgments of existence. Properly speaking, it is only in the judgment of reflection that a _determinate_ _content_, that is, a content at all, is at hand; for the content is the form-determination reflected into identity, marked off from form insofar as form is differentiated determinateness, – which is what form still amounts to as judgment. Within the judgment of existence, the content is merely something immediate, or something abstract and undetermined. – Judgments of reflection may accordingly be exemplified thus: the human being is _mortal_, things are _transient_, this thing is _useful_, _harmful_; _hardness_, _elasticity_ _in_ _bodies_, _happiness_ and so forth are predicates of that peculiar sort. What they [express] is an essentiality, one that is nevertheless a determination within a _relation_, or a _gathering_ universality. This _universality_, which will go on determining itself in the course of the judgment of reflection's movement, still stands apart from the _universality_ _of_ _the_ _concept_ as such; granted, it is no longer the abstract universality of the qualitative judgment, yet it keeps a reference to the immediate out of which it comes, and has that immediate lying at its base for its negativity. – In the first instance the concept determines existence into _relational_ _determinations_, into continuities of themselves within the diverse manifoldness of concrete existence, – so that, while the truly universal is indeed their inner essence, still, _in_ _appearance_, this _relative_ nature, or their _mark_, is not yet what they are in and for themselves.

It might well look like the obvious course to determine the judgment of reflection as the judgment of _quantity_, in the way that the judgment of existence was also determined as the _qualitative_ judgment. Yet just as the _immediacy_ there was not only the immediacy which _is_ but essentially the mediated, _abstract_ one besides, so here too the sublated immediacy in question is not simply sublated {{{ 12.72 }}} quality, hence not simply _quantity_; quantity is rather, in the same manner in which quality is the most external immediacy, the most external _determination_ belonging to mediation.

A remark remains to be made about the _determination_ as it shows itself in its movement within the judgment of reflection: in the judgment of existence the _movement_ of that determination displayed itself at the _predicate_, this judgment standing in the determination of immediacy, so that the subject appeared as what lies at the base. On the same grounds, in the judgment of reflection the onward course of determining runs off at the _subject_, since it is _reflected_ _being-in-itself_ that this judgment has for its determination. Here, then, what is essential is the _universal_, or the predicate; it therefore makes up _what_ _lies_ _at_ _the_ _base_, whereby the subject is to be measured and in conformity with which it is to be determined. – The predicate does, all the same, acquire a further determination through the ongoing formation of the subject's form, only _indirectly_ so, whereas the latter formation, on the grounds just given, shows itself as _direct_ onward determining.

Turning to the objective significance of the judgment: through its universality the singular steps into existence, yet as within an essential relational determination, an essentiality that holds itself intact throughout the manifoldness of appearance; the subject _ought_ to be what is determined in and for itself; this determinateness it possesses in its predicate. On the other side, the singular is reflected into this predicate of its own, which is its universal essence; to that extent the subject is what concretely exists and what appears. The predicate in this judgment does not any longer _inhere_ in the subject; it is rather _what_ _is_ _in_ _itself_, under which the singular in question stands _subsumed_ as something _accidental_. Whereas the judgments of existence may also be determined as _judgments_ _of_ _inherence_, those of reflection count rather as _judgments_ _of_ _subsumption_.
