## Remark

It is one of the most familiar determinations of representing that _a_ _thing_ _consists_ _of_ _many_ _self-subsistent_ _matters_. On the one side the thing is considered as having _properties_ whose _subsistence_ is the _thing_. {{{ 11.338 }}} On the other side, however, these diverse determinations are taken as matters whose subsistence is not the thing; on the contrary, the _thing_ _consists_ of them; it is itself nothing but their external combination and their quantitative limit. The properties and the matters are both alike the _same_ _determinations_ _of_ _content_, save that in the former case they are moments reflected into their negative unity as into a foundation distinguished from them, namely _thinghood_, whereas here they are diverse self-subsistent ones, each reflected into a unity of its own with itself. These matters now determine themselves further as self-subsistent subsistence; but they are also together in one thing. This thing has the two determinations: first, to be _this_, and second, to be the _also_. The _also_ is that which in outer intuition occurs as _spatial_ _extension_; _this_, however, the negative unity, is the _punctiformity_ of the thing. The matters are together in the punctiformity, and their also, or extension, is everywhere this punctiformity; for the also, as thinghood, is essentially determined as negative unity too. _Where_ one of these matters is, accordingly, there _in_ _one_ _and_ _the_ _same_ _point_ is the other; the thing's colour is not in one place, its odorous matter in another, its caloric matter in a third, and so forth; rather, at the very point at which it is warm it is also coloured, sour, electrical, and so on. Now since these matters are not outside one another but in one this, they are assumed to be _porous_, so that the one exists in the interstices of the other. But the one that finds itself in the interstices of the other is itself porous as well; in its pores, conversely, the other therefore exists; and not this one alone, but the third, the tenth, and so forth. All are porous, and in the interstices of each there are found all the others, just as it in turn, together with the rest, is found in these pores of each of them. They are therefore a multitude that so reciprocally interpenetrates that the ones doing the penetrating are just as much penetrated by the others, so that each thereby penetrates once more its own being-penetrated. Each of them is posited as being its own negation, and that negation is another's subsistence; but this subsistence is no less the negation of that other and the first one's subsistence.

The evasion by which _representing_ wards off the _contradiction_ of the _self-subsistent_ subsistence of _several_ matters _in_ _one_, or their _indifference_ towards one another in their _interpenetration_, is notoriously wont to be the _smallness_ of the parts and of the pores. Wherever difference-in-itself, contradiction, and the negation of negation come on the scene, wherever in general something is to be _comprehended_, representing lets itself drop down into the external, _quantitative_ difference; with regard to coming-to-be and ceasing-to-be it takes refuge in gradualness, and with regard to being in _smallness_, wherein what vanishes is brought down to the _imperceptible_, the {{{ 11.339 }}} contradiction to a confusion, and the true relation is played over into an indeterminate representing whose murkiness saves what is sublating itself.

Examined more closely, however, this murkiness shows itself to be the contradiction, in part the subjective one of representing, in part the objective one of the object; representing itself contains the elements of it in full. For what it first of all does itself is the contradiction of wanting to hold to _perception_ and to have before it things of _existence_, and, on the other side, of ascribing sensible existence to what is _not_ _perceptible_ and is determined through reflection; – the little parts and the pores are supposed at once to be a sensible existence, while their positedness is talked of as having that same mode of _reality_ – which belongs to colour, warmth, and so forth. If, furthermore, representing were to look more closely at this _objective_ fog, the pores and the little particles, it would recognize in them not merely a matter and _also_ its negation, such that _here_ there would be the matter, and _alongside_ _it_ its negation, the pore, and _next_ _to_ this again matter, and so on, but rather that in _this_ thing it has 1) the matter that is _self-subsistent_, 2) its _negation_, that is, porosity, together with the _other_ _self-subsistent_ matter _in_ _one_ _and_ _the_ _same_ _point_, and that this porosity, along with the matters' self-subsistent subsistence in one another as in one, is a reciprocal negation and a penetrating of the penetrating. – The more recent accounts given by physics of the diffusion of water vapor in atmospheric air and of the gases through one another bring out more determinately one side of the concept that has here emerged concerning the nature of the thing. They show, namely, that a given volume, for example, takes up just as much water vapor whether it be empty of atmospheric air or filled with it; also that the gases spread through one another in such a way that for each the other is as good as a vacuum, at least that they stand in no chemical combination with one another, each remaining uninterruptedly _continuous_ _with_ _itself_ through the other and preserving itself, in _its_ _interpenetration_ _with_ _the_ _others_, _indifferent_ towards them. – But the further moment in the concept of the thing is that in the _this_ the one matter is located where the other is, and that what penetrates is at that same point penetrated as well, or that what is self-subsistent is immediately another's self-subsistence. This is a contradiction; yet the thing is nothing else than just this contradiction; that is why it is appearance.

A situation similar to the one obtaining with these matters obtains in the spiritual domain with the representation of the _forces_ _of_ _the_ _soul_ or _faculties_ _of_ _the_ _soul_. Spirit is _this_ in a far deeper sense: the negative unity within which its determinations penetrate one another. But when it is represented as soul, it is frequently wont to be taken for a _thing_. Just as the human being at large is allowed to _consist_ of soul and body, {{{ 11.340 }}} each counting as something self-subsistent for itself, so the soul is allowed to consist of so-called _forces_ _of_ _the_ _soul_, each of which has a self-subsistence subsisting for itself, or is an immediate activity operating for itself according to its determinateness. One represents it to oneself in this way, that here the understanding, there the imagination is at work for itself, that one cultivates the understanding, the memory, and so forth, each for itself, and for the time being lets the other forces lie idle on the left hand until their turn perhaps comes, or perhaps does not. Inasmuch as they are relocated into the materially simple _soul-thing_, which is supposed to be simple in being _immaterial_, the faculties are indeed not represented as particular matters; but as _forces_ they are assumed to be quite as _indifferent_ towards one another as those matters are. Spirit, however, is not that contradiction which the thing is, the thing that dissolves itself and passes over into appearance; rather, it is in its own self already the contradiction that has gone back into its absolute unity, namely the concept, wherein the differences are no longer to be thought of as self-subsistent ones, but only as _particular_ moments within the subject, within simple individuality.
