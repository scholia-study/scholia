## A. Identity

1. Essence is the simple immediacy that is sublated immediacy. Its own negativity is its being; in its absolute negativity it is selfsame with itself, a negativity through which otherness and the relation to another have simply and utterly vanished, upon their own selves, into pure self-sameness. Essence is therefore simple _identity_ with itself.

This identity with itself is the _immediacy_ that belongs to reflection. It is not the self-sameness that _being_ is, or that the _nothing_ also is, but rather the self-sameness which is as establishing itself into unity, not a re-establishing out of an other, but this pure establishing out of and within its own self; the _essential_ identity. To that extent it is not _abstract_ identity, nor has it come about through a relative negating that would have gone on outside it and would merely have severed from it what is distinguished, leaving that, for the rest, outside it as something _that is_, now as before. Rather, being, and all determinateness of being, has sublated itself not in a relative way but upon its own self; and this simple negativity, that of being in itself, is identity itself.

To that extent it is still, in general, the same thing as essence.
