## {{{ 11.291 }}} Chapter 3. Ground

_Essence_ _determines_ _itself_ _as_ _ground_.

As the _nothing_ was at the start in simple immediate unity with _being_, so here also is the simple identity of essence at the start in immediate unity with its absolute negativity. Essence is only this its negativity, and negativity so taken is pure reflection. As the return of being into itself, essence is this pure negativity; and thus, _in_ _itself_ or for us, it stands _determined_ as the ground wherein being dissolves. This determinateness, though, has not been posited _by_ _it_ _itself_; or, it is no ground precisely for as long as it has not posited that determinateness of its own. What its reflection amounts to, however, is _positing_ itself as what _in_ _itself_ it is, as negative, and _determining_ itself. Positive and negative constitute the essential determination wherein essence has lost itself as in its own negation. Such self-subsistent determinations of reflection sublate one another, and the determination that has foundered is the genuine determination of essence.

Hence _ground_ is itself _one_ _of_ _the_ _determinations_ _of_ _reflection_ that belong to essence — the last of them, or rather no determination at all beyond that of being a determination sublated. As it founders, the determination of reflection wins its genuine sense, that of being the absolute counter-thrust of itself into its own self, which is to say that the positedness falling to essence obtains only as positedness sublated, and inversely, that only a self-sublating positedness counts as essence's own positedness. Essence, as it determines itself to be ground, determines itself as what remains undetermined, and its determining is nothing but the sublating of its being-determined. – So determined, that is, as self-sublating, it is no essence come out of another, but one that stays identical with itself throughout its negativity.

To the extent that the passage to ground starts out from determination, from what is first and immediate (owing to the nature of determination itself, which founders through its own doing), ground is initially something the first has determined. Only, this determining is on one side, being a sublating of determining, essence's identity merely restored, cleansed, or made manifest, the identity which the {{{ 11.292 }}} determination of reflection _is_ _in_ _itself_; – on the other side, this negating movement, taken as determining, first posits that reflected determinateness which showed itself as the immediate one, though it is posited only by ground's self-excluding reflection and posited therein as no more than posited, or sublated. – Thus essence, determining itself as ground, proceeds out of itself and nothing else. _As_ _ground_, then, it _posits_ itself _as_ _essence_, and its determining lies precisely in its positing itself as essence. That positing is essence's reflection, which _sublates_ itself in its own _determining_ — on the one side a _positing_, on the other _the_ positing _of_ _essence_, so that in one deed it is both.

Reflection is _pure_ _mediation_ generally, while ground is essence's _real_ _mediation_ with itself. Reflection, that movement out of nothing through nothing and back to itself, is the shining of _itself_ within an _other_; but with opposition still lacking self-subsistence in such reflection, neither is the first term, the one that shines, a positive, nor is the _other_ wherein it shines a negative. The pair are substrates, and strictly speaking substrates of the power of imagination alone; as yet they do not refer to their own selves. Mediation in its purity is nothing but _pure_ _reference_, lacking terms referred. Determining reflection posits terms of that sort, to be sure, terms self-identical and yet at the same time only _determinate_ _references_. Ground, by contrast, counts as the real mediation, since reflection is contained in it as reflection sublated; ground is the essence which _through_ _its_ _non-being_ _comes_ _back_ _into_ _itself_ and _posits_ _itself_. By this moment of sublated reflection the posited acquires the determination of _immediacy_, the determination of what stays self-identical apart from the reference or from its shine. Such an immediate is _being_ as essence has restored it; it is reflection's non-being, and through this non-being essence mediates itself. It is as something negating that essence goes back into itself; in going back it therefore confers on itself the determinateness which for just that reason is the self-identical negative, positedness sublated, and so is quite as much _something_ _that_ _is_ as it is the identity of essence with itself as ground.

Ground is _at_ _first_ _absolute_ _ground_, wherein essence serves initially as _groundwork_ in general for the ground-reference; determined more closely, though, it determines itself as _form_ and _material_ and confers a _content_ on itself.

_Second_, it is _determinate_ _ground_, being ground of a determinate content; and as the ground-reference grows external to itself in the course of its realization generally, it passes over into the mediation that _conditions_.

_Third_, a condition is presupposed by ground; yet the condition presupposes ground quite as much; their unity is the unconditioned, _the_ _matter_ _in_ _itself_, which passes over into concrete existence through the mediation of the conditioning reference.
