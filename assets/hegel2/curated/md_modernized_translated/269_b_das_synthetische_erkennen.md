## {{{ 12.209 }}} b. Synthetic Cognition

Within the syllogism taken as a whole, analytic cognition constitutes the first premise, – the concept's _immediate_ reference to the object; hence the determination which such cognizing recognizes as belonging to itself is _identity_, and all that it does is to _apprehend_ what _is_. What synthetic cognition is after, by contrast, is the _comprehending_ of what _is_ — that is, a grasp of the manifoldness of determinations within the unity that they have. In the syllogism it accordingly forms the second premise, the one where the _diverse_, taken as diverse, is set into reference. For this reason what it aims at is _necessity_ in general. – Whatever diverse terms have been bound together are bound partly within a _relation_, and in a relation they stand referred to each other quite as much as they stand indifferent and self-subsistent over against each other; but partly they are knit together in the _concept_, which is their unity, simple and yet determinate. Now insofar as synthetic cognition moves, to begin with, out of _abstract_ _identity_ over into _relation_, or out of _being_ over into _reflection_, what the concept cognizes in its subject matter is not the concept's absolute reflection; the reality it confers upon itself stands at the stage lying nearest, namely at the identity of diverse terms as such that was noted above, an identity for that very reason still _inner_ and still necessity merely, not the subjective necessity that exists for its own sake, and so not yet the concept in its proper sense. Synthetic cognition too, then, does have the concept's determinations for its content, and the object gets posited within those determinations; yet as things stand these merely occupy a _relation_ towards each other, or are held in an _immediate_ unity, which is precisely not the unity whereby the concept has the standing of subject.

This constitutes the finitude of this cognition; because in it the real side of the idea still has identity as something _inner_, the determinations of that side are still _external_ to one another; since it is not there as subjectivity, what the concept has as its own in its subject matter still lacks _singularity_, and it is indeed no longer the abstract but the _determinate_ form, hence the _particular_ of the concept, that corresponds to it in the object, but the _singular_ of the concept is still _a_ _given_ content. This cognition therefore does indeed transform the objective world into concepts, but it gives that world only the form in accordance with the determinations of the concept, and must _find_ the object according to its _singularity_, the determinate determinateness; it is not yet itself determining. In the same way it _finds_ propositions and laws, and proves their _necessity_, yet not as a necessity of the fact in and for itself, that is, from the concept, but as a necessity of cognition, which proceeds along the given determinations, the differences of appearance, and {{{ 12.210 }}} _for_ _itself_ cognizes the proposition as unity and relation, or from the _appearance_ cognizes its ground.

The more proximate moments of synthetic cognition are now to be examined.
