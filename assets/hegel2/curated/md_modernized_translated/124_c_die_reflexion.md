## C. Reflection

Shine is the very same thing that _reflection_ is; but it is reflection in the shape of the _immediate_; for shine that has gone into itself, and has thereby been estranged from its immediacy, we have the word of the foreign tongue, _reflection_.

Essence is reflection: it is the movement of becoming and of passing over that abides within its own self; a movement wherein what is differentiated stands determined simply and solely as the in-itself negative, as shine. – In the becoming that belongs to being, being underlies determinateness, and determinateness amounts to a reference to an _other_. The reflecting movement, by contrast, has the other as the _negation_ _in_ _itself_, a negation that possesses a being only as negation referring itself to itself. Or, since this reference to itself is precisely this negating of the negation, _negation_ _as_ _negation_ is there at hand, as something whose being lies in its being-negated, as shine. So the other here is not _being_ _with_ _negation_, that is, limit, but rather _negation_ _with_ _negation_. As for the _first_ set against this other, namely the immediate or being, it is nothing but this very equality of negation with itself, the negation negated, absolute negativity. Equality of this kind with itself, or _immediacy_, is therefore not a _first_ from which the start is made and which would go over {{{ 11.250 }}} into its negation; nor is it a substrate with being, one that would move itself the whole way through reflection; immediacy, rather, is nothing but this movement itself.

Becoming within essence, essence's reflecting movement, is therefore the _movement_ _from_ _nothing_ _to_ _nothing_, _and_ _thereby_ _back_ _to_ _its_ _own_ _self_. Passing over, or becoming, sublates itself in its passing over; the other which comes to be in this passing over is no non-being of a being, but the nothing of a nothing, and this — being the negation of a nothing — is what makes up being. – Being is only as the movement of nothing to nothing, and thus it is essence; and essence, for its part, does not _have_ this movement _within_ _it_; rather it is that movement, being the absolute shine itself, pure negativity, having nothing outside itself that it might negate, negating instead only its own negative, one that only is within this negating.

This pure absolute reflection, being the movement from nothing to nothing, goes on to determine its own self further.

It is _firstly_ _positing_ _reflection_;

it makes, _secondly_, its _beginning_ from the _presupposed_ _immediate_, and in that way is _external_ reflection.

_Thirdly_, however, it sublates this presupposition, and since in the sublating of the presupposition it is _at_ _the_ _same_ _time_ presupposing, it is _determining_ reflection.
