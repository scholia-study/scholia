## c. Emergence of the Fact into Concrete Existence

The absolute ground identical with its condition is the absolutely unconditioned — the immediate fact, as that which is genuinely of the nature of essence. Qua _ground_ it refers itself negatively upon itself and turns itself into positedness; into a positedness, however, that is the reflection complete in its sides and the form-reference self-identical within them, as its concept has come out. _Firstly_, therefore, this positedness is the ground sublated, the fact as the immediate devoid of reflection: the side of the conditions. That side is the _totality_ of the fact's determinations – the fact itself, yet cast out into the outwardness of being; the restored circle of being. Within the condition, essence lets go the unity of its reflection-into-itself as an immediacy, though one that now bears the determination of being a _conditioning_ presupposition and of essentially making up but a single one of essence's sides. – That the conditions are the fact's entire content rests on this, that they are the unconditioned in the shape of formless being. On account of that shape, however, they wear besides a different figure from the determinations of the content such as it is in the fact as such. They appear as a manifoldness devoid of unity, intermingled with the outwardly-essential and with other circumstances which have no place in the circle of existence, insofar as that circle makes up this _determinate_ fact's conditions. – _The_ _sphere_ _of_ _being_ _itself_ is the condition for the absolute, unrestricted fact. That sphere is posited by the ground, in the ground's going back into itself, as the first immediacy, upon which the ground refers itself as upon its unconditioned. Being sublated reflection, this immediacy is _reflection_ in the element of being, an element which accordingly builds itself up as such into a whole; as determinateness of being the form proliferates onward, and appears thereby as a manifold content, one differing from the determination of reflection and indifferent towards it. The unessential which the sphere of being carries about it, and which it strips off insofar as it is condition, is that determinateness of immediacy into {{{ 11.320 }}} which the unity of form lies sunk. In being, this unity of form, as being's reference, is present at first as _becoming_ – as the passing over of one of being's determinatenesses into another. The becoming of being, however, is further a becoming towards essence and a going back into the ground. Existence, therefore, which makes up the conditions, is in truth not determined as condition by an other and put to use as material; through its own self, rather, it makes itself into a moment of an other. – Nor, further, is its becoming a beginning from itself as from what is genuinely first and immediate; its immediacy is rather only the presupposed, and reflection's own doing is the movement of its becoming. Hence to be condition is the truth of existence; existence has its immediacy solely through the ground-reference's reflection, which posits itself as sublated. Becoming is thus, like immediacy, only the shine of the unconditioned, the unconditioned presupposing itself and having its form therein; and the immediacy of being is on that account essentially a _moment_ of form and nothing more.

This shining of the unconditioned has as its other side the ground-reference as such, determined as form as against the immediacy both of the conditions and of the content. But it is the form of the absolute fact, a fact having the unity of its form with itself, or its _content_, in its own self, and, in determining that content into condition, sublating the content's diversity in this very positing and making it into a moment; just as, conversely, the fact gives itself, qua essenceless form, the immediacy of subsistence within this identity with itself. The ground's reflection sublates the conditions' immediacy and refers them, as moments, into the unity of the fact; but the conditions are what the unconditioned fact itself has presupposed, so that in doing so the fact sublates its own positing; or its positing thereby makes itself, immediately and quite as much, into _becoming_. – Both are on that account one unity; the movement of the conditions in their own selves is becoming, a going back into the ground and a positing of the ground; the ground as posited, however — as sublated, that is — is what is immediate. Negatively referring itself upon itself, the ground turns itself into positedness and grounds the conditions; but in that immediate existence is thereby determined as a posited, the ground sublates it and only so makes itself into ground. – This reflection, then, is that mediation whereby the unconditioned fact is mediated with itself through its negation. Or rather: the unconditioned's reflection is in the first place presupposing, yet this sublating of its own self is immediately a determining positing; secondly it is therein immediately a sublating of the presupposed and a determining out of itself; and thus this determining is once again a sublating of the positing, and is becoming in its own self. Therein mediation, as a return to itself by way of negation, has vanished; it is simple reflection shining within itself, and absolute becoming without ground. That the fact is posited by its _conditions_ _on_ _the_ _one_ _side_ and on the other by its {{{ 11.321 }}} ground — this movement is nothing else than the _vanishing_ _of_ _the_ _shine_ _of_ _mediation_. The fact's being posited is hereby a _coming_ _forth_, the simple putting of itself forth _into_ _concrete_ _existence_; the fact's pure movement to its own self.

_When_ _all_ _the_ _conditions_ _of_ _a_ _fact_ _are_ _on_ _hand_, it thereupon steps into concrete existence. The fact _is_, _before_ _it_ _concretely_ _exists_; and indeed it is, first, as _essence_, or as unconditioned; secondly it has _existence_, or is determinate, and this in the twofold manner considered — on the one side in its conditions, on the other in its ground. In those conditions the fact has given itself the form of outward, groundless being, since as absolute reflection it is the negative reference upon itself and makes itself into its own presupposition. What is thus presupposed as unconditioned is accordingly the groundless immediate, and its being amounts to nothing but being there as groundless. If, then, all the fact's conditions are on hand — which is to say, if the fact's totality stands posited as groundless immediate — this scattered manifoldness _recollects_ itself in its own self. – The whole fact must be there in its conditions, or: to its concrete existence all the conditions belong; for _all_ of them make up the reflection; or, existence being condition, it is determined by the form, its determinations are therefore determinations of reflection, and with any one of them the others are essentially posited. – At first the _recollection_ of the conditions is the foundering of immediate existence and the becoming of the ground. But with that the ground is a posited, which is to say that just as much as it is ground, so much is it sublated as ground and is immediate being. If, then, all the fact's conditions are on hand, they sublate themselves qua immediate existence and qua presupposition, and just as much does the ground sublate itself. The ground shows itself only as a shine that immediately vanishes; this coming forth is thus the fact's tautological movement to itself, and the fact's mediation through the conditions and through the ground is the vanishing of both. So immediate, therefore, is the coming forth into concrete existence that it stands mediated only through the vanishing of mediation.

_The_ _fact_ _emerges_ _out_ _of_ _the_ _ground_. The ground does not ground or posit it in such a way that the ground would still stay below; the positing is rather the ground's movement out to its own self, and the ground's simple vanishing. By _union_ with the conditions the ground obtains outward immediacy and the moment of being. It obtains them, though, neither as something outward nor by an outward reference; as ground, rather, it turns itself into positedness, in the positedness its simple essentiality goes together with itself, and in this sublating of its own self it is the vanishing of its difference from its positedness, hence simple essential immediacy. It does not, then, stay behind as something diverse from the grounded; rather {{{ 11.322 }}} the truth of grounding lies in this, that therein the ground unites with its own self, so that its reflection into an other is its reflection into itself. Just as the fact is the _unconditioned_, so it is hereby also the _groundless_, and it steps out of the ground only insofar as the ground has _foundered_ and is no ground — out of the groundless, which is to say out of its own pure form or essential negativity.

Mediated through ground and condition, and through the sublating of that mediation identical with itself, this immediacy is _concrete_ _existence_.
