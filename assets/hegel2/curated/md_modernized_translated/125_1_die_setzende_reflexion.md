## 1. Positing Reflection

Shine is what is null or without essence; but what is null or without essence does not have its being in some _other_ where it shines, rather its being is its own self-sameness; this interchanging of the negative with itself has determined itself to be the absolute reflection of essence.

This negativity that refers itself to itself is accordingly the negating of its own self. It is thus, in general, negativity that is just as much _sublated_ as it is negativity. Or it is itself the negative and simple self-sameness, or immediacy. It consists, then, in being _it_ _itself_ and _not_ _it_ _itself_, and this in one single unity. –

To begin with, reflection is the movement of the nothing to the nothing, and so the negation that goes together with itself. This going together with itself is, in general, simple self-sameness; immediacy. But this coinciding is not a passing over of negation into self-sameness as into its _otherness_; rather, reflection is passing over as the sublating of passing over, since it is the immediate coinciding of the negative _with_ _itself_; so this going together is _firstly_ self-sameness, or {{{ 11.251 }}} immediacy; but _secondly_ this immediacy is the self-sameness _of_ _the_ _negative_, and hence a self-sameness negating itself; the immediacy that in itself is the negative, the negative of its own self, namely to be what it is not.

The negative's relation to itself is therefore its return into itself; as the sublating of the negative it is immediacy; but immediacy purely and only in the sense of this relation, that is, of a _return_ _from_ _something_, and hence an immediacy that sublates its own self. – This is _positedness_; immediacy purely and only as _determinateness_, or as reflecting itself. This immediacy, which is only as the negative's _return_ into itself, – is the very immediacy that makes up the determinateness of shine, and from which the reflecting movement appeared just now to begin. Far from its being possible to start out from this immediacy, the immediacy rather first is as the return, or as reflection itself. Reflection is accordingly the movement which, in being the return, only therein is that which begins or that which returns.

It is _positing_ insofar as it is immediacy as a returning; for no other is present, neither one out of which nor one into which it might return; it therefore is only as a returning, or as the negative of its own self. But further still, this immediacy is the sublated negation as well as the sublated return into itself. Reflection, being the sublating of the negative, sublates _its_ _other_, immediacy. Since it is thus immediacy as a returning, as the negative's going together with itself, it is just as much negation of the negative qua negative. Thus it is _presupposing_. – Or immediacy, as a returning, is only its own negative, only this: not to be immediacy; but reflection sublates that negative of itself, it goes together with itself; it therefore sublates its positing, and in being the sublating of positing within its positing it is presupposing. – In presupposing, reflection determines the return into itself as the negative of its own self, as that whose sublating is essence. It is its own comportment towards itself; but towards itself as its negative; only so is it the negativity that stays within itself and refers itself to itself. Immediacy comes forth, quite generally, only as return, and is that negative which is the shine of the beginning, a shine that the return negates. The return of essence is thus its repelling of itself away from itself. Or reflection into itself is essentially the presupposing of that out of which it is the return.

It is the sublating of its own self-sameness whereby essence first is self-sameness. It presupposes its own self, and it is itself the sublating of this {{{ 11.252 }}} presupposition; conversely, the presupposition itself is just this sublating of its presupposition. – Reflection thus _finds_ an immediate already _there_, one that it goes beyond and out of which it is the return. Yet this return is what first presupposes the thing found there. What is found there _comes to be_ only in that it is _left_ _behind_; its immediacy is sublated immediacy. – Sublated immediacy, conversely, is the return into itself, essence's _arriving_ at its own self, the simple being that is self-same. This arriving at itself is thereby the sublating of itself and the reflection that repels itself from itself and presupposes, and its repelling of itself is the arriving at its own self.

The reflecting movement is thus, according to what has been considered, to be taken as an _absolute_ _counter-thrust_ within itself. For the presupposition of the return into itself, – that from which essence _comes_ and which it only _is_ as this coming back –, lies only in the return itself. The going beyond the immediate from which reflection begins is rather only through this going beyond; and going beyond the immediate is arriving at it. As a going forth the movement immediately turns round within itself, and only so is it self-movement, – movement that comes out of itself, insofar as _positing_ reflection is _presupposing_, yet as _presupposing_ reflection is simply and solely _positing_.

So reflection is at once itself and its non-being; and it is itself only in being its own negative, for only so is the sublating of the negative at once a going together with itself.

The immediacy that it presupposes for itself as a sublating is simply and solely as _positedness_, as something sublated _in_ _itself_, which is not distinct from the return into itself and is itself nothing but this returning. But it is at the same time determined as _negative_, as immediately _against_ a one, and so against an other. Thus reflection is _determinate_; inasmuch as, according to this determinateness, it _has_ a presupposition and begins from the immediate, which is its other, it is _external_ _reflection_.
