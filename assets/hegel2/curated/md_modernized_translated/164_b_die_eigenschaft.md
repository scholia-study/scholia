## {{{ 11.330 }}} b. Property

_Quality_ is the _immediate_ determinateness belonging to something; the negative itself by virtue of which being is a something. Just so, the thing's _property_ is the negativity of reflection whereby concrete existence is at all something concretely existing and, as simple identity with itself, is _thing-in-itself_. The negativity of reflection, the sublated mediation, is however essentially itself mediation, and reference – not to an other in general, in the way that quality is the unreflected determinateness, but reference _to_ _itself_ as to an other; or _mediation_ that immediately is _just_ _as_ _much_ _identity_ _with_ _itself_. The abstract thing in itself is itself this comportment returning into itself out of another; thereby it is _determined_ _in_ _its_ _own_ _self_; but its determinateness is _constitution_, which as such is itself _determination_ and which, being comportment towards another, does _not_ _pass_ _over_ into otherness and is _withdrawn_ from _alteration_.

A thing has _properties_; these are, _in_ _the_ _first_ _place_, its determinate references to what is _other_; property is on hand only as a manner of comporting towards one another; it is accordingly external reflection, the side of the thing's positedness. But, _in_ _the_ _second_ _place_, in this positedness the thing is _in_ _itself_; it preserves itself in the reference to another; admittedly it is thus only a surface with which concrete existence gives itself over to the becoming of being and to alteration, yet in that surface the property does not get lost. A thing possesses the property of bringing about this or that in the other and of expressing itself in its reference after a fashion of its own. It furnishes proof of this property only on condition of an answering constitution in the other thing, yet the property is at once _peculiar_ to it and its own groundwork identical with itself; – this reflected quality is called _property_ for that reason. Therein the thing passes over into an externality, but there the property maintains itself. Through its properties the thing turns into cause, and cause is just this, to preserve itself as effect. Here, however, the thing is as yet no more than the quiescent thing of many properties; not yet determined as actual cause; it is as yet only the in-itself reflection of its determinations, not yet itself the positing one.

As has come out, then, the _thing-in-itself_ is essentially not thing-in-itself in such fashion that its properties would be positedness of an external reflection; rather, they are determinations of its own, through which it comports itself in a determinate manner; it is no determinationless groundwork lying beyond its external concrete existence, but is on hand in its properties, as ground, which is to say, as the identity with itself in its positedness; yet at the same time as {{{ 11.331 }}} _conditioned_ ground, which is to say, its positedness is quite as much self-external reflection; only to the extent that it is external is it reflected into itself and in itself. – By way of concrete existence the thing-in-itself steps into external references; and concrete existence consists in this externality; being the immediacy of being, it subjects the thing to alteration; but it is likewise the reflected immediacy of ground, and so the thing is _in_ _itself_ throughout its alteration. – This mention of the ground-reference must not be taken here, however, as if the thing were determined generally as the ground of its properties; thinghood itself as such is the ground-determination, property is not something distinguished from its ground, nor does it merely make up the positedness, but is that ground which has passed over into its own externality and is thereby truly reflected into itself; property itself as such is the ground, positedness that is in itself, or else the ground makes up the _form_ of property's _identity_ with itself; property's _determinateness_ is the ground's self-external reflection; and the whole of it is the ground which refers itself to itself in its repelling and determining, in its external immediacy. – Essentially, therefore, the _thing-in-itself_ _concretely_ _exists_, and that it concretely exists means conversely: concrete existence, as external immediacy, is at once _being-in-itself_.
