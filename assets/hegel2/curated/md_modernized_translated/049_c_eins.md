## c. One

Being-for-itself constitutes the simple unity of its own self with its moment, with being-for-one. Present in it is only one determination, the relating-to-self of sublating. Into {{{ 21.151 }}} _undifferentiatedness_ the _moments_ of being-for-itself have collapsed together, and this undifferentiatedness is immediacy or being, though an _immediacy_ resting upon the negating that stands posited as its determination. So constituted, being-for-itself is _a_ _being-for-itself_, and, since in such immediacy its inner import drops away, it is the utterly abstract limit of its own self, – _the_ _one_.

It may be pointed out in advance where the difficulty of the exposition that now follows, the exposition of the _development_ of the one, resides, and on what that difficulty rests. Inside it the _moments_ which make up the _concept_ of the one as being-for-itself fall _apart_; they are 1) negation as such, 2) _two_ negations 3) hence two of them which are the _same_, 4) which are downright opposed; 5) relating to self, identity as such, 6) a _negative_ relating and yet one _to_ _its_ _own_ _self_. The moments are driven apart here because the form of _immediacy_, of _being_, comes in upon being-for-itself as a being-for-itself; through such immediacy _each_ moment _is_ _posited_ _as_ _a_ _determination_ _of_ _its_ _own_, _one_ _that_ _is_; and yet the moments are just as much _inseparable_. Of every determination its contrary must accordingly be asserted as well; and given the abstract _constitution_ _of_ _the_ _moments_, it is this contradiction that constitutes the difficulty.
