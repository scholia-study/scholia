## C. The Mode of the Absolute

_Firstly_, the attribute is the absolute as it stands in the simple _identity_ of itself with itself. _Secondly_, the attribute is _negation_, and this _as_ negation is the formal reflection-into-itself. These two sides constitute at first the attribute's two _extremes_, and the attribute is itself their _middle_ _term_, being as it is the absolute and equally the determinateness. – Of these two extremes the second is the _negative_ as _negative_, the reflection that is _external_ to the absolute. – Or insofar as this reflection is taken as the _inner_ of the absolute, and the absolute's _own_ determination is to posit itself as mode, then the mode is the absolute's being-outside-itself, the loss of itself into the alterability and contingency of being, its having-passed-over into the opposite _without_ _return_ into itself; the totality-less manifoldness of determinations of form and of content. –

The mode, the _externality_ of the absolute, is however not this alone, but the externality _posited_ as such externality, a bare _way_ _and_ _manner_; and so the shine as shine, or _the_ _form's_ _reflection_ _into_ _itself_; and so _the_ _identity_ _with_ _itself_ _that_ _the_ _absolute_ _is_. In fact, then, it is first in the mode that the absolute stands posited as absolute identity; it is nothing but what it {{{ 11.375 }}} _is_, namely identity with itself, as negativity that refers itself to itself, as _shining_ posited _as_ _shining_.

Insofar, then, as the _exposition_ of the absolute takes its start from the absolute's absolute identity, moves on to the attribute, and thence to the mode, in all this it has traversed its moments completely. But firstly, therein it does not merely comport itself negatively towards these determinations; rather this _doing_ _of_ _its_ is the _reflecting_ _movement_ _itself_, as which the _absolute_ _only_ _truly_ is the _absolute_ _identity_. – _Secondly_, what it thereby has to do with is not merely something _external_, and the mode is not just the outermost externality; because it is the shine as shine, it is rather the return into itself, the reflection that dissolves itself, as which the absolute is absolute being. – _Thirdly_, the expounding reflection has the look of setting out from determinations of its own and from something external; of picking up the modes, or likewise the determinations of the attribute, as _found_ already outside the absolute; and of having for its doing merely the carrying of them back into the indifferent identity. But in truth the determinateness with which it begins it already has on the absolute itself. For as _first_ indifferent identity the absolute is itself nothing but the _determinate_ _absolute_, or the attribute, in that it is the absolute unmoved and as yet unreflected. Since it is determinateness, this _determinateness_ pertains to the reflecting movement; through that movement alone is the absolute determined as the _first_ _identical_, and through it alone likewise does the absolute possess the absolute form, so that it is not what is _self-equal_ but what is _self-equating_.

The genuine meaning of the mode is accordingly that it is the absolute's own reflecting movement; a _determining_, yet not one by which the absolute would come to be an _other_, but only of what it already _is_; the transparent externality which is the _showing_ of the absolute itself; a movement _out_ of itself; and yet such that this being-turned-outward is quite as much inwardness itself; and hence quite as much a positing that is not mere positedness but absolute being.

If, then, the question is put about a _content_ of the exposition, _what_ it might be that the absolute shows? the difference of form and content is in the absolute already dissolved anyway. Or just this is the content of the absolute, _to_ _manifest_ _itself_. The absolute is that absolute form which, in dirempting itself, remains simply identical with itself, the negative _as_ negative; or which closes together with itself, and only so is the absolute self-identity that is quite as much _indifferent_ _towards_ _its_ _differences_, or absolute _content_; the content, accordingly, is nothing but this exposition itself.

The absolute, as this self-sustaining movement of exposition, as the _way_ and _manner_ which is its absolute identity with itself, is expression – not of an inner, not against an other – but is only as an absolute manifesting of itself for itself; thus it is _actuality_.
