## Remark 3

Now that the first determinations of reflection — identity, diversity, and opposition — have each been set out in a principle, all the more ought the one into which they pass over as into their truth, namely _contradiction_, be caught in a principle too and stated thus: _all_ _things_ _are_ _in_ _their_ _own_ _selves_ _contradictory_, and this in the sense that, as against the rest, it is this principle which gives voice to the truth and the essence of things. – Contradiction, as it comes forward at opposition, is merely the developed nothing that identity already harboured and that came up in the phrase according to which the principle of identity says _nothing_. This negation goes on to determine itself into diversity and into opposition, and opposition is now contradiction posited.

Yet it belongs to the basic prejudices of logic as it has stood hitherto, and of the ordinary way of representing, to suppose that contradiction is not a determination as essential and as immanent as identity is; indeed, were rank at issue, and had both determinations to be kept apart, contradiction would be the one to count as the deeper and the more essential. For identity, set beside it, is no more than the determination of what is simply immediate, of dead being, whereas contradiction is the root of every movement and of vitality; a thing stirs, has drive and activity, only insofar as it carries a contradiction within it.

At first contradiction is customarily kept away from things, from whatever is, and from the true as such; the assertion runs _that_ _there_ _is_ _nothing_ _contradictory_. Secondly it is shoved instead into subjective reflection, {{{ 11.287 }}} which is said only to posit it by referring and comparing. But not even in this reflection, so it is claimed, is contradiction properly at hand, since what is _contradictory_ admits neither of being _represented_ nor of being _thought_. Whether in the actual or in thinking reflection, it passes generally for a contingency, for something like an abnormality, a passing fit of illness.

As for the assertion that contradiction does not _exist_, that it is nothing at hand, we need give no heed to such an assurance; an absolute determination of essence has to be met with in every experience, in everything actual as in every concept. The same reminder was already given above with the _infinite_, which is contradiction as it shows itself within the sphere of being. Common experience of itself declares, though, that _a_ _multitude_ at least of contradictory things _exists_, of contradictory institutions and the like, whose contradiction is at hand not merely in some external reflection but within them. Nor, moreover, is contradiction to be taken merely for an abnormality cropping up here and there; it is the negative in its essential determination, the principle of all self-movement — a movement that amounts to nothing beyond a presentation of contradiction. External, sensible movement is its immediate existence. A thing moves, not by being here in this now and there in another now, but by being, within one selfsame now, here and not here, by at once being and not being in this here. The contradictions which the ancient dialecticians exhibited in movement must be granted them, yet what follows is not that on that account movement is not, but rather that movement is _existent_ contradiction itself.

Just so, inner or genuine self-movement, _drive_ as such (a monad's appetite or nisus, the entelechy of the absolutely simple essence), amounts to nothing else than that something is, under one and the same respect, _in_ _its_ _own_ _self_ _and_ the lack, _the_ _negative_ _of_ _itself_. Abstract identity with self is not yet vitality; it is because the positive is negativity in its own self that it steps outside itself and puts itself into alteration. A thing is alive, then, only insofar as it holds contradiction within itself, and is indeed the strength to seize contradiction and to bear it. Where something concretely existing is incapable, in its positive determination, of reaching over its negative at the same time and of holding the one fast within the other, incapable of having contradiction in it, there it is not the living unity itself, not ground, but founders in contradiction. – _Speculative_ _thinking_ consists in nothing but this, that thinking holds fast to contradiction and, within it, to itself, and not in letting itself be ruled by contradiction, as befalls representing, letting contradiction dissolve its determinations merely into others or into nothing.

{{{ 11.288 }}} While in movement, in drive, and the like contradiction lies veiled for representation beneath the _simplicity_ of these determinations, in the _determinations_ _of_ _relation_ contradiction exhibits itself without any veil. The most trivial examples — of above and below, of right and left, of father and son, and so on to infinity — every one of them holds the opposition within one term. Above _is_ what is _not_ below; above is determined as just this, not to be below, and it _is_ only _insofar_ _as_ a below is; and the reverse holds too; within the one determination lies its own contrary. The father is the son's other and the son the father's other, and neither is except as being this other of the other one; at the same time the one determination is only in reference to the other; their being is a single subsistence. Apart from the reference to a son the father is also something for himself; but so taken he is no father but a man in general; as above and below, right and left, are likewise reflected into themselves, something apart from the reference; but so taken merely places in general. – Terms opposed harbour contradiction insofar as, under the same respect, they refer to one another negatively, or _mutually_ _sublate_ one another, and are yet _indifferent_ towards one another. Representation, in going over to the moment at which the determinations are _indifferent_, therein forgets the negative unity they have and so holds on to them merely as diverse ones in general, a determination in which right is right no longer, left left no longer, and so forth. But in actually having right and left before it, it has these determinations before it as negating themselves, each in the other, and in this same unity as not negating themselves, each rather being for itself indifferently.

Representing thus has contradiction for its content everywhere, no doubt, yet never arrives at a consciousness of it; it stays external reflection, which passes over from likeness to unlikeness, or from the negative reference to the being-reflected-into-self of what has been distinguished. It sets these two determinations externally over against one another and has _them_ _alone_ before its mind, not the _passing_ _over_, which is the essential thing and holds contradiction in it. – _Ingenious_ reflection, to bring it up here, consists on the contrary in apprehending and enunciating contradiction. Though it fails to express the _concept_ of things and of their relations, and has determinations of representation alone for its material and its content, it does bring these into a reference which holds their contradiction and lets _their_ _concept_ _shine_ _through_ it. – _Thinking_ reason, however, sharpens to a point, so to speak, the blunted difference of the diverse, the mere manifoldness of representation, making of it an _essential_ difference, an _opposition_. Only when driven to the pitch of contradiction do the manifold terms grow stirring and alive towards one another, gaining in contradiction that negativity which is the indwelling pulsation of self-movement and vitality.

{{{ 11.289 }}} With respect to the _ontological_ _proof_ of the _existence_ _of_ _God_ it has already been recalled that the determination laid there at the ground is _the_ _sum_ _total_ _of_ _all_ _realities_. Of this determination it is the custom to show first that it is _possible_, on the score that it holds no _contradiction_, reality being taken merely as reality without restrictions. The reminder was given that the sum total thereby becomes being simple and indeterminate, or, where the realities are in fact taken as a plurality of determinate ones, the sum total of all negations. Take the difference of reality more closely and it turns from diversity into opposition and so into contradiction, the sum total of all realities as such turning into absolute contradiction within itself. The usual horror which representational, non-speculative thinking feels before contradiction, as nature feels before a vacuum, throws this consequence out; for it halts at the one-sided consideration of the _dissolution_ of contradiction into _nothing_ and does not recognize contradiction's positive side, the side on which it becomes _absolute_ _activity_ and absolute ground.

From the consideration of the nature of contradiction it has emerged generally that it is not yet as such, so to speak, a harm, a lack, or a flaw in a matter if a contradiction can be shown up in it. On the contrary, every determination, everything concrete, every concept is essentially a unity of moments distinguished and distinguishable which pass over, through the _determinate_, _essential_ _difference_, into contradictory ones. This contradictory element does indeed dissolve into nothing; it withdraws into its negative unity. And the thing, the subject, the concept is just this negative unity itself; it is something contradictory in its own self, but quite as much the _resolved_ _contradiction_; it is the _ground_ which holds and carries its determinations. Reflected into itself within its sphere, the thing, the subject, or the concept is its resolved contradiction; but this whole sphere of it is once more a _determinate_, a _diverse_ one; so it is finite, and that is to say _contradictory_. Of that higher contradiction the sphere is not itself the resolution; for its negative unity, for its ground, it has a higher sphere instead. Hence finite things in their indifferent manifoldness are quite generally this: contradictory in their own selves, _fractured_ _within_ _themselves_ _and_ _bound_ _to_ _return_ _into_ _their_ _ground_. – As will be considered later, the true inference from something finite and contingent to an absolutely necessary essence lies not in inferring the absolutely necessary from the finite and the contingent taken as the _being_ _which_ _lies_ _at_ _the_ _ground_ and _keeps_ _lying_ _there_, but in inferring it from a being that merely falls, from a _being_ _self-contradictory_ _in_ _its_ _own_ _self_ — which is likewise what lies immediately in _contingency_ — or rather in showing that contingent being goes in its own self back into its ground, {{{ 11.290 }}} wherein it sublates itself, and further that by this return it posits the ground only so as to make itself the posited instead. In ordinary inference the finite's _being_ appears as the ground of the absolute: because a finite _is_, the absolute is. The truth, though, is that the absolute is because the finite is the opposition self-contradictory in its own self, because it _is_ _not_. Taken the first way, the proposition of the inference runs: the finite's _being_ is the absolute's _being_; taken the second way it runs: the finite's _non-being_ is the absolute's _being_.
