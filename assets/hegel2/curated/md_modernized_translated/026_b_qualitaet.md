## b. Quality

On account of the immediacy in which being and nothing are one within existence, neither reaches out beyond the other; just so far as existence is a being, just so far is it non-being, is it determinate. Neither is being the _universal_, nor determinateness the _particular_. From _being_ determinateness has _not_ yet _detached_ itself; nor indeed will it detach itself from being any more; for what now lies at the base as the true is the unity of non-being with being; and upon this as base all further determinations emerge. Yet determinateness stands here in a reference to being which is the immediate unity of the pair, so that no distinguishing of the two has as yet been posited.

Determinateness thus isolated for itself, as determinateness _that_ _is_, is _quality_; – something wholly simple, immediate. _Determinateness_ at large is the more universal term, one that may equally be the quantitative, or determinate in some further way. Owing to this simplicity there is nothing further to say of quality as such.

But existence, wherein nothing is contained quite as much as being, is itself the measure for the one-sidedness of quality as merely _immediate_ determinateness, or determinateness that merely _is_. In the determination of nothing quality is equally to be posited, whereby the immediate determinateness, the determinateness that _is_, gets posited as a differentiated, reflected one; and nothing, so taken as the determinate element of a determinateness, is likewise something reflected, a _negating_. Quality, taken as differentiated and passing for that which _is_, is _reality_; quality burdened with a negating, _negation_ at large, is likewise a quality, though one passing for a lack, and it determines itself further on as limit, restriction. {{{ 21.99 }}} An existence is what both are; but in _reality_, quality carrying the accent of being one that _is_, it stays hidden that reality holds determinateness and hence negation too within it; reality accordingly passes merely for something positive, from which negating, limitedness, lack are supposedly shut out. Were negation taken as mere lack, it would be what nothing is; an existence, though, is what it is, a quality determined merely with a non-being.
