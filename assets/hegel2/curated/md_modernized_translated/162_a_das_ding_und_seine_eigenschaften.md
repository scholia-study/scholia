## {{{ 11.327 }}} A. The Thing and Its Properties

Concrete existence, qua _something_ _concretely_ _existing_, stands posited in the form of that negative unity which it essentially is. At first, though, this negative unity is only an _immediate_ determination, and so the one belonging to _something_ at large. The concretely existing something, however, differs from the something that is. Essentially the former is an immediacy such as has come about through mediation's reflection into its own self. And so the concretely existing something is a _thing_.

One distinguishes the _thing_ from its _concrete_ _existence_ much as the _something_ may be distinguished from its _being_. Immediately, thing and what concretely exists are one and the same. Yet because concrete existence is not being's first immediacy, but carries the moment of mediation about it, the determining of concrete existence into thing, and the distinguishing of the two, is no transition but properly an analysis; and this distinguishing is itself contained in concrete existence as such, within the moment of its mediation — the difference of _thing-in-itself_ from _outward_ concrete existence.
