## b. The Particular Judgment

That non-singularity of the subject which must be posited in place of its singularity in the first judgment of reflection is _particularity_. Singularity, however, stands determined in the judgment of reflection as _essential_ _singularity_; particularity therefore cannot be some _simple_, _abstract_ determination wherein the singular would stand sublated and whatever concretely exists would have foundered, but only an expansion of that singular in external reflection; hence the subject reads: _some_ _thises_, or a _particular_ _multitude_ _of_ _singulars_.

The judgment _some_ _singulars_ _are_ _a_ _universal_ _of_ _reflection_ looks at first like a positive judgment, and yet is negative every bit as much; for _some_ harbours universality; on that score it may be regarded as _comprehensive_; but so far as it is particularity, it fails just as much to match universality. As was shown above, the _negative_ determination which the subject has acquired through the singular judgment's transition is a determination of the reference too, of the copula. – Lodged in the judgment _some_ _human_ _beings_ _are_ _happy_ _is_ _the_ _immediate_ _consequence_: _some_ _human_ _beings_ _are_ _not_ _happy_. Granted that _some_ _things_ _are_ _useful_, then for that very reason _some_ _things_ _are_ _not_ _useful_. Positive and negative judgment no longer fall asunder; the particular judgment holds both together at once, immediately, precisely on account of its being a judgment of reflection. – But for that reason the particular judgment is _indeterminate_.

Take the subject further, in the example of such a judgment, as _some_ _human_ _beings_, _animals_ and so forth: besides the particular form-determination _some_, it holds within it the content-determination _human_ _being_ and so forth. For the singular judgment the subject could read _this_ _human_ _being_, a singularity that belongs properly to outward showing; better, then, that it should run, say, _Cajus_. Yet _some_ _Caji_ can no longer serve as the subject of the particular judgment; for Cajus is meant to be a singular as such. _To_ _the_ _some_, therefore, a more universal _content_ gets attached, say _human_ _beings_, _animals_ and so forth. Such a content is not merely empirical but one determined through the judgment's form; namely, it is a _universal_, since _some_ harbours _universality_, and this universality must at the same time stand apart from the singulars, reflected singularity lying at the base. More precisely, it is also the _universal_ _nature_, or the _genus_ _human_ _being_, _animal_; – thus anticipating that universality which is the outcome {{{ 12.74 }}} of the judgment of reflection, much as the positive judgment, having _the_ _singular_ for its subject, anticipated the determination that is the outcome of the judgment of existence.

To that extent the subject, holding within it the singulars, their reference to particularity, and the universal nature, already stands posited as the totality of the determinations of the concept. Such a consideration, however, is properly an external one. What its form posits in the subject, in the first instance and in _reference_ of the terms to one another, is the _expansion_ _of_ _the_ _this_ into particularity; only, this universalizing does not match the this; _this_ is something perfectly determinate, whereas _some_ _this_ is undetermined. The expansion is meant to attach to the this, hence to be _perfectly_ _determinate_ in conformity with it; and such is totality, or in the first instance _universality_ at large.

Underlying this universality is the _this_, for the singular is here what is reflected into itself; the further determinations of the singular therefore run their course upon it _externally_, and just as particularity for that reason _determined_ itself as _some_, so the universality the subject has gained is _allness_, and with that the particular judgment has passed over into the _universal_ one.
