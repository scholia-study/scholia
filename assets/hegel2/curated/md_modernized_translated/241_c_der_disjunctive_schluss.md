## c. The Disjunctive Syllogism

Just as the hypothetical syllogism falls on the whole under the schema of the second figure, U–S–P, so the disjunctive falls under the schema of the third figure of the formal syllogism: S–U–P. Its middle term, however, is _universality_ _filled_ _out_ _with_ _the_ _form_; it has determined itself as the _totality_, as _developed_ {{{ 12.124 }}} <i>ob</i>jective universality. The Medius Terminus is on that account universality no less than particularity and singularity. In the first respect it is, to begin with, the substantial identity of the genus, but, secondly, an identity into which _particularity_ has been _taken_ _up_, and taken up _as_ _its_ _equal_, hence a universal sphere holding within it the whole of its particularization, – the genus laid apart into its species; A which is _both_ _B_ _and_ _C_ _and_ _D_ at once. Yet particularization, being differentiation, is quite as much the _either-or_ of B, C and D, _negative_ unity, _the_ _reciprocal_ shutting out of the determinations. – This shutting out, further, is now not merely reciprocal, nor the determination merely a relative one; it is quite as essentially a determination that _refers_ _to_ _itself_; the particular as _singularity_, to the exclusion of the _rest_.

A is either B or C or D

A, however, is B

Therefore A is not C nor D.

or again:

A is either B or C or D

A, however, is not C nor D

Therefore it is B.

Not in the two premises alone is A subject, but in the conclusion as well. In the first it is a universal, and in its predicate the _universal_ sphere particularized into the totality of its species; in the second it stands as something _determinate_, or as one species; in the conclusion it is posited as the shutting-out, _singular_ determinateness. – Or else, already in the minor premise it stands as shutting-out singularity, and in the conclusion is affirmatively posited as the determinate thing which it is.

What thus comes forward on the whole as the _mediated_ is the _universality_ of A along with _singularity_. What _mediates_, though, is this same A, which is at once the _universal_ sphere of its particularizations and something determined as _singular_. The truth of the hypothetical syllogism, namely the unity of what mediates and what is mediated, stands accordingly _posited_ in the disjunctive syllogism, which for just that reason is quite as much _no_ _syllogism_ any more. For the middle term posited in it as the totality of the concept has both extremes itself, in their full determinateness. Set off against this middle term, the extremes are only a positedness to which no determinateness of its own accrues over against the middle any longer.

Considered once more with more determinate regard to the hypothetical syllogism: there was at hand in the latter a _substantial_ _identity_, serving as the _inner_ bond of necessity, and, distinguished from it, a _negative_ _unity_ – namely the activity or the form which carried one existence over into another. Throughout, the disjunctive syllogism stands in the determination of {{{ 12.125 }}} _universal__ity_; its middle term is A as _genus_ and as something completely _determinate_; by virtue of this unity that formerly inner content is _posited_ too, while conversely the positedness or the form is not an outward negative unity confronting an indifferent existence, but is identical with that solid content. The concept's whole form-determination stands posited in its determinate difference and at once in the simple identity of the concept.

Herewith the _formalism_ _of_ _syllogizing_, and with it the subjectivity of syllogism and of concept generally, has sublated itself. What was formal or subjective consisted in this, that what mediates the extremes, the concept, is an _abstract_ determination and thereby _diverse_ from those extremes whose unity it is. In the syllogism's completion, on the contrary, where objective universality stands posited quite as much as the totality of the form-determinations, the difference between what mediates and what is mediated has dropped away. What is mediated is itself an essential moment of that which mediates it, and every moment is as the totality of the mediated.

Each determinateness of the concept is exhibited _singly_ by the figures of the syllogism as the middle term, which is at the same time the concept as _ought_, as the demand that what mediates be its own totality. The various kinds of syllogism, on the other hand, exhibit the degrees of _fulfilment_ or concretion of the middle term. Within the formal syllogism the middle is posited as totality only in this way, that every determinateness, each of them however _singly_, passes through the office of mediating. Within the syllogisms of reflection the middle is that unity which gathers the determinations of the extremes together _externally_. Within the syllogism of necessity it has determined itself into a unity at once developed and total and simple, and thereby the form of the syllogism, which consisted in the middle's difference from its extremes, has sublated itself.

Herewith the concept generally has been realized; more determinately, it has won a reality of the sort that is _objectivity_. The _nearest_ _reality_ lay in this, that the _concept_, being the inwardly negative unity, divides itself asunder, positing as _judgment_ its determinations in a difference determinate and indifferent, and in the syllogism placing its own self opposite them. Being thus still what is inward to this its outwardness, the outwardness gets evened out with the inward unity through the course of the syllogisms; the various determinations return into this unity by way of the mediation wherein at first they are one only in a third, and thereby the outwardness exhibits the concept on its own self, a concept which herewith is quite as much no longer distinguished from it as an inward unity.

That determination of the concept, however, which has been looked at as _reality_, is inversely quite as much a _positedness_. For it is not in this result alone that {{{ 12.126 }}} the identity of the concept's inwardness and outwardness has exhibited itself as its truth; already in the judgment the concept's moments, even in their indifference towards one another, remain determinations which have their significance only in their reference. The syllogism is _mediation_, the concept complete in its _positedness_. Its movement is the sublating of this mediation, wherein nothing is in and for itself, but each thing only by means of an other. What results is on that account an _immediacy_ that has come forth by the _sublating_ _of_ _mediation_, a _being_ quite as much identical with the mediation and the concept that has brought itself forth out of and within its otherness. Such _being_, then, is a _fact_ which is _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, – _objectivity_.
