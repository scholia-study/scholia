## c. The Complete Ground

1. Within the real ground, the ground as content and the ground as reference alike are no more than _bases_. The former is only _posited_ to be essential and to be ground; the reference, for its part, is the _something_ belonging to the grounded, an indeterminate substrate carrying a content of differing kinds, a linkage of that content which is no reflection of its own but comes from outside and hence is merely posited. What the real ground-reference amounts to is therefore rather ground in a sublated state, so that it makes up instead the side of the _grounded_, or of _positedness_. Being positedness, though, the ground has by now retreated into a ground of its own; it has become something grounded which possesses _another_ _ground_. That further ground receives thereby the following determination: _first_, it is _identical_ with the real ground taken as its grounded, and on this score the very same content belongs to either side, so that both content-determinations, together with the linkage they have in the something, turn up in the new ground as well. But _secondly_, into this new ground that merely posited outward linkage has been sublated, and the new ground, being the linkage's reflection into itself, is the _absolute_ _reference_ holding between the two content-determinations.

Since the real ground has retreated into a ground of its own, there is reinstated upon it the identity that ground and grounded have, which is to say the formal ground. For that reason the ground-reference now arisen is the _complete_ one: it harbours formal and real ground at once, and it mediates those content-determinations that confronted each other immediately in the real ground.

2. Here, then, is the closer determination the ground-reference has given itself. _First_: something has a ground, and it carries in it _the_ _content-determination_ that constitutes the _ground_, plus a _second_ one as _posited_ through the first. Yet these being indifferent content, neither is the one ground upon its own self nor the other upon its own self grounded by the first; that _reference_ is instead, within the immediacy of the content, sublated or posited, and as such it grounds itself in an _other_. Differing from the first only in point of form, this second reference carries the same content as the first — the two content-determinations, that is — yet it is their _immediate_ linkage. But since what gets linked is throughout content of differing kinds, determination therefore indifferent to its counterpart, this is not the truly absolute reference between them, which would have one determination be the self-identical element within positedness and the other be nothing but that positedness of the identical; instead a something bears the reference and constitutes it as unreflected, {{{ 11.313 }}} as merely immediate, so that it counts only as relative ground over against the linkage found in the other something. So the _two_ _somethings_ are the pair of distinguished references of content which have come out. Standing in the identical ground-reference of form, they are a single _whole_ _content_ — the pair of content-determinations plus the reference between them — and what distinguishes them is nothing but the mode of that reference, immediate in the one, posited in the other, whereby the one diverges from the other in point of _form_ alone, as ground and grounded. – _Secondly_: this ground-reference is real, and not formal merely. As has shown itself, formal ground passes over into real ground; the moments of form reflect into themselves; they amount to a self-subsistent content, and so a _content_ peculiar _as_ _ground_ and one peculiar _as_ _grounded_ belong to the ground-reference too. To begin with, what constitutes the _immediate_ identity of the formal ground's two sides is the content, and so a single content belongs to both. Form, though, adheres to the content upon its own self, which makes the _content_ twofold, standing to itself as ground and as grounded. Hence, of the two content-determinations belonging to the pair of somethings, one gets determined as being not just shared between them upon outward comparison, but as the substrate identical to both and as the base upon which their reference rests. Set against the second content-determination it counts as the essential one and grounds that second as posited — namely within the something whose reference is the grounded reference. Within the first something, the one that is the ground-reference, this second content-determination too stands linked immediately, and _in_ _itself_, with the first determination. The second something, by contrast, carries the one determination only _in_ _itself_, as the point where it is immediately identical with the first something, while it carries the second as posited within it. What makes the first content-determination ground of the second is its _original_ linkage, inside the first something, with that other content-determination.

Thus the _ground-reference_ that the content-determinations have inside the second something stands _mediated_ by the first, in-itself reference belonging to the first something. Here is the syllogism: since inside one something the determination B stands linked in itself with the determination A, B stands linked as well inside the second something, to which A alone belongs immediately. Inside the second something it is not merely this second determination that comes mediated; that the something's immediate determination should be ground comes mediated too, namely by the original reference which that determination bears to B inside the first something. Such a reference is accordingly ground of the ground A, and the _whole_ ground-reference lies [in] the second something as posited, or grounded.

3. Real ground exhibits the ground's _reflection_ turned _external_ to itself; and the ground's complete mediation consists in re-establishing the identity {{{ 11.314 }}} it has with itself. Yet since this identity has along with it taken on the outwardness proper to real ground, the formal ground-reference, in this unity of itself with real ground, is a ground positing itself quite as much as _sublating_ itself; the ground-reference mediates itself with itself _through_ _its_ _negation_. To begin with, ground is the _original_ _reference_, a reference between immediate content-determinations. Being essential form, the ground-reference has for its two sides such as are sublated ones, or moments. Accordingly, as the form of _immediate_ determinations, it is the self-identical reference and, at the same time, reference of _its_ _negation_; whence it is ground not in and for its own self, but as referring to the ground-reference _sublated_. – Secondly, the sublated reference — the immediate, that is, which within the original and within the posited reference alike is the identical _base_ — is likewise not real ground in and for its own self; on the contrary, its being ground is something posited through that original linkage. –

In its totality, therefore, the ground-reference is essentially a _presupposing_ reflection: formal ground presupposes the _immediate_ content-determination, while the latter, as real ground, presupposes form. So ground is form taken as immediate linkage, yet in such fashion that form thrusts itself away from itself, presupposing immediacy instead, and within it refers to itself as to an other. That immediate is the content-determination, the simple _ground_; but qua this, qua ground namely, it has likewise been thrust away from itself and refers to itself, again, as to an other. – The total ground-reference has determined itself in this way into _conditioning_ _mediation_.
