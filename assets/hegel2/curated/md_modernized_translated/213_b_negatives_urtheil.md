## b. The Negative Judgment

1. The ordinary representation has already been spoken of above, namely that whether a judgment is true or not hangs on its content alone, seeing that logical truth is said to bear on the form only and to require nothing beyond that this content be free of self-contradiction. Nothing is reckoned to the form of the judgment itself beyond its being the reference of _two_ concepts. Yet it has come out that these two concepts do not carry the merely relationless determination of an _amount_; rather they comport themselves as _singular_ and _universal_. Such determinations constitute the genuinely logical _content_, and in this abstraction the content of the positive judgment; whatever else by way of _content_ (_the_ _sun_ _is_ _round_, _Cicero_ _was_ _a_ _great_ _orator_ _at_ _Rome_, _it_ _is_ _day_ _now_, and so forth) turns up in a judgment is nothing to the judgment as such; all it pronounces is this: _the_ _subject_ _is_ _predicate_, or, these being mere names, more determinately: _the_ _singular_ _is_ _universal_ _and_ _conversely_. – For the sake of this _purely_ _logical_ _content_ the positive judgment is _not_ _true_; its truth lies in the negative judgment. – The content, so runs the demand, has only to avoid self-contradiction in the judgment; but in that judgment it does contradict itself, as has been shown. – It is, though, wholly a matter of indifference if one chooses to call that logical content form as well and to mean by content only the further empirical filling; the form then holds more in it than the empty identity beyond which the determination of content {{{ 12.65 }}} would fall. By its _form_ as positive judgment, then, the positive judgment possesses no truth; anyone who gave the name _truth_ to the _correctness_ of an _intuition_ or a _perception_, to the accord of the _representation_ with its object, would at least be left without a word for what philosophy has as its subject matter and its purpose. This last would at any rate have to be called truth of reason, and it will readily be conceded that judgments like this, that Cicero was a great orator, that it is day now, and so forth, are not truths of reason. They are not so, however, not because they happen as it were by chance to carry an empirical content, but because they are merely positive judgments, and such judgments neither can nor should have any content other than an immediately singular and an abstract determinateness.

In the first place the positive judgment has its truth in the negative one: _the_ _singular_ _is_ _not_ abstractly _universal_, – _but_ _rather_, since the predicate of the singular is a predicate of that sort, or, viewed for itself apart from the reference to the subject, since it is an _abstract_-universal, that predicate is itself something determinate; hence the _singular_ is _at_ _first_ a _particular_. Again, going by the other proposition that lies in the positive judgment, the negative one reads: the _universal_ is not abstractly _singular_, _but_ _rather_ this predicate, just because it is predicate, or because it stands referred to a universal subject, is something broader than bare singularity, so that the _universal_ likewise is _at_ _first_ a _particular_. – Now because this universal, as subject, is itself under the judgment-determination of singularity, the two propositions shrink to one: _the_ _singular_ _is_ _a_ _particular_.

Two things may be noticed, a) that _particularity_ turns up here for the predicate, the particularity spoken of a little while back; only here it is not put there by an outward reflection but has arisen by way of the negative reference pointed out in the judgment. b) Only for the predicate does this determination turn up here. Within the _immediate_ judgment, the judgment of existence, the subject is what underlies; so the _determination_ looks at first as though it _ran_ _its_ _course_ upon the _predicate_. In truth, however, this first negation can as yet be no determination, or strictly no _positing_ _of_ _the_ _singular_, since the singular is only the second thing, the negative of the negative.

_The_ _singular_ _is_ _a_ _particular_: such is the _positive_ way of putting the negative judgment. So far as the positive judgment, by reason of its immediacy, has none but the abstract for its extremes, while the particular arises just through the positing of the judgment's reference as the first _mediated_ determination, this way of putting it is not itself positive judgment. – This determination, though, has to be taken not merely as {{{ 12.66 }}} moment of the extreme, but also, as strictly it first is, as _determination_ of the _reference_; that is, the judgment must be looked at as _negative_ too.

The ground of this transition lies in the relation of the extremes and of their reference within the judgment generally. What the positive judgment references is the _immediately_ singular and the universal, terms therefore of which the one at once _is_ _not_ what the other is; that reference is accordingly quite as essentially _separation_, or _negative_; which is why the positive judgment had to be posited as negative. Logicians had therefore no ground for such a to-do over the _not_ of the negative judgment being pulled across into the _copula_. Whatever in the judgment counts as _determination_ of the extreme counts equally as _determinate_ _reference_. The judgment-determination, or the extreme, is not that purely qualitative determination of _immediate_ being which is to be set over against only an _other_ _outside_ itself. Nor is it a determination of reflection, whose universal form has it behaving as positive and negative, each of these posited as exclusive and identical with the other only _in_ _itself_. Being a concept-determination, the judgment-determination is in its own self a universal, posited as _continuing_ itself over into its other. The judgment's _reference_, conversely, carries the very determination that the extremes carry; for it is just this universality of theirs and their continuation into one another; and so far as they stand distinguished, negativity attaches to the reference as well.

The transition given above from the form of _reference_ to the form of _determination_ yields the _immediate_ _consequence_ that the copula's _not_ has equally to be reckoned to the predicate, and this to be determined as the _not-universal_. By a consequence just as immediate, however, the not-universal is the _particular_. – Hold the _negative_ fast in the entirely abstract determination of immediate _non-being_, and the predicate is nothing but the _wholly_ _indeterminate_ not-universal. Logic otherwise treats of this determination in connection with _contradictory_ concepts, impressing it as a matter of weight that with the _negative_ of a concept one should keep to the negative alone and take it for the merely _indeterminate_ scope of what is _other_ than the positive concept. So the bare _not-white_ would be red, yellow, blue, and so forth every bit as much as black. But _white_ as such is that determination of intuition which is _without_ _concept_; the _not_ of white is then a _non-being_ equally without concept, an abstraction looked at right at the outset of the logic, whose nearest truth was there recognized as _becoming_. Where, in examining the determinations of the judgment, conceptless content of this kind out of intuition and representation gets used for illustration, {{{ 12.67 }}} and the determinations of _being_ and those of _reflection_ are passed off as determinations of the judgment, the procedure is just as _uncritical_ as applying, on Kant's reckoning, the concepts of the understanding to the infinite idea of reason or to the so-called _thing-in-itself_; for the _concept_, and with it the _judgment_ that proceeds from it, is the genuine _thing-in-itself_, or what is _rational_, whereas those determinations are at home in _being_ or in _essence_ and are forms not yet carried through into the fashion in which they stand in their truth, in the _concept_. – Stop short at white and red as _sensuous_ representations, and then, as commonly happens, something gets called concept which is no more than a determination of representation, and then of course the not-white and the not-red are nothing positive, just as the non-triangular is something altogether indeterminate, for a determination that rests on number and on quantum at large is the essentially _indifferent_ and _conceptless_ one. Yet such sensuous content, like _non-being_ itself, is meant to be _grasped_ _in_ _its_ _concept_ and to shed the indifference and abstract immediacy that belong to it in blind, motionless representation. Even in existence the thoughtless _nothing_ turns into _limit_, and by it _something_ does after all _refer_ itself to an _other_ lying outside it. In reflection, on the other hand, it is the _negative_ which _essentially_ _refers_ itself to a _positive_ and is on that account _determinate_; a negative is by then no longer that _indeterminate_ _non-being_, it is posited as being at all only through the positive standing against it, the third term being their _ground_; the negative is thus kept inside a closed sphere, within which whatever the one _is_ _not_ is something _determinate_. – Far more, though, in the absolutely fluid continuity of the concept and of its determinations, the _not_ is straightaway something positive, and _negation_ is not determinateness merely but is drawn up into universality and posited as one with it. Hence the not-universal is directly the _particular_.

2. Since the negation bears upon the judgment's reference, and the _negative_ _judgment_ is still under consideration as such, it is _to_ _begin_ _with_ _still_ _a_ _judgment_; there is accordingly a relation of subject and predicate, of singularity and universality, and a reference between them; _the_ _form_ _of_ _the_ _judgment_. Being the underlying immediate, the subject is left untouched by the negation, and so keeps its determination of having a predicate, its reference to universality. What gets negated, then, is not universality at large in the predicate, but the predicate's abstraction or determinateness, which showed itself as _content_ over against that universality. – So the negative judgment is not total negation; the universal sphere which the predicate holds still stands; the subject's reference to the predicate is therefore still essentially _positive_; and the _determination_ of the {{{ 12.68 }}} predicate that is left over is quite as much _reference_. – Say, for instance, that the rose is _not_ red: with that only the _determinateness_ of the predicate is negated and cut away from the universality that belongs to it no less; the universal sphere, _colour_, stands fast; if the rose is not red, one takes it thereby that it does have a colour, and another colour; in respect of this universal sphere the judgment remains positive.

_The_ _singular_ _is_ _a_ _particular_, – such is the positive shape of the negative judgment, and it says as much straight out; for the particular has universality in it. Over and above that it says the predicate is not a universal only, but something determinate besides. The negative shape says the same; for granted that the rose, say, is not red, still it is to keep not only the universal sphere of colour for predicate but to have _some_ _other_ _determinate_ _colour_ as well; only the _singular_ determinateness of red, then, is sublated, and not merely is the universal sphere left standing, the determinateness too is kept, though turned into an _indeterminate_ one, a universal determinateness; and thus into particularity.

3. _Particularity_, which has come out as the positive determination of the negative judgment, is what mediates between singularity and universality; and so the negative judgment is now the mediating factor generally, towards the third step, _the_ _reflection_ of the _judgment_ _of_ _existence_ _into_ _itself_. Objectively taken, it is no more than the moment of the alteration of accidents, or, in existence, of the concrete's singularized properties. By this alteration the full determinateness of the predicate, the _concrete_, comes forward as posited.

_The_ _singular_ _is_ _particular_, on the positive way of putting the negative judgment. But the singular is also _not_ particular; for particularity has a wider extent than singularity; particularity is thus a predicate not answering to the subject, and in it the subject does not yet have its truth. _The_ _singular_ _is_ _only_ _singular_, that negativity which refers itself not to something else, positively or negatively, but to its own self alone. – The rose is not _just_ _any_ coloured thing; it has only the determinate colour which is rose-colour. The singular is not something indeterminately determinate, but the determinately determinate.

Setting out from this positive shape of the negative judgment, the negation of it looks once more like a merely _first_ negation. It is not that, however. On the contrary, in and for itself the negative judgment is already the second negation, the negation of the negation; and this that it is in and for itself has now to be posited. That is to say, what it _negates_ is the positive judgment's predicate in its _determinateness_, that predicate's _abstract_ universality, or, taken as content, the singular quality of the subject which it holds. But the negation of determinateness is already the second one, and thus the infinite going back of singularity into its own self. Herewith the _restoration_ {{{ 12.69 }}} of the concrete totality of the subject has come about, or rather only now is the subject _posited_ as singular, having been mediated with itself through the negation and the sublating of that negation. On its side the predicate has thereby gone over from its first universality into absolute determinateness, and has come level with the subject. To that extent the judgment reads: _the_ _singular_ _is_ _singular_. – Looked at from the other side: the subject was quite as much to be assumed as _universal_, and to that extent the predicate, which over against that determination of the subject is the singular, _widened_ itself in the negative judgment into _particularity_; and since the negation of this _determinateness_ is furthermore just as much the _purifying_ of the universality the predicate holds, this judgment also reads: _the_ _universal_ _is_ _the_ _universal_.

In both these judgments, which earlier came out through an external reflection, the predicate stands already expressed in its positivity. First, though, the negation of the negative judgment has itself to make its appearance in the shape of a negative judgment. It showed itself that a _positive_ _reference_ of subject to predicate, and the _universal_ _sphere_ of the latter, were still left in it. On this side, therefore, it held a universality purer of limitedness than the positive judgment held, and it is on that account all the more to be negated of the subject as singular. In this manner the predicate's _whole_ _extent_ is negated, and no positive reference is left between predicate and subject. Such is the _infinite_ _judgment_.
