## Remark

The _ratio_ of a whole that is supposed to have its determinateness in the magnitude-difference of factors qualitatively determined against one another is put to use in the case of the elliptical motion of the heavenly bodies. This example, to start with, displays only two qualities in inverse ratio, and not two sides of which each would itself be the unity of the two and their inverse ratio. Given the solidity of the empirical basis, one overlooks the consequence to which the theory brought into {{{ 21.378 }}} that basis leads, namely that of destroying the underlying fact, or, if this fact is held fast as it ought to be, of exposing the emptiness of the theory over against it. Ignoring the consequence lets the fact and the theory that contradicts it subsist peaceably side by side. – The plain fact is that in the elliptical motion of the heavenly bodies their speed accelerates as they draw near the perihelion and slackens as they draw near the aphelion. The quantitative side of this fact has been determined precisely through the tireless diligence of observation, and the fact has further been traced back to its simple law and formula, so that everything has been accomplished which may genuinely be demanded of theory. But to the reflecting understanding this has not seemed enough. In order to supply what is called an explanation of the phenomenon and its law, one assumes a _centripetal_ and a _centrifugal force_ as the qualitative moments of motion in the curved line. Their qualitative difference consists in the opposition of direction, and, quantitatively regarded, in this, that since they are determined as unequal, the one is supposed to increase as the other decreases, and conversely; and then further in this, that their ratio is supposed to turn about again, so that after the centripetal force has for a while grown while the centrifugal force has waned, a point arrives where the centripetal force wanes and the centrifugal force, by contrast, grows. This representation is contradicted, however, by the relation of their essentially qualitative determinateness towards each other. By this determinateness they simply cannot be prised apart; each has meaning only with respect to the other; hence to the extent that one had a surplus over the other, to that same extent it would bear no reference to the other and would not be at hand. – On the assumption that the one is at a given time greater than the other, then, if as the greater it stood in reference to the smaller, what was said above sets in: it would acquire absolute predominance and the other would vanish; the latter is posited as the vanishing one, the one without footing, and nothing in this determination is altered by the vanishing happening only gradually, nor by the fact that _by_ _as_ _much_ as it lost in magnitude the former is supposed to gain; the former founders along with the other, since what it is, it is only insofar as the other is. It is a very simple consideration that if, {{{ 21.379 }}} for instance, as is alleged, the centripetal force of the body is to grow as the body approaches the perihelion while the centrifugal force is to diminish by just as much, then the latter _would_ _no_ _longer_ _be_ _able_ to wrest the body from the former and carry it away again from its central body; on the contrary, since the former is once granted predominance, the other is overpowered, and the body is conveyed to its central body with accelerating speed. Just as, conversely, if the centrifugal force has the upper hand in the infinite proximity of the aphelion, it is equally contradictory that in the aphelion itself it should now be overpowered by the weaker force. – It is further evident that it would take an _alien_ _force_ to bring about _this_ _reversal_, which means that the now accelerated, now retarded speed of the motion _cannot_ be _cognized_, or, as it is called, _explained_, from the assumed determination of those very factors which were assumed precisely in order to explain this difference. That one direction or the other, and with it elliptical motion as such, must consequently vanish is passed over and kept out of sight, on the strength of the settled fact that this motion endures and moves over from accelerated to retarded speed. The assumption that the weakness of the centripetal force at the aphelion turns about into a strength preponderant over the centrifugal force, and conversely at the perihelion, contains _in_ _part_ what was developed above, that each of the sides of the inverse ratio is in its own self this whole inverse ratio; for on the side where motion runs from aphelion to perihelion – the side of the centripetal force that is supposed to preponderate – the centrifugal force is still meant to be contained, though on the wane while the former waxes; in just that inverse ratio the preponderant and ever more preponderant centrifugal force is meant to stand to the centripetal force on the side of the retarded motion, so that on neither side has either of them vanished, but each only grows ever smaller until the time when it turns about into preponderance over the other. What thus recurs on either side is simply what constitutes the defect of this inverse ratio: that either each force is taken self-subsistently on its own, and with their merely _external_ convergence into a single motion, as in the parallelogram of forces, the unity of the concept, the nature of the matter, is sublated; or else that, since the two are qualitatively related to each other through the concept, neither can acquire an indifferent, self-subsistent subsistence over against the other, such as a more would apportion to it; the form of intensity, the so-called {{{ 21.380 }}} dynamic, changes nothing here, since it too holds its determinateness in the quantum and can therefore express just as much force — that is, exists just so far — as it has an opposed force standing over against it. _In_ _part_, however, that reversal out of preponderance into the opposite involves an alternating qualitative determination as positive and as negative; whatever the one gains is by the same amount the other's loss. The inseparable qualitative connection of this qualitative opposition has, in the theory, been pulled apart into a _succession_; but with that the theory still owes us the _explanation_ both of this alternation and, above all, of this pulling apart itself. The shine of unity that still lies in the increase of the one accompanied by an equal decrease of the other here vanishes entirely; what is given is a merely _external_ sequence of events, one that does nothing but contradict the consequence of that connection, according to which, once the one has become preponderant, the other must vanish.

This same ratio has been applied to attractive and repulsive force so as to comprehend how bodies differ in _density_; and an inverse ratio between sensibility and irritability was likewise meant to make comprehensible, from the inequality arising between these factors of _life_, the whole's various determinations, those of health, and also the diversity among the genera of the living. Yet the confusion and the galimatias in which this explaining — which was to become a philosophy-of-nature foundation for physiology, nosology, and then zoology — entangled itself through the uncritical use of these conceptual determinations had here the result that this formalism was soon abandoned again, whereas in the science of physical astronomy in particular it is carried on in its full extent.

Insofar as _absolute_ _indifference_ can seem to be the fundamental determination _of_ _Spinoza's_ _substance_, it may still be remarked on this head that it is indeed such in the respect that in both of them all determinations of being, and generally every further concrete distinction of thinking and extension and so forth, are posited as having vanished. It is, if one is to stop short at the abstraction, altogether a matter of indifference how that which has gone under in this abyss may have looked in its existence. But substance as indifference is bound up, for one thing, _with_ _the_ _need_ _for_ _determining_ and with _regard_ {{{ 21.381 }}} for it; it is not meant to remain Spinoza's substance, whose one and only determination is the negative one that within it everything is swallowed up. In Spinoza the difference, the attributes, thinking and extension, and then also the modes, the affects and all the remaining determinations, arrive on the scene quite empirically; it is the understanding, itself a mode, into which this distinguishing falls; the attributes stand to substance and to each other in no _further_ _determinateness_ than that they express substance entirely, and that in content — the order of things taken as extended and taken as thoughts — they coincide. With the determination of substance as indifference, however, reflection upon the _difference_ is added; the difference is now _posited_ as what it is in itself in Spinoza, namely as _external_, and hence more precisely as _quantitative_. Indifference, like substance, does indeed remain immanent to itself within that difference, – abstractly, though, only in _itself_; and the difference is _not_ immanent _to_ _it_, being rather, as quantitative, the very contrary of immanence, while quantitative indifference is rather the unity's being-outside-itself. The difference is therefore not grasped qualitatively either, and substance is not determined as what distinguishes itself, not as subject. The nearest consequence for the category of indifference as such is that within it quantitative and qualitative determination fall apart in their difference, as the development of indifference brought out; indifference is the _dissolution_ _of_ _measure_, in which both moments had been posited immediately as one.
