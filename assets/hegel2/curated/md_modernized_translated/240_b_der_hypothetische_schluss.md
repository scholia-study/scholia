## b. The Hypothetical Syllogism

1. The hypothetical judgment carries in it only the necessary _reference_, without the immediacy of the terms so referred. _If_ _A_ _is_, _then_ _B_ _is_; or again, A's being is quite as much the being of _an_ _other_, that of B; with which it is still not said, neither that _A_ _is_, nor that _B_ _is_. To this the hypothetical syllogism adjoins the _immediacy_ of being:

If A is, then B is

Now A is,

Therefore B is.

Taken for itself, the minor premise pronounces the immediate being of A.

But it is not this alone that has come to the judgment. In the syllogism the reference of subject and predicate is contained not as the abstract copula, but as the filled-out, _mediating_ unity. A's _being_ is accordingly to be taken _not_ _as_ _bare_ _immediacy_, but essentially as the _middle_ _term_ _of_ _the_ _syllogism_. This calls for closer consideration.

2. In the first instance the reference of the hypothetical judgment is _necessity_, or _inner_ _substantial_ _identity_ under an outward diversity of concrete existence, or under the mutual indifference of the being that appears; – an identical _content_ lying inwardly at the base. Hence the two sides of the judgment are not an immediate being, but a being held fast within necessity, and so at once a _sublated_ being, or a being that only appears. As sides of the judgment they stand, further, {{{ 12.122 }}} as _universality_ and _singularity_; the one is accordingly that content as _totality_ _of_ _the_ _conditions_, the other as _actuality_. Which of the sides be taken for universality and which for singularity is, however, all one. For the conditions, so long as they remain what is _inner_ and _abstract_ in an actuality, are the _universal_, and it is their _being_ _drawn_ _together_ into a _singularity_ through which they have entered upon _actuality_. Inversely, the conditions are a _singularized_, _dispersed_ appearance which first attains _unity_ and significance, and a _universally_ _valid_ _existence_, in _actuality_.

The closer relation assumed here between the two sides, that of condition to conditioned, admits of being taken equally as cause and effect, as ground and consequence; here that is all one; yet the relation of condition answers more nearly to the reference at hand in the hypothetical judgment and syllogism, in that the condition is essentially an indifferent concrete existence, whereas ground and cause pass over of themselves; the condition is besides the more universal determination, comprehending as it does both sides of those relations, since effect, consequence and so forth are quite as much condition of cause and of ground as these are of them. –

A is now the _mediating_ being: _first_ so far as it is an immediate being, an indifferent actuality, but _second_ so far as it is quite as much a being _contingent_ _in_ _its_ _own_ _self_, a being _sublating_ itself. That which carries the conditions over into the actuality of the new shape for which they are conditions lies in this, that they are not being as the abstract immediate, but _being_ _in_ _its_ _concept_, in the first place _becoming_; yet, the concept being no longer a passing over, more determinately _singularity_, as negative unity that refers itself to itself. – As conditions they are a dispersed material awaiting and demanding its employment; this _negativity_ is what mediates, the free unity of the concept. It determines itself as _activity_, this middle term being the contradiction between _objective_ _universality_, that is, the totality of the identical content, and _indifferent_ _immediacy_. – This middle term is on that account no longer merely inner, but _necessity_ _which_ _is_; objective universality carries in it the reference to itself as _simple_ _immediacy_, as being; – within the categorical syllogism this moment is at the outset a determination belonging to the extremes; measured against the objective universality of the middle term, though, it determines itself as _contingency_, and thus as something only _posited_, and sublated too, that is to say, as withdrawn into the concept or into the middle term as unity, a unity that now, in its objectivity, is itself being as well.

{{{ 12.123 }}} The conclusion, _therefore_ _B_ _is_, gives voice to the same contradiction, that B is something which _immediately_ is, and yet is quite as much through an other, or is _mediated_. As regards its form it is accordingly the very concept that is the middle term; only that, as the _necessary_, it stands distinguished from _necessity_, – in the wholly superficial form of singularity as against universality. The absolute _content_ of A and B is one and the same; they are merely two names for a single substrate, names holding for _representation_, insofar as it clings to the appearance of the differing shape of existence and marks off from the necessary its necessity; yet were this necessity to stand severed from B, B would not be what is necessary. In it, then, the identity of what _mediates_ and what is _mediated_ is at hand.

3. The hypothetical syllogism sets forth in the first place _the_ _necessary_ _reference_ as an interconnection by way of _the_ _form_ or _negative_ _unity_, just as the categorical does by way of the positive unity, the solid _content_, objective _universality_. But _necessity_ collapses _together_ into the _necessary_; _the_ _activity_ _of_ _form_ that carries the conditioning actuality over into the conditioned is _in_ _itself_ that unity wherein the determinatenesses of the opposition, earlier set free into indifferent existence, stand _sublated_, and wherein the difference between A and B is a hollow name. Necessity is therefore a unity reflected into itself, – and so an _identical_ content; nor is it this merely _in_ _itself_, for through this syllogism it is _posited_ as well, seeing that A's being is likewise not its own but B's and conversely, the being of the one being throughout the being of the other, and that in the conclusion the immediate being or indifferent determinateness is determinately a mediated one, – externality having thus sublated itself, and its _unity_, _gone_ _into_ _itself_, being _posited_.

The mediation of the syllogism has thereby determined itself as _singularity_, as _immediacy_, and as _negativity_ _referring_ _to_ _itself_, or as the identity that differentiates and out of this difference draws itself together within itself, – as absolute form, and precisely therewith as objective _universality_, as _content_ _identical_ _with_ _itself_. In this determination the syllogism is the _disjunctive_ _syllogism_.
