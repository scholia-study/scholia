## Chapter 1. Shine

Essence, coming as it does from being, seems to stand opposite being; this immediate being is _at_ _first_ the _unessential_.

Only, _secondly_, it is something more than just unessential; it is essenceless being, it is _shine_.

_Thirdly_, this shine is not something external, an other over against essence; it is essence's very own shine. The shining of essence within its own self is _reflection_.
