## {{{ 12.90 }}} Chapter 3. The Syllogism

The _syllogism_ has emerged as the reinstatement of the _concept_ within the _judgment_, and hence as the unity and truth of the two. The concept as such keeps its moments sublated in _unity_; in the judgment this unity is something inner, or, what comes to the same, something outer, and though the moments do stand in reference, they are posited as _self-subsistent_ _extremes_. In the _syllogism_ the determinations of the concept are just as the extremes of the judgment are, and along with that their determinate _unity_ is posited.

The syllogism is thus the concept posited in full; on that account it is _the_ _rational_. – Understanding counts as the faculty of the _determinate_ concept, the concept being held fast _for_ _itself_ by way of abstraction and of the form of universality. Within reason, by contrast, the _determinate_ concepts stand posited in their _totality_ and _unity_. Hence the syllogism is not merely something rational; rather, _whatever_ _is_ _rational_ _is_ _a_ _syllogism_. Syllogizing has long since been ascribed to reason; yet on the other side people speak of reason in and for itself, of rational principles and laws, in a manner that leaves it obscure how the reason which draws a syllogism and this reason which is the wellspring of laws and of further eternal verities and absolute thoughts are connected at all. Should the former be merely formal reason while the latter is to bring forth content, then by that very difference the _form_ of reason — the syllogism — would be precisely what the latter could not do without. For all that, the two are customarily held so far apart, neither being mentioned where the other is discussed, that the reason of absolute thoughts appears as if ashamed of the reason of the syllogism, while the syllogism appears to be counted a doing of reason almost solely by inherited usage. Yet, as has just been noted, logical reason, taken as the _formal_ kind, must plainly be discernible in essence within that reason too which deals with a content; indeed, no content whatever can be rational except through the rational form. For guidance here one cannot turn to the very common chatter about reason, since it abstains from saying what is to be understood by _reason_ at all; the cognition that purports to be rational is for the most part so taken up with its subject matters that it neglects to cognize {{{ 12.91 }}} reason itself, marking reason off and naming it merely by the subject matters it is said to possess. Suppose reason is to be the cognition which knows of God, of freedom, of right and of duty, of the infinite, the unconditioned, the supersensible, or which at least yields representations and feelings of these; then partly these latter are only negative subject matters, and partly there still remains the very first question, namely what it is in all those subject matters for the sake of which they are rational? – This is what it is: that the infinite in them is no empty abstraction from the finite, no universality bare of content and of determination, but rather fulfilled universality, the concept, which is _determinate_ and which has its determinateness at itself in that genuine way of drawing distinctions within itself and of being the unity of these its own distinctions, distinctions belonging to the understanding and themselves determinate. Only thus does reason _lift_ itself above the finite, the conditioned, the sensible, or however else that may be determined, and in this negativity it is essentially _content-laden_, for it is unity precisely as unity of determinate extremes; taken so, however, _the_ _rational_ _is_ _nothing_ _other_ _than_ _the_ _syllogism_.

At first, then, the syllogism is _immediate_, as the _judgment_ was; its determinations (termini) are thus _simple_, _abstract_ determinatenesses, and it is on that account a _syllogism_ _of_ _the_ _understanding_. Where one halts at this shape of it, the rationality within it, though certainly present and posited, remains inconspicuous. Its essential moment is the extremes in their _unity_, the _middle_ _term_ conjoining them and the _ground_ upholding them. In that abstraction holds fast to the _self-subsistence_ _of_ _the_ _extremes_, it confronts them with this _unity_ as with a determinateness just as _rigid_, just as much _for_ _itself_, and in that manner takes it to be _non-unity_ _rather_ _than_ _unity_. The designation: _middle_ _term_ (medius terminus) is borrowed from spatial representation, and does its part in keeping us fixed upon the determinations' _being-outside-one-another_. _If_ _now_ _the_ _syllogism_ consists in this, _that_ _the_ _unity_ _of_ _the_ _extremes_ is _posited_ within it, while that unity is nonetheless taken flatly, on the one hand as a particular for itself, on the other as a merely external reference, and _non-unity_ is turned into the syllogism's essential relation, then the reason which the syllogism is contributes nothing to rationality.

_The_ _syllogism_ _of_ _existence_, _first_, in which the determinations are determined thus immediately and abstractly, exhibits at its own self — seeing that, like the judgment, it is _the_ _reference_ of those determinations — the fact that they are no such abstract determinations, but each of them is rather _the_ _reference_ _to_ _the_ _other_, and that the middle term holds not merely particularity over against the determinations of the extremes, but holds these _posited_ at itself.

{{{ 12.92 }}} By this dialectic of its own it makes itself into the _syllogism_ _of_ _reflection_, the _second_ _syllogism_, – one with determinations of such a sort that in each of them _the_ _other_ essentially _shines_, or that stand posited as _mediated_, which is what they are meant to be according to the syllogism as such.

_Thirdly_, once this _shining_ or being-mediated has reflected itself into its own self, the syllogism stands determined as _syllogism_ _of_ _necessity_, where the mediating factor is the objective nature of the fact. Inasmuch as this syllogism determines the extremes of the concept just as much as totalities, _the_ _syllogism_ has attained the answering of its concept, or the middle term, to its existence, or the extreme differences, and so has reached its truth, and with that has stepped over out of subjectivity into _objectivity_.
