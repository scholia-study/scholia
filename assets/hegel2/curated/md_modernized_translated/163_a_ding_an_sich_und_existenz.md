## a. Thing-in-itself and Concrete Existence

1. As the _essential_ _immediate_ on hand by way of a mediation already sublated, the _thing_ _in_ _itself_ is that which concretely exists. In this, mediation is quite as essential to the thing in itself; but within such first, immediate concrete existence the said difference comes apart into _indifferent_ _determinations_. One side of it, the mediation of the thing, namely, is the thing's _unreflected_ _immediacy_; its being pure and simple, which, on account of being determined at once as mediation, amounts to an _existence_ _other_ to itself, inwardly _manifold_ and _external_. Not existence merely, though: it stands in reference to the sublated mediation and to essential immediacy, and hence is existence as the _unessential_, as positedness. – (Once the thing is held apart from its concrete existence, it is the _possible_, the thing belonging to representation, or the thought-thing, which as such is not meant at the same time to exist. The determination of possibility, together with the opposition of the thing to its concrete existence, belongs later.) – Yet thing-in-itself and mediated being are both alike contained in concrete existence, and each is itself a concrete existence; the former concretely exists and is the thing's essential concrete existence, the latter its unessential one.

{{{ 11.328 }}} The _thing_ _in_ _itself_, as the simple being-reflected of concrete existence into itself, is no ground of the unessential existence; it is motionless, indeterminate unity, since its determination is precisely to be mediation already sublated, and hence merely the _groundwork_ of that existence. Reflection too, therefore, as the existence mediating itself through another, falls _outside_ _the_ _thing-in-itself_. The latter is to bear no determinate manifoldness on it, and comes by such only once _brought_ _before_ _external_ _reflection_, towards which it nevertheless stays indifferent. (– Color the thing-in-itself has only once brought before the eye, taste once brought before the nose, and so forth.) Its diversity amounts to respects that an other takes, determinate references which this other confers on itself in regard to the thing-in-itself, and which are no determinations belonging to the latter.

2. This other is now reflection, which, being determined as external, is _first_ _external_ _to_ _itself_ and constitutes the determinate manifoldness. _Next_ it is external to what essentially concretely exists, and _refers_ itself to that as to its absolute _presupposition_. Both these moments of external reflection, though, a manifoldness of its own and its reference to the thing-in-itself standing other to it, are one and the same. For such concrete existence is external only insofar as it refers itself to essential identity as _to_ _an_ _other_. Beyond the thing-in-itself, accordingly, the manifoldness has no self-subsistent subsistence of its own, but is only as shine as against it, in its necessary reference thereto, as the reflex breaking upon it. Diversity is thus on hand as an other's reference to the thing-in-itself; but that other is nothing subsisting for itself and is only as reference to it; simultaneously, though, it is only as the repulsion away from it, and so proves the unsupported counter-thrust of itself into itself.

To the thing-in-itself, then, essential identity of concrete existence that it is, this essenceless reflection does not accrue; external to it, rather, that reflection collapses into itself. It founders, and so becomes itself essential identity, or thing-in-itself. – One may view the matter also thus: essenceless concrete existence has its reflection-into-itself in the thing-in-itself; to begin with, it refers itself to that as to its _other_; but being the other as against what is in itself, it is only the sublating of itself, and the becoming of being-in-itself. Thereby the thing-in-itself is identical with external concrete existence.

On the thing-in-itself this displays itself thus. The thing-in-itself is essential concrete existence referring itself to itself; it is identity with itself only to the extent that it harbors within it the negativity of reflection; what showed up as concrete existence external to it is on that account a moment within it. Hence it is likewise a thing-in-itself repelling itself away from itself, one which _accordingly_ _comports_ _itself_ _towards_ _itself_ _as_ _towards_ _an_ _other_. So there are now _several_ things-in-themselves on hand, which stand to one another in the {{{ 11.329 }}} reference of external reflection. Their relation to one another as to others is this unessential concrete existence; but it is moreover itself essential to them – or rather, this unessential concrete existence, collapsing into itself, is thing-in-itself, though an _other_ than the first, since the first is immediate essentiality while this one issues out of the unessential concrete existence. Only, this second thing-in-itself is nothing more than an other in general; for, being a thing identical with itself, it holds no further determinateness as against the first, and is, as the first was, the reflection of unessential concrete existence into itself. Thus the determinateness of the diverse things-in-themselves as against one another falls to external reflection.

3. This external reflection is henceforth a comportment of the things-in-themselves towards one another, _their_ _mutual_ _mediation_ as others. In this way the things-in-themselves are the extremes of a syllogism, its middle term being made up by their external concrete existence, that concrete existence whereby they are others for one another and are differentiated. This difference of theirs falls solely within _their_ _reference_; from their surface, as it were, they merely dispatch determinations into that reference, and towards it, as absolutely reflected into themselves, they stay indifferent. – This relation now makes up the totality of concrete existence. The thing-in-itself stands in reference to a reflection external to it, and within that reflection it has manifold determinations; this is its repelling of itself away from itself into another thing-in-itself; such repelling is the counter-thrust of it into itself, since each is an other only as shining back out of the other; its positedness lies not on itself but on the other, and it is determined solely by the other's determinateness; and this other is just as much determined solely by the determinateness of the first. Since the _two_ things-in-themselves have the diversity thereby not on themselves but each only on the other, they are not differentiated at all; the thing-in-itself, supposed as it is to comport itself towards the other extreme as towards another thing-in-itself, comports itself towards what is undifferentiated from it, and external reflection, which was meant to make up the mediating reference between extremes, turns out to be a comportment of the thing-in-itself merely towards itself, or essentially its reflection into itself; it is accordingly determinateness that is in itself, the determinateness of the thing-in-itself. This determinateness it does not possess, then, in a reference external to it towards another thing-in-itself, and of the other towards it; the determinateness is no mere surface of it, but is its essential mediation with itself as with an other. – Both things-in-themselves, which were to make up the extremes of the reference, being supposed to have in themselves no determinateness as against one another, _in_ _fact_ _collapse_ _into_ _one_; there is but _One_ thing-in-itself which in external reflection comports itself towards itself, and what makes up its determinateness is its _own_ _reference_ _to_ _itself_ _as_ _to_ _an_ _other_.

Such determinateness on the part of the thing-in-itself is the _property_ _of_ _the_ _thing_.
