## B. One and Many

The one is the simple relating of being-for-itself to its own self, a relating in which its moments have fallen in together, so that it carries the form of _immediacy_ and its moments accordingly now become _existent_ ones.

Taken as the relating of the _negative_ to itself, the one is determining, – and taken as relating _to_ _itself_, it is infinite <i>self</i>-determining. Yet on account of the immediacy now in force, these _differences_ are no longer posited merely as moments belonging to one single self-determination, but rather at the same time as _existents_. In this way the _ideality_ of being-for-itself as totality veers over, to begin with, into _reality_, and indeed into the most rigid and most abstract reality, as the _one_. Being-for-itself, _in_ _the_ _one_, amounts to _the_ _posited_ unity of being with existence, to the absolute joining of relating-to-other {{{ 21.152 }}} with relating-to-self; but then the determinateness of being also steps in _against_ the determination of _infinite_ _negation_, against self-determination, with the result that what the one is _in_ _itself_ it now only _is_ _upon_ _it_, and the negative therewith becomes an other distinguished from it. That which shows itself to be _at_ _hand_ as distinguished from it is its very own self-determining; the unity of this self-determining with itself, thus distinguished from itself, is lowered to a _relating_, and, as _negative_ unity, is a negating of its own self as of an _other_, an _exclusion_ of the one, out of itself, the one, as of an _other_.
