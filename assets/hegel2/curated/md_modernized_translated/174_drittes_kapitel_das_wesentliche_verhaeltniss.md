## {{{ 11.353 }}} Chapter 3. The Essential Relation

The truth of appearance is the _essential_ _relation_. Its content possesses immediate self-subsistence, namely the immediacy _that_ _is_ together with the _reflected_ immediacy, or the reflection identical with itself. At the same time, within this self-subsistence the content is a relative one, existing simply and solely as reflection into what is other to it, or as unity of its reference with that other. Within this unity the self-subsistent content is a posited one, a sublated one; but this very unity is what makes up its essentiality and its self-subsistence; the reflection into other here is reflection into its own self. Sides belong to the relation because it is reflection into other; thus it carries within it the difference of its own self; and the sides of it are self-subsistent subsistence, seeing that in their mutually indifferent diversity they stand broken within themselves, so that whatever subsistence each has holds its meaning, in equal measure, only in the reference to the other, or in their negative unity.

The essential relation is on that score admittedly not yet the genuine _third_ term to _essence_ and to _concrete_ _existence_; but the determinate uniting of the two is already harboured in it. Essence stands realized within it in such fashion that self-subsistent concretely existing ones serve it for its subsistence; and these have gone back out of their indifference into their essential unity, so that nothing but this unity serves them for their subsistence. The determinations of reflection, the positive one and the negative one, are likewise reflected into themselves only insofar as they stand reflected into what is opposed to them; yet no determination belongs to them beyond this negative unity of theirs; the essential relation, by contrast, has for its sides such terms as stand posited as self-subsistent totalities. The opposition here is the same one as that of positive and negative; but at the same time it is opposition as an inverted world. A side of the essential relation is a totality, one that, however, essentially has something opposed to it, a _beyond_ of itself; it is appearance merely; its concrete existence is not, rather, its own, but that of its other. It is on that score something broken within its own self; but this sublatedness of its consists in its being the unity of its own self and of its other, hence a whole, and precisely for that reason it possesses self-subsistent concrete existence and is essential reflection into its own self.

Such is the _concept_ of relation. To begin with, however, the identity it harbours is not yet complete; the totality which each relative term is in its own self {{{ 11.354 }}} is as yet merely something inner; a side of the relation is at first posited within _one_ of the determinations of the negative unity; what makes up the form of the relation is the self-subsistence proper to each of the two sides. Its identity is on that score merely a _reference_, and their self-subsistence falls outside that reference, namely into the sides; there is not yet at hand the reflected unity of that identity together with the self-subsistent concrete existences, not yet the _substance_. – The concept of relation has thus indeed emerged as unity of reflected and of immediate self-subsistence. But this _concept_ is at first still _immediate_ itself, and hence its moments are immediate towards each other, their unity being their essential reference, which becomes the genuine unity answering to the concept only insofar as it realizes itself, that is, insofar as through its own movement it has _posited_ itself as that unity.

The essential relation is on that score immediately the relation of _whole_ and _parts_; – the reference of reflected and of immediate self-subsistence, such that both at once are only as mutually conditioning and presupposing each other.

Within this relation not one of the sides is as yet posited as a moment of the other, and hence their identity is itself a side; or else it is not their negative unity. It therefore passes over, _secondly_, into this, that the one is a moment of the other and is within the other as within its ground, as within what is genuinely self-subsistent of the two; – relation _of_ _force_ _and_ _its_ _expression_.

_Thirdly_, the inequality of this reference which is still at hand sublates itself, and the last relation is that of _inner_ and _outer_. – Within this difference, which has become wholly formal, the relation itself founders, and _substance_, or the _actual_, comes forth as the _absolute_ unity of immediate and of reflected concrete existence.
