## {{{ 12.148 }}} Chapter 2. Chemism

What chemism makes up, in the entirety of objectivity, is the moment of judgment; the moment of difference grown objective, and of process. Because determinateness and positedness are already there at its start, while the chemical object is objective totality besides, what follows in it runs simply, being fully determined by the presupposition it carries.
