## {{{ 11.323 }}} Second Section. Appearance

_Essence_ _must_ _appear_.

Absolute abstraction — that is being; this negativity is not something outward to being, but being is being and nothing other than being, only as this absolute negativity. On account of that negativity, being only is as being that sublates itself, and is _essence_. Essence, however, as the simple equality with itself, is conversely being just as much. In the doctrine of being there is contained the first proposition: _being_ _is_ _essence_. What makes up the content of the doctrine of essence's first section is the second proposition, _essence_ _is_ _being_. This being, however, into which essence turns itself, is _essential_ _being_, _concrete_ _existence_ — a having gone forth out of negativity and inwardness.

Thus does essence _appear_. Reflection is the _shining_ of essence _within_ _itself_. Enclosed within the unity, reflection's determinations are simply and solely posited ones, sublated ones; or reflection is the essence that in its positedness is immediately self-identical. In that essence is ground, however, it determines itself really, by way of its reflection, which sublates its own self, or returns into itself; and in that this determination, or the ground-reference's otherness, sublates itself further in the ground's reflection and turns into concrete existence, the form-determinations have in this an element of self-subsistent subsistence. Into _appearance_ their _shine_ completes itself.

Essentiality gone forward into immediacy is _at_ _first_ _concrete_ _existence_, and something concretely existing, or _thing_; as the unity, undifferentiated, of essence with its immediacy. The thing does contain reflection, yet in the thing's immediacy reflection's negativity is at first extinguished; since the thing's ground is essentially reflection, however, its immediacy sublates itself; it makes itself into a positedness.

Thus it is, _secondly_, _appearance_. What the thing is in itself, its truth, is appearance. But this concrete existence, merely posited and reflected into otherness, is just as much a going out beyond itself into its infinity; against the world of appearance there sets itself the world reflected into itself, _the_ _world_ _that_ _is_ _in_ _itself_.

{{{ 11.324 }}} Appearing being and essential being, however, stand simply and solely in reference to one another. Concrete existence is thus, _thirdly_, essential _relation_; what appears exhibits the essential, and the essential is within its appearance. – Relation is the union, still imperfect, of reflection into itself with reflection into otherness; the perfect interpenetration of the two is _actuality_.
