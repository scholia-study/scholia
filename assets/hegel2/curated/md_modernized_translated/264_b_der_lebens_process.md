## {{{ 12.187 }}} B. The Life-Process

In giving itself shape within its own self, the living individual thereby strains against that original presupposing of its own and takes up a position, as subject existing in and for itself, opposite the objective world it has presupposed. This subject is its own purpose, the concept holding its means and its subjective reality in the objectivity that has been subjected to it; by that route it stands constituted as the idea existing in and for itself, as the essentially self-subsistent, over against which the presupposed outer world carries only the value of a negative and non-self-subsistent thing. The living being, in its self-feeling, possesses this _certainty_ that the _otherness_ set over against it is in itself a _nullity_. Its drive is the need to sublate such otherness and to give that certainty its truth. Considered as subject, the individual is at the outset merely the _concept_ of life's idea; the subjective process it runs within itself, feeding upon its own self, and positing immediate objectivity as a natural means conformably to its concept, is mediated by that other process which bears on externality fully posited, on the objective totality standing _indifferently_ beside it.

_Need_ is where this process begins, that is, with the moment in which the living being _first_ determines itself and so posits itself as negated, referring itself thereby to something _other_ than itself, to indifferent objectivity; – while _secondly_ it is just as little lost in this loss of itself, maintains itself there and stays the identity of the concept that is equal to itself; hence it is the drive to posit that world which is _other_ to it as equal, _for_ _itself_, to itself, to sublate it and to objectify _itself_. By that route its self-determination wears the form of objective externality, and since it is identical with itself all the while, it is the absolute _contradiction_. Immediate shaping is the idea within its simple concept, objectivity conformable to the concept; so taken, it is by nature _good_. But once its negative moment realizes itself into objective particularity, that is, once each of the essential moments of its unity is realized on its own into a totality, then the concept is _sundered_ into absolute inequality with itself, and since it is equally the absolute identity within that sundering, the living being is this sundering for its own self and has the feeling of the contradiction, which is _pain_. _Pain_ is on that account the privilege of living natures; being the concept in existence, they are an actuality endowed with the infinite {{{ 12.188 }}} power of being within themselves the _negativity_ of their own selves, of having this _negativity_ _of_ _theirs_ _for_ _them_, of holding themselves fast in their otherness. – If it is said that contradiction cannot be thought, contradiction is on the contrary, in the pain of what lives, even a concrete existence that is actual.

Such diremption of the living being within itself is _feeling_, in that it is taken up into the simple universality of the concept, into sensibility. Out of pain arise _need_ and _drive_, which make up the transition by which the individual, being for itself as the negation of itself, shall come to be for itself as identity too, – an identity having being only as the negation of that negation. – What identity lies in drive as such is the subjective certainty of its own self, on the strength of which the individual bears itself towards its outward, indifferently existing world as towards an appearance, an actuality unessential and in itself without concept. Such a world is to receive the concept into itself only through the subject, which is the immanent purpose. That the objective world is indifferent towards determinateness, and hence towards the purpose, makes up its outward capacity for suiting the subject; whatever other specifications it may carry on it, its mechanical determinability, the absence of the immanent concept's freedom, makes up its impotence to hold out against the living being. – So far as the object stands to the living being at first as an indifferent outward thing, it can work mechanically upon it; but working so, it does not work upon it as upon something alive; so far as it does bear upon what lives, it works not as cause but _stimulates_ it. Since the living being is drive, externality reaches it and enters it only so far as externality is already in and for itself _within_ _it_; the working upon the subject accordingly consists in nothing else than that the subject _finds_ the externality which offers itself to be _corresponding_; – granted that this externality may not suit the subject's totality, it must at any rate answer to some particular side of it, and that possibility rests on the subject's being a particular precisely in bearing itself outwardly.

Now in so far as the subject, determined by its need, refers itself to the outward and thereby is itself something outward or an instrument, it exercises _force_ upon the object. Its particular character, its finitude generally, belongs to the more determinate appearing of this relation. – What is outward about the relation is the process of objectivity at large, mechanism and chemism. That process, however, is broken off at once and outwardness turned into inwardness. The external purposiveness which the subject's activity first brings about in the indifferent object is sublated in this, that over against the concept the object is no substance, so that the concept can become not merely the object's outer form but must posit itself as its essence and as the determination immanent to it and pervading it, conformably with their original identity.

{{{ 12.189 }}} With the seizing of the object, then, the mechanical process passes into the inner one, whereby the individual _appropriates_ the object in such a way that it strips it of its peculiar constitution, turns it into its means, and hands over to it its own subjectivity for substance. This assimilation thereby falls together with the individual's process of reproduction examined earlier; in that process the individual feeds at first upon itself, making its own objectivity into its object; the mechanical and chemical conflict of its members with outward things is an objective moment of it. Whatever is mechanical and chemical in the process marks a beginning of the living being's dissolution. Since life is the truth of these processes, and hence, as living, the concrete existence of that truth and its power, it reaches out over them, pervades them as their universality, and their product is through and through determined by it. Their being so transformed into living individuality makes up the latter's return into its own self, so that production, which as such would be a passing over into an other, turns into reproduction, wherein the living being posits itself _for_ _itself_ as identical with itself.

The immediate idea is likewise the immediate identity of concept and reality, an identity not existing _for_ _itself_; through the objective process the living being confers its _self-feeling_ upon itself, for therein it _posits_ itself, within its otherness posited as indifferent, as being what it is in and for itself, that which is identical with its own self, the negative unity of the negative. In this going-together of the individual with the objectivity it had at first presupposed as indifferent, the individual, while on one side constituting itself an actual singularity, has to that same degree _sublated_ _its_ _particularity_ and lifted itself into _universality_. Its particularity had consisted in the diremption by which life posited, as its species, individual life along with the objectivity outward to it. By the outward life-process, therefore, it has posited itself as real universal life, as _genus_.
