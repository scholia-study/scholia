## a. The Conditionedness of Force

Considered in the closer determinations belonging to it, force has upon it, _first_, the moment of the immediacy that is; force itself, over against this, stands determined as the negative unity. But this unity, under the determination of immediate being, is a _concretely_ _existing_ _something_. Such a something, being the negative unity in immediate shape, shows itself as the first; force, on the contrary, being the reflected side, shows itself as the positedness, and so far as belonging to the concretely existing thing or to some matter. Not that force would be the _form_ of this thing and the thing be determined through force; the thing, rather, as something immediate, stays indifferent towards it. – On this determination no ground lies in the thing for its having a force; force, on the other side, being the side of positedness, has the thing essentially for its presupposition. So when the question is raised how thing or matter comes to _have_ a force, force shows up as conjoined with it externally and as _stamped_ upon the thing by some alien power.

In this immediate subsisting, force is a _quiescent_ _determinateness_ _of_ _the_ _thing_ at large: not something expressing itself, but immediately something external. In this way force gets labelled a matter as well, and the magnetic, the electrical force, and so forth, is replaced. What is assumed in its place is a magnetic, an electrical matter, and so forth. Or, in place of the famous _attractive_ _force_, a fine _ether_ is assumed, said to hold everything together. – These are the matters into which the inert, forceless negative unity of the thing dissolves, and which came under consideration above.

Force, however, harbours immediate concrete existence as a moment, as something which is indeed condition but which goes over and sublates itself; not, therefore, as a concretely existing thing. Nor, further, is it negation in the guise of determinateness; it is negative {{{ 11.361 }}} unity reflecting itself into itself. The thing on which force was to reside thereby loses all further significance here; force is rather itself the positing of the externality that shows up as concrete existence. So neither is it a determinate matter and no more; self-subsistence of that kind long ago went over into positedness and into appearance.

_Second_, force is the unity of reflected and of immediate subsisting, or of the unity of form and external self-subsistence. It is the two in one; it is the touching of such terms of which the one is so far as the other is not; the positive reflection identical with itself, and the negated reflection. Thus force is the contradiction repelling itself from its own self; it is _active_; or it is the negative unity referring itself to itself, wherein the reflected immediacy, the essential being-within-itself, is posited as being only a sublated thing, a moment, and hence, so far as force marks itself off from immediate concrete existence, as going over into the latter. Force, then, taken as the determination of the whole's reflected unity, stands posited as coming to be, _out_ _of_ _its_ _own_ _self_, the concretely existing external manifoldness.

But _third_, force is as yet only an activity that is _in-itself_ and immediate; it is the reflected unity, and just as essentially the _negation_ _of_ _that_ _unity_; since it is diverse from this negation and yet is only as the identity of its own self with its negation, it stands essentially referred to the negation as to an immediacy external to it, and holds that immediacy for its _presupposition_ and its _condition_.

Now this presupposition is no thing found standing over against force; indifferent self-subsistence of that sort is sublated in force; as force's condition it is _a_ _self-subsistent_ _other_ _to_ _force_. But since it is no thing, and since self-subsistent immediacy has here determined itself at the same time as negative unity referring itself to itself, _it_ _is_ _itself_ _force_. – Force's activity is conditioned by force's own self as by what is other to it, by a force.

Force is in this way a relation in which either side is the same as the other. Forces are what stand in the relation, and they refer to one another essentially at that. – They are, further, at the outset merely diverse; the unity of their relation is as yet only _the_ _inner_ unity _that_ _is_ _in_ _itself_. Being conditioned by another force is thus _in_ _itself_ force's own doing; or force is so far only a _pre_supposing doing, one that refers _to_ _itself_ negatively and no more; this other force still lies _beyond_ force's _positing_ activity, beyond, that is, the reflection which in its determining _returns_ _into_ _itself_ immediately.
