## {{{ 21.97 }}} a. Existence in General

Out of becoming existence issues. Existence is being and nothing in simple oneness. Its simplicity gives it the form of an _immediate_. Its mediation, becoming, lies behind it; that mediation has sublated itself, and existence therefore makes its appearance as a first, as that from which one sets out. At first it stands under the one-sided determination of _being_; the other that it holds within it, the _nothing_, will equally come forward in it, over against the first.

It is not mere being but _existence_; taken etymologically, being at a certain _place_; the representation of space, however, has no business here. Existence, in keeping with its becoming, is _being_ at large along with a _non-being_, and this in such a way that the non-being has been taken up into simple unity with being. What constitutes _determinateness_ as such is _non-being_ taken up into being in such fashion that the concrete whole stands in the form of being, of immediacy.

The _whole_ likewise stands in the form – that is, the _determinateness_ – of being, since being too has shown itself within becoming to be no more than a moment – a sublated one, negatively determined; but so it stands _for_ _us_ _in_ _our_ _reflection_, not yet _posited_ in its own self. The determinateness of existence as such, by contrast, is the posited one, and it lies too in the expression _there_-being. – The two are always well worth telling apart; only what is _posited_ in a concept belongs to the developing consideration of that concept, to its content. Determinateness not yet posited in the concept itself belongs, rather, to our reflection, whether it concern the nature of the concept itself or be an external comparison; and to call attention to a determinateness of the latter kind can serve only for elucidation, or as a forward hint of the course which the development itself will exhibit. External reflection is at work in saying that the whole, the unity of being and of nothing, stands in the _one-sided_ _determinateness_ of being; in negation, though, in something and _other_ and so forth, it will come to be as _posited_. – The point here was to draw attention to the distinction indicated; but to render account of everything reflection might allow itself to remark would lead into the prolixity of anticipating what has to emerge from the matter itself. Reflections of this kind can serve to ease the survey and with it the understanding, yet they no doubt also bring the drawback of looking like unwarranted assertions, grounds and foundations for {{{ 21.98 }}} what is to follow. One should take them, therefore, for nothing more than what they are meant to be, and keep them apart from whatever is a moment in the advance of the matter itself.

Existence corresponds to the _being_ of the sphere before it; being, however, is the indeterminate, so that no determinations emerge in it. Existence, by contrast, is determinate being, a _concrete_; several determinations, differing relations among its moments, therefore open up in it at once.
