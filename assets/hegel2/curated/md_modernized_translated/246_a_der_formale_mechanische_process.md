## a. The Formal Mechanical Process

The mechanical process posits what lies contained in the concept of mechanism, and so at first a _contradiction_.

1. From the concept exhibited, the acting of objects upon one another follows in this way, that such acting is the _positing_ of the _identical_ reference of the objects. This consists in nothing but the granting of the form of _universality_ to the determinateness that is effected; – which is COMMUNICATION, and communication proceeds without any passing over into what stands opposed. – _Spiritual_ _communication_, going on as it anyway does within the element that constitutes the universal under the form of universality, is for {{{ 12.138 }}} itself an _ideal_ reference, wherein a _determinateness_ _continues_ itself unclouded out of the one person into the other and universalizes itself without alteration of any sort, – much as a scent spreads at large through an atmosphere that offers no resistance. But in the communication between material objects too their determinateness makes itself, so to speak, _broad_ in a manner equally ideal; personality is a _hardness_ infinitely more intensive than any that objects have. The object's formal totality at large, indifferent as it is towards determinateness and hence no self-determination, renders the object undifferentiated from the other, and so renders the acting upon it, in the first instance, an unimpeded continuation of the one's determinateness within the other.

In the spiritual sphere it is now a content infinitely manifold that lends itself to communication, since, once taken up into intelligence, it acquires this _form_ of universality in which it becomes something communicable. But the _objective_ as such is what is universal not merely through the form but in and for itself, alike in the spiritual sphere and in the corporeal; against which the singularity of external objects, and of persons likewise, counts as something unessential, incapable of offering it resistance. Laws, customs, rational representations at large are in the spiritual sphere communicables of that kind, penetrating individuals in a manner they are not conscious of and making themselves good in them. In the corporeal sphere they are motion, heat, magnetism, electricity and suchlike – which, however much one may wish to represent them as stuffs or matters, have to be determined as _imponderable_ agents, – agents lacking that in materiality which is the ground of _their_ _individualization_.

2. If now, in the acting of objects upon one another, their _identical_ universality gets posited first, then with equal necessity the other moment of the concept, _particularity_, is _to_ _be_ _posited_; the objects accordingly give proof of their _self-subsistence_ as well, hold themselves apart as external to one another, and re-establish _singularity_ within that universality. This re-establishing is _reaction_ at large. To begin with it is not to be grasped as a _bare_ _sublating_ of the action together with the determinateness communicated; what has been communicated is, as universal, positively there in the particular objects and _particularizes_ _itself_ only at their diversity. To that extent, then, what has been communicated stays what it is; it merely _distributes_ itself over the objects, or gets determined through their particularity. – In its other, the effect, the cause goes lost, and the activity of the causal substance goes lost in its efficacy; _the_ _acting_ _object_, by contrast, becomes only a _universal_; its efficacy is at first no forfeiting of its determinateness but a _particularization_, whereby the object, which at first was that whole determinateness _singular_ at it, {{{ 12.139 }}} now becomes one _kind_ of it, and the _determinateness_ is only thereby posited as a universal. The two, the raising of the singular determinateness into universality in communication, and the particularizing of it, or its lowering, which was only One, into a kind, in distribution, are one and the same.

_Reaction_ is now equal to _action_. – This shows itself _at_ _first_ in such a way that the other object has _taken_ the whole universal _up_ _into_ _itself_ and is now in that way active over against the first. Its reaction, then, coincides with the action, a _reciprocal_ _repelling_ _of_ _the_ _impact_. _Secondly_, what has been communicated is the objective; it therefore _remains_ substantial determination of the objects, given their presupposed diversity; the universal accordingly specifies itself in them at the same time, and each object accordingly does not merely render back the whole action, but has its own specific share. But _thirdly_, reaction is _wholly_ _negative_ _action_ to this extent, that each object, thanks to the _elasticity_ of its _self-subsistence_, thrusts out the positedness of an other lodged within it and keeps hold of its reference to itself. That specific _particularity_ which the communicated determinateness has in the objects, called a kind just now, goes back to _singularity_, and the object makes good its externality against the _communicated_ _universality_. By this the action passes over into _rest_. It proves to be an alteration merely _superficial_ and transient at the object's indifferent totality closed within itself.

3. This going back constitutes the _product_ of the mechanical process. _Immediately_ the _object_ _stands_ _presupposed_ as singular, further as particular over against others, thirdly however as indifferent towards its own particularity, as universal. The _product_ is that totality of the concept which was _presupposed_, _now_ _as_ _one_ _that_ _is_ _posited_. The conclusion it is, in which the communicated universal has been joined together with singularity through the object's particularity; at the same time, however, in the rest the _mediation_ is posited as one that has _sublated_ itself, or so that the product is indifferent towards this becoming-determined of its own, and the determinateness it has received is external at it.

So the product is nothing other than the object as it first goes into the process. Yet at the same time only through this movement is it _determined_; the mechanical object is _object_ _at_ _all_ _only_ _as_ _product_, because what it is comes to be at it only _through_ _the_ _mediation_ _of_ _an_ _other_. Thus as product it is what it was to be in and for [itself], something _composite_, something _mixed_, a certain _order_ _and_ _arrangement_ of parts, at large something whose determinateness is no self-determination but a _posited_ one.

On the other side, the _result_ of the mechanical process is just as much _not_ _at_ _hand_ _already_ _in_ _advance_ _of_ _the_ _process_ _itself_; its _end_ _lies_ _not_ _in_ its _beginning_, as happens with purpose. The product is a determinateness at the object as one {{{ 12.140 }}} _externally_ posited. By the _concept_, then, this product is indeed the same as what the object already is from the outset. In the beginning, however, that external determinateness is not yet there as something _posited_. To that extent the result is something _wholly_ _other_ than the object's first existence, and is for the object something simply contingent.
