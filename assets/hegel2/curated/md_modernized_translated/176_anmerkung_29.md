## Remark

Above (Division I, p. 113 ff.) _the_ _antinomy_ _of_ _the_ _infinite_ _divisibility_ _of_ _matter_ came under consideration, in connection with the concept of quantity. Quantity is the unity of continuity and discreteness; within the _self-subsistent_ one it harbours that one's _having_ _flowed_ _together_ with others, and within this _identity_ with itself, _prolonged_ without interruption, it equally harbours the _negation_ _of_ _that_ _identity_. Once the immediate reference of these moments of quantity gets voiced in the shape of the essential relation of whole and parts — quantity's _one_ counting as _part_, its _continuity_ as the _whole_ compounded out of parts — the antinomy consists in that contradiction which showed up at the relation of whole and parts and was there dissolved. – For whole and parts are quite as essentially referred to one another, making up one identity only, as they are indifferent towards one another and possess self-subsistent subsistence. The relation, therefore, is antinomy in just this sense: the one moment, by liberating itself from the other, immediately brings that other along.

Let what concretely exists be determined as whole, then, and it has parts, and the parts make up its subsistence; the unity of the whole is a posited reference merely, an external _putting_ _together_ which is no affair of the self-subsistently existing thing. Now so far as the latter is part, it is not whole, not something put together, and so _simple_. But since reference to a whole is external to it, that reference is no affair of it; and so what is self-subsistent is not in itself part either, for part it is only through such reference. Yet since it is now not part, {{{ 11.359 }}} it is whole, for the only relation at hand is this one of whole and of parts, and the self-subsistent thing is one of the two. But being whole, it is once more something put together; once more it consists of parts, and _so_ _on_ _into_ _infinity_. – This infinity amounts to nothing but the perennial alternation of the relation's two determinations, in each of which the other immediately springs up, so that the positedness of either is the vanishing of that same one. Let matter be determined as whole, then, and it consists of parts, and upon these the whole sinks to an unessential reference and vanishes. Yet the part, taken for itself in that manner, is likewise not part, but the whole. – Pressed quite closely together, the antinomy of this inference properly runs: because the whole is not the self-subsistent thing, the part is the self-subsistent thing; but because self-subsistence belongs to it only _without_ _the_ _whole_, self-subsistent it is _not_ qua part, but rather _as_ _whole_. The infinity of the resulting progress is the incapacity to hold together the two thoughts which this mediation carries, namely that either determination, precisely through its self-subsistence and its severance from the other, passes over into non-self-subsistence and into that other.
