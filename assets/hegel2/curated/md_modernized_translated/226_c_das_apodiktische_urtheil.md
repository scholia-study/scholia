## c. The Apodictic Judgment

The apodictic judgment's subject (a house of such and such a make is _good_, an action so and so _constituted_ _is_ _right_,) carries about it _first_ _the_ _universal_, _what_ _it_ _ought_ _to_ _be_, _secondly_ its _constitution_; the second of these holds the _ground_ for why a predicate belonging to the judgment of the concept {{{ 12.88 }}} does or does not attach to the _subject_ _entire_, that is, whether or not the subject measures up to its concept. – With this the judgment is _genuinely_ objective; or rather, it is the _truth_ of _judgment_ generally. Subject and predicate answer to each other, their content is one and the same, and this _content_ is itself _concrete_ _universality_ as posited; for within it lie a pair of moments — the objectively universal element, or the _genus_, together with the _singularized_. So what we have here is the universal which is _itself_, which carries itself on through _its_ _opposite_, and which counts as universal only in being _unity_ with that opposite. – A universal of this kind — such predicates as good, fitting, correct, and so on — rests upon an _ought_, and at once takes in the _answering_ of _existence_ to that ought; not that ought, nor the genus for itself, but precisely this _answering_ is the _universality_ that constitutes what is predicated in the apodictic judgment.

These two moments are contained in the _subject_ likewise, in _immediate_ unity, as the _fact_. But the truth about the fact is that it stands _broken_ within itself into its _ought_ and its _being_; such is the _absolute_ _judgment_ _upon_ _all_ _actuality_. – That this original division, the very omnipotence of the concept, is equally a return into the concept's unity and an absolute reference of ought and being to one another — this is what makes the actual into _one_ _fact_; and the inner reference between them, this concrete identity, makes up the _soul_ of the fact.

The transition from the fact's immediate simplicity over to the _answering_ that is the _determinate_ reference between its ought and its being, – or the _copula_, now proves on closer inspection to have its seat in the fact's particular _determinateness_. The genus is the universal that _is_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, which to that extent shows up as standing in no reference at all; the determinateness, by contrast, is what reflects itself within that universality _into_ _itself_ and yet at the same time _into_ _an_ _other_. Hence the judgment has its _ground_ in how the subject is constituted, and for that reason is _apodictic_. So there is now present the _determinate_ and _fulfilled_ _copula_, the one that formerly consisted in the abstract _is_ but has by now grown out into _ground_ as such. To begin with it sits at the subject as an _immediate_ determinateness, yet it is every bit as much the _reference_ _to_ the predicate, whose content is nothing but this _answering_ itself, or the subject's reference to universality.

So the judgment's form has gone under: first, because _in_ _themselves_ subject and predicate are one and the same content; but secondly, because through its determinateness the subject points out beyond itself and refers itself to the predicate, while thirdly this _referring_ has equally passed over into the predicate, makes up the predicate's whole content, and is thus the _posited_ reference, or the judgment itself. – In this way the {{{ 12.89 }}} concrete identity of the concept — which was the _result_ of the disjunctive judgment and which makes up the _inner_ groundwork of the judgment of the concept — is established throughout the _whole_, whereas at first it was posited only in the predicate.

Looked at more closely, the positive side of this result, the side that makes for the judgment's transition into another form, shows subject and predicate in the apodictic judgment, as we have seen, each of them as the concept entire. – Yet the _unity_ _of_ _the_ _concept_, being the _determinateness_ that makes up the copula which sets them in reference to each other, is at the same time _distinguished_ from the two. To begin with, it stands only over on the subject's side, as that subject's _immediate_ _constitution_. But since it is essentially the _referring_ factor, it is not just such an immediate constitution but rather what _runs_ _through_ subject and predicate alike, and is _universal_. – Given that subject and predicate have the same _content_, what that determinateness posits, by contrast, is the _form-reference_; the _determinateness_ _as_ _a_ _universal_, or _particularity_. – Thus it holds both form-determinations of the extremes within itself; and is the _determinate_ reference of subject and predicate; it is the _fulfilled_ _or_ _content-laden_ _copula_ of the judgment, the concept's unity stepped forth again out of the _judgment_ in which it had been lost in the extremes. – _Through_ _this_ _fulfilment_ of the _copula_ the judgment has turned into the _syllogism_.
