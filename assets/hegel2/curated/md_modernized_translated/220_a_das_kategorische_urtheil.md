## a. The Categorical Judgment

The _genus_ _divides_ itself, or repels itself essentially into _species_; genus it is only so far as it comprises species beneath it; and the species is species only so far as, {{{ 12.78 }}} on the one side, it concretely exists in singulars, and, on the other, possesses in the genus a universality higher than itself. – As its predicate _the_ _categorical_ _judgment_ now has a universality of that kind, wherein the subject possesses its _immanent_ nature. It is itself, however, the first, _immediate_ judgment of necessity; hence the determinateness of the subject, whereby the subject is a particular or a singular over against genus or species, belongs so far to the immediacy that external concrete existence has. – Objective universality, however, likewise has here as yet only its _immediate_ particularization; on one side it is on that account itself something determinate, over against which higher genera stand; – on the other side it is not exactly the _nearest_ genus, that is, one whose determinateness is not precisely what constitutes the principle of the subject's specific particularity. What is _necessary_ about it, though, is the _substantial_ _identity_ of the subject with the predicate, over against which whatever is peculiar to the former, marking it off from the latter, counts only as an unessential positedness, – or indeed as a mere name; in its predicate the subject is reflected back into its being-in-and-for-itself. – A predicate of this sort ought not to be lumped in with the predicates of the judgments treated so far; if the judgments, say: the rose is red, the rose is a plant, or else: this ring is yellow, it is gold, get flung together into a single class, and a property as external as the colour of a flower is taken for a predicate on a par with the flower's vegetable nature, then a difference is passed over that must strike even the crudest apprehension. – Sharply, therefore, must the categorical judgment be marked off from positive and negative judgment; in the latter two, what the subject gets credited with is a _single_ _contingent_ content, while in the former it is that totality of the form which is reflected into itself. In it, accordingly, the copula carries the meaning of _necessity_, in those others only that of abstract, immediate _being_.

At first the _determinateness_ of the subject whereby the subject is a _particular_ over against the predicate is still something _contingent_; subject and predicate stand in no necessary reference through _form_ or _determinateness_; necessity therefore still stands as something _inner_. – Subject, however, the subject is only as a _particular_, and so far as it has objective universality, it is to have that universality essentially in accordance with the determinateness which is as yet only immediate. In _determining_ itself, that is, in setting itself into the judgment, the objectively universal stands essentially in identical reference with this _determinateness_ as such repelled out of it, that is, the determinateness is essentially not to be posited as something merely contingent. Only through such _necessity_ of its immediate being does the categorical judgment answer to its objective universality, and in this manner has passed over into the _hypothetical_ _judgment_.
