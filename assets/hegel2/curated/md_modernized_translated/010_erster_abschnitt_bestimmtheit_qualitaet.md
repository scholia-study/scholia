## {{{ 21.68 }}} First Section. Determinateness (Quality)

Being is the indeterminate immediate; it stands free of the determinateness directed against essence, and free as well of any determinateness it might come to receive inside itself. Such being, void of reflection, is what being is when taken in immediacy, at nothing but its own self.

Because it is indeterminate, it is quality-less being; but _in_ _itself_ the character of indeterminateness accrues to it only in contrast with what is _determinate_ or qualitative. Yet over against being in general there stands _determinate_ being as such, and with that the indeterminateness of the former is itself what makes up its quality. What will emerge, accordingly, is that the _first_ being is determinate in itself, and hence

_Secondly_ that it passes over into _existence_, is _existence_; but that this, as finite being, sublates itself and, into the infinite relation of being to itself,

_Thirdly_, passes over into _being-for-itself_.
