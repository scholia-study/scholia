## 3. Determining Reflection

Determining reflection is on the whole the unity of _positing_ reflection and _external_ reflection. This has to be looked at more closely. –

1. External reflection takes its start from immediate being, the _positing_ one from nothing. External reflection, in becoming determining, sets an other, namely essence, in the place of the sublated being; positing does not set its determination in the place of some other; it is without any presupposition. But for that very reason it is not the finished, determining reflection; the determination it posits is accordingly _only_ something posited; it is an immediate, yet not as self-same but as negating itself, it stands in absolute relation to the return into itself, it is only within reflection into itself, but it is not this reflection itself.

What is _posited_ is therefore an _other_, yet in such a way that reflection's self-sameness is preserved throughout; for what is posited only is as something sublated, as relation to the return into its own self. – In _the_ _sphere_ of _being_, _existence_ was that being which carried negation on it, and being constituted the immediate base and element of that negation, which was for this reason itself immediate. What corresponds to existence in _the_ _sphere_ _of_ _essence_ is _positedness_. It too is an existence, but its base is being taken as essence, that is, as pure negativity; it is a determinateness or negation that is not something in being but immediately something sublated. _Existence_ _is_ _only_ _positedness_; this is the {{{ 11.256 }}} proposition of essence concerning existence. Positedness stands over against existence on the one side and over against essence on the other, and is to be regarded as the middle that joins existence together with essence and, conversely, essence with existence. – Saying of a determination that it is _only_ a positedness can accordingly bear a twofold sense; it is such either in contrast with existence or in contrast with essence. In the former sense, existence is taken for something higher than positedness, while positedness is ascribed to external reflection, to what is subjective. In truth, however, positedness is the higher; for as positedness existence is, as what it is in itself, as negative, something related purely and simply to the return into itself. That is why positedness is _only_ a positedness with regard to essence, as the negation of having returned into its own self.

2. Positedness is as yet no determination of reflection; it is merely determinateness, negation in general. But positing now stands in unity with external reflection; in this unity external reflection is absolute presupposing, which is to say, reflection's repelling of itself from itself, or the positing of determinateness _as_ _reflection's_ _own_. Positedness is therefore, as such, negation; but as presupposed it is negation reflected into itself. Thus positedness is a _determination_ _of_ _reflection_.

From the determinateness of being, from quality, the determination of reflection is distinguished; quality is immediate relation to another in general; positedness too is relation to another, but relation to being reflected into itself. Negation as quality is negation that _is_; being makes up its ground and element. The determination of reflection, by contrast, has for this ground its being reflected into itself. Positedness settles into a determination precisely because reflection is self-sameness in its own state of being negated; its being negated is therefore itself reflection into itself. The determination here subsists not through being but through its own self-sameness. Since the being that carries quality is what is unlike negation, quality is within itself unlike itself, and hence a moment that passes over, that vanishes in its other. The determination of reflection, on the contrary, is positedness _as_ negation, a negation that has for its ground its being negated, so that it is not unlike itself within itself and is therefore an _essential_, not a transitory determinateness. The _self-sameness_ _of_ _reflection_, for which the negative is only negative, only something sublated or posited, is what confers subsistence on it.

It is for the sake of _this_ _reflection_ _into_ _itself_ that the determinations of reflection appear as free _essentialities_, hovering in the void without attraction or repulsion towards one another. In them determinateness has entrenched itself through relation to itself and has fixed itself infinitely. It is the determinate that has made its passing over and its mere positedness subject to itself, or that has bent its reflection into another round into {{{ 11.257 }}} reflection into itself. By this means these determinations make up the determinate shine as it is within essence, the essential shine. For this reason _determining_ _reflection_ is reflection that has come outside itself; essence's self-sameness is lost in the negation, which is what dominates.

There are thus two sides to the determination of reflection, which at first are distinguished from each other. It is _firstly_ positedness, negation as such; it is _secondly_ reflection into itself. According to positedness it is negation as negation; this is thus already its unity with itself. But it is this only _in_ _itself_ as yet; or it is the immediate as sublating itself on itself, as the other of its own self. – To that extent reflection is a determining that stays within itself. Essence does not go outside itself therein; the differences are utterly _posited_, withdrawn back into essence. But on the other side they are not posited things but reflected into their own selves; negation _as_ negation is reflected into self-sameness with itself, not into its other, not into its non-being.

3. Now since the determination of reflection is both a reflected relation within itself and positedness, its nature becomes immediately clearer from this. For qua positedness it is negation as such, a non-being set against an other, namely _against_ absolute reflection into itself, or against essence. But as relation to itself it is reflected into itself. – This reflection of it and that positedness are diverse; its positedness is rather its sublatedness, whereas its being reflected into itself is its subsistence. Insofar, then, as positedness is what is at the same time reflection into itself, the reflective determinateness is _the_ _relation_ _to_ _its_ _otherness_ _upon_ _its_ _own_ _self_. – It is not a determinateness that simply is and rests, one that would be brought into relation with an other so that the related term and its relation are diverse from each other, the former being something within itself, a something that shuts out from itself its other and its relation to this other. Rather, upon itself the determination of reflection is the _determinate_ _side_, and it is the _relation_ of that determinate side qua determinate, namely to its negation. – Quality passes over into another by virtue of its relation; its alteration sets in with its relation. The determination of reflection, by contrast, has withdrawn its otherness into itself. It is _positedness_, negation, but a negation that bends its relation to another back into itself, and a negation that is self-same, the unity of itself and of its other, and only through that an _essentiality_. It is therefore positedness, negation, yet as reflection into itself it is at once the sublatedness of that positedness, an infinite relation to itself.
