## {{{ 11.244 }}} First Section. Essence as Reflection within Itself

Essence hails from being; so far, then, it is not straightway in and for itself but a _result_ of that movement. Or again: take essence at the outset as an immediate, and it is a determinate existence with an other set opposite it; it is only _essential_ existence as against _unessential_ existence. But essence is being sublated in and for itself; whatever is set opposite it is _shine_ merely. Only, this shine is a positing that belongs to essence itself.

Essence is _first_ _reflection_. Reflection gives itself determination; the determinations it has are a positedness that is simultaneously reflection into itself; there are

_secondly_ these _determinations of reflection_, or the _essentialities_, to be examined.

_Thirdly_, essence, as the reflection of determining into its own self, makes itself into _ground_, and thence passes over into _concrete_ _existence_ and _appearance_.
