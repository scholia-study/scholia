## c. Something

Determinateness has been marked off, on existence, as quality; and on quality, itself an existent, the difference _is_ present – the difference between reality and negation. Now however much these differences are on hand within existence, just as much are they null, sublated. Negation is contained in reality itself, which is existence, not some indeterminate, abstract being. Conversely negation is itself existence – no nothing pretending to abstractness, but posited as it stands in itself: as a being, as belonging within existence. So {{{ 21.103 }}} quality at large is nowhere severed from existence, and existence is nothing else than determinate, qualitative being.

More is at work in this sublating of the distinction than a bare withdrawal of it, an external letting-fall of it once more, or a plain going-back to the plain beginning, existence taken simply. Dropping the difference is not possible; for it _is_. So the fact on hand is threefold: existence at large, difference upon it, and the sublating of that difference; existence not as it was at the outset, without difference, but self-equal _once_ _more_, _through_ _sublation_ _of_ _the_ _difference_, its simplicity _mediated_ by that very sublating. That the difference stands sublated is existence's own determinateness; existence is thereby _being-within-itself_; existence counts as an _existent_, as _something_.

Considered as a simple relation to self that has being, the something amounts to the _first_ _negation_ _of_ _negation_. Whatever we call existence, life, thinking and the rest determines itself essentially into an _existent_, a _living_ _thing_, a _thinker_ (I), and the rest. Such determining matters supremely, lest one halt at existence, life, thinking and the rest, or indeed at God<i>head</i> (rather than God), treating these as universalities. Representation is right to reckon _something_ a _real_ _thing_. All the same, _something_ remains a determination lying very near the surface, in the way that _reality_ and _negation_ do, and existence with its determinateness: no longer the void pair, being and nothing, yet abstract determinations through and through. Hence too their currency in ordinary speech, and hence the heavy use made of them by a reflection untrained in philosophy, which decants its distinctions into them and supposes that in them it possesses something firmly and properly determined. – Taken as _something_, the negative of the negative marks no more than the subject's beginning; – being-within-itself is so far altogether indeterminate. It goes on determining itself, next as a being-for-itself, and onward, until only in the concept does it acquire the subject's concrete intensity. At the base of every one of these determinations lies negative unity with self. Yet here negation as the _first_, negation _as_ _such_, wants careful separating from the second, which is negation of negation, the concrete, _absolute_ negativity, the first being by contrast only the _abstract_ negativity.

Taken as negation of negation, _something_ _has_ _being_, for such negation restores relation to self in its simplicity; – and by the same stroke something is the _mediating_ _of_ _itself_ _with_ _itself_. That mediating of itself with itself is on hand already in the simplicity of the something, more determinately afterwards in being-for-itself, in the subject and the rest – already in becoming too, though there merely as mediation wholly abstract; mediation {{{ 21.104 }}} with _itself_ stands _posited_ in the something so far as the latter counts as simply _identical_. – Against the principle of an asserted bare immediacy of knowing, one from which mediation is to be shut out, the fact that mediation is on hand at all may well be pointed out; henceforth, though, no special notice of the moment of mediation will be needed, for mediation lodges everywhere, at every turn, within each and every concept.

Read merely as negation of negation, this mediation with self, which something _in_ _itself_ is, has nothing concretely determined for either of its sides; so it falls together into that simple unity called _being_. Something _is_, and so _is_ an existent as well; further, _in_ _itself_ it is also _becoming_, only a becoming whose moments are no longer merely being and nothing. One moment, being, has now turned into existence and then into an existent. The second is an _existent_ no less, determined however as the negative of the something – an _other_. Something as becoming is a passing-over whose moments are themselves somethings, and for that reason it is _alteration_; – a becoming grown _concrete_ already. – Still, at this stage the something alters only within its concept; nothing yet _posits_ it as mediating and as mediated; for now it merely keeps itself simply in its relation to self, its negative counting as equally qualitative, an _other_ and nothing further.
