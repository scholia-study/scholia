## {{{ 21.66 }}} General Division of Being

Being is _first_ determined over against an other in general;

_Secondly_, it is determining itself within itself;

_Thirdly_, once this provisionality in the dividing has been cast aside, being amounts to the abstract indeterminateness and the immediacy wherein it has to serve as beginning.

Under the _first_ determination, being demarcates itself from _essence_: as its development proceeds, being exhibits its own totality as but One sphere of the concept, and confronts that sphere with another one standing to it as a moment.

Under the _second_, being constitutes the sphere into which its determinations and the entire movement of its reflection fall. Within that sphere being will posit itself under three determinations

I. as _determinateness_, and in that guise, _quality_;

II. as determinateness _sublated_ – _magnitude_, _quantity_;

III. as _quantity_ determined _qualitatively_; _measure_.

This division is here, as was recalled in the introduction concerning such divisions in general, a provisional listing; its determinations have first to arise out of the movement of being itself, and thereby to define and to justify themselves. About the deviation of this division from the customary listing of the categories, namely as quantity, quality, relation and modality, which in _Kant_ are moreover supposed to be only the titles for his categories, though in fact they are themselves categories, only more general ones, – nothing is to be recalled here, since the whole exposition will show what deviates in general from the customary order and significance of the categories.

Only this much may perhaps be noted, that elsewhere the determination of _quantity_ is listed before _quality_, – and this – like most things – without further ground. It has been demonstrated already that the beginning takes being _as_ _such_ {{{ 21.67 }}} – qualitative being – as its point of departure. From the comparison of quality with quantity it is easily evident that the former is by nature the first. For quantity is quality already become negative; _magnitude_ is the determinateness that is no longer one with being but is already distinguished from it, quality sublated, become indifferent. It includes the alterability of being, without the thing itself, being, whose determination it is, being altered by it; whereas qualitative determinateness is one with its being, neither goes beyond it nor stands within it, but is its immediate restrictedness. Quality is therefore, as the _immediate_ determinateness, the first, and the beginning is to be made with it.

_Measure_ is a _relation_, but not relation in general, rather the determinate relation of quality and quantity to each other; the categories that Kant comprises under relation will take their place quite elsewhere. Measure can also be regarded as a modality, if one likes; but since in _Kant_ modality is no longer supposed to constitute a determination of the content but only to concern the content's reference to thinking, to the subjective, this is a wholly heterogeneous reference that does not belong here.

The _third_ determination _of_ _being_ falls within the section on quality, inasmuch as being, as abstract immediacy, lowers itself to a single determinateness over against its others within its own sphere.
