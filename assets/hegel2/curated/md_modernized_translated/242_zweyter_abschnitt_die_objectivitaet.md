## {{{ 12.127 }}} Second Section. Objectivity

In the objective logic's first book abstract _being_ was set forth as passing over into _existence_, yet quite as much as going back into _essence_. In the second, essence shows of itself that it determines itself into _ground_, steps thereby into _concrete_ _existence_ and realizes itself into _substance_, only to go back once more into the _concept_. Of the concept it has now first been shown that it determines itself into _objectivity_. That this last transition is, as regards its determination, the very same as what used to figure in _metaphysics_ under the head of the _syllogism_ from the _concept_, from the _concept_ _of_ _God_ that is, upon _his_ _existence_, or else under the title of the so-called _ontological_ _proof_ of the _existence_ _of_ _God_, is plain of itself. – Just as familiar is it that Descartes's loftiest thought, that God is that _whose_ _concept_ _encloses_ _his_ _being_ _within_ _itself_, once it had sunk down into the bad form of the formal syllogism, into the form namely of that very proof, at length gave way before the critique of reason and before the thought that _existence_ _will_ _not_ _let_ _itself_ _be_ _picked_ _out_ _of_ _the_ _concept_. A few matters bearing on this proof were shed light upon earlier already; in the first Part S. 47. f. where _being_ vanished into its immediate opposite, _non-being_, and _becoming_ showed itself as the truth of the two, notice was drawn to the confusion that arises when, with a determinate existence, it is not the _being_ of it but its _determinate_ _content_ that is held fast and hence meant, when _this_ _determinate_ _content_, a hundred thalers for instance, is set beside another _determinate_ _content_, the context of my perception for instance, my state of assets, and a difference is found in the matter according as the one content is added to the other or not, – as if what were then in question were the difference of being and non-being, or even the difference of being and the concept. Light was further shed in the same place (p. 64 f. and Part II, p. 289) upon the determination of a _sum_ _total_ _of_ _all_ _realities_ that turns up in the ontological proof. – What the just-completed consideration of the _concept_ bears upon, and the whole course through {{{ 12.128 }}} which the concept determines itself into _objectivity_, is precisely the essential subject matter of that proof, _the_ _interconnection_ _of_ _the_ _concept_ _and_ _of_ _existence_. As negativity absolutely identical with itself, the concept is what determines its own self; it has been observed that already in resolving itself in singularity into _judgment_ it posits itself as something _real_, as something _that_ _is_; and this reality, still abstract, brings itself to completion in _objectivity_.

Should it now look as though the transition of the concept into objectivity were something else than the transition from the concept of God to his existence, then it would have to be borne in mind, on the one side, that in the logical course the determinate _content_, God, made no difference, and that the ontological proof would be no more than an application of this logical course to that particular content. On the other side, however, one must essentially call to mind the observation made above, that the subject first obtains determinateness and content in its predicate, whereas prior to the predicate, be it for feeling, intuition and representation whatever else it may, it is for comprehending cognition only a _name_; in the predicate, though, _realization_ in general sets in together with determinateness. – The predicates have, however, to be grasped as still shut up within the concept, and hence as something subjective, with which one has not yet come out into existence; to that extent the _realization_ of the _concept_ in the judgment is, on the one hand, admittedly not yet completed. On the other hand, the bare determining of an object through predicates, where it is not at the same time the realization and objectifying of the concept, likewise stays something so subjective that it is not so much as the genuine knowledge and _determination_ of the _concept_ of the object; – subjective in the sense of abstract reflection and of representations left uncomprehended. – God as living, and still more as absolute spirit, comes to be known only in his _doing_. Early on was the human being directed to know him in his _works_; from these alone can there issue the _determinations_ which are called his _properties_; and in these his _being_ likewise lies contained. So it is that comprehending cognition of his _working_, that is, of himself, grasps God's _concept_ within his _being_, and his being within his concept. _Being_ for itself, or even _existence_, is a determination so poor and so limited that the difficulty of finding it in the concept can hardly have arisen from anything else than the failure to look at what _being_ or _existence_ itself might be. – _Being_, as the utterly _abstract_, _immediate_ _reference_ _to_ _itself_, is nothing else than the abstract moment of the concept, a moment which is abstract universality, and which performs as well what is asked of being, namely to lie _outside_ the concept; for however much it is a moment of the concept, quite as much is it the difference, or the concept's abstract judgment, in which the concept sets its own self facing itself. Even as formal, the concept holds _being_ within it immediately in a _truer_ and _richer_ form, being as it is _singularity_, negativity referring itself to itself.

{{{ 12.129 }}} Insurmountable, though, the difficulty of finding _being_ in the concept generally, and just so in the concept of God, does indeed become, if what is meant is a being such as must turn up within the _context_ of _outer_ _experience_ or under the _form_ of _sensuous_ _perception_, in the way that the _hundred_ _thalers_ turn up in my _state_ _of_ _assets_, a being merely grasped with the hand and not with the spirit, essentially visible to the outer and not to the inner eye; – if the name of being, reality, truth is given to what things possess as sensuous, temporal and perishable. – Where a philosophizing does not raise itself above the senses in the matter of being, there goes with it that in the matter of the concept, too, it does not let go of merely abstract thought; and abstract thought stands facing being.

The habit of taking the concept only for something as one-sided as abstract thought is will already balk at conceding what was put forward just now, namely at regarding the transition from the _concept_ _of_ _God_ to his _being_ in the light of an _application_ of the logical course, set out above, by which the concept objectifies itself. Yet if, as commonly happens, it is allowed that the logical, as the formal element, makes up the form for the cognition of any determinate content whatever, then that relation would at the least have to be granted, unless one is to halt outright at just this opposition of concept to objectivity, at an untrue concept and a reality every bit as untrue, taking these for something ultimate. – Only, in the exposition _of_ _the_ _pure_ _concept_ it was intimated further that this concept is the absolute, divine concept itself, so that in truth no relation of _application_ would obtain, but rather that logical course would be the immediate exhibition of God's self-determination to being. On this point, however, it is to be observed that inasmuch as the concept is to be exhibited as the concept of God, it must be apprehended as it stands already taken up into the _idea_. That pure concept runs through the finite forms of judgment and of syllogism for this reason, that it is not yet posited as being in and for itself one with objectivity, but is grasped only as in the becoming towards it. Thus this objectivity too is not yet divine concrete existence, not yet the reality that shines in the idea. Objectivity nevertheless surpasses in richness and height the ontological proof's _being_ _or_ _existence_ by exactly as much as the pure concept surpasses that metaphysical void, the _sum_ _total_ of all _reality_. – I put off to another occasion, though, the closer illumination of the manifold misunderstanding that logical formalism has carried into the ontological proof, as into the remaining so-called proofs of the existence of God, and likewise of the Kantian critique of them, and the leading back of the thoughts lying at their base to their worth and dignity by the restoring of their true sense.

{{{ 12.130 }}} Several forms of immediacy have already turned up, as has been recalled; but under different determinations. Within the sphere of being it is being itself and existence; within the sphere of essence, concrete existence and then actuality and substantiality; within the sphere of the concept, besides immediacy as abstract universality, henceforth objectivity. – These expressions may be employed as synonyms wherever exactness in philosophical distinctions of concepts is not what is wanted; those determinations have issued from the necessity of the concept; – _being_ is the _first_ immediacy generally, and _existence_ the same immediacy together with the first determinateness. _Concrete_ _existence_, taken with the thing, is that immediacy which issues out of _ground_, – out of essence's simple reflection as a mediation sublating itself. _Actuality_, though, and _substantiality_ are the immediacy issuing from the sublated difference between concrete existence, still unessential as appearance, and its essentiality. _Objectivity_, finally, is the immediacy into which the concept determines itself by sublating its abstraction and mediation. – Philosophy is entitled to select, out of the language of common life, which is cut for the world of representations, such expressions as _seem_ _to_ _come_ _near_ to the determinations of the concept. There can be no question of _establishing_, for a word selected out of the language of common life, that in common life too the same concept is bound up with it for which philosophy employs it, for common life has no concepts but representations, and philosophy itself just is to know the concept of what is otherwise mere representation. It must therefore be enough if something roughly of their difference floats before representation in the case of those of its expressions that are used for philosophical determinations; as may well be so with the expressions in question, that one recognizes in them shadings of representation which stand nearer to the corresponding concepts. – One will perhaps be slower to grant that something may _be_ without _existing_; but at any rate one will scarcely trade _being_, say as the judgment's copula, for the expression to exist, and say: this commodity _exists_ dear, suitable and so forth, the money _exists_ metal, or metallic, in place of: this commodity is dear, suitable and so forth, the money is metal[^1]; _being_, however, and _appearing_, _appearance_ and _actuality_, as also bare _being_ as against _actuality_, {{{ 12.131 }}} are elsewhere well enough distinguished, and all these expressions still more so from _objectivity_. – Yet even were they used as synonyms, philosophy will in any case be at liberty to turn such an idle superfluity of language to account for its distinctions.

[^1]: In a French report, in which the commander states that he was awaiting the wind that ordinarily rises towards morning off the island, so as to steer for land, the expression turns up: le vent _ayant_ été longtems sans _exister_; here the difference has arisen merely out of the ordinary turn of speech, for instance, il a été longtems sans m’écrire.

In the case of the apodictic judgment, where, as in the judgment's completion, the subject loses its determinateness over against the predicate, notice was drawn to the doubled sense of _subjectivity_ springing from that source, namely the sense of the concept and equally that of the externality and contingency which otherwise stands facing the concept. Just so there appears for objectivity too the doubled sense of standing _over_ _against_ the self-subsistent _concept_, and yet also of being _that_ _which_ _is_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_. Where the object in the first sense stands facing the I=I that subjective idealism pronounces the absolutely true, it is the world in its manifoldness and its immediate existence, a world with which the I, or the concept, engages only in endless struggle, so as to give, by negating this other that is _null_ _in_ _itself_, the _actual_ _truth_ of its self-sameness to the first certainty of itself. – In a less determinate sense it thus signifies an object generally for some interest or activity of the subject.

In the opposing sense, though, the objective signifies _that_ _which_ _is_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, what is free of limitation and of opposition. Rational principles, perfect works of art and so forth are called _objective_ so far as they stand free, above every contingency. Even though principles that are rational, theoretical or ethical belong only to the subjective, to consciousness, what in them is in and for itself is called objective all the same; knowledge of the truth is located in knowing the object as it is qua object, free from any addition of subjective reflection, and right conduct in the observance of objective laws, laws which are without subjective origin and admit of no arbitrary will and no handling that would pervert their necessity.

At the present standpoint of our treatise objectivity has, in the first place, the sense of the _being_ _of_ _the_ _concept_ _that_ _is_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, of the concept which has sublated the _mediation_ posited in its self-determination into an _immediate_ reference to its own self. This immediacy is thereby itself immediate and wholly shot through with the concept, just as the concept's totality is immediately identical with its being. But since the concept has further to bring forth quite as much the free being-for-itself of its subjectivity, there sets in a relation of the concept as _purpose_ to objectivity, wherein the immediacy of the latter turns into what is negative towards the concept and what is to be determined by its activity, and so receives the other sense, that of being what is null in and for itself insofar as it stands facing the concept.

{{{ 12.132 }}} _In_ _the_ _first_ _place_, then, objectivity is in its immediacy, whose moments, for the sake of the totality of all the moments, subsist in self-subsistent indifference as _objects_ _outside_ _one_ _another_, and in their relation have the _subjective_ _unity_ _of_ _the_ _concept_ only as an _inner_ one or as an _outer_; _mechanism_. – Since, however, within it

_Secondly_ that unity shows itself as the _immanent_ law of the objects themselves, their relation becomes their _peculiar_ difference grounded in their law, and a reference in which their determinate self-subsistence sublates itself; _chemism_.

_Thirdly_ this essential unity of the objects is by that very fact posited as distinguished from their self-subsistence; it is the subjective concept, yet posited as being, in and for [its] own self, referred to objectivity, as _purpose_; _teleology_.

Since purpose is the concept posited as referring on its own self to objectivity and as sublating through itself its defect of being subjective, the purposiveness at first _outer_ turns, through the realizing of purpose, into the _inner_ one, and into the _idea_.
