## The Transition

Ideality admits of being named the _quality_ of infinity; in essence, however, it is the process that _becoming_ is, and so a transition — on the model of becoming's transition into existence — which now has to be set out. Being the sublating of finitude — of finitude as such, that is, and no less of that merely negative infinity which does nothing but stand opposed to finitude — this return into itself is _reference_ _to_ _itself_, is _being_. Negation being present in this being, the being is _existence_; but inasmuch as that negation is, further, in essence a negation of negation, a negation referring to itself, it is the existence that goes by the name of _being-for-itself_.
