## Remark

In this form of existence the one is that stage of the category which turned up among the ancients as the _atomistic_ _principle_, for which the essence of things consists in the _atom_ and _the_ _void_, (τὸ ἄτομον or τὰ ἄτομα καὶ τὸ κενὸν.) Abstraction, ripened into this form, has gained a determinateness greater than that of Parmenides' _being_ and Heraclitus' _becoming_. However _high_ it pitches itself in making this simple determinateness of the one and of the void the principle of all things, in tracing back the endless manifoldness of the world to this simple opposition, and in making bold to cognize that manifoldness out of it, just as _easy_ is it for representational reflecting to picture atoms _here_ and, _alongside_ them, the void. No wonder, then, that the atomistic principle has kept itself alive at every {{{ 21.154 }}} period; the equally trivial and external relation of _composition_, which still has to be added if any semblance of something concrete and of a manifoldness is to be reached, is every bit as popular as are the atoms and the void themselves. Being-for-itself is the one together with the void, the supreme qualitative being-within-itself sunk down into utter _externality_; since the one negates all otherness, its immediacy or being is posited as being no longer determinable, no longer alterable, and for such absolute brittleness every determination, every manifoldness, every connection accordingly remains a downright external relating.

In this externality, though, the atomistic principle did not stay put among its first thinkers; over and above its abstraction it also held a speculative determination in this, that the _void_ came to be cognized as the _wellspring_ of _motion_; and that is an altogether different relating of the atom and the void than the bare side-by-side and the indifference of these two determinations towards one another. That the void is the wellspring of motion does not carry the paltry sense that something can move only into a void and not into a space already filled, for in such a space it would find no room left open; on that construal the void would be only the presupposition or condition and not the _ground_ of motion, just as motion itself would be presupposed as already at hand and the essential thing, a ground of it, forgotten. The view that the void makes up the ground of motion harbours the deeper thought that within the negative as such there lies the ground of becoming, of the unrest belonging to self-movement; though in that sense the negative has to be taken as the infinite's genuine negativity. – Only as the _negative_ relating of the one to its _negative_, to the one, i.e. to its own self, though a self posited as existent, is the void the _ground_ _of_ _motion_.

For the rest, though, further determinations of the ancients regarding a shape or position of the atoms and the direction of their motion are arbitrary and external enough; and they stand withal in direct contradiction with the fundamental determination of the atom. From the atoms, the principle of the highest externality and hence of the highest {{{ 21.155 }}} conceptlessness, physics suffers in its molecules and particles quite as much as does political science, which starts out from the singular will of individuals.
