## b. Real Ground

As has come to light, the determinateness of the ground is in one part a determinateness of the _base_, that is, a content-determination; in another part it is the otherness within the _ground-reference_ itself, namely the differentiatedness of that reference's content from its form; and the reference of ground and grounded runs out into an outward form upon a content which stands indifferent to these determinations. – In fact, though, neither of the two is external to the other; for the content is precisely this: to be the _identity_ which the _ground_ has with itself _in_ _the_ _grounded_, and which _the_ _grounded_ has in the _ground_. It has shown itself that the side of the ground is itself something posited, and that the side of the grounded is itself ground; each is in its own self this identity of the whole. Yet because they belong at the same time to form and constitute its determinate differentiatedness, each is, _in_ _its_ _determinateness_, the whole's identity with itself. Each therefore carries, over against the other, a _different_ _content_. – Or, looked at from the content's side: since the content is self-identity _as_ _the_ _ground-reference_, it carries this difference of form essentially in its own self, and is one thing as ground and another as grounded.

Now with ground and grounded holding a different content, the ground-reference is no longer a formal one; the return into the ground and the emergence out of it into what is posited is no tautology any more; the _ground_ stands realized. Whoever asks after a ground is therefore in fact demanding for that ground some content-determination other than the one whose ground is in question.

This reference now goes on to determine itself further. For to the extent that its two sides are content of differing kinds, they stand indifferent to one another; each is an immediate determination self-identical in itself. Further, taken as ground and grounded referred to one another, the ground is what stands reflected into itself within the other as within its positedness; {{{ 11.308 }}} the content, accordingly, that belongs to the side of the ground is present in the grounded as well, and the grounded, as what is posited, holds its self-identity and its subsistence in the ground alone. Over and above this content of the ground, however, the grounded from now on has a content peculiar to itself as well, and so is the _unity_ of a _twofold_ content. As a unity of what differs, this is admittedly their negative unity; but since the content-determinations stand indifferent to one another, it amounts only to their empty reference, contentless in its own self, and not to their mediation — a _one_, or a _something_, as the outward linking of the two.

Within the real ground-reference, then, a double is at hand: _for_ _one_ _thing_, the content-determination that is ground carries on with itself in the positedness, so as to constitute the simply identical element of ground and grounded; the grounded on this count harbours the ground entire within it, and the reference of the two is a differenceless essential solidity. Whatever accrues in the grounded beyond this simple _essence_ is accordingly no more than unessential form, outward content-determinations that as such go free of the ground and make up an immediate manifoldness. That essential, then, is not the ground of this unessential, nor is it the ground of the _reference_ the two bear to one another within the grounded. It is a positively identical element housed in the grounded, though it sets itself there into no difference of form and instead, as content referring itself to itself, is an indifferent positive _base_. – _For_ _another_, whatever is linked with this base in the something counts as indifferent content, yet as the unessential side. What chiefly matters is the _reference_ borne by the base and the unessential manifoldness. Since the determinations so referred are indifferent content, however, this reference too is _not_ _ground_; one of them is indeed determined as essential and the other merely as unessential or posited content, but for content referring itself to itself such form is outward to both. The _one_ _of_ _the_ _something_ that constitutes their reference is on that account no reference of form but a merely outward band, one that does not hold the unessential manifold content as something _posited_; it too, then, is nothing but _base_.

The ground, in determining itself as real, thereby breaks up into outward determinations, and does so on account of the difference of content that constitutes its reality. The two references — the _essential_ _content_, being the simple _immediate_ _identity_ that ground and grounded have; and then _the_ _something_, being the reference of the differing content — are _two_ _different_ _bases_; the self-identical form of the ground, whereby one and the same is at one time essential and at another posited, has disappeared; the ground-reference has in this way turned _external_ to its own self.

What operates now, therefore, is an outward ground, one that brings differing content into linkage and settles which of them is ground and which the thing posited through {{{ 11.309 }}} it; in the content on either side that determination is not to be found. The real ground is on that account _reference_ _to_ _other_ — of content to other content on the one hand, and on the other of the ground-reference itself (of the form) to an other, namely to an _immediate_ that it has not posited.
