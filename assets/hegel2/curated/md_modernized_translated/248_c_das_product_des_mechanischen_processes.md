## c. The Product of the Mechanical Process

What _formal_ mechanism yields as its product is the object at large, an indifferent totality at which _determinateness_ stands as something _posited_. Since through this the object has stepped into the process as something _determinate_, the outcome is, on the one side, in the going-under of that determinateness, _rest_, the object's original formalism, namely the negativity of that being-determined-for-itself of its own. On the other side, however, it is the sublating of being-determined, as the _positive_ _reflection_ _of_ _that_ _being-determined_ into itself, determinateness gone back within itself, in other words the _posited_ _totality_ _of_ _the_ _concept_; the _genuine_ _singularity_ of the object. The object, indeterminately universal to begin with, then _particular_, now stands determined as _objectively_ _singular_; so that in this the _shine_ _of_ _singularity_, which is nothing but a self-subsistence _setting_ _itself_ _over_ _against_ substantial universality, has been sublated.

This reflection into itself is now, as it has come out, the objective being-one of the objects, which is individual self-subsistence, – the _centre_. _Secondly_, negativity's reflection is that universality which is no fate confronting determinateness but one determined within itself, a rational fate, – a universality that _particularizes_ _itself_ _in_ _its_ _own_ _self_, the quiet difference holding fast amid the non-self-subsistent particularity of the objects and amid their process, the _law_. This result is the truth, and hence also the foundation, of the mechanical process.
