## a. Analytic Cognition

One sometimes finds the difference of analytic from synthetic cognition stated in this way, that the one advances from what is known to what is unknown, the other from what is unknown to what is known. Look at this difference more nearly, however, and it will prove hard to find in it any determinate thought at all, still less a concept. It may be said that cognition sets out quite generally from what one is unacquainted with, since nobody comes to know a thing he is acquainted with already. Conversely, it sets out just as well from what is known; this is a tautological proposition; – that with which it sets out, and which it therefore actually does cognize, is a known thing precisely on that account; whatever has not been cognized yet, and is to be cognized only afterwards, remains an unknown. So far one has to say that cognition, once it has made a start, advances always from what is known to what is unknown.

What marks analytic cognition off has already determined itself to this: that, being the first premise of the entire syllogism, it does not yet have mediation belonging to it, but is the concept's immediate imparting of itself, an imparting that as yet holds no otherness within it, and wherein activity divests itself of its {{{ 12.203 }}} negativity. That immediacy of the reference is nevertheless for just that reason itself a mediation, for it is the concept's negative reference to the object, one that annihilates its own self and thereby renders itself simple and identical. This reflection-into-itself is something merely subjective, since within its mediation the difference is still on hand only as the presupposed difference _that_ _is_ _in_ _itself_, as a diversity _of_ _the_ _object_ within the object. The determination brought about by this reference is accordingly the form of simple _identity_, of _abstract_ _universality_. Analytic cognition therefore takes this identity quite generally for its principle, and transition into an other, the tying together of what is diverse, is shut out from it and from its activity.

Taking analytic cognition now more nearly, the start is made from a _presupposed_ subject matter, one therefore singular and _concrete_, be it a subject matter already _finished_ for representation or else a _problem_, given, that is, only in its circumstances and conditions, not yet lifted out of them on its own and set forth in simple self-subsistence. The analysis of such a subject matter cannot consist in its being merely _dissolved_ into the particular _representations_ that it may hold; a dissolution of that kind, and the apprehending of what it yields, would be a business falling outside cognition, one touching only a closer _acquaintance_, a determining inside the sphere of _representing_. Analysis, having the concept for its ground, has essentially the determinations of the concept for its products, and these as such as are _immediately_ _contained_ in the subject matter. From the nature of the idea of cognition it has emerged that the activity of the subjective concept must, on the one side, be looked upon as nothing but a _developing_ of _what_ _is_ _already_ _in_ _the_ _object_, seeing that the object is itself nothing other than the totality of the concept. It is one-sided in just the same measure to represent analysis as though the subject matter held nothing that had not been _put_ _into_ it, as it is one-sided to suppose that the determinations which result are merely _drawn_ _out_ of it. The first representation is voiced, as everyone knows, by subjective idealism, which takes cognition's activity in analysis to be a one-sided _positing_ and nothing besides, beyond which the _thing-in-itself_ stays concealed; the second belongs to so-called realism, which grasps the subjective concept as an empty identity that _takes_ _up_ into itself the determinations of thought _from_ _without_. – Analytic cognition, which converts given material into logical determinations, has shown itself to be both at once, a _positing_ that just as immediately determines itself to be a _presupposing_; hence the logical may, on account of the latter, appear as something already _finished_ in the subject matter, and on account of the former as the {{{ 12.204 }}} _product_ of a merely subjective activity. The two moments, however, do not admit of being sundered; in that abstract form into which analysis lifts it out, the logical is on hand indeed only within cognition, just as, conversely, the logical is not a _posited_ thing merely but a thing _that_ _is_ _in_ _itself_.

So far as analytic cognition is the transformation just exhibited, it runs through no further _middle_ _terms_; the determination is rather to that extent _immediate_ and carries just this sense, of being proper to the subject matter and belonging to it in itself, hence of having been apprehended out of it with no subjective mediation. – Cognition, however, is further supposed to be a _progression_ as well, a _development_ _of_ _differences_. But since, on the determination it carries here, it is without concept and undialectical, the only difference it possesses is a _given_ _difference_, and its progression takes place solely along the determinations of the _material_. An _immanent_ progression it seems to have only so far as the derived determinations of thought admit of being analysed over again, so far, that is, as they are still something concrete; what stands highest and last in such analysing is the abstract highest essence, – or abstract subjective identity, with diversity confronting it. Such progression, however, is nothing but the repeating of the one original deed of analysis, namely a redetermining, as something _concrete_, of what was already taken up into the abstract form of the concept, then an analysis of that, then over again the determining of the abstract issuing from it as something concrete, and so on. – The determinations of thought seem all the same to hold a transition within their own selves. Once the subject matter has been determined as whole, one does indeed pass on from that to the _other_ determination, _that_ _of_ _part_; from _cause_ to the other determination, that of _effect_, and so forth. Yet no progression takes place here, inasmuch as whole and parts, cause and effect, are _relations_, and for this formal cognition relations so _finished_ that the one determination gets _found_ tied essentially to the other. A subject matter determined as _cause_ or as _part_ is thereby determined by the _whole_ relation, by both its sides at once. Something synthetic though this may be _in_ _itself_, the connection counts for analytic cognition just as much as something merely _given_ as does any other connection within its material, and hence has no place in the business peculiar to it. Whether such a connection be otherwise determined as something a priori or a posteriori makes no difference here, so long as it is grasped as something _found_ _already_ _there_, or, in the phrase that has also been used, as a _fact_ of consciousness that with the determination: _whole_, the determination: _part_ is bound up, and so on. Though Kant put forward the profound {{{ 12.205 }}} observation of _synthetic_ principles a priori and discerned as their root the unity of self-consciousness, and so the concept's identity with itself, he nonetheless takes over the _determinate_ connection, the concepts of relation and the synthetic principles themselves, as _given_ _by_ _formal_ _logic_; a deduction of them would have had to be an exhibition of how that simple unity of self-consciousness passes over into these its determinations and differences; but the showing of this genuinely synthetic progression, of the concept producing its own self, Kant spared himself the trouble of supplying.

It is well known that _arithmetic_, along with the more general _sciences_ _of_ _discrete_ _magnitude_, goes by preference under the name of _analytic_ _science_ and of _analysis_. Their way of cognizing is in fact analytic in the most immanent fashion, and it is worth considering briefly on what this rests. – Analytic cognition elsewhere starts out from a concrete material carrying a contingent manifoldness in it; every difference of content, and the progression to further content, hangs upon that material. Arithmetical and algebraic material, by contrast, is something rendered wholly abstract and indeterminate beforehand, on which every peculiarity of relation has been wiped out, so that every determination and every linking is now external to it. Of this sort is the principle of discrete magnitude, the _one_. This relationless atom admits of being multiplied into a _plurality_ and of being determined and gathered from outside into an amount; such multiplying and bounding is an empty progression and determining that stays put at this same principle of the abstract one. In what way the _numbers_ are further gathered together and parted rests solely on the positing done by the one who cognizes. _Magnitude_ is quite generally the category inside which these determinations get made; – it is determinateness turned _indifferent_, so that the subject matter possesses no determinateness immanent to it and therefore none _given_ to cognition. So far as cognition has to begin with given itself a contingent diversity of numbers, these now constitute the material for further working and for manifold ratios. Ratios of that kind, their invention and their working out, look indeed to be nothing immanent to analytic cognition but rather something contingent and given; and in keeping with that these ratios, with the operations bearing upon them, are commonly presented _one_ _after_ _the_ _other_, as _diverse_, with no remark upon any inner connection. Yet a guiding principle is easy to make out, and it is the immanent one of analytic identity, which upon the diverse shows itself as _equality_; the advance consists in reducing the unequal to an ever {{{ 12.206 }}} greater equality. To take an example from the first elements: addition is a gathering together of numbers wholly contingently _unequal_, multiplication of _equal_ ones, upon which there follows further the ratio of _equality_ between _amount_ and _unit_, and the ratio of powers sets in.

Since the determinateness both of the subject matter and of the ratios is a _posited_ one, the further operating with them is likewise wholly analytic, and analytic science accordingly has not so much _theorems_ as it has _problems_. An analytic theorem holds the problem within it as already solved for its own self, and the entirely external difference attaching to the two sides which it sets as equal is so unessential that a theorem of the kind would look like a trivial identity. Kant did, to be sure, pronounce the proposition 5+7=12 a _synthetic_ proposition, on the ground that one side sets out the same thing in the shape of several, of 5 and 7, and the other in the shape of a single one, of 12. But if the analytic is not to signify the utterly abstract identical and tautological 12=12, and if there is to be any advance within it at all, then some difference must be at hand, though a difference resting on no quality, no determinateness of reflection, and less still on any determinateness of the concept. 5+7 and 12 are throughout one and the same content; on the first side there is expressed besides the _demand_ that 5 and 7 be gathered into _one_ expression, which is to say: as five is something counted together, the breaking off having been quite arbitrary and the counting able just as well to run on, so the counting shall now run on in the same manner, with the determination that the units to be added shall number seven. So the 12 is a result yielded by 5 and 7 and by an operation which, being already posited, is by its nature a doing entirely external and thoughtless, such that a machine can carry it out as well. Here there is not the slightest transition into an _other_; there is a mere carrying on, that is, a _repeating_, of the very operation through which 5 and 7 arose.

The _proof_ of such a theorem, – and one would be required if it were a synthetic proposition – would lie merely in the operation of counting on from 5 as 7 determines, and in recognizing that what is thus counted on agrees with what one otherwise calls 12, which again is nothing further than just that determinate counting on itself. In place of the form of theorems one therefore takes up at once the form of the _problem_, of the _demand_ for the operation, that is, the pronouncing of only the _one_ side of the equation that would make up the theorem, the other side of which is now to be found. The problem holds the content and names the determinate operation to be carried out upon it. No brittle material endowed with specific relations restricts the operation; it is an external, subjective doing, and the material on {{{ 12.207 }}} which its determinations get posited takes them up indifferently. The whole difference separating the conditions set up in the problem from the result reached in the _resolution_ comes to this alone, that in the result the uniting or separating has _actually_ been carried out in the determinate manner which the problem stated.

It is therefore a most superfluous piece of scaffolding to bring in here the form of the geometrical method, which has to do with synthetic propositions, and to let a _proof_ follow the problem over and above the _resolution_. Such a proof can voice nothing but the tautology that the resolution is correct because one has operated as the problem directed. If the problem runs that several numbers are to be added, then the resolution runs: add them; and the proof shows the resolution correct on the ground that adding was what the problem called for and adding is what has been done. Where the problem holds more composite determinations and operations, for instance the multiplying of decimal numbers, and the resolution states nothing beyond the mechanical procedure, a proof does become necessary; but it can be nothing further than an analysis of those determinations and of the operation, out of which the resolution follows of itself. By thus separating off the _resolution_ as a mechanical procedure and the _proof_ as a recalling of the nature of the subject matter to be handled and of the operation itself, precisely the advantage of the analytic problem gets lost, namely that the _construction_ can be derived immediately from the problem and so set out as _intelligible_ in and for itself; taken the other way, the construction is expressly saddled with a defect proper to the synthetic method. – In the higher analysis, where, chiefly with the ratio of powers, ratios of discrete magnitudes come in that are qualitative and hang upon determinacies of the concept, the problems and theorems do certainly hold synthetic determinations; there _other_ determinations and ratios have to be taken as middle terms than those _immediately_ _stated_ by the problem or the theorem. For the rest, these determinations called in to help must also be of a kind that finds its ground in the consideration and development of one side of the problem or the theorem; the synthetic look arises solely from the problem or the theorem not itself naming that side already. – Take the problem, for instance, of finding what the powers of an equation's roots add up to: it is solved by considering, and then linking, those functions of the roots which the equation's coefficients are. The determination called in to help here, that of the functions of the coefficients and of their linking, the problem does not already express; the development, for the rest, is itself wholly analytic. Thus the resolution of the equation xᵐ−1=0 by means of the sines, and likewise the immanent algebraic resolution which Gauss, as is well known, found by means of {{{ 12.208 }}} considering the _residuum_ of xᵐ⁻¹−1 when divided by m, and the primitive roots so called, – one among the weightiest extensions that analysis has received in recent times, – is a synthetic resolution, because the determinations called in to help, the sines or the consideration of the residues, are not a determination of the problem itself.

The nature of that analysis which considers so-called infinite differences of variable magnitudes, of the differential and integral calculus, has been treated more at length in the _first_ _part_ of this logic. It was shown there that what lies at the base here is a qualitative determination of magnitude, and one graspable through the concept alone. The transition to it from magnitude as such is analytic no longer; and this is why mathematics has to this day been unable to justify by its own means, that is, mathematically, the operations resting on that transition, the transition not being of a mathematical nature. _Leibnitz_, to whom the credit is given of having shaped the reckoning with infinite differences into a _calcul_, effected the transition, as was cited in that same place, in a manner that is the most inadequate of all, as utterly devoid of concept as it is unmathematical; grant the transition once, however, – and in the present state of the science it amounts to no more than a presupposition, – and what follows is certainly nothing but a series of ordinary analytic operations.

It has been recalled that analysis turns synthetic so far as it comes upon _determinations_ no longer _posited_ by the problems themselves. The general transition from analytic to synthetic cognition, however, lies in the transition that is necessary out of the form of immediacy into mediation, out of abstract identity into difference. In its activity the analytic stops at the determinations taken generally, so far as these refer to their own selves; through their _determinateness_, however, they are essentially of this nature besides, that they _refer_ _to_ _an_ _other_. It has been recalled already that even where analytic cognition advances along relations which are not an externally given material but determinations of thought, it stays analytic all the same, so far as these relations too are for it something _given_. But since the abstract identity which this cognition alone knows as its own is essentially _identity_ _of_ _what_ _is_ _differentiated_, that identity must be its own as such as well, and for the subjective concept the _connection_ too must come to be something posited by the concept and identical with it.
