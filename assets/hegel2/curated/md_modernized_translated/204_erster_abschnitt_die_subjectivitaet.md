## {{{ 12.31 }}} First Section. Subjectivity

The concept is at the outset the _formal_ one, the concept at the _beginning_, or the concept that _is_ in the mode of the _immediate_. – Within the immediate unity, its difference or _positedness_ is _at_ _first_ still simple in itself and no more than _a_ _shine_, with the result that the difference's moments are without mediation the concept's totality and _are_ nothing but the CONCEPT _as_ _such_.

_Secondly_, however, being absolute negativity, the concept dirempts itself and posits itself as its own _negative_, as its own _other_; and since it is as yet the _immediate_ concept, this positing, or differentiating, carries the determination that each of the moments turns _indifferent_ _towards_ _the_ _others_ and comes to be on its own account; within such _partition_ the concept's unity is nothing further than _external_ _reference_. So taken, as the _reference_ of moments posited as _self-subsistent_ and _indifferent_, the concept is the JUDGMENT.

_Thirdly_, the judgment does hold within it the unity of a concept gone lost in its self-subsistent moments, but that unity is not _posited_. Posited it becomes by way of the dialectical movement of the judgment, which has thereby turned into the SYLLOGISM, into the concept completely posited; for within the syllogism the concept's moments stand posited as _self-subsistent_ extremes quite as much as does their _mediating_ _unity_.

But inasmuch as this _unity_ itself, as the unifying _middle_ _term_, and the _moments_, as _self-subsistent_ extremes, at first confront one another _immediately_, the contradictory relation obtaining in the _formal_ _syllogism_ sublates itself, and the _completeness_ of the concept goes over into the unity of _totality_, the concept's _subjectivity_ into its _objectivity_.
