## B. Nothing

_Nothing_, _the_ _pure_ _nothing_; it is simple self-equality, complete emptiness, want of determination and of content; undifferentiatedness within its own self. – To the extent that intuiting or thinking may be brought up at this point, it is taken as a difference whether what gets intuited or thought is something or _nothing_. Intuiting or thinking nothing accordingly carries a meaning; the two being held apart, nothing _is_ (exists) within our intuiting or our thinking; or rather nothing is that very empty intuiting and thinking; and it is the same empty intuiting or thinking that pure being is. – Nothing, then, is the same determination – or better, the same lack of determination – and so is throughout the same as what pure _being_ is.
