## b. The Second Figure: P-S-U

1. What the first qualitative syllogism comes to in truth is that something stands joined together with a qualitative determinateness, taken as a universal one, not in and for itself {{{ 12.100 }}} but through a contingency, or within a singularity. _The_ _subject_ of the syllogism, in a quality of that kind, has not gone back into its concept; it is grasped only in its _externality_; immediacy is what makes up the ground of the reference, and so the mediation; to that extent the middle term is in truth the singular.

Further, though, the syllogistic reference is the _sublation_ of immediacy; not an immediate reference is the conclusion, but one holding by way of a third; a _negative_ unity is therefore contained in it; and so the mediation is now determined to hold a _negative_ moment within itself.

The premises in this second syllogism are P–S and S–U; of the two, only the former is still immediate, whereas S–U is a mediated one already, namely through the first syllogism; so the second syllogism has the first for its presupposition, and inversely the first the second. – The two extremes are in this determined against one another as particular and universal; the latter so far keeps its _place_, being predicate; but the particular has swapped its own, being now subject, or _posited_ _under_ _the_ _determination_ _of_ _the_ _extreme_ _of_ _singularity_, while the _singular_, for its part, has been posited _with_ _the_ _determination_ _of_ _the_ _middle_ _term_, namely of particularity. Both of them, therefore, are no longer those abstract immediacies that they were in the first syllogism. As concretes, however, they are not yet posited; each standing in the _place_ of the other, each finds itself posited in its own determination and, though only _externally_, in the _other_ determination at the same time.

The _determinate_, _objective_ _sense_ this syllogism carries is that the universal is _not_ _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_ some determinate particular; for it is rather the totality of its particulars; rather, its being thus _one_ of its species comes about _through_ _singularity_; the rest of its species are shut out from it by immediate externality. The particular, on the other side, is likewise not the universal immediately and in and for itself; the negative unity strips the determinateness off it and raises it thereby into universality. – Singularity bears itself _negatively_ toward the particular so far as it is supposed to be the latter's predicate; predicate of the particular it is _not_.

2. At first, though, the Termini are still immediate determinatenesses; by themselves they have grown into no objective meaning; the altered _position_ two of them receive is a form that so far attaches to them only externally; as in the first syllogism generally, they are therefore still a content indifferent toward one another, two qualities knit together not in and for their own selves but through a contingent singularity.

{{{ 12.101 }}} The syllogism of the first figure was the _immediate_ one, or just as much the syllogism so far as it stands in its concept as _abstract_ _form_, a form not yet realized in its determinations. That this pure form has passed over into another figure is, on the one side, the realization of the concept begun, since the _negative_ moment of mediation, and with it a further form-determinateness, gets posited on the at first immediate, qualitative determinateness of the Terminorum. – At the same time, though, this is a _becoming-other_ of the pure form of the syllogism; the syllogism no longer answers to it completely, and the determinateness posited on its Terminis is diverse from that original form-determination. – So far as it is regarded merely as a subjective syllogism running its course in an external reflection, it passes for a _species_ of syllogism, one that ought to answer to the genus, that is, to S–P–U, the universal schema of the syllogism. At first, though, it does not answer to it; the two premises it has are P–S, or S–P, and S–U; the Medius Terminus is accordingly subsumed both times, or is both times subject, so that the two other Termini inhere in it; hence not a middle term which is to be at one time subsuming, or predicate, and at another subsumed, or subject, in which the one Terminus is to inhere while it itself inheres in the other. – That this syllogism fails to answer to the universal form of the syllogism has the genuine sense that this form has passed over into it, its truth consisting in being a subjective, contingent joining-together. Should the conclusion in the second figure be correct, (leaving aside, that is, the limitation soon to be mentioned, which turns it into something indeterminate,) it is so because it is correct for itself, and not on the strength of being this syllogism's conclusion. With the conclusion of the first figure, however, matters stand the same; it is just this truth of the first that the second figure posits. – On the view that the second figure is merely _a_ _species_, one overlooks how the first passes over of necessity into this second form, and stays put at the first as the genuine form. So far, then, as in the second figure (which out of old habit, and with no further ground, is listed as _the_ _third_) a syllogism that is _correct_ in this subjective sense is likewise supposed to have place, it would have to be adequate to the first; hence, the one premise S–U having the relation of subsumption of the Medius Terminus under the one extreme, the other premise S–P would have to acquire the relation opposite to the one it has, so that S would be what P is subsumed under. Such a relation, though, would sublate the determinate judgment: S is P, and could have place only within an indeterminate judgment, – within a particular one; whence in this figure the conclusion can only be a particular one. The particular judgment, though, is, as noted above, positive as much as negative; – a {{{ 12.102 }}} conclusion, therefore, that can hardly be credited with much worth. – So far as the particular and the universal are also the extremes, and immediate determinatenesses indifferent toward one another, their relation is itself indifferent; the one or the other may be taken at pleasure as Terminus Major or Minor, and hence the one or the other premise as major or as minor premise.

3. Being positive as much as negative, the conclusion is thus a reference indifferent toward these determinatenesses, and so a _universal_ one. Looked at more closely, the first syllogism's mediation was _in_ _itself_ contingent; in the second this contingency stands _posited_. The mediation is thus one that sublates itself; it carries the determination of singularity and of immediacy; what gets joined together by this syllogism must rather be identical _in_ _itself_ _and_ _immediately_, for that middle term, _the_ _immediate_ _singularity_, is the infinitely manifold and external being-determined. What is posited in it, then, is rather the mediation that is _external_ to itself. But singularity's externality is universality; that mediation by way of the immediate singular points out past itself to the mediation _other_ _to_ _it_, which comes about accordingly through the _universal_. – Or: whatever the second syllogism is supposed to unite must be joined together _immediately_; out of the _immediacy_ lying at its base no determinate joining-together comes to pass. The immediacy it points on to is the other over against its own, – the sublated first immediacy of being, – hence the immediacy reflected into itself, or what is _in_ _itself_, the _abstract_ _universal_.

On the side considered, this syllogism's transition was a _becoming-other_, like the passing over of being, since what lies at its base is the qualitative, namely immediate singularity. In terms of the concept, however, singularity joins particular and universal together so far as it _sublates_ _the_ _determinateness_ _of_ _the_ _particular_; which is what shows itself as this syllogism's contingency; the extremes do not get joined together through the determinate reference in which they stand to the Medius Terminus; the Medius Terminus is therefore _not_ _their_ _determinate_ _unity_, and the positive unity still accruing to it is only _the_ _abstract_ _universality_. Yet once the middle term is posited in this determination, its truth, what we have is another form of the syllogism.
