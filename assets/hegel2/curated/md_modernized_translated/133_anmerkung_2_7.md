## Remark 2

Identity is what I shall examine more closely in this Remark, and as _the_ _principle_ _of_ _identity_, the one usually put forward as the _first_ _law_ _of_ _thought_.

Taken in its positive wording, A = A, this proposition says at first nothing beyond an empty _tautology_. Hence the just remark that a law of thought like this is _without_ _content_ and carries one no further. So [it] is that empty identity which holds fast those who take it, just as it stands, for something true, and who never tire of protesting that identity is not diversity, but that identity and diversity are two diverse things. They fail to notice that already in this they are themselves saying _that_ _identity_ _is_ _something_ _diverse_; for they say that _identity_ _is_ _diverse_ from diversity; and since this has at the same time to be conceded as identity's nature, what lies in it is that identity is such — not externally, but on its own account, in its nature — as to be diverse. – But further, in clinging to this motionless identity, which has its counterpart in diversity, they fail to notice that they thereby make of it a one-sided determinateness which as such holds no truth. It is conceded that the principle of identity gives voice to only a one-sided determinateness, that it holds only the _formal_, an _abstract_, _incomplete_ _truth_. – But this correct judgment carries with it directly _that_ _only_ _in_ _the_ {{{ 11.263 }}} _unity_ _of_ _identity_ _with_ _diversity_ _is_ _truth_ _complete_, and hence that it subsists in that unity alone. Where it is maintained that such identity is imperfect, the totality by which identity gets measured and found wanting hovers before thought as the perfect; but where, on the other side, identity is held fast as absolutely separated from diversity and, in this separation, is taken for something essential, valid, true, then in these clashing assertions nothing can be seen but a failure to hold two thoughts together — that identity taken abstractly is essential, and that just so taken it is equally imperfect; a failure of consciousness regarding that negative movement as which identity gets exhibited in these assertions themselves. – Or, when the matter is put thus, that identity is _essential_ _identity_ _as_ _separation_ from diversity, or _in_ _separation_ _from_ _diversity_, then this is directly to have pronounced its truth, namely that what it consists in is being _separation_ as such, or being essential _in_ _the_ _separation_, that is, being _nothing_ _for_ _itself_ but a _moment_ _of_ _separation_.

As for the remaining credentials of the absolute _truth_ _of_ _the_ identity _principle_, they rest on _experience_ to just this extent, that every consciousness is invoked as witness to the experience that, the moment one utters this proposition to it, A is A, _a_ _tree_ _is_ _a_ _tree_, it grants the proposition straight off and is content with it, since a proposition immediately clear of itself needs no further grounding or proof.

For one thing, this appeal to experience, that every consciousness universally acknowledges it, is a mere turn of phrase. For nobody intends to claim that the abstract proposition A = A has been tried out experimentally on every consciousness. To that extent the appeal to experience actually made is not meant in earnest; it is only the _assurance_ that, were the experience to be made, the result of universal acknowledgment would follow. – Suppose, though, that what was meant were not the abstract proposition as such but the proposition in _concrete_ _application_, out of which the abstract one was first to be _developed_; then the claim to its universality and immediacy would come to this, that every consciousness, and indeed in every one of its utterances, _lays_ _it_ _at_ _the_ _base_, or that it lies _implicite_ in each of them. Yet the _concrete_ and the _application_ is precisely the _relating_ of the simple _identical_ to _a_ _manifold_ that is _diverse_ from it. Uttered in propositional form, the concrete would in the first instance be a synthetic proposition. Out of the concrete itself, or out of its synthetic proposition, abstraction would doubtless be able to draw the principle of identity by analysis; yet in truth _experience_ would not have been left as it stands, but would have been _altered_; for _experience_ held within it, rather, {{{ 11.264 }}} identity in unity with diversity, and it constitutes an _immediate_ _refutation_ of the assertion that abstract identity as such is anything true, since the very reverse — identity only as joined with diversity — turns up in every experience.

On the other side, though, the experience with the identity principle in its pure form is made only too often, and that experience shows plainly enough what regard is had for the truth it holds. For if the question _what_ _is_ _a_ _plant_? draws the answer: _a_ _plant_ _is_ – _a_ _plant_, then the truth of a proposition of this kind will be granted by the whole company on whom it is tried, and in the same breath they will say just as unanimously that _nothing_ has been said thereby. If somebody opens his mouth and undertakes to state what God is, namely that God is – God, then expectation finds itself cheated, for it was looking out for a _different_ _determination_; and if this proposition is absolute truth, such absolute oratory is thought very little of; nothing will be reckoned more tedious and more of a burden than a conversation that merely chews over the same thing, than talk of this kind which is nevertheless supposed to be truth.

Looking more closely at this effect of tedium in the case of such truth: the opening, _the_ _plant_ _is_ –, makes as if to say _something_, to bring forward some further determination. But since nothing recurs except the same, the reverse has happened instead, _nothing_ has come out. Such _identical_ talk therefore _contradicts_ _itself_. Identity, so far from being in itself the truth and absolute truth, is rather the reverse; so far from being the motionless simple, it is the passing out beyond itself into its own dissolution.

There lies, then, in the _form_ _of_ _the_ _proposition_ in which identity is uttered, _more_ than simple, abstract identity; there lies in it this pure movement of reflection wherein the other steps forth only as shine, as immediate vanishing; _A_ _is_, is a beginning before which something diverse hovers, out towards which the step is to be taken; but the diverse is never reached; _A_ _is_ – _A_; the diversity is nothing but a vanishing; the movement goes back into itself. – One may look upon the form of the proposition as the concealed necessity of adding that surplus of movement to abstract identity. – Thus too there enters some A, or a plant, or whatever other substrate, which as a useless content carries no significance; yet it makes up the diversity that seems to attach itself by accident. If identity itself is put in place of A and of every other substrate – identity is identity – then it is equally granted that any other substrate might just as well be put in place of identity. If, then, appeal is once to be made to what the appearance shows, what it shows is this, that in the utterance of identity {{{ 11.265 }}} diversity likewise turns up immediately; – or, more determinately, in keeping with the above, that this identity is the nothing, that it is negativity, the absolute difference of itself from itself.

The principle of identity has a second expression: _A_ _cannot_ _be_ _A_ _and_ _not-A_ _at_ _the_ _same_ _time_; this has negative form, and it goes by the name of the _principle_ _of_ _contradiction_. As to the way in which identity comes by the _form_ _of_ _negation_ that marks this proposition off from the previous one, no warrant is customarily given. – That form lies, however, in identity's being simple negativity, as the pure movement of reflection, and it is just this that the second expression cited above holds in more developed shape. An A is uttered, and also a not-A, that which is purely other than A, but it makes its showing only in order to vanish. In this proposition, then, identity finds expression – as the negation of negation. A and not-A stand distinguished, and both, as thus distinguished, are referred to A, one and the same. Identity is accordingly set out here as _this_ _distinctness_ _in_ _a_ _single_ _relation_, or as the _simple_ _difference_ _within_ _the_ _terms_ _themselves_.

It is plain from this that the identity principle itself, and the principle of contradiction still more so, is not of a merely _analytic_ but of a _synthetic_ nature. For the latter holds within its expression not the empty, simple likeness with itself alone, and not only the _other_ of that _in_ _general_, but even _absolute_ _unlikeness_, _contradiction_ _in_ _itself_. The principle of identity itself, however, holds, as was shown in its case, the movement of reflection, identity as the vanishing of otherness.

What issues from this consideration, then, is that _first_, the principle of identity or of contradiction, so far as it is meant to give out as true only abstract identity in opposition to difference, is no law of thought but rather the reverse of one; _second_, that what these propositions hold is _more_ than what is _meant_ by them, namely this very reverse, absolute difference itself.
