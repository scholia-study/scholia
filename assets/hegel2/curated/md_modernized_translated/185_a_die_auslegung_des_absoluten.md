## A. The Exposition of the Absolute

The absolute is neither merely _being_ nor yet _essence_. The first of these is immediacy unreflected, the second immediacy reflected; each, moreover, is totality in its own self, though a determinate one. At essence, being steps forth as _concrete_ _existence_; and the reference between being and essence has developed as far as the relation of the _inner_ to the _outer_. _Essence_ is what the _inner_ is, though as the _totality_ carrying essentially the determination of standing _referred_ to _being_ and of immediately being _being_. The _outer_, for its part, is _being_, though with the essential determination that, standing _referred_ to _reflection_, it is immediately and just as much an identity with essence devoid of relation. The absolute itself is the absolute {{{ 11.371 }}} unity of the two; it is what at bottom makes up the _ground_ of the essential relation, a relation which as relation has merely not yet gone back into this identity of its own and whose ground is not yet _posited_.

What emerges from this is that the absolute has for its determination to be the _absolute_ _form_, though at the same time not as that identity whose moments are merely simple determinatenesses; – rather as the identity each of whose moments is in its own self the _totality_ and so, indifferent as it is towards form, the complete _content_ of the whole. Conversely, though, the absolute is the absolute content in such fashion that the content, indifferent manifoldness as content is, has the negative form-reference in it, whereby its manifoldness is only _one_ solid identity.

The identity of the absolute is thus absolute in virtue of this, that its every part is itself the whole, or its every determinateness the totality, that is, that determinateness generally has become a shine wholly transparent, a difference _vanished_ _in_ _its_ _positedness_. _Essence_, _concrete_ _existence_, _world_ _that_ _is_ _in_ _itself_, _whole_, _parts_, _force_, – to representation such reflected determinations look like true being valid in and for itself; the absolute, set against them, is the ground wherein they have gone under. – Now because in the absolute form is only simple identity with itself, no _determining_ of itself goes on in the absolute; for determination is a difference of form, which in the first instance holds as such. But because it contains at the same time all difference and form-determination generally, or because absolute form and reflection is what it itself is, the _diversity_ _of_ _content_ must also come to the fore in it. Yet _absolute_ _identity_ is what the absolute itself is; this is its _determination_, since all the manifoldness of the world that is in itself and of the world that appears, of the inner and the outer totality, is sublated in it. – Within the absolute there is no _becoming_, for it is not being; nor is it the determining that _reflects_ itself, for it is not the essence determining itself merely within itself; neither is it _an_ _expressing_ _of_ _itself_, for it is as the identity of inner and outer. – But so stated, the movement of reflection stands _over_ _against_ this absolute identity of its. Sublated in that identity, reflection is only the identity's _inner_; and just with this it is _external_ to it. – To begin with, therefore, its whole business consists in sublating within the absolute what it itself does. Being the beyond of manifold differences and determinations, and of the movement these make, it _lies_ _at_ _the_ _absolute's_ _back_; hence it does indeed take those differences up, yet at the same time lets them go under; and so it is that _negative_ _exposition_ of the absolute spoken of earlier. – In its genuine presentation this exposition is the whole logical movement so far, that of the sphere _of_ _being_ _and_ _of_ _essence_, whose content was neither snatched up from outside as given and contingent, nor plunged into the absolute's abyss by a reflection {{{ 11.372 }}} standing outside it, but has determined itself in its own self through the inner necessity it has and, as being's own _becoming_ and essence's _reflection_, has gone back into the absolute as into its ground.

This exposition, however, has at the same time a _positive_ side of its own; namely so far as the finite, in foundering, proves this nature of standing referred to the absolute, or of containing the absolute in its own self. This side, however, is less the positive exposition of the absolute itself than the exposition of the _determinations_: namely that the absolute is their abyss, yet their _ground_ as well, or that what lends them, the shine, a subsistence is _the_ _absolute_ itself. – Shine is not the _nothing_; it is reflection, _reference_ to the absolute; or it _is_ shine so far as _the_ _absolute_ _shines_ _in_ _it_. In this way the positive exposition still detains the finite short of its vanishing, taking it for an expression and likeness of the absolute. Yet the finite's transparency, which lets nothing but the absolute be glimpsed through it, ends in vanishing altogether; for there is nothing about the finite that could keep for it any difference from the absolute; it is a medium swallowed up by what shines through it.

Therefore this positive exposition of the absolute is itself no more than a shining; for the truly positive that it and the expounded content contain is the absolute itself. Whatever further determinations turn up, the form wherein the absolute shines is something null, taken up by the exposition _from_ _outside_, something on which _it_ wins _a_ _beginning_ for what it does. A determination of that kind does not have its beginning in the absolute, but only _its_ _end_ there. This expounding is therefore indeed absolute doing by virtue of the _reference_ it has to the absolute, into which it _goes_ _back_, but not by virtue of its _starting_ _point_, a determination lying outside the absolute.

In fact, however, the expounding of the absolute is the absolute's _own_ doing, one that _begins_ _with_ _itself_ just as it _arrives_ _at_ _itself_. The absolute, taken merely as absolute identity, is _determinate_; namely as _identical_; reflection has _posited_ it _thus_, in contrast to opposition and manifoldness; or it is nothing but the _negative_ belonging to reflection and to determining generally. – Not only that expounding of the absolute is on that account something imperfect; so too is this _absolute_ itself, at which alone one _arrives_. Or an absolute of that sort, one that is merely as _absolute_ _identity_, amounts to no more than _the_ _absolute_ _of_ _an_ _external_ _reflection_. Hence it is not the absolutely-absolute but rather the absolute standing in a determinateness; or it is _attribute_.

{{{ 11.373 }}} But the absolute is not attribute merely because it is the _object_ of an external reflection and hence something determined by that reflection. – Or reflection is not merely _external_ to it; rather, _immediately_, and just because it is _external_ to it, it is _internal_ to it. What makes the absolute absolute is just that it is no abstract identity, but the identity of being with essence, the identity of inner with outer. So it is itself the absolute form, the form that makes the absolute shine within itself and determines it to attribute.
