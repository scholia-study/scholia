## {{{ 21.374 }}} B. Indifference as Inverse Ratio of Its Factors

We must now see how this determination of indifference is posited in indifference itself, and indifference thereby posited as _being-for-itself_.

1. The reduction of the measure-relations that at first pass for self-subsistent establishes _One_ _substrate_ for them; this substrate is their continuation into one another, hence the inseparable self-subsistent which is present _wholly_ in its differences. For this difference the determinations it harbours, quality and quantity, are at hand, and all depends only on the manner in which they are posited in the substrate. But what settles this is that the substrate figures at first as result, and _in_ _itself_ as the mediation, though the mediation is not yet posited as such _in_ _it_; by which the substrate is at first substrate, and, with respect to determinateness, is _indifference_.

In it, accordingly, the difference is at the outset essentially the merely quantitative, external one; and there are two quanta, differing from each other, of one and the same substrate, which on this showing would be _the_ _sum_ of them and hence itself determined as quantum. But indifference is this fixed measure, the absolute limit that is in itself, only in _reference_ to those differences, and in such fashion that it would not itself be a quantum, nor step forward in any way — as sum or even as exponent — over against others, be they sums or indifferences. Only the abstract determinateness falls within indifference; the two quanta, in being posited as its moments, are alterable, indifferent, now larger, now smaller with respect to each other. Restricted, however, by the fixed limit of their sum, their bearing on each other is at the same time not external but negative; and therein lies the qualitative determination under which they now stand to one another. They are on that account to each other in an _inverse_ _ratio_. This ratio parts company with the earlier, formal inverse ratio in that the whole is here a substrate that is real, while either side is posited under the requirement that _in_ _itself_ it be that whole itself.

In line with the qualitative determinateness just given, the difference is further at hand as one of _two_ _qualities_, of which the one is sublated through the other, yet, held within One unity and making it up, cannot be severed from it. The substrate, being indifference, is itself likewise in itself the unity of both {{{ 21.375 }}} qualities; each side of the ratio therefore harbours the two of them alike, and differs only in that the one quality carries a more and the other a less, and conversely; by its quantum the one quality is merely the _preponderant_ one in the one side, the second quality in the second.

Each side is thus, in its own self, an inverse ratio; as a formal ratio it returns within the distinct sides. These sides accordingly continue into one another by their qualitative determinations as well: each quality relates itself to itself in the other, and occurs in the two sides only with a differing quantum. Their quantitative difference is that indifference by which they continue into one another, and, as the qualities' self-sameness, this continuation is present within either of the two unities. – Yet the sides, each of them the whole of the determinations and so containing indifference itself, are for that reason posited at the same time as self-subsistent over against each other.

2. Being now is, as this indifference, the being-determined of measure no longer in its immediacy, but that being-determined in the developed manner just displayed; – indifference as being _in_ _itself_ the whole of being's determinations, dissolved as these are into this unity; – equally _existence_, as the totality of realization once posited, wherein the moments are themselves indifference's totality as it is in itself, sustained by indifference as their unity. Since, however, the unity is held fast only as _indifference_ and hence only as being _in_ _itself_, and since [the moments] are not yet determined as _being-for-itself_, that is, as not yet sublating themselves into unity _in their own selves_ and _through one another_, what is at hand is quite generally the _indifference_ of these moments _towards_ _themselves_ as a developed determinateness.

This self-subsistent, inseparable as it is, must now be examined more closely. It dwells immanently in all its determinations and abides in them in a unity with itself untroubled by them, but α) remaining _in_ _itself_ the totality, it has the determinatenesses sublated within it merely _coming forth_ in it groundlessly. The _in-itself_ of indifference and this its _existence_ stand unconnected; the determinatenesses make their showing in it immediately; indifference is whole in each of them; their difference is thus posited at first as sublated, hence as _quantitative_; but for just that reason not as indifference's repelling of itself from itself, indifference not as self-determining, only as _externally_ determined and coming to be determined.

β) The two moments stand in an inverse quantitative ratio; – a going back and forth in magnitude which is not determined by indifference, this being precisely indifference towards such going back and forth, but is on that account determined only externally. The pointer is towards an other lying outside indifference, an other in which the determining has its seat. The _absolute_ as indifference carries, on this side, the second {{{ 21.376 }}} defect of the _quantitative_ form: that it is not the absolute that settles the determinateness of the difference — just as its first defect on that score is that in it the differences merely _come forth_ at all, that is, that the absolute's positing is an immediate something and not its own mediation with itself.

γ) What makes up the manner of the moments' _subsistence_ — the moments being now _sides_ of the ratio — is their quantitative determinateness; their _existence_, through such indifference, is withheld from the transition belonging to the qualitative. But over and above this existence of theirs they have a subsistence that differs from it, a subsistence that is theirs _in_ _itself_, in that _in_ _themselves_ they are indifference itself, each for its part the unity of those two _qualities_ into which the qualitative moment splits. The difference of the two sides restricts itself to this: that in the one side the one quality is posited carrying a more, in the other a less, the second quality standing conversely. Each side is thus in it the totality of indifference. – Taken singly and by itself, each of the two qualities likewise remains that same sum which indifference is; it continues from the one side over into the other, and the quantitative limit posited within it in the process does not restrict it. At this point the determinations enter into an immediate opposition which develops into contradiction, and this is what must now be seen.

3. Namely, _within_ each side each quality enters into reference to the other, and does so in such wise that this reference too, as has been settled, is meant to be nothing but a quantitative difference. Should both qualities be self-subsistent – taken, say, as sensible matters independent of each other – the whole determinateness of indifference falls apart; its unity and totality would be hollow names. They are, on the contrary, determined at the same time so as to be comprised within One unity, to be inseparable, each having sense and reality solely in this one qualitative reference to the other. But now, precisely _because_ _their_ _quantitativity_ _is_ _through_ _and_ _through_ _of_ _this_ _qualitative_ _nature_, _neither_ _extends_ _further_ _than_ _the_ _other_. Were they to be distinct as quanta, the one would run out past the other and would possess, in that more of it, an indifferent existence denied to the other. But in their qualitative reference each is only to the extent that its other is. – What follows is that they stand _in_ _equilibrium_: by whatever amount the one increased or diminished, the other would grow or shrink to just that extent, and in the very same ratio.

{{{ 21.377 }}} On the ground of their _qualitative_ reference, then, no _quantitative_ difference can arise, nor any _more_ belonging to the one quality. Whatever more should carry the _one_ of the two moments in reference out past the _other_ would be a determination with no footing, or such a more _would_ _be_ _but_ _the_ _other_ _itself_ _over_ _again_; in this equality of the two, however, neither is at hand, since their existence was to rest on nothing but an inequality of their quantum. – Each of these would-be factors vanishes as surely when it is supposed to lie _out beyond_ the other as when it is supposed to be _equal_ to it. That vanishing shows itself as follows: starting from the quantitative representation, the equilibrium is disturbed and the one factor taken as greater than the other; with that, the other's quality is posited as sublated and as having no footing; the first becomes the preponderant one, so that the second diminishes at an accelerating rate and is overpowered by the first, which thereby makes itself the one and only self-subsistent; but then there are no longer two specifics and factors, only the single whole.

This unity, posited thus as the totality of determining, in the very way it is itself therein determined as indifference, is contradiction on every side; it is therefore _to_ _be_ _posited_, as this self-sublating contradiction, as determined towards a self-subsistence that is for itself — a self-subsistence whose result and truth is the absolute unity that is no longer merely indifferent but immanently negative within its own self, and this unity is _essence_.
