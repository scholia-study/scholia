## {{{ 21.153 }}} b. The One and the Void

Taken as the abstract relating of negation to its own self, the one is the void. From the simple immediacy, however, from the also affirmative being of the one, the void as the nothing is downright diverse, and, since the two stand in one relating, namely that of the one itself, their diversity is _posited_; but, being diverse from what is, the nothing as void lies _outside_ the one that is.

By determining itself in this manner as the one _and_ the void, being-for-itself has once more attained an _existence_. – What the one and the void have for their common, simple soil is the negative relating to self. Out of this unity the moments of being-for-itself step forth and become external to one another, for since the determination of _being_ comes in through the _simple_ unity of the moments, that unity lowers itself to being merely _one_ side and therewith to existence, and therein its other determination, negation as such, places itself over against it likewise as existence of the nothing, as the void.
