## {{{ 12.33 }}} A) The Universal Concept

Absolutely infinite, unconditioned, and free — such is the pure concept. Here, at the point where the treatise having the concept for its _content_ begins, a backward look at the concept's genesis is once more called for. _Essence_ has _become_ out of _being_, and the concept out of essence, thus out of being as well. This _becoming_, however, carries the sense of a _counter-thrust_ against itself, so that what has _become_ is rather the _unconditioned_ and the _original_. In passing over into essence, _being_ has turned into a _shine_ or a _positedness_, and _becoming_, the transition into an _other_, has turned into a _positing_; inversely, the _positing_, or essence's reflection, has sublated itself and reinstated itself as something _not-posited_, as an _original_ being. The concept is the mutual interpenetration of these moments, so that whatever is qualitative and originally in being is only as positing and only as return-into-itself, while this pure reflection-into-itself is utterly the _becoming-other_, the _determinateness_, which for that very reason is equally an infinite _determinateness_ that refers to itself.

Hence the concept is at the start _absolute_ _identity_ _with_ _itself_ in such wise that it is this identity solely as negation of the negation, solely as negativity's infinite unity with its own self. This _pure_ _reference_ of the concept to itself, which is such a reference by positing itself through negativity, is the universality of the concept.

_Universality_, being the supremely _simple_ determination, would seem to admit of no explanation; for an explanation must enter upon determinations and distinctions and must predicate something of its subject matter, whereas the simple is thereby altered sooner than explained. Yet it is just the nature of the universal to be a simple of this kind, one that by virtue of absolute negativity harbours _within_ _itself_ the utmost of difference and determinateness. _Being_ is simple in the manner of the _immediate_; on that account it is a merely _supposed_ something, and what it is cannot be said; hence it stands immediately at one with its other, with _non-being_. Just this is its concept — to be a simple that vanishes immediately into its opposite; it is _becoming_. The _universal_, by contrast, is the _simple_ that is at the same time the _richest_ _within_ _its_ _own_ _self_; for it is the concept.

It is therefore, _firstly_, the simple reference to itself; it is only _within_ _itself_. But this identity is, _secondly_, absolute _mediation_ within itself, and not a _mediated_ something. Of the universal that is something mediated — the _abstract_ universal, namely, the one set over against the particular and the singular — there will be {{{ 12.34 }}} occasion to speak only once the determinate concept is reached. – Yet even the _abstract_ already involves this, that obtaining it requires that other determinations of the concrete be _left_ _out_. Such determinations, being determinations at all, are _negations_; and the _leaving_ _out_ of them is likewise a _negating_. So in the case of the abstract too, negation of negation makes its appearance. This doubled negation, however, gets represented as though it were _external_ to the abstract, as though the omitted further properties of the concrete were diverse from the retained one that makes up the abstract's content, and as though this operation of dropping the rest and keeping the one went on outside the abstract itself. The universal has not yet determined itself towards such _externality_ over against that movement; within itself it still is that absolute mediation which is just the negation of negation, absolute negativity.

Given this original unity, the first negative — the _determination_ — is to begin with no restriction upon the universal; on the contrary, the universal _preserves_ _itself_ _within_ _it_ and stays positively identical with itself. Considered as concepts, the categories of being were essentially just such identities of determinations with themselves within their restriction or their otherness; that identity, however, was the concept only _in_ _itself_; it had not yet been manifested. That is why the qualitative determination as such went under in its other, having for its truth a determination _diverse_ from itself. The universal, on the contrary, though it posit itself in a determination, _stays_ therein what it is. It is the _soul_ of that concrete in which it dwells, unimpeded and equal to itself amid the concrete's manifoldness and diversity. It is not swept along into _becoming_, but _continues_ _itself_ untroubled through it, possessing the strength of an unalterable, immortal self-preservation.

Nor, again, does it merely _shine_ into its other, as a determination of reflection does. Being something _relative_, such a determination refers not merely to itself; it is a _comportment_. It _announces_ _itself_ in its other, yet as yet only _shines_ in it, and the shining of each in the other, or their reciprocal determining, takes on — given their self-subsistence — the form of an outward doing. – The _universal_, in contrast, is posited as the _essence_ of its determination, as that determination's _own_ _positive_ _nature_. For within the concept, the determination making up the universal's negative is utterly only as a _positedness_, or essentially only at the same time as the negative of the negative, and it only is as this identity that the negative has with itself — the identity which the universal is. So far, then, the universal is the _substance_ of its determinations as well; yet in such fashion that what was for substance as such something _contingent_ {{{ 12.35 }}} is the concept's own _mediation_ with itself, its own _immanent_ _reflection_. This mediation, which in the first place elevates the contingent into _necessity_, is however the reference _manifested_; the concept is no abyss of formless substance, nor necessity taken as the _inner_ identity of things or states mutually diverse and mutually limiting, but rather, as absolute negativity, it is what gives form and creates, and since determination is there not as restriction but utterly just as much as sublated, as positedness, the shine is appearance in the sense of the appearance _of_ _the_ _identical_.

Hence the universal is _free_ power; it is itself and reaches out over its other, though not as something _violent_; rather, within that other it remains at rest and _with_ _its_ _own_ _self_. Having been called free power, it might equally be called _free_ _love_ and _unrestricted_ _blessedness_, for it comports itself towards the _differentiated_ only as towards _its_ _own_ _self_; in the differentiated it has come back to itself.

_Determinateness_ has just been mentioned, even though the concept, being as yet only the universal and only _identical_ with itself, has not yet advanced so far. But the universal cannot be spoken of apart from determinateness, which more closely is particularity and singularity; for the universal holds it in and for itself within its absolute negativity; determinateness is therefore not fetched in from outside when it is spoken of in dealing with the universal. Taken as negativity in general, or according to the _first_, _immediate_ negation, the universal carries determinateness in general upon it as _particularity_; taken as a _second_ negativity, as negation of the negation, it is _absolute_ _determinateness_, that is, _singularity_ and _concretion_. – The universal is accordingly the totality of the concept; it is something concrete, not something empty, but has rather through its concept a _content_ — a content in which it not merely preserves itself but which is proper and immanent to it. Abstraction from the content is of course possible; what one thereby obtains, however, is not the concept's universal but the _abstract_, an isolated and defective moment of the concept, possessing no truth.

More closely, the universal shows itself in this way to be such a totality. So far as it holds determinateness within itself, that determinateness amounts not merely to the _first_ negation but to the reflection of this negation into itself as well. Taken on its own together with that first negation, the universal is something _particular_, as will be considered straight away; but in this determinateness it remains essentially universal, and it is this side that has still to be grasped here. – For this determinateness, being in the concept, is the total reflection, _the_ _double_ _shine_: on the one hand the shine _outwards_, reflection into an other; on the other the shine _inwards_, reflection into itself. The outward shining establishes a difference over against something _other_; accordingly the universal has a {{{ 12.36 }}} _particularity_ that finds its resolution in a higher universal. Now even so far as it is merely a relatively universal, it does not forfeit its universal character; it holds itself fast in its determinateness, and this not merely by remaining, in its conjunction with determinateness, indifferent towards it — in that case it would only be _compounded_ with it — but by being what has just been named the _shining_ _inwards_. As determinate _concept_, determinateness has been _bent_ _back_ _into_ _itself_ out of externality; it is the concept's proper, immanent _character_, essential precisely because, received into universality and permeated by it, of like extent and identical with it, it permeates universality in turn; such is the character belonging to the _genus_, determinateness undivided from the universal. To that extent it is no _restriction_ running outwards but is _positive_, standing as it does, by virtue of universality, in free reference to itself. So even the determinate concept remains within itself an infinitely free concept.

As regards the other side, however — the side on which the genus is bounded by its determinate character — the remark has been made that, being a lower genus, it finds its resolution in a universal that is higher. Such a universal may in turn be taken as a genus, only a more abstract one, but it belongs once again solely to that side of the determinate concept which runs outwards. Truly the higher universal is the one wherein this outward-running side has been withdrawn inwards, the second negation, wherein determinateness is utterly only _as_ something posited, _as_ shine. Life, the I, spirit, the absolute concept are not universals merely in the manner of higher genera; they are _concretes_ whose determinatenesses are likewise not merely species or lower genera, but which in their reality are utterly only within themselves and filled with themselves. So far as life, the I, finite spirit are indeed also only determinate concepts, their absolute resolution lies in that universal which must be grasped as the truly absolute concept, as the idea of infinite spirit, whose _positedness_ is the infinite, transparent reality wherein it beholds its _creation_ and, in that creation, its own self.

The true, infinite universal, which immediately is within itself particularity quite as much as singularity, is now to be examined more closely, and first of all as _particularity_. It _determines_ itself freely; its becoming finite is no transition of the kind that occurs only within being's sphere; _it_ _is_ _creative_ _power_, being the absolute negativity that refers to itself. As such it is the differentiating within itself, and this differentiating is _determining_, for the reason that differentiating is one with universality. It is thus a positing of the differences themselves as universal and as self-referring. By this means they turn into _fixed_, isolated differences. The finite's isolated _subsistence_, which earlier determined itself as its {{{ 12.37 }}} being-for-itself, and as thinghood too, as substance, has universality for its truth, and it is with this form that the infinite concept invests its differences – a form which is itself precisely one of those differences. Herein consists the concept's _creating_, something to be comprehended only within this innermost core of the concept itself.
