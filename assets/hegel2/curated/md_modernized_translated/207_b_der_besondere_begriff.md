## B) The Particular Concept

_Determinateness_ as such belongs to being and to the qualitative; as the concept's determinateness it is _particularity_. Particularity is no _limit_, as though it thereby stood towards an _other_ that were a _beyond_ of itself; rather, as has just been shown, it is the universal's own immanent moment; hence in particularity the universal is not in the company of an other but purely and simply at home with itself.

The particular harbours the universality that makes up its substance; the genus stays _unaltered_ in its species; the species are not diverse from the universal but only _from_ _one another_. With the _other_ particulars to which it stands related the particular shares one and the same universality. Their diversity is at once universal _as_ _such_, on account of their identity with the universal; it is _totality_. – Hence the particular does not merely _contain_ the universal but exhibits it as well _through_ _its_ _determinateness_; to that extent the universal makes up a _sphere_ which the particular has to exhaust. This totality, so far as the particular's determinateness is taken for mere _diversity_, appears as _completeness_. In that respect the species are complete when there simply _are_ no further ones. For them no inner yardstick or _principle_ is at hand, since _diversity_ is precisely the difference devoid of unity, a difference at which universality, absolute unity though it be for itself, is a merely external reflex and an unlimited, contingent completeness. Diversity, however, passes over into _opposition_, into an _immanent_ _reference_ among the diverse. Particularity, by contrast, is such an immanent reference as universality in and for itself, and not through any transition; it is totality in its own self and _simple_ determinateness, essentially _principle_. No _other_ determinateness attaches to it than the one the universal itself posits, and this issues from the universal in the following manner.

The particular is the universal itself, yet it is the universal's difference, or {{{ 12.38 }}} reference to an _other_, its _shining_ _outwards_; but apart from the universal itself no other is present from which the particular might be distinguished. – The universal determines _itself_, and thus is itself the particular; the determinateness is _its_ difference; from nothing but itself is it distinguished. Its species are accordingly just these two: a) the universal itself, and b) the particular. As the concept, the universal is both itself and its opposite, which in turn is itself as its posited determinateness; it reaches out across that opposite and within it stays at home with itself. So it is the totality and the principle of its diversity, a diversity determined wholly and only by itself.

There is accordingly no genuine division other than this: that the concept places itself over on one side as the _immediate_, indeterminate universality; and just this indeterminateness makes up its determinateness, or its being a _particular_. _Both_ are the particular, and are therefore _coordinate_. Both, as particular, are likewise the _determinate_ _over against_ the universal; to that extent they count as _subordinate_ to it. Yet just this universal, _against_ which the particular is determined, is thereby itself rather _only_ _one_ of the two facing each other. If we speak of _two_ _terms facing each other_, we must again add that the two of them make up the particular, and not merely _together_, as if for external reflection alone they were _alike_ in this, in being particulars; rather their determinateness _over against each other_ is essentially at once only _one_ determinateness, that negativity which in the universal is _simple_.

As difference displays itself here, it stands in its concept and thereby in its truth. Every earlier difference has this unity in the concept. As immediate difference in being, it is the _limit_ of an _other_; as it is in reflection, it is relative, posited as essentially referring to its other; here, then, the concept's unity begins to be _posited_, though at first that unity is merely the _shine_ upon an other. – The passing over and dissolution of these determinations carries only this true sense, that they attain their concept, their truth; being, existence, something, or whole and parts, and so forth. Substance and accidents, cause and effect are for themselves determinations of thought; they come to be grasped as determinate _concepts_ so far as each is cognized in unity with its other or its opposite. – Whole and parts, cause and effect, for instance, and so forth, are not yet diverse terms determined against each other as _particulars_, for although _in_ _themselves_ they do make up one concept, their _unity_ has not yet attained the form of _universality_; and thus the _difference_ obtaining in these relations still lacks the form of being _one_ determinateness. Cause and effect, for instance, do not constitute two diverse concepts; there is only _one_ _determinate_ concept here, and causality, like every concept, is something _simple_.

{{{ 12.39 }}} With regard to completeness it has emerged that the determinate element of particularity lies _completely_ within the difference between the _universal_ and the _particular_, and that these two by themselves make up the particular species. In _nature_, admittedly, more than two species turn up within a genus, just as these many species cannot stand to each other in the relation exhibited above. Such is nature's impotence: that it cannot hold fast to the rigour of the concept or set it forth, and loses itself in this conceptless, blind manifoldness. We may well _marvel_ at nature in the manifoldness of its genera and species and in the endless diversity of its formations, for marvelling is _without_ _concept_, and its subject matter is what lacks reason. Because nature is the concept's being-outside-itself, it is left free to indulge in such diversity, just as spirit, though possessing the concept in the shape of the concept, likewise lets itself in for representation and roams about in an endless manifoldness of it. The many natural genera or species deserve to be esteemed no higher than the arbitrary fancies that occur to spirit in its representations. Both do everywhere display traces and intimations of the concept, but they do not present it in faithful likeness, since they are the side of its free being-outside-itself; the concept is the absolute power just in this, that it can freely release its difference into the shape of self-subsistent diversity, external necessity, contingency, arbitrariness, opinion, all of which, however, must be taken for nothing beyond the abstract side of _nullity_.

The _determinateness_ of the particular is _simple_ _as_ _principle_, as we have seen, but it is so equally as a moment of the totality, as determinateness over against the _other_ determinateness. The concept, so far as it determines or differentiates itself, turns negatively upon its own unity and takes on the form of one among its ideal _moments_ _of_ _being_; as a determinate concept it possesses an _existence_ as such. This being, however, no longer carries the sense of bare _immediacy_; it carries the sense of universality, of an immediacy that through absolute mediation is equal to itself and that just as much holds within it the other moment, essence or reflection. The universality in which the determinate is here clothed is the _abstract_ one. Universality dwells in the particular as the particular's essence; but so far as the determinateness of difference is _posited_ and thereby has being, universality is _form_ in the particular, while the determinateness as such is the _content_. Universality turns into form where difference is present as the essential, whereas in the purely universal, conversely, difference is only as absolute negativity, _not_ _as_ a difference that is _posited_ as such.

Determinateness is now, to be sure, the _abstract_ over against the _other_ determinateness; but that other is nothing but universality itself, so that universality too is the {{{ 12.40 }}} _abstract_; and the concept's determinateness, or particularity, is once more nothing beyond determinate universality. In it the concept is _outside_ _itself_; and so far as what stands there outside itself _is_ _the concept_, the abstract universal harbours every moment of the concept: it is α) universality β) determinateness γ) the _simple_ unity of both; this unity, though, is an _immediate_ one, and particularity is on that account not there _as_ the totality. _In_ _itself_ particularity is this _totality_ too, and _mediation_; essentially it is an _excluding_ reference to an _other_, or the _sublation_ of the _negation_, namely of the _other_ determinateness, – of that _other_ which, however, merely hovers before the mind as a supposing, since it vanishes at once and shows itself to be the very same as what was supposed to be its _other_. What renders this universality abstract is just this, that the mediation is merely _condition_ and is not _posited_ _in_ _the universality_ itself. Since it is not _posited_, the unity of the abstract carries the form of immediacy, and the content carries the form of indifference towards its universality, for the content is not there as that totality which is the universality of absolute negativity. The abstract universal is thus the _concept_ indeed, but as something _conceptless_, as a concept not posited as such.

When talk turns to the _determinate_ _concept_, what is generally meant is purely and simply such an _abstract universal_. Even by the _concept_ in general one mostly understands no more than this _conceptless_ concept, and _understanding_ names the faculty of such concepts. _Demonstration_ falls to this understanding, so far as it is said to _proceed_ by way of _concepts_, that is, merely by way of _determinations_. Such proceeding by way of concepts consequently never gets beyond finitude and necessity; its supreme point is the negative infinite, that abstraction of a highest essence which is itself nothing but the determinateness of _indeterminateness_. Absolute substance, too, is admittedly not this empty abstraction but rather, as regards content, the totality; abstract it nevertheless is, because it lacks the absolute form, and the concept does not make up its innermost truth; identity of universality and particularity though it be, or of thinking and of being-outside-one-another, that identity is not the concept's _determinateness_; _outside_ substance there stands rather an understanding, and indeed, just because it stands outside substance, a contingent understanding, in and for which substance is in diverse attributes and modes.

Abstraction, by the way, is not _empty_, as it is commonly called; it is the _determinate_ concept; some determinateness or other constitutes its content; even the highest essence, that pure abstraction, has, as was recalled, the determinateness of indeterminateness; yet indeterminateness is itself a determinateness, since it is meant to stand _over against_ the {{{ 12.41 }}} _determinate_. But once one states what abstraction is, the very thing it was meant to be sublates itself; it is stated as one with determinateness, and in this manner the concept, and abstraction's truth, is brought forth out of abstraction. – To that extent, however, every determinate concept is indeed _empty_: it holds no totality within it, but only a one-sided determinateness. Even where it has concrete content besides, for instance human being, state, animal, and so forth, it stays an empty concept so far as its determinateness is not the _principle_ of its differences; the principle holds the beginning and the essence of the concept's development and realization, whereas any other determinateness of the concept is barren. If the concept is therefore decried as empty in general, what is being misjudged is precisely that absolute determinateness of it which constitutes the conceptual difference, and which is the sole true content within the concept's element.

Here belongs the circumstance for whose sake understanding has of late been thought little of and set so far behind reason; it is the _fixity_ which understanding imparts to determinatenesses and thereby to what is finite. This fixed quality rests on the form of abstract universality we have considered; through that form they become _unalterable_. For qualitative determinateness, like the determination of reflection, essentially is as something _limited_, and by way of its restriction it has a reference to its _other_, and hence the _necessity_ of passing over and of ceasing-to-be. The universality they possess in the understanding, however, lends them the form of reflection into themselves, whereby they are taken out of the reference to an other and have grown _imperishable_. Now if in the pure concept such eternity belongs to its nature, its abstract determinations would be eternal essentialities only as regards _their_ _form_; but their content does not match this form; hence they are not truth and imperishability. Their content does not match the form, since it is not determinateness itself in the character of the universal, that is, not the totality of conceptual difference, and thus is not itself the entire form; the form belonging to the limited understanding is, however, for just this reason the imperfect one, namely _abstract_ universality. – It should further be esteemed, though, as the infinite power of understanding that it separates the concrete into abstract determinatenesses and grasps the depth of difference, which alone is at once the power effecting their transition. The concrete of _intuition_ is _totality_, but the _sensible_ one, – a real material subsisting indifferently _outside one another_ in space and time; this want of unity in the manifold, in which the manifold is intuition's content, ought surely not to be reckoned to intuition's credit as a merit and an advantage over what pertains to the understanding. The alterability the manifold displays in intuition already points towards the universal; what of this comes before intuition is only an _other_ equally alterable, hence only the selfsame thing; it is not the universal that would step into its place {{{ 12.42 }}} and appear. Least of all, however, ought the _intuitable_ element that its material brings along be reckoned to the credit of a science, geometry and arithmetic for instance, nor its propositions be represented as thereby grounded. The material of such sciences is on the contrary of a lower nature for just that reason; intuiting figures or numbers is no help towards a science of them; only _thinking_ about them can bring such a science forth. – So far, however, as intuition is understood to mean not merely the sensible but the _objective_ _totality_, it is an _intellectual_ intuition, that is, its subject matter is not existence in its outer concrete existence but what in existence is imperishable reality and truth, – reality only so far as it is essentially in the concept and _determined_ through it, the _idea_, whose closer nature has yet to emerge. What intuition as such is credited with possessing in advance of the concept is outer reality, the conceptless, which acquires a value only through the concept.

Since understanding accordingly exhibits the infinite power that determines the universal, or conversely lends fixed subsistence, through the form of universality, to a determinateness that in and for itself has no stability, it is not now understanding's fault if no further step is taken. It is a subjective _impotence_ _of_ _reason_ that lets these determinatenesses count as they stand and cannot lead them back to unity by the dialectical force opposed to that abstract universality, that is, by their own peculiar nature, namely by the concept of those determinatenesses. By the form of abstract universality understanding does indeed lend them, so to speak, a _hardness_ of being such as they possess neither in the qualitative sphere nor in the sphere of reflection; but through this very simplification it _inspirits_ them at the same time, and hones them to such a point that only at this apex do they acquire the ability to dissolve and to pass over into what is opposed to them. The highest ripeness and rank anything whatever can reach is that in which its going-under sets in. That firmness of the determinatenesses into which understanding seems to run itself fast, the form of the imperishable, is the form of universality referring itself to itself. But that form belongs to the concept as its very own; and therefore the _dissolution_ of the finite lies expressed within it, and in infinite proximity. Such universality immediately _convicts_ the determinateness of the finite and _brings_ _out_ its inadequacy to universality. – Or rather, its adequacy is already present; what is abstractly determinate stands posited as one with universality; posited therefore precisely as not for itself, as it would be were it merely determinate, but solely as the unity of universality and itself, that is, as concept.

It is therefore to be rejected in every respect that understanding and reason should be sundered in the way usually done. If the concept is taken {{{ 12.43 }}} to be devoid of reason, what must rather be so taken is reason's own inability to know itself in the concept. The determinate, abstract concept is the _condition_, or rather _reason's_ _essential_ _moment_; it is form that has been inspirited, wherein the finite, by way of the universality in which it refers to itself, kindles itself from within, is posited as dialectical, and so constitutes the very _beginning_ of reason's appearance.

Now that the determinate concept has in the foregoing been set forth in its truth, all that remains is to indicate what it has thereby already come to be posited as. – Difference, an essential moment of the concept which in the purely universal is nevertheless not yet posited as such, comes into its right within the determinate concept. Determinateness in the form of universality is bound together with universality into something simple; this determinate universal is determinateness referring itself to itself, the determinate determinateness, or absolute negativity posited for itself. But determinateness referring itself to itself is _singularity_. Just as immediately as universality is already in and for itself particularity, just as immediately is particularity in and for itself _singularity_ as well, which is to be considered in the first place as the concept's third moment, so far as it is held fast _over against_ the first two, but also as the concept's absolute return into itself, and simultaneously as its posited loss of its own self.
