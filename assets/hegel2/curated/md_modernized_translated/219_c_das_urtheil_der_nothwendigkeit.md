## C. The Judgment of Necessity

The determination into which universality has developed is, as has come out, universality _in_ _and_ _for_ _itself_, or _objective_ _universality_, answering to which in the sphere of essence stands _substantiality_. What sets it apart from substantiality is that it belongs to the _concept_, and thereby is not the _inner_ necessity of its determinations only but the _posited_ necessity as well, or that _difference_ is immanent to it, whereas substance harbours its own difference only in its accidents and not as a principle within its own self.

In the judgment this objective universality is now _posited_; hence _first_ along with this its essential determinateness, as immanent to it, second as distinct from it as _particularity_, for which that universality makes up the substantial groundwork. Determined in this manner, it is _genus_ and _species_.
