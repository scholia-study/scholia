## B. The Relation of Causality

Substance is power, and power _reflected_ _into_ _itself_ — power that does not merely pass over but posits the _determinations_ and _distinguishes_ _them_ _from_ _itself_. Since in its determining it refers itself to its own self, _it_ _itself_ is what it posits as something negative or turns into _positedness_. Such positedness is thus, quite generally, substantiality sublated, what is merely posited, _the_ _effect_; whereas substance that is for itself is the _cause_.

At the outset this relation of causality is only that _relation_ of _cause_ _and_ _effect_; and taken so it is the _formal_ _relation of causality_.
