## b. The Determinate Relation of Causality

1. The cause's _identity_ with itself in its effect is the sublation of its power and of its negativity, hence the unity indifferent to the differences of form: the _content_. – Only _in_ _itself_, therefore, does content stand referred to form, which here is causality. Content and form are thereby posited as _diverse_, and form, as against content, is itself only an immediately actual causality, a _contingent_ one.

{{{ 11.399 }}} Further, content thus taken as determinate is a content diverse within its own self; the cause is determined in respect of its content, and thereby so is the effect. – Since being-reflected is here immediate actuality as well, content is to this extent substance – _actual_, yet _finite_ _substance_.

What we now have before us is _the_ _relation_ _of_ _causality_ _in_ _its_ _reality_ _and_ _finitude_. Taken as formal, it is absolute power's infinite relation, whose content is pure manifestation, or necessity. Taken as finite causality, by contrast, it has a _given_ content, and spends itself as a difference external to that identical element which, throughout its determinations, remains a single substance.

Because of this _identity_ _of_ _the_ _content_, such causality amounts to an _analytic_ proposition. The _same_ _fact_ is what shows up now as cause and now as effect, there as a subsistence peculiar to itself, here as positedness or as a determination in something else. Since these determinations of form are _external_ reflection, to determine an appearance as effect and to climb from it up to its cause, so as to grasp and explain the appearance, is a procedure of the _subjective_ understanding which _as_ _regards_ _the_ _fact_ is tautological; one and the same content simply gets repeated; the cause holds nothing that the effect does not. – Rain, for instance, is cause of the wetness that is its effect; – _rain_ _makes_ _wet_ is an analytic proposition; the water which the rain is, is also the wetness; as rain, this water merely has the form of a fact for itself, while as wateriness or wetness it is something adjectival, a posited item that is supposed no longer to carry its subsistence within itself; and either determination is as external to the water as the other. – So the cause of _this_ _colour_ is a colouring agent, a _pigment_ that remains one and the same actuality: once under the form, external to it, of something active, that is, hooked up externally with an active thing distinct from itself, and once under the equally external determination of being an effect. – The cause of a _deed_ is the inner disposition in an acting subject, a disposition which, as the outer existence it gains through the action, is the same in content and in worth. Where the _motion_ of a body is taken as effect, its cause is said to be an _impelling_ force; yet before the impact and after it one and the same quantum of motion is at hand, the same concrete existence that the impelling body held and passed on to the body struck; and it forfeits exactly as much as it passes on.

The cause – the painter, for example, or the impelling body – certainly carries _yet_ _another_ content: the painter, as the colours and the form binding them into the {{{ 11.400 }}} painting; the body, as a motion of determinate strength and direction. But this further content is a contingent adjunct that is no business of the cause; whatever qualities the painter has, abstracted from his being the painter of this painting, do not enter the painting at all; only so much of his properties as displays itself in the _effect_ is there in him _as_ _cause_, and by his remaining properties he is no cause. Whether the impelling body is of stone or of wood, green or yellow and so forth, none of that enters its impact; in those respects it is not cause.

Regarding _this_ _tautology_ in the causal relation, it should be observed that the relation seems not to contain it whenever what is named is not the proximate but the _remote_ _cause_ of an effect. The alteration of form which the underlying fact undergoes on its passage through several intermediate links hides the identity it keeps throughout. And in this multiplication of causes that have interposed themselves between it and the last effect, the fact simultaneously ties itself in with other things and circumstances, so that what holds the complete effect is not that first item pronounced to be the cause, but only these several causes _taken_ _together_. – Suppose, for example, a man came into circumstances that developed his talent by the loss of his father, whom a bullet struck in a battle: that shot (or, going further back, the war, or a cause of the war, and so on into infinity) might then be named as the cause of the man's skill. Yet it is evident that this shot, for instance, is not on its own that cause, but only its being tied in with other determinations at work. Or rather it is no cause whatever, only one single _moment_ belonging to the _circumstances_ _of_ _the_ _possibility_.

For above all, the _inadmissible_ _application_ of the causal relation to _relations_ _of_ _physically-organic_ and _of_ _spiritual_ _life_ still needs remarking. Here, to be sure, what gets called cause shows itself to be of a content other than the effect, _but_ _precisely_ _because_ whatever works upon a living thing is by that thing determined, altered and transformed on its own account, _because_ _the_ _living_ _thing_ _will_ _not_ _let_ _the_ _cause_ _reach_ _its_ _effect_, which is to say that it sublates the cause as cause. So it is inadmissible talk when nourishment is called the _cause_ of blood, or when these foods, or cold and damp, are called _causes_ of a fever and so forth; as inadmissible as naming the Ionian climate _the_ _cause_ of the Homeric works, or Caesar's ambition the _cause_ of the collapse of Rome's republican constitution. In _history_ at large, spiritual masses and individuals are in play and in mutual determination with one another; but it lies in the nature of spirit, in a far higher sense than in the character of what is living at large, rather {{{ 11.401 }}} _not_ _to_ _absorb_ _into_ _itself_ _another_ _original_ _thing_, or to refuse a cause any continuation into itself, breaking it off instead and transforming it. – Relations which, however, belong to the _idea_ and are first to be considered there. – One further remark may be made here: to the extent that the relation of cause and effect is allowed, albeit in a non-literal sense, an effect cannot be _greater_ than its cause, since the effect is no more than the cause made manifest. In history it has become a stock piece of wit to have _great_ _effects_ spring _from_ _small_ _causes_, and to put forward some _anecdote_ as the first cause of a far-reaching and profound occurrence. A cause of that sort is to be reckoned nothing more than an _occasion_, an _external_ _prompting_ which the _inner_ _spirit_ of the occurrence could well have done without, or in whose place it might have used any of a countless host of others as a starting point in appearance, in order to vent itself and render itself manifest. Conversely, indeed, something so petty and contingent on its own account has been _determined_ into that spirit's occasion _only_ _by_ _spirit_. That _arabesque_ _painting_ of history, letting a great figure sprout from a wavering stalk, is therefore an ingenious treatment no doubt, but an utterly superficial one. In this emergence of the great out of the small there is indeed present, generally speaking, that inversion which spirit performs upon what is external; but on that very account the external is no _cause_ _within_ _spirit_, or rather this inversion is itself a sublating of the causal relation.

2. This _determinateness_ of the causal relation, however – that content and form are diverse and mutually indifferent – reaches further than that. A _determination_ _of_ _form_ is at the same time a _determination_ _of_ _content_; hence the two sides of the relation, cause and effect, are also a _different_ _content_. Or: since content exists only as the content of some form, it carries the form's difference within its own self and is diverse in essence. Yet since the form it has is causality, and causality is a content identical in the cause and in the effect, the diverse _content_ hangs together _externally_ _with_ _the_ _cause_ on the one hand and _with_ _the_ _effect_ on the other; it therefore _does_ _not_ itself _enter_ _into_ _the_ _acting_ or into the _relation_.

This external content, then, stands in no relation; – _an_ _immediate_ _concrete_ _existence_; – or, since as content it is the identity of cause and effect that is _in_ _itself_, it too is an identity that is _immediate_, an identity _that_ _is_. So it is some _thing_ or other, carrying manifold determinations of its existence, and _among_ _others_ this one too, that _in_ _some_ _respect_ _or_ _other_ it is cause, or effect as well. In it the form-determinations, cause and effect, have their _substrate_, their essential subsistence that is, – and each of them a particular one –, for their identity is their subsistence; – yet at the same time it is their immediate subsistence, not their subsistence as unity of form, or as relation.

{{{ 11.402 }}} This thing, however, is substrate and substance both, since the identical subsistence that it is, it is only _as_ _that_ _of_ _the_ _relation_. It is moreover a _finite_ substance, being determined as immediate _in_ _contrast_ _to_ its own causality. Causality nonetheless belongs to it, for it is equally the identical element only as the identical element belonging to this relation. – Taken as cause, then, this substrate refers itself negatively to _itself_. But that self to which it refers is, _firstly_, a positedness, since the substrate stands determined as _immediately_ actual; taken as content, such positedness is any determination whatever. – _Secondly_, _causality_ falls outside it; _and_ _so_ _causality_ _is_ _what_ _makes_ _up_ _its_ _positedness_. Being causal substance, then, its causality lies in referring itself negatively to itself, that is, to its positedness and to external causality. Hence the acting of this substance sets out from something external, works itself free of that external determination, and its return into itself preserves its immediate concrete existence while sublating its posited existence, and with that its causality altogether.

A stone in motion, for example, is a cause; its motion is one determination it has, but alongside that it holds a host of others – of colour, of shape and so forth – which play no part in its causality. Since the stone's immediate concrete existence lies apart from its reference of form, that is, from causality, the latter is something _external_; its motion, and the causality accruing to it in that motion, are in the stone mere _positedness_. – Yet the causality is also _its_ _own_: this lies in the fact that its substantial subsistence is its self-identical reference to itself, while that reference now stands determined as positedness and is therefore at once a _negative_ _reference_ to self. – Its causality, aimed at itself as at the positedness, or as at something external, accordingly lies in sublating that positedness and, by _removing_ it, coming back into itself, – so that to this extent it is _not_ _in_ _its_ _positedness_ that it is identical with itself; it merely restores _its_ _abstract_ _originality_. – – Or again: rain causes the wet, and the wet is water just as the rain is. What gives this water the determination of being rain, and of being cause, is that another has posited that determination in it; – some force or other, whatever it may be, has lifted the water into the air and drawn it together into a mass whose gravity brings it down. Its distance from the earth is a determination foreign to the water's original identity with itself, to gravity; and its causality lies in doing away with that distance and re-establishing that identity, thereby sublating its causality as well.

The _second_ _determinateness_ of causality now under consideration concerns _the_ _form_; this relation is _causality_ _as_ _external_ _to_ _itself_, is the _originality_ which in its own self is quite as much _positedness_ or _effect_. Uniting the opposed determinations in this way, _in_ _a_ substrate _that_ _is_, is what makes up the _infinite_ _regress_ from causes to {{{ 11.403 }}} causes. – One starts from the effect; as effect it has a cause, that cause has a cause once more, and so onward. Why does the cause in turn have a cause? that is, why does _the_ _same_ _side_, determined a moment ago _as_ _cause_, now count as _effect_, so that a fresh cause is demanded for it? – Because the cause is something _finite_, something _determinate_ as such; determined as _one_ moment of the form in contrast with the effect, and so with its own determinateness, its negation, lying outside it; but just for that reason the cause is itself _finite_, carries _its_ _determinateness_ _within_ _it_, and so is _positedness_ or _effect_. That identity of theirs is likewise posited, yet it is a _third_ thing, the immediate substrate; and causality is external to itself just because here its _originality_ is an _immediacy_. The difference of form is accordingly _first_ _determinateness_, determinateness not yet _posited_ _as_ determinateness; it is _otherness_ _that_ _is_. Finite reflection halts at this immediate on the one side, strips the unity of form away from it, and allows it to count as cause _in_ _one_ _respect_, as effect _in_ _another_; from the other side it pushes the unity of form off into the _infinite_, and by that perennial going-on confesses its own impotence to reach it and hold it fast.

With the _effect_ it stands immediately the same way, or rather the _infinite_ _progress_ _from_ _effect_ _to_ _effect_ is wholly one and the same thing as the _regress_ _from_ _cause_ _to_ _cause_. In the regress the _cause_ turned into an _effect_ which _has_ _another_ cause in turn; and conversely the _effect_ _turns_ _into_ _cause_, which then has yet another effect. – The determinate cause we have considered sets out from an externality and, in its effect, comes back to itself not _as_ _cause_ but rather forfeits its causality there. Conversely, though, the effect reaches a substrate that is substance, a subsistence originally self-referring; on that substrate, accordingly, this positedness becomes _positedness_, which is to say that this substance, as soon as an effect gets posited within it, _behaves_ _as_ _cause_. The first effect, however – the positedness reaching the substrate _from_ _outside_ – is an _other_ as against the second, the one _brought_ _forth_ _by_ _it_; for the second stands determined as _its_ _reflection-into-itself_, the first as an _externality_ upon it. – Yet because causality here is causality external to its own self, it likewise does _not_ _return_ _into_ _itself_ in its effect; therein it becomes _external_ to itself, _its_ effect turns once more into positedness upon a substrate, – upon an _other_ _substance_, which however just as much makes of it a positedness, or manifests itself as cause, thrusts its effect away from itself again, and so onward into the bad infinite.

{{{ 11.404 }}} 3. What has come about through the movement of the determinate causal relation is now to be seen. – Formal causality is extinguished in the effect; thereby the _identical_ element of these two moments has _come_ _to_ _be_; but with that the unity of cause and effect only _as_ _in_ _itself_, a unity upon which the reference of form is external. – This identical element is thereby _immediate_ as well, in both determinations of immediacy: firstly as _being-in-itself_, a _content_ along which causality runs its external course; _secondly_ as an _existing_ substrate wherein cause and effect _inhere_ as form-determinations held apart. Therein they are one _in_ _themselves_, yet on account of this _being-in-itself_, or of the form's externality, each of them is external to its own self, and thus, in its _unity_ with the other, is determined as _other_ as against it too. So the cause does have an effect, _and_ _is_ _at_ _once_ _itself_ _effect_; while the effect, besides having a cause, _is_ _also_ _itself_ _cause_. But the effect which the cause _has_, and the effect _that_ _it_ _is_; – equally, the cause which the effect _has_, and the cause _that_ _it_ _is_, are diverse.

What the movement of the determinate causal relation has now brought about, however, is this: that the cause is _not_ _only_ _extinguished_ in the effect, and with it the effect as well, as happened in formal causality, but that in _its_ _extinction_ the cause _comes_ _to_ _be_ once more in the effect, and that the _effect_, though it _disappears_ into cause, equally _comes_ _to_ _be_ once more there. Every one of these determinations _sublates_ _itself_ _in_ _positing_ _itself_, and _posits_ _itself_ _in_ _sublating_ _itself_; there is no _external_ _transition_ of causality from one substrate to another here, for this _becoming-other_ on causality's part is at once its _own_ _positing_. Causality thus _presupposes_ itself, or _conditions_ _itself_. That identity which formerly only _was_ _in_ _itself_, the substrate, now stands _determined_ as a _presupposition_, or as something _posited_ _over_ _against_ _acting_ causality, and the _reflection_ that was until now merely _external_ to the identical element now _stands_ _in_ _relation_ to it.
