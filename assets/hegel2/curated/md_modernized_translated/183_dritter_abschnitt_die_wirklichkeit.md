## {{{ 11.369 }}} Third Section. Actuality

Actuality is _essence_ _and_ _concrete_ _existence_ in their _unity_; in it the _shapeless_ essence and the _unsteady_ appearance; – or the subsistence that lacks determination and the manifoldness that lacks subsistence, have their truth. _Concrete_ _existence_ is indeed the immediacy that has come forth out of ground, but it has not yet posited form in itself; in determining and shaping itself it is _appearance_; and as this subsistence, determined merely as reflection-into-an-other, works its way on into reflection-into-self, it turns into _two_ _worlds_, two _totalities_ _of_ _content_, one of them carrying the determination of being _reflected_ _into_ _itself_, the other that of being _reflected_ _into_ _an_ _other_. The essential relation, however, exhibits their _form-reference_, and the completion of that reference is the _relation_ of _inner_ and _outer_: that the _content_ of the two is but one _identical_ _foundation_ and quite as much but _one_ _identity_ _of_ _form_. – Since this identity has now emerged with respect to form as well, the form-determination of their diversity is sublated, and it stands _posited_ that they are _one_ absolute totality.

This unity of the inner with the outer is _absolute_ _actuality_. Such actuality, though, is _at first_ the _absolute_ as such; – insofar as it stands posited as a unity wherein form has sublated itself and turned itself into the _empty_ _or_ _external_ _difference_ of an outer and an inner. Towards this absolute, _reflection_ bears itself as something _external_, one that contemplates the absolute rather than being the absolute's own movement. Yet since reflection essentially is that movement, it is, as the absolute's own negative return into itself,

_Secondly_, _actuality_ proper. _Actuality_, _possibility_ and _necessity_ make up _the_ _formal_ _moments_ of the absolute, or the reflection belonging to it.

_Thirdly_, the absolute and its reflection in their unity make up the _absolute_ _relation_, or rather the absolute in relating to its own self; _substance_.
