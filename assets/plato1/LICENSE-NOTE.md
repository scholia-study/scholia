# plato1 — licence

Two editions with different positions. Neither is covered by a blanket claim.

## The Greek edition (`politeia`)

The text is **public domain**. It is the Greek constituted by John Burnet for
*Platonis Opera*, Tomus IV (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1905) — the words, the
punctuation, Burnet's editorial brackets and the Stephanus pagination are all
his, and their copyright has long expired. Nothing here places them back under
licence, and nothing in this repository should be read as trying to.

What is ours in this edition, and licensed **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0** with the rest of
`assets/`:

- the division of each book into titled sections, and those English titles
- the footnotes identifying the poets Plato quotes
- the arrangement of the curated markdown

The NFC normalisation and the unification of elision marks on U+2019 are
mechanical transformations and carry no copyright.

## The English edition (`republic`)

Scholia's own translation, prepared from Burnet's Greek. **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**
throughout.

## Transcription provenance

The Greek was taken from the Perseus Digital Library's `canonical-greekLit`
corpus (Tufts University), whose TEI encoding is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Only
Burnet's public-domain text and his Stephanus milestones were used; Perseus's
own editorial contributions — their section divisions, paragraph milestones,
speaker attribution, bibliographic identifications and URNs — were discarded and
the structure re-derived. Perseus is credited by name in the edition's about
text as a courtesy.
