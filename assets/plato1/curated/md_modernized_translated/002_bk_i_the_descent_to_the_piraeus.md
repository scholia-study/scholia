## The Descent to the Piraeus

{{{ 327a }}} I went down to the Piraeus yesterday with Glaucon, the son of Ariston, both to say a prayer to the goddess and because I wanted to see how they would conduct the festival, since they were holding it for the first time. Now the procession the locals put on struck me as beautiful, but the one the Thracians conducted seemed no less fitting. {{{ 327b }}} Once we had said our prayers and watched the spectacle, we set off back toward the city. Polemarchus, the son of Cephalus, spotted us from a distance as we were heading home, and told his boy to run and ask us to wait for him. The boy caught hold of my cloak from behind and said, Polemarchus asks you to wait. I turned around and asked where he himself was. He's coming up behind you, the boy said; just wait. All right, we'll wait, Glaucon said. {{{ 327c }}}

A little later Polemarchus arrived, along with Adeimantus, Glaucon's brother, Niceratus, the son of Nicias, and a few others, apparently coming from the procession.

Polemarchus said, Socrates, you two look to me as though you had set off for the city, meaning to be gone.

Your guess isn't a bad one, I said.

Do you see, he said, how many of us there are?

How could I not?

Then, he said, either you prove stronger than we are, or you stay right here.

Well then, I said, isn't there still one thing left — that we persuade you it's right to let us go?

But could you persuade us, he said, if we refuse to listen?

Not a chance, Glaucon said.

Then make your plans on the basis that we won't be listening. {{{ 328a }}}

Adeimantus said, Do you two not even know that there's going to be a torch race on horseback this evening, in honor of the goddess?

On horseback? I said — that's a new one. Will they carry torches and pass them to each other as they race on horseback? Or how do you mean?

Just so, Polemarchus said. And besides, they're going to hold an all-night vigil worth seeing; we'll get up after dinner and go watch it. We'll also meet lots of the young men there, and we'll talk with them. So stay {{{ 328b }}} and don't do otherwise.
