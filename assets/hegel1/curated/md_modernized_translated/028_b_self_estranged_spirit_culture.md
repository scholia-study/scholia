## {{{ 429 }}} B. Self-Estranged Spirit; Culture

Ethical substance kept the opposition shut inside its simple consciousness, and kept that consciousness immediately at one with its essence. Hence, for the consciousness that is aimed immediately at it and whose custom it is, the essence carries the simple determinateness of _being_; consciousness does not count in its own eyes as _this excluding self_, nor does substance signify an existence shut out from consciousness, one with which consciousness would have to make itself one only by estranging itself while at the same time bringing that existence forth. But a spirit whose self is what is absolutely discrete finds its content over against it as an actuality just as unyielding, and the world is here determined as something external, as self-consciousness's negative. Yet this world is a spiritual essence; in itself it is being and individuality interpenetrating each other; the world's existence is self-consciousness's _work_, yet no less an actuality immediately on hand and alien to it, one possessing a being peculiarly its own, in which self-consciousness fails to cognize itself. This world is the external {{{ 430 }}} essence, the free content belonging to right; but the external actuality that the master of the world of right comprises within himself is not merely this elemental essence, on hand for the self by chance, but is the self's labor, though not a positive one, — its negative labor rather. It receives its existence through self-consciousness's _own_ externalization and loss of essence, which the external violence of the unbound elements, amid the devastation that reigns in the world of right, seems to inflict upon it. Taken for themselves, these elements are only sheer devastating and the dissolution of themselves; but this dissolution, this negative essence of theirs, is precisely the self; the self is their subject, their doing and their becoming. {{ 265 }} This doing and becoming, though, by which substance becomes actual, is the estrangement of personality, for the self that is valid in and for itself _immediately_, that is, _without estrangement_, has no substance and is the play of those raging elements; _its_ substance is accordingly its own externalization, and the externalization is the substance, or the spiritual powers that order themselves into a world and thereby maintain themselves.

In this manner substance is _spirit_, that is, the self and the essence in self-conscious _unity_, though each of the two also signifies estrangement for the other. Spirit is the _consciousness_ of an objective actuality that is free for itself; yet that unity of the self and of the essence {{{ 431 }}} stands opposed to this consciousness: to the _actual_ consciousness, the _pure consciousness_. On the one hand, actual self-consciousness passes over through its externalization into the actual world, and that world passes back into it; on the other hand, this very actuality, the person as much as the objectivity, is sublated; they are purely universal. Their estrangement is precisely the _pure consciousness_, or the _essence_. What is present has its opposite immediately in its _beyond_, the beyond being the present's thinking and the present's being-thought; and the beyond likewise has its opposite in the here below, which is the actuality estranged from it.

Accordingly this spirit builds up for itself not just _one_ world but a twofold one, split apart and set in opposition. — For ethical spirit the world is its own _present;_ and so every power within that world falls inside this unity and, insofar as the two are distinguished, stands in balance with the whole. Nothing there signifies self-consciousness's negative; the departed spirit itself is present in kinship's _blood_, in the family's _self_, and government's universal _power_ is the _***will,***_ which is the people's self. Here, by contrast, what is present signifies merely objective _actuality_ whose consciousness lies in the beyond; every singular moment, taken as _essence_, receives that consciousness, and with it actuality, from something else, and to the extent that it is actual, its essence is other than its actuality. Nothing possesses a spirit that is grounded in it and dwells within it, {{{ 432 }}} but each thing is outside itself in something alien — the whole's balance is not a unity that abides with itself, nor a repose that has come back into itself, but rests instead upon the estrangement of what is opposed. The whole, therefore, like every singular moment, is a self-estranged reality; it breaks apart into a realm where _self-consciousness is actual_, being both itself and its object, and into another, that of _pure_ consciousness, which, situated beyond the first, has no actual present but is in _faith_. {{ 266 }} Just as the ethical world, out of the separation of divine from human law and of their shapes, and just as its consciousness, out of the separation into knowledge and into unconsciousness, go back into their destiny, into the _self_ as this opposition's _negative power_, so these two realms of self-estranged spirit will likewise turn back into the _self_; but if the first was the immediately valid _self_ that came first, the singular _person_, then this second one, returning into itself out of its externalization, will be the _universal self_, the consciousness that takes hold of the _concept_; and those spiritual worlds, whose moments one and all lay claim to a fixed actuality and an unspiritual subsistence, will dissolve away in _pure insight_. As the self that _takes hold of_ itself, pure insight brings culture to completion; what it apprehends is the self alone, and it apprehends everything as the self, that is, it _comprehends_ everything, wipes out all objectivity, and converts {{{ 433 }}} all being-_in-itself_ into a being-_for-itself_. Once directed against faith, which is the realm of _essence_, alien and lying in the beyond, pure insight is the _Enlightenment_. In this realm too, into which estranged spirit rescues itself as into the consciousness of a repose equal to itself, the Enlightenment carries estrangement through to completion; it disorders the housekeeping that spirit conducts here, in that it carries in the implements of the world here below, implements that spirit cannot deny to be its own property, since its consciousness belongs to that world as well. — Through this negative business pure insight simultaneously makes itself real, and produces its own object: the uncognizable _absolute essence_, together with the _useful_. Because actuality has in this way forfeited all substantiality, so that nothing _in itself_ remains within it, the realm of faith is overthrown and so too is that of the real world, and this revolution produces _absolute freedom_, whereby spirit, estranged until now, has withdrawn completely into itself, quits this land of culture, and crosses over into a different land, the land of _moral consciousness_.
