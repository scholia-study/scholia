## {{{ 548 }}} C. Spirit Certain of Itself. Morality

The ethical world exhibited the spirit that within it was merely a departed one, _the singular self_, as that world's fate and its truth. This _person_ of _right_, however, has its substance and its fulfillment outside itself. This abstraction of the person is sublated by the movement belonging to the world of culture and to the world of faith, and, through an estrangement carried to completion, through the highest abstraction, substance turns for spirit's self into the _universal will_ at first, and at last into spirit's own property. So knowledge appears here to have attained at last a complete equality with its truth; for its truth is just this knowledge, and the entire opposition of the two sides has fallen away; not merely _for us_ or _in itself_ has it fallen away, but for self-consciousness itself. {{ 324 }} For it has made itself master of the opposition that belongs to consciousness as such, an opposition grounded in the antithesis between the object and consciousness's certainty of its own self; now, however, the object is for it that very certainty, is knowledge — while its certainty of itself, taken as {{{ 549 }}} such, has purposes of its own no longer, and so stands no longer within determinateness but is pure knowledge.

The knowledge of self-consciousness is therefore for it the _substance_ itself. For it, substance is in one undivided unity just as much _immediate_ as it is absolutely _mediated_. _Immediate_ — as the ethical consciousness does, it too knows duty and does duty itself, and belongs to duty as to its own nature; yet it is not _character_, which the ethical consciousness is, since immediacy makes the latter a determinate spirit, one belonging to but a single ethical essentiality and carrying with it the side of _not knowing_. — _Absolute mediation_ it is as well, in the manner of the consciousness that undergoes culture and the consciousness of faith; for what it essentially is, is the self's movement of sublating the abstraction of _immediate existence_, so as to make itself something universal; — but this comes about neither through a sheer estrangement and tearing apart of its self and of actuality, — nor through flight. On the contrary, in its substance it is _present_ to itself _immediately_, for that substance is its knowledge, is the pure certainty of itself as intuited; and just _this immediacy_, which constitutes its own actuality, is all actuality, since what is immediate is _being_ itself, and as an immediacy purified through absolute negativity it is something pure, it is _being_ in general, or _all_ being.

The absolute essence is therefore not used up in the determination of being the simple _essence_ of _thinking_ {{{ 550 }}}; on the contrary, it is _actuality_ in its entirety, and such actuality exists only as knowledge; whatever consciousness were not to know would carry no sense and can count as no power for it; into consciousness's knowing will every objectivity, the whole world, has retreated. Its absolute freedom lies in this, that it knows its own freedom, and this very knowledge of its freedom is what constitutes its substance, its purpose, and its one and only content.
