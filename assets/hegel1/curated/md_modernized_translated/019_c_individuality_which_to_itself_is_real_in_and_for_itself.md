## {{{ 330 }}} C. Individuality, Which to Itself Is Real In and For Itself

Self-consciousness has now seized upon the concept of itself which to begin with was merely our concept of it, namely, that in the certainty of its own self it is all reality; and what now stands to it as purpose and as essence is the self-moving interpenetration of the universal — endowments and abilities — with individuality. — The purposes considered up to this point are the singular moments of that fulfillment and interpenetration, taken _before the unity_ in which they came together. They have disappeared as abstractions and chimeras belonging to those first vapid shapes of spiritual self-consciousness, shapes whose truth lies solely in the being merely meant by the heart, by imagination, and by talk, and not in reason, which, now certain in and for itself of its reality, no longer strives to bring itself forth first as _purpose_ set in _opposition_ to the actuality that immediately is, but rather takes the category as such to be the object of its consciousness. — For what has been sublated is the determination of the self-consciousness _that is for itself_ {{{ 331 }}} or is _negative_, the determination in which reason made its entrance; _it found_ an _actuality_ on hand that would be the negative of itself, and only by sublating that actuality did it make its _purpose_ real. {{ 215 }} But now that _purpose_ and _being-in-itself_ have shown themselves to be one and the same with what _being_ for an _other_ and the _actuality found on hand_ are, truth no longer separates itself from certainty; it is now indifferent whether one takes the posited purpose for the certainty of itself and its realizing for the truth, or instead the purpose for the truth and actuality for certainty; rather, what essence and purpose are in and for themselves is immediate reality's own certainty, the interpenetration of the _in-itself_ with _being-for-itself_, and of the universal with individuality; in the doing itself lie its truth and its actuality, and the _presentation_ or the _uttering of individuality_ is for it a purpose in and for itself.

With this concept, then, self-consciousness has returned into itself out of the opposed determinations which the category had for it and which its relating to the category had, first as observing and then as active. The pure category itself is now its object, or rather it is the category that has become conscious of itself. Its reckoning with its previous shapes is thereby closed; they lie behind it, forgotten, {{{ 332 }}} and they do not confront it as a world it finds on hand, but develop only inside it as transparent moments. Still, within its consciousness they do come apart as a _movement_ of differentiated moments, one that has not yet drawn itself together into its substantial unity. Yet in _all_ of them it holds firmly to the simple unity of being and of the self, which is their _genus_. —

Consciousness has herewith thrown off all opposition and every condition of its doing; it starts out freshly _from itself_, directed not at _another_ but _at its own self_. Because individuality is, in its own self, actuality, both the _material_ that is worked upon and the _purpose_ of the doing lie within the doing itself. The doing therefore takes on the appearance of a circle in motion, one that moves within its own self freely in the void, now widening and now narrowing without hindrance, and playing, wholly content, only in and with its own self. Whatever element individuality presents its shape in has the significance of a pure receiving of that shape; it is the day in general, to which consciousness wants to show itself. This doing changes nothing and is turned against nothing; it is the pure form of translation out of _not being seen_ into _being seen_, and the content that is brought to light and presents itself is nothing else than what this doing already is in itself. It is _in itself_ — such is its form qua unity that is _thought_; and it is _actual_ — such is its form qua {{{ 333 }}} unity that _is_; it is itself _content_ only under this determination of simplicity, in contrast to the determination in which it transitions and moves.
