## {{{ 382 }}} A. True Spirit, Ethical Life

Spirit, in its simple truth, is consciousness, and it strikes its moments apart from one another. _Action_ divides spirit into the substance and the consciousness of that substance; and it divides the substance just as much as it divides the consciousness. {{ 241 }} The substance steps forth as universal _essence_ and _purpose_, set over against itself as _singularized_ actuality; the infinite middle is self-consciousness, which _in itself_ already is the unity of substance and self and now becomes so _for itself_, joining the universal essence with its singularized actuality, lifting this actuality up into that essence and thereby acting ethically, — and letting that essence down into this actuality, and thereby executing the purpose, the substance that had merely been thought; what it brings forth, as _its work_ and hence as _actuality_, is the unity of substance with its own self.

As consciousness comes apart, the simple substance has in part taken on the opposition directed against self-consciousness, and in part it thereby exhibits, just as much on its own account, the nature of consciousness, {{{ 383 }}} which is to differentiate itself within itself, as a world structured into its masses. So it splits into an ethical essence that is differentiated within itself, into a law that is human and a law that is divine. In the same way, the self-consciousness that steps up over against it assigns itself, in keeping with its essence, to one of these powers, and, as knowledge, splits into non-knowledge of what it is doing and into knowledge of that same doing, which is for that very reason a deceived knowledge. In its deed, then, it undergoes both the contradiction of _those powers_ that the substance had split itself into, along with their mutual destruction, and equally the contradiction between its own knowledge of the ethical life of its acting — and what is ethical in and for itself, and so it comes upon _its own_ downfall. In fact, though, this movement has made the ethical substance into _actual self-consciousness_, or has made _this_ self into one that exists _in_ and _for itself_, yet it is precisely in this that ethical life has foundered.
