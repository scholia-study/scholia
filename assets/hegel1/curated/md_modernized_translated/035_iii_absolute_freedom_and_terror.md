## {{{ 533 }}} III. Absolute Freedom and Terror

It is in utility that consciousness has found its own concept. But this concept is in part still an _object_, and for that very reason still a _purpose_ as well, one in possession of which consciousness does not yet immediately stand. As yet, utility remains something predicated of the object, not the subject itself, or its immediate and sole _actuality_. It is the same thing that appeared earlier in this guise: that _being-for-itself_ has not yet shown itself to be what is substance for the other moments, which would make the useful immediately nothing but the self of consciousness, and would thereby put it in consciousness's possession. — This retraction of the form of objectivity in the useful has, however, _in itself_ already taken place, and out of this inner overturning there emerges the actual overturning of actuality, the new shape of consciousness, _absolute freedom_.

For in fact what is present is no more than objectivity's empty semblance, and it is this that holds {{{ 534 }}} self-consciousness apart from possession. For, on the one side, whatever subsistence and validity the determinate members of the organization of the actual and the believed world had has withdrawn altogether into this simple determination, which is their ground and their spirit; on the other side, that determination retains nothing of its own any longer, being instead pure metaphysics, pure concept, or the knowledge self-consciousness has. What consciousness cognizes, in the useful as object with respect to its being _in_ and _for itself_, is that _its being-in-itself_ is, in essence, _being for another_; _being-in-itself_, as what is _selfless_, is in truth the passive, or what is there for another self. {{ 317 }} For consciousness, though, the object has this abstract form, that of _pure being-in-itself_. Consciousness, after all, is pure _insight_, and the differences within it have the pure form of concepts. — But the _being-for-itself_ into which being for another withdraws, the self, is not some self of its own, distinct from the I, belonging to what goes by the name of object; for consciousness as pure insight is not a _singular_ self confronted by the object as an equally _own_ self, but is the pure concept, self looking into self, the absolute doubled seeing of _one's own self_; its certainty of itself constitutes the universal subject, and the concept it knows constitutes the essence of all actuality. So if the useful amounted to nothing but an interchange of moments that never came back into its own _unity_, and was on that account still an object for knowledge, it now stops being one, since knowledge {{{ 535 }}} is itself that movement of the abstract moments; knowledge is the universal self, self of itself no less than of the object, and, being universal, the unity into which this movement returns.

Spirit is hereby present as _absolute freedom_; it is that self-consciousness which grasps itself thus: that its own certainty of itself is what is essence in every spiritual mass, in the real world no less than in the supersensible one, or, turned about, that essence and actuality are nothing but consciousness's knowledge of _itself_. — Aware of its pure personality, it is therein aware of spiritual reality in its entirety, and reality is nothing but the spiritual; the world, for it, is quite simply its own will, and that will is a universal will. And this will, be it noted, is not the empty thought of a will posited in tacit or represented consent, but is really and truly a universal will, a will belonging to each and every _singular_ one as such. For in itself the will is the consciousness that personality, or anyone whatever, has, and it ought to exist as this genuine, actual will, as that _self_-conscious essence which belongs to all personality and to each, so that everyone always does the whole undividedly, and whatever comes on the scene as the whole's doing is the immediate, conscious doing of _each one_.

This undivided substance of absolute freedom mounts the world's throne, and no power whatever is able to withstand it. For consciousness is in truth the sole element wherein spiritual essences, or {{{ 536 }}} powers, possess their substance, and so their whole system, which organized and maintained itself by dividing into masses, has fallen apart, once singular consciousness grasped the object as having no essence other than self-consciousness itself, that is, as being absolutely the concept. What made the concept into an existent _object_ was its differentiation into separated, _subsisting_ masses; but as the object becomes the concept, nothing subsisting remains in it; negativity has penetrated all its moments. {{ 318 }} It steps into existence in this way: each singular consciousness raises itself out of the sphere allotted to it, ceases to locate its essence and its work in that particularized mass, and instead apprehends its own self to be the _concept_ of the will and all the masses to be this will's essence, so that it can actualize itself only in a labor that is labor entire. In this absolute freedom, then, every estate is wiped out, all those spiritual essences the whole articulates itself into; the singular consciousness that had belonged to such a member, willing and accomplishing within it, has sublated its barrier; the universal purpose is its purpose, the universal law its language, the universal work its work.

Object and _difference_ have here lost the meaning of _utility_, which had served as predicate for all real being; consciousness no longer starts its movement from the object as from _something alien_ out of which it {{{ 537 }}} would first have to return into itself, for the object is to it consciousness itself; opposition accordingly reduces to the one difference between _singular_ and _universal_ consciousness; but the singular is immediately, for itself, that which merely bore _the semblance_ of opposition, and it is universal consciousness and will. Over the corpse of that vanished self-subsistence belonging to real or believed being, the _beyond_ of this its actuality floats as nothing more than the reek of a stale gas, the empty Etre suprême.

Once the differentiated spiritual masses have been sublated, along with the restricted life of individuals and spirit's two worlds, what remains present is nothing but universal self-consciousness moving within itself, moving as an interaction of itself under the form _of universality_ with itself as _personal_ consciousness; the universal will withdraws _into itself_ and is a _singular_ will, faced by the universal law and work. Yet this _singular_ consciousness knows itself, with the same immediacy, to be universal will; it knows that its object is law which it has itself given and work which it has itself accomplished; and so, passing over into activity and bringing objectivity into being, it produces nothing singular, but only laws and acts of state.

By this the movement becomes consciousness's interaction with its own self, one in which it discharges nothing that would take on the shape of an _object_ standing _free_ over against it {{{ 538 }}}. The consequence is that it can attain no positive work at all: neither universal works of language nor of actuality, neither laws and universal institutions of _conscious_ freedom — nor deeds and works of freedom _as willing_. — The work that freedom, in giving itself _consciousness_, might make of itself would amount to this: that as _universal_ substance it should turn itself into an _object_ and into _enduring being_. {{ 319 }} This otherness would be the difference within it, according to which it would divide itself into subsisting spiritual masses and into the members of various powers; partly in that these masses would be the _thought-things_ of a separate legislative, judicial, and executive _power_, partly in that they would be the _real essences_ that emerged in the real world of culture and, once the content of universal doing were considered more closely, the particular masses of laboring, which are further differentiated as more specific _estates_. — Universal freedom, having in this way separated itself into its members and by that very act made itself into _existent_ substance, would thereby be free of singular individuality and would apportion the _multitude_ of _individuals_ among its various members. Personality's doing and being would thereby be confined, though, to a single branch of the whole, to a single sort of doing and being; placed into _being_ as its element, personality would take on the significance of something _determinate_; it would stop being universal self-consciousness in truth. Universal self-consciousness will {{{ 539 }}} not be swindled out of _actuality_ in the process, neither by the _representation_ of obedience to _self-given_ laws that would allot it a part, nor by its _representation_ in legislating and in universal doing, — not swindled out of the actuality of giving the law _itself_, and of accomplishing not one singular work but the universal _itself_; for where the self is merely _represented_ and merely _a representation_, there it is not something _actual_; where it is _stood in for_, it simply is not.

Singular self-consciousness fails to find itself in absolute freedom's _universal work_ as an existent substance, and it fails just as surely in the proper _deeds_ and _individual_ actions of freedom's will. If the universal is to arrive at a deed, it has to draw itself together into individuality's One and put a singular self-consciousness at the head; for only within a self that is one is the universal will an _actual_ will. By that very move, however, _all the other singulars_ are shut out from the _whole_ of this deed, sharing in it only within limits, with the result that the deed would not be a deed of _actual universal_ self-consciousness. — Universal freedom can therefore bring forth no positive work or deed; nothing is left to it but _negative doing_; it is merely the _fury_ of vanishing.

But there remains one actuality that stands highest and most opposed to universal freedom, or rather one object that still comes to be for it: the {{{ 540 }}} freedom and singularity belonging to actual self-consciousness itself. For universality of that sort, which never lets things come to the reality of an organic articulation and whose purpose lies in preserving itself within undivided continuity, differentiates itself internally all the same, being as it is movement, or consciousness as such. And precisely for the sake of its own abstraction it splits into extremes equally abstract: on the one side universality, simple, unbending, cold; on the other the discrete, absolute, hard brittleness and stubborn pointlikeness of actual self-consciousness. {{ 320 }} Having finished off the real organization and now subsisting for itself, universality has this and nothing else for its object; — an object stripped of all further content, of possession, existence, and outward extension, an object that is nothing but this knowledge of itself as a singular self, absolutely pure and free. There is nothing about it to lay hold of except its _abstract_ existence as such. — The relationship of these two, then, since they are indivisibly and absolutely for themselves and can therefore send no part into the middle by which they might link up with each other, is wholly _unmediated_ pure negation; and indeed the negation of the singular as an _existent_ within the universal. Universal freedom's sole work and deed is accordingly _death_, a _death_, moreover, with no inner extent or fulfillment, since what gets negated is the unfulfilled point of the self that is absolutely free; and so it is the {{{ 541 }}} coldest, shallowest death of all, meaning no more than the hacking apart of a cabbage head or a swallow of water.

In the shallowness of this syllable lies the wisdom of the government, the understanding possessed by the universal will in accomplishing itself. The government is itself nothing other than the point that fixes itself in place, or the individuality of the universal will. Being a willing and accomplishing that issues from one point, it wills and accomplishes, at one and the same time, an ordinance and an action that are determinate. In doing so it shuts all remaining individuals out of its deed, while at the same time constituting itself as something that is a determinate will and hence set against the universal will; it therefore has flatly no way of presenting itself other than as a _faction_. Only the _victorious_ faction is called the government, and precisely in its being a faction there lies, immediately, the necessity of its downfall; and conversely, that it is the government is what makes it a faction and guilty. Should the universal will hold the government's actual acting against it as the crime committed upon it, the government in return possesses nothing determinate, nothing external, in which the guilt of the will set against it might display itself; for over against the government, which is the _actual_ universal will, there stands only the unactual pure will, the _intention_. _Falling under suspicion_ accordingly steps into the place of _being guilty_, or carries the same meaning and effect {{{ 542 }}}, and the reaction from outside directed at this actuality, which resides in the bare inwardness of intention, consists in the dry extermination of this existent self, a self from which there is nothing to remove save its very being.

In this _work_ peculiar to it, absolute freedom turns itself into an object, and self-consciousness learns what freedom _is_. _In itself_ it is precisely this _abstract self-consciousness_, which eradicates within itself all difference and all subsistence of difference. {{ 321 }} As this it is its own object; death's _terror_ is the intuition of this negative essence of its own. This reality of freedom's, however, absolutely free self-consciousness finds to be wholly other than freedom's own concept of itself had been, the concept, namely, that the universal will is nothing but personality's _positive_ essence, and that personality knows itself in that will only positively, only as preserved. Here, instead, for this consciousness, which as pure insight draws a flat separation between its positive and its negative essence, — the absolute without predicates as pure _thinking_ and equally as pure _matter_, — the absolute _transition_ from the one to the other is present in its actuality. — The universal will, as absolutely _positive_ actual self-consciousness, turns over into the _negative_ essence, because it is this self-conscious actuality _heightened_ into _pure_ thinking or into _abstract_ matter, and shows itself to be just as much the _sublating_ of _thinking of itself_, or of self-consciousness.

{{{ 543 }}} Being, then, the universal will's _pure_ self-equality, absolute freedom carries _negation_ within it, and with negation _difference_ as such, and it develops that difference once again into _actual_ difference. For in the self-equal universal will pure _negativity_ finds its _element_ of subsistence, its _substance_, wherein its moments become real; it finds the matter it can put to use for its own determinateness; and since that substance has proved to be the negative for singular consciousness, the organization of the spiritual masses builds itself up once more, and among these masses the multitude of individual consciousnesses is distributed. These consciousnesses, having felt the fear of their absolute master, death, once more put up with negation and with the differences, subordinate themselves to the masses, and return to a divided and restricted work, but thereby to their substantial actuality.

This tumult would have flung spirit back to where it started, the real and ethical world of culture, a world that the fear of the master, come once again into people's hearts, had done no more than freshen and make young again. Spirit would have to traverse this circuit of necessity afresh and repeat it endlessly, were the result nothing more than self-consciousness and substance thoroughly penetrating each other, — a penetration in which self-consciousness, having undergone the force its universal essence turns {{{ 544 }}} negatively against it, would want to know and find itself to be, not this particular, but only something universal, and could on that account also bear the objective actuality of universal spirit, which shuts it out as a particular. — But in absolute freedom there was nothing, neither consciousness, which is sunk into a manifold existence or which fixes determinate purposes and thoughts for itself, — nor an _external_ valid world, be it one of actuality or of thought, standing in reciprocal interaction with one another; rather, there was the world simply in the form of consciousness, as universal will, and equally self-consciousness drawn together out of all extended existence, out of manifold purpose and judgment, into the simple self. {{ 322 }} The culture it gains through interaction with that essence is therefore the loftiest and the final one: watching its pure, simple actuality vanish immediately and pass over into the emptiness of nothing. Within the world of culture itself, consciousness never reaches the point of intuiting its negation or estrangement in this form of pure abstraction; there its negation is a filled one: either the honor or the wealth won in place of the self from which it estranged itself; — or the language of spirit and of insight that torn consciousness attains, or else it is faith's heaven, or the useful of the Enlightenment. Every one of these determinations goes under in the loss the self suffers in absolute {{{ 545 }}} freedom; its negation is death without meaning, the negative's sheer terror, holding within it nothing positive, nothing that fills. — Yet this negation, in its actuality, is not anything _alien_ at the same time: it is neither that universal _necessity_ lying beyond, in which the ethical world perishes, nor the singular contingency of private possession or of the owner's whim, on which torn consciousness sees its own dependence, — it is rather the _universal will_, and this will, in the last abstraction it has reached, holds nothing positive and can therefore return nothing for the sacrifice, — and just for that reason it stands unmediatedly at one with self-consciousness; being the purely negative, it is for that very reason the purely positive; and death without meaning, the self's unfulfilled negativity, reverses within the inner concept into positivity that is absolute. The immediate unity that consciousness has with the universal will, the demand it makes that it know itself to be this determinate point within that will, is transformed for consciousness into the flatly opposite experience. What vanishes for it here is abstract _being_, the immediacy of a point without substance, and the universal will is itself just this vanished immediacy, as which consciousness now knows itself, insofar as it is _sublated immediacy_, insofar as it amounts to pure knowledge or pure will. Consciousness thereby knows that will as itself and knows itself as essence, though not {{{ 546 }}} as the essence that is _immediately existent_, knowing neither that will as the revolutionary government or as the anarchy striving to constitute anarchy, nor knowing itself as the midpoint of this faction or of its opposite; rather, the _universal will_ is its _pure knowledge and willing_, and _it_ is universal will as this pure knowledge and willing. {{ 323 }} In this it does not lose _itself_, for pure knowledge and willing is what it is, rather than the atomic point of consciousness. It is thus pure knowledge interacting with its own self; pure _knowledge_ taken as _essence_ is the universal will; yet this _essence_ is simply and solely pure knowledge. Self-consciousness is therefore the pure knowledge of essence as pure knowledge. Further, as _singular self_ it is nothing but the form of the subject, the form of actual doing, and this form it knows precisely as _form_; and likewise _objective_ actuality, _being_, is for it a form that is simply selfless; for such actuality would be what goes unknown; but this knowledge knows knowledge as the essence.

Absolute freedom has thus settled with itself the opposition between universal and singular will; self-estranged spirit, driven to the peak of its own opposition, where pure willing and what purely wills still stand differentiated, brings the opposition down to a form that is transparent, and finds itself there. — As the actual world's realm makes its transition into the realm of faith and of insight, so absolute freedom departs its self-destroying {{{ 547 }}} actuality for a different country of spirit conscious of itself, one in which, amid such unactuality, freedom counts as the true, on whose thought spirit regales itself so long as _it is thought_ and stays thought, and knows this being locked up inside self-consciousness as the perfect and complete essence. The new shape of _moral spirit_ has arisen.
