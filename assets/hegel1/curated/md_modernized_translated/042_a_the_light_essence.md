## a, The Light-Essence

Spirit, as the _essence_ that is _self-consciousness_, — in other words, as the self-conscious essence that constitutes the whole of truth and knows every actuality to be itself, remains, over against the reality that it bestows on itself through the movement of its consciousness, nothing more as yet than _its concept_; and against the daylight of this unfolding that concept is the night of spirit's essence, while against the existence of spirit's moments as self-subsistent shapes it is the creative mystery from which spirit is born. This mystery contains its own revelation within itself; for existence draws its necessity from this concept, inasmuch as the concept is spirit knowing itself and therefore holds in its essence the moment whereby it is consciousness and gives itself objective representation. {{ 371 }} — It is the pure I that, in externalizing itself, has in itself, as a _universal object_, the certainty of its own self; or rather, for it this object is the permeation of all thinking and all actuality.

{{{ 641 }}} In the first, immediate splitting-in-two of absolute spirit as it knows itself, the shape spirit takes carries the determination proper to _immediate consciousness_, that is, to _sense_-certainty. Spirit intuits itself under the form of _being_, though not that spiritless _being_, replete with contingent determinations of sensation, which pertains to sense-certainty; rather, it is being that spirit has filled. It likewise includes within itself the form that came up already with immediate _self-consciousness_, namely the _master_'s form as against that self-consciousness of spirit which draws back from its own object. — This _being_, which the concept of spirit has filled, is accordingly the _shape_ taken by spirit's _simple_ relation to itself, or, equally, the shape of shapelessness. In virtue of this determination that shape is the pure _light-essence_ of the sunrise, which contains and fills all things and which sustains itself in a substantiality devoid of form. What it has as its otherness is the negative, simple in just the same way, namely _darkness_; the movements by which it externalizes itself, the creations it brings forth in the unresisting element of its otherness, are outpourings of light, and, simple as they are, these outpourings are at the same time both its becoming-for-itself and a returning out of its existence, streams of fire that devour all shaping. The difference which it confers on itself does indeed run riot within the substance of existence, taking shape as the forms of nature; yet the simplicity essential to its thinking roves among them, without subsistence and without understanding, stretching {{{ 642 }}} their limits to the measureless and dissolving, in its own sublimity, the beauty they have raised to splendor.

Whatever content this pure _being_ unfolds, that is, its perceiving, is therefore an essenceless playing-alongside upon this substance, which merely _rises_ and never _sinks_ into itself so as to become subject and to secure its differences by way of the self; the determinations of this substance are mere attributes, which never flourish into self-subsistence but stay nothing more than names for the One of many names. The One wears the manifold forces of existence, together with the shapes of actuality, the way one wears a selfless ornament; they are merely messengers of its power, destitute of any will of their own, intuitions of its magnificence and voices that sing its praise.

This reeling life, however, has to determine itself toward _being-for-itself_, and to lend subsistence to its vanishing shapes. That _immediate being_, in which this life sets itself over against its own consciousness, is none other than the _negative_ power which dissolves its own differences. In truth, then, it is the _self_; and this is why spirit proceeds to know itself under the form of the self. {{ 372 }} Pure light casts its simplicity apart into an infinity of forms and makes of itself an offering to being-for-itself, in order that the singular may take its subsistence from light's substance.
