## {{{ 298 }}} a. Pleasure and Necessity

Self-consciousness, which quite generally is _reality_ to itself, has its object in itself, yet has it as an object that so far it holds only _for itself_ and that is not yet in being; over against it stands _being_ as an actuality different from the one it is itself; and it now aims, by carrying out its being-for-itself, to intuit itself as another self-subsistent essence. Its _first purpose_ is this: that it should become conscious, within the other self-consciousness, of itself as a singular essence, or, to put it otherwise, that it should turn this other into itself; and it proceeds with the certainty that _in itself_ the other is already it itself. — To the extent that it has lifted itself out of the ethical substance and out of the settled being of thought and up into its _being-for-itself_, the law of custom and of existence, the acquaintances gathered by observation, and theory all lie behind it like a gray shade just now fading away, since what these amount to is instead a knowledge whose object has a being-for-itself and an actuality different from those belonging to self-consciousness. The earth-spirit has entered into it, displacing the seemingly heavenly spirit that belonged to universality in knowledge and in doing, a universality in which the sensation and the enjoyment of singularity fall mute; and to the earth-spirit only the being that constitutes the actuality of the singular consciousness counts as the true actuality.

{{ 199 }} It therefore hurls itself into life and carries out the pure individuality in which it makes its appearance. It is less a matter of its making its happiness than of its taking that happiness immediately and enjoying it. The shades of science, of laws and of principles, which are all that intervenes between it and an actuality of its own, vanish like a lifeless fog that cannot contend with the certainty of its reality; it takes life for itself as a ripe fruit is plucked, a fruit that comes toward the hand quite as much as it is taken.

Only according to one moment is its doing a doing of _desire_; it is directed not at the extermination of the whole objective essence, but solely at the form of that essence's otherness, or of its self-subsistence, which is an essenceless shine; for _in itself_ this essence counts for it as the same essence, or as the selfhood belonging to it. _Living existence_ is the element within which desire and desire's object have their subsistence, self-subsistent and indifferent toward each other; and the enjoyment of desire sublates that element in so far as it accrues to desire's object. Here, however, this element, which gives each of the two its separated actuality, is rather the category, {{{ 300 }}} a being that is essentially a _represented_ one; it is therefore the _consciousness_ of self-subsistence; — whether it be the natural consciousness, or the consciousness elaborated into a system of laws, which maintains the individuals each for itself. For a self-consciousness that knows the other to be _its own_ selfhood, this separation has no being in itself. And so it attains the enjoyment of _pleasure_: consciousness of its own actualization within a consciousness that has the appearance of self-subsistence, or intuition of the unity of the two self-subsistent self-consciousnesses. It does reach its purpose, but in doing so it undergoes the experience of what the purpose truly is. What it comprehends itself to be is _this singular, for-itself-existing_ essence, yet actualizing that purpose amounts to sublating it, since it becomes an object to itself not as _this singular_ but instead as the _unity_ that it forms together with the other self-consciousness, and hence as a sublated singular or as a _universal_.

Pleasure once enjoyed does bear the positive significance of having become _itself_ in the form of objective self-consciousness, but it bears no less the negative significance of having sublated _itself_; and because it grasped its actualization under the former significance alone, what it has undergone makes its way into its consciousness as a contradiction, one in which the actuality that its singularity had reached sees itself annihilated by the negative _essence_, which, itself void of actuality, {{{ 301 }}} confronts that actuality as an emptiness and is nevertheless the power that devours it. {{ 200 }} This essence amounts to nothing other than the _concept_ of what, in itself, this individuality is. Yet among the shapes of the spirit that actualizes itself, this individuality still remains the poorest; for as yet it is to itself no more than the _abstraction_ of reason, or the _immediacy_ belonging to the _unity_ of the _for-itself_ and of _being-in-itself_; hence its essence is merely the _abstract_ category. The form of _immediate, simple_ being, however, is no longer what it has, as it did for observing spirit, where it is abstract _being_, or, when posited as something alien, _thinghood_ as such. What has now entered into this thinghood is being-for-itself along with mediation. Accordingly it steps forward as a _circle_, and its content is the pure relation of the simple essentialities in developed form. Hence the actualization this individuality has achieved consists in nothing beyond this: that it has flung the circle of abstractions out of the closed confines of simple self-consciousness and into the element of _being-for-it_, the element of objective spreading-out. So what becomes the _object_ for self-consciousness, as its essence, in the pleasure it enjoys, is the spreading-out of those empty essentialities: pure unity, pure difference, and the relation between them; apart from that, the object that individuality undergoes as its _essence_ possesses no content at all. This object is what one calls _necessity_; for necessity, along with _fate_ {{{ 302 }}} and similar names, is just that of which one cannot say _what_ it does, what determinate laws and positive content belong to it, because it is itself the absolute pure concept intuited as _being_, the _relation_ that is simple and empty yet unstoppable and undisturbable, whose work is merely the nothingness of singularity. Necessity is this _firm interconnection_ because what hangs together in it are the pure essentialities, the empty abstractions; unity, difference and their relation are categories of which each, in and for itself, is nothing and has being only in relation to its opposite, so that they can never fall apart from one another. What relates them to each other is their _concept_, since they are the pure concepts themselves; and necessity is constituted by this _absolute relation_, this abstract movement. The merely singular individuality, whose content so far is nothing but the pure concept of reason, has therefore not hurled itself out of dead theory and into life; what it has hurled itself into is rather the consciousness of a lifelessness that is its own, and it falls to its own lot only as the empty and alien necessity, as _dead_ actuality.

The transition takes place out of the form of the _One_ and over into the form of _universality_, out of one absolute abstraction and over into another; out of a purpose that is pure _being-for-itself_, one that has thrown off community with _others_, and over into the _pure_ opposite, into the _being-in-itself_ that is thereby {{{ 303 }}} just as abstract. {{ 201 }} Thus the matter appears as though the individual has merely gone to ruin, and as though the absolute brittleness of singularity has been pulverized against an actuality that is just as hard but continuous. — Because, as consciousness, it constitutes the unity of itself with its own opposite, this downfall is still there for it: there for it are its purpose and the actualization of that purpose, together with the contradiction between what counted _for it_ as the essence and what _in itself_ is the essence; — it undergoes the double sense residing in what it did, namely in having _taken_ its own _life_; life is what it took, but what it thereby seized was death instead.

This _transition_, whereby its living being passes into lifeless necessity, therefore strikes it as an inversion for which nothing at all supplies the mediation. A mediating term would have to be something in which the two sides were one, so that consciousness could cognize each moment within the other: its purpose and its doing within fate, its fate within its purpose and its doing, and _its own essence_ within this _necessity_. For this consciousness, however, that unity is precisely pleasure itself, that is, the _simple, singular_ feeling, and when it passes from the moment constituted by this purpose of its own over to the moment of its true essence, this is for it a bare leap into the opposite; for feeling neither contains these moments nor links them together; they are contained and linked solely within the pure self, a self that is something universal, or {{{ 304 }}} thinking. By way of its experience, in which its truth was to have come to it, consciousness has therefore turned into a riddle for itself instead: the consequences of its deeds do not count for it as its own deeds; what happens to it is not, _for it_, an experiencing of what it _in itself_ is; and the transition is not a mere change of form in one and the same content and essence, represented at one time as the content and essence of consciousness, at another as its object or as its own essence _intuited_. _Abstract necessity_ therefore counts as the merely negative and uncomprehended _power of universality_, against which individuality is dashed to pieces.

The appearance that this shape of self-consciousness makes reaches just this far; the final moment of its existence lies in thinking its own loss within necessity, or in thinking itself to be an essence that is absolutely _alien_ to itself. _In itself_, however, self-consciousness has outlived this loss, for _its own_ essence is precisely this necessity, or pure universality. Consciousness reflected into itself in this way, knowing necessity as _itself_, constitutes a new shape of consciousness.
