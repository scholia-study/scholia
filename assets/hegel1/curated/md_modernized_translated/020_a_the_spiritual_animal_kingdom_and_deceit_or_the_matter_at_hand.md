## a. The Spiritual Animal Kingdom and Deceit, or the Matter at Hand

{{ 216 }} This individuality real in itself is at first once more a _singular_ and _determinate_ one; hence absolute reality, which is what it knows itself to be, has for it, in the manner in which it grows conscious of that reality, the form of the _abstract universal_, and this form, void of fulfillment and of content, amounts to no more than this category thought emptily. — What remains to be seen is how this concept of individuality real in itself gains determination in its moments, and how individuality's own concept of itself steps into consciousness for it.

The concept of this individuality, as being, as such, all reality for itself, is at first a _result;_ it has not yet exhibited its movement and its reality, and is here posited _immediately_ as _simple being-in-itself_. Negativity, however, being nothing other than what shows itself as movement, attaches to the _simple in-itself_ in the guise of _determinateness;_ and so _being_, the simple in-itself, takes on a determinate compass. {{{ 334 }}} Individuality accordingly makes its entrance as a nature originally determinate, — as an _original_ nature, since it _is in itself_, — as originally — _determinate_, since the negative attaches to the _in-itself_, which is thereby a quality. Yet this restriction upon being leaves _the doing_ of consciousness _unrestricted_, since that doing is here a consummated _self-to-self_ relating; what has been sublated is precisely the relation to something other, which would have restricted it. Nature's original determinateness thus amounts to no more than a simple principle, — to an element at once universal and transparent, in which individuality stays every bit as free and equal to itself as it unfolds its distinctions there unimpeded, and in which, throughout its actualization, it is pure interplay with itself. Just as indeterminate animal life breathes its breath into, say, the element of water, of air, or of earth, and within these into further determinate principles, immerses all its moments in them, yet, that limitation of the element notwithstanding, keeps them in its power and keeps itself in its own oneness, and as this particular organization remains the same universal animal life.

This determinate original _nature_ of a consciousness that stays free and entire within it shows up as the sole and directly given _content_ of whatever counts as the individual's purpose; it is _determinate_ content, granted, yet it is _content_ at all only so long as we isolate being-_in-itself_ for consideration; in {{{ 335 }}} truth, though, this content is reality shot through with individuality, the actuality which consciousness, as a singular, has upon itself, and which stands posited to begin with _as being_, not yet as doing. {{ 217 }} For doing, on one side, that determinateness is no barrier that doing would seek to overstep, since, taken as a quality that simply is, it supplies the plain coloring of the element within which doing moves; on the other side, negativity is _determinateness_ only where being is concerned, whereas _doing_ is itself nothing but negativity; in the individuality that acts, then, determinateness has been dissolved into negativity as such, into the epitome of every determinateness.

Now, in _doing_ and in the consciousness of doing, this simple original nature passes over into the difference proper to doing. It is present _first_ as an object, and indeed as an _object_ such as still belongs to _consciousness_, as a _purpose_, and so as opposed to an actuality present at hand. The _other_ moment is the _movement_ of that purpose which had been represented as at rest, namely actualization, purpose set in relation to actuality taken as wholly formal, and with this the representation of _transition_ as such, or the _means_. Lastly, the _third_ is the object no longer as purpose, of which the doer is immediately conscious as _its own_, but as it stands outside the doer and is, _for it_, an _other_. — These various sides {{{ 336 }}} must now, though, be held to in keeping with the concept of this sphere, so that one and the same content persists throughout them and no difference creeps in: not between individuality and being as such, nor of _purpose_ as against _individuality_ qua _original nature_, nor as against the actuality present at hand, nor again of the _means_ as against individuality qua absolute _purpose_, nor of the _actuality brought about_ as against purpose, or original nature, or means.

To begin with, then, individuality's originally determinate nature, its immediate essence, has not yet been posited as something that does, and under that description it goes by the name of a _particular_ aptitude, talent, character, and so on. Such a peculiar tincture of spirit must be viewed as the one and only content of the purpose itself, and as reality and nothing besides. Were one to represent consciousness as passing beyond this tincture, bent on bringing some further content into actuality, one would be representing it as _a nothing_ that labors into _nothingness_. — This original essence furnishes more than the purpose's content; in itself it is also that _actuality_ which otherwise shows up as the _given_ stuff on which doing works, as an actuality _already found there_ and awaiting formation in the doing. For doing is only the pure translating of being out of the form of the not-yet-exhibited into that of the exhibited; the being-in-itself of that actuality opposed to consciousness has sunk to a mere empty semblance. {{{ 337 }}} {{ 218 }} This consciousness, in resolving upon action, is accordingly not thrown off course by the semblance that the actuality present at hand puts up, and it has likewise to gather itself, out of any drifting among empty thoughts and purposes, back onto the original content of its essence. — This original content is admittedly only _for_ consciousness _once consciousness has actualized it_; but the difference between something _that for_ consciousness is only _within consciousness_, and an actuality that is in itself outside it has fallen away. — Only that there may be _for it_ what it is _in itself_ must consciousness act; acting, in other words, is precisely spirit's becoming _as consciousness_. It comes to know from its actuality, then, what it is _in itself_. The individual cannot, therefore, know what _it is_ before it has brought itself to actuality through doing. — But it seems on this account unable to determine the _purpose_ of its doing before it has done; yet at the same time, being consciousness, it must have the action before it beforehand as _wholly its own_, that is, as _purpose_. The individual setting about action therefore looks to be caught in a circle where either moment presupposes the other in advance, and so to be without any starting point, since it learns what its original essence is, the essence that has to serve as its purpose, _only out of the deed_, and yet, in order to do anything, must _have the purpose beforehand_. For just that reason, however, it must make a start _immediately_ and, in whatever circumstances {{{ 338 }}} it finds itself, move on into activity without further pondering over _beginning, means_, and _end_; for its essence, its nature as it is _in itself_, is all of these at once: beginning, means, and end. As _beginning_, that nature lies ready in the _circumstances_ of acting, and whatever _interest_ the individual takes in something is already the answer given to the question whether there is anything to do here and what it is. For what looks like an actuality already found there is in itself the individual's own original nature, which carries only the semblance of a _being_ — a semblance grounded in the concept of a doing that splits itself in two — but which, being _its_ nature originally, gives voice to itself in the _interest_ the individual takes in it. — The _how_, that is, the _means_, is in the same way determined in and for itself. Nor is _talent_ anything else than determinate original individuality regarded as the _inner means_, or as the purpose's _transition_ into actuality. What forms the _actual_ means, though, the real transition, is talent taken in unity with the nature of the matter as that nature is present in the interest; talent supplies, within the means, the side of doing, the nature of the matter the side of content, and both are individuality itself, as the interpenetration of being and doing. What is present at hand, then, is _circumstances_ found already there, which _in themselves_ are the original nature of the individual; then the interest, which posits them precisely as _its own_ or as _purpose_; finally the linking and sublation of this opposition in the _means_. This linkage still falls {{{ 339 }}} inside consciousness itself, and the whole just surveyed forms but one term of an opposition. {{ 219 }} What still lingers of this semblance of opposedness gets sublated by the _transition_ itself, by the _means_; — for the means constitutes the _unity_ of outer and inner, the reverse of that determinateness which belongs to it qua _inner_ means, so that it sublates that determinateness and posits this unity of doing and being as _outer_ as well, as individuality itself grown actual, individuality, that is, posited _for its own self_ as what _is_. In this way the whole action steps outside itself neither as the _circumstances_, nor as _purpose_, nor _means_, nor as _work_.

With the work, though, a difference among the original natures appears to set in; the work is something _determinate_, exactly as the original nature it voices is determinate, for, released from doing as _actuality that is_, it carries negativity upon it as quality. Consciousness, however, determines itself over against the work as that which bears determinateness upon itself as negativity _as such_, as doing; over against that determinateness of the work it is thus the universal, so that it can _compare_ the work with others and on that basis take the individualities themselves to be _various_; the individual whose work reaches out further is taken as an energy of will more powerful, or as a nature of greater wealth, one, that is, whose original determinateness is less confined; — another, by contrast, as a nature weaker [and] {{{ 340 }}} more scanty. Set against this inessential difference of _magnitude_, the pair _good_ and _bad_ would state a difference that is absolute; here, however, no such difference occurs. Take it the one way or the other, what we have is equally a doing and a bustling about, an individuality exhibiting and voicing itself, and hence everything is good, and there would strictly be no saying what the bad might be. What one would call a bad work is nothing but the individual living of a determinate nature that actualizes itself therein; only the comparing thought would spoil it into a bad work, and that thought is an empty affair, since it reaches past what the work essentially is, an individuality voicing itself, and searches in it, and demands of it, one knows not what besides. — That thought could bear only on the difference cited a moment ago; but such a difference, being one of magnitude, is in itself inessential, and it counts as inessential here because what would stand compared would be individualities, or works, that differ; and these have nothing to do with one another; each relates solely to itself. {{ 220 }} The original nature alone is the _in-itself_, the one thing that could be laid down as a standard for judging the work and the work for judging it; yet the two answer to each other exactly: nothing is _for_ individuality that is not also _through_ it, or, put otherwise, no _actuality_ exists that is not individuality's nature and its {{{ 341 }}} doing, and no doing or in-itself of individuality exists that is not actual, and it is these moments alone that admit of comparison.

Hence there is in general neither _exaltation_, nor _lament_, nor remorse; for all such things come from the thought that imagines to itself another _content_ and another _in-itself_ than the original nature of the individual and its execution present in actuality are. Whatever it does and whatever befalls it, this it has done, and this it is itself; all it can have is the consciousness of purely translating _its own self_ out of the night of possibility into the daylight of the present, translating the _abstract in-itself_ over into the sense that belongs to _actual_ being, together with the certainty that what meets it in the daylight is none other than what lay asleep in that night. Being conscious of this unity is admittedly a comparing too, yet what gets compared bears no more than the _semblance_ of opposition; a semblance belonging to form, one which, for reason's self-consciousness, that individuality is on its own account actuality, counts as nothing more than semblance. Since the individual knows that in its actuality it can find nothing other than actuality's unity with itself, or only the certainty of itself in actuality's truth, and that it therefore always attains its purpose, it can _only experience joy in itself_.

{{{ 342 }}} Such is the concept that consciousness forms of itself when it is certain of itself as the absolute interpenetration of individuality with being; the question now is whether experience bears that concept out for it, and whether its reality tallies with the concept. Reality as consciousness confers it upon itself is the work; in the work the individual is, for itself, what it is _in itself_, and does so in a way such that the consciousness _for which_ the individual thus comes about is, within the work, not particular consciousness but the _universal_ one; in the work consciousness has set itself out into the element of universality at large, into the space of being that is void of determinateness. Consciousness as it withdraws from its work really is the universal, — since within this opposition it becomes the _absolute negativity_, or the doing, — set over against its work, which is the _determinate_ one; consciousness therefore reaches out past itself qua work, and is itself that space void of determinateness which finds no filling in its work. If a moment ago their unity did maintain itself within the concept, that happened precisely through the work's being sublated qua work that _is_. Yet the work is to _be_, and it must be seen how, within the work's _being_, individuality will keep the work's universality intact and will find its way to satisfaction. — First of all the work that has come to be is to be considered for itself. {{ 221 }} Along with everything else it has taken up individuality's whole nature; its _being_ is on that account itself a doing, one in which all differences penetrate one another {{{ 343 }}} and dissolve; the work is thus flung out into a _subsistence_ where the original nature's _determinateness_ does indeed turn outward against other determinate natures, encroaches upon them as they encroach upon it, and loses itself as a vanishing moment within this universal movement. While _within_ the _concept_ of individuality real in and for itself all the moments, namely circumstances, purpose, means, and actualization, stand equal to one another, and the original determinate nature holds good merely as a universal element, once that element turns into objective being its _determinateness_ qua determinateness breaks into daylight in the work, and wins its truth in being dissolved. Looked at more closely, that dissolution presents itself thus: within this determinateness the individual has made itself actual as _this_ one; but determinateness furnishes not merely actuality's content, it furnishes actuality's form as well, or rather actuality at large just is this determinateness of standing opposed to self-consciousness. From this side actuality shows itself as that which has vanished out of the concept, as a merely _alien actuality found already there_. The work _is_, that is, it exists for other individualities, and for these it is an actuality that is alien, one in whose room _they_ have to install their own if they are to give themselves, by way of _their_ doing, the consciousness of _their_ unity with actuality; or else _their_ interest in that {{{ 344 }}} work, posited through _their_ own original nature, differs from the interest _peculiar_ to this work, which is thereby made into something else. The work, then, is on the whole a perishable thing, snuffed out by the counterplay of other forces and interests, and it exhibits individuality's reality as vanishing rather than as brought to completion.

So it is that in its work consciousness sees the opposition of doing and being arise, an opposition that in consciousness's earlier shapes served at once as the _beginning_ of doing, whereas here it is merely a _result_. In fact, however, it likewise lay at the ground when consciousness, as individuality real _in itself_, set about acting; for the _determinate original nature_ was presupposed to the acting as the _in-itself_, and pure accomplishing for the sake of accomplishing had that nature as its _content_. But pure doing is the form _equal to itself_, and to that form the original nature's _determinateness_ is on this account unequal. Here as elsewhere nothing hangs on which of the two we name _concept_ and which _reality_; the original nature is what is _thought_, the _in-itself_, as against the doing wherein it first acquires reality; or, put the other way, the original nature constitutes the _being_ both of individuality as such and of individuality as work, while doing is the original _concept_, absolute transition, or _becoming_. This _inadequacy_ between concept and reality, an inadequacy lodged in the concept's own essence, is what {{{ 345 }}} consciousness undergoes in its work; there, accordingly, it becomes for itself what it truly is, while the empty concept it had of itself disappears.

{{ 222 }} Within this basic contradiction of the work, in which the truth of this individuality real in itself consists, every side of that individuality steps forward once more as contradictory; or rather the work, being the whole individuality's content as brought out of the _doing_ that is the negative unity and holds all the moments captive, and set forth into _being_, now releases them; and in the element of subsistence they grow indifferent to one another. Concept and reality thus part company, the one as purpose, the other as what constitutes the _original essentiality_. Whether the purpose has a true essence, or whether the in-itself gets made into purpose, is a matter of contingency. In the same way concept and reality once again step apart, the one as _transition_ into actuality, the other as _purpose_; or, what comes to the same, whether the _means_ that voices the purpose gets chosen is contingent. And finally, taking these inner moments together, whether or not they possess a unity among themselves, the individual's _doing_ is once more contingent as against _actuality_ at large; _luck_ decides, and it decides in favor of an ill-determined purpose and ill-chosen means quite as readily as against them.

If, then, what thus arises for consciousness on its work is the _opposition_ between willing and accomplishing, between purpose and means, and again between these inner ones taken together and actuality itself, {{{ 346 }}} everything covered by the phrase _the contingency of its doing taken in itself_, all the same its _unity_ and its _necessity_ are no less at hand; this latter side overreaches the former, and the _experience_ of _doing's contingency_ is itself no more than a _contingent experience_. What the _necessity_ of doing consists in is this, that _purpose_ stands related without qualification to _actuality_, and that unity is the very concept of doing; one acts because doing, in and for itself, is what actuality essentially is. In the work, admittedly, there shows itself the contingency that _being-accomplished_ has as against _willing_ and _accomplishing_, and this experience, which seems bound to count as the truth, stands in contradiction with the concept of action just given. Should we take this experience's content in its full compass, however, that content is the _vanishing work;_ what _maintains itself_ is not the _vanishing_, for the vanishing is itself actual and bound up with the work, and vanishes along with it; the _negative perishes_ together with the _positive of which it is the negation_, and perishes _in its own right_.

That the vanishing should itself vanish is something already contained in the concept of individuality real in itself; for whatever it is that the work vanishes into, or whatever on the work vanishes, and whatever was to lend what has been called experience its ascendancy over the concept individuality holds of itself, is _objective actuality_; but that {{{ 347 }}} actuality is a moment which, even within this consciousness itself, retains no truth of its own, that truth lying solely in the unity of actuality with doing, and the _true work_ consists in nothing else than that unity of _doing and being_, of _willing_ and _accomplishing_. {{ 223 }} For consciousness, then, on the strength of the certainty underlying its acting, the actuality _opposed_ to that certainty is itself something which is merely _for it_; to consciousness as a self-consciousness gone back into itself, for which every opposition has fallen away, no opposition can arise any longer in this shape of its _being-for-itself_ as against _actuality_; instead, the opposition and the negativity that surface on the work bear not just on the work's content, or on the content of consciousness, but on actuality as such, and with that on the opposition which exists only through actuality and upon it, and on the work's vanishing. Consciousness accordingly reflects itself, out of its perishable work, back into itself, and holds fast to its concept and its certainty as what _is_ and what _abides_, in the face of the experience of doing's _contingency_; what it undergoes is in truth its own concept, wherein actuality figures only as a moment, as something _for it_, not as the in-and-for-itself; it undergoes actuality as a moment that vanishes, so that actuality is valid for it merely as _being_ at large, whose universality is one and the same with doing. That unity is what the work truly is; it is _the matter at hand_, which asserts itself without qualification and {{{ 348 }}} is experienced as what abides, independently of the matter that consists in the _contingency_ of individual doing as such, of circumstances, of means, and of actuality.

_The matter at hand_ is opposed to these moments only insofar as they are supposed to count in isolation, but essentially, as the interpenetration of actuality and individuality, it is their unity; it is just as much a doing, and as doing _pure doing_ as such, _and thereby just as much the doing of this individual_, and this doing, as still belonging to the individual in opposition to actuality, is _purpose;_ likewise it is the _transition_ out of this determinateness into the opposed one; and finally an _actuality_ which is present _for consciousness_. What _the matter at hand_ voices, accordingly, is that _spiritual_ essentiality wherein all these moments stand sublated so far as they were valid on their own account, and are therefore valid only as universal ones, and wherein consciousness finds its certainty of itself to be an objective essence, _a matter_; an object to which self-consciousness has given birth as to what is _its own_, and which does not thereby stop being a free object in its own right. — The _thing_ of sense-certainty and of perceiving now has its significance for self-consciousness solely through self-consciousness; on this rests the difference between a _thing_ and a _matter_. — A movement corresponding to that of sense-certainty and perception will be run through on the matter.

{{{ 349 }}} In _the matter at hand_, then, as the interpenetration of individuality and objectivity itself that has become objective, self-consciousness has arrived at its true concept of itself, or has come to the consciousness of its substance. As it stands here, this consciousness of substance has only just come about and is on that account an _immediate_ one, and that is the determinate way in which spiritual essence is on hand at this stage, not yet having ripened into substance truly real. {{ 224 }} Within this immediate consciousness of it, _the matter at hand_ takes the form of _simple essence_, an essence that, being universal, holds all its various moments inside itself and belongs to them, yet is also once more indifferent toward them qua determinate moments and free on its own account, and that, as this free, _simple, abstract_ matter at hand, _is valid as the essence_. Taken singly, the moments belonging to the original determinateness, that is, to the _matter of this_ individual, to its purpose, to the means, to the doing itself, and to actuality, count for such a consciousness on one side as singular moments which it may forsake and surrender for the sake of _the matter at hand_; on the other side, all of them have the matter at hand for their essence only in this sense, that the matter at hand, as their _abstract_ universal, is to be found _upon_ each one of these several moments and can serve as their _predicate_. The matter at hand is not itself the subject as yet; those moments are what counts as subject, since it is on the side of _singularity_ at large that they fall, whereas the matter at hand as yet {{{ 350 }}} amounts only to what is simply universal. It is the _genus_ that discovers itself throughout all these moments as so many _species_ of it, and is just as free of them.

We call a consciousness _honest_ when it has, for one thing, reached the idealism voiced by _the matter at hand_, and, for another, possesses what is true in that matter taken as this formal universality; a consciousness whose sole concern is always the matter at hand, which therefore knocks about among the matter's various moments or species, and which, failing to reach the matter in one of them or under one signification, gets hold of it precisely thereby under another, so that it does in fact always win the satisfaction that, by its own concept, was to be its portion. Let matters turn out as they may, it has accomplished and attained _the matter at hand_, since the matter, being that _universal_ genus of those moments, is predicate of them all.

Should it fail to bring some _purpose_ to _actuality_, it has at least _willed_ that purpose, which is to say that it turns _purpose_ qua purpose, that _pure doing_ which accomplishes nothing, into _the matter at hand;_ and it can accordingly put the case to itself, and take comfort, by saying that something at any rate got _done_ and _bustled about_. Since the universal has the negative, or vanishing, under it, the work's annihilating itself is likewise _its_ own doing; it goaded the others into that, and it still finds satisfaction in the _vanishing_ of its actuality, much as naughty boys enjoy _their own selves_ in {{{ 351 }}} the box on the ear they receive, namely as the cause of it. Or if it has _not even tried_ to carry out the matter at hand and has _done nothing at all_, then it did not _want to; the matter at hand_ is for it precisely the _unity_ of its _resolve_ and of _reality_; it asserts that _actuality_ would be nothing other than its own _wanting_. {{ 225 }} — Finally, if something interesting to it has come about without any doing on its part, then this _actuality_ is for it the matter at hand precisely in the interest it finds in it, even though the actuality was not brought forth by it; if it is a piece of luck that has befallen it personally, it insists on this as its _deed_ and _merit;_ if it is some world event that otherwise does not concern it, it makes that event just as much its own, and _deedless interest_ counts for it as the _party_ it has taken for or against the event, and as having _fought_ it or _upheld_ it.

As is plain, the _honesty_ of this consciousness, and equally the satisfaction it meets with on every side, in fact rests on this: that it fails to bring the _thoughts_ it entertains about the matter at hand _into connection. The matter at hand_ is for it quite as much _its own_ matter as it is _no work at all_, or the _pure doing_ and the _empty purpose_, or else an _actuality without deed;_ each signification in turn is made subject of this predicate, and each in turn is forgotten. Now, in the mere _having-willed_, or else in the _not-having-wanted_, the matter at hand carries the signification {{{ 352 }}} of the _empty purpose_ and of the merely _thought_ unity of willing with accomplishing. Consolation over the purpose's annihilation, on the score of having at least _willed_ it or at least _done something pure_, together with the satisfaction of having handed the others a task, elevates _pure doing_, or the utterly bad work, into the essence; for what deserves to be called bad is what is no work whatever. And in the lucky event of _coming upon_ actuality ready-made, this being-without-deed becomes the matter at hand.

The truth of this honesty, however, is that it is not so honest as it looks. For it cannot be thoughtless enough actually to leave these several moments lying apart in this fashion; it must rather be immediately conscious of the opposition between them, seeing that they are related to one another without qualification. _Pure_ doing is essentially the doing of _this_ individual, and this doing is just as essentially an _actuality_, or a matter. Conversely, _actuality_ is essentially only as _its_ doing, just as it is only as _doing as such;_ and _its_ doing, just as it is doing as such, is at the same time also actuality. So while it looks as though its concern were only with _the matter at hand_ qua _abstract actuality_, there is present in this the further fact that its concern is with the matter as _its own_ doing. But equally, while it is concerned only with _doing_ and _bustling about_, it is not in earnest about that either; rather, it is concerned with _a matter_, and with the matter as _its own_. And finally, while it seems to want only _its own_ {{{ 353 }}} matter and _its own_ doing, it is again concerned with the _matter as such_, or with the actuality that endures in and for itself.

Just as the matter at hand and its moments appear here as _content_, so too they are just as necessarily present _as forms_ on consciousness. They make their entrance as content solely so as to disappear again, each yielding its place to the next. Their presence, then, must have the determinateness of the _sublated_; and so present, they are sides of consciousness itself. {{ 226 }} _The matter at hand_ is there as the _in-itself_, or as consciousness's _reflection into itself_, whereas the way the moments _crowd_ one another out finds expression upon consciousness in this, that they are posited upon it not in themselves but only for _another_. Consciousness exposes one moment of the content to the daylight and represents it _for others_; yet consciousness stands at the same time reflected out of that moment into itself, and the opposed moment is no less present within it; this latter it holds back for itself, as its own. Nor, for that matter, is there any particular one of them that would be _solely_ put outside while another would be kept purely within; consciousness rather alternates between them, since it has to make the one no less than the other into what is essential both for itself and for the other party. _The whole_ is individuality and the universal interpenetrating in movement; but since for this consciousness the whole is on hand merely as the _simple_ essence, and hence as the abstraction _of the matter at hand_, its moments fall, in their separateness, {{{ 354 }}} outside the matter and away from one another; and _as a whole_ it gets exhausted and exhibited only through the separating alternation of putting on show and holding back for itself. Given that in this alternation consciousness holds one moment as its own and as essential within its reflection, while it holds another only outwardly upon _itself_ or for the _others_, what sets in is a game individualities play with each other, in which they find themselves and one another alike deceiving and deceived.

An individuality, then, sets about carrying something out; it thereby seems to have made something _into a matter_; it acts, in acting comes to be for others, and it seems to be concerned with _actuality_. The others therefore take its doing for an interest in the matter as such, and for the purpose _that the matter be carried out in itself;_ indifferently whether by the first individuality or by them. So when they either exhibit this matter as one they have already brought off, or, failing that, offer their help and give it, that first consciousness has in fact already moved on from where they take it to stand; what interests it in the matter is _its own_ doing and bustling about, and once they notice that this was _the matter at hand_, they find themselves fooled. — Yet their hurrying up to lend a hand was in truth nothing but a wish to see and display _their_ doing rather than _the matter at hand;_ that is, they meant to deceive the other exactly as they now protest at having been deceived {{{ 355 }}} themselves. — Now that it has come out into the open that _one's own doing_ and _bustling about_, the play _of one's own forces_, counts as the matter at hand, consciousness seems to be pursuing its essence _for itself_ and not for the others, and, concerned only with the doing as _its own_ and not as a doing of the _others_, to be leaving the others just as free in _their_ matter. {{ 227 }} But they are mistaken again; consciousness is already out of the place where they supposed it to be. It is not concerned with the matter as _this singular matter of its own_, but with the matter as _matter_, as something universal that is for all. It meddles, then, in their doing and their work, and where the work can no longer be taken out of their hands, it makes itself a party to it at least by busying itself with passing judgment; should it set upon the work the stamp of its approval and praise, what is meant is that in praising the work it praises along with it _its own_ magnanimity and restraint in having spoiled the work neither as work nor by its censure. In taking an interest in the _work_, what it enjoys therein is _its own self_; and by the same token the _work_ that it censures is welcome to it, precisely because that same enjoyment of _its own_ doing is thereby procured for it. Those, however, who take themselves to be deceived by such meddling, or who give themselves out as deceived, were rather bent on deceiving in just the same fashion. They pass off their doing and bustling about as something existing only for themselves, wherein they {{{ 356 }}} had as their purpose nothing but _themselves_ and _their own_ essence. Yet in doing something, and so exhibiting themselves and bringing themselves before the daylight, they give their pretension the lie by the very deed, the pretension of meaning to shut out daylight itself, universal consciousness, and everyone's participation; actualization is on the contrary a putting of what is one's own on display within the universal element, whereby it becomes, and is meant to become, the _matter_ of all.

It is therefore just as much a deceit of itself and of the others if what is supposedly at issue is only the _pure matter_; a consciousness that opens up some matter rather learns from experience that the others come rushing up, like flies to milk just set out, and want to know themselves busy with it; and they learn from it that what concerns it is likewise not the matter as an object, but the matter as _its own_. If, on the other hand, the essential element is supposed to be only the _doing itself_, the employment of forces and capacities, or this individuality's giving utterance to itself, then reciprocally the same experience arises: _all_ bestir themselves and reckon that the invitation extends to them, and what gets opened up, rather than a _pure_ doing or a _singular_ doing peculiar to one person, proves to be something that holds just as much _for others_, that is, _a matter at hand_. What occurs is the same in either case; it merely carries a different sense from the one assumed in it and meant to be valid. Both sides come to be experienced by consciousness as moments of equal {{{ 357 }}} essentiality, and therein it experiences what the _nature of the matter at hand_ is: neither a mere matter, one that would stand over against doing in general and against singular doing, nor a doing standing over against subsistence, one that would be the _genus_ free of these moments as its _species_, but rather an essence, the _being_ of which is the _doing_ of the _singular_ individual as well as of all individuals, and the doing of which is immediately _for others_, hence a _matter_, and which is a matter only as the _doing of All_ and _of Each_; the essence, namely, which is the essence of every essence, the _spiritual essence_. {{ 228 }} Consciousness experiences that none of those moments is _subject_, but that each rather dissolves in the _universal matter at hand_; the moments of individuality, which for the thoughtlessness of this consciousness counted one after another as the subject, gather themselves together into the simple individuality that, as _this_ one, is just as immediately universal. With this the matter at hand loses the relationship of predicate as well as the determinateness of a lifeless, abstract universality; it is instead substance shot through with individuality, the subject in which individuality is present just as much as itself, or as _this_ individual, as it is present as _all_ individuals, and the universal that has _being_ only as this doing of All and of Each, having its actuality in the fact that _this_ consciousness knows it to be at once its own singular actuality and the actuality of All. That pure _matter at hand_ is precisely what was determined earlier on as the _category_, where being is the I and the I is being, though as _thinking_ that still holds itself apart from _actual {{{ 358 }}} self-consciousness_; here, by contrast, the moments belonging to actual self-consciousness, whether we name them its content, its purpose, its doing and its actuality, or name them its form, being-for-itself and being for another, are posited together with the simple category itself as being one, and it is for that reason all content at the same time.
