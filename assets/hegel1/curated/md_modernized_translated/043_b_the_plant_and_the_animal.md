## {{{ 643 }}} b. The Plant and the Animal

Self-conscious spirit, having gone into itself out of the shapeless essence, or having raised its immediacy into the self in general, gives its simplicity the determination of a manifoldness of being-for-itself; it is the religion of spiritual _perception_, in which spirit breaks apart into the countless plurality of weaker and stronger, richer and poorer spirits. This pantheism, which is at first the _tranquil_ subsistence of these spirit-atoms, turns within itself into a _hostile_ movement. The innocence belonging to the _flower-religion_, which is nothing but a selfless representation of the self, crosses over into the earnestness of a life of struggle, into the guilt of the _animal-religion_, and the repose and powerlessness of the intuiting individuality crosses over into a being-for-itself that destroys. — Nothing is gained by having stripped the things of perception of the _death of abstraction_ and having raised them into essences of spiritual perception; the ensouling of this realm of spirits carries that death within it by way of determinateness and the negativity residing in determinateness, which reaches out across the innocent indifference of those things. Owing to that negativity, the scattering into the manifoldness of quiet plant-shapes is made into a hostile movement, one in which the hatred that belongs to their being-for-itself wears them away. — This scattered spirit's _actual_ self-consciousness {{{ 644 }}} is a multitude of singularized, unsociable spirits of peoples that fight one another to the death out of their hatred, growing conscious of determinate animal-shapes as their essence, for they are nothing other than animal spirits, animal lives that cut themselves off and are conscious of themselves without universality.

Within this hatred, though, the determinateness proper to a being-for-itself that is purely negative wears itself away, and it is this movement of the concept that carries spirit over into a different shape. _Sublated being-for-itself_ constitutes the _form_ belonging to the _object_, a form which the self has brought forth, or rather which is the very self that has been brought forth, the self that wears itself away, that is, that becomes a thing. The one who labors therefore prevails over animal spirits that merely tear things apart, since what he does is not only negative but pacified and positive. {{ 373 }} From now on, then, the consciousness of spirit is the movement that lies beyond immediate _being-in-itself_ just as much as beyond abstract _being-for-itself_. Since the in-itself has been demoted through opposition into a determinateness, it is no longer the form proper to absolute spirit, but an actuality which the consciousness of spirit finds set over against itself as ordinary existence and which it sublates, and consciousness is likewise not merely this sublating being-for-itself but also brings forth its representation of it, namely being-for-itself put out into the form of an object. This bringing-forth is not yet the accomplished kind, however, but a conditioned activity and the forming of something already at hand.
